# 考研英语作文模板

> [!info] 使用说明
> 下方模板适用于**图画作文 / 图表作文**的**第二段（论证段）**，按话题倾向分为"正面积极"与"负面问题"两类。`**[...]**` 为占位符，需根据具体题目替换。模板采用三论点递进结构（First and foremost / Furthermore / Last but not least），可灵活复用。

---

## 一、正面积极类话题

> [!tip] 适用场景
> 阐述某种美德、好的现象或积极趋势，如：**创新、合作、坚持、自律、勤奋**等。
>
> 整体用词升级为更地道、更具学术感的表达。

### 模板正文

> [!quote] First and foremost, it is undeniable that **[完整句子：核心论点一]**. This is powerfully evidenced by the fact that a vast majority of accomplished individuals credit their triumphs to this invaluable quality, seamlessly integrating it into their daily endeavors.
>
> Furthermore, it is evident that **[完整句子：核心论点二]**. This establishes a virtuous cycle where the cultivation of **[名词 / 代词]** inevitably fosters **[名词：积极结果]**.
>
> Last but not least, one cannot overlook the truth that **[完整句子：核心论点三]**. The underlying logic is straightforward: the more actively one engages in **[动名词 Doing / 名词]**, the more profound the long-term benefits become.

### 核心表达速查

| 位置 | 关键句式 | 功能 |
|------|----------|------|
| 段首总起 | `It is undeniable that...` | 引出不可争议的事实 |
| 论据支撑 | `This is powerfully evidenced by the fact that...` | 提供事实论据 |
| 逻辑递进 | `it is evident that...` | 自然过渡到第二点 |
| 良性循环 | `establishes a virtuous cycle where... inevitably fosters...` | 强调正反馈机制 |
| 总结升华 | `one cannot overlook the truth that...` | 引出最终论点 |
| 深层逻辑 | `The underlying logic is straightforward: the more..., the more...` | 揭示越……越…… 的规律 |

## 二、负面问题类话题

> [!warning] 适用场景
> 应对批判类、社会问题类图画，如：**过度消费、沉迷网络、盲目从众、环境污染、资源浪费**等。
>
> 将"成功、良性循环、益处"对冲替换为"危害、恶性循环、后果"。

### 模板正文

> [!quote] First and foremost, it is undeniable that **[完整句子：核心论点一：负面现象 / 原因]**. This is alarmingly illustrated by the reality that an increasing number of individuals suffer from this very issue, which severely disrupts their daily lives.
>
> Furthermore, it is evident that **[完整句子：核心论点二]**. This triggers a vicious cycle where the prevalence of **[名词：负面现象]** inevitably breeds **[名词：不良恶果]**.
>
> Last but not least, one cannot overlook the truth that **[完整句子：核心论点三]**. The underlying logic is clear: the more deeply one is entangled in **[动名词 Doing / 名词]**, the more detrimental the resulting consequences will become.

### 核心表达速查

| 位置 | 关键句式 | 功能 |
|------|----------|------|
| 段首总起 | `It is undeniable that...` | 引出不可争议的事实 |
| 论据支撑 | `This is alarmingly illustrated by the reality that...` | 强调问题的严重性 |
| 逻辑递进 | `it is evident that...` | 自然过渡到第二点 |
| 恶性循环 | `triggers a vicious cycle where... inevitably breeds...` | 强调负反馈机制 |
| 总结升华 | `one cannot overlook the truth that...` | 引出最终论点 |
| 深层逻辑 | `The underlying logic is clear: the more..., the more detrimental...` | 揭示越陷越深的后果 |

## 三、两类话题对照

> [!note] 一图看懂两套模板的差异

| 维度 | 正面积极类 | 负面问题类 |
|------|------------|------------|
| 段首连接词 | First and foremost | First and foremost |
| 论据修饰词 | powerfully evidenced | alarmingly illustrated |
| 中段逻辑词 | it is evident that | it is evident that |
| 循环机制 | **virtuous** cycle | **vicious** cycle |
| 关键动词 | fosters（培养） | breeds（滋生） |
| 深层逻辑句 | the more actively..., the more profound... | the more deeply entangled..., the more detrimental... |
| 长期结果 | long-term **benefits** | detrimental **consequences** |

## 四、套用示例

> [!example] 以"创新"（正面）为例
>
> - 核心论点一：it is undeniable that **innovation serves as the driving force behind social progress**.
> - 核心论点二：it is evident that **companies that prioritize R&D investment consistently outperform their competitors**.
> - 核心论点三：one cannot overlook the truth that **nurturing a culture of innovation at the educational level lays the foundation for future breakthroughs**.
>
> The underlying logic is straightforward: the more actively one engages in **creative thinking**, the more profound the long-term benefits become.

> [!example] 以"沉迷网络"（负面）为例
>
> - 核心论点一：it is undeniable that **internet addiction has become a pervasive issue, particularly among the youth**.
> - 核心论点二：it is evident that **excessive screen time corrodes face-to-face communication skills**.
> - 核心论点三：one cannot overlook the truth that **prolonged immersion in virtual worlds erodes one's capacity for sustained attention**.
>
> The underlying logic is clear: the more deeply one is entangled in **mindless scrolling**, the more detrimental the resulting consequences will become.

## 五、备考提示

- [x] 三段式结构稳定：**现象描述 → 原因/影响分析 → 总结建议**
- [x] 区分话题类型，**正面换 virtuous，负面换 vicious**
- [x] 每个核心论点预留 **1 个具体例子**（数据 / 名人 / 现象）以增强说服力
- [x] 控制字数在 **160–200 词**之间，避免模板痕迹过重
- [ ] 考前默写 **2 遍**，确保考场上 5 分钟内可默出
