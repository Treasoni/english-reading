# 内疚感与道德情绪 精读笔记

---

## 背景

本文是 **2019 年考研英语（一）Text 1**（第 21—25 题），主题是**"内疚感（guilt）并非纯粹的负面情绪，适量而为反而有益"**——即为一向"名声不佳"的内疚感翻案。文章从发展心理学的角度切入：与 `so-called basic emotions`（**所谓的基本情绪，如悲伤、恐惧、愤怒**）不同，内疚感的出现 `a little later`，它 `in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms`（**伴随孩子对社会与道德规范的理解而发展**），因此 `researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing`（**第 21 题**：内疚之所以可能是好事，是因为它有助于**促进孩子的道德发展** → [A] `foster a child's moral development`）。

结构上，全文是一条**"提出内疚（道德情绪）→ 流行看法过时、情绪并非二元 → 内疚是社会黏合剂 → 内疚可弥补同情心的不足 → 实验证据 → 结语：亲社会来自内疚"**的推进链：P1 立论（内疚是随道德认知发展的情绪，适量即好）；P2 先摆出**"流行看法"**——`guilt still gets a bad rap`、`It is deeply uncomfortable`（**第 22 题**：许多人仍认为内疚 `burdensome`（**沉重的**）→ [D]，直接对应 `the emotional equivalent of wearing a jacket weighted with stones`），随即用 `Yet this understanding is outdated` 转折，借 **Amrisha Vaish** 之口指出 `this revival … is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary`——**第 23 题**（一种情绪可以发挥相反的作用 → [B] `an emotion can play opposing roles`，对应 `advantageous in one context … harmful in another`）。

文章的重心在 **P4—P5 的证据链**：P3 先用比喻收束——内疚 `can help hold a cooperative species together`，`a kind of social glue`；P4 提出核心命题——`Viewed in this light, guilt is an opportunity`，`guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency`，`guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing`（**第 24 题**：合作与分享可源于同情心或内疚 → [B] `can result from either sympathy or guilt`）；P5 用 2014 年实验佐证——`For the low-sympathy kids, how much they shared appeared to turn on how inclined they were to feel guilty`，`The guilt-prone ones shared more`；末段以 **Tina Malti** 的引语作结——`We can be prosocial because we caused harm and we feel regret`（**第 25 题**词义题落在实验描述中的 `transgressions`（**越界行为、过错**）→ [D] `wrongdoings`）。

**一句话概括**：这篇文章真正想说的是——**"内疚感不是需要消除的坏情绪：它随孩子的道德认知一同成长，能像黏合剂一样维系合作、并弥补同情心的不足；在恰当的分量下，它反而驱动人们弥补过错、修复关系，成为亲社会行为的源泉。"**

## 文章原文

# 2019 Passage 1

Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger, **guilt** **emerges** a little later, **in conjunction with** a child's growing **grasp** of social and moral **norms**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger, **guilt** **emerges** a little later, **in conjunction with** a child's growing **grasp** of social and moral **norms**.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 比较状语 A（介词短语）: Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger
> - 主语 S: guilt
> - 谓语 V: emerges
> - 时间状语 A: a little later
> - 伴随状语 A（介词短语）: in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语作比较状语 | Unlike … | 主句 guilt emerges |
> | 举例同位补充 | such as sadness, fear, and anger | basic emotions |
> | 时间状语 | a little later | emerges |
> | 介词短语作伴随状语 | in conjunction with … | emerges |
> | 名词化结构 | a child's growing grasp of … | 介词宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Unlike so-called basic emotions (such as …)〕+ guilt + emerges + a little later, 〔in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms〕
>   ├── 比较状语: Unlike …（引出对比对象）
>   └── 伴随状语: in conjunction with …（说明"与……同步发生"）
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：与所谓的基本情绪（如悲伤、恐惧和愤怒）不同，内疚感的出现要稍晚一些，它伴随着孩子对社会与道德规范的理解不断增长而产生。
>
> **考点提示**：① 句首 `Unlike …` 是**介词短语作比较状语**（不是分词）：`Unlike` 后接名词短语，与主句主语 `guilt` 构成同类比较。② 主干很简单——`guilt emerges`（内疚感出现）。③ `such as …` 举例补充 `basic emotions`。④ `in conjunction with` = "与……同时、伴随着"，是说明内疚"出现时机"的**伴随状语**。⑤ 本句是**第 21 题的定位依据**：内疚伴随"对社会与道德规范的理解"产生 → 对应"促进孩子的道德发展（moral development）"。

Children aren't born knowing how to say "I'm sorry"; rather, they learn over time that such statements **appease** parents and friends—and their own **consciences**.

This is why researchers generally **regard** so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：This is why researchers generally **regard** so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: This
> - 系动词 V: is
> - 表语从句: why researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing
> - 表语从句·主语 S: researchers
> - 表语从句·状语 A: generally
> - 表语从句·谓语 V: regard
> - 表语从句·宾语 O: so-called moral guilt
> - 表语从句·插入语: in the right amount
> - 表语从句·宾语补足语 C: to be a good thing
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | why 引导表语从句 | This is why … | This |
> | 插入语（双逗号） | in the right amount | moral guilt |
> | 复合结构（宾语 + 宾补） | regard … (to be) a good thing | moral guilt |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: This + is + 〔why researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, (in the right amount), to be a good thing〕
>   └── 表语从句: researchers + regard + moral guilt + (to be) a good thing
>         └── 插入语: in the right amount（修饰 moral guilt）
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：正因如此，研究者普遍认为，所谓"道德内疚"只要分量恰当，就是一件好事。
>
> **考点提示**：① `This is why …` 是**表语从句**句型，意为"这就是……的原因 / 正因如此"。② `regard A (to be) B` 是**复合结构**：`to be a good thing` 作宾语 `moral guilt` 的**补足语**，`to be` 可省略。③ `in the right amount` 是**插入语**（双逗号隔开），修饰 moral guilt，意为"在恰当的分量下"；正是它限定了"适量才是好事"的语气。

In the popular imagination, of course, guilt still **gets a bad rap**. It is deeply uncomfortable—it's the emotional **equivalent** of wearing a jacket weighted with stones. Yet this understanding is **outdated**.

"There has been a kind of **revival** or a **rethinking** about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve," says Amrisha Vaish, a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia, adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't **binary**—feelings that may be **advantageous** in one context may be harmful in another.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**："There has been a kind of **revival** or a **rethinking** about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve," says Amrisha Vaish, a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia, adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't **binary**—feelings that may be **advantageous** in one context may be harmful in another.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 引语（提前，倒装）: "There has been a kind of revival or a rethinking about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve"
> - 谓语 V: says
> - 主语 S: Amrisha Vaish
> - 同位语（双逗号插入）: a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia
> - 伴随状语（现在分词）: adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition …
> - 宾语从句: that this revival is part of a larger recognition
> - 同位语从句: that emotions aren't binary
> - 破折号补充（独立分句）: feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 引述句倒装（引语提前） | "…," says … | 主句 |
> | 同位语（双逗号插入） | a psychology researcher at … | Amrisha Vaish |
> | 现在分词作伴随状语 | adding that … | says |
> | that 引导宾语从句 | that this revival is … | adding |
> | that 引导同位语从句 | that emotions aren't binary | a larger recognition |
> | 破折号解释（同位补充） | —feelings that … | emotions aren't binary |
> | that 引导限制性定语从句 | that may be advantageous in one context | feelings |
> | 介词短语作状语 | in one context / in another | advantageous / harmful |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句（引述倒装）: 〔"There has been a kind of revival or a rethinking about (what guilt is) and (what role guilt can serve)"〕+ says + Amrisha Vaish, 〔a psychology researcher at UVA〕
>   └── 伴随状语: adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition 〔that emotions aren't binary〕—〔feelings (that may be advantageous in one context) may be harmful in another〕
>         ├── 宾语从句: that this revival is part of a larger recognition
>         ├── 同位语从句: that emotions aren't binary
>         └── 破折号补充: feelings … may be harmful in another
> ```
>
> **参考译文**："人们正在以某种方式重新审视、重新思考内疚感究竟是什么、它能发挥什么作用，"弗吉尼亚大学的心理学研究者 Amrisha Vaish 说，并补充道，这种"复兴"是一场更大认知的一部分：情绪并非非此即彼——在一种情境下可能有利的情感，在另一种情境下可能有害。
>
> **考点提示**：① 这是**引述倒装句**：直接引语提前，主句 `says Amrisha Vaish`——先跳过引语，抓 `…, says Vaish, adding that …`。② `a psychology researcher at …` 是**同位语**，交代身份。③ `adding that …` 是**现在分词作伴随状语**，逻辑主语为 Vaish。④ `a larger recognition **that** emotions aren't binary` 中 `that` 引导**同位语从句**（解释 recognition 的内容），`that` 不作成分，区别于定语从句。⑤ 破折号后 `feelings that … may be harmful in another` 是**独立分句**，对"情绪并非二元"作解释；`that may be advantageous in one context` 是**限制性定语从句**，插在主语 `feelings` 与谓语 `may be harmful` 之间，须注意主句谓语落在从句之后。⑥ 本句是**第 23 题的答案句**——"一种情绪可发挥相反作用"（an emotion can play opposing roles）对应 `advantageous in one context … harmful in another`。

Jealousy and anger, for example, may have evolved to alert us to important **inequalities**. Too much happiness can be **destructive**.

And guilt, by **prompting** us to think more deeply about our goodness, can encourage humans to **make up for** errors and fix relationships. Guilt, in other words, can help hold a **cooperative** species together. It is a kind of **social glue**.

**Viewed in this light**, guilt is an opportunity. Work by Tina Malti, a psychology professor at the University of Toronto, suggests that guilt may **compensate for** an emotional **deficiency**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：**Viewed in this light**, guilt is an opportunity. Work by Tina Malti, a psychology professor at the University of Toronto, suggests that guilt may **compensate for** an emotional **deficiency**.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 状语 A（过去分词）: Viewed in this light
> - 第一句·主语 S: guilt
> - 第一句·系动词 V: is
> - 第一句·表语: an opportunity
> - 第二句·主语 S: Work (by Tina Malti)
> - 第二句·同位语（双逗号插入）: a psychology professor at the University of Toronto
> - 第二句·谓语 V: suggests
> - 第二句·宾语从句: that guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 过去分词作状语 | Viewed in this light | 第一句 |
> | 同位语（双逗号插入） | a psychology professor at … | Tina Malti |
> | that 引导宾语从句 | that guilt may compensate for … | suggests |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 第一句: 〔Viewed in this light〕, guilt + is + an opportunity.
> 第二句: Work by Tina Malti, 〔a psychology professor at the University of Toronto〕, + suggests + 〔that guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency〕
>   ├── 同位语: a psychology professor at …（说明 Malti 身份）
>   └── 宾语从句: that guilt may compensate for …
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：从这个角度看，内疚感是一种机会。多伦多大学心理学教授 Tina Malti 的研究表明，内疚感或许能弥补某种情感上的缺失。
>
> **考点提示**：① `Viewed in this light` 是**过去分词短语作状语**，可还原为 `If (it is) viewed in this light`（若从这个角度看），表**条件 / 角度**。② `Tina Malti, a psychology professor at …` 中名词短语是**同位语**；先跳过它抓主干 `Work by Tina Malti … suggests that …`，注意主语是 `Work`（研究），谓语用**单数 suggests**。③ `that guilt may compensate for …` 是**宾语从句**；`suggest` 表"表明"时**不用虚拟语气**。

In a number of studies, Malti and others have shown that guilt and sympathy may represent different **pathways** to cooperation and sharing.

Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that **shortfall** by experiencing more guilt, which can **rein in** their nastier **impulses**.

> **参考译文**：对同情心弱的孩子而言，他们分享多少，似乎取决于他们有多容易感到内疚。
>
> **考点提示**：① 本句主干是 `how much they shared appeared to turn on how …`，两个 `how` 从句**首尾套叠**——前者作**主语**，后者作**宾语**。② `appear to do` = "似乎……"；`turn on` 此处 = "取决于"（**熟词生义**，非"打开"）。③ `how inclined they were to feel guilty` 是"how + 形容词 + 主语 + 系动词"结构。④ 名词性从句（尤其 how / what / why 引导）是考研高频难点，须先辨清从句在整个句子中作什么成分。

The **guilt-prone** ones shared more, even though they hadn't magically become more sympathetic to the other child's **deprivation**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The **guilt-prone** ones shared more, even though they hadn't magically become more sympathetic to the other child's **deprivation**.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: The guilt-prone ones
> - 谓语 V: shared
> - 状语 A: more
> - 让步状语从句: even though they hadn't magically become more sympathetic to the other child's deprivation
> - 让步从句·主语 S: they
> - 让步从句·系动词 V: hadn't become
> - 让步从句·表语: more sympathetic
> - 让步从句·状语 A: to the other child's deprivation
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | even though 引导让步状语从句 | even though … | 主句 shared more |
> | 副词修饰 | magically | hadn't become |
> | 介词短语作状语 | to the other child's deprivation | sympathetic |
> | 复合形容词 | guilt-prone | ones |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: The guilt-prone ones + shared + more
>   └── 让步状语从句: even though + they + hadn't (magically) become + more sympathetic (to the other child's deprivation)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：那些容易内疚的孩子分享得更多，尽管他们并没有神奇地对另一个孩子所遭受的匮乏产生更多同情。
>
> **考点提示**：① `even though` 引导**让步状语从句**，表"尽管……（但……）"，句子重心在**主句** `The guilt-prone ones shared more`。② `hadn't become` 是**过去完成时**（相对前面实验动作的"此前"状态）。③ `guilt-prone` 是**复合形容词**，后缀 `-prone` 表"易于……的"（如 accident-prone 易出事故的）。④ `ones` 是**代词**，回指上文复数名词 `kids`。⑤ `be sympathetic to` = "对……同情"。

"That's good news," Malti says. "We can be **prosocial** because we caused harm and we feel **regret**."

## 翻译对照

# 2019 Passage 1 中英对照翻译

## Paragraph 1

Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger, guilt emerges a little later, in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms. Children aren't born knowing how to say "I'm sorry"; rather, they learn over time that such statements appease parents and friends—and their own consciences. This is why researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing.

与所谓的基本情绪（如悲伤、恐惧和愤怒）不同，内疚感的出现要稍晚一些，它伴随着孩子对社会与道德规范的理解不断增长而产生。孩子并非生来就懂得如何说"对不起"；相反，他们是在成长过程中逐渐认识到，这样的表达能够安抚父母和朋友——也能安抚自己的良心。正因如此，研究者普遍认为，所谓"道德内疚"只要分量恰当，就是一件好事。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `in conjunction with` = "与……同时、伴随着"；`grasp` = "理解、领会"（此处作名词）。
> - `social and moral norms` = "社会与道德规范"；`appease` = "安抚、平息"；`consciences` = "良心、良知"。
> - `regard A as B` 中的 `as` 此处写作 `to be`：`regard ... to be a good thing` = "认为……是好事"。`in the right amount` = "在恰当的分量下"。
> - 句首 `Unlike ...` 是**介词短语作比较状语**，主句主语为 `guilt`。

## Paragraph 2

In the popular imagination, of course, guilt still gets a bad rap. It is deeply uncomfortable—it's the emotional equivalent of wearing a jacket weighted with stones. Yet this understanding is outdated. "There has been a kind of revival or a rethinking about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve," says Amrisha Vaish, a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia, adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary—feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another. Jealousy and anger, for example, may have evolved to alert us to important inequalities. Too much happiness can be destructive.

当然，在一般人的想象中，内疚感仍然名声不佳。它让人极度不适——在情绪上，这好比穿着一件灌满石头的夹克。然而这种看法已经过时了。"人们正在以某种方式重新审视、重新思考内疚感究竟是什么、它能发挥什么作用，"弗吉尼亚大学的心理学研究者 Amrisha Vaish 说，并补充道，这种"复兴"是一场更大认知的一部分：情绪并非非黑即白——在一种情境下可能有利的情感，在另一种情境下可能有害。例如，嫉妒和愤怒或许进化而来，是为了提醒我们注意重要的不平等。而过多的快乐也可能具有破坏性。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `gets a bad rap` = "（被）名声不好、遭到恶评"，为固定习语。
> - `the emotional equivalent of ...` = "在情感上相当于……"；`weighted with stones` 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 `a jacket`。
> - `revival` = "复兴、重新兴起"；`rethinking` = "重新思考"；`binary` = "二元的、非此即彼的"。
> - `advantageous` = "有利的"；`destructive` = "破坏性的"。
> - `adding that ...` 是**现在分词作伴随状语**，逻辑主语为 Vaish。
> - `alert sb to sth` = "使某人警觉到某事"；`may have evolved to ...` = "或许进化成……（的目的）"。

## Paragraph 3

And guilt, by prompting us to think more deeply about our goodness, can encourage humans to make up for errors and fix relationships. Guilt, in other words, can help hold a cooperative species together. It is a kind of social glue.

而内疚感，通过促使我们更深入地思考自身的善，能够鼓励人们弥补过错、修复关系。换言之，内疚感有助于把一种以合作为生的物种凝聚在一起。它是一种社会黏合剂。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `by prompting us to ...` = "通过促使我们……"（介词 by + 动名词，表方式）。
> - `make up for` = "弥补、补偿"；`fix relationships` = "修复关系"。
> - `hold ... together` = "把……凝聚在一起"；`a cooperative species` 指人类这一"合作的物种"。
> - `social glue` = "社会黏合剂"，是比喻说法。

## Paragraph 4

Viewed in this light, guilt is an opportunity. Work by Tina Malti, a psychology professor at the University of Toronto, suggests that guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency. In a number of studies, Malti and others have shown that guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing. Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that shortfall by experiencing more guilt, which can rein in their nastier impulses. And vice versa: High sympathy can substitute for low guilt.

从这个角度来看，内疚感是一种机会。多伦多大学心理学教授 Tina Malti 的研究表明，内疚感或许能弥补某种情感上的缺失。在多项研究中，Malti 及其他人已表明，内疚感与同情心可能是通往合作与分享的两条不同路径。一些同情心较弱的孩子，也许会通过体验更多的内疚感来弥补这一不足，而这能约束他们更恶劣的冲动。反之亦然：高度的同情心可以替代低度的内疚感。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `Viewed in this light` = "从这个角度 / 这样来看"（过去分词短语作状语）。
> - `compensate for` = "弥补、补偿"；`deficiency` = "缺失、不足"；`shortfall` = "不足、缺口"。
> - `pathways to ...` = "通往……的路径"；`Tina Malti, a psychology professor ...` 中名词短语是**同位语**，补充说明身份。
> - `rein in` = "约束、勒住"；`impulses` = "冲动"；`substitute for` = "替代"；`vice versa` = "反之亦然"。
> - `which can rein in their nastier impulses` 是**非限制性定语从句**，先行词为前面"通过体验更多内疚感来弥补"这一整件事。

## Paragraph 5

In a 2014 study, for example, Malti looked at 244 children. Using caregiver assessments and the children's self-observations, she rated each child's overall sympathy level and his or her tendency to feel negative emotions after moral transgressions. Then the kids were handed chocolate coins, and given a chance to share them with an anonymous child. For the low-sympathy kids, how much they shared appeared to turn on how inclined they were to feel guilty. The guilt-prone ones shared more, even though they hadn't magically become more sympathetic to the other child's deprivation.

例如，在 2014 年的一项研究中，Malti 考察了 244 名儿童。她借助照料者的评估与儿童的自我观察，给每个孩子的总体同情心水平，以及他（她）在道德越界之后产生负面情绪的倾向打了分。随后，孩子们分到了巧克力硬币，并获得与一名匿名儿童分享它们的机会。对同情心弱的孩子而言，他们分享多少，似乎取决于他们有多容易感到内疚。那些容易内疚的孩子分享得更多，尽管他们并没有神奇地对另一个孩子所遭受的匮乏产生更多同情。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `caregiver assessments` = "照料者（家长 / 看护人）的评估"；`self-observations` = "自我观察"。
> - `rated` = "评定、打分"；`transgressions` = "越界行为、违规、过失"（对应第 25 题）。
> - `anonymous` = "匿名的"；`turn on` 此处 = "取决于"（并非"打开"）。
> - `inclined to` = "倾向于"；`guilt-prone` = "易于内疚的、有内疚倾向的"（后缀 -prone 表"易……的"）。
> - `deprivation` = "匮乏、剥夺"；`even though` 引导**让步状语从句**。
> - `Using caregiver assessments ...` 是**现在分词短语作方式状语**，逻辑主语为 `she`。

## Paragraph 6

"That's good news," Malti says. "We can be prosocial because we caused harm and we feel regret."

Malti 说："这是好消息。我们可以因为自己造成了伤害、并且感到懊悔，而做出亲社会行为。"

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `prosocial` = "亲社会的"（指利他、乐于助人、有利于他人的行为）。
> - `regret` = "懊悔、遗憾"。
> - `because we caused harm and we feel regret` 中两个分句由 `and` 并列，同由 `because` 统辖，说明"亲社会"的原因。

## 一、从句

### 从句：what 引导的宾语从句（并列）

**概述**：`what` 兼具"先行词 + 关系代词"功能，本身即"……的事/什么"，此处两个 `what` 从句由 `and` 并列，共同作介词 `about` 的宾语。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `about what A is and what role B can serve` | 两个 what 从句并列作介词宾语 | … a kind of revival or a rethinking **about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve**. |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> `what` 引导的名词性从句**没有先行词**：`what guilt is` 在从句中作 `is` 的表语，`what role guilt can serve` 中 `what` 作 `serve` 的宾语。识别 `what` 从句的关键是先看它是否"缺一个成分"。

### 从句：that 引导的宾语从句

**概述**：`that` 引导宾语从句时**不作任何成分、且常可省略**，用于陈述内容。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `say / suggest / show / realise + that 从句` | 动词后接宾语从句 | Malti and others have shown **that guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing**. |
| `adding that 从句` | 分词后接 that 从句 | … says Amrisha Vaish … , adding **that this revival is part of a larger recognition**… |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `suggests that guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency` 中，`suggest` 表"表明、暗示"（非"建议"），故从句**不用虚拟语气**，用陈述语气 `may compensate`。

### 从句：that 引导的同位语从句

**概述**：`that` 从句解释前面抽象名词的具体内容，与名词是**同位关系**（而非修饰关系）。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `抽象名词（recognition / fact / idea …）+ that 从句` | 说明名词内容 | … a larger recognition **that emotions aren't binary** … |

> [!warning] 易混淆点：同位语从句 vs. 定语从句
> 判断看 `that`：同位语从句的 `that` 是**纯连接词**，不作成分（`that emotions aren't binary` 主系表完整）；
> 定语从句的 `that` 是**关系代词**，须在从句中充当主语或宾语。本句 `that` 不作成分 → 同位语从句。

### 从句：why 引导的表语从句

**概述**：`This is why …` 中 `why` 引导表语从句，说明"这就是……的原因"。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `This is why + 从句` | 表语从句 | **This is why** researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing. |

> [!note] 补充说明
> 考研高频句型 `This is why …` = "这就是……的原因 / 正因如此……"，与 `This is because …`（这是因为……）注意区分因果方向。

### 从句：how 引导的主语从句

**概述**：`how much they shared` 作整个句子的**主语**，`how` 表程度（"多少"）。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `how much + 从句 + 谓语` | 主语从句作主语 | For the low-sympathy kids, **how much they shared** appeared to turn on how inclined they were to feel guilty. |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 本句可先划主干：`how much they shared`（主语） + `appeared to turn on`（谓语） + `how inclined they were to feel guilty`（介词 on 的宾语从句）。两个 `how` 从句首尾呼应，是本句难点。

### 从句：how 引导的宾语从句

**概述**：`how + 形容词 + 主语 + 系动词` 结构，作介词 `on` 的宾语。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `turn on + how adj. + 主语 + be` | 宾语从句作介词宾语 | … appeared to turn on **how inclined they were to feel guilty**. |

### 从句：which 引导的非限制性定语从句（先行词为整句）

**概述**：`which` 指代前面**整件事**（而非某个名词），在从句中作主语，用逗号与主句隔开。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `… , which + 谓语 …` | 指代前句内容 | … may make up for that shortfall by experiencing more guilt, **which can rein in their nastier impulses**. |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 非限制性定语从句中 `which` 可指代**整个主句**，此处即"通过体验更多内疚感来弥补"这一做法。若 `which` 后是 `can / may + 动词`，多半指代"前面那件事"。

### 从句：that 引导的限制性定语从句

**概述**：`that` 在从句中作主语，紧跟先行词 `feelings`，**不加逗号**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `名词 + that + 谓语` | 限定先行词 | … feelings **that may be advantageous in one context** may be harmful in another. |

> [!warning] 易混淆点：主句谓语在哪里？
> 本句有两个 `may be`：第一个在定语从句内（修饰 `feelings`），第二个才是主句谓语。主干为 `feelings … may be harmful in another`，从句插在主语与谓语之间。

### 从句：who 引导的定语从句

**概述**：`who` 指人，在从句中作主语，修饰 `kids`。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `人 + who + 谓语` | 限定先行词 | Some kids **who are low in sympathy** may make up for that shortfall by experiencing more guilt … |

### 从句：even though 引导的让步状语从句

**概述**：`even though` = "尽管、即使"，引导让步从句，主句用"出乎意料"的结果。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `主句 + even though + 从句` | 让步 | The guilt-prone ones shared more, **even though they hadn't magically become more sympathetic** to the other child's deprivation. |

> [!note] 补充说明（联动）
> 让步与转折常并现：本篇 `Yet this understanding is outdated` 用 `Yet`（并列连词）实现句间转折，`even though` 则实现句内让步。转折连词（but / yet / however）与让步连词（although / even though / while）的区分见 [[语法总结笔记.md]]。

## 二、非谓语动词

### 非谓语：现在分词作伴随状语

**概述**：现在分词短语与主句动作**同时发生**，逻辑主语即主句主语。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `主句, doing …` | 伴随 / 补充说明 | … says Amrisha Vaish …, **adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition** … |

### 非谓语：现在分词作方式状语

**概述**：现在分词短语置于句首，表"通过……的方式"，逻辑主语与主句主语一致。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `Doing …, 主语 + 谓语` | 方式状语 | **Using caregiver assessments and the children's self-observations**, she rated each child's overall sympathy level … |

### 非谓语：过去分词作状语

**概述**：过去分词短语作状语，表**时间 / 条件 / 被动**，逻辑主语与主句主语构成被动关系。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `Done …, 主语 + 谓语` | 条件 / 时间状语 | **Viewed in this light**, guilt is an opportunity. |

> [!tip] 解题技巧（联动）
> `Viewed in this light` = "if it is viewed in this light / 从这个角度看"。过去分词作状语可还原为**被动状语从句**（If it is viewed …），是判断"是不是被动"的有效还原法。

### 非谓语：过去分词作后置定语

**概述**：单个过去分词或短语跟在名词后，表**被动、完成**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `名词 + done + …` | 被动后置定语 | It's the emotional equivalent of wearing a jacket **weighted with stones**. |

### 非谓语：动名词作介词宾语

**概述**：介词后接名词性成分，动名词（doing）作介宾。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `by + doing` | 方式状语 | And guilt, **by prompting us to think more deeply about our goodness**, can encourage humans to make up for errors … |
| `介词 + doing` | 介词宾语 | … guilt may represent different pathways **to cooperation and sharing**. |

### 非谓语：不定式作后置定语

**概述**：不定式跟在名词后作定语，`to` 后与所修饰名词常有动宾或同位关系。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `名词 + to do` | 不定式作定语 | … given a **chance to share** them with an anonymous child. |
| `名词 + to do` | 不定式作定语 | … his or her **tendency to feel** negative emotions after moral transgressions. |

### 非谓语：不定式作宾语补足语

**概述**：`prompt sb to do` 等结构中，不定式补充说明宾语的动作。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `prompt sb to do` | 宾语补足语 | … **by prompting us to think** more deeply about our goodness … |

### 非谓语：不定式作目的状语

**概述**：不定式表目的，可译为"为了……"。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `… + to do（目的）` | 目的状语 | Jealousy and anger, for example, may have evolved **to alert us to important inequalities**. |

### 非谓语：be born + 现在分词

**概述**：`be born` 后接现在分词，表示"一出生就处于某种状态"。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `be born + doing` | 伴随状态 | Children aren't born **knowing** how to say "I'm sorry" … |

## 三、语态

### 语态：被动语态

**概述**：强调动作承受者，本篇在描述"儿童"与"实验操作"时多次使用。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `be + done` | 被动 | Children **aren't born** knowing how to say "I'm sorry" … |
| `be + done` | 被动 | Then the kids **were handed** chocolate coins, and **given** a chance to share them with an anonymous child. |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `were handed … and (were) given …` 中第二个 `were` **承前省略**，两个被动并列共享主语 `the kids`，是并列句中的省略现象。

## 四、时态与情态

### 时态：现在完成时

**概述**：`have / has + done` 表示"由过去持续到现在"或"已完成"，本篇用以概括既有的研究结论。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `have + done` | 现在完成 | Malti and others **have shown** that guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways … |
| `there has been` | 存在句完成时 | **There has been** a kind of revival or a rethinking about what guilt is … |

### 情态：情态动词 + 完成式

**概述**：`may have done` 表对**过去**的推测。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `may have done` | 对过去的推测 | Jealousy and anger, for example, **may have evolved** to alert us to important inequalities. |

> [!tip] 解题技巧（联动）
> `may have evolved to …` = "或许（在过去）进化成……的目的"。`may have done` 表示**对已发生之事的推测**，与 `may do`（对现在/将来的推测）区分。情态动词推测用法见 [[语法总结笔记.md]]。

## 五、介词、连词与复合结构

### 结构：regard … to be / as 复合结构

**概述**：`regard A (to be) B` / `regard A as B` 意为"把 A 看作 B"，`B` 为宾语补足语。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `regard + 宾语 + (to be) + 宾补` | 认为……是…… | … researchers generally **regard** so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, **to be** a good thing. |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `regard … as …` 与 `regard … to be …` 同义；同类结构还有 `think / consider / believe + 宾语 + (to be) + 宾补`。

### 结构：介词短语作比较状语

**概述**：`Unlike …` 引出**比较对象**，置于句首，主句表"与之不同"。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `Unlike + 名词短语, 主句` | 比较状语 | **Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger**, guilt emerges a little later … |

> [!note] 补充说明（联动）
> `Unlike A, B …` 中 `B` 是主句主语，**A 与 B 须为同类可比对象**。此处 A = basic emotions，B = guilt，皆为情绪。`such as …` 举例，属**同位补充**。

## 六、修饰与插入（补充要点）

### 修饰：同位语

**概述**：名词（短语）紧跟另一名词后，用逗号隔开，**补充说明身份或内容**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `人名, + 名词短语, + 谓语` | 同位语 | … says Amrisha Vaish, **a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia**, adding that … |
| `人名, + 名词短语, + 谓语` | 同位语 | Work by Tina Malti, **a psychology professor at the University of Toronto**, suggests that … |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 双逗号（或双破折号）夹住的部分多为**插入成分**，先跳过读主干，可快速锁定 `Vaish says …` / `Work by Malti … suggests …`。

### 修饰：破折号插入语

**概述**：破折号引出**解释 / 补充**，可对被解释成分下定义或加注。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `被解释成分—解释` | 破折号解释 | … feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another. → emotions aren't binary**—feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another**. |
| `—并列补充` | 破折号并列 | … such statements appease parents and friends**—and their own consciences**. |

> [!warning] 易混淆点
> 破折号可引出**同位解释**（对前文下定义），也可引出**并列补充**。判断看破折号后成分与前面的关系：本段 `—feelings that …` 是对 `not binary` 的**解释**；`—and their own consciences` 则是 `parents and friends` 的**并列补充**。

### 修饰：并列结构

**概述**：并列连词 `and / or` 连接**同层级**成分，须保持词性、结构一致。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `A, B, and C` | 三项并列 | basic emotions such as sadness, **fear, and anger** |
| `do A and do B` | 动词短语并列 | … encourage humans to **make up for errors and fix relationships**. |
| `从句 and 从句` | 从句并列 | … a rethinking about **what guilt is and what role guilt can serve** … |
| `从句 + and + 从句` | 分句并列 | … because **we caused harm and we feel regret**. |

### 修饰：代词指代

**概述**：`it / they / the ones / his or her` 等须回原文确指，是阅读定位的关键。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `it` 指代前文名词 | 回指 | It is deeply uncomfortable—**it's** the emotional equivalent of wearing a jacket weighted with stones.（it = guilt） |
| `the ones` 指代复数名词 | 回指 | The guilt-prone **ones** shared more …（ones = kids） |
| `his or her` 呼应单数 | 一致性 | … she rated each child's overall sympathy level and **his or her** tendency to feel negative emotions … |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 22、23 题需回原文定位 `it`、`this understanding`、`this revival` 的指代对象：`this understanding` = "内疚令人不适"的旧看法，`this revival` = "重新思考内疚"的思潮。**指代还原**是细节题的关键动作。

## 心得

> [!tip] 主旨把握
> 全文回答的是**"内疚感究竟是应该消除的坏情绪，还是一种有益的道德情绪"**，行文是一条**"提出内疚（道德情绪）→ 流行看法过时、情绪并非二元 → 内疚是社会黏合剂 → 内疚可弥补同情心的不足 → 实验证据 → 结语"**的递进链。
> 第一层**立论**：与 `so-called basic emotions`（**基本情绪**）不同，内疚 `emerges a little later`，且 `in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms`（**伴随孩子对社会与道德规范的理解而发展**）；故 `researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing`（P1）。
> 第二层**破旧立新**：先摆出流行看法——`guilt still gets a bad rap`、`It is deeply uncomfortable—it's the emotional equivalent of wearing a jacket weighted with stones`；再以 `Yet this understanding is outdated` 转折，借 Vaish 之口点明 `a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary—feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another`（P2）。
> 第三层**收束比喻并推进**：内疚 `by prompting us to think more deeply about our goodness, can encourage humans to make up for errors and fix relationships`，`a kind of social glue`（P3）。
> 第四层**是全文论证核心**：`Viewed in this light, guilt is an opportunity`——`guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency`，`guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing`，`Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that shortfall by experiencing more guilt`（P4）。
> 第五层**以实验佐证**：`For the low-sympathy kids, how much they shared appeared to turn on how inclined they were to feel guilty`，`The guilt-prone ones shared more`（P5）。
> 第六层**引语作结**：`We can be prosocial because we caused harm and we feel regret`（P6）。
> **一句话概括**：这篇文章真正的意思是**"内疚感不是需要消除的坏情绪——它随道德认知一同成长，既像黏合剂一样维系合作，又能弥补同情心的不足；在恰当的分量下，它驱动人们弥补过错、修复关系，成为亲社会行为的源泉"**。最能定性的是三处：`in the right amount, to be a good thing`（**适量即好**）、`a kind of social glue`（**社会黏合剂**）、`different pathways to cooperation and sharing`（**通往合作与分享的两条路径**）。

> [!tip] 命题线索
> - **细节题（第 21 题）**：题干 `Researchers think that guilt can be a good thing because it may help ____`。P1 依据句 `guilt emerges a little later, in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms` → **[A] foster a child's moral development**（`a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms` ↔ `foster a child's moral development`）。[B] `regulate a child's basic emotions` 是**张冠李戴**——文章说内疚"不同于（unlike）"基本情绪，并未说内疚"调节"基本情绪；[C] `intellectual ability`、[D] `positive feelings` 均**无据**。**对策：因果题回到"内疚随什么产生"，答案落在 `social and moral norms` 的同义概括。**
> - **细节题（第 22 题）**：题干 `According to Paragraph 2, many people still consider guilt to be ____`。P2 `It is deeply uncomfortable—it's the emotional equivalent of wearing a jacket weighted with stones` → **[D] burdensome**（**沉重的**；`weighted with stones` ↔ `burdensome`）。[A] `inexcusable`、[B] `deceptive`、[C] `addictive` 均**无据**。**对策：注意 `burdensome` 是对"灌满石头的夹克"这一比喻的抽象概括。**
> - **细节题（第 23 题）**：题干 `Vaish holds that the rethinking about guilt comes from an awareness that ____`。P2 `… a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary—feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another` → **[B] an emotion can play opposing roles**（`advantageous … harmful` ↔ `opposing roles`）。[A] `context-independent`（与情境无关）与原文 `in one context … in another` **正相反**；[C] `socially constructive`、[D] `emotional stability can benefit health` 均**无据**。**对策：抓住"同一情绪两种效果"的对比结构。**
> - **细节题（第 24 题）**：题干 `Malti and others have shown that cooperation and sharing ____`。P4 `guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing` → **[B] can result from either sympathy or guilt**。**注意 [A] `may help correct emotional deficiencies` 是主客颠倒**——原文是"内疚（guilt）弥补情感缺失（emotional deficiency）"，而非"合作与分享纠正情感缺失"；[C]、[D] **无据**。**对策：警惕"主语与宾语对调"的干扰项。**
> - **词义题（第 25 题）**：题干 `The word "transgressions" (Para. 5) is closest in meaning to ____`。P5 `… his or her tendency to feel negative emotions after moral transgressions`，语境为"在（做了）……之后产生负面情绪"，且紧随其后的 `The guilt-prone ones shared more` 指向"做错事之后内疚" → **[D] wrongdoings**（过错、不当行为）。[A] `teachings`、[B] `discussions`、[C] `restrictions` 均不符。**对策：词义题看**搭配与语境褒贬**——`moral transgressions`（道德上的越界）与褒义的 teachings 明显相斥。**

> [!warning] 易错点
> - **`Unlike` 表同类比较而非否定**：`Unlike so-called basic emotions …, guilt emerges a little later` 是**把"内疚"与"基本情绪"作对比**，不是"内疚不算情绪"。
> - **`This is why` 是表语从句**：意为"**正因如此**"，与 `This is because`（**这是因为**）**因果方向相反**，勿混。
> - **同位语从句 vs. 定语从句**：`a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary` 中 `that` 是**同位语从句**（`that` 不作任何成分）；若 `that` 在从句中作主语/宾语，才是定语从句。
> - **`which` 可指代整句**：`… , which can rein in their nastier impulses` 中 `which` 指代"靠体验更多内疚来弥补"**这整件事**，而非某个名词。
> - **`turn on` 是熟词生义**：此处 = "**取决于**"（= depend on），不是"打开"。
> - **`regard … to be / as …` 是复合结构**：`to be a good thing` 作宾语补足语（`to be` 可省略），同类还有 `consider / think / believe + 宾语 + (to be) + 宾补`。
> - **过去分词作状语可还原被动从句**：`Viewed in this light` = `If (it is) viewed in this light`（**若从这个角度看**）。
> - **后缀 `-prone` 表"易于……的"**：`guilt-prone` = **易于内疚的**（同类 accident-prone 易出事故的）。
> - **`may have evolved to …` 是对过去的推测**：`may have done` 表"**或许（在过去）已经……**"，与 `may do`（对现在/将来的推测）区分。
> - **`suggest` 表"表明"时不用虚拟语气**：`… suggests that guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency`，从句用**陈述语气**。

## 延伸

- **语体背景**：本篇是**心理学观点评述 / 科普论证（science commentary）**，与 [[2018阅读/2018-passage4-deep-work-精读笔记]]（为"休息"翻案）、[[2017阅读/2017-passage2-screen-time-精读笔记]]（对"屏幕时间有害论"的翻案）同属**为某一被误解的事物翻案的议论文**，但本篇立场最为"**反直觉**"：**"内疚不是坏情绪，适量反而有益"**。其句法特征是**"让步—转折 + 引语论证 + 名词性从句套叠 + 比喻说理"四件套**：用 `Yet this understanding is outdated`（**转折**）推翻常识、用 Vaish 与 Malti 的**直接引语**（`"…," says Amrisha Vaish, …` / `"…," Malti says.`）立论、用 `This is why …`（**表语从句**）与 `what guilt is and what role guilt can serve`（**两个 what 从句并列**）收束点题、用 `a jacket weighted with stones` 与 `a kind of social glue`（**比喻**）化抽象为具体。**"文章想为什么翻案"决定了它的句法选择**：一篇"为内疚翻案"的评述，必然向"**让步后转折**"与"**引语 + 比喻**"倾斜。
- **读法建议**：本篇的**命题坐标是"一段一个功能"**——P1 立论、P2 破旧立新（第 22、23 题）、P3 比喻收束、P4 核心命题（第 24 题）、P5 实验佐证（第 25 题词义）、P6 引语作结。考研阅读的惯例是**"题干问什么，就回哪一段找"**。三个好习惯：① **遇"许多人认为"就找转折词**——`guilt still gets a bad rap … Yet this understanding is outdated`，`Yet` 之后才是作者立场（第 22、23 题）；② **遇引语先辨"谁说的"**——Vaish（P2）与 Malti（P4—P6）分属不同论据层，题干点名谁就锁谁；③ **遇比喻要"抽象回译"**——`weighted with stones` 是"沉重（burdensome）"、`social glue` 是"维系合作"，第 22 题正考此处的同义概括。
- **概念延伸**：**"道德情绪（moral emotions）"** 与 **"亲社会行为（prosocial behavior）"** 是发展心理学的一组核心范畴：**内疚（guilt）** 与 **同情（sympathy）** 被看作通往 `cooperation and sharing` 的**两条不同路径**（`different pathways`）——同情心弱者可以借"**更易内疚**"（`guilt-prone`）来补足 `shortfall`，从而 `rein in their nastier impulses`。这与社会心理学中"**负性情绪未必有害**"的研究相呼应（如"有益的焦虑"、`too much happiness can be destructive`）。文中 **`get a bad rap`、`outdated`、`binary`、`compensate for`、`deficiency`、`shortfall`** 构成一条线索：**被想当然地判为"坏"的情绪，换一个语境可能正好相反**。可与 [[2015阅读/2015-passage1-stress-at-home-精读笔记]]（压力的再认识）并读——**几篇都在追问"流行的情绪叙事是否可靠"。**
- **对比阅读**：**"常识与反例"这一母题在题库中反复出现**——本篇用"**内疚令人不适却有益**"（`a good thing`）与"**情绪并非二元**"（`emotions aren't binary`）两次反转常识，与 [[2018阅读/2018-passage4-deep-work-精读笔记]]（"无所事事竟不可或缺"）**形异而神同**：**都主张在事实与机制上重新审视被想当然的结论**。串起来读，可以看到考研阅读如何围绕"**主流叙事 vs 反直觉的证据**"反复命题：本篇是典型的"**翻案式评述**"——读者须紧盯 `Yet` 与 `paradoxically` 之后的转折，切勿把"内疚是坏事"这一**被推翻的预设**误当成主旨。
- **词汇复习**：`so-called / basic emotions / emerge / in conjunction with / grasp / norms / appease / conscience / in the right amount / get a bad rap / discomfort / equivalent / weighted with stones / outdated / revival / rethinking / binary / advantageous / destructive / inequality / prompt / make up for / cooperative / social glue / viewed in this light / compensate for / deficiency / pathway / shortfall / rein in / impulse / vice versa / substitute for / caregiver / assessment / self-observation / rate / tendency / transgression / anonymous / turn on / inclined / guilt-prone / sympathetic / deprivation / prosocial / regret`。
- **联动复习**：语法结构见 [[语法总结笔记]]，固定搭配与辨析见 [[固定搭配与词组笔记]]；建议用 `This is why researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing.`（why 表语从句 + regard … to be 复合结构）、`Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that shortfall by experiencing more guilt, which can rein in their nastier impulses.`（限制性定语从句 + 非限制性定语从句指代整句）、`For the low-sympathy kids, how much they shared appeared to turn on how inclined they were to feel guilty.`（how 主语从句 + how 宾语从句）各写一句，以巩固本篇最核心的三组结构。

## 思考题

1. P1 的 `Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger, guilt emerges a little later, in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms.` 中，句首 `Unlike …` 是什么成分、它引出的是"否定"还是"比较"？主干是什么？`such as …` 修饰谁？`in conjunction with …` 是什么状语、修饰什么？再想：**这一句如何为第 21 题"内疚有助于道德发展"提供依据？**
2. P1 的 `This is why researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing.` 中，`why` 引导的是什么从句？`in the right amount` 是什么成分、修饰谁？`regard … to be …` 是什么结构、`to be` 能否省略？再想：**删掉插入语 `in the right amount` 后，句意会发生什么变化、这为何影响第 21 题的判断？**
3. P2 的 `"There has been a kind of revival or a rethinking about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve," says Amrisha Vaish, a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia, adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary—feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another.` 中，直接引语与主句 `says Amrisha Vaish` 是怎样的语序？两个 `what` 从句各作什么成分？`a psychology researcher at …` 是什么成分？`adding that …` 由什么引导、逻辑主语是谁？`a larger recognition that …` 与 `feelings that …` 中的两个 `that` 用法是否相同？破折号后的内容与前面是什么关系？再想：**这一句如何对应第 23 题"一种情绪可以发挥相反的作用"？**
4. P4 的 `Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that shortfall by experiencing more guilt, which can rein in their nastier impulses.` 中，`who are low in sympathy` 是什么从句、`who` 在从句中作什么成分？`by experiencing more guilt` 是什么状语、`experiencing` 是什么形式？逗号后的 `which` 指代什么——某个名词还是整件事？`rein in` 在此什么意思？再想：**这一句与 P4 前文 `guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency` 构成怎样的论证关系？**
5. P4 的 `Viewed in this light, guilt is an opportunity. Work by Tina Malti, a psychology professor at the University of Toronto, suggests that guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency.` 中，`Viewed in this light` 是什么形式作状语、可还原成什么从句？`Tina Malti, a psychology professor …,` 是什么成分、阅读时应如何处理？`Work by Tina Malti …` 的主语是什么、谓语为何用**单数** `suggests`？`suggest` 表"表明"时从句为何**不用虚拟语气**？再想：**这一句如何为第 24 题"合作与分享可源于同情心或内疚"定下基调？**
6. P5 的 `For the low-sympathy kids, how much they shared appeared to turn on how inclined they were to feel guilty.` 中，`how much they shared` 是什么从句、在句中作什么成分？`appeared to turn on` 是什么结构？`how inclined they were to feel guilty` 在句中作什么成分？`turn on` 在此是什么意思？再想：**把这一句与紧随其后的 `The guilt-prone ones shared more …` 合起来看，如何支撑第 25 题中对 `transgressions` 的理解？**
