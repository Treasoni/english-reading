The line chart illustrates the temporal evolution of museum, with ploted points showing a overall clearly trajectory. From 2013 to 2015, the number of tourists surged from 6378 to 7811. During the same time, the figure of museum climbs from 4165 to 4692. Taken together, these figures demonstrate the development of museum.

Seen against the backdrop of everyday life, museum tends to develop naturally when multiple factors move in the same direction. First of all, sustained economic growth is an indispensable driver of it. A further factor lies in the fact that people no longer merely satisfied with basic subsistence, but are instead turning toward a higher level of spirital fulfillment

To sustain the development of the phenomenon, actions must be targeted. Government can formulate stratgies concerning it, so that it can keep pace with the time. Individuals should think the role of it calmly and rationaly, and offer suggestions from a mature perspective.

这张折线图说明了博物馆随时间的演变，图上标出的点显示出一个总体清楚的轨迹。2013 年至 2015 年，游客的数量从 6378 激增到 7811。在同一时期，博物馆的数字从 4165 上升到 4692。综合来看，这些数字表明了博物馆的发展。

放在日常生活的背景下看，当多种因素朝同一方向发展时，博物馆往往会自然发展。首先，持续的经济增长是它不可或缺的驱动力。另一个因素在于，人们不再仅仅满足于基本生计，而是在转向更高层次的精神满足

为了维持这一现象的发展，行动必须有针对性。政府可以制定关于它的策略，让它能够跟上时代。个人应冷静而理性地思考它的作用，并从成熟的角度提出建议。

## 六、改稿 A｜最小改动版

The line chart illustrates how the number of museums and museum visits in China changed from 2013 to 2015. Over this period, museum visits rose from 637.8 million to 781.1 million. During the same period, the number of museums climbed from 4,165 to 4,692. Taken together, these figures show that visits grew faster than the number of museums.

Several factors may explain these increases. First of all, sustained economic growth may have made it easier to fund new museums. A further factor is that as people meet their basic needs, they may look for more opportunities to learn about art and history.

To sustain this growth, the government could help museums improve their exhibitions and make them easier to understand. Individuals can offer suggestions about what they would like to see, helping museums plan exhibitions that appeal to more visitors.

改动说明：首段保留「点题—先人次、后馆数—收束」，补清单位与对比，统一过去时（#1–3、#6–15）；第二段保留「经济增长—需求升级」，写出资金与参观动机（#4、#16–20）；末段仍写政府和个人，但各自给出可以实施的行动（#5、#21–27）。

### 参考译文

这张折线图说明了 2013—2015 年中国博物馆数量和参观人次的变化。这一时期，参观人次从 6.378 亿增至 7.811 亿。同期，博物馆数量从 4,165 家增至 4,692 家。综合来看，参观人次的增幅超过了博物馆数量的增幅。

几个因素可能解释这两项增长。首先，持续的经济增长可能使新建博物馆更容易获得资金。另一个因素是，人们在基本需求得到满足后，可能会寻找更多了解艺术和历史的机会。

为了维持这一增长，政府可以帮助博物馆改善展览，并让展览更容易看懂。个人可以就自己想看什么提出建议，帮助博物馆策划能吸引更多参观者的展览。

## 七、改稿 B｜高分重写版

From 2013 to 2015, the number of museums in China rose from 4,165 to 4,692, an increase of about 13 percent. Over the same period, museum visits climbed from 637.8 million to 781.1 million, an increase of roughly 22 percent. Visits thus grew faster than the number of museums.

This rise in attendance matters because museums give people a chance to see objects they might otherwise encounter only in textbooks. Economic growth may have helped fund new museums, while rising living standards may have encouraged more families to spend their leisure time visiting museums.

In my view, the next priority should be to make those visits more rewarding, rather than simply opening more museums. Museums could offer short, hands-on tours for children and ask families which exhibits they found most engaging. That feedback could help museums plan programs that give people a reason to come back.

改动理由：

1. 首段用约 13% 和约 22% 比较两条线，解决「家」与「人次」不能直接比绝对差值的问题。
2. 第二段先评论增长的意义——公众可以接触课本里的实物——再用 `while` 写出馆数和人次上升的两种可能原因。`may have` 区分推测与图表事实。
3. 末段明确优先级：让参观有收获，而不只是多建馆；把「提出建议」落实为家庭对展览的反馈，并交代反馈如何用于活动策划。

### 参考译文

2013—2015 年，中国的博物馆数量由 4,165 家增至 4,692 家，增幅约为 13%。同期，博物馆参观人次由 6.378 亿增至 7.811 亿，增幅约为 22%。因此，参观人次的增长快于博物馆数量的增长。

参观人次增加之所以重要，是因为博物馆让人们有机会亲眼看到一些东西，否则他们也许只能在课本上见到。经济增长可能为新建博物馆提供了资金，而生活水平的提高可能让更多家庭愿意在闲暇时间参观博物馆。

我认为，下一步应优先让参观更有收获，而不是单纯多建博物馆。博物馆可以为儿童提供简短、可动手参与的导览，并询问家庭访客哪些展品最吸引他们。这样的反馈能帮助博物馆设计让人愿意再来的活动。
