# Deliberate Practice and Expert Performance 精读笔记

## 背景

本文选自**2007 年考研英语阅读理解 Passage 1**，主题为**刻意练习（deliberate practice）与卓越表现（expert performance）**。文章通过"足球运动员出生月份现象"引出对"天赋 vs 后天努力"的探讨，最终论证：卓越表现者几乎总是"后天造就"而非"天生如此"。

> [!abstract] 文章概要
> 文章采用"现象引入 → 提出猜想 → 逐一否定 → 研究佐证 → 结论升华"的经典论述结构，属于**观点论证型**议论文。开头以世界杯球员的出生日期怪象制造悬念，随后引出心理学家 Anders Ericsson 关于刻意练习的研究，论证核心观点：天赋被高估，刻意练习才是卓越表现的关键。

- **难度**：intermediate
- **体裁**：议论文（argumentation）
- **题源**：2007 年考研英语阅读理解 Passage 1
- **核心概念**：deliberate practice（刻意练习）

## 文章原文

### Deliberate Practice and Expert Performance

If you were to examine the birth certificates of every soccer player in 2006's World Cup tournament, you would most likely find a **noteworthy quirk**: elite soccer players are more likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year than in the later months.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：If you were to examine the birth certificates of every soccer player in 2006's World Cup tournament, you would most likely find a **noteworthy quirk**: elite soccer players are more likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year than in the later months.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: you
> - 谓语 V: would find
> - 宾语 O: a noteworthy quirk
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句 | If | 条件状语，修饰主句 |
> | 介词短语 | in | 后置定语，修饰 every soccer player |
> | 同位语 | 冒号 | 解释说明 a noteworthy quirk |
> | 比较结构 | more...than... | 对比 earlier months 与 later months |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: you would most likely find a noteworthy quirk
>   ├── 状从(If): If you were to examine the birth certificates of every soccer player in 2006's World Cup tournament → 条件状语
>   │     └── 介短(in): in 2006's World Cup tournament → 修饰 every soccer player
>   └── 同位语(冒号): elite soccer players are more likely to have been born in the earlier months than in the later months → 解释 a noteworthy quirk
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如果你查看 2006 年世界杯赛事中每一位足球运动员的出生证明，你很可能会发现一个值得注意的怪象：顶尖足球运动员更可能出生在一年中的前几个月，而非后几个月。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 虚拟语气条件句：`If you were to examine...` 用 `were to do` 表对将来的虚拟假设，语气比 `If you examined` 更正式、更具假设性
> 2. 冒号同位语：冒号后内容具体解释 `a noteworthy quirk`，考研阅读常利用冒号后信息推断词义
> 3. 比较结构：`more likely to have been born in the earlier months than in the later months`，强调出生月份的倾向性

If you then examined the European national youth teams that feed the World Cup and professional ranks, you would find this strange phenomenon to be even more **pronounced**.

What might **account for** this strange phenomenon?

Here are a few guesses: a) certain **astrological signs** confer superior soccer skills; b) winter-born babies tend to have higher **oxygen capacity**, which increases soccer **stamina**; c) soccer-mad parents are more likely to **conceive** children in springtime, at the annual peak of soccer **mania**; d) none of the above.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Here are a few guesses: a) certain **astrological signs** confer superior soccer skills; b) winter-born babies tend to have higher **oxygen capacity**, which increases soccer **stamina**; c) soccer-mad parents are more likely to **conceive** children in springtime, at the annual peak of soccer **mania**; d) none of the above.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 谓语 V: are
> - 主语 S: a few guesses
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 倒装结构 | Here | 地点副词前置，全部倒装 |
> | 非限制性定语从句 | which | 指代 higher oxygen capacity |
> | 介词短语 | at | 时间状语，修饰 conceive |
> | 列举结构 | 冒号 + 分号 | 具体说明 a few guesses |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句(倒装): Here are a few guesses
>   └── 同位语(冒号+分号列举):
>         ├── a) certain astrological signs confer superior soccer skills
>         ├── b) winter-born babies tend to have higher oxygen capacity
>         │     └── 非限定定从(which): which increases soccer stamina → 修饰 oxygen capacity
>         ├── c) soccer-mad parents are more likely to conceive children in springtime
>         │     └── 介短(at): at the annual peak of soccer mania → 时间状语
>         └── d) none of the above
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：这里有一些猜测：a) 某些星座赋予人卓越的足球技能；b) 冬季出生的婴儿往往具有更高的携氧能力，这增强了足球运动中的耐力；c) 狂热的足球父母更可能在春天怀上孩子，因为春天是每年足球狂热的高峰期；d) 以上都不是。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `Here` 引导倒装句：`Here are a few guesses` 为全部倒装，主语 `a few guesses` 位于谓语之后
> 2. 非限制性定语从句：`which` 指代前项 `higher oxygen capacity`，在从句中作主语，不可省略
> 3. 冒号+分号列举结构：考研阅读中常见的信息组织方式，需快速梳理四项并列内容
> 4. `none of the above`：本段以"以上都不是"收尾，制造悬念，引出 Ericsson 的否定态度

Anders Ericsson, a 58-year-old psychology professor at Florida State University, says he believes strongly in "none of the above."

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Anders Ericsson, a 58-year-old psychology professor at Florida State University, says he believes strongly in "none of the above."
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Anders Ericsson
> - 谓语 V: says
> - 宾语 O: he believes strongly in "none of the above"（宾语从句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 同位语 | 逗号 | a 58-year-old psychology professor at Florida State University → 解释 Anders Ericsson |
> | 名词性从句 | 省略 that | he believes strongly in... → 作 says 的宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Anders Ericsson says (that) he believes strongly in "none of the above."
>   ├── 同位语(逗号): a 58-year-old psychology professor at Florida State University → 解释 Anders Ericsson
>   │     └── 介短(at): at Florida State University → 修饰 professor
>   └── 宾语从句(省略that): he believes strongly in "none of the above." → 作 says 的宾语
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：安德斯·埃里克森是佛罗里达州立大学一位 58 岁的心理学教授，他表示自己坚信"以上都不是"。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 同位语结构：名词短语 `a 58-year-old psychology professor...` 补充说明人物身份，考研阅读中常用于介绍人物
> 2. 宾语从句省略 that：动词 `say` 后的 that 可省略
> 3. `believe in`：此处意为"相信、坚信"，与 `believe`（认为）含义略有不同

Ericsson grew up in Sweden, and studied nuclear engineering until he realized he would have more opportunity to conduct his own research if he switched to psychology.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Ericsson grew up in Sweden, and studied nuclear engineering until he realized he would have more opportunity to conduct his own research if he switched to psychology.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Ericsson
> - 谓语 V: grew up; studied
> - 宾语 O: nuclear engineering
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | in | 地点状语，修饰 grew up |
> | 状语从句 | until | 时间状语，修饰 studied |
> | 名词性从句 | 省略 that | 作 realized 的宾语 |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to conduct | 目的状语，修饰 would have |
> | 状语从句 | if | 条件状语，修饰 would have |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Ericsson grew up in Sweden, and studied nuclear engineering
>   ├── 介短(in): in Sweden → 地点状语
>   └── 并列谓语: and studied nuclear engineering
>         └── 状从(until): until he realized... → 时间状语
>               └── 名从(省略that): (that) he would have more opportunity → 作 realized 的宾语
>                     ├── 非谓语(不定式): to conduct his own research → 目的状语
>                     └── 状从(if): if he switched to psychology → 条件状语
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：埃里克森在瑞典长大，最初学习核工程，直到他意识到如果转学心理学，就会有更多机会从事自己的研究。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 并列谓语：`grew up` 和 `studied` 由 `and` 连接，共用主语 Ericsson
> 2. `until` 引导时间状语从句：意为"直到……"，主句动作持续到从句动作发生
> 3. 宾语从句省略 that：`realized` 后的 that 可省略
> 4. `if` 真实条件句：一般过去时，表示"如果转学心理学"这一真实可能性

His first experiment, nearly 30 years ago, involved memory: training a person to hear and then repeat a random series of numbers.

"With the first subject, after about 20 hours of training, his **digit span** had risen from 7 to 20," Ericsson recalls.

"He kept improving, and after about 200 hours of training he had risen to over 80 numbers."

This success, coupled with later research showing that memory itself is not **genetically** determined, led Ericsson to conclude that the act of memorizing is more of a **cognitive** exercise than an **intuitive** one.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：This success, coupled with later research showing that memory itself is not **genetically** determined, led Ericsson to conclude that the act of memorizing is more of a **cognitive** exercise than an **intuitive** one.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: This success
> - 谓语 V: led
> - 宾语 O: Ericsson
> - 补语 C: to conclude that...（宾语补足语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（过去分词短语） | coupled with | 伴随状语，逻辑主语为 This success |
> | 非谓语（现在分词短语） | showing | 后置定语，修饰 research |
> | 名词性从句 | that | 作 showing 的宾语 |
> | 名词性从句 | that | 作 conclude 的宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: This success led Ericsson to conclude that...
>   ├── 非谓语(过去分词): coupled with later research → 伴随状语，逻辑主语为 This success
>   │     └── 非谓语(现在分词): showing that memory itself is not genetically determined → 后置定语修饰 research
>   │           └── 名从(that): memory itself is not genetically determined → 作 showing 的宾语
>   └── 宾语补足语: to conclude that the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one
>         └── 名从(that): the act of memorizing is more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one → 作 conclude 的宾语
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：这一成功，加上后来表明记忆本身并非由基因决定的诸多研究，使埃里克森得出结论：记忆行为更像是一种认知练习，而非直觉行为。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 非谓语作伴随状语：`coupled with...` 过去分词短语作伴随状语，逻辑主语是句子主语 `This success`（表被动）
> 2. 非谓语作后置定语：`showing that...` 现在分词短语修饰 `research`，相当于 `research that shows...`
> 3. `lead sb. to do sth.`：意为"使某人做某事"，不定式作宾语补足语
> 4. `more of A than B`：意为"更多是 A 而非 B"，考研翻译常考比较方向的判断

In other words, whatever **inborn** differences two people may exhibit in their abilities to memorize, those differences are **swamped** by how well each person "**encodes**" the information.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In other words, whatever **inborn** differences two people may exhibit in their abilities to memorize, those differences are **swamped** by how well each person "**encodes**" the information.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: those differences
> - 谓语 V: are swamped
> - 状语 A: by how well each person "encodes" the information
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句 | whatever | 让步状语，修饰主句 |
> | 介词短语 | in | 方面状语，修饰 exhibit |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to memorize | 后置定语，修饰 abilities |
> | 名词性从句 | how | 作介词 by 的宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: those differences are swamped by how well each person "encodes" the information
>   ├── 状从(whatever): whatever inborn differences two people may exhibit in their abilities to memorize → 让步状语
>   │     └── 介短(in): in their abilities → 方面状语，修饰 exhibit
>   │           └── 非谓语(不定式): to memorize → 后置定语修饰 abilities
>   └── 介短(by) + 名从(how): by how well each person "encodes" the information → 引出动作执行者
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：换句话说，无论两个人在记忆能力上表现出怎样的先天差异，这些差异都会淹没在每个人"编码"信息的能力之中。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `whatever` 引导让步状语从句：意为"无论……"，相当于 `no matter what`，从句语序正常
> 2. 被动语态 + 介词宾语从句：`are swamped by` 后接 `how` 引导的宾语从句作 by 的宾语
> 3. `in other words`：标志性短语，提示下文是对上文的同义改写，考研阅读重点信息
> 4. `encode` 词义：此处意为"（将信息）编码"，指对信息的加工处理，与下文的 `encode information` 呼应

And the best way to learn how to encode information meaningfully, Ericsson determined, was a process known as **deliberate practice**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：And the best way to learn how to encode information meaningfully, Ericsson determined, was a process known as **deliberate practice**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: the best way to learn how to encode information meaningfully
> - 谓语 V: was
> - 补语 C: a process known as deliberate practice
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 插入语 | 逗号 | Ericsson determined，补充信息来源 |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to learn | 后置定语，修饰 the best way |
> | 特殊疑问词+不定式 | how to | 作 learn 的宾语 |
> | 非谓语（过去分词短语） | known as | 后置定语，修饰 a process |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the best way was a process known as deliberate practice
>   ├── 主语 S: the best way
>   │     └── 非谓语(不定式): to learn how to encode information meaningfully → 后置定语修饰 the best way
>   │           └── 名从(how): how to encode information meaningfully → 作 learn 的宾语
>   ├── 插入语: Ericsson determined → 主谓插入语，补充信息来源
>   └── 表语 C: a process
>         └── 非谓语(过去分词): known as deliberate practice → 后置定语修饰 a process
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：埃里克森认定，学习如何有意义地编码信息的最佳方法，就是被称为"刻意练习"的过程。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 插入语识别："Ericsson determined" 为"主谓插入语"，阅读时可先跳过，直接连读主干 the best way... was a process
> 2. 不定式短语作后置定语："to learn how to encode information meaningfully" 修饰 the best way
> 3. 特殊疑问词+不定式："how to encode information meaningfully" 作 learn 的宾语，相当于宾语从句
> 4. 过去分词短语作后置定语："known as deliberate practice" 修饰 a process，相当于 which is known as...

Deliberate practice **entails** more than simply repeating a task. Rather, it involves setting specific goals, obtaining **immediate feedback** and concentrating as much on technique as on outcome.

Ericsson and his colleagues have thus taken to studying expert performers in a wide range of **pursuits**, including soccer. They gather all the data they can, not just performance statistics and **biographical** details but also the results of their own laboratory experiments with high achievers.

Their work makes a rather **startling assertion**: the trait we commonly call talent is highly **overrated**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Their work makes a rather **startling assertion**: the trait we commonly call talent is highly **overrated**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Their work
> - 谓语 V: makes
> - 宾语 O: a rather startling assertion
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 同位语 | 冒号 | 解释说明 a rather startling assertion |
> | 定语从句 | 省略 that | 修饰 the trait |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Their work makes a rather startling assertion
>   └── 同位语(冒号): the trait we commonly call talent is highly overrated → 解释 assertion
>         └── 定从(省略that): (that/which) we commonly call talent → 修饰 the trait
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：他们的研究提出了一个相当惊人的论断：我们通常称之为天赋的特质被大大高估了。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `make an assertion`：意为"提出论断"，`assertion` 与 `claim`、`argument` 近义
> 2. 冒号同位语：冒号后内容具体说明 `assertion` 的内容
> 3. 定语从句关系代词省略：`the trait (that) we commonly call talent`，关系代词作宾语可省略
> 4. `highly overrated`："被严重高估"，副词 `highly` 加强程度

Or, put another way, expert performers—whether in memory or surgery, ballet or computer programming—are nearly always ==made, not born==.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Or, put another way, expert performers—whether in memory or surgery, ballet or computer programming—are nearly always ==made, not born==.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: expert performers
> - 谓语 V: are
> - 补语 C: nearly always made, not born
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 插入语 | 逗号 | put another way，逻辑衔接 |
> | 插入语（让步） | whether...or... | 破折号之间，举例限定领域范围 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: expert performers are nearly always made, not born
>   ├── 插入语(逗号): Or, put another way → 逻辑衔接，表"换句话说"
>   └── 插入语(破折号): whether in memory or surgery, ballet or computer programming → 让步举例，限定领域范围
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：或者换句话说，卓越的表现者——无论是在记忆、外科手术、芭蕾舞还是计算机编程领域——几乎总是后天造就的，而非天生如此。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `put another way`：固定表达，意为"换句话说"，标志观点重申
> 2. 破折号插入语：阅读时可先跳过，`whether...or...` 表"无论……还是……"
> 3. `made, not born`：反义对仗，为全文核心论点（后天 vs 先天），对应 Q25 谚语题

## 翻译对照

### Deliberate Practice and Expert Performance 刻意练习与卓越表现 中英对照

If you were to examine the birth certificates of every soccer player in 2006's World Cup tournament, you would most likely find a **noteworthy quirk**: elite soccer players are more likely to have been born in the earlier months of the year than in the later months. If you then examined the European national youth teams that feed the World Cup and professional ranks, you would find this strange phenomenon to be even more **pronounced**.

如果你查看 2006 年世界杯赛事中每一位足球运动员的出生证明，你很可能会发现一个**值得注意的怪象**：顶尖足球运动员更可能出生在一年中的前几个月，而非后几个月。如果你再查看那些为世界杯和职业球队**输送**人才的欧洲国家青年队，你会发现这一奇怪的现象甚至更为**明显**。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"noteworthy quirk"**：译为"值得注意的怪象"。"quirk" 指"奇特的怪癖/异常之处"，考研词汇中需注意与 "oddity"、"peculiarity" 近义。
> - **"feed the World Cup and professional ranks"**："feed" 此处为比喻用法，意为"为……输送（人才）"，不可直译为"喂养"。

What might **account for** this strange phenomenon? Here are a few guesses: a) certain **astrological signs** confer superior soccer skills; b) winter-born babies tend to have higher **oxygen capacity**, which increases soccer **stamina**; c) soccer-mad parents are more likely to **conceive** children in springtime, at the annual peak of soccer **mania**; d) none of the above.

什么可能**解释**这一奇怪的现象呢？这里有一些猜测：a) 某些**星座**赋予人卓越的足球技能；b) 冬季出生的婴儿往往具有更高的**携氧能力**，这增强了足球运动中的**耐力**；c) 狂热的足球父母更可能在春天怀上孩子，因为春天是每年足球**狂热**的高峰期；d) 以上都不是。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"account for"**：固定搭配，意为"解释、说明（原因）"，考研阅读中高频短语。
> - **"confer"**：此处意为"赋予、授予"，`confer superior soccer skills` 即"赋予卓越的足球技能"。
> - **"none of the above"**：意为"以上都不是"，常见于选择题选项，此处为 Ericsson 对四种猜测的否定回答。

Anders Ericsson, a 58-year-old psychology professor at Florida State University, says he believes strongly in "none of the above." Ericsson grew up in Sweden, and studied nuclear engineering until he realized he would have more opportunity to conduct his own research if he switched to psychology. His first experiment, nearly 30 years ago, involved memory: training a person to hear and then repeat a random series of numbers. "With the first subject, after about 20 hours of training, his **digit span** had risen from 7 to 20," Ericsson recalls. "He kept improving, and after about 200 hours of training he had risen to over 80 numbers."

安德斯·埃里克森是佛罗里达州立大学一位 58 岁的**心理学教授**，他表示自己坚信"以上都不是"。埃里克森在瑞典长大，最初学习核工程，直到他意识到如果转学心理学，就会有更多机会从事自己的研究。他在近 30 年前进行的第一个实验与记忆有关：训练一个人听并复述随机排列的数字序列。埃里克森回忆道："第一个受试者在经过约 20 小时的训练后，他的**数字记忆广度**从 7 个上升到了 20 个。""他一直在进步，经过大约 200 小时的训练后，他已经能记住 80 多个数字了。"

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"digit span"**：心理学专业术语，指"数字记忆广度"——一个人能正确复述的数字串长度。不能直译为"数字跨度"。
> - **"the first subject"**："subject" 在实验语境中意为"受试者、实验对象"，而非"主题"。
> - 本段引语较长，翻译时按中文引语习惯处理，保持引导语（"埃里克森回忆道"）的位置。

This success, coupled with later research showing that memory itself is not **genetically** determined, led Ericsson to conclude that the act of memorizing is more of a **cognitive** exercise than an **intuitive** one. In other words, whatever **inborn** differences two people may exhibit in their abilities to memorize, those differences are **swamped** by how well each person "**encodes**" the information. And the best way to learn how to encode information meaningfully, Ericsson determined, was a process known as **deliberate practice**. Deliberate practice **entails** more than simply repeating a task. Rather, it involves setting specific goals, obtaining **immediate feedback** and concentrating as much on technique as on outcome.

这一成功，加上后来表明记忆本身并非由**基因**决定的诸多研究，使埃里克森得出结论：记忆行为更像是一种**认知**练习，而非**直觉**行为。换句话说，无论两个人在记忆能力上表现出怎样的**先天**差异，这些差异都会**淹没**在每个人"**编码**"信息的能力之中。埃里克森认定，学习如何有意义地编码信息的最佳方法，就是被称为"**刻意练习**"的过程。刻意练习**不仅仅**是简单重复一项任务。相反，它涉及设定具体目标、获取**即时反馈**，并且既关注技巧，也关注结果。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"more of A than B"**：意为"更多是 A 而非 B"，考研翻译常考结构，须准确传达比较方向。
> - **"swamped"**："be swamped by" 意为"被……淹没/盖过"，此处引申为"先天差异被后天的编码能力所掩盖"。
> - **"deliberate practice"**：心理学核心术语，译为"刻意练习"（也译"有目的的练习"），为全文中心概念。

Ericsson and his colleagues have thus taken to studying expert performers in a wide range of **pursuits**, including soccer. They gather all the data they can, not just performance statistics and **biographical** details but also the results of their own laboratory experiments with high achievers. Their work makes a rather **startling assertion**: the trait we commonly call talent is highly **overrated**. Or, put another way, expert performers—whether in memory or surgery, ballet or computer programming—are nearly always ==made, not born==.

因此，埃里克森和他的同事们开始研究广泛**领域**中的卓越表现者，包括足球运动员。他们尽可能收集所有数据，不仅是成绩统计和**生平**细节，还包括他们自己在实验室里对高成就者进行实验的结果。他们的研究提出了一个相当**惊人的论断**：我们通常称之为天赋的特质被大大**高估**了。或者换句话说，卓越的表现者——无论是在记忆、外科手术、芭蕾舞还是计算机编程领域——几乎总是**后天造就的，而非天生如此**。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"put another way"**：固定表达，意为"换句话说"，用于引出同义改写，考研阅读中标志下文为观点重申。
> - **"made, not born"**：本段点睛之笔，"made"（后天造就）与"born"（天生）形成对比，翻译保留"造就/天生"的对仗以体现原文修辞。

## 心得

> [!tip] 文章主旨
> 本文核心论点：**天赋被高估（talent is highly overrated），卓越表现几乎总是"后天造就"而非"天生如此"（made, not born）**。掌握这一主线，可轻松应对主旨题与态度题。

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> - **Q21（结构题）**：开头提到的出生月份现象是"引出话题"（introduce the topic of what makes expert performance），答案 **C**。
> - **Q22（词义题）**：`mania` 由上下文 "at the annual peak of soccer mania" 推知为"狂热"，答案 **B**（craze）。
> - **Q23（细节题）**：good memory 取决于 "meaningful processing of information"（有意义的信息加工），答案 **A**。
> - **Q24（态度/细节题）**：高成就者主要将成功归功于后天培养（nurture），答案 **D**。
> - **Q25（主旨谚语题）**：文章最接近的谚语是"熟能生巧"（Practice makes perfect），答案 **C**。

> [!warning] 易错点
> 1. Q22 中 `hysteria`（歇斯底里）虽含"过度兴奋"义，但带贬义病态色彩，不符合语境，易误选。
> 2. 注意 `more of a cognitive exercise than an intuitive one` 的比较方向——是"认知练习"而非"直觉行为"。
> 3. `in other words`、`put another way` 等标志词后常接作者真实观点，考研阅读中为重点信息。

> [!question] 反思
> 1. 作者如何用"出生月份现象"引出主题？这种"现象 → 观点"的开头手法在考研阅读中叫什么？
> 2. "天赋被高估"是否意味着天赋完全无关？文中用了哪些限定词（如 `highly overrated`、`nearly always`）？
