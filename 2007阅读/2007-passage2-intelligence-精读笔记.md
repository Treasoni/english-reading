# Intelligence Testing and IQ Scores 精读笔记

## 背景

本文选自**2007 年考研英语阅读理解 Passage 2**，主题为**智力测验（intelligence testing）与智商分数（IQ scores）**。文章以"世界最高智商" Marilyn vos Savant 的案例开篇，介绍 IQ 测试的任务类型与两种主要量表，进而分析计分方式的演变，最后借心理学家 Robert J. Sternberg 的研究质疑标准化测验的局限。

> [!abstract] 文章概要
> 文章采用"案例引入 → 概念界定 → 计分演变 → 提出质疑 → 研究佐证"的论述结构，属于**观点论证型**议论文。前两段以 vos Savant 的案例引出"智力是否等于测验分数"的核心疑问；第三段说明 IQ 测试的形式、施测与计分变化；第四段集中论证 IQ 测验的不足——难以覆盖创造力和实践知识，且预测力随人群与情境变化而失效，作者态度趋于**怀疑（Skeptical）**。

- **难度**：intermediate
- **体裁**：议论文（argumentation）
- **题源**：2007 年考研英语阅读理解 Passage 2
- **核心概念**：IQ scores（智商分数）、standardized tests（标准化测验）

## 文章原文

### Intelligence Testing and IQ Scores

For the past several years, the Sunday newspaper supplement *Parade* has featured a column called "Ask Marilyn."

People are invited to query Marilyn vos Savant, who at age 10 had tested at a **mental level** of someone about 23 years old; that gave her an **IQ** of 228—the highest score ever recorded.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：People are invited to query Marilyn vos Savant, who at age 10 had tested at a **mental level** of someone about 23 years old; that gave her an **IQ** of 228—the highest score ever recorded.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: People
> - 谓语 V: are invited（被动语态）
> - 主语补足语 C: to query Marilyn vos Savant
> - 并列分句主语 S: that（指代前文所述测试结果）
> - 并列分句谓语 V: gave
> - 并列分句宾语 O: her an IQ of 228（双宾语结构）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非限制性定语从句 | who | 修饰 Marilyn vos Savant |
> | 介词短语 | at age 10 | 时间状语，修饰 had tested |
> | 介词短语 | at a mental level | 程度状语，修饰 had tested |
> | 介词短语 | of someone about 23 years old | 后置定语，修饰 a mental level |
> | 同位语 | 破折号 | the highest score ever recorded → 解释 an IQ of 228 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: People are invited to query Marilyn vos Savant
>   └── 非限定定从(who): who at age 10 had tested at a mental level of someone about 23 years old → 修饰 Marilyn vos Savant
>         ├── 介短(at age 10): 时间状语
>         └── 介短(at a mental level): 程度状语
>               └── 介短(of someone...): 后置定语，修饰 a mental level
> 并列分句(分号+that): that gave her an IQ of 228 → that 指代前句内容
>   └── 同位语(破折号): the highest score ever recorded → 解释 an IQ of 228
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：人们受邀向玛丽莲·沃斯·萨万特提问，她在 10 岁时测得的智力水平相当于约 23 岁的人；这使她获得了 228 的智商——这是有史以来录得的最高分。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 非限制性定语从句 `who` 修饰人名，逗号后引导，翻译时常单独成句
> 2. 分号连接两个独立分句，`that` 指代前句所述内容，指代对象判断为常考点
> 3. 破折号同位语解释说明前文名词，阅读时可先跳过细节

IQ tests ask you to complete **verbal and visual analogies**, to **envision** paper after it has been folded and cut, and to **deduce** numerical sequences, **among** other similar tasks.

So it is a bit confusing when vos Savant fields such queries from the average Joe (whose IQ is 100) as, What's the difference between love and fondness? Or what is the nature of luck and coincidence?

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：So it is a bit confusing when vos Savant fields such queries from the average Joe (whose IQ is 100) as, What's the difference between love and fondness? Or what is the nature of luck and coincidence?
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 形式主语 S: it
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: a bit confusing
> - 状语从句 A: when vos Savant fields such queries from the average Joe（时间/情景状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句 | when | 时间/情景状语，说明"令人困惑"的语境 |
> | 非限制性定语从句 | whose | 修饰 the average Joe |
> | 介词短语 | from | 修饰 fields，说明提问来源 |
> | such...as 结构 | as | 引出具体问题实例 |
> | 直接引语 | 逗号 + as | What's...? / Or what...? → 解释 such queries 的内容 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: it is a bit confusing
>   └── 状从(when): when vos Savant fields such queries from the average Joe → 时间/情景状语
>         ├── 介短(from): from the average Joe → 说明提问来源
>         ├── 非限定定从(whose): whose IQ is 100 → 修饰 the average Joe
>         └── such...as 结构: as, "What's the difference...? Or what is the nature...?" → 具体说明 such queries
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：因此，当沃斯·萨万特回答普通人（智商为 100）提出的诸如"爱与喜欢有什么区别？"或"运气与巧合的本质是什么？"这类问题时，多少有些令人困惑。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `it is + 形容词 + when 从句` 结构，when 表"当……时"，it 为非人称主语
> 2. `such...as` 搭配：as 后列举具体例子，识别 such...as 的呼应关系为考点
> 3. 直接引语作名词性成分，两个疑问句整体理解为 queries 的内容

It's not obvious how the capacity to visualize objects and to figure out numerical patterns suits one to answer questions that have **eluded** some of the best poets and philosophers.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It's not obvious how the capacity to visualize objects and to figure out numerical patterns suits one to answer questions that have eluded some of the best poets and philosophers.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 形式主语 S: It
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: not obvious
> - 真正主语 S: how the capacity to visualize objects and to figure out numerical patterns suits one to answer questions（how 引导主语从句）
>   - 主语从句内主语 S: the capacity
>   - 主语从句内谓语 V: suits
>   - 主语从句内宾语 O: one
>   - 主语从句内目的状语 A: to answer questions
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 名词性从句 | how | 引导主语从句，作 it 的真正主语 |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to visualize / to figure out | 后置定语，修饰 the capacity |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to answer | 目的状语，修饰 suits |
> | 定语从句 | that | 修饰 questions |
> | 介词短语 | of the best poets and philosophers | 后置定语，修饰 some |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: It is not obvious（it 形式主语）
>   └── 主语从句(how): how the capacity to visualize objects and to figure out numerical patterns suits one to answer questions
>         ├── 主语 S: the capacity
>         │     └── 不定式后置定语: to visualize objects and to figure out numerical patterns → 修饰 the capacity
>         ├── 谓语 V: suits
>         ├── 宾语 O: one
>         └── 目的状语: to answer questions
>               └── 定从(that): that have eluded some of the best poets and philosophers → 修饰 questions
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：尚不清楚的是，这种想象物体形象和推演数字模式的能力，如何使一个人能够回答那些连最优秀的诗人和哲学家都未能解答的问题。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `it` 形式主语 + `how` 主语从句：真正主语后置，翻译时可先译主语从句
> 2. 不定式作后置定语（the capacity **to visualize...**）与目的状语（suits one **to answer...**）的区分是难点
> 3. 多层嵌套：主语从句内又含 `that` 定语从句修饰 questions

Clearly, intelligence **encompasses** more than a score on a test.

Just what does it mean to be smart?

How much of intelligence can be specified, and how much can we learn about it from **neurology**, **genetics**, computer science and other fields?

The defining term of intelligence in humans still seems to be the IQ score, even though IQ tests are not given as often as they used to be.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The defining term of intelligence in humans still seems to be the IQ score, even though IQ tests are not given as often as they used to be.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: The defining term of intelligence in humans
> - 谓语 V: seems
> - 表语 C: to be the IQ score（不定式作表语）
> - 让步状语从句 A: even though IQ tests are not given as often as they used to be
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | of intelligence | 后置定语，修饰 the defining term |
> | 介词短语 | in humans | 范围定语，修饰 intelligence |
> | 让步状语从句 | even though | 主句与从句构成转折让步 |
> | 比较结构 | as often as | 施测频率的对比 |
> | 比较状语从句 | as they used to be | 与过去频率相比（省略 given） |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: The defining term of intelligence in humans still seems to be the IQ score
>   ├── 主语 S: The defining term of intelligence in humans
>   │     ├── 介短(of): of intelligence → 后置定语，修饰 term
>   │     └── 介短(in): in humans → 范围定语，修饰 intelligence
>   ├── 谓语 V: seems
>   └── 表语 C: to be the IQ score
> 让步状从(even though): even though IQ tests are not given as often as they used to be
>   └── 比较结构: as often as they used to be → 与过去施测频率对比（省略 given）
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：对人类智力的界定性指标似乎仍然是智商分数，尽管智商测试已不像过去那样频繁施测。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `seem to be` 系动词结构，不定式作表语，翻译为"似乎是"
> 2. `even though` 让步状语从句，与 although 同义，主从句为"转折让步"逻辑
> 3. `as often as they used to be` 比较结构，从句省略了 given，理解时需还原

The test comes primarily in two forms: the Stanford-Binet Intelligence Scale and the Wechsler Intelligence Scales (both come in adult and children's version).

Generally costing several hundred dollars, they are usually given only by psychologists, although variations of them populate bookstores and the World Wide Web.

Superhigh scores like vos Savant's are no longer possible, because scoring is now based on a **statistical population distribution** among age peers, rather than simply dividing the **mental age** by the **chronological age** and multiplying by 100.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Superhigh scores like vos Savant's are no longer possible, because scoring is now based on a **statistical population distribution** among age peers, rather than simply dividing the **mental age** by the **chronological age** and multiplying by 100.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Superhigh scores like vos Savant's
> - 谓语 V: are
> - 表语 C: no longer possible
> - 原因状语从句 A: because scoring is now based on a statistical population distribution among age peers
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | like | 后置定语，修饰 Superhigh scores |
> | 状语从句 | because | 原因状语，说明"不再可能"的原因 |
> | 介词短语 | on a statistical population distribution | 与 is based 构成搭配 |
> | 介词短语 | among age peers | 后置定语，修饰 population distribution |
> | 并列结构 | rather than | 连接 based on... 与 dividing.../multiplying... |
> | 非谓语（动名词） | dividing / multiplying | 与 based on... 并列的动名词短语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Superhigh scores like vos Savant's are no longer possible
>   ├── 介短(like): like vos Savant's → 后置定语，修饰 scores
>   └── 原因状从(because): because scoring is now based on a statistical population distribution among age peers
>         ├── 谓宾: is based on a statistical population distribution
>         │     └── 介短(among): among age peers → 后置定语，修饰 distribution
>         └── 并列(rather than): rather than simply dividing the mental age by the chronological age and multiplying by 100
>               ├── 动名词1: dividing the mental age by the chronological age
>               └── 动名词2: multiplying by 100
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：像沃斯·萨万特那样的超高分数已不再可能出现，因为现在的计分方式是基于同龄人中的统计总体分布，而不是简单地将心理年龄除以生理年龄再乘以 100。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `because` 原因状语从句解释核心观点，为 Q28 答案出处（计分方式不同）
> 2. `rather than` 连接并列结构，表示"而不是"，考研翻译常考其连接成分的对等性
> 3. 动名词 `dividing` 与 `multiplying` 为并列，介词 `by` 分别表示"除以"和"乘以"

Other **standardized tests**, such as the Scholastic Assessment Test (SAT) and the Graduate Record Exam (GRE), capture the main aspects of IQ tests.

Such standardized tests may not assess all the important elements necessary to succeed in school and in life, argues Robert J. Sternberg.

In his article "How Intelligent Is Intelligence Testing?", Sternberg notes that traditional tests best assess **analytical and verbal skills** but fail to measure **creativity** and **practical knowledge**, components also critical to problem solving and life success.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In his article "How Intelligent Is Intelligence Testing?", Sternberg notes that traditional tests best assess **analytical and verbal skills** but fail to measure **creativity** and **practical knowledge**, components also critical to problem solving and life success.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Sternberg
> - 谓语 V: notes
> - 宾语 O: that 引导的宾语从句
> - 状语 A: In his article "How Intelligent Is Intelligence Testing?"（介词短语前置）
>   - 宾语从句内主语 S: traditional tests
>   - 宾语从句内并列谓语 V: assess ... but fail to measure
>   - 宾语从句内宾语 O: analytical and verbal skills / creativity and practical knowledge
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | In his article... | 状语，说明 notes 的出处 |
> | 名词性从句 | that | 作 notes 的宾语从句 |
> | 并列连词 | but | 连接 assess 与 fail to measure 两个并列谓语 |
> | 名词短语同位语 | components... | 解释说明 creativity and practical knowledge |
> | 形容词短语 | critical to | 作后置定语，修饰 components |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Sternberg notes that ...
>   ├── 介短(In): In his article "How Intelligent Is Intelligence Testing?" → 状语
>   └── 宾语从句(that): that traditional tests best assess analytical and verbal skills but fail to measure creativity and practical knowledge
>         ├── 主语 S: traditional tests
>         ├── 谓语 V1: assess → 宾语 O1: analytical and verbal skills
>         ├── 并列连词: but
>         ├── 谓语 V2: fail to measure → 宾语 O2: creativity and practical knowledge
>         └── 同位语(逗号+名词短语): components also critical to problem solving and life success → 解释 creativity and practical knowledge
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在他的文章《智力测试有多聪明？》中，斯腾伯格指出，传统测试最能评估分析和语言能力，却未能衡量创造力和实践知识，而这些成分同样对解决问题和人生成功至关重要。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 介词短语前置作状语，主句主语 Sternberg 紧跟其后，注意定位主干
> 2. `that` 宾语从句中 `but` 连接并列谓语 `assess...but fail to measure`，译为"最能评估……却未能衡量"
> 3. 逗号后名词短语（components...）作无动词同位语，补充说明前文两个名词，为 Q29 依据

Moreover, IQ tests do not necessarily predict so well once populations or situations change.

Research has found that IQ predicted **leadership skills** when the tests were given under low-stress conditions, but under high-stress conditions, IQ was **negatively correlated** with leadership—that is, it predicted the opposite.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Research has found that IQ predicted **leadership skills** when the tests were given under low-stress conditions, but under high-stress conditions, IQ was **negatively correlated** with leadership—that is, it predicted the opposite.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Research
> - 谓语 V: has found
> - 宾语 O: that 引导的宾语从句
>   - 宾语从句分句1 主语 S: IQ
>   - 宾语从句分句1 谓语 V: predicted
>   - 宾语从句分句1 宾语 O: leadership skills
>   - 宾语从句分句2 主语 S: IQ
>   - 宾语从句分句2 谓语 V: was negatively correlated with
>   - 宾语从句分句2 宾语 O: leadership
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 名词性从句 | that | 作 has found 的宾语从句 |
> | 时间状语从句 | when | 修饰 predicted，说明低压力条件 |
> | 并列连词 | but | 连接两个对比性分句 |
> | 介词短语 | under low-stress / high-stress conditions | 条件状语 |
> | 破折号解释 | that is | 解释 negatively correlated 的含义 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Research has found that ...
>   └── 宾语从句(that): that IQ predicted leadership skills when..., but under high-stress conditions, IQ was negatively correlated with leadership
>         ├── 分句1: IQ predicted leadership skills
>         │     └── 时间状从(when): when the tests were given under low-stress conditions → 修饰 predicted
>         │           └── 介短(under): under low-stress conditions → 条件状语
>         ├── 并列连词: but
>         ├── 分句2: IQ was negatively correlated with leadership
>         │     └── 介短(under): under high-stress conditions → 前置条件状语
>         └── 破折号解释: that is, it predicted the opposite → 解释 negatively correlated
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：研究发现，在低压力条件下施测时，智商能够预测领导能力；但在高压力条件下，智商与领导能力呈负相关——也就是说，它预测的结果恰恰相反。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `that` 宾语从句内 `but` 连接两个对比分句，构成"低压力→正相关 / 高压力→负相关"的对比
> 2. `when` 时间状语从句插入分句1，理解时注意主谓间隔
> 3. `that is` 引导解释说明，破折号后内容为理解负相关含义的关键

>

> |------|--------|----------|
> | 定语从句 | who | 修饰 Anyone |
> | 介词短语 | through SAT | 与 toiled 构成固定搭配 toil through |
> | 名词性从句 | that | 作 will testify 的宾语从句 |
> | 让步状语从句 | whether | 说明 matters 的两种情形 |
> | 非谓语（动名词） | knowing | 作 it's 的表语 |
> | 疑问词 + 不定式 | when to guess / what questions to skip | 作 knowing 的宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Anyone who has toiled through SAT will testify that ...
>   ├── 定从(who): who has toiled through SAT → 修饰 Anyone
>   │     └── 介短(through): through SAT → 与 toiled 搭配
>   └── 宾语从句(that): that test-taking skill also matters
>         └── 让步状从(whether): whether it's knowing when to guess or what questions to skip
>               └── 表语: knowing [when to guess / what questions to skip] → 疑问词+不定式作宾语
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：任何苦读过 SAT 的人都会证明，应试技巧同样重要，无论是知道何时该猜，还是知道哪些题目该跳过。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `who` 定语从句使主语拉长，谓语 `will testify` 前修饰成分多，需先找主干
> 2. `whether` 引导让步状语从句，译为"无论是……还是……"
> 3. 疑问词 + 不定式（`when to guess`、`what questions to skip`）作 `knowing` 的宾语，为考研语法常考点

## 翻译对照

### Intelligence Testing and IQ Scores 智力测验与智商分数 中英对照

For the past several years, the Sunday newspaper supplement *Parade* has featured a column called "Ask Marilyn." People are invited to query Marilyn vos Savant, who at age 10 had tested at a **mental level** of someone about 23 years old; that gave her an **IQ** of 228—the highest score ever recorded. IQ tests ask you to complete **verbal and visual analogies**, to **envision** paper after it has been folded and cut, and to **deduce** numerical sequences, among other similar tasks. So it is a bit confusing when vos Savant fields such queries from the average Joe (whose IQ is 100) as, What's the difference between love and fondness? Or what is the nature of luck and coincidence? It's not obvious how the capacity to visualize objects and to figure out numerical patterns suits one to answer questions that have eluded some of the best poets and philosophers.

在过去的几年里，周日报纸的增刊《Parade》一直设有一个名为"问问玛丽莲"的专栏。人们受邀向玛丽莲·沃斯·萨万特提问，她在 10 岁时测得的智力水平相当于约 23 岁的人；这使得她的智商达到了 228——这是有史以来录得的最高分。智商测试要求你完成语言和视觉类比、想象纸张被折叠和裁剪后的样子、推导数字序列，以及其他类似的题目。因此，当沃斯·萨万特回答普通人（智商为 100）提出的诸如"爱与喜欢有什么区别？"或"运气与巧合的本质是什么？"这类问题时，多少有些令人困惑。尚不清楚的是，这种想象物体形象和推演数字模式的能力，如何使一个人能够回答那些连最优秀的诗人和哲学家都未能解答的问题。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"the average Joe"**：美式口语，指"普通人、一般人"，不可直译为"平均的乔"。
> - **"fields such queries"**："field" 此处作动词，意为"回答、应对（问题）"，与 "answer" 近义。
> - **"suits one to"**："suit ... to" 意为"使……适合/胜任"，此处指"使某人有资格去回答"。
> - **"eluded"**："elude" 意为"使……无法理解/难倒"，此处指"难倒了最优秀的诗人和哲学家"。

Clearly, intelligence **encompasses** more than a score on a test. Just what does it mean to be smart? How much of intelligence can be specified, and how much can we learn about it from **neurology**, **genetics**, computer science and other fields?

显然，智力所包含的远不止测试中的一个分数。聪明究竟意味着什么？智力有多少可以被明确定义，我们能从神经学、遗传学、计算机科学及其他领域中对它了解多少？

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"encompasses more than a score"**："encompass" 意为"包含、涵盖"，此处译为"智力所包含的远不止一个分数"。
> - 本段三个连续疑问句，翻译保留疑问语气以体现原文的思辨色彩。

The defining term of intelligence in humans still seems to be the IQ score, even though IQ tests are not given as often as they used to be. The test comes primarily in two forms: the Stanford-Binet Intelligence Scale and the Wechsler Intelligence Scales (both come in adult and children's version). Generally costing several hundred dollars, they are usually given only by psychologists, although variations of them populate bookstores and the World Wide Web. Superhigh scores like vos Savant's are no longer possible, because scoring is now based on a **statistical population distribution** among age peers, rather than simply dividing the **mental age** by the **chronological age** and multiplying by 100. Other **standardized tests**, such as the Scholastic Assessment Test (SAT) and the Graduate Record Exam (GRE), capture the main aspects of IQ tests.

对人类智力的界定性指标似乎仍然是智商分数，尽管智商测试已不像过去那样频繁施测。这种测试主要有两种形式：斯坦福-比奈智力量表和韦克斯勒智力量表（两者都有成人和儿童版本）。这类测试通常要花费数百美元，一般只由心理学家施测，尽管它们的各种变体在书店和互联网上随处可见。像沃斯·萨万特那样的超高分数已不再可能出现，因为现在的计分方式是基于同龄人中的统计总体分布，而不是简单地将心理年龄除以生理年龄再乘以 100。其他标准化测试，如学术能力评估测试（SAT）和研究生入学考试（GRE），也涵盖了智商测试的主要方面。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"The defining term of intelligence"**："defining term" 指"界定性术语/标志性指标"，此处指人们仍以 IQ 分数作为智力的界定性指标。
> - **"populate bookstores and the World Wide Web"**："populate" 意为"遍布、充斥"，此处译为"在书店和互联网上随处可见"。
> - **"age peers"**：指"同龄人"，不可直译为"年龄同伴"。
> - **"mental age" / "chronological age"**：心理学术语，分别指"心理年龄"（智力发展水平对应的年龄）与"生理年龄/实足年龄"。

Such standardized tests may not assess all the important elements necessary to succeed in school and in life, argues Robert J. Sternberg. In his article "How Intelligent Is Intelligence Testing?", Sternberg notes that traditional tests best assess **analytical and verbal skills** but fail to measure **creativity** and **practical knowledge**, components also critical to problem solving and life success. Moreover, IQ tests do not necessarily predict so well once populations or situations change. Research has found that IQ predicted **leadership skills** when the tests were given under low-stress conditions, but under high-stress conditions, IQ was **negatively correlated** with leadership—that is, it predicted the opposite. Anyone who has toiled through SAT will testify that test-taking skill also matters, whether it's knowing when to guess or what questions to skip.

罗伯特·J·斯腾伯格指出，这类标准化考试可能无法评估在学校和生活中取得成功所必需的所有重要因素。在他的文章《智力测试有多聪明？》中，斯腾伯格指出，传统测试最能评估分析和语言能力，却未能衡量创造力和实践知识，而这些成分同样对解决问题和人生成功至关重要。此外，一旦人群或情境发生变化，智商测试就不一定能预测得那么准确。研究发现，在低压力条件下施测时，智商能够预测领导能力；但在高压力条件下，智商与领导能力呈负相关——也就是说，它预测的结果恰恰相反。任何苦读过 SAT 的人都会证明，应试技巧同样重要，无论是知道何时该猜，还是知道哪些题目该跳过。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"negatively correlated with"**：统计学术语，意为"与……呈负相关"，即一个上升、另一个下降。
> - **"toiled through"**："toil" 意为"辛苦劳作"，"toiled through SAT" 译为"苦读/苦熬过 SAT"。
> - **"test-taking skill"**：指"应试技巧"，区别于真正的学术能力，为 Q29 的考点。

## 语法要点

### 固定搭配：Such as 的两种结构

`such as` 有两种结构：**拆分式** `such + N + as` 与**连用式** `N + such as`，意思相同，但拆分式更书面化、更有"高级感"。考研阅读中识别 `such...as` 的呼应关系，可快速定位举例内容。

> [!tip] 记忆方法
> 两种结构意思完全一样，但拆开写的 **such + N + as** 在高考阅读、四六级和写作中显得**更有高级感**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **such + 复数名词/不可数名词 + as + 举例内容** | 拆分结构（更书面化），as 引出举例内容 | I like **such fruits as** apples and pears.<br>He deals with **such matters as** complaints and refunds. |
| **名词 + such as + 举例内容** | 连用结构（日常最常用），such as 直接引出例子 | I like fruits **such as** apples and pears. |

> [!note] 文章联动
> 原文第 1 段 "fields **such** queries from the average Joe ... **as**, What's the difference..." 即拆分式结构，`as` 后引出两个具体问题，是 `such...as` 呼应关系的实例。

### 补充要点：四则运算的英语表达

英语中四则运算的**动词动作短语**与**算式读法**各有固定搭配。本文第 3 段 "dividing the **mental age** by the **chronological age** and multiplying by 100" 即用到 `divide A by B` 结构。

> [!tip] 记忆要点
> 加、减、乘、除的动作短语介词各有不同——**add to / subtract from / multiply by / divide by**，其中减法和除法方向相反（from B 减去、by B 除以）。

**除法（Divide ÷）**

| 要素 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| **动作短语** | **Divide A by B**（用 B 除 A / 把 A 除以 B，即 A ÷ B） |
| **动作例句** | **Divide** 12 **by** 3.（把 12 除以 3。） |
| **算式读法** | **A divided by B is / equals C** |
| **读法例句** | 12 ÷ 3 = 4 → 12 **divided by** 3 **is / equals** 4. |

> [!warning] 常见错误：by vs into
>
> | 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
> |------|------|------|
> | **Divide A by B** | 数学除法算式（A ÷ B） | Divide 12 **by** 3. |
> | **Divide A into B** | 切分/分组（把 A 分成 B 份） | Divide the cake **into** 4 pieces.（把蛋糕切成 4 块。） |

**乘法（Multiply ×）**

| 要素 | 表达 |
|------|------|
| **动作短语** | **Multiply A by B**（用 B 乘以 A / A 乘以 B，即 A × B） |
| **动作例句** | **Multiply** 3 **by** 4.（用 4 乘以 3。） |
| **算式读法** | 书面/正式：**A multiplied by B equals C** ／ 日常/口语：**A times B equals C**（最常用） |
| **读法例句** | 3 × 4 = 12 → 3 **multiplied by** 4 **is** 12. 或 3 **times** 4 **equals** 12. |

**完整四则运算速查表**

| 运算 | 符号 | 动词/动作短语 | 算式读法（介词/连词） | 算式示例与读法 |
|------|------|--------------|---------------------|---------------|
| **加** | ➕ | **Add A to B** | A **plus** B is C | 2 + 3 = 5 → Two **plus** three **is** five. |
| **减** | ➖ | **Subtract A from B** | B **minus** A is C | 5 − 2 = 3 → Five **minus** two **is** three. |
| **乘** | ✖️ | **Multiply A by B** | A **times** B / A **multiplied by** B is C | 3 × 4 = 12 → Three **times** four **equals** twelve. |
| **除** | ➗ | **Divide A by B** | A **divided by** B is C | 12 ÷ 3 = 4 → Twelve **divided by** three **is** four. |

> [!note] 文章联动
> 原文第 3 段 "dividing the **mental age** by the **chronological age** and multiplying by 100" 正是 `divide A by B` 与 `multiply A by B` 的并列使用，为 Q28"计分方式变化"的理解基础。

   - B. 包含；涵盖 includes
   - C. 排除 excludes
   - D. 限制 limits

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **B** — "elude" 意为"使……无法理解/难倒"，此处指这些问题连最优秀的诗人和哲学家都未能解答；"confused" 表"混淆"，对象不同，不符合语境。
> 2. **B** — "negatively correlated with" 为统计术语，意为"与……呈负相关"，即一个上升、另一个下降；"positively related" 方向相反。
> 3. **B** — "encompass" 意为"包含、涵盖"，下文 "more than a score" 提示智力包含的内容不止分数一项；"equals" 意为"等同于"，与原意不符。

## 心得

> [!tip] 文章主旨
> 本文并非全盘否定 IQ 测试，而是**辩证看待**其作为智力指标的有效性：作者承认 IQ 分数至今仍是智力的界定性指标，但借 Sternberg 的研究指出其局限——未测创造力与实践知识，且预测力随人群/情境变化而失效。这种"承认现实 → 指出不足"的论证方式，是考研议论文的常见姿态，也决定了作者态度的方向。

> [!warning] 易错点
> Q30 态度题易误选 **Impartial（不偏不倚）**。文章对 IQ 测试明显持**怀疑（Skeptical）**态度：第 4 段连续使用 "may not assess"、"fail to measure"、"do not necessarily predict" 等否定性表达，且用 "negatively correlated"（负相关）暗示 IQ 在特定情境下的预测力反转——负面信号密集，非中立立场。

> [!question] 反思
> 1. IQ 测试测量的是"智力"本身，还是特定认知技能（类比、折叠、数列）？（构念效度问题）
> 2. 第 3 段计分方式改为"同龄人常模分布"后，为何超高分数不再可能？（统计含义：分数是对群体的相对位置）
> 3. Sternberg 认为成功还需哪些 IQ 测不到的能力？（创造力、实践知识）——这是 Q29 的命题源头。
