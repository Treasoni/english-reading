# The US Jury System 精读笔记

## 背景

本文介绍美国陪审团制度所宣称的民主原则、历史上的代表性缺陷，以及 1968 年《陪审员选拔与服务法》和 1975 年 Taylor v. Louisiana 判决带来的改革。文章以陪审员资格、随机抽选、种族与性别平等为线索，呈现制度从理想走向更具代表性的历史发展过程。

- **文章来源**：2010 年考研英语阅读 Passage 4（原文来源见 `2010阅读/passage_4.md`）。
- **文章难度**：中等偏上；难点集中在多重同位语从句、被动结构、非谓语动词和历史事件之间的时间关系。
- **阅读价值**：适合训练“先抓制度原则，再按时间顺序定位问题与解决方案”的说明文阅读方法。

# The US Jury System

## Reading Passage

Many Americans regard the **jury system** as a concrete expression of **crucial** **democratic values**, including the principles that all citizens who meet minimal qualifications of age and literacy are equally competent to serve on juries; that jurors should be selected randomly from a **representative** cross section of the community; that no citizen should be denied the right to serve on a jury on account of race, religion, sex, or national origin; that **defendants** are entitled to trial by their peers; and that verdicts should represent the conscience of the community and not just the letter of the law.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Many Americans regard the **jury system** as a concrete expression of **crucial** **democratic values**, including the principles that all citizens who meet minimal qualifications of age and literacy are equally competent to serve on juries; that jurors should be selected randomly from a representative cross section of the community; that no citizen should be denied the right to serve on a jury on account of race, religion, sex, or national origin; that defendants are entitled to trial by their peers; and that verdicts should represent the conscience of the community and not just the letter of the law.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Many Americans
> - 谓语 V: regard
> - 宾语 O: the jury system
> - 宾语补语 C: a concrete expression of crucial democratic values
>
> **主干简化**：Many Americans regard the jury system as a concrete expression of democratic values.
>

>
> **结构图解**：

>
> **参考译文**：许多美国人认为，陪审团制度是关键民主价值的具体体现，其中包括以下原则：所有达到最低年龄和读写能力资格的公民，都同样有能力担任陪审员；陪审员应从社区具有代表性的各阶层中随机选出；任何公民都不应因种族、宗教、性别或国籍而被剥夺担任陪审员的权利；被告有权接受同辈审判；判决应体现社区的良知，而不只是法律条文。
>
> **考点提示**：`regard A as B` 表示“认为 A 是 B”；`including` 后接多个由 `that` 引导的并列同位语从句，集中说明 `the principles` 的具体内容；长句中的分号用于分隔并列原则。

The jury is also said to be the best surviving example of direct rather than representative democracy. In a direct democracy, citizens take turns governing themselves, rather than electing representatives to govern for them.

But as recently as in 1968, jury selection procedures conflicted with these democratic ideals. In some states, for example, jury duty was limited to persons of supposedly superior intelligence, education, and moral character.

Although the Supreme Court of the United States had prohibited intentional racial discrimination in jury selection as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia, the practice of selecting so-called **elite or blue-ribbon juries** provided a convenient way around this and other **antidiscrimination laws**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Although the Supreme Court of the United States had prohibited intentional racial discrimination in jury selection as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia, the practice of selecting so-called **elite or blue-ribbon juries** provided a convenient way around this and other **antidiscrimination laws**.
>

>

> **参考译文**：尽管美国最高法院早在1880年的“斯特劳德诉西弗吉尼亚州案”中就禁止在陪审员选拔中进行故意的种族歧视，但选出所谓的精英陪审团或蓝带陪审团，为规避这项以及其他反歧视法律提供了一条便利途径。

> **考点提示**：句首 `Although` 引导让步状语从句；`had prohibited` 是过去完成时，表示该禁令早于后面所述的规避行为；`the practice of doing` 中 `of` 后接动名词。

The system also failed to regularly include women on juries until the mid-20th century. Although women first served on state juries in Utah in 1898, it was not until the 1940s that a majority of states made women eligible for jury duty.

Even then several states automatically exempted women from jury duty unless they personally asked to have their names included on the jury list.

## 翻译对照

# The US Jury System 美国陪审团制度｜中英对照翻译

## Reading Passage 阅读文章

Many Americans regard the **jury system** as a concrete expression of **crucial** **democratic values**, including the principles that all citizens who meet minimal qualifications of age and literacy are equally competent to serve on juries; that jurors should be selected randomly from a representative cross section of the community; that no citizen should be denied the right to serve on a jury on account of race, religion, sex, or national origin; that defendants are entitled to trial by their peers; and that verdicts should represent the conscience of the community and not just the letter of the law. The jury is also said to be the best surviving example of direct rather than representative democracy. In a direct democracy, citizens take turns governing themselves, rather than electing representatives to govern for them.

许多美国人认为，**陪审团制度**是关键**民主价值**的具体体现，其中包括以下原则：所有达到最低年龄和读写能力资格的公民，都同样有能力担任陪审员；陪审员应从社区具有代表性的各阶层中随机选出；任何公民都不应因种族、宗教、性别或国籍而被剥夺担任陪审员的权利；被告有权接受同辈审判；判决应体现社区的良知，而不只是法律条文。陪审团也被称为现存的直接民主而非代议民主的最佳例证。在直接民主中，公民轮流管理自己，而不是选举代表替他们治理。

But as recently as in 1968, jury selection procedures conflicted with these democratic ideals. In some states, for example, jury duty was limited to persons of supposedly superior intelligence, education, and moral character. Although the Supreme Court of the United States had prohibited intentional racial discrimination in jury selection as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia, the practice of selecting so-called **elite or blue-ribbon juries** provided a convenient way around this and other **antidiscrimination laws**.

然而，直到1968年，陪审员的选拔程序仍然与这些民主理想相冲突。例如，在一些州，陪审员职责仅限于那些被认为智力、受教育程度和道德品质优越的人。尽管美国最高法院早在1880年的“斯特劳德诉西弗吉尼亚州案”中就禁止在陪审员选拔中进行故意的种族歧视，但选出所谓的**精英陪审团或蓝带陪审团**，为规避这项以及其他**反歧视法律**提供了一条便利途径。

The system also failed to regularly include women on juries until the mid-20th century. Although women first served on state juries in Utah in 1898, it was not until the 1940s that a majority of states made women eligible for jury duty. Even then several states automatically exempted women from jury duty unless they personally asked to have their names included on the jury list. This practice was justified by the claim that women were needed at home, and it kept juries unrepresentative of women through the 1960s.

直到20世纪中叶，这一制度仍未能定期让女性加入陪审团。尽管女性于1898年首次在犹他州担任州陪审员，但直到20世纪40年代，大多数州才规定女性有资格履行陪审员职责。即便如此，一些州仍自动免除女性的陪审员义务，除非她们亲自要求将自己的名字列入陪审员名单。这种做法以“女性需要在家照料家庭”为理由，导致陪审团在整个20世纪60年代仍不能代表女性。

In 1968, the Congress of the United States passed the **Jury Selection and Service Act**, ushering in a new era of democratic reforms for the jury. This law abolished special educational requirements for federal jurors and required them to be selected at random from a cross section of the entire community. In the landmark 1975 decision Taylor v. Louisiana, the Supreme Court extended the requirement that juries be representative of all parts of the community to the state level. The Taylor decision also declared sex discrimination in jury selection to be **unconstitutional** and ordered states to use the same procedures for selecting male and female jurors.

1968年，美国国会通过了《**陪审员选拔与服务法**》，由此开启了陪审团民主改革的新时代。该法律取消了对联邦陪审员的特殊教育要求，并规定陪审员必须从整个社区各阶层中随机选出。在具有里程碑意义的1975年“泰勒诉路易斯安那州案”判决中，最高法院将陪审团应代表社区各个部分的要求扩大到州一级。泰勒案判决还宣布，在陪审员选拔中实行性别歧视属于**违宪**，并要求各州采用相同的程序选拔男性和女性陪审员。

## 语法要点

# The US Jury System 语法要点整理

> [!note]
> 本笔记根据文章原文归纳，未使用独立语法讲义；例句均取自本文或保留本文原有结构。

---

### 名词性从句：同位语从句 (Noun Clauses: Appositive Clauses)

文章用多个 `that` 从句具体解释 **the principles** 的内容。判断同位语从句时，要看从句是否说明抽象名词的具体内容，而不是修饰某个名词。

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 遇到 `the principle(s) that...`、`the claim that...`、`the requirement that...`，先把从句整体括起来，再回到主句寻找谓语和主干。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **the principles that + 从句** | 说明 principles 的具体内容 | including the principles **that all citizens ... are equally competent**; **that jurors should be selected randomly**; and ... |
| **the claim that + 从句** | 说明 claim 的具体内容 | This practice was justified by the claim **that women were needed at home**. |
| **the requirement that + 从句** | 说明 requirement 的具体内容 | The Supreme Court extended the requirement **that juries be representative of all parts of the community**. |

> [!warning] 易错点
> 第一段中多个 `that` 从句由分号和 `and` 并列，共同受 `the principles` 支配；不要把每个 `that` 都误判为定语从句。

---

### 从句：定语从句 (Attributive Clauses)

定语从句修饰前面的名词，为其增加限定或补充信息。本文中的 `who` 从句修饰 `all citizens`。

| 类型 | 引导词 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 限制性定语从句 | **who** | all citizens **who meet minimal qualifications of age and literacy** |

> [!note]
> `who meet ...` 的先行词是复数名词 `citizens`，因此谓语使用 `meet`；从句内部的 `of age and literacy` 修饰 `qualifications`。

---

### 从句：让步与条件状语从句 (Adverbial Clauses)

文章通过 `although` 引出历史背景中的让步信息，通过 `unless` 表达女性只有主动申请才不会被免除陪审义务的条件。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **Although + 从句, 主句** | 表示“尽管……但是……” | **Although** the Supreme Court ... had prohibited intentional racial discrimination ..., the practice ... provided a convenient way around ... |
| **unless + 从句** | 表示“除非……”，可理解为 `if ... not` | Several states automatically exempted women from jury duty **unless** they personally asked ... |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> `Although` 从句通常是让步背景，主句才是作者推进论述的核心；`unless` 要结合主句一起翻译，避免误解为一般的时间条件。

---

### 时态 (Tenses)

本文以一般现在时说明制度原则，以一般过去时叙述改革历史，并用过去完成时标明禁令先于后文规避行为发生。

| 时态 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般现在时 | 陈述制度原则、普遍事实 | Many Americans **regard** the jury system ...; verdicts **should represent** the conscience of the community. |
| 一般过去时 | 叙述过去发生的法律或制度变化 | Congress **passed** the Jury Selection and Service Act. |
| 过去完成时 | 表示过去某一动作发生在另一过去动作之前 | Although the Supreme Court ... **had prohibited** intentional racial discrimination ..., the practice ... provided ... |
| `until` 时间结构 | 表示某状态持续到某个时间点 | The system failed to regularly include women on juries **until** the mid-20th century. |

> [!note]
> `as early as the 1880 case` 强调时间之早；它与 `had prohibited` 共同表明最高法院的禁令早于后来对制度漏洞的描述。

---

### 语态：被动语态 (Voice: Passive Voice)

被动语态突出动作承受者，本文大量用于描述陪审员被选出、被剥夺权利或被免除义务的制度安排。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **should be + 过去分词** | 表示应当被…… | jurors **should be selected** randomly |
| **should be denied** | 表示不应被剥夺 | no citizen **should be denied** the right to serve on a jury |
| **be said to + 动词原形** | `say` 的被动表达，表示“据说……” | The jury **is also said to be** the best surviving example ... |
| **be limited to** | 表示“被限制在……” | jury duty **was limited to** persons of supposedly superior ... |
| **be + 过去分词** | 说明制度动作的承受关系 | women were ...; jurors ... **be selected** at random |

> [!warning] 翻译注意
> 中文翻译可根据语境灵活转换为主动表达。例如 `jurors should be selected` 可译为“应随机选出陪审员”，不必机械译成“陪审员应被选出”。

---

### 非谓语动词 (Non-finite Verbs)

非谓语动词压缩了动作关系，是本文长句信息密集的重要来源。分析时应先判断其逻辑主语，再判断主动或被动。

| 形式 | 功能 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **including + 名词/从句** | 现在分词短语，补充说明前面的 democratic values | democratic values, **including the principles that ...** |
| **to serve / to govern** | 不定式，表示目的或动作内容 | competent **to serve** on juries; representatives **to govern** for them |
| **rather than + V-ing** | 表示“而不是做……”的对比 | take turns governing themselves, **rather than electing** representatives |
| **of + V-ing** | 动名词作介词宾语，说明行为内容 | the practice **of selecting** so-called elite ... juries |
| **ushering in + 名词** | 现在分词短语，补充说明前句结果 | passed the Act, **ushering in** a new era ... |
| **to have + 宾语 + 过去分词** | 使役结构，表示“让某物被……” | asked **to have their names included** on the jury list |
| **for + V-ing** | 动名词短语作后置修饰 | procedures **for selecting** male and female jurors |

> [!tip] 结构辨析
> `have + 宾语 + 过去分词` 中，宾语与过去分词是被动关系：`their names` 被 `included`。不要把 `included` 当作主句谓语。

---

### 虚拟语气：要求类 that 从句 (Subjunctive Mood)

在 `requirement that ...` 中，`that` 从句表达要求或规范内容，`juries be representative` 使用动词原形 `be`，体现正式英语中的要求类虚拟结构。

> [!tip] 识别方法
> 表示要求、建议、命令、必要性的名词或动词后，`that` 从句有时使用“主语 + 动词原形”；本文的关键词是 `requirement`。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **requirement that + 主语 + 动词原形** | 表达规范或要求 | the requirement **that juries be representative** of all parts of the community |

---

### 倒装与强调：It is/was not until ... that ...

`it was not until ... that ...` 是强调句型，强调时间状语“直到……才……”。还原后可理解为 `A majority of states did not make women eligible for jury duty until the 1940s.`

> [!tip] 考试提示
> 看到 `not until` 时，先锁定“直到……才……”的时间逻辑；强调句中的 `that` 不承担普通从句连接词的名词修饰功能。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **It was not until + 时间 + that + 从句** | 强调动作发生的时间 | **it was not until the 1940s that** a majority of states made women eligible for jury duty |

---

### 宾语补足语与并列谓语 (Object Complements & Coordination)

本文多次使用“动词 + 宾语 + 补语”结构，并通过 `and` 连接两个谓语或两个不定式结构。识别宾语补足语有助于快速还原句子主干。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **regard A as B** | 认为 A 是 B；`as B` 作宾语补足语 | Many Americans **regard the jury system as a concrete expression** of democratic values. |
| **make A + 形容词** | 使 A 处于某种状态 | a majority of states **made women eligible** for jury duty |
| **declare A to be B** | 宣布 A 是 B；`to be B` 作宾语补足语 | The Taylor decision **declared sex discrimination ... to be unconstitutional**. |
| **order A to do B** | 命令 A 做 B；`to do B` 作宾语补足语 | ordered **states to use** the same procedures ... |
| **abolish A and require B** | `and` 连接同一主语的并列谓语 | This law **abolished** special educational requirements ... **and required** them to be selected ... |

> [!note]
> `require + 宾语 + to do` 与 `order + 宾语 + to do` 结构相似；若不定式为被动形式，则使用 `to be + 过去分词`，如 `required them to be selected`。

---

### 介词与连词：对比、范围和原因 (Prepositions & Conjunctions)

介词短语和连接词共同构成文章的逻辑骨架，尤其要注意 `rather than`、`on account of`、`from a cross section of` 等结构的整体含义。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **rather than** | 表示对比选择，而不是 | direct **rather than representative** democracy |
| **on account of** | 表示原因，由于 | denied ... **on account of** race, religion, sex, or national origin |
| **be entitled to** | 表示有权享有 | defendants are **entitled to** trial by their peers |
| **from a cross section of** | 表示从具有代表性的各类群体中 | selected ... **from a cross section of** the entire community |
| **through + 时间段** | 表示持续到某一时间段 | it kept juries unrepresentative of women **through the 1960s** |

> [!note]
> `not just the letter of the law` 与前面的 `the conscience of the community` 构成“不是仅仅……而是还……”的语义对比，理解时不要漏译 `not just`。

   - A. legally punished
   - B. capable and qualified
   - C. randomly selected
   - D. morally superior

2. In “a **representative** cross section of the community”, **representative** means:
   - A. made up of different parts in a way that reflects the whole

## 心得

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 1. **先抓文章主线**：本文基本遵循“民主原则 → 历史缺陷 → 法律改革”的结构，最后一题可优先寻找能够覆盖“问题及解决办法”的选项。
> 2. **利用时间标记定位**：`1880`、`1898`、`1940s`、`1968`、`1975` 构成清晰时间轴，细节题通常可以据此快速回原文定位。
> 3. **区分法律层级**：1968 年法案主要针对联邦陪审员；1975 年 Taylor 判决将代表性要求扩展到州一级。不要把两项改革的适用范围混淆。

> [!warning] 易错点
> - `unless` 表示“除非”，原文意为女性必须主动提出请求，才可能被列入陪审员名单。
> - `elite or blue-ribbon juries` 是绕开反歧视法律的做法，并不代表陪审团真正实现了民主代表性。
> - 题 39 的正确判断来自法案之后的直接效果：联邦陪审员的特殊教育要求被取消，因此教育资格“不再那么严格”。

> [!note] 文章论证结构
> 本文不是单纯介绍陪审团制度的性质或传统，而是围绕制度的民主理想、现实问题及法律解决方案展开。
