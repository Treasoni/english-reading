# Art Market in Decline 艺术品市场低迷精读笔记

## 背景

本文围绕2008年全球金融危机前后的艺术品市场展开，重点说明艺术市场在长期繁荣后进入低迷期的表现、原因与市场特征。文章将艺术品市场的下跌与雷曼兄弟破产并置，突出市场转折的戏剧性。

> [!abstract] 文章概要
> 文章的论证脉络是：艺术市场繁荣见顶 → 金融危机冲击消费 → 艺术品价格与销售额下跌 → 市场仍有买家，但优质作品供给不足。

- **主题**：艺术品市场的衰退及其成因
- **体裁**：经济与社会类说明性报道
- **难度**：intermediate
- **核心背景**：2008年全球金融危机

---

## 文章原文

The longest **bull** run in a century of art-market history ended on a dramatic note with a sale of 56 works by Damien Hirst, *Beautiful Inside My Head Forever*, at Sotheby's in London on September 15th 2008.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The longest **bull** run in a century of art-market history ended on a dramatic note with a sale of 56 works by Damien Hirst, *Beautiful Inside My Head Forever*, at Sotheby's in London on September 15th 2008.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: The longest bull run
> - 谓语 V: ended
> - 状语 A: on a dramatic note
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 内容 | 修饰对象 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 介词短语 | in a century of art-market history | run |
> | 介词短语 | with a sale of 56 works | ended |
> | 介词短语 | by Damien Hirst | works |
> | 同位语 | *Beautiful Inside My Head Forever* | a sale of 56 works by Damien Hirst（拍卖会名称） |
> | 介词短语 | at Sotheby's in London | sale |
> | 时间状语 | on September 15th 2008 | ended |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句：The longest bull run ended
> ├── 介短：in a century of art-market history → 修饰 run
> ├── 介短：with a sale of 56 works → 伴随的具体事件
> │   ├── 介短：by Damien Hirst → 修饰 works
> │   └── 同位语：*Beautiful Inside My Head Forever* → 拍卖会名称
> ├── 介短：at Sotheby's in London → 修饰 sale
> └── 时间状语：on September 15th 2008
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：一个世纪以来艺术品市场历史上持续时间最长的牛市行情，以一场于2008年9月15日在伦敦苏富比举行、名为《永远美丽地存在于我脑海中》的达明安·赫斯特56件作品拍卖会戏剧性地收场。
>
> **考点提示**：长句中多个介词短语连续后置修饰；`with + 名词` 表示伴随或方式。

All but two pieces sold, fetching more than £70m, a record for a sale by a single artist. It was a last victory. As the auctioneer called out bids, in New York one of the oldest banks on Wall Street, Lehman Brothers, filed for bankruptcy.

The world art market had already been losing momentum for a while after rising bewilderingly since 2003. At its peak in 2007 it was worth some $65 billion, reckons Clare McAndrew, founder of Arts Economics, a research firm—double the figure five years earlier.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：At its peak in 2007 it was worth some $65 billion, reckons Clare McAndrew, founder of Arts Economics, a research firm—double the figure five years earlier.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 状语 A: At its peak in 2007
> - 主语 S: it
> - 谓语 V: was worth
> - 补语 C: some $65 billion
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 内容 | 修饰对象 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 介词短语 | in 2007 | peak |
> | 插入语 | reckons Clare McAndrew | 说明信息来源 |
> | 同位语 | founder of Arts Economics | Clare McAndrew |
> | 同位语 | a research firm | Arts Economics |
> | 比较结构 | double the figure five years earlier | $65 billion |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 状语：At its peak in 2007
> 主句：it was worth some $65 billion
> ├── 插入语：reckons Clare McAndrew
> │   ├── 同位语：founder of Arts Economics
> │   └── 同位语：a research firm
> └── 比较补充：double the figure five years earlier
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：艺术品市场在2007年达到顶峰时价值约650亿美元，据艺术经济学公司——一家研究公司——的创始人克莱尔·麦克安德鲁估计，这一数字是五年前的两倍。
>
> **考点提示**：`reckons Clare McAndrew` 是插入性的倒装表达，正常语序为 `Clare McAndrew reckons`；破折号后的比较短语补充说明前面的金额。

Since then it may have come down to $50 billion. But the market generates interest far beyond its size because it brings together great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy in a way matched by few other industries.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：But the market generates interest far beyond its size because it brings together great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy in a way matched by few other industries.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: the market
> - 谓语 V: generates
> - 宾语 O: interest
> - 状语 A1: far beyond its size
> - 原因状语 A2: because it brings together great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy in a way matched by few other industries
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 内容 | 修饰对象 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 原因状语从句 | because it brings together... | generates interest |
> | 介词短语 | far beyond its size | interest 的程度（修饰 generates interest） |
> | 介词短语 | in a way | brings together |
> | 非谓语/分词短语 | matched by few other industries | way |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句：the market generates interest
> ├── 程度状语：far beyond its size
> └── 原因状语从句：because it brings together...
>     ├── 宾语：great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy
>     └── 介短：in a way
>         └── 过去分词短语：matched by few other industries
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：但艺术品市场引发的关注远远超出其自身规模，因为它以一种几乎没有其他行业能够匹敌的方式，将巨额财富、极度膨胀的自我、贪婪、激情和争议汇聚在一起。
>
> **考点提示**：`matched by few other industries` 是过去分词短语，完整形式为 `which is matched by few other industries`，修饰 `a way`。

In the weeks and months that followed Mr. Hirst's sale, spending of any sort became deeply unfashionable.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In the weeks and months that followed Mr. Hirst's sale, spending of any sort became deeply unfashionable.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 状语 A: In the weeks and months that followed Mr. Hirst's sale
> - 主语 S: spending of any sort
> - 系动词 V: became
> - 表语 C: deeply unfashionable
>
> **主干简化**：Spending became unfashionable.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 内容 | 修饰对象 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 定语从句 | that followed Mr. Hirst's sale | the weeks and months |
> | 介词短语 | of any sort | spending |
> | 副词 | deeply | unfashionable |
> | 时间状语 | In the weeks and months... | became |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句：spending of any sort became deeply unfashionable
> ├── 时间状语：In the weeks and months...
> │   └── 定语从句：that followed Mr. Hirst's sale
> ├── 主语：spending
> │   └── 介短：of any sort
> ├── 系动词：became
> └── 表语：deeply unfashionable
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在赫斯特先生的拍卖之后的数周和数月里，任何形式的消费都变得极不时髦，也就是非常不受欢迎。
>
> **考点提示**：`spending` 是动名词，作主语；`that followed Mr. Hirst's sale` 是定语从句；`become + 形容词` 表示“变得……”；`of any sort` 表示“任何种类的”。

In the art world that meant collectors stayed away from galleries and salerooms. Sales of contemporary art fell by two-thirds, and in the most **overheated** sector, they were down by nearly 90% in the year to November 2008.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Sales of contemporary art fell by two-thirds, and in the most overheated sector, they were down by nearly 90% in the year to November 2008.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S1: Sales of contemporary art
> - 谓语 V1: fell
> - 状语 A1: by two-thirds
> - 主语 S2: they
> - 谓语 V2: were down
> - 状语 A2: in the most overheated sector; by nearly 90%; in the year to November 2008
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 内容 | 修饰对象 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 介词短语 | of contemporary art | Sales |
> | 并列连词 | and | 连接两个分句 |
> | 介词短语 | in the most overheated sector | 第二分句 |
> | 时间状语 | in the year to November 2008 | were down |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 并列句
> ├── 分句1：Sales of contemporary art fell by two-thirds
> └── 分句2：they were down by nearly 90%
>     ├── 介短：in the most overheated sector
>     └── 时间状语：in the year to November 2008
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：当代艺术的销售额下降了三分之二；在最为过热的板块，截至2008年11月的一年里，销售额下降了近90%。
>
> **考点提示**：`they` 指代前面的 `Sales`；`fall by` 和 `be down by` 都表示“下降了……幅度”。

Within weeks the world's two biggest auction houses, Sotheby's and Christie's, had to pay out nearly $200m in guarantees to clients who had placed works for sale with them.

The current downturn in the art market is the worst since the Japanese stopped buying Impressionists at the end of 1989. This time experts reckon that prices are about 40% down on their peak on average, though some have been far more **fluctuant**. But Edward Dolman, Christie's chief executive, says: “I'm pretty confident we're at the bottom.”

What makes this slump different from the last, he says, is that there are still buyers in the market. Almost everyone who was interviewed for this special report said that the biggest problem at the moment is not a lack of demand but a lack of good work to sell.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Almost everyone who was interviewed for this special report said that the biggest problem at the moment is not a lack of demand but a lack of good work to sell.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Almost everyone
> - 谓语 V: said
> - 宾语 O: that the biggest problem at the moment is not a lack of demand but a lack of good work to sell
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 内容 | 修饰对象 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 定语从句 | who was interviewed for this special report | everyone |
> | 名词性从句 | that the biggest problem ... is not ... but ... | said |
> | 介词短语 | at the moment | problem |
> | 并列结构 | not a lack of demand but a lack of good work | is 的表语 |
> | 不定式短语 | to sell | good work |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句：Almost everyone said...
> ├── 定语从句：who was interviewed for this special report
> └── 宾语从句：the biggest problem is not A but B
>     ├── 状语：at the moment
>     ├── 表语 A：a lack of demand
>     └── 表语 B：a lack of good work to sell
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：几乎所有接受这份特别报道采访的人都说，目前最大的问题不是需求不足，而是缺少适合出售的优秀作品。
>
> **考点提示**：主句后接 `that` 宾语从句；`not...but...` 表示“不是……而是……”；`to sell` 修饰 `good work`，表示“供出售的作品”。

The three Ds—death, debt and divorce—still deliver works of art to the market. But anyone who does not have to sell is keeping away, waiting for confidence to return.

## 翻译对照

The longest bull run in a century of art-market history ended on a dramatic note with a sale of 56 works by Damien Hirst, *Beautiful Inside My Head Forever*, at Sotheby's in London on September 15th 2008. All but two pieces sold, fetching more than £70m, a record for a sale by a single artist. It was a last victory. As the auctioneer called out bids, in New York one of the oldest banks on Wall Street, Lehman Brothers, filed for bankruptcy.

一个世纪以来艺术品市场历史上最长的牛市行情，在2008年9月15日于伦敦苏富比拍卖行举行的达明安·赫斯特作品《永远美丽地存在于我脑海中》（*Beautiful Inside My Head Forever*）拍卖会上，以一种戏剧性的方式落幕。除两件作品外，其余全部售出，成交额超过7000万英镑，创下单个艺术家作品拍卖的纪录。这是最后的胜利。拍卖师喊出竞价时，在纽约，华尔街最古老的银行之一雷曼兄弟申请了破产。

The world art market had already been losing momentum for a while after rising bewilderingly since 2003. At its peak in 2007 it was worth some $65 billion, reckons Clare McAndrew, founder of Arts Economics, a research firm—double the figure five years earlier. Since then it may have come down to $50 billion. But the market generates interest far beyond its size because it brings together great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy in a way matched by few other industries.

自2003年以来令人难以置信地上涨之后，世界艺术品市场已经有一段时间开始失去动力。艺术经济学公司（Arts Economics）的创始人、研究公司负责人克莱尔·麦克安德鲁估计，市场在2007年达到顶峰时价值约650亿美元，是五年前数字的两倍。此后，市场规模或许已经降至500亿美元。但艺术品市场引发的关注远远超出其自身规模，因为它以其他少数行业难以匹敌的方式，将巨额财富、极度膨胀的自我、贪婪、激情和争议汇聚在一起。

In the weeks and months that followed Mr. Hirst's sale, spending of any sort became deeply unfashionable. In the art world that meant collectors stayed away from galleries and salerooms. Sales of contemporary art fell by two-thirds, and in the most overheated sector, they were down by nearly 90% in the year to November 2008. Within weeks the world's two biggest auction houses, Sotheby's and Christie's, had to pay out nearly $200m in guarantees to clients who had placed works for sale with them.

在赫斯特先生作品拍卖后的数周和数月里，任何形式的消费都变得极不时髦。在艺术界，这意味着收藏家避开画廊和拍卖厅。当代艺术的销售额下降了三分之二；在最为过热的板块，截至2008年11月的一年里，销售额下降了近90%。几周之内，世界上最大的两家拍卖行——苏富比和佳士得——不得不向委托它们代售作品的客户支付近2亿美元的保证金。

The current downturn in the art market is the worst since the Japanese stopped buying Impressionists at the end of 1989. This time experts reckon that prices are about 40% down on their peak on average, though some have been far more fluctuant. But Edward Dolman, Christie's chief executive, says: “I'm pretty confident we're at the bottom.”

当前的艺术品市场低迷是自1989年底日本人停止购买印象派作品以来最严重的一次。这一次，专家估计，平均而言，艺术品价格比顶峰时下降了约40%，尽管有些艺术品的价格波动要大得多。但佳士得首席执行官爱德华·多尔曼说：“我相当有信心，我们已经触底了。”

What makes this slump different from the last, he says, is that there are still buyers in the market. Almost everyone who was interviewed for this special report said that the biggest problem at the moment is not a lack of demand but a lack of good work to sell. The three Ds—death, debt and divorce—still deliver works of art to the market. But anyone who does not have to sell is keeping away, waiting for confidence to return.

他说，这次低迷与上一次不同之处在于，市场中仍然有买家。几乎所有接受这份特别报道采访的人都说，目前最大的问题不是需求不足，而是缺少适合出售的优秀作品。“三个D”——死亡（death）、债务（debt）和离婚（divorce）——仍然会促使艺术品进入市场。但凡没有出售必要的人都暂时离开了市场，等待信心恢复。

## 语法要点

### 介词与连词：but 短语四兄弟

**All but —— 双重身份（极常考！）**

> [!tip] 核心判断
> **all but** 是最特殊的成员，根据后面接的词性不同，意思完全相反：接名词或数词时表示“除了……都……”；接形容词、副词或动词时表示“几乎、差不多”。

| 接法 | 含义 | 等同于 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 接**名词 / 数词** | 除了……都……（部分否定） | except | All but **two books** are good.（除了两本书之外，其余全都是好书。） |
| 接**形容词 / 副词 / 动词** | 几乎 / 差不多 | almost / nearly | The game **was all but over**.（比赛几乎结束了。） |

**两种用法的对比：**

| 用法 | 关键词 | 翻译 |
|------|------|------|
| **A：接名词** | 接具体事物 | “除了……都……” |
| **B：接形容词** | 接抽象状态 | “几乎 / 差不多” |

> [!note] 记忆技巧
> **all but** 接形容词是 **almost**，接名词是“除了都”。

**Everything but —— 除了……之外的一切**

**核心语义：** `everything except...`（范围最大，只留一个例外）

**例句：**

- He will eat **everything but** tomatoes.（除了西红柿，他什么都吃。）
- She talked about **everything but** the main problem.（她谈天说地，唯独绝口不提核心问题。）

**Anything but —— 根本不 / 绝不**

**逻辑推理：**“可以是世界上任何东西，但绝不能是这个”→ 强烈的**绝对否定**。

| 例句 | 翻译 |
|------|------|
| He is **anything but** honest. | 他根本不诚实。 |
| The hotel room **was anything but** comfortable. | 那家旅馆房间一点也不舒服。 |

**Nothing but —— 只有 / 仅仅**

**逻辑推理：**“除了这个，什么都没有”→ 强烈的**排他肯定**。

| 例句 | 翻译 |
|------|------|
| Don't listen to him; it's **nothing but** a rumor. | 别听他的，那只不过是谣言。 |
| He does **nothing but** play video games all day. | 他整天只会打游戏。 |

**四大短语核心速查表**

| 短语 | 核心等义词 | 中文精要 | 逻辑记忆 |
|------|----------|---------|---------|
| **all but** | almost / except | 几乎 / 除了都…… | 接形容词是 almost；接名词是 except |
| **everything but** | all except | 除了……全都…… | 范围最大，留一个例外 |
| **anything but** | NOT | 绝不、根本不 | “任何东西都行，唯独不是它”→ 否定 |
| **nothing but** | ONLY | 仅仅、只有 | “除了它什么都没有”→ 只/仅仅 |

> [!tip] 记忆口诀
> “all but 双面孔（except+almost），  
> everything 留一个例外空；  
> anything 任何东西都不行，nothing 只剩这一个通！”

> [!note] 一句话总结
> “all but 看词性，everything 全包含，anything 必否定，nothing 仅肯定。”

> [!note] 联动复习
> **all but** 要先判断后面词性；**anything but** 与 **nothing but** 都具有排他性，但前者表示强烈否定，后者表示强烈肯定。

### 固定搭配：file for

**核心语义：** 向官方或法院正式提出某种法律程序或资格的申请（**file** = 提交归档）。

**黄金搭配**

| 搭配 | 含义 |
|------|------|
| **file for bankruptcy** | 申请破产 ⭐ |
| **file for divorce** | 申请离婚 |
| **file for unemployment benefits** | 申请失业救济金 |
| **file for a patent** | 申请专利 |

**搭配辨析：带不带 for**

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **file for + 状态/权益** | 申请某种资格 | file **for** bankruptcy（申请破产） |
| **file + 诉讼/文件** | 提交具体文书 | file **a lawsuit** against sb（起诉某人）<br>file **a complaint**（提交投诉） |

> [!warning] 易混点
> **file for** 后接要申请的状态或权益；直接用 **file + 名词** 时，名词通常是要提交的具体诉讼或文件。

> [!note] 联动复习
> 文章中的 **file for bankruptcy** 与下方 **Bankrupt / bankruptcy** 词族互相对应：前者强调“提出申请”的搭配，后者强调“破产”这一状态。

### 补充要点：词根 -rupt（break / burst）

**词根含义：** break / burst（破裂、打碎）。

> [!tip] 记忆秘诀
> 记住前缀方向，就能秒记所有派生词。

**五大派生词全解**

| 单词 | 前缀拆解 | 画面本义 | 词性 | 高频搭配 |
|------|---------|---------|------|---------|
| **Bankrupt** | bank（钱庄柜台）+ rupt（砸烂） | 柜台被砸烂 → 破产 | adj./v. | go bankrupt（破产）<br>declare / **file for** bankruptcy（申请破产） |
| **Corrupt** | cor-（彻底）+ rupt（败坏） | 道德与制度彻底崩坏 → 腐败 | adj./v. | corrupt officials（腐败官员）<br>corruption（贪污腐败） |
| **Disrupt** | dis-（打散）+ rupt（打碎） | 把秩序彻底打碎打乱 → 扰乱 | v. | disrupt traffic / class（扰乱交通/课堂）<br>**disruptive** technology（颠覆性技术）⭐ 阅读常客 |
| **Interrupt** | inter-（在……之间）+ rupt（插断） | 在两者中间插一刀 → 打断 | v. | Sorry to interrupt, but...（抱歉打断一下）<br>interruption（打扰/中断） |
| **Erupt** | e-（向外）+ rupt（喷出） | 熔岩/情绪向外冲破 → 喷发 | v. | The volcano **erupted**（火山喷发）<br>Violence **erupted**（暴力爆发） |

**词根逻辑速查图**

```text
          -rupt（破裂）
              │
   ┌──────────┼──────────┐
bank         cor-       dis-        inter-        e- (ex-)
（柜台）     （彻底）   （打散）     （之间）       （向外）
   │           │          │            │             │
Bankrupt    Corrupt    Disrupt     Interrupt      Erupt
  破产        腐败       扰乱         打断          喷发
```

> [!tip] 记忆口诀
> “bank 柜台砸烂破产，cor 彻底败坏贪腐；  
> dis 打散扰乱秩序，inter 中间插话打断；  
> e-向外冲破喷发，五兄弟同根 -rupt！”

### 补充要点：困惑类近义词辨析

**四词核心含义对照**

| 单词 | 词性 | 核心含义 |
|------|------|---------|
| **confuse** | v. | 使困惑；使混乱；混淆 |
| **perplex** | v. | 使困惑；使费解；使复杂化 |
| **puzzle** | v./n. | 使迷惑；使苦思；谜、难题 |
| **bewildering** | adj. | 令人困惑的；令人不知所措的；令人眼花缭乱的 |

**四大近义词侧重点对比**

| 单词 | 侧重点 | 典型场景 |
|------|--------|---------|
| **confuse** | 思维混乱 / 混淆 | 事物不够清晰；把两者搞混（搞混两份文件、路标不清迷路） |
| **perplex** | 复杂难解 | 问题错综复杂、难以理解（复杂的数学难题、费解的行为动机） |
| **puzzle** | 苦思解谜 | 像谜团一样引起好奇反复琢磨（离奇失踪案、悬疑剧情） |
| **bewildering** | 不知所措 / 发懵 | 情况过于突发 / 信息过载而彻底失去头绪（初到大都市面对复杂交通） |

**程度梯度图**

```text
困惑程度：轻 ────────────────────────────────► 重

confuse  →  perplex  →  puzzle  →  bewildering
（混淆）    （费解）    （解谜）    （懵乱）
```

| 等级 | 词汇 | 状态描述 |
|------|------|---------|
| ⭐⭐ | confuse | 思路有点乱，分不清 |
| ⭐⭐⭐ | perplex | 问题复杂，搞不懂 |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | puzzle | 像谜一样，想不通 |
| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | bewildering | 彻底懵了，无所适从 |

**典型例句对比**

| 例句 | 翻译 |
|------|------|
| The directions **confused** me.（路标把我搞糊涂了。） | 简单混淆 |
| The math problem **perplexed** the students.（数学题让学生困惑不解。） | 复杂难解 |
| The detective story **puzzled** me for hours.（侦探小说让我苦思几小时。） | 像谜一样 |
| The **bewildering** array of options overwhelmed me.（眼花缭乱的选项让我不知所措。） | 信息过载 |

> [!warning] 形容词辨析
> **bewildering** 是“令人困惑的”，修饰事物；**bewildered** 是“感到困惑的”，修饰人。

**写作高分升级建议**

| 普通表达 | 升级表达 |
|---------|---------|
| very confusing | **bewildering** |
| makes me confused | **puzzles** me / **perplexes** me |
| I'm confused | I'm **bewildered** / **perplexed** |

> [!note] 一句话总结
> “but 短语看词性，-rupt 前缀定方向，困惑四词按程度挑——由轻到重：confuse / perplex / puzzle / bewilder。”

### 生词练习

**一、选词填空**

从方框中选择合适的词汇填入空白处（每词限用一次）：

> bull run / downturn / overheated / fluctuant / bankruptcy / disruptive / perplexed / bewildering / momentum

1. The art market's long ________ ended just before the financial crisis.

2. The global economic ________ made investors more cautious.

3. Prices in the most ________ sector fell sharply.

4. The value of the artwork has been highly ________ this year.

5. Lehman Brothers filed for ________ in 2008.

6. The new technology is highly ________ and may change the industry.

7. The complicated explanation left the students ________.

8. The ________ number of choices made it difficult to decide.

9. The market began to lose ________ after several years of rapid growth.

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **bull run**（牛市行情）
> 2. **downturn**（衰退；下滑）
> 3. **overheated**（过热的）
> 4. **fluctuant**（波动的）
> 5. **bankruptcy**（破产）
> 6. **disruptive**（颠覆性的）
> 7. **perplexed**（困惑不解的）
> 8. **bewildering**（令人眼花缭乱的）
> 9. **momentum**（动力；发展势头）

**二、短语翻译**

将下列短语翻译成中文：

1. all but two pieces

2. file for bankruptcy

3. waiting for confidence to return

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **all but two pieces** = 除两件作品之外的全部作品
> 2. **file for bankruptcy** = 申请破产
> 3. **waiting for confidence to return** = 等待信心恢复

**三、语境理解**

根据上下文，选择正确的词义：

1. “**All but** two pieces sold”中的 **all but** 是什么意思？
   - A. 几乎所有作品都卖出了
   - B. 除两件作品之外，其余都卖出了
   - C. 只有两件作品卖出了
   - D. 所有作品都没有卖出

2. “in the most **overheated** sector”中的 **overheated** 是什么意思？
   - A. 过度活跃、过热的
   - B. 没有买家的
   - C. 已经停止交易的
   - D. 难以理解的

3. “some have been far more **fluctuant**”中的 **fluctuant** 是什么意思？
   - A. 稳定不变的
   - B. 波动更大的
   - C. 迅速上涨的
   - D. 完全停止的

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **B** — `all but + 名词` 表示“除了……之外都……”，所以是除两件外全部售出。
> 2. **A** — `overheated` 在此处表示艺术市场中的某个板块过度活跃、价格虚高或过热。
> 3. **B** — `fluctuant` 表示“波动的、变化不定的”。


## 心得

> [!tip] 阅读技巧
> 文章按照“市场繁荣—危机冲击—价格下跌—供需变化”的顺序展开。阅读经济类文章时，可优先抓住表示趋势和转折的词，如 **bull run**、**downturn**、**fall**、**bottom**，快速建立文章的时间线和态度走向。

> `spending of any sort became deeply unfashionable` 并不是说“消费这种时尚过时了”，而是说在金融危机影响下，各种消费行为都变得不受欢迎。`all but` 也要根据后面的词性判断含义：接名词时表示“除……之外都……”，接形容词时表示“几乎”。
