# Will the European Union make it? 精读笔记

## 背景

本文讨论欧盟及其单一货币欧元区面临的结构性危机，重点比较德国与法国对欧元区治理方式的不同主张。文章一方面指出债务、人口下降和经济增长放缓等问题，另一方面强调欧盟单一市场的开放性及其缓和全球化冲击的积极意义。

> [!note] 文章信息
> - **主题**：欧盟治理、欧元区危机与欧洲一体化
> - **难度**：中高级；长句集中使用宾语从句、定语从句、非谓语和介词结构
> - **作者态度**：审慎但并不绝望，结尾整体偏向 **hopeful**

---

## 文章原文

# Will the European Union make it?

The question would have sounded strange not long ago.

Now even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk of a continent facing a **“Bermuda triangle”** of debt, population decline and lower growth.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Now even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk of a continent facing a “Bermuda triangle” of debt, population decline and lower growth.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: even the project's greatest cheerleaders
> - 谓语 V: talk
> - 介词结构：talk of + 介词宾语，表示“谈论……”
> - 介词宾语 O（of 的宾语）: a continent facing a “Bermuda triangle”
> - 状语 A: Now
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | even | 强调副词 | even | the project's greatest cheerleaders，突出“连……都” |
> | of a continent... | 介词短语 | of | talk，构成 talk of，表示“谈论……” |
> | facing a “Bermuda triangle”... | 非谓语（现在分词短语） | facing | a continent，表示“欧洲大陆正面临” |
> | of debt, population decline and lower growth | 介词短语 | of | a “Bermuda triangle”，具体说明“三角困境”由什么构成 |
> | debt, population decline and lower growth | 并列名词短语 | and | of 的三个并列宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 状语: Now
> 主句: even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk
>   └── 介词结构: of a continent → 构成 talk of
>         └── 介词宾语: a continent
>               └── 非谓语: facing a “Bermuda triangle” → 修饰 a continent
>                     └── 介短: of debt, population decline and lower growth
>                           → 具体说明 “Bermuda triangle” 的三个组成部分
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如今，甚至连这个项目最热心的支持者也开始谈论：欧洲大陆正面临一个由债务、人口下降和经济增长放缓构成的“百慕大三角”。
>
> **考点提示**：`talk of` 是“谈论、提及”的固定搭配；`facing` 是现在分词短语作后置定语，逻辑主语是 `a continent`，不是主句主语 `cheerleaders`；`even` 常译为“甚至、连……都”，用于突出出乎意料的对象。

As well as those **chronic problems**, the EU faces an **acute crisis** in its economic core, the 16 countries that use the single currency.

Markets have lost faith that the euro zone's economies, weaker or stronger, will one day **converge** thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of **devaluation**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Markets have lost faith that the euro zone's economies, weaker or stronger, will one day converge thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of devaluation.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Markets
> - 谓语 V: have lost
> - 宾语 O: faith
> - 宾语从句内容: that the euro zone's economies will one day converge
> - 状语 A: thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | that the euro zone's economies...converge | 名词性从句（同位语从句） | that | faith，说明“信念”的具体内容 |
> | weaker or stronger | 插入语（形容词短语） | — | economies，表示“无论强弱” |
> | thanks to the discipline...currency | 介词短语 | thanks to | converge，表示原因或依据 |
> | sharing a single currency | 非谓语（动名词） | sharing | discipline，说明这种约束来自何处 |
> | which denies...devaluation | 定语从句 | which | single currency，说明单一货币带来的限制 |
> | of devaluation | 介词短语 | of | the quick fix，说明“快捷办法”具体是什么 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Markets have lost faith
>   └── 名词性从句: that the euro zone's economies will one day converge
>         ├── 插入语: weaker or stronger → 修饰 economies
>         ├── 状语: thanks to the discipline
>         │     └── 非谓语: sharing a single currency → 修饰 discipline
>         └── 定语从句: which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix
>               └── 介短: of devaluation → 修饰 the quick fix
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：市场已经不再相信，欧元区的经济体无论强弱，终有一天能够凭借共同使用单一货币所带来的约束而趋于一致；而单一货币使缺乏竞争力的成员无法通过货币贬值这一快捷方式来解决问题。
>
> **考点提示**：`faith that...` 中的 that 从句是名词性同位语从句，具体说明 faith 的内容；`weaker or stronger` 是插入成分，阅读时可先跳过；末尾 `which` 引导非限制性定语从句，指向 `single currency`。

Yet the debate about how to save Europe's single currency from **disintegration** is **stuck**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Yet the debate about how to save Europe's single currency from disintegration is stuck.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: the debate
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: stuck
> - 定语：about how to save Europe's single currency from disintegration（修饰 the debate）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | about how to save...disintegration | 介词短语 | about | the debate |
> | how to save...disintegration | “疑问词 + 不定式”结构 | how + to | about 的宾语，说明讨论的具体内容 |
> | Europe's single currency | 介词短语中的名词短语 | Europe's | save 的宾语 |
> | from disintegration | 介词短语 | from | save，表示避免的结果 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the debate is stuck
>   └── 介短: about how to save Europe's single currency from disintegration
>         └── 疑问词 + 不定式: how to save...
>               ├── 宾语: Europe's single currency
>               └── 介短: from disintegration → 表示避免的结果
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：然而，关于如何拯救欧洲单一货币、避免其解体的讨论陷入了僵局。
>
> **考点提示**：`how to save...` 是“疑问词 + 不定式”结构，整体作介词 `about` 的宾语；主干是 `the debate is stuck`，其中 `stuck` 是形容词补语，表示“陷入僵局”。

It is stuck because the euro zone's dominant powers, France and Germany, agree on the need for greater **harmonisation** within the euro zone, but disagree about what to harmonise.

Germany thinks the euro must be saved by **stricter rules** on borrowing, spending and competitiveness, **backed** by quasi-automatic sanctions for governments that do not obey.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Germany thinks the euro must be saved by stricter rules on borrowing, spending and competitiveness, backed by quasi-automatic sanctions for governments that do not obey.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Germany
> - 谓语 V: thinks
> - 宾语 O: the euro must be saved by stricter rules
> - 被动结构内部主语 S: the euro
> - 被动结构谓语 V: must be saved
> - 状语 A: by stricter rules on borrowing, spending and competitiveness
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | the euro must be saved... | 名词性从句（宾语从句） | 省略 that | thinks 的宾语 |
> | must be saved | 情态动词 + 被动语态 | must + be + V-ed | euro，表示“必须被拯救” |
> | on borrowing, spending and competitiveness | 介词短语 | on | rules，说明规则涉及的领域 |
> | backed by quasi-automatic sanctions... | 过去分词短语 | backed | stricter rules，表示规则由制裁作保障 |
> | for governments that do not obey | 介词短语 + 定语从句 | for / that | sanctions / governments |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Germany thinks [宾语从句]
>   └── 宾语从句: the euro must be saved by stricter rules
>         ├── 被动谓语: must be saved
>         ├── 介短: on borrowing, spending and competitiveness → 修饰 rules
>         └── 过去分词短语: backed by quasi-automatic sanctions
>               └── 定语从句: that do not obey → 修饰 governments
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：德国认为，必须通过对借贷、支出和竞争力实行更严格的规则来拯救欧元，并以近乎自动的制裁作为保障，惩罚那些不遵守规则的政府。
>
> **考点提示**：`thinks` 后接省略 `that` 的宾语从句；`must be saved` 是情态动词与被动语态叠加；句末 `backed...` 为过去分词短语，逻辑上修饰 `stricter rules`，不要误判为修饰最近的 `competitiveness`。

These might include threats to freeze EU funds for poorer regions and EU mega-projects, and even the suspension of a country's voting rights in EU ministerial councils.

It insists that economic co-ordination should involve all 27 members of the EU club, among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism and economic rigour; in the inner core alone, Germany fears, a small majority favour French interference.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It insists that economic co-ordination should involve all 27 members of the EU club, among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism and economic rigour; in the inner core alone, Germany fears, a small majority favour French interference.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: It（指上文的 Germany）
> - 谓语 V: insists
> - 宾语 O: that economic co-ordination should involve all 27 members of the EU club（that 引导的宾语从句）
> - 宾语从句主语 S: economic co-ordination
> - 宾语从句谓语 V: should involve
> - 宾语从句宾语 O: all 27 members of the EU club
> - 非限制性定语从句结构: among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism and economic rigour（其中 `there` 为引导存在句的形式主语，`a small majority` 为后置的实际主语）
> - 分号后并列分句状语 A: in the inner core alone
> - 分号后并列分句插入语: Germany fears
> - 分号后并列分句主语 S: a small majority
> - 分号后并列分句谓语 V: favour
> - 分号后并列分句宾语 O: French interference
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | that economic co-ordination should involve... | 名词性从句（宾语从句） | that | insists 的宾语 |
> | of the EU club | 介词短语 | of | members，说明这 27 名成员所属的组织 |
> | among whom there is...rigour | 非限制性定语从句 | among whom | all 27 members，说明这些成员中的立场分布 |
> | for free-market liberalism and economic rigour | 介词短语 | for | a small majority，说明该多数支持的立场 |
> | in the inner core alone | 介词短语（状语） | in | 分号后的并列分句，限定范围 |
> | Germany fears | 插入语 | — | 表示德国的担忧，不影响句子主干 |
> | French interference | 名词短语 | — | favour 的宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: It insists [宾语从句]
>   └── 宾语从句: economic co-ordination should involve all 27 members of the EU club
>         ├── 介短: of the EU club → 修饰 members
>         └── 非限制性定语从句: among whom there is a small majority...
>               ├── 介短: among whom → 表示“在这些成员中”
>               ├── 存在句: there is a small majority
>               │     └── 介短: for free-market liberalism and economic rigour → 修饰 majority
>               └── 修饰对象: all 27 members
> 分号后的并列分句: in the inner core alone, Germany fears, a small majority favour French interference
>   ├── 状语: in the inner core alone → 限定范围
>   ├── 插入语: Germany fears
>   └── 主干: a small majority favour French interference
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：它坚持认为，经济协调应当涉及欧盟这个“俱乐部”的全部27个成员；在这些成员中，支持自由市场自由主义和经济严谨政策的只占微弱多数。然而德国担心，仅在核心内部，赞成法国式干预的也占有微弱多数。
>
> **考点提示**：`that` 引导宾语从句；`among whom` 是介词 `among` 前置的非限制性定语从句，内部使用 `there is` 存在句；分号连接两个并列分句。后半句的 `Germany fears` 是插入语，去掉后可直接识别主干 `a small majority favour French interference`。注意 `favour` 在此表示“支持”，`for` 表示“支持某种立场”。

A **“southern” camp** headed by French wants something different: **“European economic government”** within an inner core of euro-zone members.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：A “southern” camp headed by French wants something different: “European economic government” within an inner core of euro-zone members.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: A “southern” camp
> - 谓语 V: wants
> - 宾语 O: something different
> - 后置说明: “European economic government” within an inner core of euro-zone members
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | headed by French | 过去分词短语 | headed | camp，说明阵营的领导者 |
> | “European economic government” | 同位语/解释性补语 | 冒号 | something different，具体说明“不同的方案” |
> | within an inner core of euro-zone members | 介词短语 | within | European economic government，说明实施范围 |
> | of euro-zone members | 介词短语 | of | inner core，说明核心成员构成 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: A “southern” camp wants something different
>   ├── 过去分词短语: headed by French → 修饰 camp
>   └── 冒号后的同位说明: “European economic government”
>         └── 介短: within an inner core of euro-zone members → 修饰 government
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：由法国领导的“南方阵营”则想要一种不同的方案：在欧元区成员组成的内部核心中建立“欧洲经济政府”。
>
> **考点提示**：`headed by French` 是过去分词短语作后置定语；冒号后并非新的完整句子，而是对 `something different` 的具体解释。原文中的 `French` 在语境中指法国方面或法国政府。

Translated, that means politicians intervening in **monetary** policy and a system of **redistribution** from richer to poorer members, via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds or complete fiscal transfers.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Translated, that means politicians intervening in monetary policy and a system of redistribution from richer to poorer members, via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds or complete fiscal transfers.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 状语 A: Translated（换言之）
> - 主语 S: that（指代前文的 “European economic government”）
> - 谓语 V: means
> - 宾语 O: politicians intervening in monetary policy and a system of redistribution
> - 第一并列成分: politicians intervening in monetary policy（名词短语，`intervening...` 后置修饰 `politicians`）
> - 第二并列成分: a system of redistribution from richer to poorer members
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | Translated | 过去分词短语（句首评注状语） | — | means 所在的整个句子，表示“换言之” |
> | politicians intervening in monetary policy | 名词短语 + 现在分词后置定语 | intervening | 与第二个并列成分共同作 means 的宾语；修饰 politicians |
> | in monetary policy | 介词短语 | in | intervening，说明干预的领域 |
> | a system of redistribution | 名词短语 | of | 与前一成分并列，共同作 means 的宾语 |
> | from richer to poorer members | 介词短语 | from...to... | redistribution，说明重新分配的方向 |
> | via cheaper borrowing for governments | 介词短语 | via | system of redistribution，说明再分配的一种实现方式 |
> | for governments | 介词短语 | for | borrowing，说明借贷的对象 |
> | through common Eurobonds | 介词短语 | through | cheaper borrowing for governments，说明获得低成本借贷的途径 |
> | or complete fiscal transfers | 省略结构 | or | 与 `via cheaper borrowing...` 并列，说明再分配的另一种实现方式 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 句首状语: Translated → “换言之”
> 主句: that means [并列宾语]
>   ├── 名词短语: politicians intervening in monetary policy
>   │     └── 现在分词后置定语: intervening in monetary policy → 修饰 politicians
>   └── 名词短语: a system of redistribution
>         ├── 介短: from richer to poorer members → 修饰 redistribution
>         └── 再分配方式（via... or...）
>               ├── via cheaper borrowing for governments
>               │     ├── 介短: for governments → 修饰 borrowing
>               │     └── 介短: through common Eurobonds → 修饰 cheaper borrowing
>               └── （via）complete fiscal transfers
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：换句话说，这意味着政治家干预货币政策，并建立一种把资源从较富裕成员重新分配给较贫困成员的制度，具体方式包括：通过共同发行欧元债券为各国政府提供成本更低的借贷，或实施完整的财政转移。
>
> **考点提示**：`Translated` 是句首过去分词构成的评注状语，语义上相当于 “put differently”，应译为“换句话说”；`politicians intervening...` 与 `a system...` 是 `means` 后的两个并列宾语，其中 `intervening...` 后置修饰 `politicians`；`from richer to poorer` 中省略了 `members`。注意 `via ... or ...` 表示再分配的两种方式，`complete fiscal transfers` 不应误解为获得借贷的途径。

Finally, figures close to the French government have **murmured**, euro-zone members should agree to some fiscal and social harmonisation: e.g., curbing competition in corporate-tax rates or labour costs.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Finally, figures close to the French government have murmured, euro-zone members should agree to some fiscal and social harmonisation: e.g., curbing competition in corporate-tax rates or labour costs.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 状语 A: Finally
> - 主语 S: figures close to the French government
> - 谓语 V: have murmured
> - 宾语 O: （省略 that 的宾语从句）euro-zone members should agree to some fiscal and social harmonisation
> - 宾语从句主语 S: euro-zone members
> - 宾语从句谓语 V: should agree
> - 宾语从句补足成分: to some fiscal and social harmonisation（agree to 的介词补足成分）
> - 举例说明: e.g., curbing competition in corporate-tax rates or labour costs（对 harmonisation 的具体化说明）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | close to the French government | 形容词短语 | close to | figures，说明这些人士与法国政府关系密切 |
> | euro-zone members should agree... | 名词性从句（宾语从句） | 省略 that | murmured 的宾语 |
> | to some fiscal and social harmonisation | 介词短语 | to | agree，说明赞成的内容 |
> | e.g., curbing competition... | 动名词短语（举例） | curbing | 对 fiscal and social harmonisation 的具体说明 |
> | in corporate-tax rates or labour costs | 介词短语 | in | competition，说明竞争所涉及的方面；in 的作用域覆盖 or 连接的两项 |
> | corporate-tax rates or labour costs | 并列名词短语 | or | in 的两个并列宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 状语: Finally
> 主句: figures close to the French government have murmured
>   ├── 形容词短语: close to the French government → 修饰 figures
>   └── 宾语从句（省略 that）: euro-zone members should agree to harmonisation
>         ├── 主语: euro-zone members
>         ├── 谓语: should agree
>         ├── 介词补足成分: to some fiscal and social harmonisation → agree 的补足成分
>         └── 举例说明: e.g., curbing competition in corporate-tax rates or labour costs
>               ├── 动名词短语: curbing competition → 具体说明 harmonisation
>               └── 介短: in corporate-tax rates or labour costs → 修饰 competition
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：最后，接近法国政府的人士低声表示，欧元区成员应该在财政和社会政策方面实现一定程度的协调统一，例如限制各国在企业税率或劳动力成本方面的竞争。
>
> **考点提示**：`murmured` 后接省略 `that` 的宾语从句；`close to` 在此为形容词 `close` 后接介词 `to` 的形容词短语；冒号后的 `e.g., curbing...` 是对 `fiscal and social harmonisation` 的举例说明，`curbing` 为动名词而非主句谓语；`in` 的作用域覆盖 `corporate-tax rates` 和 `labour costs` 两项。

It is too soon to write off the EU. It remains the world's largest trading block. At its best, the European project is remarkably liberal: built around a single market of 27 rich and poor countries, its internal borders are far more open to goods, capital and labour than any comparable trading area.

It is an ambitious attempt to **blunt the sharpest edges of globalization**, and make **capitalism** benign.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It is an ambitious attempt to blunt the sharpest edges of globalization, and make capitalism benign.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: It（指代 the European project）
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: an ambitious attempt
> - 后置定语：to blunt the sharpest edges of globalization and make capitalism benign（修饰 attempt，说明尝试的内容）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | to blunt...and make... | 非谓语（并列不定式） | to | attempt，说明尝试的目的或内容 |
> | of globalization | 介词短语 | of | edges，说明“棱角”属于全球化 |
> | capitalism benign | 省略 to 的不定式复合结构 | make + 宾语 + 补语 | make 的宾语和宾语补足语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: It is an ambitious attempt
>   └── 并列不定式: to blunt ... and make ...
>         ├── to blunt the sharpest edges of globalization
>         │     └── 介短: of globalization → 修饰 edges
>         └── (to) make capitalism benign
>               ├── 宾语 O: capitalism
>               └── 宾补 C: benign
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：这是一次雄心勃勃的尝试，旨在削弱全球化最尖锐的棱角，让资本主义变得更加温和。
>
> **考点提示**：`to blunt... and make...` 是两个并列的不定式，共用第一个 `to`；`make capitalism benign` 是典型的“make + 宾语 + 宾语补足语”结构，`benign` 不能译成“良性的”而应结合语境译为“温和的、对人有益的”。

## 翻译对照

# Will the European Union make it? 欧盟能撑过去吗？

The question would have sounded strange not long ago. Now even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk of a **“Bermuda triangle”** of debt, population decline and lower growth.

不久前，这个问题听起来还很奇怪。如今，即便是欧盟这一项目最热心的支持者，也开始谈论欧洲大陆所面临的由债务、人口下降和经济增长放缓构成的**“百慕大三角”**。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> **“Bermuda triangle”** 在这里是比喻，指三个相互交织、令欧盟陷入困境的长期问题：债务、人口下降和经济增长放缓。

As well as those **chronic problems**, the EU faces an **acute crisis** in its economic core, the 16 countries that use the single currency. Markets have lost faith that the euro zone's economies, weaker or stronger, will one day converge thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of **devaluation**.

除了这些**长期存在的问题**之外，欧盟的经济核心——使用单一货币的16个国家——还面临着一场**严重的危机**。市场已经不再相信，欧元区无论经济实力强弱，各国经济终有一天能够凭借共同使用单一货币所带来的约束而趋于一致；这种共同货币机制也使缺乏竞争力的成员无法通过**货币贬值**这一快捷方式来解决问题。

Yet the debate about how to save Europe's single currency from **disintegration** is stuck. It is stuck because the euro zone's dominant powers, France and Germany, agree on the need for greater **harmonisation** within the euro zone, but disagree about what to harmonise.

然而，关于如何拯救欧洲单一货币、避免其**解体**的讨论陷入了僵局。之所以陷入僵局，是因为欧元区的主导力量法国和德国都同意有必要在欧元区内部实现更大程度的**协调统一**，却对究竟应该统一什么意见不一。

Germany thinks the euro must be saved by **stricter rules** on borrowing, spending and competitiveness, backed by quasi-automatic sanctions for governments that do not obey. These might include threats to freeze EU funds for poorer regions and EU mega-projects, and even the suspension of a country's voting rights in EU ministerial councils. It insists that economic co-ordination should involve all 27 members of the EU club, among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism and economic rigour; in the inner core alone, Germany fears, a small majority favour French interference.

德国认为，要拯救欧元，就必须对借贷、支出和竞争力实行**更严格的规则**，并对不遵守规则的政府实施近乎自动的制裁。这些制裁可能包括威胁冻结对贫困地区的欧盟资金和欧盟大型项目资金，甚至暂停某个国家在欧盟部长理事会中的投票权。德国坚持认为，经济协调应该涉及欧盟俱乐部的全部27个成员；在这些成员中，支持自由市场自由主义和经济严谨性的国家占有微弱多数。德国担心，如果只看欧元区这一内部核心，支持法国式干预的国家反而占有微弱多数。

A **“southern” camp** headed by French wants something different: **“European economic government”** within an inner core of euro-zone members. Translated, that means politicians intervening in monetary policy and a system of **redistribution** from richer to poorer members, via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds or complete fiscal transfers. Finally, figures close to the French government have murmured, euro-zone members should agree to some fiscal and social harmonisation: e.g., curbing competition in corporate-tax rates or labour costs.

由法国领导的**“南方阵营”**则想要另一种方案：在欧元区成员组成的内部核心中建立**“欧洲经济政府”**。换句话说，这意味着由政治家干预货币政策，并通过共同发行的欧元债券为政府提供更低成本的借贷，或进行完整的财政转移，从而建立一种把财富从较富裕成员重新分配给较贫困成员的制度。最后，法国政府身边的人士还含蓄地表示，欧元区成员应该在财政和社会政策方面实现一定程度的协调统一，例如限制各国在企业税率或劳动力成本方面的竞争。

It is too soon to write off the EU. It remains the world's largest trading block. At its best, the European project is remarkably liberal: built around a single market of 27 rich and poor countries, its internal borders are far more open to goods, capital and labour than any comparable trading area. It is an ambitious attempt to **blunt the sharpest edges of globalization**, and make capitalism benign.

现在就认定欧盟无可救药还为时过早。它仍然是世界上最大的贸易集团。在其表现最佳时，欧洲一体化项目具有显著的自由主义色彩：它以一个由27个富裕国家和贫穷国家组成的单一市场为基础，其内部边界对商品、资本和劳动力的开放程度，远远超过任何类似的贸易区域。这是一次雄心勃勃的尝试，旨在**削弱全球化最尖锐的棱角**，让资本主义变得更温和、更有利于人类。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> **write off** 表示“认为……无望、放弃对……的希望”；**blunt the sharpest edges** 表示“削弱最尖锐的负面影响”。

## 语法要点

# 2011 Passage 4 语法要点整理

本篇源笔记主要整理 **talk** 的介词搭配、及物结构和固定短语。完整的语义辨析、语体差异、感情色彩及全部例句保存在 [[固定搭配与词组笔记]]；本笔记只提取可迁移的语法结构。

### 动词的及物与不及物：talk 的基本结构

**talk** 通常作不及物动词，后面需要借助介词引出谈论内容或交流对象；但在 `talk sb into/out of doing sth` 和 `talk business/sense/nonsense` 等结构中，可以构成特殊的及物搭配。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **talk + of/about + 名词** | 不及物动词 + 介词短语，引出谈论的内容 | They sat by the fire and **talked of** the past. |
| **talk + to/with + 人** | 不及物动词 + 介词短语，引出交流对象 | The manager is **talking with** the client. |
| **talk + 人 + into/out of + doing sth** | `talk` 后直接接人作宾语，再由介词短语补充劝说结果 | She **talked him into** buying the expensive car. |
| **talk + business/sense/nonsense** | `talk` 直接接特定名词，构成无介词及物短语 | **talk business** / **talk sense** / **talk nonsense** |

> [!tip] 识别技巧
> 看到 `talk` 后先判断后面是介词短语还是直接宾语：`talk of/about/to/with` 属于介词搭配；`talk sb into/out of...` 和 `talk business/sense/nonsense` 属于特殊及物结构。

### 介词与动词补语：talk + 介词

不同介词决定 `talk` 后面成分的语法角色：`of/about` 后接谈论内容，`to/with` 后接交流对象。具体的语义侧重点、语体和语感见 [[固定搭配与词组笔记]]。

| 结构 | 语法功能 | 介词后的成分 |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **talk of + 名词** | 引出被提及的内容 | 事物、话题或传闻 |
| **talk about + 名词** | 引出被讨论的具体内容 | 主题或项目 |
| **talk to + 人** | 引出单向或一般交流对象 | 人 |
| **talk with + 人** | 引出双方交谈对象 | 人 |

> [!note] 交叉复习
> `talk of/about` 都可引出谈论内容，但 `talk of` 偏“提及”，`talk about` 偏“讨论”；`talk to/with` 都可引出人，前者更强调讲话对象，后者更强调双向互动。完整辨析见 [[固定搭配与词组笔记]]。

### 非谓语动词：介词后接动名词

在 `talk sb into/out of doing sth` 中，`into` 和 `out of` 是介词，因此后面必须接动名词 `doing`，不能接动词原形或不定式。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **talk sb into doing sth** | 说服某人做某事；`into` 后接动名词 | She **talked him into** buying the expensive car. |
| **talk sb out of doing sth** | 劝阻某人做某事；`out of` 后接动名词 | I tried to **talk him out of** resigning. |

> [!warning] 常见错误
> 介词 `into / out of` 后面必须使用 **-ing** 形式：`talk him into buying`、`talk him out of resigning`，不能写成 `talk him into buy` 或 `talk him out of resign`。

### 副词小品词与固定短语

`talk` 还可以与副词小品词或名词构成固定短语。小品词改变整体意义，不能按 `talk` 的单一词义逐词硬译。

| 结构 | 语法类型 | 基本含义 |
|------|----------|----------|
| **talk back (to sb)** | 动词 + 副词小品词 + 介词短语 | 顶嘴、顶撞 |
| **talk down to sb** | 动词 + 副词小品词 + 介词短语 | 居高临下地对某人说话 |
| **the talk of the town** | 名词短语 | 街头巷尾的谈资、焦点话题 |

> [!note] 固定搭配笔记
> 完整的搭配家族、语体差异、经典例句和速记对比卡片见 [[固定搭配与词组笔记]]。

## 固定搭配与词组

### talk 短语家族核心辨析（of / about / to / with + 及物搭配）

> 📌 **本笔记背景：** 笔记指出 talk of（谈及 / 提及）这一偏正式的书面用法。在实际考试与日常表达中，talk 作为典型的不及物动词，其后接不同介词（about、of、to、with）在语体风格、交流深度与方向性上有细致区分；同时它在特殊句型中还具备及物用法。

相关语法结构整理见 [[grammar-notes]]。

---

#### 📘 第一部分：核心介词搭配辨析

| 搭配 | 核心语义与侧重 | 语体 / 语感 | 经典例句 |
|------|--------------|------------|---------|
| **talk of** | 谈及 / 提及 / 传言（轻描淡写地提起某事，或指社会上关于某事的传闻） | 偏正式、文学或书面语 | They sat by the fire and **talked of** the past.（他们坐在火旁谈及往事。）<br>There is **talk of** an election.（社会上有传闻说要举行选举。） |
| **talk about** | 讨论 / 谈论具体内容（就某个主题展开深入或详细的交流） | 通用、日常口语及书面均适用 | We spent hours **talking about** the new project.（我们花了几个小时讨论新项目。） |
| **talk to** | 对……讲话 / 与……谈话（强调交流对象；在严肃语境下可带"训话 / 单向指示"色彩） | 最常用表达（美式英语尤为普遍） | I need to **talk to** you about your performance.（我需要和你谈谈你的表现。） |
| **talk with** | 与……交谈 / 探讨（强调双方平等的双向互动与协商） | 强调双向交流 | The manager is **talking with** the client.（经理正在与客户协商 / 交谈。） |

---

#### 📘 第二部分：talk 的及物与固定搭配（高考、四六级顶级考点）

> 💡 talk 通常是不及物动词，但在以下两类结构中是绝对的高频考题。

##### 一、说服句型：talk sb into / out of doing sth（核心高频）

| 句型 | 含义 | 等同于 | 例句 |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **talk sb into doing sth** | 说服 / 劝服某人做某事 | = persuade sb to do | She **talked him into** buying the expensive car.（她说服了他买下那辆昂贵的车。） |
| **talk sb out of doing sth** | 劝阻某人不要做某事 | = persuade sb not to do | I tried to **talk him out of** resigning.（我试图劝阻他不要辞职。） |

> ⚠️ **避坑提示：** 介词 into / out of 后面跟动词必须使用**动名词 -ing** 形式。

##### 二、及物短语（无介词，直接接特定名词）

| 短语 | 翻译 |
|------|------|
| **talk business** | 谈正事 / 谈生意 |
| **talk sense** | 讲合乎情理的话 / 说正经话 |
| **talk nonsense** | 胡说八道 |

---

#### 📘 第三部分：常考高频动副短语

| 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **talk back (to sb)** | 顶嘴 / 顶撞 | Don't **talk back to** your teachers.（不要跟老师顶嘴。） |
| **talk down to sb** | 居高临下地同某人说话 / 带有优越感地说话 | Speak naturally and don't **talk down to** your audience.（自然发言，不要用居高临下的口吻对听众说话。） |
| **the talk of the town**（名词短语） | 街头巷尾的谈资 / 焦点话题 | Their engagement became **the talk of the town**.（他们的订婚成了满城热议的话题。） |

---

#### 🎯 速记对比卡片

> **深入讨论用 about，泛泛提及用 of；**
> **劝服做某事 talk sb into doing，劝阻不做 out of doing；**
> **双向协商用 with，切莫随意 talk back（顶嘴）！**

## 心得

### 1. 文章论证脉络

文章先提出欧盟面临的“债务—人口—增长”三重困境，再聚焦欧元区内部的制度危机，随后分别介绍德国的规则约束方案和法国主导的经济干预与再分配方案，最后回到欧盟项目本身的开放价值。因此，阅读时要抓住“问题提出 → 德法分歧 → 欧盟价值重估”的推进链条。

### 2. 解题技巧

> [!tip] 观点态度题
> 结尾的 `It is too soon to write off the EU` 和 `remarkably liberal` 直接限制了作者态度。虽然全文大量讨论危机，但作者并非悲观或绝望，而是承认问题严重的同时保留希望。

> [!tip] 细节题定位
> 第37—39题分别对应德法分歧、德国方案和法国方案。做题时先回到包含题干关键词的段落，再区分“原文明确陈述”和“选项过度推断”。

> [!warning] 长句阅读易错点
> 看到 `that`、`which`、`among whom` 等从句引导词时，先提取主句主干，再处理插入语、非谓语和介词短语。不要把 `which` 从句或 `Germany fears` 插入语误当作主句核心。

### 3. 词汇与表达迁移

- `write off`：认定……无望；放弃对……的希望
- `converge`：趋于一致、趋同
- `harmonisation`：协调统一
- `redistribution`：再分配
- `blunt the sharpest edges of...`：削弱……最尖锐的负面影响
- `talk of`：谈及、提及，语体上比 `talk about` 更偏正式

- **欧洲一体化**：可继续整理欧盟单一市场、欧元区和财政联盟之间的关系。
