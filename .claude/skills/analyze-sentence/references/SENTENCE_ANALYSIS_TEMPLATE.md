# AI 文章 长难句分析

---

### 句子 1

**原文**：
> While tech companies push forward with ever more sophisticated algorithms, governments struggle to establish meaningful frameworks for oversight.

**主干**：
- 主句：governments + struggle + to establish frameworks
- 状从（让步）：While + tech companies + push forward + with algorithms

**结构图解**：
```
While tech companies push forward [with ever more sophisticated algorithms],
  └── 介短: with algorithms → 伴随状语
主句: governments struggle [to establish meaningful frameworks for oversight]
  └── 非谓语: to establish frameworks → 目的状语
        └── 介短: for oversight → 修饰 frameworks
```

**修饰成分**：
| 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
|------|------|--------|---------|
| While tech companies...algorithms | 状从（让步） | While | 主句整体 |
| to establish...oversight | 非谓语（不定式） | to | struggle 的补语 |
| for oversight | 介短 | for | frameworks |

**参考译文**：
当科技公司不断推出越来越精密的算法时，各国政府却难以及时建立有效的监管框架。

> [!tip] 考点提示
> **While 的双重含义**：此处 While 引导让步状语从句（= Although），而非时间状语。考研真题中 While 的让步用法是高频考点，常设"推断作者态度"类题目。识别方法：While 在句首 + 主从句存在对立逻辑 = 让步。

### 句子 2

**原文**：
> The primary concern among policymakers, who have struggled to keep pace with rapid technological advances, is the disparity between the speed of innovation and the capacity of regulatory bodies to respond effectively.

**主干**：
- 主句：The primary concern + is + the disparity
- 定从：who + have struggled + to keep pace

**结构图解**：
```
The primary concern [among policymakers] [定从...] is the disparity
  ├── 介短: among policymakers → 修饰 concern
  ├── 定从: (who have struggled to keep pace [with rapid technological advances]) → 修饰 policymakers
  │     └── 介短: with...advances → 修饰 keep pace
  └── 介短: [between the speed of innovation and the capacity [of regulatory bodies] [to respond effectively]] → 修饰 disparity
        ├── 介短: of regulatory bodies → 修饰 capacity
        └── 非谓语: to respond effectively → 修饰 capacity
```

**修饰成分**：
| 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
|------|------|--------|---------|
| among policymakers | 介短 | among | concern |
| who have struggled...advances | 定从 | who | policymakers |
| between the speed...effectively | 介短 | between | disparity |
| to respond effectively | 非谓语（不定式） | to | capacity |

**参考译文**：
那些难以跟上技术快速发展步伐的政策制定者们，他们主要担忧的是创新速度与监管机构有效响应能力之间的差距。

> [!tip] 考点提示
> **分隔修饰 (Discontinuous Modification)**：主句主语 `The primary concern` 和系动词 `is` 之间被 `among policymakers` 和 `who...` 定语从句分隔。这是考研长难句的经典模式——在阅读时要跳过分隔成分，先抓住主谓宾主干 `concern is disparity`。
