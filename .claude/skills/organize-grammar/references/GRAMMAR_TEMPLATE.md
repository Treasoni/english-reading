# AI 文章 语法要点整理

---

### 非谓语动词 (Non-finite Verbs)

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 非谓语动词在考研英语中高频出现。找准逻辑主语，判断主被动关系，是解题关键。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **V-ing** (现在分词) | 主动/正在进行 | AI systems **adapting** to new data show improved performance. |
| **V-ed** (过去分词) | 被动/已完成 | The regulations, **designed** by policymakers, aim to protect privacy. |
| **to V** (不定式) | 目的/将来 | Tech companies move fast **to capture** market share. |

> [!warning] 常见错误
> **混淆主被动关系**：`The problems facing by companies...` ❌ → `The problems facing companies...` ✅（face 在此为主动含义"面对"）

---

### 定语从句 (Attributive Clauses)

> [!note]
> 本篇文章中多次出现 which/that 引导的定语从句，用于修饰先行词，增加信息密度——这是考研英语长难句的典型特征。

| 类型 | 引导词 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 限制性 | that / which | algorithms **that** can detect diseases |
| 非限制性 | , which | AI systems, **which** are increasingly integrated, raise questions. |

---

### 名词性从句 (Noun Clauses)

> [!tip]
> 识别名词性从句在长难句中的位置，是快速抓住主干的关键。

| 类型 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| 宾语从句 | Experts believe **that AI will transform healthcare**. |
| 表语从句 | The concern is **whether regulation can keep pace**. |
| 同位语从句 | The question **whether AI should be regulated** remains open. |

---

### 被动语态 (Passive Voice)

| 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| be + V-ed | AI systems **are increasingly integrated** into our daily lives. |
| can be + V-ed | The benefits **can be maximized** through careful oversight. |

> [!warning] 翻译注意
> 英文被动语态在中文翻译时通常转为主动表达，如 "are integrated" → "融入" 而非 "被融入"。
