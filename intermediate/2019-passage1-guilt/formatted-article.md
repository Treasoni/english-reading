# 2019 Passage 1

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger, **guilt** **emerges** a little later, **in conjunction with** a child's growing **grasp** of social and moral **norms**.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 比较状语 A（介词短语）: Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger
> - 主语 S: guilt
> - 谓语 V: emerges
> - 时间状语 A: a little later
> - 伴随状语 A（介词短语）: in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语作比较状语 | Unlike … | 主句 guilt emerges |
> | 举例同位补充 | such as sadness, fear, and anger | basic emotions |
> | 时间状语 | a little later | emerges |
> | 介词短语作伴随状语 | in conjunction with … | emerges |
> | 名词化结构 | a child's growing grasp of … | 介词宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Unlike so-called basic emotions (such as …)〕+ guilt + emerges + a little later, 〔in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms〕

> ```
