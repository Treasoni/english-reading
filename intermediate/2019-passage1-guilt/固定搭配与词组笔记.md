# 2019 Passage 1 固定搭配与词组

> [!note] 来源说明
> 本篇**用户未提供词组源笔记**，以下词组由 `formatted-article.md` 与 `translation.md` 提取，收录本篇实际出现的**固定搭配、动词短语与重点表达**。
> 纯语法现象见 [[intermediate/2019-passage1-guilt/grammar-notes.md|2019 Passage 1 语法整理]]。

---

## 一、情绪与道德主题词

| 词组 | 中文 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| so-called basic emotions | 所谓的基本情绪 | Unlike **so-called basic emotions** such as sadness, fear, and anger … |
| social and moral norms | 社会与道德规范 | … in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of **social and moral norms**. |
| moral guilt | 道德内疚 | … researchers generally regard so-called **moral guilt**, in the right amount, to be a good thing. |
| sympathy | 同情、同情心 | … guilt and **sympathy** may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing. |
| conscience | 良心、良知 | … such statements appease parents and friends—and their own **consciences**. |
| a cooperative species | 合作的物种（指人类） | Guilt, in other words, can help hold **a cooperative species** together. |
| social glue | 社会黏合剂 | It is a kind of **social glue**. |
| be prosocial | 亲社会的（利他的） | We can be **prosocial** because we caused harm and we feel regret. |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> `guilt`（内疚感）是全篇**核心概念词**。第 21 题问"研究者认为内疚感有用，是因为它有助于什么"，答案落在"**道德发展**（moral development）"，其依据即第一段"内疚伴随孩子对社会与道德规范的理解而产生"。抓住 `social and moral norms` 即可定位。

## 二、研究与实验方法词

| 词组 | 中文 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| look at 244 children | 考察 244 名儿童 | In a 2014 study, for example, Malti **looked at 244 children**. |
| caregiver assessments | 照料者的评估 | Using **caregiver assessments** and the children's self-observations … |
| self-observations | 自我观察 | … **self-observations**, she rated each child's overall sympathy level … |
| rate … level | 给……的水平打分 | … she **rated** each child's overall sympathy **level** … |
| a tendency to do | 做……的倾向 | … and his or her **tendency to feel** negative emotions after moral transgressions. |
| moral transgressions | 道德越界、违规行为 | … negative emotions after **moral transgressions**. |
| be handed | 被分给 | Then the kids **were handed** chocolate coins … |
| be given a chance to | 获得机会做…… | … and **given a chance to share** them with an anonymous child. |
| an anonymous child | 匿名儿童 | … share them with **an anonymous child**. |
| turn on | 取决于 | … how much they shared appeared to **turn on** how inclined they were to feel guilty. |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 24 题（Malti 等人的研究表明合作与分享……）答案落在"**可由同情心或内疚感产生**（can result from either sympathy or guilt）"。定位依据即 `guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing`——`pathways to`（通往……的路径）是解题信号词。

## 三、逻辑连接与论述表达

| 词组 | 中文 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| in conjunction with | 与……同时、伴随着 | … guilt emerges a little later, **in conjunction with** a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms. |
| in the popular imagination | 在一般人的想象中 | **In the popular imagination**, of course, guilt still gets a bad rap. |
| view … in this light | 从这个角度看 | **Viewed in this light**, guilt is an opportunity. |
| in other words | 换言之 | Guilt, **in other words**, can help hold a cooperative species together. |
| vice versa | 反之亦然 | And **vice versa**: High sympathy can substitute for low guilt. |
| for example | 例如 | Jealousy and anger, **for example**, may have evolved … |
| even though | 尽管 | … shared more, **even though** they hadn't magically become more sympathetic … |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `in conjunction with` 表**伴随关系**，可换用 `along with`；`viewed in this light`（= seen from this angle）是议论文常用的**过渡短语**，标志作者开始正面立论。

## 四、评价与程度表达

| 词组 | 中文 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| get a bad rap | 名声不佳、遭恶评 | In the popular imagination, of course, guilt still **gets a bad rap**. |
| the emotional equivalent of | 在情感上相当于 | It's **the emotional equivalent of** wearing a jacket weighted with stones. |
| weighted with stones | 装满了石头的 | … wearing a jacket **weighted with stones**. |
| outdated | 过时的 | Yet this understanding is **outdated**. |
| a kind of revival | 某种复兴 | There has been **a kind of revival** or a rethinking about what guilt is … |
| a rethinking about | 对……的重新思考 | … or **a rethinking about** what guilt is and what role guilt can serve. |
| advantageous in one context | 在一种情境下有利 | … feelings that may be **advantageous in one context** may be harmful in another. |
| be destructive | 具有破坏性 | Too much happiness can be **destructive**. |
| important inequalities | 重要的不平等 | … may have evolved to alert us to **important inequalities**. |
| compensate for | 弥补、补偿 | … suggests that guilt may **compensate for** an emotional deficiency. |
| an emotional deficiency | 情感缺失 | … compensate for **an emotional deficiency**. |
| make up for the shortfall | 弥补这一不足 | … may **make up for that shortfall** by experiencing more guilt. |
| rein in | 约束、勒住 | … which can **rein in** their nastier impulses. |
| nastier impulses | 更恶劣的冲动 | … which can rein in their **nastier impulses**. |
| substitute for | 替代 | High sympathy can **substitute for** low guilt. |
| be inclined to do | 倾向于做 | … how **inclined** they were **to feel** guilty. |
| guilt-prone | 易于内疚的 | The **guilt-prone** ones shared more … |
| be sympathetic to | 对……同情 | … they hadn't magically become more **sympathetic to** the other child's deprivation. |
| deprivation | 匮乏、剥夺 | … the other child's **deprivation**. |
| cause harm | 造成伤害 | We can be prosocial because we **caused harm** … |
| feel regret | 感到懊悔 | … and we **feel regret**. |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 22 题问"许多人仍认为内疚感是……"，答案 `burdensome`（沉重的）对应原文 `a jacket weighted with stones`（灌满石头的夹克）——**比喻对应的同义替换**是本题的解题机关。
> 第 23 题问 Vaish 认为"重新思考内疚"源于何种认识，答案 `an emotion can play opposing roles`（一种情绪可发挥相反作用）对应 `advantageous in one context … harmful in another`。**抓住 `in one context … in another` 的对比结构**即可锁定。
