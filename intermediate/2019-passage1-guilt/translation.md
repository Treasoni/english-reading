# 2019 Passage 1 中英对照翻译

## Paragraph 1

Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger, guilt emerges a little later, in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms. Children aren't born knowing how to say "I'm sorry"; rather, they learn over time that such statements appease parents and friends—and their own consciences. This is why researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing.

与所谓的基本情绪（如悲伤、恐惧和愤怒）不同，内疚感的出现要稍晚一些，它伴随着孩子对社会与道德规范的理解不断增长而产生。孩子并非生来就懂得如何说"对不起"；相反，他们是在成长过程中逐渐认识到，这样的表达能够安抚父母和朋友——也能安抚自己的良心。正因如此，研究者普遍认为，所谓"道德内疚"只要分量恰当，就是一件好事。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `in conjunction with` = "与……同时、伴随着"；`grasp` = "理解、领会"（此处作名词）。
> - `social and moral norms` = "社会与道德规范"；`appease` = "安抚、平息"；`consciences` = "良心、良知"。
> - `regard A as B` 中的 `as` 此处写作 `to be`：`regard ... to be a good thing` = "认为……是好事"。`in the right amount` = "在恰当的分量下"。
> - 句首 `Unlike ...` 是**介词短语作比较状语**，主句主语为 `guilt`。

## Paragraph 2

In the popular imagination, of course, guilt still gets a bad rap. It is deeply uncomfortable—it's the emotional equivalent of wearing a jacket weighted with stones. Yet this understanding is outdated. "There has been a kind of revival or a rethinking about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve," says Amrisha Vaish, a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia, adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary—feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another. Jealousy and anger, for example, may have evolved to alert us to important inequalities. Too much happiness can be destructive.

当然，在一般人的想象中，内疚感仍然名声不佳。它让人极度不适——在情绪上，这好比穿着一件灌满石头的夹克。然而这种看法已经过时了。"人们正在以某种方式重新审视、重新思考内疚感究竟是什么、它能发挥什么作用，"弗吉尼亚大学的心理学研究者 Amrisha Vaish 说，并补充道，这种"复兴"是一场更大认知的一部分：情绪并非非黑即白——在一种情境下可能有利的情感，在另一种情境下可能有害。例如，嫉妒和愤怒或许进化而来，是为了提醒我们注意重要的不平等。而过多的快乐也可能具有破坏性。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `gets a bad rap` = "（被）名声不好、遭到恶评"，为固定习语。
> - `the emotional equivalent of ...` = "在情感上相当于……"；`weighted with stones` 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 `a jacket`。
> - `revival` = "复兴、重新兴起"；`rethinking` = "重新思考"；`binary` = "二元的、非此即彼的"。
> - `advantageous` = "有利的"；`destructive` = "破坏性的"。
> - `adding that ...` 是**现在分词作伴随状语**，逻辑主语为 Vaish。
> - `alert sb to sth` = "使某人警觉到某事"；`may have evolved to ...` = "或许进化成……（的目的）"。

## Paragraph 3

And guilt, by prompting us to think more deeply about our goodness, can encourage humans to make up for errors and fix relationships. Guilt, in other words, can help hold a cooperative species together. It is a kind of social glue.

而内疚感，通过促使我们更深入地思考自身的善，能够鼓励人们弥补过错、修复关系。换言之，内疚感有助于把一种以合作为生的物种凝聚在一起。它是一种社会黏合剂。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `by prompting us to ...` = "通过促使我们……"（介词 by + 动名词，表方式）。
> - `make up for` = "弥补、补偿"；`fix relationships` = "修复关系"。
> - `hold ... together` = "把……凝聚在一起"；`a cooperative species` 指人类这一"合作的物种"。
> - `social glue` = "社会黏合剂"，是比喻说法。

## Paragraph 4

Viewed in this light, guilt is an opportunity. Work by Tina Malti, a psychology professor at the University of Toronto, suggests that guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency. In a number of studies, Malti and others have shown that guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing. Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that shortfall by experiencing more guilt, which can rein in their nastier impulses. And vice versa: High sympathy can substitute for low guilt.

从这个角度来看，内疚感是一种机会。多伦多大学心理学教授 Tina Malti 的研究表明，内疚感或许能弥补某种情感上的缺失。在多项研究中，Malti 及其他人已表明，内疚感与同情心可能是通往合作与分享的两条不同路径。一些同情心较弱的孩子，也许会通过体验更多的内疚感来弥补这一不足，而这能约束他们更恶劣的冲动。反之亦然：高度的同情心可以替代低度的内疚感。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `Viewed in this light` = "从这个角度 / 这样来看"（过去分词短语作状语）。
> - `compensate for` = "弥补、补偿"；`deficiency` = "缺失、不足"；`shortfall` = "不足、缺口"。
> - `pathways to ...` = "通往……的路径"；`Tina Malti, a psychology professor ...` 中名词短语是**同位语**，补充说明身份。
> - `rein in` = "约束、勒住"；`impulses` = "冲动"；`substitute for` = "替代"；`vice versa` = "反之亦然"。
> - `which can rein in their nastier impulses` 是**非限制性定语从句**，先行词为前面"通过体验更多内疚感来弥补"这一整件事。

## Paragraph 5

In a 2014 study, for example, Malti looked at 244 children. Using caregiver assessments and the children's self-observations, she rated each child's overall sympathy level and his or her tendency to feel negative emotions after moral transgressions. Then the kids were handed chocolate coins, and given a chance to share them with an anonymous child. For the low-sympathy kids, how much they shared appeared to turn on how inclined they were to feel guilty. The guilt-prone ones shared more, even though they hadn't magically become more sympathetic to the other child's deprivation.

例如，在 2014 年的一项研究中，Malti 考察了 244 名儿童。她借助照料者的评估与儿童的自我观察，给每个孩子的总体同情心水平，以及他（她）在道德越界之后产生负面情绪的倾向打了分。随后，孩子们分到了巧克力硬币，并获得与一名匿名儿童分享它们的机会。对同情心弱的孩子而言，他们分享多少，似乎取决于他们有多容易感到内疚。那些容易内疚的孩子分享得更多，尽管他们并没有神奇地对另一个孩子所遭受的匮乏产生更多同情。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `caregiver assessments` = "照料者（家长 / 看护人）的评估"；`self-observations` = "自我观察"。
> - `rated` = "评定、打分"；`transgressions` = "越界行为、违规、过失"（对应第 25 题）。
> - `anonymous` = "匿名的"；`turn on` 此处 = "取决于"（并非"打开"）。
> - `inclined to` = "倾向于"；`guilt-prone` = "易于内疚的、有内疚倾向的"（后缀 -prone 表"易……的"）。
> - `deprivation` = "匮乏、剥夺"；`even though` 引导**让步状语从句**。
> - `Using caregiver assessments ...` 是**现在分词短语作方式状语**，逻辑主语为 `she`。

## Paragraph 6

"That's good news," Malti says. "We can be prosocial because we caused harm and we feel regret."

Malti 说："这是好消息。我们可以因为自己造成了伤害、并且感到懊悔，而做出亲社会行为。"

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `prosocial` = "亲社会的"（指利他、乐于助人、有利于他人的行为）。
> - `regret` = "懊悔、遗憾"。
> - `because we caused harm and we feel regret` 中两个分句由 `and` 并列，同由 `because` 统辖，说明"亲社会"的原因。
