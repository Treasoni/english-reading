# Intelligence Testing and IQ Scores 智力测验与智商分数 中英对照

For the past several years, the Sunday newspaper supplement *Parade* has featured a column called "Ask Marilyn." People are invited to query Marilyn vos Savant, who at age 10 had tested at a **mental level** of someone about 23 years old; that gave her an **IQ** of 228—the highest score ever recorded. IQ tests ask you to complete **verbal and visual analogies**, to **envision** paper after it has been folded and cut, and to **deduce** numerical sequences, among other similar tasks. So it is a bit confusing when vos Savant fields such queries from the average Joe (whose IQ is 100) as, What's the difference between love and fondness? Or what is the nature of luck and coincidence? It's not obvious how the capacity to visualize objects and to figure out numerical patterns suits one to answer questions that have eluded some of the best poets and philosophers.

在过去的几年里，周日报纸的增刊《Parade》一直设有一个名为"问问玛丽莲"的专栏。人们受邀向玛丽莲·沃斯·萨万特提问，她在 10 岁时测得的智力水平相当于约 23 岁的人；这使得她的智商达到了 228——这是有史以来录得的最高分。智商测试要求你完成语言和视觉类比、想象纸张被折叠和裁剪后的样子、推导数字序列，以及其他类似的题目。因此，当沃斯·萨万特回答普通人（智商为 100）提出的诸如"爱与喜欢有什么区别？"或"运气与巧合的本质是什么？"这类问题时，多少有些令人困惑。尚不清楚的是，这种想象物体形象和推演数字模式的能力，如何使一个人能够回答那些连最优秀的诗人和哲学家都未能解答的问题。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"the average Joe"**：美式口语，指"普通人、一般人"，不可直译为"平均的乔"。
> - **"fields such queries"**："field" 此处作动词，意为"回答、应对（问题）"，与 "answer" 近义。
> - **"suits one to"**："suit ... to" 意为"使……适合/胜任"，此处指"使某人有资格去回答"。
> - **"eluded"**："elude" 意为"使……无法理解/难倒"，此处指"难倒了最优秀的诗人和哲学家"。

Clearly, intelligence **encompasses** more than a score on a test. Just what does it mean to be smart? How much of intelligence can be specified, and how much can we learn about it from **neurology**, **genetics**, computer science and other fields?

显然，智力所包含的远不止测试中的一个分数。聪明究竟意味着什么？智力有多少可以被明确定义，我们能从神经学、遗传学、计算机科学及其他领域中对它了解多少？

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"encompasses more than a score"**："encompass" 意为"包含、涵盖"，此处译为"智力所包含的远不止一个分数"。
> - 本段三个连续疑问句，翻译保留疑问语气以体现原文的思辨色彩。

The defining term of intelligence in humans still seems to be the IQ score, even though IQ tests are not given as often as they used to be. The test comes primarily in two forms: the Stanford-Binet Intelligence Scale and the Wechsler Intelligence Scales (both come in adult and children's version). Generally costing several hundred dollars, they are usually given only by psychologists, although variations of them populate bookstores and the World Wide Web. Superhigh scores like vos Savant's are no longer possible, because scoring is now based on a **statistical population distribution** among age peers, rather than simply dividing the **mental age** by the **chronological age** and multiplying by 100. Other **standardized tests**, such as the Scholastic Assessment Test (SAT) and the Graduate Record Exam (GRE), capture the main aspects of IQ tests.

对人类智力的界定性指标似乎仍然是智商分数，尽管智商测试已不像过去那样频繁施测。这种测试主要有两种形式：斯坦福-比奈智力量表和韦克斯勒智力量表（两者都有成人和儿童版本）。这类测试通常要花费数百美元，一般只由心理学家施测，尽管它们的各种变体在书店和互联网上随处可见。像沃斯·萨万特那样的超高分数已不再可能出现，因为现在的计分方式是基于同龄人中的统计总体分布，而不是简单地将心理年龄除以生理年龄再乘以 100。其他标准化测试，如学术能力评估测试（SAT）和研究生入学考试（GRE），也涵盖了智商测试的主要方面。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"The defining term of intelligence"**："defining term" 指"界定性术语/标志性指标"，此处指人们仍以 IQ 分数作为智力的界定性指标。
> - **"populate bookstores and the World Wide Web"**："populate" 意为"遍布、充斥"，此处译为"在书店和互联网上随处可见"。
> - **"age peers"**：指"同龄人"，不可直译为"年龄同伴"。
> - **"mental age" / "chronological age"**：心理学术语，分别指"心理年龄"（智力发展水平对应的年龄）与"生理年龄/实足年龄"。

Such standardized tests may not assess all the important elements necessary to succeed in school and in life, argues Robert J. Sternberg. In his article "How Intelligent Is Intelligence Testing?", Sternberg notes that traditional tests best assess **analytical and verbal skills** but fail to measure **creativity** and **practical knowledge**, components also critical to problem solving and life success. Moreover, IQ tests do not necessarily predict so well once populations or situations change. Research has found that IQ predicted **leadership skills** when the tests were given under low-stress conditions, but under high-stress conditions, IQ was **negatively correlated** with leadership—that is, it predicted the opposite. Anyone who has toiled through SAT will testify that test-taking skill also matters, whether it's knowing when to guess or what questions to skip.

罗伯特·J·斯腾伯格指出，这类标准化考试可能无法评估在学校和生活中取得成功所必需的所有重要因素。在他的文章《智力测试有多聪明？》中，斯腾伯格指出，传统测试最能评估分析和语言能力，却未能衡量创造力和实践知识，而这些成分同样对解决问题和人生成功至关重要。此外，一旦人群或情境发生变化，智商测试就不一定能预测得那么准确。研究发现，在低压力条件下施测时，智商能够预测领导能力；但在高压力条件下，智商与领导能力呈负相关——也就是说，它预测的结果恰恰相反。任何苦读过 SAT 的人都会证明，应试技巧同样重要，无论是知道何时该猜，还是知道哪些题目该跳过。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"negatively correlated with"**：统计学术语，意为"与……呈负相关"，即一个上升、另一个下降。
> - **"toiled through"**："toil" 意为"辛苦劳作"，"toiled through SAT" 译为"苦读/苦熬过 SAT"。
> - **"test-taking skill"**：指"应试技巧"，区别于真正的学术能力，为 Q29 的考点。

    - [D] Choosing words or graphs similar to the given ones. — 选择与给定词语或图形相似的选项

    从最后一段我们可以得出结论：____。

    - [B] IQ scores and SAT results are highly correlated — 智商分数与 SAT 成绩高度相关
    - [C] testing involves a lot of guesswork — 测试包含很多猜测成分
    - [D] traditional tests are out of date — 传统测试已经过时
