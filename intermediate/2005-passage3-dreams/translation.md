## Text 3

Of all the components of a good night's sleep, dreams seem to be least within our control. In dreams, a window opens into a world where logic is suspended and dead people speak. A century ago, Freud formulated his revolutionary theory that dreams were the disguised shadows of our unconscious desires and fears; by the late 1970s, neurologists had switched to thinking of them as just "mental noise" — the random byproducts of the neural-repair work that goes on during sleep. Now researchers suspect that dreams are part of the mind's **emotional thermostat**, regulating moods while the brain is "off-line." And one leading authority says that these intensely powerful mental events can be not only **harnessed** but actually brought under **conscious control**, to help us sleep and feel better. "It's your dream," says Rosalind Cartwright, chair of psychology at Chicago's Medical Center. "If you don't like it, change it."

在一个好觉的所有因素中，梦似乎是其中最难控制的。在梦中，一扇窗户通向了这样一个世界，在这里逻辑失去效用，死人能开口说话。一个世纪之前，弗洛伊德阐述了他一个革命性理论：梦是我们潜意识里的欲望和恐惧伪装后的影子；到了20世纪70年代晚期，精神病学专家转而认为梦是"精神噪音"——睡眠过程中神经修复活动产生的副产品。现在，研究人员猜想梦是大脑情绪调节器的一部分，在大脑"下线"时调节我们的情绪。一位主要的权威人士说，梦这种非常强烈的精神活动不仅能被驾驭，事实上还可以有意识地加以控制，以帮助我们睡得更好，感觉更佳。芝加哥医学中心心理科主任罗莎琳德·卡特莱特说："梦是你自己的，如果你不喜欢，那就改变它。"

> [!note] 时间线索的处理
> 原文通过 "A century ago... by the late 1970s... Now..." 展示观点演变，中文保留时间顺序为"一个世纪之前...到了20世纪70年代晚期...现在..."，清晰呈现三个阶段的认知变化。

---

Evidence from brain imaging supports this view. The brain is as active during REM (rapid eye movement) sleep — when most vivid dreams occur — as it is when fully awake, says Dr. Eric Nofzinger at the University of Pittsburgh. But not all parts of the brain are equally involved; the **limbic system** (the "emotional brain") is especially active, while the **prefrontal cortex** (the center of intellect and reasoning) is relatively quiet. "We wake up from dreams happy or depressed, and those feelings can stay with us all day," says Stanford sleep researcher Dr. William Dement.

大脑成像的证据也支持这种观点。匹兹堡大学的埃里克·诺夫戈尔博士说，大脑在出现快速动眼睡眠期间——大多数鲜活的梦就发生在此时——和其在完全清醒时一样活跃。但并不是大脑的每个部分都同等地参与做梦过程；脑边缘系统（情绪大脑）最为活跃，而前额皮层（智力和推理中心）就相对安静。斯坦福大学睡眠研究专家威廉·迪蒙特博士说："我们从梦中醒来时，或者高兴，或者悲伤，这些情绪会伴随我们一整天。"

> [!note] 术语翻译
> "limbic system"译为"脑边缘系统"（俗称"情绪大脑"）；"prefrontal cortex"译为"前额皮层"（智力和推理中心）。括号内补充说明便于理解。

---

The link between dreams and emotions shows up among the patients in Cartwright's clinic. Most people seem to have more bad dreams early in the night, progressing toward happier ones before awakening, suggesting that they are ==working through negative feelings== generated during the day. Because our conscious mind is occupied with daily life we don't always think about the emotional significance of the day's events — until, it appears, we begin to dream.

梦和情绪之间的联系也显示在卡特莱特诊所的病人身上。大多数人似乎在夜晚刚入睡时做更多不好的梦，而在快醒来的时候会逐渐做一些快乐的梦，这意味着他们正在梦里克服白天产生的不良情绪。因为清醒时我们的大脑被日常琐事占据着，所以我们无暇考虑白天发生的事对情绪的影响，直到开始做梦时这种影响才出现。

---

And this process need not be left to the unconscious. Cartwright believes one can exercise conscious control over **recurring bad dreams**. As soon as you awaken, identify what is upsetting about the dream. **Visualize** how you would like it to end instead; the next time it occurs, try to wake up just enough to control its course. With much practice people can learn to, literally, do it in their sleep.

这个过程不一定是无意识的。卡特莱特认为，人们可以对反复出现的噩梦进行有意识地控制。只要你醒过来，马上确定是什么在梦中困扰着你。设想一下你想让梦怎样结束；再做梦时，尽量醒来以控制梦的进程。通过反复练习，人们真的能学会在梦中这样做。

> [!note] "need not be left to"
> 双重否定结构，原文 "need not be left to the unconscious" 意为"不必留给无意识处理"，译为"不一定是无意识的"更自然。

---

At the end of the day, there's probably little reason to pay attention to our dreams at all unless they keep us from sleeping or "we wake up in a panic," Cartwright says. Terrorism, economic uncertainties and general feelings of insecurity have increased people's anxiety. Those suffering from persistent nightmares should seek help from a therapist. For the rest of us, the brain has its ways of working through bad feelings. Sleep — or rather dream — on it and you'll feel better in the morning.

卡特莱特说，说到底，除非噩梦不断打断我们的睡眠或使我们"从噩梦中惊醒"，也许就没有多少理由太在意所做的梦。恐怖主义、经济不稳定以及普遍的不安全感都增加了人们的焦虑。那些长期受噩梦折磨的人应该寻求治疗师的帮助。而对其他人来说，大脑有自己的办法消除不良情绪。放心睡觉，甚至是做梦，在清晨时你会感觉更好。
