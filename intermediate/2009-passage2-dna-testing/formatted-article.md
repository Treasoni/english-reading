# DNA Testing

## Reading Passage

It is a wise father that knows his own child, but today a man can **boost** his paternal (fatherly) wisdom—or at least confirm that he’s the kid’s dad.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It is a wise father that knows his own child, but today a man can boost his paternal (fatherly) wisdom—or at least confirm that he’s the kid’s dad.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: It（强调句的形式主语）；第二分句为 a man
> - 谓语 V: is；第二分句为 can boost / confirm
> - 宾语 O: 第二分句中 boost 的宾语为 his paternal wisdom；confirm 的宾语为 that 从句
> - 补语 C: a wise father（被强调的成分）
> - 状语 A: today（修饰第二分句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 强调结构 | It is a wise father that... | 强调 a wise father，基本结构可还原为 A wise father knows his own child |
> | 定语从句/强调分句 | that knows his own child | 说明被强调的 a wise father |
> | 并列结构 | but today a man... | 连接前后两个转折分句 |
> | 名词性从句 | that he’s the kid’s dad | 作 confirm 的宾语 |
> | 省略结构 | or at least confirm... | 与 can boost 并列，共用 can；完整形式为 can at least confirm... |
>
> **结构图解**：
> `text
> 复合句
> ├── 分句一：It is a wise father that knows his own child
> │   ├── 强调成分：a wise father
> │   └── that 从句：knows his own child
> └── but 分句二：today a man can boost his paternal wisdom
>     └── or（省略 can）at least confirm [that he’s the kid’s dad]
> `
>
> **参考译文**：只有聪明的父亲才了解自己的孩子；但如今，一个男人可以增强自己作为父亲的“智慧”——或者至少确认自己就是孩子的父亲。
>
> **考点提示**：重点掌握 `It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who...` 强调句；注意 `or` 后省略与前面相同的情态动词 `can`，以及 `that he’s the kid’s dad` 作 `confirm` 的宾语从句。

All he needs to do is shell out $30 for a **paternity testing kit (PTK)** at his local drugstore—and another $120 to get the results.

More than 60,000 people have purchased the **PTKs** since they first became available without prescriptions last year, according to Doug Fogg, chief operating officer of Identigene, which makes the over-the-counter kits.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：More than 60,000 people have purchased the PTKs since they first became available without prescriptions last year, according to Doug Fogg, chief operating officer of Identigene, which makes the over-the-counter kits.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: More than 60,000 people
> - 谓语 V: have purchased
> - 宾语 O: the PTKs
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: since they first became available without prescriptions last year；according to Doug Fogg 为来源状语
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 状语从句 | since they first became available without prescriptions last year | 说明购买行为发生的起点时间 |
> | 介词短语 | without prescriptions | 修饰 became available，表示无需处方 |
> | 介词短语 | according to Doug Fogg | 表明信息来源 |
> | 同位语 | chief operating officer of Identigene | 补充说明 Doug Fogg 的身份 |
> | 定语从句 | which makes the over-the-counter kits | 修饰 Identigene |
>
> **结构图解**：
> `text
> 主句：More than 60,000 people have purchased the PTKs
> ├── since 从句：they first became available without prescriptions last year
> ├── according to Doug Fogg
> │   └── 同位语：chief operating officer of Identigene
> │       └── 定从：which makes the over-the-counter kits
> `
>
> **参考译文**：Identigene 公司首席运营官道格·福格表示，自这些亲子鉴定工具包去年首次可以无需处方购买以来，已有超过 6 万人购买。
>
> **考点提示**：`have purchased` 是现在完成时，常与 `since` 引导的时间状语从句搭配；`chief operating officer of Identigene` 是 `Doug Fogg` 的同位语，句末 `which` 定语从句修饰公司名 Identigene，而非前面的具体职位。

More than two dozen companies sell **DNA tests** directly to the public, ranging in price from a few hundred dollars to more than $2,500.

Among the most popular: **paternity and kinship testing**, which adopted children can use to find their biological relatives and families can use to track down kids put up for adoption.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Among the most popular: paternity and kinship testing, which adopted children can use to find their biological relatives and families can use to track down kids put up for adoption.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: paternity and kinship testing
> - 谓语 V: [are]（省略）
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: among the most popular
> - 状语 A: 无
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 介词短语前置 | Among the most popular | 作表语，完整语序为 Paternity and kinship testing are among the most popular |
> | 定语从句 | which adopted children can use... and families can use... | 修饰 paternity and kinship testing |
> | 不定式短语 | to find their biological relatives | 表示 adopted children 使用检测的目的 |
> | 不定式短语 | to track down kids put up for adoption | 表示 families 使用检测的目的 |
> | 过去分词短语 | put up for adoption | 修饰 kids，意为被送养的孩子 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> `text
> 省略倒装结构：Among the most popular [are] paternity and kinship testing
> └── which 定从：
>     ├── adopted children can use [it] to find their biological relatives
>     └── families can use [it] to track down kids
>         └── put up for adoption
> `
>
> **参考译文**：其中最受欢迎的项目包括亲子关系和亲属关系检测。被收养的孩子可以利用这类检测寻找自己的生物学亲属，家庭也可以借此追踪那些被送养的孩子。
>
> **考点提示**：冒号前的 `Among the most popular` 是前置表语，句中省略了 `are`；`which` 引导定语从句，且从句内部有两个由 `and` 连接的并列谓语；`put up for adoption` 是过去分词短语，修饰 `kids`。

**DNA testing** is also the latest rage among passionate genealogists—and supports businesses that offer to search for a family’s geographic roots.

Most tests require collecting cells by swabbing saliva in the mouth and sending it to the company for testing. All tests require a potential candidate with whom to compare DNA.

But some observers are skeptical. “There’s a kind of **false precision** being hawked by people claiming they are doing **ancestry testing**,” says Troy Duster, a New York University sociologist. He notes that each individual has many ancestors—numbering in the hundreds just a few centuries back. Yet most ancestry testing only considers a single lineage, either the Y chromosome inherited through men in a father’s line or mitochondrial DNA, which is passed down only from mothers.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Yet most ancestry testing only considers a single lineage, either the Y chromosome inherited through men in a father’s line or mitochondrial DNA, which is passed down only from mothers.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: most ancestry testing
> - 谓语 V: considers
> - 宾语 O: a single lineage
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: Yet（转折副词）；only（限制 considers 的范围）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 同位语 | either the Y chromosome... or mitochondrial DNA | 具体解释 a single lineage 包含的两种可能 |
> | 过去分词短语 | inherited through men in a father’s line | 修饰 the Y chromosome |
> | 介词短语 | through men in a father’s line | 说明 Y 染色体的传递路径 |
> | 定语从句 | which is passed down only from mothers | 修饰 mitochondrial DNA |
> | 介词短语 | from mothers | 说明线粒体 DNA 的传递来源 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> `text
> 主句：most ancestry testing only considers a single lineage
> └── 同位语：either the Y chromosome or mitochondrial DNA
>     ├── 过去分词短语：inherited through men in a father’s line
>     └── which 定从：which is passed down only from mothers
> `
>
> **参考译文**：然而，大多数祖源检测只考虑单一谱系：要么是沿父系男性传递的 Y 染色体，要么是只由母亲传递的线粒体 DNA。
>
> **考点提示**：`either...or...` 在此作同位语，具体说明 `a single lineage`；过去分词 `inherited` 作后置定语，`which` 引导的非限制性定语从句补充说明 `mitochondrial DNA`。

This DNA can reveal genetic information about only one or two ancestors, even though, for example, just three generations back people also have six other great-grandparents or, four generations back, 14 other great-great-grandparents.

Critics also argue that commercial genetic testing is only as good as the **reference collections** to which a sample is compared.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Critics also argue that commercial genetic testing is only as good as the reference collections to which a sample is compared.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Critics
> - 谓语 V: argue
> - 宾语 O: that commercial genetic testing is only as good as the reference collections to which a sample is compared（that 引导的宾语从句）
> - 补语 C: only as good as the reference collections...（宾语从句中的表语结构）
> - 状语 A: also（修饰 argue）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 名词性从句 | that commercial genetic testing is only as good as... | 作 argue 的宾语 |
> | 比较结构 | only as good as the reference collections | 表示“质量只能达到参照对象的水平” |
> | 定语从句 | to which a sample is compared | 修饰 the reference collections；介词 to 前置 |
> | 被动结构 | a sample is compared | sample 是 compare 的承受者，完整含义为 compared to the collections |
>
> **结构图解**：
> `text
> 主句：Critics also argue [宾语从句]
> └── commercial genetic testing is only as good as the reference collections
>     └── to which a sample is compared
> `
>
> **参考译文**：批评者还认为，商业基因检测的质量取决于样本所比对的参考数据库。
>
> **考点提示**：识别 `argue that...` 引导的宾语从句；`only as good as...` 是“与……一样好/其好坏取决于……”的比较结构；`to which` 是“介词 + 关系代词”引导的定语从句，不能将 `to` 遗漏。

Databases used by some companies don't rely on data collected systematically but rather lump together information from different research projects. This means that a DNA database may have a lot of data from some regions and not others, so a person's test results may differ depending on the company that processes the results. In addition, the computer programs a company uses to estimate relationships may be patented and not subject to **peer review** or outside evaluation.
