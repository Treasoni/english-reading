# 2010 完形填空 固定搭配与词组

> 本篇搭配密度高，8 个错题里有 2 个直接栽在搭配上（第 6 空 `in the absence of`、第 7 空 `come to notice`）。以下按类别整理，📌 为本篇直接考点。

## 疫情与公共卫生

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文 / 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| be declared a global epidemic | 被宣布为全球流行病 | The outbreak … **was declared a global epidemic** on June 11, 2009. |
| be designated (as) | 被认定/指定为 | the first worldwide epidemic **designated by** the WHO |
| a heightened alert | 升级的警报 | **The heightened alert** followed an emergency meeting … |
| a sharp rise in cases | 病例急剧上升 | **after a sharp rise in cases** in Australia |
| in severity | 在严重程度上 | the epidemic is "moderate" **in severity** |
| mild symptoms | 轻微症状 | patients experiencing only **mild symptoms** |
| a full recovery | 完全康复 | and **a full recovery** |
| seasonal flu | 季节性流感 | not **seasonal flu** |
| high-risk groups | 高风险人群 | vaccinate people in other **high-risk groups** |

## 高频固定搭配（📌 本篇考点）

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文 / 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 📌 in the absence of | 在没有……的情况下 | often **in the absence of** any medical treatment（第 6 空） |
| 📌 come to (public / global) notice | 引起（公众/全球）注意 | The outbreak **came to global notice** in late April 2009（第 7 空） |
| 📌 crop up | 突然出现；冒出来 | cases began to **crop up** in New York City（第 9 空） |
| 📌 take orders from | 接受……的订单 | began **taking orders from** the states（第 15 空） |
| 📌 be recommended for | 被推荐用于 | which is **not recommended for** pregnant women（第 18 空） |
| 📌 care for | 照护；照顾 | people **caring for** infants（第 20 空） |
| 📌 at the height of | 在……的顶点/高峰 | **at the height of** a panic |
| ahead of expectations | 早于预期 | is available **ahead of expectations**（第 16 空） |
| the overwhelming majority of | 绝大多数 | **the overwhelming majority of** patients |
| a large number of | 大量 | an unusually **large number of** hospitalizations |

## 动词搭配与干扰项

| 搭配 | 释义 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| release sth. from a stockpile | 从储备中发放某物 | 第 14 空 **released** Tamiflu … from the national stockpile |
| place an order | 下订单 | 第 15 空干扰项 **placing**，方向与本句 from the states 相反 |
| take orders | 接受订单 / 听命 | 第 15 空正确答案 |
| ward off | 抵御；避开 | 第 20 空干扰项 **warding off** infants，语义不通 |
| be involved in | 参与；卷入 | 第 20 空干扰项，不能表示"照护者"身份 |
| be concerned with | 与……有关；关心 | 第 20 空干扰项，语义偏"关注" |
| inject sb. with sth. | 给某人注射某物 | 第 13 空干扰项 **injected**，需带 with，不能直接接人 |
| be immersed in | 沉浸在 | 第 13 空干扰项，与"感染"无关 |
| impart sth. to sb. | 把……传授给某人 | 第 13 空干扰项，搭配方向不符 |
| relieve | 缓解 | 第 14 空干扰项，不能接 from the stockpile |
| relay | 转播；转达 | 第 14 空干扰项 |
| remain | 保持；剩下 | 第 14 空干扰项，不及物用法不符 |

## 主题词（可与生词表联动）

| 词 | 释义 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| outbreak | 爆发 | the **outbreak** of swine flu |
| epidemic / pandemic | 流行病 / 大流行 | 本篇用 epidemic |
| hospitalization | 住院 | a large number of **hospitalizations** |
| stockpile | 储备（物资） | the national **stockpile** |
| dose | 剂量；一剂 | three million **doses** |
| nasal spray | 鼻喷剂 | FluMist **nasal spray** type |
| vaccinate | 给……接种疫苗 | **vaccinate** people in other high-risk groups |
| pregnant | 怀孕的 | **pregnant** women |
| breathing difficulties | 呼吸困难 | those with **breathing difficulties** |
