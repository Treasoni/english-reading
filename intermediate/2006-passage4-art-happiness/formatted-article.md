# Art and Happiness

Many things make people think artists are **weird**.

But the weirdest may be this: artists' only job is to **explore emotions**, and yet they choose to focus on the ones that feel bad.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：But the weirdest may be this: artists' only job is to **explore emotions**, and yet they choose to focus on the ones that feel bad.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: the weirdest
> - 谓语 V: may be
> - 补语 C: this
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 同位语 | 冒号 | 补充说明 this |
> | 并列句 | and yet | 与前半句并列 |
> | 定语从句 | that | the ones |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the weirdest may be this
>   ├── 同位语(冒号): artists' job is to explore emotions
>   └── 并列连词 → 并列句: and yet they choose to focus on the ones
>         └── 定从(that): feel bad → 修饰 the ones
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：但最古怪的可能是这一点：艺术家唯一的工作就是探索情感，然而他们却选择关注那些糟糕的情感。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 冒号同位语结构：冒号后内容对 `this` 进行具体解释，考研阅读中常利用冒号后信息推断前文含义
> 2. 定语从句关系代词在从句中作主语时不可省略

This wasn't always so. The earliest forms of art, like painting and music, are those best suited for expressing joy.

But somewhere in the 19th century onward, more artists began seeing happiness as **insipid**, **phony** or, worst of all, boring as we went from Wordsworth's daffodils to Baudelaire's flowers of evil.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：But somewhere in the 19th century onward, more artists began seeing happiness as **insipid**, **phony** or, worst of all, boring as we went from Wordsworth's daffodils to Baudelaire's flowers of evil.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: more artists
> - 谓语 V: began seeing
> - 宾语 O: happiness
> - 补语 C: as insipid, phony or boring
> - 状语 A: somewhere in the 19th century onward
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | in / onward | 时间状语，修饰主句 |
> | 插入语 | 逗号分隔 | worst of all，插入修饰 |
> | 状语从句 | as | 伴随/时间状语，修饰主句 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: more artists began seeing happiness as insipid, phony or boring
>   ├── 介短: (somewhere) in the 19th century onward → 时间状语
>   ├── 插入语: worst of all → 程度强调
>   └── 状从(as): as we went from Wordsworth's daffodils to Baudelaire's flowers of evil → 伴随状语
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：但从19世纪某个时候起，越来越多的艺术家开始将幸福视为平淡乏味、虚假，或者最糟糕的是，令人厌倦——从华兹华斯的水仙花到波德莱尔的《恶之花》，我们见证了这种转变。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `see...as...` 结构：意为"将……视为……"，考研阅读中常见 `regard...as...`、`view...as...`、`consider...as...` 等同义替换
> 2. `as` 引导时间/伴随状语从句：此处 `as we went...` 表"随着……"
> 3. 文学隐喻词义推断：Wordsworth's daffodils（欢乐）↔ Baudelaire's flowers of evil（丑恶），利用对比推断作者意图

You could argue that art became more **skeptical** of happiness because modern times have seen such misery.

But it's not as if earlier times didn't know **perpetual** war, disaster and the **massacre** of innocents.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：But it's not as if earlier times didn't know **perpetual** war, disaster and the **massacre** of innocents.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: It
> - 谓语 V: is ('s)
> - 补语 C: not as if earlier times didn't know perpetual war, disaster and the massacre of innocents
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 表语从句 | as if | 作主句的表语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: It is not as if ...
>   └── 表语从句(as if): earlier times didn't know perpetual war, disaster and the massacre of innocents → 作主句的表语
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：但这并非是说早期时代就没有经历过无休止的战争、灾难和对无辜者的大屠杀。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `It's not as if...` 固定句式：意为"并非好像……"，表达对某种假设的否定
> 2. 双重否定结构：`not as if ... didn't know` 实质表肯定，强调"过去也知道同样的苦难"

The reason, in fact, may be just the opposite: there is too much damn happiness in the world today.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The reason, in fact, may be just the opposite: there is too much damn happiness in the world today.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: The reason
> - 谓语 V: may be
> - 补语 C: just the opposite
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 插入语 | 逗号分隔 | in fact，插入修饰 |
> | 同位语 | 冒号 | 补充说明 the opposite |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: The reason may be just the opposite
>   ├── 插入语: in fact → 强调语气
>   └── 同位语(冒号): there is too much damn happiness in the world today → 解释 the opposite
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：事实上，原因可能恰恰相反：当今世界有太多该死的幸福了。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 插入语识别：`in fact` 置于主谓之间，阅读时可先跳过，连读主干
> 2. 冒号同位语结构：冒号后内容具体说明 `the opposite` 是什么

After all, what is the one modern form of expression almost completely dedicated to **depicting** happiness? ==Advertising.==

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：After all, what is the one modern form of expression almost completely dedicated to **depicting** happiness? ==Advertising.==
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: the one modern form of expression
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: what
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 话语标记 | After all | 逻辑衔接，修饰全句 |
> | 非谓语（过去分词短语） | dedicated to | 后置定语，修饰 expression |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 疑问句: what is the one modern form of expression ...
>   ├── 话语标记: After all → 逻辑衔接
>   ├── 主语: the one modern form of expression
>   │     └── 非谓语(过去分词): dedicated to depicting happiness → 后置定语修饰 expression
>   └── 独立回答: Advertising. → 省略句，回答what
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：说到底，几乎完全致力于描绘幸福的现代表达形式是什么？广告。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 设问修辞（rhetorical question）：作者自问自答，考研阅读中常利用这种结构强调核心论点
> 2. 过去分词短语作后置定语：`dedicated to depicting happiness` 修饰 `expression`
> 3. 省略句回答：`Advertising.` 单独成句，省略了系表结构

The rise of **anti-happy art** almost exactly tracks the emergence of **mass media**, and with it, a **commercial culture** in which happiness is not just an ideal but an **ideology**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The rise of **anti-happy art** almost exactly tracks the emergence of **mass media**, and with it, a **commercial culture** in which happiness is not just an ideal but an **ideology**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: The rise of anti-happy art
> - 谓语 V: tracks
> - 宾语 O: the emergence of mass media, and (tracks) with it, a commercial culture
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 定语从句 | in which | a commercial culture |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: The rise of anti-happy art tracks the emergence of mass media
>   └── 并列宾语: and (tracks) with it, a commercial culture
>         └── 定从(in which): happiness is not just an ideal but an ideology → 修饰 commercial culture
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：反幸福艺术的兴起几乎与大众传媒的出现同步，随之而来的还有一种商业文化，在其中幸福不仅是一种理想，更是一种意识形态。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 并列宾语省略动词：第二个宾语 `a commercial culture` 前省略了 `tracks`
> 2. `in which` 定语从句：等于 `where`，修饰 `commercial culture`
> 3. `not just...but...` 结构：强调递进关系
> 4. `tracks` 词义判断：此处意为"与……同步发生"

People in earlier eras were surrounded by reminders of misery. They worked until exhausted, lived with few protections and died young.

In the West, before mass communication and literacy, the most powerful mass medium was the church, which reminded worshippers that their souls were in **peril** and that they would someday be meat for worms.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In the West, before mass communication and literacy, the most powerful mass medium was the church, which reminded worshippers that their souls were in peril and that they would someday be meat for worms.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: the most powerful mass medium
> - 谓语 V: was
> - 补语 C: the church
> - 状语 A: In the West
> - 状语 A: before mass communication and literacy
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | In | 地点状语 |
> | 介词短语 | before | 时间状语 |
> | 定语从句 | which | the church |
> | 名词性从句 | that (×2) | 作 reminded 的宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the most powerful mass medium was the church
>   ├── 介短: In the West → 地点状语
>   ├── 介短: before mass communication and literacy → 时间状语
>   └── 定从(which): which reminded worshippers that...and that... → 修饰 the church
>         ├── 名从(that): their souls were in peril → reminded 的宾语1
>         └── 名从(that): they would someday be meat for worms → reminded 的宾语2
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在西方，在大众传播和读写普及之前，最强大的大众媒介是教堂，它提醒信徒们：他们的灵魂处于危险之中，终有一天会成为蛆虫的食物。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 非限制性定语从句：`which` 对先行词 `the church` 补充说明
> 2. 双宾语从句并列：`reminded worshippers that...and that...`
> 3. `meat for worms` 修辞：借喻死亡的卑微与必然，考研阅读常考比喻义推断

Today the messages the average Westerner is surrounded with are not religious but commercial, and forever happy.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Today the messages the average Westerner is surrounded with are not religious but commercial, and forever happy.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: the messages (the average Westerner is surrounded with)
> - 谓语 V: are
> - 补语 C: not religious but commercial, and forever happy
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 定语从句 | 省略 that/which | the messages |
> | 时间状语 | Today | 修饰全句 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the messages are not religious but commercial, and forever happy
>   ├── 时间状语: Today → 修饰全句
>   └── 定从(省略关系代词): (that/which) the average Westerner is surrounded with → 修饰 the messages
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如今，普通西方人周围的信息不是宗教性的，而是商业性的，而且永远是快乐的。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 定语从句关系代词省略：关系代词作宾语时可省略
> 2. `not...but...` 结构：意为"不是……而是……"，考研阅读中表对比
> 3. `be surrounded with` 短语：介词 `with` 不可省略

Fast-food eaters, news anchors, text messengers, all smiling, smiling, smiling. Our magazines feature beaming celebrities and happy families in perfect homes. 

And since these messages have an agenda — to lure us to open our wallets — they make the very idea of happiness seem unreliable.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：And since these messages have an agenda — to lure us to open our wallets — they make the very idea of happiness seem unreliable.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: they
> - 谓语 V: make
> - 宾语 O: the very idea of happiness
> - 补语 C: seem unreliable
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句 | since | 原因状语，修饰主句 |
> | 插入语（不定式） | 破折号 | 同位解释 agenda |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 状从(since): since these messages have an agenda
>   └── 插入语(破折号): to lure us to open our wallets → 同位解释 agenda
> 主句: they make the very idea of happiness seem unreliable
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：而且由于这些信息都带有目的——诱使我们打开钱包——它们让幸福这个概念本身显得不可靠。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `since` 引导原因状语从句：语气比 `because` 弱，常引导已知原因
> 2. 破折号不定式同位语：`to lure us to open our wallets` 解释 `agenda`
> 3. `make + O + do` 结构：省略 `to` 的不定式作宾补
> 4. `the very idea`：`very` 为形容词，表"正是/恰恰是"，起强调作用

But what we forget — what our economy depends on us forgetting — is that happiness is more than pleasure without pain.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：But what we forget — what our economy depends on us forgetting — is that happiness is more than pleasure without pain.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: what we forget（主语从句）
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: that happiness is more than pleasure without pain（表语从句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 插入语 | 破折号 | 对主语从句的同位补充 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: [S what we forget] is [C that happiness is more than pleasure without pain]
>   └── 插入语(破折号): what our economy depends on us forgetting → 同位解释主语从句
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：但我们所忘记的——我们的经济正依赖于我们遗忘的——是幸福不仅仅是无痛苦的快乐。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 主语从句：`what we forget` 作主语，谓语动词 `is` 用单数
> 2. 破折号插入语：可先跳过，直接连读主干部分
> 3. 表语从句：`that` 在正式文体中保留

The things that bring the greatest joy carry the greatest potential for loss and disappointment.

Today, surrounded by promises of easy happiness, we need someone to tell us as religion once did, **Memento mori**: remember that you will die, that everything ends, and that happiness comes not in denying this but in living with it.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Today, surrounded by promises of easy happiness, we need someone to tell us as religion once did, **Memento mori**: remember that you will die, that everything ends, and that happiness comes not in denying this but in living with it.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: we
> - 谓语 V: need
> - 宾语 O: someone
> - 补语 C: to tell us（不定式短语作宾补）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（过去分词） | surrounded by | 伴随状语 |
> | 状语从句 | as | 方式状语，修饰 to tell |
> | 同位语 | 冒号 | 补充说明 Memento mori |
> | 名词性从句 | that (×3) | 作 remember 的宾语 |
> | 并列结构 | not...but... | 第三个 that 从句内 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: we need someone to tell us
>   ├── 时间状语: Today
>   ├── 非谓语(过去分词): surrounded by promises of easy happiness → 伴随状语
>   └── 不定式宾补: to tell us
>         ├── 方式状语从句(as): as religion once did → 修饰 to tell
>         └── 宾语: Memento mori
>               └── 同位语(冒号): remember that...
>                     ├── 名从(that): you will die → 宾语1
>                     ├── 名从(that): everything ends → 宾语2
>                     └── 名从(that): happiness comes not in denying this but in living with it → 宾语3
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如今，在唾手可得的幸福的承诺包围之中，我们需要有人像宗教曾经所做的那样告诉我们：记住你终将死去，记住一切都会结束，而幸福不是否认这一点，而是与之共存。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 过去分词短语作状语：`surrounded by...` 逻辑主语为主句主语 `we`，表被动和状态
> 2. `as religion once did` 方式状语从句：意为"如同……一样"
> 3. `Memento mori` 拉丁语借词：上下文给出解释（冒号后），考研阅读中外来语常伴随解释
> 4. 三重宾语从句并列：第三个 `that` 从句内含 `not...but...` 结构，强调对比
