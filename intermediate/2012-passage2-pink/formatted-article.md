# Pretty in Pink: The Marketing of Gender Colours

Pretty in pink: adult women do not remember being so obsessed with the colour, yet it is **pervasive** in our young girls' lives.

It is not that pink is **intrinsically** bad, but it is ==such a tiny slice of the rainbow== and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also repeatedly and firmly **fuses** girls' identity to appearance.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It is not that pink is **intrinsically** bad, but it is ==such a tiny slice of the rainbow== and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also repeatedly and firmly **fuses** girls' identity to appearance.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 分句一 主语 S: it（指代 pink 这一话题）
> - 分句一 谓语 V: is not
> - 分句一 表语 C: that pink is intrinsically bad（that 引导的表语从句）
> - 分句二 主语 S: it（= pink）
> - 分句二 谓语 V: is
> - 分句二 表语 C: such a tiny slice of the rainbow
> - 分句三 主语 S: it（= pink）
> - 分句三 谓语 V: fuses
> - 分句三 宾语 O: girls' identity
> - 分句三 状语 A: repeatedly and firmly；to appearance（与 fuse 构成 fuse A to B）
> - 简化句: Pink is not intrinsically bad, but it is a tiny slice of the rainbow, and it fuses girls' identity to appearance.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 表语从句 | that | 作 is not 的表语，说明"并非……" |
> | 并列连词 | but | 连接前后两个对比分句 |
> | 让步状语从句 | though | 插在并列结构中作让步：尽管粉色能赞美少女时代 |
> | 介词短语 | of | 修饰 slice，说明是彩虹的"一抹" |
> | 副词 | repeatedly and firmly | 修饰 fuses，强调反复而牢固 |
> | 介词短语 | to | 与 fuse 构成 fuse A to B，"把 A 熔合到 B 上" |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 并列句: [It is not (that pink is intrinsically bad)], but [it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow] and [it also repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance]
>   ├── 分句一: It is not + 表语从句 [that pink is intrinsically bad]
>   ├── 分句二: it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow
>   │     └── of the rainbow → 修饰 slice
>   └── 分句三: it also repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance
>         ├── O: girls' identity
>         ├── A: repeatedly and firmly
>         └── to appearance → fuse A to B
>   ※ though it may celebrate girlhood in one way → 插入的让步状语从句，只修饰分句三
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：并不是说粉色本身有什么不好，而是它不过是彩虹中那么微小的一抹；尽管它从某个角度说可以赞美少女时代，但它也一再而坚定地把女孩的身份与外表熔合在一起。
>
> **考点提示**：`not that...but (that)...` 是考研高频对比结构，but 后的 that 常省略，本句 but 后直接跟完整分句。`fuse A to B` 意为"把 A 与 B 熔合"，此处为比喻用法。though 从句插在并列结构中间，须判定它只管分句三而非全句。

Then it presents that connection, even among two-year-olds, between girls as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Then it presents that connection, even among two-year-olds, between girls as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: it（指粉色所承载的观念）
> - 谓语 V: presents
> - 宾语 O: that connection
> - 宾语补足语 C: as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence
> - 状语 A: even among two-year-olds（插入状语）
> - 简化句: It presents that connection as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 介词短语 | between | 修饰 connection，说明是"女孩之间"的联系（被插入语隔开） |
> | 插入状语 | even among two-year-olds | 强调即使在两岁孩子身上也如此 |
> | 介词短语 | as（两处，并列） | 作宾语补足语，说明把这一联系呈现为什么 |
> | 并列连词 | not only...but... | 连接 innocent 与 evidence of innocence |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 主句: Then it presents that connection as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence
>   ├── S: it
>   ├── V: presents
>   ├── O: that connection
>   │     └── between girls → 修饰 connection（被 even among two-year-olds 隔开）
>   ├── 插入状语: even among two-year-olds
>   └── 宾补 C: as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：随后，它把女孩之间的这种联系——即便在两岁孩子身上也存在——不仅呈现为天真，而且呈现为天真的证据。
>
> **考点提示**：`present A as B` 意为"把 A 呈现为 B"，本句 as 短语被 not only...but... 拆成两半，须还原结构才能读懂。`between girls` 被插入语隔开，属考研常见的"修饰语后置＋分隔"干扰。

Looking around, I despaired at the **singular** lack of imagination about girls' lives and interests.

Girls' attraction to pink may seem **unavoidable**, somehow **encoded** in their DNA, but according to Jo Paoletti, an associate professor of American Studies, it is not.

Children were not **colour-coded** at all until the early 20th century: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter, since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Children were not **colour-coded** at all until the early 20th century: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter, since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 分句一 主语 S: Children
> - 分句一 谓语 V: were not colour-coded（被动语态）
> - 分句一 状语 A: at all；until the early 20th century（not...until 结构）
> - 分句二 主语 S: all babies
> - 分句二 谓语 V: wore
> - 分句二 宾语 O: white
> - 分句二 状语 A: in the era before domestic washing machines；as a practical matter
> - 分句三 主语 S: the only way of getting clothes clean
> - 分句三 谓语 V: was
> - 分句三 表语 C: to boil them（since 引导的原因状语从句中的系表结构）
> - 简化句: Children were not colour-coded until the early 20th century, because all babies wore white and the only way to clean clothes was to boil them.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 否定＋时间状语 | not...until | 修饰主句谓语，表示"直到……才" |
> | 冒号 | : | 引出对前半句的解释说明 |
> | 介词短语 | in the era before... | 作时间状语，界定"洗衣机之前"这一时限 |
> | 介词短语 | as a practical matter | 作评注性状语，说明是出于实际考虑 |
> | 原因状语从句 | since | 解释为何所有婴儿都穿白色 |
> | 非谓语（动名词） | of getting clothes clean | 修饰 way，作后置定语 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 主句: Children were not colour-coded at all until the early 20th century
>   └── 冒号 → 解释说明: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter
>         ├── S: all babies
>         ├── V: wore / O: white
>         ├── A: in the era before domestic washing machines
>         ├── A: as a practical matter
>         └── since [the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them] → 原因状语从句
>               ├── S: the only way of getting clothes clean
>               │     └── of getting clothes clean → 修饰 way，作后置定语
>               └── V: was / C: to boil them
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：直到20世纪初，孩子们才被按颜色区分开来：在家用洗衣机出现之前的年代，出于实际考虑，所有婴儿都穿白色，因为把衣服洗净的唯一办法就是把它们煮沸。
>
> **考点提示**：`not...until` 译作"直到……才"，是考研翻译高频句式，不可译成"没有……直到"。冒号后是解释而非并列，翻译时可加"即"或"因为"。`the way of doing sth` 中 `of + 动名词短语` 作后置定语修饰 way；其中 clean 是动名词 getting 的宾语补足语（get sth clean），不要误判为修饰 clothes 的形容词。

What's more, both boys and girls wore what were thought of as **gender-neutral** dresses. When nursery colours were introduced, pink was actually considered ==the more masculine colour==, a **pastel** version of red, which was associated with strength. Blue, with its **intimations** of the Virgin Mary, **constancy** and faithfulness, **symbolised** femininity.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It was not until the mid-1980s, when **amplifying** age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy, that pink fully came into its own, when it began to seem **inherently** attractive to girls, part of what defined them as female, at least for the first few critical years.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 强调句框架: It was ... that ...
> - 被强调成分: not until the mid-1980s（时间状语）
> - 主句 主语 S: pink
> - 主句 谓语 V: came into its own
> - 主句 状语 A: fully
> - 简化句: Pink fully came into its own only after the mid-1980s.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 强调句型 | It was...that... | 强调时间状语 not until the mid-1980s |
> | 定语从句 | when | 修饰 the mid-1980s，说明该年代的特征 |
> | 非谓语（动名词） | amplifying age and sex differences | 作第一个定语从句的主语 |
> | 定语从句 | when | 修饰 came into its own 所对应的时间，进一步说明粉色兴起的表现 |
> | 代词指代 | it | 指代 pink |
> | 同位补充语 | part of... | 补充说明"具天然吸引力"的性质 |
> | 名词性从句 | what | 作介词 of 的宾语，what defined them as female |
> | 介词短语 | at least for the first few critical years | 限定范围的时间状语 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 强调句: It was [not until the mid-1980s] that pink fully came into its own
>   ├── 被强调成分: not until the mid-1980s
>   │     └── 定语从句: [when amplifying age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy]
>   │           ├── S: amplifying age and sex differences（动名词短语作主语）
>   │           └── V: became / C: a dominant children's marketing strategy
>   ├── 主干: S: pink / V: came into its own / A: fully
>   └── 定语从句: [when it began to seem inherently attractive to girls, part of what defined them as female]
>         ├── S: it → 指代 pink
>         ├── V: began to seem
>         ├── C: inherently attractive to girls
>         ├── 同位补充: part of what defined them as female
>         │     └── 名词性从句: [what defined them as female] → 作 of 的宾语
>         └── A: at least for the first few critical years
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：直到20世纪80年代中期，当放大年龄与性别差异成为儿童市场营销的主导策略时，粉色才真正盛行起来；从那时起，它开始显得对女孩具有天然的吸引力，成为定义她们身为女性的因素之一，至少在最初那几个关键年份里是如此。
>
> **考点提示**：`It was not until...that...` 是强调句与 not until 叠加，按"直到……才……"翻译，不能译成"它不是……"。句中两个 when 都引导定语从句：第一个修饰 the mid-1980s，第二个修饰其前所述的时间点，须与状语从句区分。`come into its own` 意为"充分发挥价值、盛行起来"。

I had not realised how **profoundly** marketing trends **dictated** our **perception** of what is natural to kids, including our core beliefs about their psychological development.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：I had not realised how **profoundly** marketing trends **dictated** our **perception** of what is natural to kids, including our core beliefs about their psychological development.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: I
> - 谓语 V: had not realised
> - 宾语 O: how profoundly marketing trends dictated our perception of what is natural to kids（how 引导的宾语从句）
> - 从句内部 主语 S: marketing trends
> - 从句内部 谓语 V: dictated
> - 从句内部 宾语 O: our perception
> - 从句内部 状语 A: how profoundly
> - 简化句: I had not realised that marketing trends dictated our perception.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 名词性从句 | how | 作 realised 的宾语；how 修饰从句中的副词 profoundly |
> | 副词 | profoundly | 修饰 dictated，表示"深刻地" |
> | 介词短语 | of | 修饰 perception，说明认知的对象 |
> | 名词性从句 | what | 作介词 of 的宾语，相当于 the thing that is natural to kids |
> | 非谓语（现在分词） | including | 补充说明 perception 的范围 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 主句: I had not realised [宾语从句]
>   └── 宾语从句: how profoundly marketing trends dictated our perception of what is natural to kids
>         ├── A: how profoundly → 修饰 dictated
>         ├── S: marketing trends
>         ├── V: dictated
>         └── O: our perception
>               ├── of what is natural to kids → 修饰 perception
>               │     └── 名词性从句: [what is natural to kids]
>               └── including our core beliefs about their psychological development → 补充说明 perception 的范围
>                     └── about their psychological development → 修饰 beliefs
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：我此前没有意识到，营销趋势在多大程度上深刻地支配着我们对孩子天性的认知，包括我们对他们心理发展的核心看法。
>
> **考点提示**：`how + 副词` 引导的宾语从句常译作"在多大程度上……"，how 本身修饰从句内的副词。`what is natural to kids` 是介词 of 的宾语从句，不能误判为定语从句。`including...` 是分词短语作补充说明，其逻辑主语是 our perception，不是 I。

Take the **toddler**. I assumed that phase was something experts developed after years of research into children's behaviour: wrong.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Trade publications **counselled** department stores that, in order to increase sales, they should create a “third stepping stone” between infant wear and older kids’ clothes.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Trade publications
> - 谓语 V: counselled
> - 间接宾语 O1: department stores
> - 直接宾语 O2: that they should create a “third stepping stone” between infant wear and older kids’ clothes（that 引导的宾语从句）
> - 从句内部 主语 S: they（指代 department stores）
> - 从句内部 谓语 V: should create
> - 从句内部 宾语 O: a “third stepping stone”
> - 简化句: Trade publications counselled department stores that they should create a stepping stone.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 名词性从句 | that | 作 counselled 的宾语从句，不是定语从句 |
> | 目的状语（插入） | in order to | 插在 that 与从句主语之间，说明"为了增加销量" |
> | 介词短语 | between...and... | 修饰 stepping stone，说明其所处位置 |
> | 引号比喻 | “third stepping stone” | 指介于婴儿装与大孩子服装之间的过渡品类 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 主句: Trade publications counselled department stores [宾语从句]
>   ├── S: Trade publications
>   ├── V: counselled
>   ├── O1: department stores
>   └── 宾语从句: that [in order to increase sales] they should create a “third stepping stone” between infant wear and older kids’ clothes
>         ├── 目的状语（插入）: in order to increase sales
>         ├── S: they → 指代 department stores
>         ├── V: should create
>         ├── O: a “third stepping stone”
>         └── between infant wear and older kids’ clothes → 修饰 stepping stone
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：行业刊物建议百货商店：为了增加销量，应在婴儿装和大孩子的衣服之间设置一块"第三级踏脚石"。
>
> **考点提示**：`counsel sb that...` 中 that 从句是宾语从句，不修饰 department stores；目的状语 `in order to increase sales` 插在 that 与从句主语之间造成"分隔"，是考研常见干扰项。`counsel` 在此作动词"建议"，与 advise 同义。

It was only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term that it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It was only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term that it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 强调句框架: It was ... that ...
> - 被强调成分: only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term（时间状语从句）
> - 主句 主语 S: it（指代 toddler 所代表的说法/阶段）
> - 主句 谓语 V: evolved
> - 主句 状语 A: into a broadly accepted developmental stage
> - 简化句: Toddler evolved into a developmental stage only after the word became common.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 强调句型 | It was...that... | 强调时间状语 only after... |
> | 时间状语从句 | after | 说明"只有在……之后"，从句内 "toddler" 作主语 |
> | 介词短语 | into | 与 evolve 搭配，表示"演变为" |
> | 形容词短语 | broadly accepted | 作前置定语修饰 developmental stage |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 强调句: It was [only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term] that it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage
>   ├── 被强调成分: only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term
>   │     └── 时间状语从句: after [“toddler” became a common shoppers’ term]
>   │           ├── S: “toddler” → 指"学步儿童"这一说法
>   │           └── V: became / C: a common shoppers’ term
>   └── 主干: S: it → 指代 toddler / V: evolved / A: into a broadly accepted developmental stage
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：只有在"toddler（学步儿童）"成为购物者常用的说法之后，它才演变为一个被广泛接受的发展阶段。
>
> **考点提示**：这是 `It was...that...` 强调句强调时间状语，判别方法是去掉 It was 和 that 后句子仍完整。与第2段的 `not until` 强调句同源，可对照记忆。`evolve into` 意为"演变为"，into 短语作结果状语。
