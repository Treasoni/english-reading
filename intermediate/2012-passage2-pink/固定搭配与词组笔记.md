# Pretty in Pink: The Marketing of Gender Colours 固定搭配与词组

> [!abstract] 说明
> 本笔记整理本篇文章中的固定搭配、词义和考研语境。句法结构详见 [[grammar-notes]]；跨篇章汇总见 [[固定搭配与词组笔记]]。

### 呈现、归因与身份表达

| 搭配 | 含义 | 文章语境 |
|------|------|------|
| **fuse A to B** | 把 A 与 B 熔合/紧密联系起来 | pink repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance |
| **present A as B** | 把 A 呈现为 B | present that connection as innocent / as evidence of innocence |
| **be encoded in** | 被编码在……中；隐含于……中 | attraction somehow encoded in their DNA |
| **be associated with** | 与……联系在一起 | red was associated with strength |
| **symbolise femininity** | 象征女性气质 | Blue ... symbolised femininity |
| **define A as B** | 将 A 界定为 B | what defined them as female |

> [!tip] 📌 考研核心考点
> `present A as B` 与 `define A as B` 都是“把 A 视为/呈现为 B”，但 `present` 强调呈现方式，`define` 强调界定身份或性质。

### 状态转化与结果表达

| 搭配 | 含义 | 文章语境 |
|------|------|------|
| **come into one's own** | 开始充分发挥作用；崭露头角；真正盛行 | pink fully came into its own |
| **evolve into** | 逐渐演变为 | it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage |
| **turn out** | 结果是；后来发现 | Turns out ... it was popularised as a marketing trick |
| **become a common term** | 成为常用说法 | “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term |
| **be popularised as** | 作为……推广开来 | it was popularised as a marketing trick |

> [!note] 语义辨析
> `evolve into` 强调渐进式变化；`come into one's own` 强调某事物开始显现价值或影响力；`turns out` 用来引出后来发现的事实。

### 市场营销与利润表达

| 搭配 | 含义 | 文章语境 |
|------|------|------|
| **boost profits** | 提高利润 | a way to boost profits |
| **segment a market** | 细分市场 | one of the easiest ways to segment a market |
| **magnify differences** | 放大差异 | magnify gender differences |
| **invent differences** | 凭空制造差异 | invent them where they did not previously exist |
| **a marketing strategy** | 营销策略 | a dominant children's marketing strategy |
| **a marketing trick** | 营销伎俩/营销手段 | popularised as a marketing trick |
| **a sure-fire way to do sth** | 做某事的万无一失/稳赚不赔的方法 | a sure-fire way to boost profits |
| **in order to do sth** | 为了做某事 | in order to increase sales |

> [!warning] 语义色彩
> `segment` 本身是中性词“细分”；但 `magnify` 与 `invent ... where they did not previously exist` 在本文中带有批评色彩，暗示商家为盈利而放大甚至制造性别差异。

### 介词与名词搭配

| 搭配 | 含义 | 文章语境 |
|------|------|------|
| **be obsessed with** | 痴迷于 | obsessed with the colour |
| **a lack of imagination about** | 缺乏对……的想象力 | a lack of imagination about girls' lives and interests |
| **research into** | 对……的研究 | research into children's behaviour |
| **as a practical matter** | 出于实际考虑；实际上 | all babies wore white as a practical matter |
| **at least for** | 至少在……期间/范围内 | at least for the first few critical years |
| **be thought of as** | 被认为是 | gender-neutral dresses |

### 词义辨析：文章中的高频词

| 词语 | 本文义项 | 易混/易错提醒 |
|------|------|------|
| **pervasive** | 无处不在的；遍布的 | 不等于 merely popular，强调渗透范围广 |
| **intrinsically** | 本质上；固有地 | 与 `inherently` 近义，都可表示“内在地” |
| **singular** | 异常的；非同寻常的 | 本文不是“单数的” |
| **pastel** | 淡雅柔和的（颜色） | 作名词或形容词均可 |
| **intimations of** | 令人联想到……的暗示/意味 | `intimation` 不只表示“正式通知” |
| **constancy** | 恒心；坚定不移 | 与 faithfulness 并列，表示稳定的品质 |
| **toddler** | 学步儿童；幼儿 | 本文强调该词和发展阶段的营销建构 |
| **counsel** | 建议；劝告（动词） | `counsel sb that...` 中不是“咨询”名词 |
| **sure-fire** | 万无一失的；稳赚不赔的 | 常修饰 way、method、success |
