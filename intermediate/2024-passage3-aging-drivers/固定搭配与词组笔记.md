# 2024 Passage 3 固定搭配与词组

> [!note] 使用说明
> 本笔记只收录**固定搭配、短语与主题词**，纯语法结构见 [[intermediate/2024-passage3-aging-drivers/grammar-notes.md|2024 Passage 3 语法整理]]。
> 每条均给原文语境，便于回原文核对；标注题号者，表示该搭配是此题的**直接依据**或**干扰源**。

---

## 一、主题词与核心概念

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **aging drivers** | 年长驾驶者、高龄司机 | keeping unsafe **aging drivers** off the road |
| **older drivers** | 年长驾驶者（同义替换） | keeping **older drivers** safe |
| **driving impairment** | 驾驶能力受损 | clear evidence of substantial **driving impairment** |
| **driving privileges** | 驾驶资格、驾驶特权 | advice to discontinue **driving privileges** |
| **license renewal** | 驾照续期 | report in person for **license renewal** |
| **in-person renewal** | 本人到场续期 | mandatory **in-person renewal** |
| **vision test** | 视力测试 | Passing **vision tests** also produced a similar decline |
| **fatal crash** | 致命车祸、致死性撞车 | a 31 percent reduction in **fatal crashes** |
| **risk manager** | 风险经理 | a former **risk manager** Anne M. Menke |
| **gerontologist** | 老年学家（geronto- 老年 + -logist 学者） | including **gerontologists**, highway administration officials |
| **primary care provider** | 基层医疗服务提供者、全科医生 | **Primary care providers** have their hands full |
| **confidentiality** | 保密（义务） | a breach of **confidentiality** |
| **liability**（liable 派生） | （法律）责任 | There could be **liability** and penalties |
| **penalty** | 处罚、罚则 | liability and **penalties** |
| **self-driving car** | 自动驾驶汽车 | **self-driving cars** won't solve the problems of crashes |
| **algorithm** | 算法 | would require too many **algorithms** |
| **car pedal** | 汽车踏板 | reach **car pedals** easily |
| **seat setting** | 座椅（位置）设置 | haven't changed their **seat settings** |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 全文概念链：**turn over the keys**（问题起点：劝老人交钥匙）→ **impairment / privileges**（医学判断）→ **report / privacy**（制度分歧）→ **piecemeal**（缺乏统筹）→ **in-person renewal / vision test**（有效做法）→ **algorithms**（自动驾驶的局限）→ **drive comfortably**（出路）。五道题正好沿这条链排布。

> [!warning] 熟词僻义
> `privilege` 在此**不是"特权、优待"**，而是法律文本中"（被许可的）资格"：`driving privileges` = 驾驶资格，被吊销即 `lose one's driving privileges`。同类：`a license privilege` 执照许可。

## 二、动词短语

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **turn over the keys** | 交出钥匙（引申：放弃驾驶） | it's time to **turn over the keys** |
| **keep ... off the road** | 使……不上路、让……远离道路 | **keeping** unsafe aging drivers **off the road** |
| **advise sb that ...** | 建议某人（某事） | **advises physicians that** ... |
| **advise sb to do** | 建议某人做某事 | —（本文以 `advice to do` 名词形式出现） |
| **discontinue doing sth** | 停止做某事 | advice to **discontinue driving** |
| **notify sb of sth** | 通知某人某事 | **notify** the Department of Motor Vehicles |
| **require sb to do** | 要求某人做某事 | Some states **require physicians to report** |
| **mandate sth** | 强制要求某事（正式用语） | allow but do not **mandate** reports |
| **address a problem** | 处理、应对问题 | the difficulties are **addressed** piecemeal |
| **follow through with** | 对……持续跟进、负责到底 | may not be able to **follow through with** patients |
| **have trouble doing** | 做某事有困难 | patients who **have trouble driving** |
| **turn one's head** | 转头 | they can't **turn their heads** |
| **pose a danger / threat** | 构成危险／威胁 | dangers **posed by** all those human drivers |
| **improve safety** | 提升安全性 | do more to **improve safety** |
| **meet demand** | 满足需求 | （本文未出现，与 `combining` 段同属"需求—供给"语义场） |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 33 题 [B] `need to be well coordinated` 的直接依据是 `are addressed piecemeal by different professions`：`piecemeal`（零散地）↔ `coordinated`（统筹协调）构成**反义改写**。看到"处理方式"类动词（`address / tackle / handle`），立刻留意其后是"分散"还是"统一"。

> [!warning] 易错点
> `follow through with patients` 不可译为"跟随病人"，`follow through` 强调"**把承诺的事做到底**"；`have trouble driving` 中 `driving` 是动名词，不能换成 `to drive`。

## 三、介词与名词搭配

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **a threat to sb/sth** | 对……的威胁 | a strong **threat to** patient and public safety |
| **a reduction in sth** | 在……方面的减少 | a 31 percent **reduction in** fatal crashes |
| **a decline in sth** | 在……方面的下降 | a similar **decline in** fatal crashes |
| **a breach of sth** | 对……的违反 | a **breach of** confidentiality |
| **evidence on sth** | 关于……的证据 | better **evidence on** what makes drivers unsafe |
| **in accordance with** | 依照、按照 | does not act **in accordance with** state laws |
| **in person** | 本人亲自 | report **in person** |
| **in situations where** | 在……情况下 | **in situations where** clear evidence ... implies a threat |
| **be associated with** | 与……相关 | **was associated with** a 31 percent reduction |
| **no benefit from doing** | 做某事没有益处 | there appeared to be **no benefit from combining** the two |
| **as long as** | 只要 | **As long as** there are other cars on the roads |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `a reduction in` 与 `a reduction of` 的区别：`in` 后接**被减少的指标**（事故率），`of` 后接**减少的幅度**（31 percent）。本文 `a 31 percent reduction in fatal crashes` 两者并存，翻译顺序为"致命车祸减少 31%"。

## 四、习语与表达

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **a complete life-changer** | 彻底改变人生的事 | "It's **a complete life-changer**" when someone stops driving |
| **have one's hands full** | 忙得不可开交 | Primary care providers **have their hands full** |
| **can't afford to** | 负担不起（后省略动词） | don't see eye doctors or **can't afford to** |
| **there's not a ...** | 根本没有…… | **There's not a** National Institute of Older Driver Studies |
| **go far enough**（联动） | 力度足够 | 见 [[intermediate/2024-passage2-uk-timber/固定搭配与词组笔记.md\|2024 Passage 2]] |
| **too many + 复数** | 多到不现实（委婉否定） | would require **too many algorithms** |
| **drive comfortably** | 舒适地驾驶 | cars that a 90-year-old can **drive comfortably** |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `life-changer` 不是"改变生活的人"，而是"**改变人生的事物/事件**"。`-changer` 类合成词还有 `game-changer`（改变格局的事物）、`deal-breaker`（导致谈判破裂的因素），考研阅读中常作态度标记。

## 五、易混辨析

| 词对 | 区别 | 原文体现 |
|------|------|----------|
| **impairment / damage** | `impairment` 多指**功能减退**（视力、听力、驾驶能力），`damage` 多指**物理损坏** | substantial driving **impairment** |
| **mandate / require** | 二者都表"强制"，`mandate` 更正式，常见于法律、政策文本 | do not **mandate** reports；**require** physicians to report |
| **allow / permit** | 同义，`allow` 更中性；`permit` 多用于正式许可 | others **allow** but do not mandate |
| **decline / reduction** | `decline` 强调**自然下降趋势**，`reduction` 强调**被减少的结果** | a similar **decline**；a 31 percent **reduction** |
| **piecemeal / partial** | `piecemeal` 强调"**零散、不成体系**"，带批评色彩；`partial` 强调"部分" | are addressed **piecemeal** |
| **counsel / consult** | `counsel` = 建议、劝告（动词）；`consult` = 咨询、请教 | she **counseled** |
| **imply / infer** | `imply` 是**说话人隐含**，`infer` 是**听话人推断** | **implies** a strong threat |
| **desirable / desired** | `desirable` = 值得做的、可取的；`desired` = 被想要的 | it is **desirable** and ethical to notify |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> `imply` 与 `infer` 的区分是考研高频陷阱：原文说"证据 `implies` 威胁"（证据本身**意味着**威胁），题目若写"the author `infers` ..."，方向就反了。方向题一律先确认主语是"发出者"还是"接收者"。

## 六、写作可迁移句

> 以下句式可直接用于考研写作的"现象—分析—建议"类话题。

| 功能 | 可迁移句式 | 原文原型 |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 提出核心难题 | One of the biggest challenges in doing X is convincing sb that ... | One of the biggest challenges in keeping unsafe aging drivers off the road is convincing them that it's time to turn over the keys |
| 指出制度分歧 | Some ... require ..., others allow ..., while a few consider ... | Some states require physicians to report, others allow but do not mandate reports, while a few consider a report a breach of confidentiality |
| 指出缺乏统筹 | Part of the problem is that the difficulties are addressed piecemeal by ... | Part of the problem ... is that the difficulties are addressed piecemeal by different professions |
| 引用研究结论 | X was associated with a N percent reduction in Y, according to one study | Mandatory in-person renewal was associated with a 31 percent reduction in fatal crashes |
| 限定方案边界 | As long as there are ..., X won't solve the problems of ... | As long as there are other cars on the roads, self-driving cars won't solve the problems of crashes |
| 提出长远目标 | If we're going to have ..., we need ... that ... | If we're going to have 100-year lives, we need cars that a 90-year-old can drive comfortably |

> [!note] 补充说明
> 这六句共同构成一套"**问题—分歧—归因—证据—限定—出路**"的论证链，可直接套用到"老龄化社会""技术治理""公共安全"等话题，且每句都带有考研常考的**让步、对比、弱因果**结构。
