# 2024 Passage 3

One of the biggest challenges in keeping unsafe **aging drivers** off the road is convincing them that it's time to **turn over the keys**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：One of the biggest challenges in keeping unsafe **aging drivers** off the road is convincing them that it's time to **turn over the keys**.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: One of the biggest challenges
> - 后置定语: in keeping unsafe aging drivers off the road
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: convincing them that it's time to turn over the keys
> - 宾语从句 O: it's time to turn over the keys
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | in keeping ... | challenges |
> | 动名词短语作介词宾语 | keeping ... off the road | in |
> | 动名词短语作表语 | convincing ... | is |
> | 宾语从句（that 可省） | that | convincing |
> | 形式主语 | it | is time |
> | 不定式作后置定语 | to turn over the keys | time |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔One (of the biggest challenges)〕+ is + 〔convincing them ...〕
>   ├── 后置定语: in keeping 〔unsafe aging drivers〕off the road → 修饰 challenges
>   ├── 表语（动名词短语）: convincing + 〔them〕+ 〔that 宾语从句〕
>   │     └── 宾从: 〔it〕+ is + 〔time〕
>   │           └── 后置定语: to turn over the keys → 修饰 time
>   └── 形式对称: keeping（"使……远离"）↔ convincing（"说服"）→ 难在落实
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：让不安全的高龄驾驶者远离道路，最大的挑战之一就是说服他们"该交出车钥匙了"。
>
> **考点提示**：① `one of + 复数名词` 作主语，谓语用单数 `is`；② `challenges in doing sth` = "在……方面遇到的挑战"，`in` 引出领域；③ 主语 `keeping ...` 与表语 `convincing ...` **同为动名词短语**，构成"问题—难点"的形式对称；④ `turn over the keys` 是习语"交出车钥匙"，引申为"放弃驾驶权"，第 31 题 [C] `can be a tough task to complete` 即此句的同义改写。

> **原句**："The American Medical Association advises physicians that 'in situations where clear evidence of **substantial driving impairment** implies a strong threat to patient and public safety, and where the physician's advice to discontinue driving privileges is ignored, it is desirable and ethical to notify the Department of Motor Vehicles,' " Menke wrote.

> **参考译文**：她说，要避开所有这些人类驾驶者带来的危险，将需要过多的算法。
>
> **考点提示**：① `posed` 是**过去分词作后置定语**，不是谓语；若误当谓语，会出现"两个谓语"的结构错误；② `Avoiding dangers` 是动名词短语作主语，视为单数；③ `would + too many` 构成**委婉否定**——"算法多到不现实"即"自动驾驶解决不了"，这是态度题的判断依据；④ `she said` 后置表明观点归属 **Dugan**，不是 Menke；⑤ 第 35 题 [A] `upgrading self-driving vehicles` 与本句态度**正相反**，可直接排除。

But we need to do more to improve safety, said Dugan. "If we're going to have 100-year lives, we need cars that a 90-year-old can drive comfortably."

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**："If we're going to have 100-year lives, we need cars that a 90-year-old can drive comfortably."
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 条件状语从句 A: If we're going to have 100-year lives
> - 主语 S: we
> - 谓语 V: need
> - 宾语 O: cars
> - 定语从句: that a 90-year-old can drive comfortably
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 条件状语从句 | If | 主句 |
> | be going to 表将来 | we're going to have | — |
> | 复合形容词作定语 | 100-year | lives |
> | 定语从句 | that | cars |
> | 复合名词作从句主语 | a 90-year-old | can drive |
> | 方式副词 | comfortably | drive |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔we〕+ need + 〔cars〕
>   ├── 条件状语从句: If + 〔we〕+ are going to have + 〔100-year lives〕→ 前提
>   └── 定语从句: that + 〔a 90-year-old〕+ can drive + 〔comfortably〕→ 修饰 cars
>         ※ that 在从句中作 drive 的宾语（drive cars comfortably）
> ```
>
> **参考译文**："如果我们要活到 100 岁，就需要 90 岁的人也能舒适驾驶的汽车。"
>
> **考点提示**：① `a 90-year-old` 在此作**名词**"90 岁的人"，与前置定语用法（`a 90-year-old driver`）区分；② 定语从句 `that a 90-year-old can drive comfortably` 中 `that` 是关系代词，作 `drive` 的**宾语**，还原为 `a 90-year-old can drive cars comfortably`；③ 条件句 `If we're going to have 100-year lives` 提出"人类寿命延长"的前提，是作者立论的现实基础；④ 第 35 题 [B] `developing senior-friendly cars` 即本句的同义改写，[D] `adjusting the age limit for drivers` 属**无据延伸**——文中从未提及年龄限制。
