# The US Jury System 语法要点整理

> [!note]
> 本笔记根据文章原文归纳，未使用独立语法讲义；例句均取自本文或保留本文原有结构。

---

### 名词性从句：同位语从句 (Noun Clauses: Appositive Clauses)

文章用多个 `that` 从句具体解释 **the principles** 的内容。判断同位语从句时，要看从句是否说明抽象名词的具体内容，而不是修饰某个名词。

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 遇到 `the principle(s) that...`、`the claim that...`、`the requirement that...`，先把从句整体括起来，再回到主句寻找谓语和主干。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **the principles that + 从句** | 说明 principles 的具体内容 | including the principles **that all citizens ... are equally competent**; **that jurors should be selected randomly**; and ... |
| **the claim that + 从句** | 说明 claim 的具体内容 | This practice was justified by the claim **that women were needed at home**. |
| **the requirement that + 从句** | 说明 requirement 的具体内容 | The Supreme Court extended the requirement **that juries be representative of all parts of the community**. |

> [!warning] 易错点
> 第一段中多个 `that` 从句由分号和 `and` 并列，共同受 `the principles` 支配；不要把每个 `that` 都误判为定语从句。

---

### 从句：定语从句 (Attributive Clauses)

定语从句修饰前面的名词，为其增加限定或补充信息。本文中的 `who` 从句修饰 `all citizens`。

| 类型 | 引导词 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 限制性定语从句 | **who** | all citizens **who meet minimal qualifications of age and literacy** |

> [!note]
> `who meet ...` 的先行词是复数名词 `citizens`，因此谓语使用 `meet`；从句内部的 `of age and literacy` 修饰 `qualifications`。

---

### 从句：让步与条件状语从句 (Adverbial Clauses)

文章通过 `although` 引出历史背景中的让步信息，通过 `unless` 表达女性只有主动申请才不会被免除陪审义务的条件。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **Although + 从句, 主句** | 表示“尽管……但是……” | **Although** the Supreme Court ... had prohibited intentional racial discrimination ..., the practice ... provided a convenient way around ... |
| **unless + 从句** | 表示“除非……”，可理解为 `if ... not` | Several states automatically exempted women from jury duty **unless** they personally asked ... |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> `Although` 从句通常是让步背景，主句才是作者推进论述的核心；`unless` 要结合主句一起翻译，避免误解为一般的时间条件。

---

### 时态 (Tenses)

本文以一般现在时说明制度原则，以一般过去时叙述改革历史，并用过去完成时标明禁令先于后文规避行为发生。

| 时态 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| 一般现在时 | 陈述制度原则、普遍事实 | Many Americans **regard** the jury system ...; verdicts **should represent** the conscience of the community. |
| 一般过去时 | 叙述过去发生的法律或制度变化 | Congress **passed** the Jury Selection and Service Act. |
| 过去完成时 | 表示过去某一动作发生在另一过去动作之前 | Although the Supreme Court ... **had prohibited** intentional racial discrimination ..., the practice ... provided ... |
| `until` 时间结构 | 表示某状态持续到某个时间点 | The system failed to regularly include women on juries **until** the mid-20th century. |

> [!note]
> `as early as the 1880 case` 强调时间之早；它与 `had prohibited` 共同表明最高法院的禁令早于后来对制度漏洞的描述。

---

### 语态：被动语态 (Voice: Passive Voice)

被动语态突出动作承受者，本文大量用于描述陪审员被选出、被剥夺权利或被免除义务的制度安排。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **should be + 过去分词** | 表示应当被…… | jurors **should be selected** randomly |
| **should be denied** | 表示不应被剥夺 | no citizen **should be denied** the right to serve on a jury |
| **be said to + 动词原形** | `say` 的被动表达，表示“据说……” | The jury **is also said to be** the best surviving example ... |
| **be limited to** | 表示“被限制在……” | jury duty **was limited to** persons of supposedly superior ... |
| **be + 过去分词** | 说明制度动作的承受关系 | women were ...; jurors ... **be selected** at random |

> [!warning] 翻译注意
> 中文翻译可根据语境灵活转换为主动表达。例如 `jurors should be selected` 可译为“应随机选出陪审员”，不必机械译成“陪审员应被选出”。

---

### 非谓语动词 (Non-finite Verbs)

非谓语动词压缩了动作关系，是本文长句信息密集的重要来源。分析时应先判断其逻辑主语，再判断主动或被动。

| 形式 | 功能 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **including + 名词/从句** | 现在分词短语，补充说明前面的 democratic values | democratic values, **including the principles that ...** |
| **to serve / to govern** | 不定式，表示目的或动作内容 | competent **to serve** on juries; representatives **to govern** for them |
| **rather than + V-ing** | 表示“而不是做……”的对比 | take turns governing themselves, **rather than electing** representatives |
| **of + V-ing** | 动名词作介词宾语，说明行为内容 | the practice **of selecting** so-called elite ... juries |
| **ushering in + 名词** | 现在分词短语，补充说明前句结果 | passed the Act, **ushering in** a new era ... |
| **to have + 宾语 + 过去分词** | 使役结构，表示“让某物被……” | asked **to have their names included** on the jury list |
| **for + V-ing** | 动名词短语作后置修饰 | procedures **for selecting** male and female jurors |

> [!tip] 结构辨析
> `have + 宾语 + 过去分词` 中，宾语与过去分词是被动关系：`their names` 被 `included`。不要把 `included` 当作主句谓语。

---

### 虚拟语气：要求类 that 从句 (Subjunctive Mood)

在 `requirement that ...` 中，`that` 从句表达要求或规范内容，`juries be representative` 使用动词原形 `be`，体现正式英语中的要求类虚拟结构。

> [!tip] 识别方法
> 表示要求、建议、命令、必要性的名词或动词后，`that` 从句有时使用“主语 + 动词原形”；本文的关键词是 `requirement`。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **requirement that + 主语 + 动词原形** | 表达规范或要求 | the requirement **that juries be representative** of all parts of the community |

---

### 倒装与强调：It is/was not until ... that ...

`it was not until ... that ...` 是强调句型，强调时间状语“直到……才……”。还原后可理解为 `A majority of states did not make women eligible for jury duty until the 1940s.`

> [!tip] 考试提示
> 看到 `not until` 时，先锁定“直到……才……”的时间逻辑；强调句中的 `that` 不承担普通从句连接词的名词修饰功能。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **It was not until + 时间 + that + 从句** | 强调动作发生的时间 | **it was not until the 1940s that** a majority of states made women eligible for jury duty |

---

### 宾语补足语与并列谓语 (Object Complements & Coordination)

本文多次使用“动词 + 宾语 + 补语”结构，并通过 `and` 连接两个谓语或两个不定式结构。识别宾语补足语有助于快速还原句子主干。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **regard A as B** | 认为 A 是 B；`as B` 作宾语补足语 | Many Americans **regard the jury system as a concrete expression** of democratic values. |
| **make A + 形容词** | 使 A 处于某种状态 | a majority of states **made women eligible** for jury duty |
| **declare A to be B** | 宣布 A 是 B；`to be B` 作宾语补足语 | The Taylor decision **declared sex discrimination ... to be unconstitutional**. |
| **order A to do B** | 命令 A 做 B；`to do B` 作宾语补足语 | ordered **states to use** the same procedures ... |
| **abolish A and require B** | `and` 连接同一主语的并列谓语 | This law **abolished** special educational requirements ... **and required** them to be selected ... |

> [!note]
> `require + 宾语 + to do` 与 `order + 宾语 + to do` 结构相似；若不定式为被动形式，则使用 `to be + 过去分词`，如 `required them to be selected`。

---

### 介词与连词：对比、范围和原因 (Prepositions & Conjunctions)

介词短语和连接词共同构成文章的逻辑骨架，尤其要注意 `rather than`、`on account of`、`from a cross section of` 等结构的整体含义。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **rather than** | 表示对比选择，而不是 | direct **rather than representative** democracy |
| **on account of** | 表示原因，由于 | denied ... **on account of** race, religion, sex, or national origin |
| **be entitled to** | 表示有权享有 | defendants are **entitled to** trial by their peers |
| **from a cross section of** | 表示从具有代表性的各类群体中 | selected ... **from a cross section of** the entire community |
| **through + 时间段** | 表示持续到某一时间段 | it kept juries unrepresentative of women **through the 1960s** |

> [!note]
> `not just the letter of the law` 与前面的 `the conscience of the community` 构成“不是仅仅……而是还……”的语义对比，理解时不要漏译 `not just`。
