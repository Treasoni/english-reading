# Data Insecurity: Corporate America's New Challenge

==It never rains but it pours.== Just as bosses and boards have finally **sorted out** their worst **accounting and compliance troubles**, and improved their feeble **corporation governance**, a new problem threatens to earn them—especially in America—the sort of **nasty headlines** that inevitably lead to **heads rolling** in the **executive suite**: **data insecurity**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Just as bosses and boards have finally **sorted out** their worst **accounting and compliance troubles**, and improved their feeble **corporation governance**, a new problem threatens to earn them—especially in America—the sort of **nasty headlines** that inevitably lead to **heads rolling** in the **executive suite**: **data insecurity**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: a new problem
> - 谓语 V: threatens to earn（threaten to do 结构）
> - 宾语 O: them（间接宾语）；the sort of nasty headlines（直接宾语）
> - 状语 A: Just as bosses and boards have finally sorted out...（时间状语从句）
> - 同位语 C: data insecurity（冒号后，解释 headlines 的内容）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句 | Just as | 时间状语，说明新问题出现的时间背景 |
> | 插入语 | 破折号 | especially in America，补充强调地点范围 |
> | 定语从句 | that | 修饰 the sort of nasty headlines |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to earn | 与 threaten 搭配，threaten to do |
> | 介词短语 | in the executive suite | 与 lead to 的宾语 heads rolling 搭配 |
> | 同位语 | 冒号 | data insecurity → 解释 headlines 的具体内容 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: a new problem threatens to earn them the sort of nasty headlines: data insecurity
>   ├── 时间状从(Just as): Just as bosses and boards have finally sorted out their worst accounting
>   │     and compliance troubles, and improved their feeble corporation governance
>   │     ├── 分句1: bosses and boards have finally sorted out their worst accounting and compliance troubles
>   │     │     ├── 主语 S: bosses and boards
>   │     │     ├── 谓语 V: have sorted out
>   │     │     └── 宾语 O: their worst accounting and compliance troubles
>   │     └── 分句2(and): and improved their feeble corporation governance
>   │           ├── 主语 S: (省略 bosses and boards)
>   │           ├── 谓语 V: improved
>   │           └── 宾语 O: their feeble corporation governance
>   ├── 主语 S: a new problem
>   ├── 插入语: —especially in America—
>   ├── 谓语 V: threatens to earn
>   ├── 宾语 O: them（间宾）/ the sort of nasty headlines（直宾）
>   │     └── 定从(that): that inevitably lead to heads rolling in the executive suite → 修饰 headlines
>   └── 同位语(冒号): data insecurity → 解释 headlines 所指
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：正当老板们和董事会终于解决了最棘手的会计与合规问题、改善了薄弱的企业治理之际，一个新问题又威胁着让他们——尤其是在美国——登上那种不可避免会导致高管层人事变动的恶劣头条：数据不安全。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `Just as` 引导时间状语从句，主句为 a new problem threatens to earn them...，先定位主句再理解从句
> 2. 破折号插入语 especially in America 将谓语与宾语分隔，阅读时先跳过
> 3. 冒号引出同位语 data insecurity，具体解释"恶劣头条"的内容
> 4. `threaten to do`（威胁要做某事）、`lead to`（导致）为高频搭配

Left, until now, to odd, low-level IT staff to **put right**, and seen as a concern only of **data-rich industries** such as banking, telecoms and air travel, **information protection** is now **high on the boss's agenda** in businesses of every variety.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Left, until now, to odd, low-level IT staff to **put right**, and seen as a concern only of **data-rich industries** such as banking, telecoms and air travel, **information protection** is now **high on the boss's agenda** in businesses of every variety.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: information protection
> - 谓语 V: is（系动词）
> - 表语 C: high on the boss's agenda
> - 状语 A: now / in businesses of every variety
> - 非谓语（过去分词）状语: Left... / seen...（逻辑主语为 information protection，表被动）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（过去分词） | Left | 句首状语，表被动"被留给……处理" |
> | 非谓语（过去分词） | seen | 与 Left 并列，表"被视为……" |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to put right | 与 left...to 搭配，leave sth. to sb. to do |
> | 介词短语 | until now | 时间状语，修饰 Left |
> | 介词短语 | of data-rich industries | 后置定语，修饰 concern |
> | 介词短语 | such as | 举例说明 data-rich industries |
> | 介词短语 | on | 与 high 搭配，high on the agenda |
> | 介词短语 | in businesses of every variety | 范围状语，修饰主句 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: information protection is now high on the boss's agenda in businesses of every variety
>   ├── 过去分词状语(Left): Left, until now, to odd, low-level IT staff to put right
>   │     ├── 时间状语: until now
>   │     ├── 介短(to): to odd, low-level IT staff → 引出处理对象
>   │     └── 不定式(to put right): to put right → 目的
>   ├── 过去分词状语(seen): seen as a concern only of data-rich industries
>   │     └── 介短(as): as a concern ... → 引出认知内容
>   │           └── 介短(of): of data-rich industries such as banking, telecoms and air travel → 修饰 concern
>   ├── 主语 S: information protection
>   ├── 系动词 V: is
>   ├── 表语 C: high on the boss's agenda
>   │     └── 介短(on): on the boss's agenda → 与 high 搭配
>   └── 范围状语: in businesses of every variety
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：此前，数据安全问题一直被留给处理杂务的底层 IT 员工去处理，并被视为只有银行业、电信业和航空业等数据密集型行业才需要担心的问题；而现在，信息保护已成为各类企业老板议程上的头等大事。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 过去分词 Left / seen 作状语，逻辑主语为 information protection，表被动——判断非谓语逻辑主语与语态是高频考点
> 2. `leave sth. to sb. to do`："把某事留给某人去做"
> 3. `be seen as`："被视为"；`high on the agenda`："议程上的头等大事"

Several massive **leakages** of customer and employee data this year—from organizations as diverse as Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley—have left managers hurriedly peering into their **intricate IT systems** and business processes in search of **potential vulnerabilities**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Several massive **leakages** of customer and employee data this year—from organizations as diverse as Time Warner, the American defense contractor Science Applications International Corp and even the University of California, Berkeley—have left managers hurriedly peering into their **intricate IT systems** and business processes in search of **potential vulnerabilities**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Several massive leakages of customer and employee data this year
> - 谓语 V: have left
> - 宾语 O: managers
> - 宾语补足语 C: hurriedly peering into their intricate IT systems and business processes（leave sb. doing 结构）
> - 插入语: from organizations as diverse as Time Warner, SAIC, and UC Berkeley
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | of customer and employee data | 后置定语，修饰 leakages |
> | 插入语 | 破折号 | from organizations... → 举例说明泄露来源机构 |
> | 比较结构 | as...as | as diverse as，说明机构类型的多样性 |
> | 非谓语（现在分词） | peering | 宾语补足语（leave sb. doing） |
> | 副词 | hurriedly | 修饰 peering，表匆忙 |
> | 介词短语 | into | 与 peer 搭配，peer into 仔细检查 |
> | 介词短语 | in search of | 目的状语，修饰 peering |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Several massive leakages ... have left managers hurriedly peering into their intricate IT systems and business processes
>   ├── 主语 S: Several massive leakages of customer and employee data this year
>   │     └── 介短(of): of customer and employee data → 修饰 leakages
>   ├── 插入语(破折号): —from organizations as diverse as Time Warner, SAIC, and UC Berkeley—
>   │     └── 比较(as...as): as diverse as Time Warner, ... → 说明多样性
>   ├── 谓语 V: have left
>   ├── 宾语 O: managers
>   └── 宾补(现在分词): hurriedly peering into their intricate IT systems and business processes
>         ├── 介短(into): into their intricate IT systems and business processes → 与 peer 搭配
>         └── 目的状语(in search of): in search of potential vulnerabilities
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：今年发生的多起大规模客户和员工数据泄露事件——涉及时代华纳、美国国防承包商科学应用国际公司，甚至加州大学伯克利分校等形形色色的机构——让管理者们匆忙审视其错综复杂的 IT 系统和业务流程，以寻找潜在漏洞。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `leave sb. doing` 结构：使某人处于某种状态，现在分词作宾语补足语
> 2. 破折号插入语将主语与谓语分隔，阅读时先跳过插入语还原主谓（Q37 相关）
> 3. `peer into`（仔细查看）、`in search of`（寻找）为高频搭配
> 4. 主语核心词为 leakages，of 短语作后置定语

==“Data is becoming an **asset** which needs to be **guarded** as much as any other asset,”== says Haim Mendelson of Stanford University's business school. “The ability to guard customer data is the key to **market value**, which the board is responsible for **on behalf of shareholders**.” Indeed, just as there is the concept of **Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP)**, perhaps it is time for **GASP, Generally Accepted Security Practices**, suggested Eli Noam of New York's Columbia Business School. “Setting the proper investment level for security, **redundancy**, and **recovery** is a management issue, not a technical one,” he says.

The mystery is that this should come as a surprise to any boss. Surely it should be obvious to the **dimmest** executive that **trust**, that most valuable of **economic assets**, is easily destroyed and hugely **expensive to restore**—and that few things are more likely to destroy trust than a company letting **sensitive personal data** get into the **wrong hands**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Surely it should be obvious to the dimmest executive that **trust**, that most valuable of **economic assets**, is easily destroyed and hugely **expensive to restore**—and that few things are more likely to destroy trust than a company letting **sensitive personal data** get into the **wrong hands**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 形式主语: it
> - 谓语 V: should be
> - 表语 C: obvious to the dimmest executive
> - 真实主语（that 名词性从句 1）: that trust, that most valuable of economic assets, is easily destroyed and hugely expensive to restore
> - 真实主语（that 名词性从句 2）: that few things are more likely to destroy trust than a company letting sensitive personal data get into the wrong hands
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 名词性从句（主语从句） | that | 真实主语 1：trust 易被摧毁且修复昂贵 |
> | 名词性从句（主语从句） | that | 真实主语 2：比公司泄露数据更能摧毁信任的事物极少 |
> | 同位语 | that most valuable of economic assets | 解释 trust 的身份 |
> | 介词短语 | to the dimmest executive | 状语，表对象 |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to restore | 与 expensive 搭配，it is expensive to do |
> | 非谓语（现在分词） | letting | 修饰 a company（主动关系） |
> | 比较结构 | than | 引出比较对象 a company letting... |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: it should be obvious to the dimmest executive that... and that...
>   ├── 形式主语: it
>   ├── 谓语 V: should be
>   ├── 表语 C: obvious
>   ├── 介短: to the dimmest executive → 表对象
>   ├── 真实主语(that 名从1): that trust, that most valuable of economic assets,
>         is easily destroyed and hugely expensive to restore
>         ├── 主语 S: trust
>         ├── 同位语: that most valuable of economic assets → 解释 trust
>         ├── 谓语 V: is
>         └── 表语 C: easily destroyed and hugely expensive to restore
>               └── 不定式(to restore): to restore → 与 expensive 搭配
>   └── 真实主语(that 名从2): and that few things are more likely to destroy trust than
>         a company letting sensitive personal data get into the wrong hands
>         ├── 主语 S: few things
>         ├── 谓语 V: are more likely to destroy
>         ├── 宾语 O: trust
>         └── 比较结构(than): than a company letting sensitive personal data get into the wrong hands
>               └── 现在分词(letting): letting sensitive personal data get into the wrong hands → 修饰 company
>                     └── 使役结构: let sensitive personal data get into...（let sb./sth. do 省略 to）
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：即便最迟钝的高管也应该明白：信任——这一最具价值的经济资产——很容易被摧毁，而要修复却代价高昂；而且，很少有事情比一家公司让敏感的个人数据落入不当之手更能摧毁信任。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 形式主语 it + 真实主语（that 从句）句型，阅读时先跳过 it 找 that 从句
> 2. 两个 that 名词性从句由 and 并列，均作真实主语，构成并列主语从句
> 3. 同位语 that most valuable of economic assets 解释 trust（Q39 相关）
> 4. 比较结构 more likely to destroy... than a company letting...，let 后接省略 to 的不定式

The current state of affairs may have been encouraged—though not justified—by the lack of **legal penalty** (in America, but not Europe) for **data leakage**. Until California recently passed a law, American firms did not have to tell anyone, even the victim, when data **went astray**. That may change fast: lots of proposed data-security legislation is now **doing the rounds** in Washington, D. C. Meanwhile, the **theft** of information about some 40 million **credit-card accounts** in America, disclosed on June 17th, **overshadowed** a hugely important decision a day earlier by America's **Federal Trade Commission (FTC)** that puts corporate America **on notice** that regulators will act if firms fail to provide **adequate data security**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Meanwhile, the **theft** of information about some 40 million **credit-card accounts** in America, disclosed on June 17th, **overshadowed** a hugely important decision a day earlier by America's **Federal Trade Commission (FTC)** that puts corporate America **on notice** that regulators will act if firms fail to provide **adequate data security**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: the theft of information about some 40 million credit-card accounts in America
> - 谓语 V: overshadowed
> - 宾语 O: a hugely important decision
> - 状语 A: Meanwhile（时间状语）
> - 定语从句: that puts corporate America on notice that regulators will act if firms fail...（修饰 decision）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | of information | 后置定语，修饰 theft |
> | 介词短语 | about some 40 million credit-card accounts | 后置定语，修饰 information |
> | 介词短语 | in America | 后置定语，修饰 accounts |
> | 非谓语（过去分词） | disclosed | 后置定语，修饰 theft |
> | 介词短语 | by America's FTC | 施动者，修饰 decision（决策发布者） |
> | 定语从句 | that | 修饰 a hugely important decision |
> | 名词性从句（同位语从句） | that | 解释 notice 的内容 |
> | 状语从句 | if | 条件状语，说明监管机构行动的条件 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the theft of information ... overshadowed a hugely important decision
>   ├── 时间状语: Meanwhile
>   ├── 主语 S: the theft of information about some 40 million credit-card accounts in America
>   │     ├── 介短(of): of information → 修饰 theft
>   │     │     └── 介短(about): about some 40 million credit-card accounts → 修饰 information
>   │     │           └── 介短(in): in America → 修饰 accounts
>   │     └── 过去分词定语(disclosed): disclosed on June 17th → 修饰 theft
>   ├── 谓语 V: overshadowed
>   └── 宾语 O: a hugely important decision
>         ├── 时间状语: a day earlier
>         └── 介短(by): by America's Federal Trade Commission (FTC) → 施动者
>               └── 定从(that): that puts corporate America on notice that regulators will act
>                     if firms fail to provide adequate data security → 修饰 decision
>                     ├── 谓语 V: puts
>                     ├── 宾语 O: corporate America
>                     ├── 宾补 C: on notice
>                     ├── 同位语从句(that): that regulators will act → 解释 notice
>                     └── 条件状从(if): if firms fail to provide adequate data security
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：与此同时，6 月 17 日披露的美国约 4000 万个信用卡账户信息被盗事件，使美国联邦贸易委员会（FTC）前一天做出的一项极为重要的决定黯然失色——该决定提醒美国企业界：如果企业未能提供充分的数据安全，监管机构将会采取行动。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 过去分词 disclosed 作后置定语修饰 theft，阅读时跳过插入成分找回主谓
> 2. 定语从句 that 修饰 decision，被 by America's FTC 分隔——定语从句与被修饰名词分隔是高频考点
> 3. `put...on notice that...`："正式告知/警告……"，notice 后接 that 同位语从句
> 4. 多层嵌套：定从中套同位语从句，再套 if 条件状语从句（Q40 相关）
