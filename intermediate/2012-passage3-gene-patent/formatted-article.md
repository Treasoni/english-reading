# The Battle over Gene Patents

In 2010, a federal judge shook America's biotech industry to its core. Companies had won **patents** for **isolated DNA** for decades—by 2005 some $20\%$ of human genes were patented. But in March 2010 a judge ruled that genes were **unpatentable**. Executives were ==violently agitated==. The Biotechnology Industry Organisation (BIO), a **trade group**, assured members that this was just a “**preliminary step**” in a longer battle.

On July 29th they were relieved, at least temporarily. A federal appeals court **overturned** the prior decision, ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of **breast cancer**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：A federal appeals court **overturned** the prior decision, ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of **breast cancer**.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: A federal appeals court（一家联邦上诉法院）
> - 谓语 V: overturned（推翻）
> - 宾语 O: the prior decision（先前的裁决）
> - 状语 A: 无（主句无状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（V-ing） | ruling | 主句谓语 overturned，作结果状语 |
> | 宾语从句 | that | ruling 的宾语从句 |
> | 定语从句 | that | two genes |
> | 介词短语 | to two genes | 修饰 patents |
> | 介词短语 | of breast cancer | 修饰 risk |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: A federal appeals court + overturned + the prior decision
>   └── 非谓语: (ruling ...) → 结果状语
>         └── 宾从: (that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes)
>               ├── 介短: (to two genes) → 修饰 patents
>               └── 定从: (that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer) → 修饰 two genes
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：一家联邦上诉法院推翻了先前的裁决，判定迈里亚德基因公司（Myriad Genetics）确实可以持有两项基因的专利，这两项基因有助于预测女性患乳腺癌的风险。
>
> **考点提示**：ruling 为现在分词作结果状语，其逻辑主语与主句主语一致（a federal appeals court），这是判断非谓语归属的核心依据；句末 that 引导的定语从句修饰 two genes，与前面的 that 宾语从句形成"从句套从句"，翻译时须把定语从句拆成独立分句。

The chief executive of Myriad, a company in Utah, said the ruling was ==a blessing to firms and patients alike==.

But as companies continue their attempts at **personalised medicine**, the courts will remain rather busy. The Myriad case itself is probably not over. Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a ==product of nature==, so it may not be patented; gene patents **suppress** innovation rather than reward it; and patents' **monopolies** restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a ==product of nature==, so it may not be patented; gene patents **suppress** innovation rather than reward it; and patents' **monopolies** restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Critics（批评者）
> - 谓语 V: make（提出）
> - 宾语 O: three main arguments（三条主要理由）
> - 状语 A: 无（主句无状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | against | three main arguments |
> | 同位解释 | 冒号 | three main arguments 的具体内容 |
> | 并列连词（结果） | so | 连接 a gene is a product of nature 与 it may not be patented |
> | 并列比较 | rather than | suppress innovation 与 reward it |
> | 同位举例 | such as | genetic tests |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Critics + make + three main arguments
>   ├── 介短: (against gene patents) → 修饰 arguments
>   └── 冒号解释: arguments 的三项内容
>         ├── 分句一: a gene is a product of nature
>         │     └── 并列(结果): (so it may not be patented)
>         ├── 分句二: gene patents suppress innovation
>         │     └── 比较并列: (rather than reward it)
>         └── 分句三: patents' monopolies restrict access to genetic tests
>               └── 同位举例: (such as Myriad's)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：批评者提出三条反对基因专利的主要理由：基因是自然的产物，因此不应被授予专利；基因专利抑制而非奖励创新；专利垄断限制了人们获得迈里亚德这类基因检测的机会。
>
> **考点提示**：冒号后接三个并列分句，是考研阅读最常见的"总—分"展开结构，三个分句正好对应题目中的三条反对理由，定位题极爱在此设题。rather than 前后须保持形式一致（suppress ... rather than reward ...）；此处的 so 是并列连词引导结果分句，而非从属连词。

A growing number seem to agree. Last year a federal task-force urged reform for patents related to genetic tests. In October the Department of Justice filed a **brief** in the Myriad case, arguing that an isolated DNA molecule “is no less a product of nature...than are cotton fibres that have been **separated** from cotton seeds.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In October the Department of Justice filed a **brief** in the Myriad case, arguing that an isolated DNA molecule “is no less a product of nature...than are cotton fibres that have been **separated** from cotton seeds.”
>
> **主干提取**
> - 状语 A: In October（时间状语）
> - 主语 S: the Department of Justice（司法部）
> - 谓语 V: filed（提交）
> - 宾语 O: a brief（一份意见陈述书）
> - 状语 A: in the Myriad case（范围状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（V-ing） | arguing | 伴随状语，逻辑主语 = the Department of Justice |
> | 宾语从句 | that | arguing 的宾语从句 |
> | 比较结构 | no less ... than | product of nature 与 cotton fibres |
> | 定语从句 | that | cotton fibres |
> | 介词短语 | from cotton seeds | 修饰 been separated |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the Department of Justice + filed + a brief
>   ├── 介短: (In October) → 时间状语
>   ├── 介短: (in the Myriad case) → 范围状语
>   └── 非谓语: (arguing ...) → 伴随状语
>         └── 宾从: (that an isolated DNA molecule is no less a product of nature than are cotton fibres)
>               ├── 比较结构: (no less A than B) → "A 与 B 同样是"
>               └── 定从: (that have been separated from cotton seeds) → 修饰 cotton fibres
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：10月，司法部在迈里亚德案中提交了一份意见陈述书，主张分离出的DNA分子"与从棉籽上分离出来的棉纤维一样，同样是自然的产物"。
>
> **考点提示**：`no less A than B` 意为"A 和 B 一样（都是）"，语气上强调 A 绝不亚于 B，是考研翻译的经典结构（可与 no more than / not more than 的否定含义对比记忆）。than 之后 are cotton fibres 为主谓倒装，因主语较长且自带定语从句；引文中的省略号表示跳读，不影响句法分析。

Despite the appeals court's decision, big questions remain unanswered. For example, it is unclear whether the **sequencing** of a whole **genome** violates the patents of individual genes within it.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：For example, it is unclear whether the **sequencing** of a whole **genome** violates the patents of individual genes within it.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 形式主语 S: it
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: unclear（不清楚的）
> - 真正主语 S: whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents of individual genes within it（whether 引导的主语从句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 主语从句 | whether | 真正主语，it 为形式主语 |
> | 介词短语 | of a whole genome | 修饰 sequencing |
> | 介词短语 | of individual genes | 修饰 patents |
> | 介词短语 | within it | 修饰 individual genes（it = a whole genome） |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: it(形式主语) + is + unclear
>   └── 主从: (whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents of individual genes within it) → 真正主语
>         ├── 介短: (of a whole genome) → 修饰 sequencing
>         └── 介短: (of individual genes) → 修饰 patents
>               └── 介短: (within it) → 修饰 individual genes
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：例如，目前尚不清楚对完整基因组进行测序是否会侵犯其中单个基因的专利。
>
> **考点提示**：it 作形式主语、whether 引导的主语从句后置，是考研高频句型；主语从句只能用 whether 不能用 if。名词短语内的 it 回指 a whole genome，翻译时须还原为"其中"，否则句子读不通。sequencing 由动词 sequence 加 -ing 变为名词，属"动名词化"的抽象名词，是考研阅读中常见的名词化现象。

The case may yet reach the Supreme Court.

As the industry advances, however, other suits may have an even greater impact. Companies are unlikely to file many more patents for human DNA molecules—most are already patented or in the **public domain**. Firms are now studying how genes **interact**, looking for **correlations** that might be used to determine the causes of disease or predict a drug's **efficacy**. Companies are eager to win patents for “==connecting the dots==,” explains Hans Sauer, a lawyer for the BIO.

Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue, brought by the Mayo Clinic, which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue, brought by the Mayo Clinic, which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Their success（他们的成功）
> - 谓语 V: may be determined（可能被决定，被动语态）
> - 状语 A: by a suit（由一桩诉讼）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | by a suit | 说明 determined 的施动者 |
> | 非谓语（V-ed） | related to | a suit |
> | 非谓语（V-ed） | brought by | a suit |
> | 定语从句（非限定） | which | a suit（关系代词作 hear 的宾语） |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Their success + may be determined + (by a suit)
>   └── 后置修饰链 → a suit
>         ├── 非谓语: (related to this issue)
>         ├── 非谓语: (brought by the Mayo Clinic) → 说明诉讼的提起者
>         └── 定从: (which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：他们能否成功，或许取决于梅奥诊所（Mayo Clinic）就这一问题提起的一桩诉讼，最高法院将在下一个开庭期审理此案。
>
> **考点提示**：a suit 后接"两个过去分词短语 + 一个定语从句"三重后置修饰，是考研"名词 + 多重修饰"的典型考法。which 在从句中作 hear 的宾语，故翻译时须把定语从句拆成独立分句（"最高法院将……审理此案"），不能直译成"……的诉讼"。这句还暗含因果链：Mayo Clinic 案的结果 → BIO 成员企业的专利前景。

The BIO recently held a convention which included sessions to coach lawyers on the **shifting landscape** for patents. ==Each meeting was packed.==
