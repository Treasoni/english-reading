# 2014 Passage 4：保障性住房

## 文章原文

When the government talks about **infrastructure** contributing to the economy the focus is usually on roads, railways, **broadband** and energy.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：When the government talks about infrastructure contributing to the economy the focus is usually on roads, railways, broadband and energy.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: the focus
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: on roads, railways, broadband and energy
> - 状语 A: When the government talks …（时间／场景）；usually（频率）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 时间状语从句 | When the government talks … | 从句在 the economy 结束；原文虽无逗号，两组主谓仍可划界 |
> | 非谓语结构 | infrastructure contributing to the economy | 整体置于 about 之后；contributing 的逻辑主语是 infrastructure |
> | 介词短语及并列 | on roads, railways, broadband and energy | on 统领并列的四类基础设施，说明焦点所在 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: the focus is usually on [roads, railways, broadband and energy]
>   └── 时间／情境: When the government talks about
>         └── 话题: infrastructure [contributing to the economy]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：政府谈到基础设施对经济的贡献时，通常关注的是公路、铁路、宽带和能源。
>
> **考点提示**：遇到无逗号的前置从句，先找限定谓语 talks 和 is，再确定从句收束位置；usually 表通常，并非绝对。

Housing is seldom mentioned.

Why is that? To some extent the housing sector must **shoulder the blame**. We have not been good at communicating the real value that housing can contribute to economic growth. Then there is the scale of the typical housing project. It is hard to **shove for attention** among multibillion-pound infrastructure projects, so it is inevitable that the attention is focused elsewhere.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It is hard to shove for attention among multibillion-pound infrastructure projects, so it is inevitable that the attention is focused elsewhere.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: it（第一分句，形式主语）；to shove … projects（后置主语）
> - 谓语 V: is（第一分句）；表语 C: hard
> - 主语 S: it（第二分句，形式主语）；that the attention is focused elsewhere（后置主语）
> - 谓语 V: is（第二分句）；表语 C: inevitable
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 不定式 | to shove for attention … | 作第一分句后置主语；shove 在此形容争抢关注 |
> | 介词短语 | among multibillion-pound infrastructure projects | 说明住房项目置身其中的竞争环境 |
> | 并列连词 | so | 将争取关注困难连到注意力旁落，表结果 |
> | 主语从句 | that the attention is focused elsewhere | 作第二分句后置主语，focused 表关注集中于别处 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 并列结构（so 引出结果）
>   ├── 第一分句: it is hard [to shove for attention [among … projects]]
>   └── so + 第二分句: it is inevitable
>         └── 后置主语: that the attention is focused elsewhere
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在一众动辄数十亿英镑的基础设施项目中，住房项目很难争得关注，因此注意力难免集中到别处。
>
> **考点提示**：两个 it 都不是用来指代住房；沿 so 的方向找因果，这一句解释的是为什么受到的关注少。

But perhaps the most significant reason is that the issue has always been so **politically charged**.

Nevertheless, the **affordable housing** situation is desperate. **Waiting lists** increase all the time and we are simply not building enough new homes.

The comprehensive **spending review** offers an opportunity for the government to help **rectify** this. It needs to **put historical prejudices to one side** and take some steps to **address** our urgent housing need.

There are some indications that it is preparing to do just that. The communities minister, Don Foster, has hinted that George Osborne, Chancellor of the Exchequer, may introduce more flexibility to the current **cap** on the amount that local authorities can **borrow against** their **housing stock** debt.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The communities minister, Don Foster, has hinted that George Osborne, Chancellor of the Exchequer, may introduce more flexibility to the current cap on the amount that local authorities can borrow against their housing stock debt.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: The communities minister（即 Don Foster）
> - 谓语 V: has hinted
> - 宾语 O: that George Osborne … may introduce more flexibility …
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 同位语 | Don Foster；Chancellor of the Exchequer | 分别说明社区事务部长是谁、George Osborne 是什么职务 |
> | 宾语从句 | that George Osborne … may introduce … | 作 hinted 的宾语；第一个 that 不担任从句成分 |
> | 介词短语 | to the current cap on the amount … | 指出灵活性加在哪里；on 所引短语说明上限针对可借数额 |
> | 定语从句 | that local authorities can borrow … | 限定 amount；第二个 that 是 borrow 的宾语，against 短语补充借款背景 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: The communities minister [同位: Don Foster] has hinted
>   └── 宾从: that George Osborne [同位: Chancellor of the Exchequer] may introduce more flexibility
>         └── 对象: to the current cap [on the amount]
>               └── 定从: that local authorities can borrow __ [against their housing stock debt]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：社区事务部长唐·福斯特暗示，财政大臣乔治·奥斯本可能会对地方政府与存量住房债务相关的现行借款上限作更灵活的安排。
>
> **考点提示**：Q38 找 Osborne 后的 may introduce。政策是可能放宽举债上限，既不是已实施，也没有说要削减存量住房债务或公布 GDP 预测。

Evidence shows that 60,000 extra new homes could be built over the next five years if the cap were **lifted**, increasing GDP by 0.6%.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Evidence shows that 60,000 extra new homes could be built over the next five years if the cap were lifted, increasing GDP by 0.6%.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Evidence
> - 谓语 V: shows
> - 宾语 O: that 60,000 extra new homes could be built …
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 宾语从句 | that 60,000 extra new homes could be built … | could be built 是情态被动，主语为额外新建的住房 |
> | 时间介词短语 | over the next five years | 指预测覆盖的五年，以原文时点为基准 |
> | 条件从句 | if the cap were lifted | 用 were lifted 设置假设情境，并非通报已取消上限 |
> | 现在分词短语 | increasing GDP by 0.6% | 说明该建房情境带来的预计结果；by 指增幅，不是增至的水平 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: Evidence shows
>   └── 宾从: that 60,000 extra new homes could be built
>         ├── 时间: over the next five years
>         ├── 条件: if the cap were lifted
>         └── 预计结果: increasing GDP by 0.6%
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：有证据表明，如果取消这一上限，未来五年有可能额外新建 6 万套住房，从而使 GDP 增加 0.6%。
>
> **考点提示**：shows 不会消除内层的假设和可能性；不要把 6 万套或 0.6% 当成实际业绩，也不要把证据中的数字当成 Osborne 将发布的预测。

Ministers should also look at creating greater certainty in the **rental environment**, which would have a significant impact on the ability of **registered providers** to fund new developments from **revenues**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Ministers should also look at creating greater certainty in the rental environment, which would have a significant impact on the ability of registered providers to fund new developments from revenues.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Ministers
> - 谓语 V: should look（与 at 连用）
> - 补足语 C: at creating greater certainty in the rental environment
> - 状语 A: also（提出另一项建议）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 动名词短语 | creating greater certainty … | 作介词 at 的宾语，说明应考虑的做法 |
> | 非限制性定语从句 | which would have a significant impact … | 承接前面提高租赁环境可预期性的做法或形成的情形；which 作从句主语 |
> | 不定式 | to fund new developments | 补足 ability 的内容；逻辑主语是 registered providers |
> | 介词短语 | from revenues | 说明资助新项目的钱来自供应机构自身收入 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: Ministers should also look at [creating greater certainty in the rental environment]
>   └── 非限: which would have a significant impact
>         └── on the ability [of registered providers]
>               └── to fund new developments [from revenues]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：部长们还应考虑提高住房租赁环境的可预期性，这会显著影响注册住房供应机构用自身收入为新开发项目提供资金的能力。
>
> **考点提示**：Q39 把 creating greater certainty 改写为 stable，把 impact on the ability to fund 改写为有助于筹资。没有依据推断成本降低。

But it is not just **down to** the government. While these measures would be welcome in the short term, we must **face up to** the fact that the existing £4.5bn programme of **grants** to fund new affordable housing, set to **expire** in 2015, is unlikely to be extended beyond then.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：While these measures would be welcome in the short term, we must face up to the fact that the existing £4.5bn programme of grants to fund new affordable housing, set to expire in 2015, is unlikely to be extended beyond then.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: we
> - 谓语 V: must face up（与 to 连用）
> - 补足语 C: to the fact that …
> - 状语 A: While these measures would be welcome in the short term（让步）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 让步状语从句 | While … would be welcome | 先承认短期措施会受欢迎；in the short term 限定这一判断 |
> | 内容从句 | the fact that … | 从句说明 fact 的内容，that 不占句法位置；主语中心是 programme |
> | 不定式及分词 | to fund …；set to expire in 2015 | 分别说明拨款用途与计划预定的到期点；set 短语是插入的补充说明 |
> | 表语及不定式被动 | is unlikely to be extended … | 表达对是否续期的预测；then 回指 2015 年而非文章此刻 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: we must face up to the fact
>   ├── 让步: While these measures would be welcome [in the short term]
>   └── 内容从句: that the existing £4.5bn programme
>         ├── 用途: of grants [to fund new affordable housing]
>         ├── 插入: set to expire in 2015
>         └── 谓语部分: is unlikely [to be extended beyond then]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：尽管短期来看这些措施会受到欢迎，但我们必须正视：用于资助新建保障性住房的 45 亿英镑拨款计划预定于 2015 年到期，此后不大可能续期。
>
> **考点提示**：Q40 要抓主句所强调的前景：大额支持不太可能延续，不是已经停止一切政府支持。删去插入语后可见 programme 与 is 呼应。

The Labour Party has recently announced that it will retain a large part of the **coalition**'s spending plans if it returns to power. The housing sector needs to accept that we are very unlikely to ever return to the era of large-scale public grants. We need to adjust to this changing climate.

While the government's **commitment** to long-term funding may have changed, the very **pressing** need for more affordable housing is real and is not going away.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：While the government's commitment to long-term funding may have changed, the very pressing need for more affordable housing is real and is not going away.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: the very pressing need for more affordable housing
> - 谓语 V: is；is not going away（并列）
> - 表语 C: real（对应第一项 is）
> - 状语 A: While the government's commitment … may have changed（让步）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 让步状语从句 | While … may have changed | 承认长期拨款承诺可能已有变化，但不将变化说成事实 |
> | 情态完成式 | may have changed | 对先前变化作推测；to long-term funding 从属于 commitment，to 是介词 |
> | 形容词及介词短语 | very pressing；for more affordable housing | 分别写需求之迫切与需求对象；more 指需要更多保障性住房 |
> | 并列谓语 | is real and is not going away | 同一主语承接两个判断：确实存在，且不会消失 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: the very pressing need [for more affordable housing]
>   ├── is real
>   ├── and is not going away
>   └── 让步: While the government's commitment [to long-term funding] may have changed
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：尽管政府对长期拨款的承诺可能已经改变，但增建保障性住房的迫切需求确实存在，而且不会消失。
>
> **考点提示**：资金承诺与住房需求不是同一个主语；while 后可变的政策承诺，衬出主句中不会消失的需求。
