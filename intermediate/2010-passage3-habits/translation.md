# Manufactured Habits 人为塑造的习惯｜中英对照翻译

## Reading Passage 阅读文章

Over the past decade, many companies had perfected the art of creating **automatic behaviors**—habits—among consumers. These habits have helped companies earn billions of dollars when customers eat snacks or wipe counters almost without thinking, often in response to a carefully designed set of daily cues.

在过去十年里，许多公司已经熟练掌握了在消费者中培养**自动行为**——也就是习惯——的本领。当顾客几乎不假思索地吃零食或擦拭台面时，这些习惯帮助公司赚取了数十亿美元；而这种行为往往是对精心设计的一组日常提示作出的反应。

“There are fundamental public health problems, like dirty hands instead of a soap habit, that remain killers only because we can’t figure out how to change people’s habits,” said Dr. Curtis, the director of the Hygiene Center at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine. “We wanted to learn from private industry how to create new behaviors that happen automatically.”

“有一些基本的公共卫生问题，比如人们手脏，却没有养成用肥皂洗手的习惯，之所以仍然会造成死亡，只是因为我们无法想出改变人们习惯的办法，”伦敦卫生与热带医学学院卫生中心主任柯蒂斯博士说。“我们想向私营企业学习，了解如何创造能够自动发生的新行为。”

The companies that Dr. Curtis turned to—Procter & Gamble, Colgate-Palmolive and Unilever—had invested hundreds of millions of dollars finding the subtle cues in consumers' lives that corporations could use to introduce new routines.

柯蒂斯博士求助的公司——宝洁、高露洁-棕榄和联合利华——已经投入数亿美元，寻找消费者生活中的细微提示，企业可以利用这些提示来引入新的日常行为。

If you look hard enough, you'll find that many of the products we use every day—chewing gums, skin moisturizers, disinfecting wipes, air fresheners, water purifiers, health snacks, teeth whiteners, fabric softeners, vitamins—are results of **manufactured habits**. A century ago, few people regularly brushed their teeth multiple times a day. Today, because of shrewd advertising and public health campaigns, many Americans habitually give their pearly whites a cavity-preventing scrub twice a day, often with Colgate, Crest or one of the other brands.

只要你仔细观察，就会发现，我们每天使用的许多产品——口香糖、润肤霜、消毒湿巾、空气清新剂、净水器、健康零食、美白牙齿产品、织物柔顺剂和维生素——都是**人为塑造的习惯**所产生的结果。一个世纪前，很少有人每天定期刷牙多次。如今，由于精明的广告和公共卫生宣传，许多美国人已经养成了每天两次刷牙、预防蛀牙的习惯，使用的往往是高露洁、佳洁士或其他品牌的产品。

A few decades ago, many people didn't drink water outside of a meal. Then beverage companies started bottling the production of far-off springs, and now office workers unthinkingly sip bottled water all day long. Chewing gum, once bought primarily by adolescent boys, is now featured in commercials as a breath freshener and teeth cleanser for use after a meal. Skin moisturizers are advertised as part of morning beauty rituals, slipped in between hair brushing and putting on makeup.

几十年前，许多人不在吃饭以外的时间喝水。后来，饮料公司开始把远方泉水的水装瓶出售，而现在，办公室职员整天都在不假思索地啜饮瓶装水。口香糖曾主要由青春期男孩购买，如今却在广告中被宣传为饭后使用的清新口气、清洁牙齿的产品。润肤霜则被宣传为早晨美容仪式的一部分，夹在梳头和化妆之间使用。

"Our products succeed when they become part of daily or weekly patterns," said Carol Berning, a consumer psychologist who recently retired from Procter & Gamble, the company that sold $76 billion of Tide, Crest and other products last year. "Creating **positive habits** is a huge part of improving our consumers' lives, and it's essential to making new products **commercially viable**."

“我们的产品只有在成为人们每日或每周生活模式的一部分时，才能取得成功，”宝洁公司消费者心理学家卡罗尔·伯宁说。她最近从宝洁退休；该公司去年销售汰渍、佳洁士等产品的金额达760亿美元。“创造**积极习惯**是改善消费者生活的重要组成部分，也是使新产品在商业上**具有可行性**的关键。”

Through experiments and observation, social scientists like Dr. Berning have learned that there is power in tying certain behaviors to **habitual cues** through **ruthless advertising**. As this new science of habit has emerged, controversies have erupted when the tactics have been used to sell questionable beauty creams or unhealthy foods.

通过实验和观察，像伯宁博士这样的社会科学家已经认识到，通过**无情的广告**把某些行为与**习惯性提示**联系起来具有很大力量。随着这门关于习惯的新科学发展起来，当这些策略被用于推销可疑的美容霜或不健康食品时，争议也随之出现。

## Questions 阅读理解题

### 31. According to Dr. Curtis, habits like hand washing with soap ____.

根据柯蒂斯博士的说法，像用肥皂洗手这样的习惯____。

- [A] should be further cultivated
- [B] should be changed gradually
- [C] are deeply rooted in history
- [D] are basically private concerns

中文选项：

- [A] 应进一步培养
- [B] 应逐步改变
- [C] 在历史中根深蒂固
- [D] 基本上是个人私事

### 32. Bottled water, chewing gum and skin moisturizers are mentioned in Paragraph 5 so as to ____.

第5段提到瓶装水、口香糖和润肤霜，是为了____。

- [A] reveal their impact on people's habits
- [B] show the urgent need of daily necessities
- [C] indicate their effect on people's buying power
- [D] manifest the significant role of good habits

中文选项：

- [A] 揭示它们对人们习惯的影响
- [B] 说明日常必需品的迫切需求
- [C] 表明它们对人们购买力的影响
- [D] 体现良好习惯的重要作用

### 33. Which of the following does NOT belong to products that help create people's habits?

以下哪项不属于有助于塑造人们习惯的产品？

- [A] Tide.
- [B] Crest.
- [C] Colgate.
- [D] Unilever.

中文选项：

- [A] 汰渍（Tide）
- [B] 佳洁士（Crest）
- [C] 高露洁（Colgate）
- [D] 联合利华（Unilever）

### 34. From the text we know that some of consumers' habits are developed due to _.

根据文章可知，消费者的一些习惯是由于____而养成的。

- [A] perfected art of products
- [B] automatic behavior creation
- [C] commercial promotions
- [D] scientific experiments

中文选项：

- [A] 产品打造技艺的完善
- [B] 自动行为的创造
- [C] 商业推广
- [D] 科学实验

### 35. The author's attitude toward the influence of advertisement on people's habits is ____.

作者对于广告对人们习惯的影响所持的态度是____。

- [A] indifferent
- [B] negative
- [C] positive
- [D] biased

中文选项：

- [A] 无动于衷
- [B] 消极
- [C] 积极
- [D] 有偏见
