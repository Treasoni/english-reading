# 2014 Passage 3：机器与就业

## 文章原文

The concept of **man versus machine** is at least as old as the industrial revolution, but this phenomenon tends to be most acutely felt during **economic downturns** and **fragile recoveries**. And yet, it would be a mistake to think we are right now simply experiencing the painful side of a **boom and bust cycle**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：And yet, it would be a mistake to think we are right now simply experiencing the painful side of a boom and bust cycle.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: it（形式主语）；后置主语 S: to think … cycle
> - 谓语 V: would be
> - 表语 C: a mistake
> - 状语 A: And yet（承接并转折）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 衔接副词 | And yet | 将前一句的经济周期背景转向对「只是周期波动」的反驳 |
> | 不定式 | to think … | 作后置主语；think 后省略引导内容从句的 that |
> | 宾语从句 | we are … experiencing … | 作 think 的宾语；right now 指眼下，simply 限定经历的性质 |
> | 介词短语 | of a boom and bust cycle | 修饰 the painful side，界定是哪一种难熬阶段 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: It would be a mistake
>   └── 后置主语: to think
>         └── 宾语从句: (that) we are right now simply experiencing
>               └── 宾语: the painful side [of a boom and bust cycle]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：然而，如果以为我们眼下只是在经历经济繁荣与衰退周期中难熬的一段，那就错了。
>
> **考点提示**：作者否定的是「只是暂时的周期波动」，不是否认经济处在低迷阶段；后文的 for good 与 simply 构成关键对照。

Certain jobs have gone away **for good**, **outmoded** by machines. Since technology has such an **insatiable appetite for** eating up human jobs, this phenomenon will continue to **restructure** our economy in ways we can't immediately foresee.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Since technology has such an insatiable appetite for eating up human jobs, this phenomenon will continue to restructure our economy in ways we can't immediately foresee.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: this phenomenon
> - 谓语 V: will continue
> - 补足语 C: to restructure our economy
> - 状语 A: Since technology … jobs（原因）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 原因状语从句 | Since technology has … | since 在这里是「因为」，并非时间起点 |
> | 介词短语 | for eating up human jobs | 说明 appetite 的对象；eating up 把岗位被吞噬写成比喻 |
> | 介词短语 | in ways … | 修饰 restructure，表示重塑经济的方式 |
> | 定语从句 | we can't immediately foresee | 限制 ways；foresee 的宾语关系词可省，immediately 限定预见的时间 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: this phenomenon will continue
>   ├── 原因: Since technology has such an insatiable appetite [for eating up human jobs]
>   └── 不定式补足语: to restructure our economy
>         └── 方式: in ways [定从: (that) we can't immediately foresee __]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：技术对吞噬人类工作岗位的胃口似乎永远填不满，因此这一现象会继续重塑经济，具体方式眼下还难以预见。
>
> **考点提示**：问「预见什么」可找到 foresee 的宾语缺位；对比 A7，in which 填的是从句内部的方式状语。can't immediately 不等于「永远无法」。

When there is rapid improvement in the price and performance of technology, jobs that were once thought to be **immune from** automation suddenly become threatened.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：When there is rapid improvement in the price and performance of technology, jobs that were once thought to be immune from automation suddenly become threatened.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: jobs（由 that 从句限定）
> - 谓语 V: become
> - 表语 C: threatened
> - 状语 A: When there is …（时间或情境）；suddenly（突变）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 时间状语从句 | When there is … | 存在句围绕单数中心词 improvement；in … 说明改善的维度 |
> | 限制性定语从句 | that were once thought … | 修饰 jobs；that 充当从句主语，were thought 是从句的被动谓语 |
> | 不定式 | to be immune from automation | 给出过去人们所作的判断；immune from 表「不受……影响」 |
> | 结果状态 | become threatened | become 是主句系动词，threatened 表进入受威胁的状态 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: jobs [定从: that were once thought [to be immune from automation]] suddenly become threatened
>   └── 时间／情境: When there is rapid improvement [in the price and performance of technology]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：当技术成本迅速下降、性能迅速提升时，一些曾被认为不受自动化影响的岗位突然面临威胁。
>
> **考点提示**：先跳过 jobs 后的定语从句，读出 jobs become threatened；once thought 与 suddenly become 是旧判断和新变化的对照，不能用旧判断回答 Q32。

This argument has attracted a lot of attention, via the success of the book Race Against the Machine, by Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee, who both **hail from** MIT's Center for Digital Business.

This is a powerful argument, and a scary one. And yet, John Hagel, author of The Power of Pull and other books, says Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason why these jobs are so **vulnerable to** technology **in the first place**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：And yet, John Hagel, author of The Power of Pull and other books, says Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason why these jobs are so vulnerable to technology in the first place.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: John Hagel
> - 谓语 V: says
> - 宾语 O: (that) Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason …
> - 状语 A: And yet（转折衔接）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 同位语 | author of The Power of Pull and other books | 补充 John Hagel 的身份，不参与主干 |
> | 宾语从句 | Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss … | 省略 that；主语为两位作者，不是 Hagel |
> | 定语从句 | why these jobs are so vulnerable … | 限定 reason；why 是原因状语，so 修饰 vulnerable，in the first place 强调追溯根本原因 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: John Hagel [同位语: author of …] says
>   └── 宾语从句: (that) Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason
>         └── 定从: why these jobs are so vulnerable to technology in the first place
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：不过，《拉动的力量》等书的作者约翰·哈格尔认为，布林约尔松和麦卡菲没看清这些岗位为何一开始就这么容易受到技术冲击。
>
> **考点提示**：哈格尔质疑的是两位作者没有说清岗位脆弱的成因，并没有否认自动化威胁本身。抓住 says 与 miss 各自的主语。

Hagel says we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be “**tightly scripted**” and “**highly standardized**” ones that **leave no room for** “individual initiative or creativity.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Hagel says we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be “tightly scripted” and “highly standardized” ones that leave no room for “individual initiative or creativity.”
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Hagel
> - 谓语 V: says
> - 宾语 O: (that) we have designed jobs …
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 宾语从句 | (that) we have designed jobs | 作 says 的宾语；现在完成时把既有岗位设计和眼下后果联系起来 |
> | 介词短语 | in the U.S. | 交代设计或岗位所在的美国语境；不改变第一个 that 指向 jobs |
> | 定语从句 | that tend to be … ones | 修饰 jobs；tend to 表倾向，两个形容性短语共同描述 ones |
> | 定语从句 | that leave no room for … | 修饰替代名词 ones；that 作 leave 的主语，for 后指出岗位没有为哪些能力留出空间 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: Hagel says
>   └── 宾语从句: (that) we have designed jobs [in the U.S.]
>         └── 定从: that tend to be [tightly scripted and highly standardized] ones
>               └── 定从: that leave no room for [individual initiative or creativity]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：哈格尔说，我们在美国设计的岗位往往流程规定得很死且高度标准化，不给个人主动性或创造力留余地。
>
> **考点提示**：ones 回指 jobs，不是另一批岗位。Q33 把 leave no room for creativity 改写为设计不利于创造力，并非岗位设计者有意阻止创新。

In short, these are the types of jobs that machines can perform much better at than human beings. That is how we have put a giant target sign on the backs of American workers, Hagel says.

It's time to **reinvent** the formula for how work is conducted, since we are still relying on a very 20th century notion of work, Hagel says.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It's time to reinvent the formula for how work is conducted, since we are still relying on a very 20th century notion of work, Hagel says.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: It（时间表达中的虚主语）
> - 谓语 V: is（It's）
> - 表语 C: time to reinvent the formula …
> - 状语 A: since we are still relying on …（原因）
> - 以上为报道内容；句末报道语的主语 S: Hagel；谓语 V: says
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 不定式 | to reinvent the formula … | 补足 time，说明该做什么 |
> | 嵌入式 how 从句 | how work is conducted | 作 for 的宾语，保持陈述语序，is conducted 为被动 |
> | 原因状语从句 | since we are still relying … | 解释为什么现在该重设工作方式；still 说明旧观念仍在延续 |
> | 报道语 | Hagel says | 句末标示前述判断出自哈格尔，并非 since 从句的一部分 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 报道内容: It's time [to reinvent the formula]
>   ├── formula 的补充: for [how work is conducted]
>   └── 原因: since we are still relying on [a very 20th century notion of work]
> 句末报道语: Hagel says
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：哈格尔说，我们仍沿用一种很有二十世纪色彩的工作观念，因此现在是时候重新设计工作的组织方式了。
>
> **考点提示**：how work is conducted 是嵌在介词后的从句，不倒装；对照末段直接提问 how do we innovate … 的疑问语序。

In our rapidly changing economy, we more than ever need people in the workplace who can **take initiative** and **exercise their imagination** “to respond to unexpected events.” That’s not something machines are good at. They are designed to perform very predictable activities.

As Hagel notes, Brynjolfsson and McAfee indeed **touched on** this point in their book. We need to **reframe** race against the machine as race with the machine. In other words, we need to look at the ways in which machines can **augment** human labor rather than replace it.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In other words, we need to look at the ways in which machines can augment human labor rather than replace it.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: we
> - 谓语 V: need
> - 补足语 C: to look at the ways …
> - 状语 A: In other words（换言之）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 不定式 | to look at the ways … | 补足 need；这里是「研究、考察」而非肉眼观看 |
> | 定语从句 | in which machines can … | 限定 ways；in which 相当于 in those ways，在从句内作方式状语 |
> | 对照结构 | augment … rather than replace … | 比较增强与取代，it 回指 human labor；replace 在 can 的范围内理解 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: we need [to look at the ways]
>   ├── 衔接: In other words
>   └── 定从: in which machines can
>         ├── augment human labor
>         └── rather than replace it
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：换句话说，我们需要研究机器可以怎样增强人的工作能力，而不是取代人的劳动。
>
> **考点提示**：Q34 的归属线索是前句的 *indeed touched on this point*：两位作者的书也谈到上一段所说的「工作仍需要人的主动性与想象力」。本句再说明机器应辅助人而非替代人；不能只凭本句判断两位作者讨论了什么。

So then the problem is not really about technology, but rather, “how do we innovate our institutions and our work practices?”
