# 2003-Passage 2: Animal Rights vs Biomedical Research 中英对照翻译

## Paragraph 1

（1）To paraphrase 18th-century statesman Edmund Burke, "all that is needed for the triumph of a misguided cause is that good people do nothing."

（1）18 世纪政治家埃德蒙·伯克有一句话，可以这样诠释："一项误入歧途的事业要得逞，所需要的只是好人无所作为。"

（2）One such cause now seeks to end biomedical research because of the theory that animals have rights ruling out their use in research.

（2）如今就有这样一项事业，试图终止生物医学研究，因为有一种理论认为动物拥有权利，从而将动物排除在研究用途之外。

> [!note] 句子结构
> `the theory that animals have rights ruling out their use in research` 中 `ruling out` 是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 `rights`，意为"使……成为不可能"。同位语从句 `that animals have rights` 解释 `theory` 内容。

（3）Scientists need to respond forcefully to animal rights advocates, whose arguments are confusing the public and thereby threatening advances in health knowledge and care.

（3）科学家必须有力地回应动物权利倡导者，因为他们的言论正在混淆公众视听，进而威胁到健康知识与医疗保健的进步。

（4）Leaders of the animal rights movement target biomedical research because it depends on public funding, and few people understand the process of health care research.

（4）动物权利运动的领袖把矛头对准生物医学研究，原因有二：其一，该研究依赖公共资金；其二，几乎没有人了解医学研究的过程。

（5）Hearing allegations of cruelty to animals in research settings, many are perplexed that anyone would deliberately harm an animal.

（5）听到研究环境中虐待动物的指控，许多人感到困惑——怎么竟会有人故意伤害动物呢？

## Paragraph 2

（1）For example, a grandmotherly woman staffing an animal rights booth at a recent street fair was distributing a brochure that encouraged readers not to use anything that comes from or is tested in animals—no meat, no fur, no medicines.

（1）例如，在最近的一次街头集市上，一位慈眉善目的老奶奶驻守在动物权利宣传摊位，向路人分发宣传册，鼓励读者不要使用任何来自动物或在动物身上试验过的东西——不吃肉，不穿毛皮，不用药物。

（2）Asked if she opposed immunizations, she wanted to know if vaccines come from animal research.

（2）当被问到她是否反对免疫接种时，她反问疫苗是否来自动物研究。

（3）When assured that they do, she replied, "Then I would have to say yes."

（3）当被确认的确如此时，她答道："那我就不得不说，是的（我也反对疫苗）。"

（4）Asked what will happen when epidemics return, she said, "Don't worry, scientists will find some way of using computers."

（4）当被问到如果流行病再次暴发该怎么办时，她说："别担心，科学家总会找到办法用计算机来解决的。"

（5）Such well-meaning people just don't understand.

（5）这样的好心人压根儿就不明白（其中的道理）。

> [!note] 文化背景
> 段落 2 的例子通过一位慈祥老奶奶的"天真无知"，反衬动物权利倡导者对科学研究的认知盲区：他们反对一切来自动物的产品，却不知道疫苗、救命药都依赖动物实验。

## Paragraph 3

（1）Scientists must communicate their message to the public in a compassionate, understandable way—in human terms, not in the language of molecular biology.

（1）科学家必须以富有同理心、通俗易懂的方式向公众传达他们的信息——用人话，而不是用分子生物学的术语。

（2）We need to make clear the connection between animal research and a grandmother's hip replacement, a father's bypass operation, a baby's vaccinations, and even a pet's shots.

（2）我们需要让人们看清动物研究与一位老奶奶的髋关节置换、一位父亲的搭桥手术、一个婴儿的疫苗接种、甚至宠物打防疫针之间的联系。

（3）To those who are unaware that animal research was needed to produce these treatments, as well as new treatments and vaccines, animal research seems wasteful at best and cruel at worst.

（3）对于那些不知道动物研究是生产这些疗法以及新疗法和新疫苗所必需的人来说，动物研究往好里说是浪费，往坏里说是残忍。

> [!note] 翻译技巧
> `at best` 与 `at worst` 的对仗翻译：分别译为"往好里说"和"往坏里说"，比"充其量/最坏的是"更口语化，与上文"compassionate, understandable way"（富有人情味的方式）的语境相符。

## Paragraph 4

（1）Much can be done.

（1）可以做的事情很多。

（2）Scientists could "adopt" middle school classes and present their own research.

（2）科学家可以"认领"中学班级，向学生展示他们自己的研究。

（3）They should be quick to respond to letters to the editor, lest animal rights misinformation go unchallenged and acquire a deceptive appearance of truth.

（3）他们应当迅速回复读者来信（致编辑的信），以免动物权利方面的错误信息畅行无阻，披上一层貌似真理的伪装。

> [!note] 语法点
> `lest` 引导目的状语从句，从句谓语用虚拟式 `(should) go unchallenged and acquire`。注意主句用陈述语气，从句用 `(should) + 动词原形`，与 `so that ... may/might` 表达"以免"类似。

（4）Research institutions could be opened to tours, to show that laboratory animals receive humane care.

（4）研究机构可以向公众开放参观，展示实验动物受到了人道的对待。

（5）Finally, because the ultimate stakeholders are patients, the health research community should actively recruit to its cause not only well-known personalities such as Stephen Cooper, who has made courageous statements about the value of animal research, but all who receive medical treatment.

（5）最后，由于最终的利益相关者是患者，卫生研究界应当积极争取更多人加入他们的事业——不仅要争取像斯蒂芬·库珀这样勇敢地为动物研究的价值发声的名人，也要争取所有接受过医疗救治的普通人。

（6）If good people do nothing, there is a real possibility that an uninformed citizenry will extinguish the precious embers of medical progress.

（6）如果好人无所作为，那么不知情的公众就有可能真正扑灭医学进步的宝贵火种。

> [!note] 修辞手法
> 末句与开篇伯克的名言首尾呼应：`good people do nothing` 与 `extinguish the precious embers of medical progress`（扑灭医学进步的宝贵火种）形成强烈对照。"embers"（余烬）一词暗喻医学进步来之不易、可能因公众的冷漠而前功尽弃。
