# Overfishing and the Shifting Baseline 过度捕捞与变动基线

## 文章正文 中英对照

When prehistoric man arrived in new parts of the world, something strange happened to the large animals. They suddenly became **extinct**. Smaller species survived. The large, slow-growing animals were easy game, and were quickly hunted to **extinction**. Now something similar could be happening in the oceans.

当史前人类到达世界上新的区域时，大型动物发生了奇怪的变化。它们突然**灭绝**了。较小的物种存活了下来。那些生长缓慢的大型动物很容易成为猎物，很快就被猎杀至**灭绝**。如今，类似的情况可能正在海洋中发生。

---

That the seas are being **overfished** has been known for years.

海洋被**过度捕捞**这一事实多年来已为人所知。

---

What researchers such as Ransom Myers and Boris Worm have shown is just how fast things are changing. They have looked at half a century of data from fisheries around the world.

Ransom Myers 和 Boris Worm 等研究人员所揭示的，正是事态变化的速度有多快。他们研究了全球渔业半个世纪的数据。

---

Their methods do not attempt to estimate the actual **biomass** (the amount of living biological matter) of fish species in particular parts of the ocean, but rather changes in that biomass over time.

他们的方法并非试图估算海洋特定区域鱼类实际的**生物量**（活的生物物质总量），而是估算该生物量随时间发生的变化。

---

According to their latest paper published in *Nature*, the biomass of large **predators** (animals that kill and eat other animals) in a new fishery is reduced on average by 80% within 15 years of the start of exploitation. In some long-fished areas, it has halved again since then.

根据他们发表在《自然》杂志上的最新论文，在新渔场开始开发的 15 年内，大型**捕食者**（捕杀并食用其他动物的动物）的生物量平均减少了 80%。在一些长期捕捞的区域，自那以后又减少了一半。

---

Dr. Worm acknowledges that these figures are **conservative**. One reason for this is that fishing technology has improved. Today's vessels can find their prey using satellites and sonar, which were not available 50 years ago. That means a higher proportion of what is in the sea is being caught, so the real difference between present and past is likely to be worse than the one recorded by changes in catch sizes. In the early days, too, **longlines** would have been more saturated with fish. Some individuals would therefore not have been caught, since no **baited hooks** would have been available to trap them, leading to an underestimate of fish stocks in the past. Furthermore, in the early days of longline fishing, a lot of fish were lost to sharks after they had been hooked. That is no longer a problem, because there are fewer sharks around now.

Worm 博士承认这些数字是**保守的**。原因之一是渔业技术已经进步。如今的渔船可以使用卫星和声纳寻找猎物，而这些技术在 50 年前是不存在的。这意味着海洋中更高比例的鱼类被捕获，因此现在与过去之间的实际差异可能比渔获量变化所记录的数据更糟。此外，在早期，**延绳**上的鱼本应更多。有些鱼因此未被捕获，因为没有足够的**诱饵钩**来捕获它们，导致对过去鱼类资源量的低估。而且，在早期延绳钓作业中，许多鱼在上钩后被鲨鱼吃掉。这现在已经不成问题，因为附近的鲨鱼少多了。

---

Dr. Myers and Dr. Worm argue that their work gives a correct **baseline**, which future management efforts must take into account. They believe the data support an idea current among marine biologists, that of the "**shifting baseline**." The notion is that people have failed to detect the massive changes which have happened in the ocean because they have been looking back only a relatively short time into the past. That matters because theory suggests that the **maximum sustainable yield** that can be cropped from a fishery comes when the biomass of a target species is about 50% of its original levels. Most fisheries are well below that, which is a bad way to do business.

Myers 博士和 Worm 博士认为，他们的工作提供了一个正确的**基线**，未来的管理努力必须将其考虑在内。他们相信这些数据支持了海洋生物学家中流行的一个概念，即"**变动基线**"。这一概念的意思是，人们未能察觉海洋中发生的巨大变化，因为他们回顾过去的时限相对较短。这很关键，因为理论表明，当目标物种的生物量达到其原始水平的约 50% 时，渔场才能维持**最大可持续产量**。而大多数渔场远远低于这个水平，这是一种糟糕的经营方式。

---

31. The extinction of large prehistoric animals is noted to suggest that
    - [A] large animals were vulnerable to the changing environment
    - [B] small species survived as large animals disappeared
    - [C] large sea animals may face the same threat today
    - [D] slow-growing fish outlive fast-growing ones

31. 文中提及大型史前动物灭绝是为了说明
    - [A] 大型动物易受环境变化影响
    - [B] 大型动物消失时小型物种存活下来
    - [C] 如今大型海洋动物可能面临同样的威胁
    - [D] 生长缓慢的鱼类比生长快的鱼类活得更久

32. We can infer from Dr. Myers and Dr. Worm's paper that
    - [A] the stock of large predators in some old fisheries has reduced by 90%
    - [B] there are only half as many fisheries as there were 15 years ago
    - [C] the catch sizes in new fisheries are only 20% of the original amount
    - [D] the number of larger predators dropped faster in new fisheries than in the old

32. 从 Myers 和 Worm 博士的论文中可以推断出
    - [A] 一些旧渔场中大型捕食者的储量减少了 90%
    - [B] 现在的渔场数量只有 15 年前的一半
    - [C] 新渔场的渔获量仅为原数量的 20%
    - [D] 新渔场中大型捕食者数量比旧渔场下降得更快

33. By saying "These figures are conservative" (Line 1, paragraph 3), Dr. Worm means that
    - [A] fishing technology has improved rapidly
    - [B] the catch-sizes are actually smaller than recorded
    - [C] the marine biomass has suffered a greater loss
    - [D] the data collected so far are out of date

33. Worm 博士说"这些数字是保守的"（第 3 段第 1 行），意思是
    - [A] 渔业技术已经迅速进步
    - [B] 实际渔获量比记录的要小
    - [C] 海洋生物量遭受了更大的损失
    - [D] 目前收集的数据已经过时

34. Dr. Myers and other researchers hold that
    - [A] people should look for a baseline that can work for a longer time
    - [B] fisheries should keep the yield below 50% of the biomass
    - [C] the ocean biomass should be restored to its original level
    - [D] people should adjust the fishing baseline to the changing situation

34. Myers 博士和其他研究者认为
    - [A] 人们应该寻找一个能够更长时间适用的基线
    - [B] 渔业应保持产量低于生物量的 50%
    - [C] 海洋生物量应恢复到原始水平
    - [D] 人们应根据变化的情况调整渔业基线

    - [A] management efficiency
    - [B] biomass level
    - [C] catch-size limits
    - [D] technological application

35. 作者似乎主要关心大多数渔场的
    - [A] 管理效率
