# Postwar American Housing Style

## Article

We tend to think of the decades **immediately** following World War II as a time of **prosperity and growth**, with soldiers returning home by the millions, going off to college on the G. I. Bill and lining up at the marriage bureaus.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：We tend to think of the decades **immediately** following World War II as a time of **prosperity and growth**, with soldiers returning home by the millions, going off to college on the G. I. Bill and lining up at the marriage bureaus.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: We
> - 谓语 V: tend to think
> - 宾语 O: the decades immediately following World War II
> - 补语 C: as a time of prosperity and growth（“think of A as B”结构中的补足成分）
> - 状语 A: with soldiers returning home..., going off to college..., and lining up...（with 复合结构，表示伴随）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 定语 | immediately following World War II | 修饰 the decades，following 为现在分词 |
> | 介词短语 | of prosperity and growth | 修饰 a time |
> | with 复合结构 | with + soldiers + returning/going/lining | 说明士兵回国后的一系列伴随动作 |
> | 介词短语 | by the millions | 修饰 returning home，表示“数以百万计地” |
> | 介词短语 | on the G. I. Bill | 修饰 going off to college，表示凭借该法案上大学 |
> | 介词短语 | at the marriage bureaus | 修饰 lining up |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: We + tend to think + of the decades + as a time of prosperity and growth
>   ├── 定语: (immediately following World War II) → 修饰 the decades
>   └── 伴随状语: (with soldiers ...)
>         ├── returning home by the millions
>         ├── going off to college on the G. I. Bill
>         └── lining up at the marriage bureaus
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：我们往往认为，紧随第二次世界大战之后的几十年是一个繁荣与发展的时期：数以百万计的士兵返回家乡，凭借《退伍军人权利法案》上大学，并在婚姻登记处排队。
>
> **考点提示**：`think of A as B` 表示“把 A 看作 B”；`with + 名词 + V-ing` 是常见的独立伴随结构，后面的三个现在分词短语由 `and` 连接，逻辑主语都是 soldiers。

But when it came to their houses, it was a time of common sense and a belief that ==less could truly be more==.

During the Depression and the war, Americans had learned to live with less, and that restraint, in combination with the postwar confidence in the future, made **small, efficient housing** positively **stylish**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：During the Depression and the war, Americans had learned to live with less, and that restraint, in combination with the postwar confidence in the future, made **small, efficient housing** positively stylish.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Americans（第一个并列分句）；that restraint（第二个并列分句）
> - 谓语 V: had learned；made
> - 宾语 O: to live with less（learned 的不定式宾语）；small, efficient housing（made 的宾语）
> - 补语 C: positively stylish（宾语补足语，说明 housing 的状态）
> - 状语 A: During the Depression and the war；in combination with the postwar confidence in the future（分别修饰两个分句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 介词短语 | During the Depression and the war | 修饰 had learned，表示时间背景 |
> | 不定式短语 | to live with less | 作 learned 的宾语，表示学会做什么 |
> | 介词短语 | with less | 修饰 live，表示“用更少的东西生活” |
> | 插入介词短语 | in combination with... | 插入说明 that restraint 的伴随因素 |
> | 形容词并列 | small, efficient | 修饰 housing |
> | 宾语补足语 | positively stylish | 补充说明 small, efficient housing 的结果状态 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 并列句: [Americans had learned ...] + and + [that restraint made ... stylish]
>   ├── 分句1: Americans + had learned + to live with less
>   │     ├── 时间状语: (During the Depression and the war)
>   │     └── 介短: (with less) → 修饰 live
>   └── 分句2: that restraint + made + small, efficient housing + positively stylish
>         └── 插入状语: (in combination with the postwar confidence in the future)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在经济大萧条和战争期间，美国人学会了节俭生活；这种克制与战后对未来的信心相结合，使小巧而高效的住宅变得格外时髦。
>
> **考点提示**：`and` 连接两个完整分句；`make + 宾语 + 形容词` 是“使……变得……”的典型宾语补足语结构。`in combination with...` 是插入的介词短语，不改变主干。

Economic condition was only a stimulus for the trend toward efficient living.

The phrase “less is more” was actually first popularized by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated to the United States before World War II and **took up posts** at American architecture schools.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The phrase “less is more” was actually first popularized by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated to the United States before World War II and took up posts at American architecture schools.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: The phrase “less is more”
> - 谓语 V: was popularized
> - 宾语 O: 无（被动句中原主动句的宾语 phrase 已作主语）
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: actually first；by a German；before World War II
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 同位语 | the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe | 解释说明 a German |
> | 定语从句 | who ... emigrated ... and took up ... | 修饰 a German / Ludwig Mies van der Rohe |
> | 比较插入语 | like other people associated with the Bauhaus | 修饰从句主语 who，表示“和其他相关人士一样” |
> | 分词短语 | associated with the Bauhaus | 修饰 people |
> | 同位语 | a school of design | 解释说明 the Bauhaus |
> | 介词短语 | to the United States | 修饰 emigrated |
> | 并列谓语 | emigrated ... and took up ... | 共同受 who 支配 |
> | 介词短语 | at American architecture schools | 修饰 took up posts |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: The phrase “less is more” + was first popularized + by a German
>   ├── 同位语: (the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe) → 解释 a German
>   └── 定语从句: (who ... emigrated ... and took up posts ...)
>         ├── 插入比较语: (like other people associated with the Bauhaus)
>         │     ├── 分词短语: (associated with the Bauhaus) → 修饰 people
>         │     └── 同位语: (a school of design) → 解释 the Bauhaus
>         ├── 谓语1: emigrated to the United States
>         ├── 时间状语: (before World War II)
>         └── 谓语2: took up posts at American architecture schools
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：“少即是多”这一说法实际上最早由德国建筑师路德维希·密斯·凡·德·罗推广开来。他与其他同包豪斯这一设计学校有关的人一样，在第二次世界大战前移民到美国，并在美国的建筑院校任职。
>
> **考点提示**：被动语态 `was popularized by...` 的施动者由 `by` 引出；逗号之间的 `the architect...` 和 `a school of design` 都是同位语；定语从句中 `emigrated` 与 `took up` 是由 `and` 连接的并列谓语。

These designers came to exert enormous influence on the course of American architecture, but none more so than Mies.

Mies's signature phrase means that less decoration, properly organized, has more impact than a lot. Elegance, he believed, did not derive from abundance. Like other modern architects, he employed metal, glass and laminated wood—materials that we take for granted today but that in the 1940s **symbolized** the future. Mies's sophisticated presentation masked the fact that the spaces he designed were small and efficient, rather than big and often empty.

The apartments in the elegant towers Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive, for example, were smaller—two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet—than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The apartments in the elegant towers Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive, for example, were smaller—two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet—than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: The apartments in the elegant towers Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive
> - 谓语 V: were
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: smaller than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast
> - 状语 A: for example
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 介词短语 | in the elegant towers | 修饰 apartments |
> | 定语从句 | Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive | 修饰 towers，关系代词作 built 的宾语并被省略 |
> | 介词短语 | on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive | 修饰 built，说明建造地点 |
> | 插入语 | for example | 修饰全句，表示举例 |
> | 同位语 | two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet | 解释 apartments |
> | 比较结构 | than those in their older neighbors... | 与沿黄金海岸的老建筑中的公寓作比较 |
> | 介词短语 | along the city's Gold Coast | 修饰 older neighbors / buildings，说明位置 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: The apartments ... + were + smaller than those ...
>   ├── 介短: (in the elegant towers ... ) → 修饰 apartments
>   │     └── 定从: (Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive) → 修饰 towers
>   ├── 插入状语: (for example)
>   ├── 系表结构: were + smaller
>   │     └── 同位语: (two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet) → 说明 apartments
>   └── 比较结构: (than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：例如，密斯在芝加哥湖滨大道建造的那些优雅高塔中的公寓，面积比该市黄金海岸沿线老建筑中的公寓更小——两居室套房面积不到 1,000 平方英尺。
>
> **考点提示**：过去分词或关系代词省略会使定语从句紧跟名词后出现；破折号之间的内容是同位语，对前面的 apartments 作补充说明；`比较级 + than` 构成比较结构，`those` 指代前文的 apartments。

But they were popular because of their airy glass walls, the views they afforded and the elegance of the buildings' details and proportions, the architectural equivalent of the abstract art so popular at the time.

The trend toward “less” was not entirely foreign. In the 1930s Frank Lloyd Wright started building more modest and efficient houses—usually around 1,200 square feet—than the spreading two-story ones he had designed in the 1890s and the early 20th century.

The “Case Study Houses” commissioned from talented modern architects by California Arts & Architecture magazine between 1945 and 1962 were yet another homegrown influence on the ==“less is more” trend==.

Aesthetic effect came from the landscape, new materials and forthright detailing.

In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life—few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers—but his belief that **self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable** was widely shared.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life—few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers—but his belief that **self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable** was widely shared.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Ralph Rapson（第一个分句）；his belief（第二个分句）
> - 谓语 V: may have mispredicted；was widely shared
> - 宾语 O: just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life（mispredicted 的宾语，即 how 引导的名词性从句）
> - 补语 C: both desirable and inevitable（名词性从句中 self-sufficiency 的表语）
> - 状语 A: In his Case Study House；though most eventually got clothes dryers（让步状语从句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 介词短语 | In his Case Study House | 修饰 mispredicted，说明语境 |
> | 名词性从句 | just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life | 作 mispredicted 的宾语 |
> | 破折号插入说明 | few American families acquired helicopters | 补充说明前面对机械革命影响的预测 |
> | 让步状语从句 | though most eventually got clothes dryers | 修饰 acquired，形成对比让步 |
> | 同位语从句 | that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable | 解释 belief 的具体内容 |
> | 并列表语 | both desirable and inevitable | 说明 self-sufficiency 的两种性质 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 并列句: [Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted ...] + but + [his belief ... was widely shared]
>   ├── 分句1: Ralph Rapson + may have mispredicted + how-clause
>   │     ├── 时间/场景状语: (In his Case Study House)
>   │     ├── 宾语从句: (just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life)
>   │     └── 破折号插入说明
>   │           └── 让步从句: (though most eventually got clothes dryers)
>   └── 分句2: his belief + was widely shared
>         └── 同位语从句: (that self-sufficiency was both desirable and inevitable)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在他设计的案例研究住宅中，拉尔夫·拉普森或许错误地预判了机械革命究竟会如何影响日常生活——很少有美国家庭拥有直升机，尽管大多数家庭最终都配备了烘干机——但他认为自给自足既值得追求又不可避免，这一信念得到了广泛认同。
>
> **考点提示**：`just how...` 引导名词性从句并作 `mispredicted` 的宾语；`that...` 引导同位语从句，说明 `belief` 的内容；破折号插入语和 `though` 让步从句共同制造补充与对比关系。
