The world is going through the biggest wave of **mergers and acquisitions** ever witnessed. The process sweeps from hyperactive America to Europe and reaches the emerging countries with unsurpassed might. Many in these countries are looking at this process and worrying: "Won't the wave of business concentration turn into an uncontrollable anti-competitive force?"

世界正在经历有史以来规模最大的**并购**浪潮。这一进程从异常活跃的美国席卷到欧洲，并以无可匹敌的力量触及新兴国家。这些国家中的许多人正注视着这一进程并担忧："企业集中的浪潮会不会变成一股不可控的反竞争力量？"

> [!note] 翻译要点
> - **mergers and acquisitions**：并购，常缩写为 M&A，是本文的核心术语
> - **hyperactive**：这里形容美国经济异常活跃，暗示并购活动特别频繁
> - **anti-competitive force**：反竞争力量，指可能抑制市场竞争的垄断行为

---

There's no question that the big are getting bigger and more powerful. **Multinational corporations** accounted for less than 20% of international trade in 1982. Today the figure is more than 25% and growing rapidly. International affiliates account for a fast-growing segment of production in economies that open up and welcome foreign investment. In Argentina, for instance, after the reforms of the early 1990s, multinationals went from 43% to almost 70% of the industrial production of the 200 largest firms. This phenomenon has created serious concerns over the role of smaller economic firms, of national businessmen and over the ultimate stability of the world economy.

毫无疑问，大企业正变得更大、更强。1982年，**跨国公司**仅占国际贸易的不到20%。如今这一数字已超过25%，并且还在快速增长。国际附属公司在那些开放并欢迎外国投资的经济体中，占据了生产中快速增长的份额。以阿根廷为例，在20世纪90年代初的改革之后，跨国公司在200家最大企业的工业产出中所占比例从43%上升到近70%。这一现象引发了人们对小型经济企业、民族商人角色的严重担忧，以及对世界经济最终稳定性的忧虑。

> [!note] 翻译要点
> - **International affiliates**：国际附属公司，指跨国公司在海外设立的子公司或分支机构
> - **ultimate stability**：最终稳定性，强调对全球经济长期安全的深层担忧

---

I believe that the most important forces behind the massive M&A wave are the same that underlie the **globalization** process: falling transportation and communication costs, lower trade and investment barriers and enlarged markets that require enlarged operations capable of meeting customer's demands. All these are beneficial, not detrimental, to consumers. As productivity grows, the world's wealth increases.

我认为，推动这股大规模并购浪潮的最重要力量与推动**全球化**进程的力量相同：运输和通信成本的下降、贸易和投资壁垒的降低，以及需要扩大运营规模以满足客户需求的扩大市场。所有这些对消费者都是有益的而非有害的。随着生产力的增长，世界财富也在增加。

> [!note] 翻译要点
> - **underlie**：构成……的基础，此处指并购浪潮和全球化背后有相同的驱动力
> - **detrimental**：有害的，与 beneficial 形成对比，强调作者对并购的正面评价

---

Examples of benefits or costs of the current concentration wave are scanty. Yet it is hard to imagine that the merger of a few oil firms today could re-create the same threats to competition that were feared nearly a century ago in the U.S., when the **Standard Oil Trust** was broken up. The mergers of telecom companies, such as **WorldCom**, hardly seem to bring higher prices for consumers or a reduction in the pace of technical progress. On the contrary, the price of communications is falling rapidly. In cars, too, concentration is increasing—witness **Daimler and Chrysler**, **Renault and Nissan**—but it does not appear that consumers are being hurt.

当前集中化浪潮的利弊实例还很稀少。然而，很难想象今天几家石油公司的合并会重新制造出近一个世纪前美国**标准石油托拉斯**被拆分时所担忧的那种对竞争的威胁。电信公司的合并，如**世通公司**，似乎不会给消费者带来更高的价格或减缓技术进步的步伐。相反，通信价格正在快速下降。在汽车行业，集中度也在增加——见证**戴姆勒与克莱斯勒**、**雷诺与日产**的合并——但消费者似乎并未受到损害。

> [!note] 翻译要点
> - **Standard Oil Trust**：标准石油托拉斯，1911年因垄断被美国最高法院强制拆分，是反垄断史上的标志性事件
> - **WorldCom**：世通公司，美国电信巨头，2002年因财务丑闻破产，是当时最大的企业破产案之一
> - **witness**：此处用作动词，意为"见证"，用于举例说明

---

Yet the fact remains that the merger movement must be watched. A few weeks ago, **Alan Greenspan** warned against the megamergers in the banking industry. Who is going to supervise, regulate and operate as lender of last resort with the gigantic banks that are being created? Won't multinationals shift production from one place to another when a nation gets too strict about infringements to fair competition? And should one country take upon itself the role of "defending competition" on issues that affect many other nations, as in the U.S. vs. Microsoft case?

然而，事实仍然是，并购运动必须受到监督。几周前，**艾伦·格林斯潘**警告了银行业中的超大规模合并。谁来监督、监管并作为最后贷款人运作那些正在创建的巨型银行？当一个国家对公平竞争的侵权行为过于严格时，跨国公司会不会将生产从一个地方转移到另一个地方？一个国家是否应该在影响许多其他国家的问题上承担"捍卫竞争"的角色，就像美国诉微软案那样？

> [!note] 翻译要点
> - **lender of last resort**：最后贷款人，金融术语，指在金融机构面临倒闭风险时提供紧急贷款的机构（通常为中央银行）
> - **megamergers**：超大规模合并，前缀 mega- 强调规模之大
> - **infringements to fair competition**：对公平竞争的侵权/侵犯
> - **U.S. vs. Microsoft case**：美国政府诉微软反垄断案（1998-2001），是当时最受关注的反垄断案件之一
