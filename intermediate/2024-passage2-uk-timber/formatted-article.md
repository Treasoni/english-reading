# 2024 Passage 2

The UK is facing a future **construction crisis** because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood, Confor has warned.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The UK is facing a future **construction crisis** because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood, Confor has warned.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 宾语从句 O（前置至句首）: (that) the UK is facing a future construction crisis
> - 主语 S（后置）: Confor
> - 谓语 V: has warned
> - 从句主干: the UK + is facing + a future construction crisis
> - 原因状语 A: because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 宾语从句（前置，that 省略） | (that) | has warned |
> | 介词短语作原因状语 | because of | is facing |
> | 名词化 + 不定式作后置定语 | a failure to do | failure |
> | 不定式作后置定语／目的 | to produce wood | plant trees |
> | 前置定语（时间） | future | construction crisis |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句（引述框架，后置）: 〔Confor〕+ has warned + 〔(that) 宾语从句〕
>   └── 宾语从句（前置至句首）: 〔The UK〕+ is facing + 〔a future construction crisis〕
>         ├── 原因状语: because of 〔a failure〕→ 说明"为何面临危机"
>         │     └── 后置定语: to plant trees → 修饰 failure
>         │           └── 目的／结果: to produce wood → 修饰 plant trees
>         └── 定语: future → 修饰 construction crisis
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：英国木材行业组织 Confor 警告称，由于未能种植树木以生产木材，英国未来将面临建筑危机。
>
> **考点提示**：① **引述框架后置**：句首整段是 `warned` 的宾语从句，读到句尾才知道"谁在警告"，考研常在此设"观点归属"题；② `because of + 名词` 与 `because + 从句` 的互换：`a failure to plant trees` = `the UK has failed to plant trees`，名词化把整句因果压进介词宾语；③ `failure / attempt / refusal / hesitation + to do` 一律按"（未）做某事"理解，不可译成"失败的人"；④ `construction crisis` 在第 2 段引语中以 `future construction crisis` 复现，重复即考点，是第 26 题的定位句。

The forestry and wood trade body has called for urgent action to reduce the country's **reliance** on **timber imports** and provide a stable supply of wood for future generations.

Currently only 20 per cent of the UK's wood requirement is **home-grown** while it remains the second-largest **net importer** of timber in the world.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Currently only 20 per cent of the UK's wood requirement is **home-grown** while it remains the second-largest **net importer** of timber in the world.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 状语 A（两句共用）: Currently
> - 分句 1: only 20 per cent of the UK's wood requirement + is + home-grown
> - 对比连词: while
> - 分句 2: it + remains + the second-largest net importer of timber
> - 状语 A（分句 2）: in the world
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 时间状语 | Currently | 两个分句共用 |
> | 对比连词 | while（"而"） | 连接两个分句 |
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | of the UK's wood requirement | 20 per cent |
> | 序数词 + 最高级 | the second-largest | net importer |
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | of timber | importer |
> | 介词短语作状语 | in the world | the second-largest net importer |
> | 代词指代 | it = the UK | 分句 2 主语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔only 20 per cent (of the UK's wood requirement)〕+ is + 〔home-grown〕
>   └── 对比分句: while + 〔it〕+ remains + 〔the second-largest net importer (of timber)〕
>         ├── 指代: it = the UK（不是 wood requirement）
>         └── 状语: in the world → 排名范围
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：目前，英国木材需求中只有 20% 是本国种植的，而英国仍是全球第二大木材净进口国。
>
> **考点提示**：① `while` 在此表**对比**（"而"），不是"当……时"；两个分句都是客观事实，删掉 `while` 换成"而"仍通顺；② `per cent of + 不可数名词` 作主语时，谓语随 `of` 后的名词用单数 `is`；③ `the second-largest net importer`（序数词 + 最高级）与 `only 20 per cent` 构成"**排名 + 数据**"双证据，指向"供应能力不足"而非"需求过大"，是第 26 题 [A] 的唯一依据；④ `it` 必须还原为 `the UK`——若误指 `wood requirement`，"需求量是净进口国"逻辑不通，代词还原是判断正误的硬标准。

Coming at a time of fresh **incentives** from the UK government for landowners to grow more trees, the trade body says these don't go far enough and fail to promote the benefits of planting them to boost timber supplies.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Coming at a time of fresh **incentives** from the UK government for landowners to grow more trees, the trade body says these don't go far enough and fail to promote the benefits of planting them to boost timber supplies.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 时间状语 A（分词短语）: Coming at a time of fresh incentives ... for landowners to grow more trees
> - 主语 S: the trade body
> - 谓语 V: says
> - 宾语从句 O（that 省略）: these don't go far enough and fail to promote the benefits of planting them to boost timber supplies
> - 宾从主干: these + don't go far enough + and + fail to promote + the benefits
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 现在分词短语作时间状语 | Coming at a time of ... | 全句（逻辑主语 the trade body） |
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | from the UK government | incentives |
> | 不定式复合结构作后置定语 | for landowners to grow more trees | incentives |
> | 宾语从句（that 省略） | (that) | says |
> | 并列谓语（宾从内部） | and fail to | these don't go far enough |
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | of planting them | benefits |
> | 不定式作目的状语 | to boost timber supplies | planting them |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Coming at a time of fresh incentives ...〕, 〔the trade body〕+ says + 〔(that) 宾语从句〕
>   ├── 时间状语（分词）: Coming at a time of → 交代发言时机
>   │     ├── 后置定语: from the UK government → 修饰 incentives
>   │     └── 不定式复合结构: for landowners to grow more trees → 激励对象与内容
>   └── 宾语从句: 〔these〕+ don't go far enough + and + fail to promote + 〔the benefits〕
>         ├── 指代: these = fresh incentives
>         ├── 后置定语: of planting them → 修饰 benefits
>         │     └── 目的状语: to boost timber supplies → 修饰 planting
>         └── 否定叠加: don't go far enough + fail to → 双重"不足"
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：就在英国政府刚刚推出激励措施、鼓励土地所有者种植更多树木之际，该行业机构表示，这些措施力度远远不够，也未能宣传种树对增加木材供应的好处。
>
> **考点提示**：① 句首分词短语的逻辑主语是主句主语 `the trade body`（主动关系），不是 `incentives`；② `these` 指代 `fresh incentives`，还原后 `these don't go far enough` = "这些激励措施力度不够"，正是第 27 题 [A] `can hardly address a construction crisis` 的同义改写；③ `go far enough` 是熟词短语（"力度足够"），不可按字面读成"走得够远"；④ `don't go far enough` 与 `fail to promote` 由 `and` 并列，**同属否定评价**，两重否定叠加强调"措施无用"；⑤ 分词状语只是**背景**，第 27 题的判断依据在主句，不能因"时机"误选 [B] `come at a wrong time`。

"Not only are we facing a **carbon crisis** now, but we will also be facing a future construction crisis because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood," said Stuart Goodall, chief executive of Confor.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**："Not only are we facing a **carbon crisis** now, but we will also be facing a future construction crisis because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood," said Stuart Goodall, chief executive of Confor.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 引述框架（倒装）: said + Stuart Goodall + (chief executive of Confor)
> - 引语分句 1（部分倒装）: Not only + are + we + facing + a carbon crisis
> - 状语 A: now
> - 引语分句 2: but + we + will also be facing + a future construction crisis
> - 原因状语 A: because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 部分倒装（否定词置首） | Not only | 引语分句 1 |
> | 并列连词（递进） | but ... also | 两个引语分句 |
> | 介词短语作原因状语 | because of | will be facing |
> | 名词化 + 不定式 | a failure to plant trees to produce wood | because of 的宾语 |
> | 倒装引语（引述框架） | said + 主语 | 引语整体 |
> | 同位语 | chief executive of Confor | Stuart Goodall |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 引述框架（后置 + 倒装）: 〔"引语"〕, said + 〔Stuart Goodall〕, 〔chief executive of Confor〕
>   └── 引语（并列复句，前分句倒装）:
>         ├── 分句 1: Not only + are + 〔we〕+ facing + 〔a carbon crisis〕
>         └── 分句 2: but + 〔we〕+ will also be facing + 〔a future construction crisis〕
>               └── 原因状语: because of 〔a failure to plant trees〕→ 危机的根源
>                     └── 目的／结果: to produce wood → 修饰 plant trees
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：Confor 首席执行官斯图尔特·古德尔说："现在我们不仅面临碳危机，而且由于未能种植树木以生产木材，未来还将面临建筑危机。"
>
> **考点提示**：① `Not only` 置首 → 所在分句**部分倒装**（`are we facing`），后分句用正常语序；还原为 `We are facing not only a carbon crisis but also a future construction crisis`，倒装只为强调，不改变句意；② `said Stuart Goodall, chief executive of Confor` 是**倒装引语 + 同位语**，`chief executive of Confor` 整体是同位语，不可当成新主语，否则下一句引语会误读成"首席执行官说"；③ 引语中的 `carbon crisis / construction crisis` 与第 1 段 `construction crisis` 构成**同义复现链**，"双危机并列"说明问题出在**供给能力**而非"需求过大"，第 26 题可据此排除 [B]；④ `will also be facing` 用将来进行时表"届时正面临"，强调危机的**持续存在**，比一般将来时更有压迫感。

"For decades we have not taken responsibility for investing in our domestic wood supply, leaving us **exposed** to **fluctuating prices** and fighting for future supplies of wood as global demand rises and our own supplies fall."

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**："For decades we have not taken responsibility for investing in our domestic wood supply, leaving us **exposed** to **fluctuating prices** and fighting for future supplies of wood as global demand rises and our own supplies fall."
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 时间状语 A1: For decades
> - 主语 S: we
> - 谓语 V: have not taken
> - 宾语 O: responsibility
> - 后置定语: for investing in our domestic wood supply
> - 结果状语: leaving us exposed to fluctuating prices and fighting for future supplies of wood
> - 状语从句 A2: as global demand rises and our own supplies fall
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 时间状语 | For decades | 主句谓语（配合现在完成时） |
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | for investing in our domestic wood supply | responsibility |
> | 动名词作介词宾语 | investing in | for |
> | 现在分词短语作结果状语 | leaving | 整个主句 |
> | leave + 宾 + 宾补 1（形容词短语） | exposed to fluctuating prices | us |
> | leave + 宾 + 宾补 2（现在分词短语） | fighting for future supplies | us |
> | as 引导伴随／原因状语从句 | as | leaving 的结果 |
> | 反义对举 | rises / fall | 两个从句谓语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔For decades〕〔we〕+ have not taken + 〔responsibility〕
>   ├── 后置定语: for investing in our domestic wood supply → 修饰 responsibility
>   └── 结果状语: leaving + 〔us〕+ 〔exposed to fluctuating prices〕+ and + 〔fighting for future supplies〕
>         ├── 宾补 1（形容词短语）: exposed to ... → us 处于"易受影响"状态
>         │     └── 介短: to fluctuating prices → 受影响的对象
>         ├── 宾补 2（现在分词短语）: fighting for ... → us 持续"争抢"
>         │     └── 状语从句: as global demand rises and our own supplies fall → 争抢的成因
>         └── 反义对举: rises ↔ fall → 需求与供给的剪刀差
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：几十年来，我们一直未承担起投资国内木材供应的责任，这使我们暴露在价格波动的风险之下，并且随着全球需求上升、我们自身供应下降，我们还要为未来的木材供应而争抢。
>
> **考点提示**：① `For decades + 现在完成时否定` 表示"几十年来一直没做（且至今未变）"，不是一般过去时的"过去没做"，这是第 28 题 [B] `inadequate investment in growing wood` 的**时态依据**；② `leaving` 不是"离开"，而是 `leave + 宾语 + 宾补`"使……处于某状态"，宾补共**两个且形式不同**（形容词短语 / 现在分词短语）——平行成分不要求形式一致；③ `exposed to` 是形容词短语、不是谓语，`and fighting` 的并列对象是 `exposed`（同作宾补），判据是**逻辑主语**："争抢"的是 `us` 而非主句主语 `we`；④ `as ... rises and ... fall` 用反义词构成剪刀差，正是 `fluctuating prices` 的成因，也是第 28 题的因果链起点。

The UK has ideal conditions for growing wood to build low-carbon homes and is a global leader in **certifying** that its forests are **sustainably managed**, Confor says.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The UK has ideal conditions for growing wood to build low-carbon homes and is a global leader in **certifying** that its forests are **sustainably managed**, Confor says.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: The UK
> - 谓语 V1: has
> - 宾语 O: ideal conditions for growing wood to build low-carbon homes
> - 并列连词: and
> - 谓语 V2: is
> - 表语 C: a global leader in certifying that its forests are sustainably managed
> - 引述框架（后置）: Confor says
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 并列谓语（共享主语） | and is | The UK |
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | for growing wood | conditions |
> | 不定式作目的状语 | to build low-carbon homes | growing wood |
> | 动名词作介词宾语 | in certifying | in |
> | 宾语从句 | that its forests are sustainably managed | certifying |
> | 被动语态 | are managed | its forests |
> | 方式副词 | sustainably | are managed |
> | 引述框架后置 | Confor says | 全句 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔The UK〕+ has + 〔ideal conditions〕+ and + is + 〔a global leader〕
>   ├── 谓语 1: has → 后置定语 for growing wood
>   │     └── 目的状语: to build low-carbon homes → 修饰 growing wood
>   ├── 谓语 2: is → 表语 a global leader
>   │     └── 后置定语: in certifying + 〔that 宾语从句〕
>   │           └── 宾从: 〔its forests〕+ are + 〔sustainably managed〕（被动）
>   └── 引述框架（后置）: Confor says → 全句观点来源
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：Confor 表示，英国拥有种植木材以建造低碳住宅的理想条件，并且在认证其森林得到可持续管理方面处于全球领先地位。
>
> **考点提示**：① **一个主语带两个并列谓语**（`has` / `is`），中间隔着长宾语，极易误判为两句——判据是 `and` 后**没有新主语**；② `in certifying that ...` 中 `that` 是**宾语从句**（认证的内容），不是定语从句，因为它不修饰某个具体名词；③ `Confor says` 后置作引述框架，全句观点归属 Confor，是题干 "According to Confor" 的定位依据；④ `ideal conditions` 与第 29 题 [B] `Unfavorable conditions for growing wood` **正相反**，反向干扰项就靠这个形容词设置。

It also **encompasses** significant **hesitation** on behalf of farmers and other landowners to invest in longer-term planting projects.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It also **encompasses** significant **hesitation** on behalf of farmers and other landowners to invest in longer-term planting projects.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: It (= The causes)
> - 状语 A: also
> - 谓语 V: encompasses
> - 宾语 O: significant hesitation
> - 后置定语 1: on behalf of farmers and other landowners
> - 后置定语 2: to invest in longer-term planting projects
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 代词指代 | It = The causes | 主语 |
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | on behalf of farmers and other landowners | hesitation |
> | 不定式作后置定语 | to invest in longer-term planting projects | hesitation |
> | 介词短语 | in longer-term planting projects | invest |
> | 复合形容词作定语 | longer-term | planting projects |
> | 程度形容词 | significant | hesitation |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔It (= The causes)〕+ encompasses + 〔significant hesitation〕
>   ├── 后置定语 1: on behalf of farmers and other landowners → 指出"犹豫"归属哪一方
>   └── 后置定语 2: to invest in longer-term planting projects → 指出"犹豫"的具体内容
>         └── 介短: in longer-term planting projects → invest 的对象
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：其中还包括农民和其他土地所有者对投资长期种植项目的明显犹豫。
>

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：While productive tree planting can deliver real financial benefits to rural economies and contribute to the UK's **net-zero** strategy, the focus of government support continues to be on food production and the **rewilding** and planting of native woodland solely for **biodiversity**.
>
