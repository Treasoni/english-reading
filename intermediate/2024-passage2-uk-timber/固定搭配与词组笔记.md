# 2024 Passage 2 固定搭配与词组

> [!note] 使用说明
> 本笔记只收录**固定搭配、短语与主题词**，纯语法结构见 [[intermediate/2024-passage2-uk-timber/grammar-notes.md|2024 Passage 2 语法整理]]。
> 每条均给原文语境，便于回原文核对；方括号内为题号时，表示该搭配是此题的直接依据。

---

## 一、主题词与核心概念

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **construction crisis** | 建筑危机 | facing a future **construction crisis** |
| **carbon crisis** | 碳危机 | we are facing a **carbon crisis** now |
| **timber** | 木材、木料（英式；美式多用 lumber） | reduce the reliance on **timber** imports |
| **timber imports** | 进口木材 | reliance on **timber imports** |
| **home-grown** | 本国种植的、国产的 | only 20 per cent ... is **home-grown** |
| **net importer** | 净进口国 | the second-largest **net importer** of timber |
| **wood requirement** | 木材需求（量） | 20 per cent of the UK's **wood requirement** |
| **forestry**（forest 派生） | 林业 | The **forestry** and wood trade body |
| **trade body** | 行业机构、行业协会 | The forestry and wood **trade body** |
| **woodland** | 林地 | planting of native **woodland** |
| **rewilding**（wild 派生） | 再野化（恢复自然生态） | the **rewilding** and planting of native woodland |
| **biodiversity**（bio + diversity） | 生物多样性 | solely for **biodiversity** |
| **net-zero** | 净零排放 | the UK's **net-zero** strategy |
| **low-carbon** | 低碳的 | to build **low-carbon** homes |
| **sustainable**（sustain 派生） | 可持续的 | forests are **sustainably managed** |
| **productive forestry** | 生产性林业（以产出木材为目的的林业） | outdated perceptions of **productive forestry** |
| **landowner** | 土地所有者 | incentives for **landowners** to grow more trees |
| **rural economies** | 农村经济 | deliver financial benefits to **rural economies** |
| **manufacturing** | 制造业 | wood for construction, **manufacturing** |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 全文核心概念链：**construction crisis / carbon crisis**（问题）→ **reliance on timber imports / net importer**（现状）→ **failure to plant trees / hesitation**（成因）→ **net-zero / biodiversity**（政府关注点偏移）→ **impetus / ambitions**（呼吁）。第 26 题 A 项对应第二环（提高国产供应），第 29 题 D 项对应第三环（农民不愿种树），第 30 题 C 项对应最后一环（加大支持力度）。

> [!warning] 熟词僻义
> `body` 在 `trade body` 中不是"身体"，而是"**机构、团体**"（= organization）。同类：`a governing body` 管理机构、`a regulatory body` 监管机构。这是考研阅读的高频陷阱词。

## 二、动词短语

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **call for** | 呼吁、要求 | has **called for** urgent action |
| **call for much greater impetus** | 呼吁更大的推动力 | Confor is now **calling for much greater impetus** |
| **reduce reliance on** | 减少对……的依赖 | **reduce** the country's **reliance on** timber imports |
| **provide a stable supply of** | 提供稳定的……供应 | **provide a stable supply of** wood |
| **fail to do** | 未能做到 | **fail to** promote the benefits |
| **go far enough** | （措施）力度足够、走得够远 | these **don't go far enough** |
| **take responsibility for** | 为……承担责任 | we **have not taken responsibility for** investing |
| **invest in** | 投资于 | **investing in** our domestic wood supply |
| **be exposed to** | 暴露于、易受……影响 | leaving us **exposed to** fluctuating prices |
| **fight for** | 为……而争抢 | **fighting for** future supplies of wood |
| **range from A to B** | 从 A 到 B 不等、不一而足 | **range from** outdated perceptions **to** the decimation of trees |
| **encompass** | 包含、涵盖 | It also **encompasses** significant hesitation |
| **certify that** | 认证（某事实） | a global leader in **certifying that** its forests are sustainably managed |
| **be built from** | 由……建造 | homes are **built from** Scottish timber |
| **deliver benefits** | 带来收益／好处 | can **deliver** real financial **benefits** |
| **contribute to** | 有助于、为……作贡献 | **contribute to** the UK's net-zero strategy |
| **state an ambition for** | 表明对……的雄心 | has **stated its ambition for** more tree planting |
| **ensure** | 确保 | **to ensure** we have enough wood |
| **meet demand** | 满足需求 | to **meet** increasing **demand** |
| **boost** | 提升、增加 | to **boost** timber supplies |

> [!tip] 同义替换
> `fail to do` = `not do`；`don't go far enough` ≈ `be inadequate`；`call for` = `urge / demand`；`deliver benefits` = `bring benefits`；`contribute to` = `help`。第 27 题 A 项 `can hardly address a construction crisis` 正是 `don't go far enough` 的**同义改写**，这是细节题最典型的改写方式：把"程度不足"换成"几乎无法解决"。

## 三、名词短语与介词搭配

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **reliance on** | 对……的依赖 | **reliance on** timber imports |
| **incentives for sb to do** | 激励某人做某事 | fresh **incentives** ... **for landowners to** grow more trees |
| **conditions for doing** | 做某事的条件 | ideal **conditions for** growing wood |
| **a leader in doing** | 在……方面领先 | a global **leader in** certifying |
| **perceptions of** | 对……的看法／观念 | outdated **perceptions of** productive forestry |
| **decimation of** | 对……的大面积毁坏 | the **decimation of** trees by grey squirrels |
| **hesitation on behalf of sb** | 某人一方的犹豫 | significant **hesitation on behalf of** farmers |
| **investment in** | 对……的投资 | inadequate **investment in** growing wood |
| **focus on** | 对……的关注／重点 | the **focus of** government support ... **on** food production |
| **ambition for** | 对……的雄心 | its **ambition for** more tree planting |
| **impetus behind** | ……背后的推动力 | much greater **impetus behind** those aspirations |
| **supplies of wood** | 木材供应 | secure **supplies of wood** |
| **demand for** | 对……的需求 | increasing **demand** for wood |
| **solely for** | 仅仅为了 | **solely for** biodiversity |
| **exposure to** | 暴露于、易受……影响 | the UK's **exposure to** fluctuating wood prices |
| **fluctuating prices** | 波动的价格 | **fluctuating prices** |
| **be of + 抽象名词** | = be + 形容词 | are clearly **of critical importance** |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `hesitation on behalf of farmers` 中的 `on behalf of` **不译"代表"**，而是"（存在于）农民这一方"——即"农民自身的犹豫"。真正的"代表"义见 `speak on behalf of the trade body`。第 29 题 [D] `Farmers' unwillingness to plant trees` 直接对应此处。
> `be of critical importance` = `be critically important`，`of` 后必须是**抽象名词**（importance / value / use / help），不能说 `of important`。

## 四、固定句式与逻辑连接

| 句式 | 释义／功能 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **Not only ... but ... also ...** | 不仅……而且……（置首时前句倒装） | **Not only** are we facing a carbon crisis, **but** we will **also** be facing ... |
| **While + 让步, 主句** | 尽管……，但…… | **While** productive tree planting can deliver benefits, the focus ... continues to be on food production |
| **While + 对比, 主句** | 而（两个事实对照） | **While** three quarters of Scottish homes are built from Scottish timber, the use in England is only 25 per cent |
| **Coming at a time of ...** | 在……之际（分词状语，交代时机） | **Coming at a time of** fresh incentives |
| **For decades + 现在完成** | 几十年来一直…… | **For decades** we have not taken responsibility |
| **there has been little ...** | 几乎没有…… | **there has been little action on the ground** |
| **on the ground** | 在实地、在实际层面 | little action **on the ground** |
| **because of + 名词** | 由于（后接名词短语） | **because of** a failure to plant trees |
| **range from A to B** | 从 A 到 B 不等 | **range from** outdated perceptions **to** the decimation |
| **X is only around 25 per cent** | 数据对照句式 | the use of home-grown wood in England **is only around 25 per cent** |
| **don't go far enough** | 力度远远不够 | these **don't go far enough** |
| **continues to be on ...** | 重点仍然停留在…… | the focus of government support **continues to be on** food production |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 本文的逻辑骨架是"**承认 → 转折 → 诉求**"：`While` 让步句负责"承认"，`don't go far enough / continues to be / little action` 负责"转折"，`is calling for` 负责"诉求"。第 30 题的答案在"诉求"句：`calling for much greater impetus ... to provide secure supplies of wood`，对应 [C] `Provide more support for productive tree planting`；[A] `Subsidise low-carbon homes`、[B] `boosting rural economies`、[D] `net-zero strategy` 都是原文**提到的旁支信息**，被用来充当"看似相关"的干扰项。

> [!note] 联动复习
> 逻辑信号词的完整语法用法（`While` 四种语义、`Not only` 倒装、`there be` 的否定量词）见 [[intermediate/2024-passage2-uk-timber/grammar-notes.md|2024 Passage 2 语法整理]] 第一节与第四节。
