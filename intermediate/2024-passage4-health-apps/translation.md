# 2024 Passage 4 中英对照翻译

## Paragraph 1

If you look at the apps on your phone, chances are you have at least one related to your health—and probably several. Whether it is a mental health app, a fitness tracker, a connected health device or something else, many of us are taking advantage of this technology to keep better track of our health in some shape or form. Recent research from the Organization for the Review of Care and Health Applications found that 350,000 **health apps** were available on the market, 90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone.

如果你看看手机上的应用，很可能至少有一个与健康有关——而且可能有好几个。无论是心理健康应用、健身追踪器、联网健康设备，还是别的什么，我们中的许多人都正在以某种形式利用这项技术，来更好地追踪自己的健康状况。医疗与健康应用评估组织最近的一项研究发现，市场上可用的**健康应用**有 35 万个，其中仅 2020 年就有 9 万个上线。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `chances are (that) you have ...` 是**省略 that 的表语从句**，`chances are` = "很可能"，比 `probably` 更口语化；`at least one related to your health` 中 `related to ...` 是**过去分词短语作后置定语**，修饰被省略的 `app`。
> - `Whether it is A, B, C or something else` 是**让步状语从句**"无论是……还是……"，列举四类产品后引出主句，阅读时可整体跳过。
> - `take advantage of` = "利用"（中性偏褒），与 `make use of` 近义；`keep better track of` = "更好地追踪/记录"，`track` 在此为名词。
> - `in some shape or form` 是**习语**"以某种形式"，相当于 `in one way or another`。
> - `90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone` 是**"名词/数词 + of which"引导的非限制性定语从句**，`which` 指 `350,000 health apps`；`alone` 在此意为"仅仅、单是"，不是"独自"。
> - 本段用"人人都在用 + 35 万/9 万"两组数据，功能是**引出话题并渲染普遍性**，对应第 36 题 [A] `the prevalence of health apps`；数据本身不是论点，而是论点的支撑。

## Paragraph 2

While these apps have a great deal to offer, it is not always clear how the personal information we input is collected, **safeguarded** and shared online. Existing health privacy law, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, is primarily focused on the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online. The health information these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting typically do not receive the same legal protections.

尽管这些应用能提供很多东西，但我们输入的个人信息如何被收集、保护和在线共享，却并不总是清晰透明。现有的健康隐私法，例如《健康保险携带和责任法案》，主要关注医院、医生诊所、诊所和保险公司如何在线存储健康记录。而这些应用和健康数据追踪可穿戴设备所收集的健康信息，通常得不到同等的法律保护。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `While these apps have a great deal to offer` 中 `while` 表**让步**"尽管"，不是"当……时"；`have a great deal to offer` = "有很多可取之处"。
> - `it is not always clear how ...` 中 `it` 是**形式主语**，真正主语是 `how` 引导的**主语从句**；`how the personal information ... is collected, safeguarded and shared` 中三个过去分词并列，共用 `is`。
> - `the personal information we input` 是**省略关系代词**的定语从句（= `that we input`），`input` 在此为动词"输入"。
> - `is primarily focused on the way hospitals ... store health records online` 中 `the way` 后**省略了 `that/in which`**，是考研高频考点；`doctors' offices` 指"医生诊室"，不是"医生办公室"的行政含义。
> - `The health information these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting` 同样是**省略关系代词的定语从句**，主语核心是 `The health information`，谓语 `do not receive` 用复数是因为主语中心词 `information` 在此按"各类信息"理解（也可视为命题者笔误，考试时以"这些信息"整体理解即可）。
> - 本段是**转折段**：`While ... to offer` 先扬，`it is not always clear` 后抑，落点在"法律保护不一致"，为第 37 题"覆盖范围需扩大"埋下伏笔。

## Paragraph 3

Without additional protections in place, companies may share (and potentially **monetize**) personal health information in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated. In 2021, Flo Health faced a Federal Trade Commission(FTC) investigation. The FTC alleged in a complaint that “despite express privacy claims, the company took control of users’ **sensitive fertility data** and shared it with third parties.” Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a **Consent Order** requiring the company to get app users’ **express affirmative consent** before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.

如果没有额外的保护措施，公司可能会以消费者未曾授权或预料的方式共享（并可能从中获利）个人健康信息。2021 年，Flo Health 公司面临联邦贸易委员会（FTC）的调查。FTC 在一份投诉中称，"尽管有明确的隐私声明，该公司却控制了用户的**敏感生育数据**，并将其与第三方共享。"Flo Health 与 FTC 以一项**《同意令》**了结此事，该命令要求公司在共享用户健康信息之前，必须获得应用用户的**明确肯定同意**，并要求其指示第三方删除已获取的数据。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `Without additional protections in place` 是**介词短语表条件**"如果没有……"，`in place` = "到位、落实"，不是"在地方"。
> - `may share (and potentially monetize) personal health information` 中括号是**插入补充**，`monetize` = "变现、从中获利"，是本文批评商业公司的关键词。
> - `in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated` 中 `in a way` 后**省略 `that/in which`**，`consumers may not have ...` 是定语从句；`authorize`"授权"与 `anticipate`"预料"并列，强调"既未同意也未想到"。
> - `alleged in a complaint that ...` 中 `allege` = "（未经证实地）指控、宣称"，新闻法律类文章高频词；`complaint` 在此是法律术语"起诉书、投诉书"。
> - `despite express privacy claims` 中 `express` 是形容词"明确的"（不是动词"表达"），`claims` 指"（隐私）声明"。
> - `took control of users' sensitive fertility data` 中 `fertility data` 指"生育/生殖健康数据"，是本段最敏感的数据类型；`took control of` 比 `collected` 语气更重，暗含"擅自掌控"。
> - `settled the matter with a Consent Order` 中 `settle` = "了结（纠纷）"，`Consent Order` 是法律术语"同意令"（企业不认罪但接受监管要求）。
> - `requiring the company to get ... as well as to instruct ...` 中 `requiring` 是**现在分词作后置定语**修饰 `Consent Order`，后接**两个并列的不定式** `to get` 与 `to instruct`；`express affirmative consent` = "明确、肯定的同意"，`affirmative` 强调"主动作出"而非默示，对应第 38 题 [D] `obtain their explicit permission`。

## Paragraph 4

Section 5 of the FTC Act empowers the FTC to initiate **enforcement action** against unfair or **deceptive** acts, meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm. While the FTC is doing what it can to ensure apps are keeping their promises to consumers around the handling of their sensitive health information, the rate at which these health apps are hitting the market demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is.

《联邦贸易委员会法》第 5 条授权 FTC 对不公平或**欺骗性**行为提起**执法行动**，这意味着，只有当公司的隐私做法具有误导性、或造成不合理的消费者损害时，FTC 才能在事后采取行动。尽管 FTC 正尽其所能确保应用在敏感健康信息的处理上对消费者信守承诺，但这些健康应用进入市场的速度表明，这一挑战有多么巨大。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `empowers the FTC to initiate enforcement action against ...` 中 `empower sb to do` = "授权某人做某事"；`initiate` = "发起、提起"；`enforcement action` = "执法行动"。
> - `unfair or deceptive acts` 是**法律固定表述**"不公平或欺骗性行为"，`deceptive` = "欺骗性的"，与后文 `misleading`（误导性的）呼应。
> - `meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if ...` 中 `meaning` 是**现在分词作解释性状语**（= "也就是说"），后接 `if` 条件从句；`after the fact` = "事后"，点明监管的**滞后性**。
> - `cause unjustified consumer harm` 中 `unjustified` = "无正当理由的、不合理的"，是法律文本的限定词，不能漏译。
> - `While the FTC is doing what it can to ensure ...` 中 `while` 表**让步**；`what it can` 是**宾语从句**（= "它所能做的"）；`ensure apps are keeping their promises` 后省略 `that`。
> - `the rate at which these health apps are hitting the market` 是**"介词 + which"引导的定语从句**，`at the rate` 是固定搭配；`hit the market` = "进入市场、上市"。
> - `demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is` 中 `how immense of a challenge this is` 是**感叹意义的宾语从句**，`immense` = "巨大的"；全句逻辑是"监管只能事后补救 + 新应用涌入速度极快 = 挑战巨大"，对应第 39 题 [B]。

## Paragraph 5

As to the prospects for federal legislation, commentators suggest that **comprehensive federal privacy legislation** seems unlikely in the short term. States have begun implementing their own solutions to **shore up** protections for consumer-generated health data. California has been at the forefront of state privacy efforts with the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018. Virginia, Colorado and Utah have also recently passed state consumer data privacy legislation.

至于联邦立法的前景，评论人士认为，**全面的联邦隐私立法**短期内似乎不太可能。各州已开始实施自己的解决方案，以**加强**对消费者生成的健康数据的保护。加利福尼亚州凭借 2018 年《加州消费者隐私法》，一直走在州级隐私保护努力的前列。弗吉尼亚州、科罗拉多州和犹他州最近也通过了州级消费者数据隐私立法。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `As to the prospects for federal legislation` 中 `as to` = "至于、关于"，`prospects` 用复数表"前景"；`legislation` 是**不可数名词**"立法"，不是"法律条文"。
> - `commentators suggest that ... seems unlikely` 中 `suggest` 在此是"认为、表示"，**不用虚拟语气**（虚拟用法常见于 `suggest that sb (should) do`）。
> - `comprehensive federal privacy legislation` = "全面的联邦隐私立法"，`comprehensive` 是本文与"碎片化州法"对照的关键词。
> - `implementing their own solutions to shore up protections` 中 `to shore up` 是**不定式表目的**，`shore up` 原义"支撑、加固"，引申为"加强、巩固"，是外刊高频短语。
> - `consumer-generated health data` = "消费者生成的健康数据"，指用户自己上传、记录的数据，与医院病历相对。
> - `at the forefront of` = "处于……的最前沿"，`with the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018` 是**with 短语表凭借/伴随**。
> - 本段结构：**联邦层面不行（unlikely）→ 各州先行（States have begun）→ 加州领跑 → 另三州跟进**，落点是"州层面已有立法支持"，对应第 40 题 [D] `has gained legislative support in some states`。
