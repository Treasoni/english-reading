# 2024 Passage 4 固定搭配与词组

> [!note] 使用说明
> 本笔记只收录**固定搭配、短语与主题词**，纯语法结构见 [[intermediate/2024-passage4-health-apps/grammar-notes.md|2024 Passage 4 语法整理]]。
> 每条均给原文语境，便于回原文核对；标注题号者，表示该搭配是此题的**直接依据**或**干扰源**。

---

## 一、主题词与核心概念

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **health app** | 健康应用 | 350,000 **health apps** were available on the market |
| **mental health app** | 心理健康应用 | a **mental health app**, a fitness tracker, a connected health device |
| **fitness tracker** | 健身追踪器 | a **fitness tracker** |
| **wearable**（名词化） | 可穿戴设备 | health data tracking **wearables** are collecting |
| **connected health device** | 联网健康设备 | a **connected health device** |
| **health data** | 健康数据 | **health data** tracking wearables |
| **privacy** | 隐私 | express **privacy** claims / **privacy** practices / **privacy** law |
| **privacy law** | 隐私法 | Existing health **privacy law** |
| **legal protection** | 法律保护 | do not receive the same **legal protections** |
| **consent** | 同意 | express affirmative **consent** |
| **legislation** | 立法（不可数） | comprehensive federal privacy **legislation** |
| **enforcement** | 执法、执行 | **enforcement** action |
| **consumer harm** | 消费者损害 | cause unjustified **consumer harm** |
| **fertility data** | 生育数据 | users' sensitive **fertility data** |
| **health record** | 健康记录、病历 | store **health records** online |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 全文概念链：**health apps 普及**（P1 现象）→ **privacy law 覆盖不足**（P2 问题）→ **Flo Health 案：数据被共享/变现**（P3 例证）→ **FTC 只能事后执法 + 应用激增**（P4 困境）→ **联邦难立法、各州先行**（P5 出路）。五道题正好沿这条链排布：P1→36、P2→37、P3→38、P4→39、P5→40。

> [!warning] 熟词僻义
> `wearable` 原本是形容词"可穿戴的"，本文名词化指"**可穿戴设备**"（如智能手表、手环）。同类：`edible`（可食用的 → 食品）、`consumable`（可消耗的 → 消耗品）。

## 二、动词短语

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **take advantage of** | 利用（中性偏褒） | many of us are **taking advantage of** this technology |
| **keep track of** | 追踪、记录 | to **keep better track of** our health |
| **be related to** | 与……有关 | at least one **related to** your health |
| **share ... with ...** | 与……共享…… | **shared** it **with** third parties |
| **monetize** | 变现、从中获利 | (and potentially **monetize**) personal health information |
| **take control of** | 控制、掌控（语气重于 collect） | the company **took control of** users' sensitive fertility data |
| **settle the matter** | 了结此事 | **settled the matter** with a Consent Order |
| **allege** | （未经证实地）指控 | The FTC **alleged** in a complaint that ... |
| **initiate enforcement action against** | 对……提起执法行动 | **initiate enforcement action against** unfair or deceptive acts |
| **cause harm** | 造成损害 | **cause** unjustified consumer **harm** |
| **keep one's promise to** | 对……信守承诺 | ensure apps are **keeping their promises to** consumers |
| **hit the market** | 进入市场、上市 | these health apps are **hitting the market** |
| **shore up** | 加强、巩固（原义"支撑"） | **shore up** protections for consumer-generated health data |
| **implement** | 实施、落实 | States have begun **implementing** their own solutions |
| **pass legislation** | 通过立法 | have also recently **passed** state ... **legislation** |
| **instruct sb to do** | 指示某人做某事 | **instruct** the third parties **to delete** the data |
| **obtain** | 获得、取得 | the data they had **obtained** |
| **authorize** | 授权、批准 | consumers may not have **authorized** |
| **anticipate** | 预料、预期 | or **anticipated** |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 38 题问 Flo Health 被要求做什么，核心动词是 `get ... consent`（获得同意），同义改写为 [D] `obtain their explicit permission`。**`get / obtain / secure + consent / permission / approval` 是同一语义族**，考研喜欢在这组词上做同义替换。

> [!warning] 易错点
> `take control of` 比 `collect` 语气重得多：`collect` 是"收集"，`take control of` 是"**擅自掌控**"。FTC 用这个词是为了强调公司**越界**，不是中性描述。若把 `took control of` 理解为"收集了"，会漏掉作者的批评态度。

## 三、法律与监管术语

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act** | 《健康保险携带和责任法案》（HIPAA） | Existing health privacy law, such as the **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act** |
| **Federal Trade Commission (FTC)** | 联邦贸易委员会 | faced a **Federal Trade Commission(FTC)** investigation |
| **investigation** | 调查 | faced an **investigation** |
| **complaint**（法律义） | 起诉书、投诉书 | alleged in a **complaint** that ... |
| **Consent Order** | 同意令（企业不认罪但接受监管要求） | settled the matter with a **Consent Order** |
| **express affirmative consent** | 明确、肯定的同意 | get app users' **express affirmative consent** |
| **Section 5 of the FTC Act** | 《联邦贸易委员会法》第 5 条 | **Section 5 of the FTC Act** empowers the FTC |
| **empower sb to do** | 授权某人做某事 | **empowers the FTC to initiate** enforcement action |
| **unfair or deceptive acts** | 不公平或欺骗性行为 | against **unfair or deceptive acts** |
| **after the fact** | 事后 | the FTC can only act **after the fact** |
| **misleading** | 误导性的 | if a company's privacy practices are **misleading** |
| **unjustified** | 无正当理由的、不合理的 | cause **unjustified** consumer harm |
| **settle**（法律义） | 了结（纠纷） | **settled** the matter |
| **shore up protections** | 加强保护 | **shore up protections** for consumer-generated health data |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `Consent Order` 是美国监管中的常见和解工具：企业**不承认违法**，但接受监管机构的具体要求（如本案的"获得明确同意 + 要求第三方删数据"）。它与"罚款"不同，重点在**行为整改**。

> [!warning] 易错点
> `allege` ≠ `prove`。`The FTC alleged ...` 只是"FTC 指控"，**未经法院证实**。选项若写 `The FTC proved that ...`，即为**偷换语气**。同理，`complaint` 在此不是"抱怨"，而是法律文书"起诉书/投诉书"。

## 四、介词短语与固定表达

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **in some shape or form** | 以某种形式 | in **some shape or form** |
| **in place** | 到位、落实（不是"在地方"） | Without additional protections **in place** |
| **in a way** | 以……的方式 | **in a way** consumers may not have authorized |
| **at the rate (of)** | 以……的速度 | **the rate at which** these health apps are hitting the market |
| **at the forefront of** | 处于……的最前沿 | California has been **at the forefront of** state privacy efforts |
| **in the short term** | 短期内 | seems unlikely **in the short term** |
| **as to** | 至于、关于 | **As to** the prospects for federal legislation |
| **such as** | 例如 | **such as** the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act |
| **without + 名词** | 如果没有…… | **Without** additional protections in place |
| **despite + 名词** | 尽管（后不接句子） | **despite** express privacy claims |
| **primarily focused on** | 主要聚焦于 | **is primarily focused on** the way hospitals ... store health records |
| **be associated with**（对比记忆） | 与……相关（见 Passage 3） | — |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> `at the forefront of` + `has been`（现在完成时）是第 40 题的关键：加州"一直处于最前沿"，说明**州层面已有立法行动**，对应 [D] `has gained legislative support in some states`。[C] `has encountered opposition in California` 与 `at the forefront` 的正面语义相反，可直接排除。

> [!warning] 易错点
> `in place` 是本文最高频的"假熟词"：`protections in place` = "已落实的保护措施"，`the law is in place` = "法律已生效"。若译成"在地方"，整句逻辑断裂。

## 五、易混词辨析

| 词对 | 区别 | 本文用法 |
|------|------|----------|
| **privacy vs. confidentiality** | `privacy` 是"隐私（权）"，侧重个人控制信息；`confidentiality` 是"保密（义务）"，侧重不向外泄露 | 本文用 **privacy**（health privacy law）；Passage 3 用 **confidentiality**（a breach of confidentiality） |
| **allege vs. claim** | `allege` 是法律语境"（未经证实）指控"；`claim` 更中性"声称" | The FTC **alleged** ... |
| **ensure vs. assure** | `ensure` = "确保（某事发生）"，接 that 从句；`assure` = "向某人保证"，接 sb | **ensure** apps are keeping their promises |
| **authorize vs. anticipate** | `authorize` = "授权、同意"；`anticipate` = "预料、预期" | consumers may not have **authorized or anticipated** |
| **protect vs. safeguard** | 同义，`safeguard` 更正式，常与数据/权利搭配 | is collected, **safeguarded** and shared |
| **share vs. disclose** | `share` 中性"共享"；`disclose` 正式"披露"（常含"本应保密"意味） | companies may **share** ... personal health information |
| **unfair vs. deceptive** | `unfair` = "不公平"（结果层面）；`deceptive` = "欺骗性"（手段层面） | **unfair or deceptive** acts |
| **comprehensive vs. overall** | `comprehensive` = "全面的、综合的"（强调覆盖面）；`overall` = "总体的" | **comprehensive** federal privacy legislation |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `privacy` 与 `confidentiality` 的区分是本文与 Passage 3 的**联动考点**：Passage 3 讲医生上报是否构成 `breach of confidentiality`（违反保密义务），本文讲健康应用是否侵犯 `privacy`（隐私）。前者是**职业伦理**，后者是**数据权利**。

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 37 题问"现有健康隐私法暗示了什么"，答案 [A] `Its coverage needs to be extended` 的依据是：法律只覆盖 `hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies`，而 `health apps and wearables ... do not receive the same legal protections`。**"覆盖范围"对应 `the same legal protections` 的缺失**，不是"执法不力"（[B]）——本文批评的是**覆盖范围**，监管滞后是 P4 的另一层问题。

## 六、跨篇联动

| 关联点 | 联动内容 |
|--------|---------|
| `privacy` ↔ Passage 3 `confidentiality` | 数据隐私 vs. 职业保密，同属"信息边界"主题 |
| `enforcement action` ↔ Passage 3 `act in accordance with` | 执法/守法词汇族，法律类文章高频 |
| `shore up protections` ↔ Passage 3 `do more to improve safety` | "加强保护/做得更多"的目的表达 |
| `at the forefront of` ↔ Passage 3 `piecemeal` | 正面"领先" vs. 负面"零散"，评价性副词/短语是态度题信号 |
| `seems unlikely` ↔ Passage 3 `won't solve` | 委婉否定 vs. 直接否定，推断题需区分语气强弱 |
