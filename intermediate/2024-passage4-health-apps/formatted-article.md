# 2024 Passage 4

If you look at the apps on your phone, chances are you have at least one related to your health—and probably several.

Whether it is a mental health app, a fitness tracker, a connected health device or something else, many of us are taking advantage of this technology to keep better track of our health in some shape or form.

Recent research from the Organization for the Review of Care and Health Applications found that 350,000 **health apps** were available on the market, 90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Recent research from the Organization for the Review of Care and Health Applications found that 350,000 **health apps** were available on the market, 90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Recent research
> - 后置定语: from the Organization for the Review of Care and Health Applications
> - 谓语 V: found
> - 宾语从句 O: that 350,000 health apps were available on the market
> - 宾语从句主干: 350,000 health apps + were + available
> - 非限制性定语从句: 90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | from the Organization ... | research |
> | 宾语从句 | that | found |
> | 数词 + of which 引导的非限制性定语从句 | 90,000 of which | 350,000 health apps |
> | 形容词作表语 | available | were |
> | 介词短语作地点状语 | on the market | were available |
> | 时间状语 | in 2020 alone | launched |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Recent research (from the Organization ...)〕+ found + 〔that 宾语从句〕
>   ├── 宾从主干: 〔350,000 health apps〕+ were + 〔available〕+ (on the market)
>   │     └── 非限定定从: 90,000 of which + launched + (in 2020 alone)
>   │           ※ which = 350,000 health apps，从存量中拆出 9 万增量
>   └── 数据功能: 35 万（存量）+ 9 万（2020 单年增量）→ 渲染"普遍性"
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：医疗与健康应用评估组织最近的一项研究发现，市场上可用的健康应用有 35 万个，其中仅 2020 年就有 9 万个上线。
>
> **考点提示**：① `found that ...` 是**宾语从句**，`that` 在正式书面语中通常保留（语法上仍可省略），从句内部 `350,000 health apps were available` 自带主谓；② `90,000 of which launched` 是**"数词 + of which"引导的非限制性定语从句**，`which` 指 `350,000 health apps`，不是指 `the market`；③ `launched` 在此是**不及物动词过去式**"上线、推出"，不是被动语态；④ `alone` 意为"仅仅、单是"，不是"独自"；⑤ 本句功能是**用数据渲染普遍性**，第 36 题 [A] `the prevalence of health apps` 即其同义概括，[C] `the popularity of smartphones` 只抓了句首的手机，属**以偏概全**。

While these apps have a great deal to offer, it is not always clear how the personal information we input is collected, **safeguarded** and shared online.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：While these apps have a great deal to offer, it is not always clear how the personal information we input is collected, **safeguarded** and shared online.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 让步状语从句 A: While these apps have a great deal to offer
> - 形式主语: it
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: not always clear
> - 真正主语（主语从句）: how the personal information we input is collected, safeguarded and shared online
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 让步状语从句 | While | 主句 |
> | 形式主语 | it | is not always clear |
> | 主语从句（真正主语） | how | 形式主语 it |
> | 省略关系代词的定语从句 | (that/which) | the personal information |
> | 平行被动 | is + collected / safeguarded / shared | the personal information |
> | 地点状语 | online | is shared |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔it〕+ is + 〔not always clear〕+ 〔how 主语从句〕
>   ├── 让步状语从句: While + 〔these apps〕+ have + 〔a great deal to offer〕→ 先扬后抑
>   └── 主语从句: 〔the personal information (we input)〕+ is + collected, safeguarded and shared + (online)
>         ├── 省略关系代词: we input → 修饰 information
>         └── 三个过去分词共用 is → 平行被动：被收集、被保护、被共享
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：尽管这些应用能提供很多东西，但我们输入的个人信息如何被收集、保护和在线共享，却并不总是清晰透明。
>
> **考点提示**：① `While` 在句首且主句用 `not always clear` 转折，是**让步**"尽管"，不是"当……时"；② `it is not always clear how ...` 中 `it` 是**形式主语**，真正主语是 `how` 引导的**主语从句**，从句用陈述语序，**不倒装**；③ `the personal information we input` 是**省略关系代词**的定语从句，`we input` 不是主句谓语；④ `is collected, safeguarded and shared` 是**三个过去分词共用一个 `is`** 的平行被动，翻译时三个动作要分别落地；⑤ `not always clear` 是**部分否定**（`not always` = "并不总是"），不是全部否定——作者并未说"完全不清楚"，这为下文批评"法律覆盖不足"留出余地。

Existing health privacy law, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, is primarily focused on the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Existing health privacy law, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, is primarily focused on the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Existing health privacy law
> - 插入语（举例）: such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
> - 谓语 V: is primarily focused on
> - 宾语 O: the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online
> - 定语从句: (that/in which) hospitals ... store health records online
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 举例插入语 | such as | Existing health privacy law |
> | 省略关系词的定语从句 | (that/in which) | the way |
> | 四个并列名词作从句主语 | and | hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics, insurance companies |
> | 方式副词 | primarily | is focused |
> | 地点/方式状语 | online | store |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Existing health privacy law〕+ is + primarily focused on + 〔the way ...〕
>   ├── 插入语: such as the HIPAA → 举例，删除后主干完整
>   └── 定从（省略 that/in which）: 〔hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies〕+ store + 〔health records〕+ (online)
>         └── 四个主体并列 → 法律的覆盖范围
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：现有的健康隐私法，例如《健康保险携带和责任法案》，主要关注医院、医生办公室、诊所和保险公司如何在线存储健康记录。
>
> **考点提示**：① `the way` 后接定语从句时用 `that` 或 `in which`，**且两者都可省略**；**不能用 `how`**（`the way how ...` 是错误搭配）；② `such as ...` 是**插入举例**，删除后 `Existing health privacy law is primarily focused on ...` 主干完整；③ `is primarily focused on` 中 `primarily` 修饰 `focused`，强调"主要"而非"全部"，为后文"应用不受保护"留出对比空间；④ 本句列出**四类受监管主体**，恰好对应第 37 题 [A] `Its coverage needs to be extended` 中"coverage（覆盖范围）"一词——法律只管医院诊所，不管应用。

The health information these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting typically do not receive the same legal protections.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The health information these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting typically do not receive the same legal protections.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: The health information
> - 定语从句: (that/which) these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting
> - 状语 A: typically
> - 谓语 V: do not receive
> - 宾语 O: the same legal protections
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 省略关系代词的定语从句 | (that/which) | The health information |
> | 并列主语 | and | these apps / health data tracking wearables |
> | 复合名词作定语 | health data tracking | wearables |
> | 频度副词作状语 | typically | do not receive |
> | 形容词短语作定语 | the same | legal protections |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔The health information (these apps and ... wearables are collecting)〕+ typically + do not receive + 〔the same legal protections〕
>   ├── 定从: 〔these apps and health data tracking wearables〕+ are collecting → 修饰 information
>   │     └── 主语并列: these apps ↔ health data tracking wearables
>   └── 主谓一致: information（不可数）+ do not receive → 按"各类信息"理解
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：而这些应用和健康数据追踪可穿戴设备所收集的健康信息，通常得不到同等的法律保护。
>
> **考点提示**：① 句子真正的谓语是 `do not receive`，前面的 `are collecting` 是**定语从句谓语**，读到 `are collecting` 就断句会找不到主句；② `The health information ... do not receive` 中 `information` 通常不可数，此处用复数谓语属**意义一致**（按"各类信息"理解），不要误判主语为 `wearables`；③ `health data tracking wearables` 是**多层名词定语**，中心词是 `wearables`，整体译"健康数据追踪可穿戴设备"；④ `the same legal protections` 中的 `same` 回指上句医院病历所受的保护，是第 37 题 [A] `Its coverage needs to be extended` 的**直接依据**——"同等保护"缺失即"覆盖范围需扩大"。

Without additional protections in place, companies may share (and potentially **monetize**) personal health information in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Without additional protections in place, companies may share (and potentially **monetize**) personal health information in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 条件状语 A: Without additional protections in place
> - 主语 S: companies
> - 谓语 V: may share
> - 宾语 O: personal health information
> - 插入语: (and potentially monetize)
> - 方式状语（定语从句）: in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语表条件 | Without | 主句 |
> | 后置定语/补足语 | in place | additional protections |
> | 括号插入并列谓语 | and potentially | share |
> | 省略关系词的定语从句 | (that/in which) | a way |
> | 现在完成时推测 | may not have + 过去分词 | consumers |
> | 并列过去分词 | or | authorized / anticipated |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: (Without additional protections in place) + 〔companies〕+ may share + 〔personal health information〕
>   ├── 括号插入: (and potentially monetize) → 与 share 并列，加强批评
>   └── 方式状语（定从）: in a way (consumers may not have authorized or anticipated)
>         └── 推测语气: may not have done → "可能从未同意过"
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如果没有额外的保护措施，公司可能会以消费者未曾授权或预料的方式共享（并可能从中获利）个人健康信息。
>
> **考点提示**：① `Without ...` 是**介词短语表条件**"如果没有"，相当于 `If there are no ...`；② `in place` 是习语"**到位、落实**"，`protections in place` = "已落实的保护措施"，不能译"在地方"；③ 括号 `(and potentially monetize)` 是**插入的并列谓语**，`potentially` 是**态度词**——不只是"共享"，还可能"变现"，作者对公司的批评正在此处；④ `in a way consumers may not have authorized` 中 `in a way` 后**省略了 `that/in which`**，`consumers may not have ...` 是定语从句；⑤ `may not have authorized or anticipated` 是**"情态 + 完成式"的推测**："可能从未授权、也未曾预料"，语气比 `did not` 委婉，但否定含义明确。

In 2021, Flo Health faced a Federal Trade Commission(FTC) investigation.

The FTC alleged in a complaint that “despite express privacy claims, the company took control of users’ **sensitive fertility data** and shared it with third parties.”

Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a **Consent Order** requiring the company to get app users’ **express affirmative consent** before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a **Consent Order** requiring the company to get app users’ **express affirmative consent** before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Flo Health and the FTC
> - 谓语 V: settled
> - 宾语 O: the matter
> - 方式状语 A: with a Consent Order
> - 现在分词后置定语: requiring the company to get ... as well as to instruct ...
> - 宾语补足语 1: to get app users' express affirmative consent
> - 时间状语: before sharing their health information
> - 宾语补足语 2: to instruct the third parties to delete the data
> - 定语从句: they had obtained
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 现在分词短语作后置定语 | requiring ... | a Consent Order |
> | 不定式作宾语补足语 | to get ... | the company |
> | 时间状语（介词 + 动名词） | before sharing | to get |
> | 并列不定式 | as well as to instruct | to get |
> | 不定式作宾语补足语（嵌套） | to delete | the third parties |
> | 省略关系代词的定语从句 | (that/which) | the data |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Flo Health and the FTC〕+ settled + 〔the matter〕+ (with a Consent Order)
>   └── 分词定语: requiring + 〔the company〕
>         ├── 要求 1: to get + 〔app users' express affirmative consent〕+ (before sharing their health information)
>         └── 要求 2: as well as to instruct + 〔the third parties〕+ to delete + 〔the data (they had obtained)〕
>               ※ as well as 后为附加要求，层次低于要求 1
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：Flo Health 与 FTC 以一项《同意令》了结此事，该命令要求公司在共享用户健康信息之前，必须获得应用用户的明确肯定同意，并要求其指示第三方删除已获取的数据。
>
> **考点提示**：① `requiring ...` 是**现在分词作后置定语**，逻辑主语是 `a Consent Order`（命令"要求"公司），不是 `Flo Health`；② `require sb to do` 中 `to get` 是**宾语补足语**；③ `before sharing ...` 是**介词 + 动名词**作时间状语，`sharing` 的逻辑主语是 `the company`；④ `as well as to instruct` 与前面的 `to get` **并列**，但 `as well as` 后的成分层次较低（附加要求）；⑤ `instruct the third parties to delete` 是**嵌套的宾补结构**（instruct sb to do），`to delete` 的逻辑主语是 `the third parties`；⑥ 第 38 题 [D] `obtain their explicit permission` 正是 `get app users' express affirmative consent` 的同义改写，[A] `seek the approval of the FTC` 主客颠倒——是 FTC 要求公司，不是公司求 FTC 批准。

Section 5 of the FTC Act empowers the FTC to initiate **enforcement action** against unfair or **deceptive** acts, meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Section 5 of the FTC Act empowers the FTC to initiate **enforcement action** against unfair or **deceptive** acts, meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Section 5 of the FTC Act
> - 谓语 V: empowers
> - 宾语 O: the FTC
> - 宾语补足语 C: to initiate enforcement action
> - 状语 A: against unfair or deceptive acts
> - 解释性状语（分词）: meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if ...
> - 条件状语从句: if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | of the FTC Act | Section 5 |
> | 不定式作宾语补足语 | to initiate ... | the FTC |
> | 介词短语作状语 | against ... | initiate |
> | 现在分词作解释性状语 | meaning | 前句整句 |
> | 条件状语从句 | if | can only act |
> | 并列谓语 | or | are misleading / cause |
> | 形容词作定语 | unjustified | consumer harm |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Section 5 (of the FTC Act)〕+ empowers + 〔the FTC〕+ 〔to initiate enforcement action〕+ (against unfair or deceptive acts)
>   └── 解释状语: meaning + 〔the FTC〕+ can only act + (after the fact) + 〔if 条件从句〕
>         └── 条件: 〔a company's privacy practices〕+ are misleading + or + cause + 〔unjustified consumer harm〕
>   ※ only ... if = 必要条件："只有……才能"
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：《联邦贸易委员会法》第 5 条授权 FTC 对不公平或欺骗性行为提起执法行动，这意味着，只有当公司的隐私做法具有误导性、或造成不合理的消费者损害时，FTC 才能在事后采取行动。
>
> **考点提示**：① `empower sb to do` = "授权某人做某事"，`to initiate` 是**宾语补足语**，不是目的状语；② `against` 与 `initiate enforcement action` 搭配，表"对……采取行动"；③ `meaning ...` 是**现在分词作解释性状语**，逻辑主语是前句整句，作用是把"授权"限定为"只能事后执法"；④ `only ... if ...` 是**必要条件**"只有……才"，不是"如果……就"，命题若改成"FTC 可随时执法"即为曲解；⑤ `misleading`（误导性的）与 `deceptive`（欺骗性的）是**两个不同层次的词**：前者看结果，后者看手段，不能混为一谈；⑥ 第 37 题 [B] `Its enforcement needs strengthening` 是**干扰项**——本段讲的是 FTC 执法**权限有限、只能事后**，而第 37 题问的是"现有健康隐私法"的覆盖范围（上段），两者不能张冠李戴。

While the FTC is doing what it can to ensure apps are keeping their promises to consumers around the handling of their sensitive health information, the rate at which these health apps are hitting the market demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is.

> ```
> 主句: 〔the rate (at which these health apps are hitting the market)〕+ demonstrates + 〔just how immense of a challenge this is〕
>   ├── 让步状语从句: While + 〔the FTC〕+ is doing + 〔what it can〕+ (to ensure (that) apps are keeping their promises ...)
>   │     ├── 宾语从句: what it can → doing 的宾语
>   │     └── ensure 后省略 that → apps are keeping their promises
>   └── 感叹宾语从句: how + immense + of a challenge + this is → "挑战有多么巨大"
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：尽管 FTC 正尽其所能确保应用在敏感健康信息的处理上对消费者信守承诺，但这些健康应用进入市场的速度表明，这一挑战有多么巨大。
>
> **考点提示**：① `While` 引导**让步**状语从句，主句 `demonstrates ... immense challenge` 是对从句"FTC 在努力"的转折；② `what it can` 是**宾语从句**，作 `is doing` 的宾语（= "它所能做的"），`what` 在从句中作省略的 `do` 的宾语（= what it can do）；③ `ensure apps are keeping ...` 中 `ensure` 后**省略了 `that`**，`apps are keeping` 是宾语从句；④ `the rate at which ...` 是**"介词 + which"定语从句**，`at` 来自 `at the rate` 的固定搭配；⑤ `how immense of a challenge this is` 是**感叹意义的宾语从句**，`how + 形容词 + of a + 名词` 是固定感叹结构；⑥ `this` 指代前文"监管只能事后 + 应用大量涌入"这一整体局面，第 39 题 [B] `The rapid increase in new health apps` 正是对 `the rate at which ... are hitting the market` 的同义改写，[A] `The complexity of health information` 与原文无关。

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：As to the prospects for federal legislation, commentators suggest that **comprehensive federal privacy legislation** seems unlikely in the short term.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 状语 A: As to the prospects for federal legislation
> - 主语 S: commentators
> - 谓语 V: suggest
> - 宾语从句 O: that comprehensive federal privacy legislation seems unlikely in the short term
> - 宾语从句主干: comprehensive federal privacy legislation + seems + unlikely
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语作状语（话题） | As to | 全句 |
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | for federal legislation | the prospects |
> | 宾语从句 | that | suggest |
> | 形容词作表语 | unlikely | seems |
> | 时间状语 | in the short term | seems unlikely |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: (As to the prospects for federal legislation) + 〔commentators〕+ suggest + 〔that 宾语从句〕
>   └── 宾从: 〔comprehensive federal privacy legislation〕+ seems + 〔unlikely〕+ (in the short term)
>         ※ suggest = "认为"，从句用陈述语气，非虚拟
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：至于联邦立法的前景，评论人士认为，全面的联邦隐私立法短期内似乎不太可能。
>
> **考点提示**：① `As to` = "至于、关于"，是**话题转换标记**，标志文章从"问题"转向"出路"；② `suggest` 在此是"**认为、表示**"，从句用**陈述语气** `seems`，不是虚拟语气（虚拟用法为 `suggest that sb (should) do`）；③ `seems unlikely` 是**委婉判断**——"似乎不太可能"，不是 `impossible`，且被 `in the short term` 限定为"短期内"；④ 第 40 题 [B] `has been a focus of federal policy-making` 与本句**正相反**：联邦层面恰恰"不太可能"，真正的行动在州层面，属**正反混淆**式干扰。
