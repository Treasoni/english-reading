# 2024 Passage 1 固定搭配与词组

> [!note] 来源说明
> 本篇固定搭配与词组由 `formatted-article.md` **从本文提取**，仅收录本篇实际出现的表达。
> 纯语法内容见 [[intermediate/2024-passage1-ai-inequality/grammar-notes.md|2024 Passage 1 语法整理]]。

---

## 一、主题词与核心概念

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **inequality** | 不平等、不均等 | tech's **inequality** problem could be a roadblock |
| **evenly shared** | 均衡共享的 | the gains will have to be more **evenly shared** |
| **prosperity** | 繁荣、富足 | increasing **prosperity** for more people |
| **prosperous** | 繁荣的（prosperity 的形容词） | elites in a handful of **prosperous** cities |
| **gig worker** | 零工劳动者、打零工者 | tech millionaires or billionaires and **gig workers** |
| **automation** | 自动化 | middle-income jobs undercut by **automation** |
| **disruption** | 颠覆、扰乱 | We're talking about **disruption** |
| **transformative** | 变革性的、带来根本改变的 | These are **transformative** technologies |
| **buy-in** | （对变革的）认同与支持 | you need social **buy-in** |
| **resentment** | 不满、怨恨 | **resentment** is simmering among many |
| **elite** | 精英（阶层） | the benefits are perceived to go to **elites** |
| **dominance** | 主导地位、支配地位 | The **dominance** of a few cities |
| **geographical disparities** | 地域差距 | **geographical disparities** in wealth will continue to soar |
| **unrest** | 动荡、动乱 | political and social **unrest** |
| **stranglehold** | 钳制、扼制 | loosening the **stranglehold** that Big Tech has |
| **agenda** | 议程、议题设定 | defining the AI **agenda** |
| **Big Tech** | 大型科技公司（巨头） | **Big Tech** has on defining the AI agenda |
| **the tech giants** | 科技巨头（= Big Tech） | independent of **the tech giants** |
| **invention and commercialization** | 发明与商业化 | in the **invention and commercialization** of AI |
| **assets and capabilities** | 资产与能力（合指"实力"） | two-thirds of the AI **assets and capabilities** |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 全文核心概念链：**inequality / evenly shared**（问题）→ **disruption / transformative**（技术特性）→ **buy-in**（缺失的条件）→ **concentrated / dominance / disparities**（现状证据）→ **loosen the stranglehold / broaden imaginations**（对策）。第 21 题 C 项 `benefit people equally` 对应第一环，第 23 题 D 项 `unwelcome to the public` 是 `buy-in` 的**反面改写**，第 24 题 A 项对应第三环，第 25 题 D 项对应最后一环。

## 二、动词短语

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **argue that** | 主张、论证（提出观点） | Coyle **argues that** the digital economy requires new ways of thinking |
| **boost productivity** | 提升生产率 | to **boost productivity** in various sectors |
| **embrace** | 欣然接受、拥抱（变化） | people can't be expected to **embrace** the changes |
| **undercut** | 削弱、从根基上挤垮 | jobs **undercut** by automation |
| **deploy** | 部署、应用 | a roadblock to **deploying** AI |
| **simmer** | （情绪）暗自积聚、将沸未沸 | resentment is **simmering** among many |
| **go to** | 流向、归于 | the benefits are perceived to **go to** elites |
| **estimate that** | 估计（后接宾语从句） | Brookings's Mark Muro and Sifan Liu **estimate that** ... |
| **account for** | 占（比例、份额） | just 15 cities **account for** two-thirds of the AI assets |
| **soar** | 猛增、剧增 | geographical disparities will continue to **soar** |
| **foster** | 滋生、助长（多接负面事物） | **foster** political and social unrest |
| **hold back** | 阻碍、抑制 | **hold back** the sorts of AI technologies |
| **loosen** | 放松、松开 | **loosening** the stranglehold |
| **conceive of** | 构想出、设想出 | to **conceive of** AI technologies that ... |
| **expand opportunities** | 拓展机会 | **expand** opportunities in the sectors |
| **care about** | 关心、在意 | the sectors that different parts of the country **care most about** |
| **lie in** | 在于（对策所在） | Part of the solution could **lie in** ... |
| **take** | 需要、耗费（≈ require） | That will likely **take** increased federal funding |
| **broaden** | 拓宽、扩大 | **broaden** our digital imaginations |

> [!warning] 易错点
> - `account for` 有**两义**：① "占（比例）"（本句用法）；② "解释、说明"（`account for the delay`）。数据题里几乎都是前者。
> - `undercut` 原义"从底部削掉"，引申为"以低价挤压、削弱"；不要理解为"砍掉"。
> - `foster` 中性偏负，常接 `unrest / resentment / inequality`，也可接 `innovation / growth`，但要按**上下文感情色彩**判断。
> - `hold back` 与 `hold on`（坚持、稍等）、`hold up`（延误、举起）意思完全不同。
> - `take` 表"需要"时主语常为事物（`It takes time.` / `That will take funding.`），是熟词僻义高频点。
> - `conceive of` 中 `of` 不可省略；`conceive` 单独用多为"怀孕、构思（具体方案）"。

## 三、名词短语与介词搭配

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **a roadblock to doing sth** | 做某事的障碍 | could be **a roadblock to deploying** AI |
| **a handful of** | 少数几个、一把 | **a handful of** prosperous cities |
| **a short list of** | 短短一份……名单 | **a short list of** eight American cities |
| **in the recent past** | 在近来、在过去一段时期 | more evenly shared than **in the recent past** |
| **new ways of thinking about** | 关于……的新思路 | requires **new ways of thinking about** progress |
| **living standards** | 生活水平 | **Improving living standards** |
| **middle-income jobs** | 中等收入岗位 | **middle-income jobs** undercut by automation |
| **business models** | 商业模式 | change **business models** that succeed |
| **a stranglehold on doing sth** | 对做某事的钳制 | the stranglehold that Big Tech has **on defining** the AI agenda |
| **federal funding** | 联邦资金 | increased **federal funding** for research |
| **independent of** | 独立于、不依赖于 | research **independent of** the tech giants |
| **digital imaginations** | 对数字世界的想象 | broaden our **digital imaginations** |
| **regional economies** | 地方（区域）经济 | needed for **regional economies** to grow |
| **in the United States** | 在美国 | the AI assets and capabilities **in the United States** |
| **political and social unrest** | 政治与社会动荡 | political and social **unrest** |
| **by 2019** | 截至 2019 年 | roughly 38% of all tech jobs **by 2019** |

> [!warning] 易错点
> - `a roadblock to` / `an obstacle to` / `a key to` / `the solution to` 中的 `to` 都是**介词**，后接动名词。
> - `independent of` 的介词是 **of**，不是 `from`（`independent from` 较少且语体不同）。
> - `by + 时间` 表"截至……为止"，与 `in + 时间`（在……期间）、`until`（直到……才）区分。
> - `a handful of` 后接可数名词复数，`handful` 前不用 `the`（除特指）。
> - `in the recent past` 固定用 `the`，且常与比较级、现在完成时搭配。

## 四、固定句式与逻辑连接

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **Whatever we mean by ... , ...** | 无论我们把……理解为什么 | **Whatever we mean by** the economy growing, the gains will have to be shared |
| **will have to** | 将不得不、必须（规范性要求） | the gains **will have to** be more evenly shared |
| **be expected to do** | 被指望做某事 | people can't **be expected to** embrace the changes |
| **be perceived to do** | 被认为做某事 | the benefits **are perceived to** go to elites |
| **Not only will ..., but ...** | 不仅会……，而且会……（部分倒装） | **Not only will** this foster unrest, **but** it could hold back ... |
| **not simply ... but ...** | 不只是……而是……（重心在后） | **don't simply** replace jobs **but** expand opportunities |
| **Part of the solution could lie in ...** | 解决办法之一可能在于…… | **Part of the solution could lie in** loosening the stranglehold |
| **A more immediate response is to ...** | 更直接的应对是…… | **A more immediate response is to** broaden our digital imaginations |
| **says Coyle / she adds** | 插入语，标明出处 | ..., **says Coyle** / **she adds** |
| **as Coyle suggests** | 正如科伊尔所言（方式） | it could, **as Coyle suggests**, hold back ... |
| **according to** | 根据（引述来源） | **According to** the Brookings Institution |
| **such as / like / including** | 例如 | **including** health care and construction / **like** health care, education, and manufacturing |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 对策类文章的**两个对策标记句**——`Part of the solution could lie in ...` 与 `A more immediate response is to ...`——就是"作者建议"的全部落点，第 25 题答案必在二者之间。A 项 `raising funds to start new AI projects` 只对上 `increased federal funding`，却丢掉了 `independent of the tech giants` 这层限定；D 项 `redefining the role of AI technologies` 才是第 8 段"不只取代岗位、而要拓展机会"的**完整概括**。
