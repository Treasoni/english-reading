# Scientific Publishing 科学出版 中英对照

It used to be so straightforward. A team of researchers working together in the laboratory would submit the results of their research to a journal. A journal editor would then remove the authors' names and affiliations from the paper and send it to their peers for **review**. Depending on the comments received, the editor would accept the paper for publication or decline it. **Copyright** rested with the journal publisher, and researchers seeking knowledge of the results would have to subscribe to the journal.

过去一切都非常简单明了。一组在实验室共同工作的研究人员会将研究成果提交给某家期刊。期刊编辑随后会从论文中删去作者的姓名和所属机构信息，并将论文送交同行**评审**。根据收到的意见，编辑会接受论文发表或拒绝发表。**版权**归期刊出版商所有，而想要了解研究成果的研究人员则必须订阅该期刊。

==No longer.== The Internet—and pressure from funding agencies, who are questioning why commercial publishers are making money from **government-funded research** by restricting access to it—is making access to scientific results a reality. The Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) has just issued a report describing the far-reaching consequences of this. The report, by John Houghton of Victoria University in Australia and Graham Vickery of the OECD, makes heavy reading for publishers who have, so far, made handsome profits. But it goes further than that. It signals a change in what has, until now, been a key element of **scientific endeavor**.

==不再如此。== 互联网以及资助机构施加的压力——这些机构质疑商业出版商为何通过限制人们获取**政府资助的研究成果**来赚钱——正使获取科学研究成果成为现实。经济合作与发展组织（OECD）刚刚发布了一份报告，描述了这一变化所产生的深远影响。这份报告由澳大利亚维多利亚大学的约翰·霍顿和OECD的格雷厄姆·维克里撰写，对于那些迄今为止获利丰厚的出版商来说，读起来相当沉重。但报告的意义不止于此：它标志着科学**研究活动**中一个迄今为止一直十分关键的要素正在发生变化。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> “No longer.” 是对上文传统出版流程的否定，意为“情况不再如此”，而不是简单理解为“不再了”。“makes heavy reading for”表示“对……来说是沉重的阅读”，在此引申为“对……不是好消息”。

The value of knowledge and the return on the public investment in research depends, in part, upon **wide distribution and ready access**. It is big business. In America, the core scientific publishing market is estimated at between $7 billion and $11 billion. The International Association of Scientific, Technical and Medical Publishers says that there are more than 2,000 publishers worldwide specializing in these subjects. They publish more than 1.2 million articles each year in some 16,000 journals.

知识的价值以及公众在科研上的投资回报，部分取决于知识的**广泛传播和便捷获取**。这是一个庞大的产业。在美国，核心科学出版市场的规模估计在70亿美元至110亿美元之间。国际科学、技术与医学出版商协会称，全球有超过2000家出版商专门从事这些领域的出版工作。他们每年在约16000种期刊上发表超过120万篇文章。

This is now changing. According to the OECD report, some $75\%$ of scholarly journals are now online. Entirely new business models are emerging; three main ones were identified by the report's authors. There is the so-called big deal, where institutional subscribers pay for access to a collection of online journal titles through site-licensing agreements. There is **open-access publishing**, typically supported by asking the author (or his employer) to pay for the paper to be published. Finally, there are **open-access archives**, where organisations such as universities or international laboratories support institutional repositories. Other models exist that are hybrids of these three, such as delayed open-access, where journals allow only subscribers to read a paper for the first six months, before making it freely available to everyone who wishes to see it. All this could change the traditional form of the **peer-review process**, at least for the publication of papers.

这种情况现在正在发生变化。根据OECD的报告，目前约有75%的学术期刊已经上线。全新的商业模式正在出现；报告作者确定了其中三种主要模式。一种是所谓的“大宗交易”模式，即机构订阅者通过网站许可协议付费，以获取一系列在线期刊的阅读权限。另一种是**开放获取出版**，通常要求作者（或其雇主）支付论文的出版费用。最后一种是**开放获取档案**，由大学或国际实验室等机构支持机构知识库。此外，还存在这三种模式的混合形式，例如延迟开放获取：期刊只允许订阅者在论文发表后的前六个月阅读，之后再向所有希望阅读的人免费开放。所有这些模式都可能改变传统的**同行评审流程**，至少会改变论文发表环节的同行评审形式。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> “big deal” 在学术出版语境中不是“重大交易”，而是指机构批量购买一组在线期刊的访问权限。“delayed open-access” 指“延迟开放获取”，即先限制访问，过一段时间后再免费开放。

## Questions 题目

**26.** In the first paragraph, the author discusses ____.

[A] the background information of journal editing  
[A] 期刊编辑的背景信息

[B] the publication routine of laboratory reports  
[B] 实验室报告的出版流程

[C] the relations of authors with journal publishers  
[C] 作者与期刊出版商之间的关系

[D] the traditional process of journal publication  
[D] 期刊出版的传统流程

**27.** Which of the following is true of the OECD report?

关于OECD报告，下列哪项说法正确？

[A] It criticizes government-funded research.  
[A] 它批评政府资助的研究。

[B] It introduces an effective means of publication.  
[B] 它介绍了一种有效的出版方式。

[C] It upsets profit-making journal publishers.  
[C] 它令营利性期刊出版商感到不安。

[D] It benefits scientific research considerably.  
[D] 它对科学研究大有裨益。

**28.** According to the text, online publication is significant in that ____.

根据文章，在线出版的重要意义在于____。

[A] it provides an easier access to scientific results  
[A] 它为获取科学研究成果提供了更便捷的途径。

[B] it brings huge profits to scientific researchers  
[B] 它为科研人员带来巨额利润。

[C] it emphasizes the crucial role of scientific knowledge  
[C] 它强调了科学知识的关键作用。

[D] it facilitates public investment in scientific research  
[D] 它促进了公众对科学研究的投资。

**29.** With the open-access publishing model, the author of a paper is required to ____.

在开放获取出版模式下，论文作者需要____。

[A] cover the cost of its publication  
[A] 承担论文的出版费用。

[B] subscribe to the journal publishing it  
[B] 订阅发表该论文的期刊。

[C] allow other online journals to use it freely  
[C] 允许其他在线期刊免费使用该论文。

[D] complete the peer-review before submission  
[D] 在投稿前完成同行评审。

**30.** Which of the following best summarizes the text?

下列哪项最能概括文章内容？

[A] The Internet is posing a threat to publishers.  
[A] 互联网正在对出版商构成威胁。

[B] 一种新的出版模式正在出现。

[C] 作者欢迎新的出版渠道。

[D] 网络服务使出版变得更加容易。
