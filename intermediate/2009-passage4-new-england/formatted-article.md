# New England Intellectual Life

The most thoroughly studied **intellectuals** in the history of the New World are the ministers and political leaders of seventeenth-century New England. According to the standard history of American philosophy, nowhere else in colonial America was “so much importance attached to **intellectual pursuits**.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：According to the standard history of American philosophy, nowhere else in colonial America was “so much importance attached to **intellectual pursuits**.”
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: so much importance
> - 谓语 V: was attached
> - 宾语 O: 无（被动结构中没有宾语）
> - 补语 C: to **intellectual pursuits**（介词补足语）
> - 状语 A: According to the standard history of American philosophy；nowhere else in colonial America
> - 简化句：So much importance was attached to intellectual pursuits nowhere else in colonial America.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语（来源状语） | According to | 整个主句，说明判断依据 |
> | 介词短语（地点状语） | in | colonial America，说明范围 |
> | 副词短语（地点状语） | nowhere else | 整个被动结构，表示“其他任何地方都没有” |
> | 介词短语（补足语） | to | intellectual pursuits，说明重视的对象 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句（地点状语前置引起倒装）: [nowhere else in colonial America] + [was attached] + [so much importance]
>   ├── 来源状语: (According to the standard history of American philosophy)
>   ├── 地点状语前置: (nowhere else in colonial America)
>   └── 被动结构: was attached to intellectual pursuits
>         └── 介词补足语: (to intellectual pursuits)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：根据美国哲学的通行史，在殖民地美洲，没有任何其他地方像这里一样如此重视知识追求。
>
> **考点提示**：否定意义的地点状语 **nowhere else** 置于句首时，主句采用部分倒装，将系动词 **was** 提到主语 **so much importance** 之前；同时要识别 **attach importance to** 的被动形式。

According to many books and articles, New England’s leaders established the basic themes and preoccupations of an unfolding, dominant **Puritan tradition** in American intellectual life.

To take this approach to the New Englanders normally means to start with the Puritans' **theological innovations** and their distinctive ideas about the church—important subjects that we may not neglect.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：To take this approach to the New Englanders normally means to start with the Puritans' **theological innovations** and their distinctive ideas about the church—important subjects that we may not neglect.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: To take this approach to the New Englanders
> - 谓语 V: means
> - 宾语 O: 无（mean 后接不定式作补足成分）
> - 补语 C: to start with the Puritans' theological innovations and their distinctive ideas about the church
> - 状语 A: normally
> - 简化句：To take this approach normally means to start with important subjects.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（不定式） | To take | 作主语，表示“采取这种方式” |
> | 介词短语 | to | approach，说明“针对新英格兰人” |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to start | 作 mean 的补足成分，说明意味着什么 |
> | 介词短语 | with | start，说明从哪些内容开始 |
> | 介词短语 | about | ideas，说明观点涉及的对象 |
> | 同位语 | 破折号 | important subjects 同位说明前面的两类内容 |
> | 定语从句 | that | 修饰 important subjects；that 在从句中作 neglect 的宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [To take this approach to the New Englanders] + [normally means] + [to start with ...]
>   ├── 主语不定式: To take this approach to the New Englanders
>   ├── 状语: normally
>   └── 补足成分不定式: to start with
>         ├── with 的并列宾语: the Puritans' theological innovations
>         └── with 的并列宾语: their distinctive ideas about the church
>               └── 同位语: important subjects
>                     └── 定语从句: that we may not neglect
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：通常，采用这种方式来审视新英格兰人，就意味着要从清教徒的神学创新及其对教会的独特见解入手——这些都是我们不应忽视的重要议题。
>
> **考点提示**：句首 **To take...** 是不定式短语作主语；**mean to do** 表示“意味着做某事”。破折号后的 **important subjects** 是前文并列名词短语的同位语，末尾 **that** 引导定语从句且在从句中作宾语。

But in keeping with our examination of southern intellectual life, we may consider the original Puritans as carriers of European culture, adjusting to New World circumstances. The New England colonies were the scenes of important episodes in the pursuit of widely understood ideals of **civility** and **virtuosity**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The New England colonies were the scenes of important episodes in the pursuit of widely understood ideals of **civility** and **virtuosity**.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: The New England colonies
> - 谓语 V: were
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: the scenes
> - 状语 A: 无（后面的介词短语均修饰表语名词 scenes 或其内部名词）
> - 简化句：The New England colonies were the scenes.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | of | scenes，说明这些场所中发生的内容 |
> | 介词短语 | in | episodes，说明这些事件发生于何种活动中 |
> | 非谓语（过去分词） | widely understood | ideals，表示“被广泛认可的” |
> | 介词短语 | of | pursuit，说明所追求的具体理想 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [The New England colonies] + [were] + [the scenes]
>   └── 表语名词的后置修饰: of important episodes
>         └── 介词短语: in the pursuit
>               └── 介词短语: of widely understood ideals
>                     ├── 过去分词短语: widely understood → 修饰 ideals
>                     └── 介词短语: of civility and virtuosity → 修饰 ideals
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：新英格兰殖民地曾是许多重要事件的舞台；这些事件发生在追求普遍认同的文明与精湛技艺理想的过程中。
>
> **考点提示**：**be the scene(s) of...** 中 **of** 短语修饰表语名词 **scenes**，不能误判为谓语 **were** 的宾语；多个介词短语层层嵌套时，要沿着中心名词逐级确定修饰对象。**widely understood** 是过去分词短语作前置定语，可还原为 **ideals that are widely understood**。

The early settlers of Massachusetts Bay included men of impressive education and influence in England. Besides the ninety or so learned ministers who came to Massachusetts churches in the decade after 1629, there were political leaders like John Winthrop, an educated gentleman, lawyer, and official of the Crown before he journeyed to Boston.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Besides the ninety or so learned ministers who came to Massachusetts churches in the decade after 1629, there were political leaders like John Winthrop, an educated gentleman, lawyer, and official of the Crown before he journeyed to Boston.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: political leaders like John Winthrop
> - 谓语 V: were（there be 结构中的谓语）
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: 无（后置同位语不属于主句补语）
> - 状语 A: Besides the ninety or so learned ministers...；before he journeyed to Boston
> - 简化句：There were political leaders like John Winthrop.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语（范围/补充状语） | Besides | there were political leaders，表示“除……之外” |
> | 定语从句 | who | 修饰 learned ministers |
> | 介词短语（时间状语） | in | the decade，说明 came 的时间 |
> | 介词短语 | after | 1629，限定 decade |
> | 介词短语 | like | political leaders，举例说明领袖类型 |
> | 同位语 | 逗号 | an educated gentleman, lawyer, and official of the Crown 同位说明 John Winthrop |
> | 状语从句 | before | he journeyed to Boston，说明同位描述成立的时间背景 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句（there be 存在句）: There + [were] + [political leaders like John Winthrop]
>   ├── 补充状语: Besides the ninety or so learned ministers ...
>   │     └── 定语从句: who came to Massachusetts churches
>   │           └── 时间状语: in the decade after 1629
>   ├── 举例短语: like John Winthrop
>   └── 同位语: an educated gentleman, lawyer, and official of the Crown
>         └── 时间状语从句: before he journeyed to Boston
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：除了1629年后十年间来到马萨诸塞教会的约90名博学牧师外，还有约翰·温思罗普这样的政治领袖；在前往波士顿之前，他是一位受过教育的绅士、律师和王室官员。
>
> **考点提示**：**there be** 结构中真正的主语是其后的名词短语 **political leaders**；**who came...** 是修饰 ministers 的定语从句；John Winthrop 后的名词并列成分是同位语，不是新的谓语。

These men wrote and published extensively, reaching both New World and Old World audiences, and giving New England an atmosphere of **intellectual earnestness**.

We should not forget, however, that most New Englanders were less well educated. While few craftsmen or farmers, let alone dependents and servants, left literary compositions to be analyzed, it is obvious that their views were less fully intellectualized.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：While few craftsmen or farmers, let alone dependents and servants, left literary compositions to be analyzed, it is obvious that their views were less fully intellectualized.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: it（形式主语）
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: obvious
> - 状语 A: While few craftsmen or farmers, let alone dependents and servants, left literary compositions to be analyzed
> - 真正主语（后置主语）: that their views were less fully intellectualized
> - 简化句：It is obvious that their views were less fully intellectualized.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句（让步/对比） | While | 主句 it is obvious，说明背景 |
> | 插入成分 | let alone | dependents and servants，表示递进排除“更不用说” |
> | 非谓语（被动不定式） | to be analyzed | literary compositions，表示“可供分析” |
> | 名词性从句（主语从句后置） | that | is obvious，具体说明“显而易见”的内容 |
> | 副词 | less fully | intellectualized，修饰过去分词，表示程度 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [It] + [is] + [obvious]
>   └── 真正主语: that their views were less fully intellectualized
>         ├── 主语: their views
>         └── 谓语: were less fully intellectualized
> 状语从句: While [few craftsmen or farmers ... left literary compositions]
>   ├── 插入递进: let alone dependents and servants
>   └── 宾语补足成分: to be analyzed → literary compositions
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：虽然很少有手工业者或农民，更不用说被抚养者和仆人，留下可供分析的文学作品，但显而易见，他们的观点没有得到充分的理性化。
>
> **考点提示**：**It is obvious that...** 使用形式主语 **it**，真正的主语是后置的 **that** 从句；**while** 在此表示让步或对比；**let alone** 表示递进，“更不用说”。

Their thinking often had a traditional **superstitious** quality. A tailor named John Dane, who emigrated in the late 1630s, left an account of his reasons for leaving England that is filled with signs. Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope—all came together in a decisive moment when he opened the Bible, told his father that the first line he saw would settle his fate, and read the magical words: “Come out from among them, touch no unclean thing, and I will be your God and you shall be my people.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope—all came together in a decisive moment when he opened the Bible, told his father that the first line he saw would settle his fate, and read the magical words: “Come out from among them, touch no unclean thing, and I will be your God and you shall be my people.”
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope
> - 谓语 V: came together
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: in a decisive moment；when he opened..., told..., and read...
> - 简化句：Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope all came together in a decisive moment.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 同位/强调成分 | all | 回指并强调三个并列主语 |
> | 介词短语（时间状语） | in | a decisive moment，说明 came together 的时间 |
> | 定语从句（时间） | when | 修饰 a decisive moment |
> | 并列谓语 | opened, told, and read | when 从句的三个并列动作 |
> | 名词性从句（宾语从句） | that | told 的宾语，说明告诉父亲的内容 |
> | 定语从句 | he saw | 修饰 the first line，关系代词省略且作 saw 的宾语 |
> | 情态结构 | would settle | the first line he saw 的谓语，表示过去视角下的将来 |
> | 直接引语 | 冒号 | 说明 the magical words 的具体内容 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope] + [all came together] + [in a decisive moment]
>   └── 时间定语从句: when he opened the Bible, told his father ..., and read the magical words
>         ├── 并列谓语1: opened the Bible
>         ├── 并列谓语2: told his father
>         │     └── 宾语从句: that the first line he saw would settle his fate
>         │           └── 定语从句: he saw → 修饰 the first line
>         └── 并列谓语3: read the magical words
>               └── 直接引语: “Come out ... my people.”
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：性方面的困惑、经济上的挫折和宗教上的希望——这一切都汇聚在一个决定性的时刻：他打开《圣经》，告诉父亲，他看到的第一行字将决定自己的命运，然后读到以下带有魔力的话语：“你们务要从他们中间出来，与不洁净的不要接触；我就要作你们的上帝，你们也要作我的子民。”
>
> **考点提示**：主句使用三个并列名词作主语，破折号后的 **all** 起回指强调作用；**when** 引导修饰 moment 的时间定语从句，内部又包含三个并列谓语；**that** 引导 told 的宾语从句，且其中 **he saw** 是修饰 **the first line** 的定语从句。

One wonders what Dane thought of the careful sermons explaining the Bible that he heard in Puritan churches.

Meanwhile, many settlers had slighter religious commitments than Dane's, as one clergyman learned in confronting folk along the coast who mocked that they had not come to the New World for religion.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Meanwhile, many settlers had slighter religious commitments than Dane's, as one clergyman learned in confronting folk along the coast who mocked that they had not come to the New World for religion.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: many settlers
> - 谓语 V: had
> - 宾语 O: slighter religious commitments
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: Meanwhile；as one clergyman learned in confronting folk...
> - 比较成分：than Dane's（省略了 religious commitments，补全为 than Dane's religious commitments）
> - 简化句：Many settlers had slighter religious commitments than Dane's.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 副词（时间/语篇状语） | Meanwhile | 整个主句，表示“与此同时” |
> | 比较结构 | than | slighter，比较定居者与 Dane 的宗教投入程度 |
> | 状语从句（补充说明） | as | 整个主句，说明这一判断的来源或依据 |
> | 介词短语（动名词结构） | in | learned，说明牧师了解到这一点的情境 |
> | 介词短语 | along | folk，说明民众所在的位置 |
> | 定语从句 | who | 修饰 folk；who 在从句中作主语 |
> | 名词性从句（宾语从句） | that | mocked 的宾语，说明民众嘲笑的内容 |
> | 介词短语 | to | come，说明前往的目的地 |
> | 介词短语 | for | come，说明来到新大陆的目的 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [Meanwhile] + [many settlers] + [had] + [slighter religious commitments] + [than Dane's]
>   └── 补充说明状语从句: as one clergyman learned in confronting folk ...
>         ├── 主干: one clergyman learned
>         ├── 情境状语: in confronting folk along the coast
>         │     ├── 地点修饰: along the coast → folk
>         │     └── 定语从句: who mocked ... → folk
>         │           └── 宾语从句: that they had not come to the New World for religion
>         │                 ├── 主语: they
>         │                 ├── 谓语: had not come
>         │                 ├── 目的地: to the New World
>         │                 └── 目的状语: for religion
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：与此同时，许多定居者的宗教信仰不如戴恩虔诚，正如一位牧师在与沿海民众打交道时所了解到的那样；那些人嘲笑说，他们来到新大陆并不是为了宗教。
>
> **考点提示**：**as** 引导的从句在此不是原因状语，而是补充说明牧师如何了解到前述事实，可译为“正如……所了解到的那样”。注意 **who mocked...** 修饰 **folk**，而 **that they had not come...** 是 **mocked** 的宾语从句；**than Dane's** 中省略了与前文相同的名词 **religious commitments**。

"Our main end was to catch fish."
