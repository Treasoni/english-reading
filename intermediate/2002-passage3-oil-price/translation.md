⑴Could the bad old days of economic decline be about to return? ⑵Since OPEC agreed to supply-cuts in March, the price of crude oil has jumped to almost $26 a barrel, up from less than $10 last December. ⑶This near tripling of oil prices calls up scary memories of the 1973 oil shock, when prices quadrupled, and 1979-80, when they also almost **tripled**. ⑷Both previous shocks resulted in double-digit inflation and global economic decline. ⑸So where are the headlines warning of gloom and doom this time?

⑴经济衰退的糟糕旧日是否即将卷土重来？⑵自今年三月欧佩克（OPEC）同意减产以来，原油价格已从去年12月的不到10美元飙升至接近每桶26美元。⑶油价近三倍的涨幅唤起了人们对1973年石油危机的可怕记忆——当时油价翻了两番；以及1979-1980年的危机——当时油价也几乎涨了两倍。⑷前两次石油危机都导致了两位数的通货膨胀和全球经济衰退。⑸那么这一次，为何没有报刊头条发出悲观与厄运的警告呢？

> [!note] gloom and doom：固定搭配，指"悲观与厄运"，常用于描述对未来前景的忧虑。

---

⑴The oil price was given another push up this week when Iraq suspended oil exports. ⑵Strengthening economic growth, at the same time as winter grips the northern hemisphere, could push the price higher still in the short term.

⑴本周伊拉克暂停石油出口，再次推动油价上涨。⑵经济增长的加强，加之北半球进入冬季，短期内可能进一步推高油价。

---

⑴Yet there are good reasons to expect the economic consequences now to be less severe than in the 1970s. ⑵In most countries the cost of crude oil now accounts for a smaller share of the price of petrol than it did in the 1970s. ⑶In Europe, taxes account for up to four-fifths of the retail price, so even quite big changes in the price of crude have a more muted effect on pump prices than in the past.

⑴然而我们有充分的理由预期，当前的经济影响不会像1970年代那样严重。⑵在大多数国家，原油成本在汽油价格中所占的份额比1970年代更小。⑶在欧洲，税收在零售价中占比高达五分之四，因此即使原油价格出现较大波动，对加油站零售价的影响也比过去更为温和。

> [!note] pump prices：英美国家口语，指"加油站的零售油价"，源于加油站的油泵（petrol pump）。

---

⑴Rich economies are also less dependent on oil than they were, and so less sensitive to swings in the oil price. ⑵Energy conservation, a shift to other fuels and a decline in the importance of heavy, energy-intensive industries have reduced oil consumption. ⑶Software, consultancy and mobile telephones use far less oil than steel or car production. ⑷For each dollar of GDP (in constant prices) rich economies now use nearly 50% less oil than in 1973. ⑸The OECD estimates in its latest Economic Outlook that, if oil prices averaged $22 a barrel for a full year, compared with $13 in 1998, this would increase the oil import bill in rich economies by only 0.25-0.5% of GDP. ⑹That is less than one-quarter of the income loss in 1974 or 1980. ⑺On the other hand, oil-importing emerging economies—to which heavy industry has shifted—have become more energy-intensive, and so could be more seriously squeezed.

⑴富裕经济体对石油的依赖度也比过去更低，因此对油价波动的敏感度也更低。⑵节能、转向其他燃料以及重工业等高能耗产业重要性的下降，都降低了石油消耗。⑶软件、咨询和移动电话行业对石油的消耗远低于钢铁或汽车生产。⑷按不变价格计算，富裕经济体每创造一美元GDP所消耗的石油比1973年减少近50%。⑸经合组织（OECD）在其最新一期《经济展望》中估计，如果油价全年平均为每桶22美元（1998年为13美元），那么富裕经济体的石油进口账单将仅增加GDP的0.25%-0.5%。⑹这一数字不到1974年或1980年收入损失的四分之一。⑺另一方面，承接了重工业转移的石油进口新兴经济体已变得更高能耗，因此可能受到更为严重的挤压。

> [!note] squeezed：原意"被挤压"，此处引申为"受到严重挤压/压力"，形容经济体在油价上涨背景下面临的困境。

---

⑴One more reason not to lose sleep over the rise in oil prices is that, unlike the rises in the 1970s, it has not occurred against the background of general commodity-price inflation and global excess demand. ⑵A sizable portion of the world is only just emerging from economic decline. ⑶The Economist's commodity price index is broadly unchanging from a year ago. ⑷In 1973 commodity prices jumped by 70%, and in 1979 by almost 30%.

⑴不必为油价上涨而忧心忡忡的另一个原因是，与1970年代不同，本轮上涨并非发生在商品价格普遍上涨和全球需求过旺的背景下。⑵世界上相当一部分地区才刚刚走出经济衰退。⑶《经济学人》商品价格指数与一年前基本持平。⑷而1973年商品价格跃升了70%，1979年也上涨了近30%。

> [!note] lose sleep over：字面"因……睡不着觉"，引申为"为……担忧/焦虑"，是英语中常见的惯用表达。
