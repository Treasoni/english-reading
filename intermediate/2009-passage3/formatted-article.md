# 2009 阅读理解 Passage 3

## 文章原文

The relationship between formal education and economic growth in poor countries is widely misunderstood by economists and politicians alike. Progress in both areas is undoubtedly necessary for the social, political, and intellectual development of these and all other societies; however, the conventional view that education should be one of the very highest priorities for promoting rapid economic development in poor countries is wrong.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Progress in both areas is undoubtedly necessary for the social, political, and intellectual development of these and all other societies; however, the conventional view that education should be one of the very highest priorities for promoting rapid economic development in poor countries is wrong.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Progress in both areas
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: necessary for the social, political, and intellectual development of these and all other societies
> - 转折分句主语 S: the conventional view
> - 转折分句谓语 V: is
> - 转折分句补语 C: wrong
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语（介短） | in | 修饰主句主语 Progress，说明“两个领域” |
> | 介词短语（介短） | for | 修饰 necessary，说明进步的作用对象 |
> | 同位语从句（名从） | that | 解释 view 的具体内容 |
> | 非谓语短语 | for promoting | 修饰 priorities，表示“用于促进……” |
> | 介词短语（介短） | in poor countries | 修饰 economic development |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: Progress in both areas + is + necessary ...
>   ├── 介短: (in both areas) → 修饰 Progress
>   ├── 介短: (for the ... development) → 修饰 necessary
>   └── 转折分句: however, the conventional view + is + wrong
>         └── 同位语从句: (that education should be ... priorities)
>               └── 非谓语: (for promoting rapid economic development ...)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：两个领域的进步无疑对这些社会以及其他所有社会的社会、政治和智力发展都是必要的；然而，认为教育应当成为促进贫困国家快速经济发展的最高优先事项之一的传统观点是错误的。
>
> **考点提示**：分号连接两个独立分句，however 表示转折；that 从句是 view 的同位语从句；for promoting 是介词 for 加动名词结构。

We are fortunate that it is, because building new educational systems there and putting enough people through them to improve economic performance would require two or three generations.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：We are fortunate that it is, because building new educational systems there and putting enough people through them to improve economic performance would require two or three generations.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: We
> - 谓语 V: are
> - 补语 C: fortunate
> - 原因状语从句主语 S: building new educational systems there and putting enough people through them
> - 原因状语从句谓语 V: would require
> - 原因状语从句宾语 O: two or three generations
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 名词性从句（名从） | that | 作 fortunate 的补充说明；it 指代前句“这种观点是错误的” |
> | 状语从句（状从） | because | 表示“幸运”的原因 |
> | 非谓语短语 | building... and putting... | because 从句的并列主语 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | through them | 修饰 putting，them 指 educational systems |
> | 非谓语短语 | to improve economic performance | 表示目的，修饰 putting enough people through them |
> | 副词 | there | 修饰 building，指在贫困国家当地 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: We + are + fortunate
>   ├── 名从: (that it is [wrong]) → 说明 fortunate 的具体内容
>   └── 原因状从: (because ... would require two or three generations)
>         ├── 并列主语: building ... and putting ...
>         │     └── 目的非谓语: (to improve economic performance)
>         └── 谓语: would require + 宾语: two or three generations
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：幸运的是，这种观点确实是错误的，因为在那里建立新的教育体系，并让足够多的人接受教育以改善经济表现，需要两三代人的时间。
>
> **考点提示**：that 从句补充说明形容词 fortunate；because 引导原因状语从句；两个动名词短语 building 和 putting 构成并列主语；would require 表示在假设情境下的结果。

The findings of a research institution have consistently shown that workers in all countries can be trained on the job to achieve radically higher productivity and, as a result, radically higher standards of living.

Ironically, the first evidence for this idea appeared in the United States. Not long ago, with the country entering a recession and Japan at its pre-bubble peak, the U.S. workforce was derided as poorly educated and one of the primary causes of the poor U.S. economic performance.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Not long ago, with the country entering a recession and Japan at its pre-bubble peak, the U.S. workforce was derided as poorly educated and one of the primary causes of the poor U.S. economic performance.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 状语 A: Not long ago
> - 主语 S: the U.S. workforce
> - 谓语 V: was derided
> - 补语 C: as poorly educated and one of the primary causes of the poor U.S. economic performance
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 独立主格结构 | with | 说明主句发生时的背景 |
> | 非谓语短语 | entering a recession | country 的逻辑动作，作独立主格中的补足成分 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | at its pre-bubble peak | 说明 Japan 所处状态 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | as | 引出 workforce 被嘲讽的身份或原因 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | of | 修饰 causes，说明原因所针对的对象 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: the U.S. workforce + was derided + as ...
>   ├── 时间状语: (Not long ago)
>   ├── 独立主格: (with the country entering a recession
>   │              and Japan at its pre-bubble peak)
>   └── 并列补语: poorly educated + one of the primary causes ...
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：不久前，正值美国步入衰退期、日本处于泡沫破裂前的峰值之际，美国劳动力被嘲讽为受教育程度低，并被视为美国经济表现不佳的主要原因之一。
>
> **考点提示**：with + 名词 + 分词/介词短语构成独立主格结构；was derided as...表示“被嘲讽为/被认为是……”；poorly educated 与 one of... 是并列补语。

Japan was, and remains, the global leader in automotive-assembly productivity. Yet the research revealed that the U.S. factories of Honda, Nissan, and Toyota achieved about 95 percent of the productivity of their Japanese counterparts—a result of the training that U.S. workers received on the job.

More recently, while examining housing construction, the researchers discovered that illiterate, non-English-speaking Mexican workers in Houston, Texas, consistently met best-practice labor productivity standards despite the complexity of the building industry's work.

What is the real relationship between education and economic development? We have to suspect that continuing economic growth promotes the development of education even when governments don't force it. After all, that's how education got started. When our ancestors were hunters and gatherers 10,000 years ago, they didn't have time to wonder much about anything besides finding food. Only when humanity began to get its food in a more productive way was there time for other things.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Only when humanity began to get its food in a more productive way was there time for other things.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 状语 A: Only when humanity began to get its food in a more productive way
> - 谓语 V: was
> - 主语 S: time for other things
> - 存现引导词 there: 引出主语 time
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句（状从） | when | 说明“有时间”发生的条件或时间 |
> | 副词 | only | 修饰 when 从句，表示“只有……” |
> | 非谓语/动词不定式结构 | to get its food | 作 began 的补足成分 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | in a more productive way | 修饰 get，说明获取食物的方式 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | for other things | 修饰 time，说明时间用途 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 正常语序: There was time for other things
>   └── 条件/时间状从: (only when humanity began to get its food ...)
>         ├── 主语: humanity
>         ├── 谓语: began
>         └── 补足成分: to get its food in a more productive way
> 倒装语序: Only when ... + was + there + time ...
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：只有当人类开始以更高效的方式获取食物时，才有时间做其他事情。
>
> **考点提示**：Only + 状语从句置于句首时，主句采用部分倒装，将谓语 was 提到主语 there 之前；不要把 when 从句误判为主句。

As education improved, humanity's productivity potential increased as well. When the competitive environment pushed our ancestors to achieve that potential, they could in turn afford more education. This increasingly high level of education is probably a necessary, but not a sufficient, condition for the complex political systems required by advanced economic performance.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：This increasingly high level of education is probably a necessary, but not a sufficient, condition for the complex political systems required by advanced economic performance.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: This increasingly high level of education
> - 状语 A: probably
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: a necessary, but not a sufficient, condition
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语（介短） | for | 修饰 condition，说明该条件对应的对象 |
> | 非谓语短语（过去分词） | required by advanced economic performance | 修饰 political systems，等同于 which are required by... |
> | 介词短语（介短） | by | 修饰 required，表示“由……所要求” |
> | 插入成分 | but not | 补充限制 sufficient，构成必要但不充分的对比 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: This increasingly high level of education + is + a ... condition
>   ├── 副词: probably
>   ├── 对比插入: necessary, but not sufficient
>   └── 介短: (for the complex political systems)
>         └── 过去分词短语: (required by advanced economic performance)
>               └── 介短: (by advanced economic performance)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：这种不断提高的教育水平，很可能是复杂政治制度的必要条件，但不是充分条件；而这些制度正是先进经济运行所需要的。
>
> **考点提示**：necessary but not sufficient 是“必要但不充分”的固定逻辑表达；required by...是过去分词短语作后置定语，可还原为 which are required by...。

Thus poor countries might not be able to escape their poverty traps without political changes that may be possible only with broader formal education. A lack of formal education, however, doesn't constrain the ability of the developing world's workforce to substantially improve productivity for the foreseeable future. On the contrary, constraints on improving productivity explain why education isn't developing more quickly there than it is.
