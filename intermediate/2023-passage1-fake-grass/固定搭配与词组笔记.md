# 2023 Passage 1 固定搭配与词组

> [!note] 来源说明
> 本篇固定搭配与词组由 `formatted-article.md` **从本文提取**，仅收录本篇实际出现的表达。
> 纯语法内容见 [[intermediate/2023-passage1-fake-grass/grammar-notes.md|2023 Passage 1 语法整理]]。

---

## 一、主题词与核心概念

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **plastic grass** | 塑料草坪（本文出现最多的说法） | About eight million square metres of **plastic grass** is sold each year |
| **fake grass** | 假草坪（口语化，带贬义） | The Chelsea Flower Show has banned **fake grass** |
| **artificial grass** | 人造草坪（中性/行业用语） | the "greenwash" of **artificial grass** |
| **fake lawns** | 假草坪（lawn 强调整片草地） | another petition last year about banning **fake lawns** |
| **ethos** | （机构/群体的）理念、精神特质 | declaring it to be not part of its **ethos** |
| **biodiversity** | 生物多样性 | the damage plastic grass does to the environment and **biodiversity** |
| **sustainability strategy** | 可持续发展战略 | We launched our **sustainability strategy** last year |
| **greenwash** | "漂绿"、绿色伪装（以环保之名粉饰） | claims to "cut through the **greenwash**" of artificial grass |
| **ecological damage tax** | 生态损害税 | calling for an "**ecological damage**" tax on such lawns |
| **weed killer** | 除草剂 | real grass requires considerable amounts of water, **weed killer** or other treatments |
| **habitat** | 栖息地 | provides **habitat** for insects |
| **sustainable drainage** | 可持续排水（系统） | ensure **sustainable drainage** |
| **biodiversity duty** | 生物多样性义务（英国法定义务） | measures such as the strengthened **biodiversity duty** |
| **public authorities** | 公共机构、政府当局 | encourage **public authorities** to consider sustainable alternatives |
| **sustainable alternatives** | 可持续的替代方案 | consider **sustainable alternatives** |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 全文用**三种说法**指同一事物：`plastic grass`（中性偏贬）、`fake grass`（**贬义**，多用于反对者）、`artificial grass`（**中性/行业**，多用于支持者与政府）。**称呼的变化就是立场的变化**：第 21 题问 RHS 的看法，原文用 `plastic grass` 并直指其对环境与生物多样性的**损害**；第 24 题问政府态度，原文用中性的 `artificial grass` 并强调"**遵守现行规则**"。抓称呼即可定立场。

## 二、动词短语

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **take a shortcut** | 走捷径、图省事 | homeowners … are **taking a shortcut** |
| **pay the price** | 付出代价 | it is the environment that is **paying the price** |
| **be in line with** | 与……一致、符合 | fake grass is just not **in line with** our ethos |
| **launch a strategy** | 启动一项战略 | We **launched** our sustainability strategy last year |
| **raise awareness of** | 提高对……的认识 | campaigners try to **raise awareness of** the problems fake grass causes |
| **cut through** | 揭穿、穿透（喻突破粉饰） | claims to "**cut through** the greenwash" |
| **sign a petition** | 签署请愿书 | encourage people to **sign two petitions** |
| **call for** | 呼吁、要求 | one **calling for** a ban on the sale of plastic grass |
| **gather signatures** | 征集签名 | They have **gathered** 7,276 and 11,282 **signatures** |
| **point out that** | 指出…… | supporters of fake grass **point out that** there is also an environmental impact |
| **lay fake grass** | 铺设假草坪 | people who **lay fake grass** tend to use their garden more |
| **consume electricity** | 消耗电力 | usually **consume electricity** or petrol |
| **legislate on** | 就……立法 | rather than **legislating on** such matters |
| **comply with** | 遵守、符合 | the use of artificial grass must **comply with** the legal and policy safeguards |
| **serve to encourage** | 起到鼓励……的作用 | should **serve to encourage** public authorities |
| **ban sth from sth** | 禁止某物出现在某场合 | has **banned** fake grass **from** this year's event |

> [!warning] 易错点
> ① `take a shortcut` 在本文**不是褒义**（"高效"），而是**批评**"图省事、以环境为代价"。② `cut through` 字面"切穿"，此处是**比喻**"揭穿（绿色伪装）"，与 `greenwash` 搭配使用。③ `lay fake grass` 中 `lay` = "**铺设**"（过去式/过去分词均为 `laid`），勿与 `lie`（躺）混淆。④ `comply with` 是**正式用语**，常用于"**法律法规、标准**"；口语中同义表达为 `follow / obey the rules`——第 24 题选项 `obey existing rules` 正是它的**同义改写**。

## 三、名词与固定表达

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **homeowners** | 房主、业主 | **homeowners** across the country |
| **gardening circles** | 园艺界、园艺圈子 | spread to the highest **gardening circles** |
| **the Royal Horticultural Society (RHS)** | 皇家园艺学会 | **The Royal Horticultural Society (RHS)**, which runs the annual show |
| **the annual show** | 年度花展 | runs the **annual show** in west London |
| **environmental benefits** | 环境益处 | because of its **environmental benefits** |
| **environmental impact** | 环境影响 | there is also an **environmental impact** with natural lawns |
| **campaigners** | 活动人士、倡导者 | as **campaigners** try to raise awareness |
| **followers** | （社交媒体的）粉丝、关注者 | more than 20,000 **followers** |
| **signatures** | 签名（数） | 7,276 and 11,282 **signatures** |
| **supporters** | 支持者 | **supporters** of fake grass point out … |
| **considerable amounts of** | 大量的 | **considerable amounts of** water |
| **the legal and policy safeguards** | 法律与政策保障措施 | must comply with **the legal and policy safeguards** |
| **in place** | 现行的、已到位的 | the legal and policy safeguards **in place** |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `safeguards in place` 是**后置定语**搭配（= safeguards **that are** in place），译作"**现行的保障措施**"，不能译成"在地方"。`environmental benefits`（益处）与 `environmental impact`（影响，**常暗指负面**）是本文**双方各说各话**的用词对照：RHS 讲真草的"**益处**"，行业讲真草的"**影响**"——**同一个对象，两套措辞**，是作者暗示倾向的细节。

## 四、形容词与副词

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **considerable** | 相当大的 | requires **considerable** amounts of water |
| **alleviating** | 缓解（现在分词） | **alleviating** flooding |
| **sustainable** | 可持续的 | **sustainable** drainage / **sustainable** alternatives |
| **annual** | 年度的 | the **annual** show |
| **ecological** | 生态的 | an "**ecological** damage" tax |
| **strengthened** | 强化了的 | such as the **strengthened** biodiversity duty |
| **considerable** vs. **considerate** | 相当大的 vs. 体贴的 | 形近易混，本文用前者 |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `alleviate`（缓解，**动词**）常与 `flooding / pain / pressure` 搭配；`elevate`（提升）与 `alleviate` **拼写相近、意义相反**，务必区分。`strengthened biodiversity duty` 中 `strengthened` 是**过去分词作前置定语**（= 被强化的），不是谓语。

## 五、逻辑连接与观点标记

| 搭配 | 释义 | 原文语境 |
|------|------|----------|
| **however** | 然而（转折） | **However**, supporters of fake grass point out … |
| **in response to** | 作为对……的回应 | **In response to** another petition last year |
| **rather than** | 而不是 | prefer to help … **rather than** legislating |
| **while** | 而、同时（对比） | **while** measures such as … should serve to encourage |
| **in the quest for** | 在追求……的过程中 | **In the quest for** the perfect lawn |
| **because of** | 由于 | **because of** the damage plastic grass does |
| **It added** | 它（政府）补充道 | **It added:** "We prefer to help …" |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 全文的**论证骨架**由这几个连接词搭成：`In the quest for`（立现象）→ `However`（**转**到行业反驳）→ `In response to`（**转**到政府回应）→ `rather than` + `while`（政府立场**双路径**）。考研阅读的**段落功能题**（"第 4 段的作用是什么"）几乎都靠这类**连接词 + 段落首句**定位：`However, supporters … point out` 一句即表明第 4 段是**反方观点**，因此第 23 题选"真草种植的缺点"，而非"假草的好处"。
