# Women under Stress: Gender Differences in Stress Response

While still catching up to men in some **spheres** of modern life, women appear to be way ahead in at least one **undesirable category**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：While still catching up to men in some **spheres** of modern life, women appear to be way ahead in at least one **undesirable category**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: women
> - 谓语 V: appear to be（系动词性短语）
> - 表语 C: way ahead in at least one undesirable category
> - 状语 A: While still catching up to men in some spheres of modern life（while 让步/对比状语从句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句 | While | 全句状语，表对比/让步"虽然……" |
> | 非谓语（现在分词） | catching up | 与 while 搭配，省略主语的 while 从句（while they are catching up） |
> | 介词短语 | to men | 与 catch up 搭配，catch up to 赶上 |
> | 介词短语 | in some spheres | 修饰 catching up，表领域范围 |
> | 介词短语 | in at least one undesirable category | 表语中的范围状语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: women appear to be way ahead in at least one undesirable category
>   ├── 主语 S: women
>   ├── 谓语 V: appear to be
>   ├── 表语 C: way ahead
>   │     └── 介短(in): in at least one undesirable category → 表语范围
>   └── 状从(While): While still catching up to men in some spheres of modern life
>         ├── 省略主语+be: while (they are) still catching up
>         └── 介短(to/in): to men / in some spheres → 与 catch up 搭配
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：尽管在现代生活的某些领域仍在追赶男性，女性至少在一个不受欢迎的类别上似乎已经遥遥领先。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `while` 引导让步/对比状语从句，从句主语与主句一致时可省略"主语+be"（while (they are) still catching up）——省略结构是高频考点
> 2. `appear to be`（似乎是）后接表语 way ahead
> 3. `catch up to`（赶上）为固定搭配

==“Women are particularly **susceptible** to developing **depression** and **anxiety disorders** in response to **stress** compared to men,”== according to Dr. Yehuda, chief **psychiatrist** at New York's Veteran's Administration Hospital.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：“Women are particularly **susceptible** to developing **depression** and **anxiety disorders** in response to **stress** compared to men,” according to Dr. Yehuda, chief **psychiatrist** at New York's Veteran's Administration Hospital.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Women
> - 谓语 V: are（系动词）
> - 表语 C: particularly susceptible to developing depression and anxiety disorders
> - 状语 A: in response to stress / compared to men（比较状语）
> - 插入语: according to Dr. Yehuda（表消息来源）
> - 同位语 C: chief psychiatrist at New York's Veteran's Administration Hospital（解释 Dr. Yehuda 的身份）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | to | 与 susceptible 搭配，be susceptible to sth. |
> | 非谓语（动名词） | developing | to 的宾语，其后接 depression and anxiety disorders |
> | 介词短语 | in response to | 表原因/响应，修饰 susceptible |
> | 非谓语（过去分词） | compared | compared to men 作比较状语，修饰整句 |
> | 插入语 | according to | 表消息来源，阅读时先跳过 |
> | 同位语 | 逗号 | chief psychiatrist ... 解释 Dr. Yehuda 的身份 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Women are particularly susceptible to developing depression and anxiety disorders
>   ├── 主语 S: Women
>   ├── 谓语 V: are
>   ├── 表语 C: particularly susceptible to developing depression and anxiety disorders
>   │     ├── 介短(to): to developing → 与 susceptible 搭配
>   │     └── 动名词(developing): developing depression and anxiety disorders → to 的宾语
>   ├── 介短(in response to): in response to stress → 表原因
>   ├── 比较状语(compared to): compared to men → 比较对象
>   └── 插入语(according to): according to Dr. Yehuda, chief psychiatrist at NY Veteran's Administration Hospital
>         └── 同位语: chief psychiatrist ... → 解释 Dr. Yehuda
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：“与男性相比，女性在应对压力时特别容易患上抑郁症和焦虑症。”纽约退伍军人管理局医院精神科主任耶胡达博士如是说。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `be susceptible to`（易受……影响/容易患……），to 为介词，后接动名词 developing
> 2. `compared to`（与……相比）过去分词短语作比较状语
> 3. `according to` 插入语表消息来源，理解时可先跳过
> 4. 逗号同位语 `chief psychiatrist` 解释人名身份（Q21 相关）

Studies of both animals and humans have shown that **sex hormones** somehow affect the **stress response**, causing females under stress to produce more of the **trigger chemicals** than do males under the same conditions.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Studies of both animals and humans have shown that **sex hormones** somehow affect the **stress response**, causing females under stress to produce more of the **trigger chemicals** than do males under the same conditions.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Studies of both animals and humans
> - 谓语 V: have shown
> - 宾语 O: that sex hormones somehow affect the stress response（that 宾语从句）
> - 非谓语（现在分词）: causing females under stress to produce more of the trigger chemicals（结果状语）
> - 比较从句: than do males under the same conditions（倒装）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | of both animals and humans | 后置定语，修饰 Studies |
> | 名词性从句（宾语从句） | that | 作 have shown 的宾语 |
> | 非谓语（现在分词） | causing | 结果状语，表"从而致使……" |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to produce | 与 cause 搭配，cause sb. to do |
> | 介词短语 | under stress | 修饰 females |
> | 比较从句 | than | 比较对象，倒装 than do males |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Studies of both animals and humans have shown that ...
>   ├── 主语 S: Studies
>   │     └── 介短(of): of both animals and humans → 修饰 Studies
>   ├── 谓语 V: have shown
>   ├── 宾语从句(that): that sex hormones somehow affect the stress response
>   │     ├── 主语 S: sex hormones
>   │     ├── 状语 A: somehow
>   │     └── 谓语 V+宾语: affect the stress response
>   ├── 结果状语(现在分词): causing females under stress to produce more of the trigger chemicals
>   │     ├── 宾语 O: females under stress
>   │     ├── 宾补(不定式): to produce more of the trigger chemicals
>   │     └── 介短(of): of the trigger chemicals
>   └── 比较从句(than): than do males under the same conditions（倒装 = than males do）
>         └── 介短(under): under the same conditions
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：对动物和人类的研究都表明，性激素会以某种方式影响压力反应，使处于压力之下的雌性比处于相同条件下的雄性产生更多的引发应激的化学物质。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `that` 引导宾语从句作 have shown 的宾语，从句内主谓宾完整
> 2. `causing...` 现在分词作结果状语，逻辑主语是前面整个 that 从句的内容
> 3. 比较句倒装 `than do males` = `than males do`，比较的是"产生化学物质"这一动作（Q21 相关）
> 4. `cause sb. to do`（使某人做某事）固定结构

In several of the studies, when **stressed-out** female rats had their **ovaries** (the female **reproductive organs**) removed, their chemical responses became equal to those of the males.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In several of the studies, when **stressed-out** female rats had their **ovaries** (the female **reproductive organs**) removed, their chemical responses became equal to those of the males.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: their chemical responses
> - 谓语 V: became（系动词）
> - 表语 C: equal to those of the males
> - 状语 A: In several of the studies（范围状语）
> - 状语 A: when stressed-out female rats had their ovaries removed（时间状语从句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | In several of the studies | 句首范围状语 |
> | 状语从句 | when | 时间状语从句 |
> | 使役结构 | have ... done | had their ovaries removed |
> | 同位语 | 括号 | (the female reproductive organs) 解释 ovaries |
> | 介词短语 | to those | 与 equal 搭配，be equal to |
> | 代词替代 | those | 替代 chemical responses |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: their chemical responses became equal to those of the males
>   ├── 主语 S: their chemical responses
>   ├── 谓语 V: became
>   ├── 表语 C: equal to those of the males
>   │     ├── 介短(to): to those → 与 equal 搭配
>   │     └── 代词替代: those = chemical responses → of the males
>   ├── 范围状语: In several of the studies
>   └── 时间状从(when): when stressed-out female rats had their ovaries removed
>         ├── 主语 S: stressed-out female rats
>         ├── 谓语 V: had
>         ├── 宾语 O: their ovaries
>         ├── 宾补(过去分词): removed（have sth. done 结构）
>         └── 括号同位语: (the female reproductive organs) → 解释 ovaries
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在其中几项研究中，当压力过大的雌性大鼠被切除卵巢（雌性生殖器官）之后，它们的化学物质反应变得与雄性相同。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `when` 时间状语从句 + 主句；从句中 `have sth. done`（使某事被做）是高频考点
> 2. `equal to`（与……相等/相同），to 为介词
> 3. `those` 替代上文 chemical responses，避免重复
> 4. 括号同位语 (the female reproductive organs) 解释 ovaries，阅读时跳过不影响主干

Adding to a woman's increased dose of stress chemicals, are her increased “opportunities” for stress.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Adding to a woman's increased dose of stress chemicals, are her increased “opportunities” for stress.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: her increased “opportunities” for stress（倒装后置）
> - 谓语 V: are adding to（现在进行时）
> - 宾语 O: a woman's increased dose of stress chemicals
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（现在分词） | Adding | 倒装结构中的谓语部分提前，与 to 搭配 |
> | 介词短语 | to | 与 add 搭配，add to 增加 |
> | 介词短语 | of stress chemicals | 后置定语，修饰 dose |
> | 介词短语 | for stress | 后置定语，修饰 opportunities |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 全倒装句: [are adding to a woman's increased dose of stress chemicals] + 主语后置
>   正常语序: Her increased "opportunities" for stress are adding to a woman's
>             increased dose of stress chemicals
>   ├── 主语 S: her increased "opportunities" for stress
>   │     └── 介短(for): for stress → 修饰 opportunities
>   ├── 谓语 V: are adding to（现在进行时）
>   └── 宾语 O: a woman's increased dose of stress chemicals
>         └── 介短(of): of stress chemicals → 修饰 dose
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：加剧女性体内应激化学物质剂量增加的，是她们承受压力的“机会”也在增多。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. **全倒装**：为强调"机会增多"，将谓语 are adding to 与宾语前置、主语 her increased "opportunities" 后置；识别技巧是先找主语还原语序
> 2. `add to`（增加、加剧）固定搭配
> 3. `dose`（剂量）此处为比喻用法，指"应激化学物质的剂量"

“It's not necessarily that women don't **cope** as well. It's just that they have so much more to **cope** with,” says Dr. Yehuda.

“Their capacity for **tolerating stress** may even be greater than men's,” she observes, “it's just that they're dealing with so many more things that they become **worn out** from it more visibly and sooner.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：“Their capacity for **tolerating stress** may even be greater than men's,” she observes, “it's just that they're dealing with so many more things that they become **worn out** from it more visibly and sooner.”
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 引语 1: Their capacity for tolerating stress may even be greater than men's
> - 插入语: she observes（分隔两个直接引语）
> - 引语 2: it's just that they're dealing with so many more things that they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | for tolerating stress | 修饰 capacity |
> | 非谓语（动名词） | tolerating | for 的宾语 |
> | 比较结构 | than | 比较对象 men's（= men's capacity） |
> | 插入语 | she observes | 分隔两个直接引语 |
> | 名词性从句（表语从句） | that | it's just that... 表语从句 |
> | 结果状语从句 | so...that | so many more things that they become worn out |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 直接引语结构: "引语1," she observes, "引语2"
>   ├── 引语1: Their capacity for tolerating stress may even be greater than men's
>   │     ├── 主语 S: Their capacity
>   │     │     └── 介短(for): for tolerating stress → 修饰 capacity
>   │     ├── 谓语 V: may be
>   │     ├── 表语 C: even greater than men's
>   │     └── 比较: than men's（= men's capacity）
>   ├── 插入语: she observes
>   └── 引语2: it's just that they're dealing with so many more things that they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner
>         ├── 形式主语: it
>         ├── 谓语 V: is
>         ├── 表语从句(that): that they're dealing with so many more things
>         └── 结果状从(so...that): so many more things that they become worn out from it more visibly and sooner
>               └── 介短(from): from it → 与 worn out 搭配
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：“她们承受压力的能力甚至可能比男性更强，”她观察到，“只是因为她们要处理的事情实在太多，以至于她们会更快、更明显地从这种压力中变得筋疲力尽。”
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 两个直接引语被插入语 `she observes` 分隔，阅读时跳过插入语即可连贯理解
> 2. `it's just that...`（只是……）表语从句，说明原因
> 3. `so...that` 结果状语从句（如此……以至于……）是高频考点（Q22 相关）
> 4. `capacity for doing`（做某事的能力），for 后接动名词

Dr. Yehuda notes another difference between the sexes.

“I think that the kinds of things that women are exposed to tend to be in more of a **chronic** or **repeated** nature.

Men go to war and are exposed to **combat stress**.

Men are exposed to more acts of random **physical violence**.

The kinds of **interpersonal violence** that women are exposed to tend to be in **domestic situations**, by, unfortunately, parents or other family members, and they tend not to be **one-shot deals**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The kinds of **interpersonal violence** that women are exposed to tend to be in **domestic situations**, by, unfortunately, parents or other family members, and they tend not to be **one-shot deals**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: The kinds of interpersonal violence that women are exposed to
> - 谓语 V: tend to be
> - 表语 C: in domestic situations
> - 并列分句: and they tend not to be one-shot deals
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | of interpersonal violence | 后置定语，修饰 The kinds |
> | 定语从句 | that | 修饰 The kinds of interpersonal violence |
> | 介词短语 | to | 与 exposed 搭配，be exposed to |
> | 介词短语 | in domestic situations | 表语 |
> | 插入语 | by, unfortunately, parents... | 补充施暴者 |
> | 并列连词 | and | 连接两个并列分句 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 分句1: The kinds of interpersonal violence that women are exposed to tend to be in domestic situations
>   ├── 主语 S: The kinds of interpersonal violence
>   │     ├── 介短(of): of interpersonal violence → 修饰 kinds
>   │     └── 定从(that): that women are exposed to → 修饰 violence
>   │           ├── 主语 S: women
>   │           └── 谓语 V: are exposed to（被动）
>   ├── 谓语 V: tend to be
>   ├── 表语 C: in domestic situations
>   │     └── 介短(in): in domestic situations
>   └── 插入语: by, unfortunately, parents or other family members → 补充施暴者
> 分句2(and): and they tend not to be one-shot deals
>   ├── 主语 S: they（= the kinds of interpersonal violence）
>   ├── 谓语 V: tend not to be
>   └── 表语 C: one-shot deals
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：女性所面对的种种人际暴力往往发生在家庭环境之中——不幸的是，施暴者是父母或其他家庭成员——而且这些暴力往往不是一次性的事件。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `that` 定语从句修饰 The kinds of interpersonal violence，紧接先行词
> 2. `be exposed to`（接触到/暴露于）固定搭配，to 为介词（Q23 相关）
> 3. 插入语 `by, unfortunately, parents or other family members` 补充施暴者，阅读时先跳过
> 4. `and` 并列第二个分句，`tend not to be`（往往不是）

The **wear-and-tear** that comes from these longer relationships can be quite **devastating**.”

Adeline Alvarez married at 18 and gave birth to a son, but was **determined** to finish college.

“I struggled a lot to get the college degree.

I was living in so much **frustration** that that was my **escape**, to go to school, and get ahead and do better.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：I was living in so much **frustration** that that was my **escape**, to go to school, and get ahead and do better.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: I
> - 谓语 V: was living
> - 状语 A: in so much frustration（状态状语）
> - 结果状语从句: that that was my escape, to go to school, and get ahead and do better
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | in so much frustration | 状态状语，修饰 was living |
> | 结果状语从句 | so...that | so much frustration that ... |
> | 名词性从句（主语从句） | that | that was my escape（第二个 that 作从句主语） |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to go to school | 同位语，解释 escape 的内容 |
> | 并列连词 | and | get ahead and do better 并列 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: I was living in so much frustration
>   ├── 主语 S: I
>   ├── 谓语 V: was living
>   └── 介短(in): in so much frustration → 状态状语
> 结果状从(so...that): that that was my escape, to go to school, and get ahead and do better
>   ├── 结果引导词: that（so...that 结构）
>   ├── 主语从句(that): that was my escape
>   │     ├── 主语 S: that（指示代词，指"上学这件事"）
>   │     ├── 谓语 V: was
>   │     └── 表语 C: my escape
>   │           └── 不定式同位语(to): to go to school, and get ahead and do better → 解释 escape
>   └── 并列不定式: to go to school / (to) get ahead / (to) do better
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：我生活在极大的挫败感之中，以至于上学、取得进步、做得更好成了我的逃避方式。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `so...that` 结果状语从句：第一个 that 引导从句，第二个 that 作从句主语（指代"上学"这件事）——两个 that 连用是易混考点
> 2. 不定式 `to go to school, and get ahead and do better` 作 escape 的同位语，解释逃避的具体内容
> 3. 并列不定式第二、三项省略 to：to go..., (to) get ahead and (to) do better

Later, her marriage ended and she became a **single mother**.

==Alvarez's experience demonstrates the importance of finding ways to **diffuse stress** before it **threatens** your health and your ability to function.==

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Alvarez's experience demonstrates the importance of finding ways to **diffuse stress** before it **threatens** your health and your ability to function.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Alvarez's experience
> - 谓语 V: demonstrates
> - 宾语 O: the importance of finding ways to diffuse stress
> - 状语 A: before it threatens your health and your ability to function（时间状语从句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | of finding ways | 后置定语，修饰 the importance |
> | 非谓语（动名词） | finding | of 的宾语 |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to diffuse stress | 后置定语/目的，修饰 ways |
> | 状语从句 | before | 时间状语从句 |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to function | 后置定语，修饰 ability |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Alvarez's experience demonstrates the importance of finding ways to diffuse stress
>   ├── 主语 S: Alvarez's experience
>   ├── 谓语 V: demonstrates
>   ├── 宾语 O: the importance
>   │     └── 介短(of): of finding ways → 修饰 importance
>   │           └── 动名词(finding): finding ways
>   │                 └── 不定式(to diffuse): to diffuse stress → 修饰 ways（表目的）
>   └── 时间状从(before): before it threatens your health and your ability to function
>         ├── 主语 S: it（= stress）
>         ├── 谓语 V: threatens
>         └── 宾语 O: your health and your ability to function
>               └── 不定式(to function): to function → 修饰 ability
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：阿尔瓦雷斯的经历表明，在压力威胁到你的健康和工作能力之前，找到疏导压力的方法十分重要。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `of finding ways to diffuse stress`：介词 of + 动名词短语作 importance 的后置定语，动名词后再接不定式表目的
> 2. `before` 时间状语从句（在……之前），本文点题句（Q25 相关）
> 3. `ability to function`（正常工作/运作的能力），不定式作 ability 的后置定语
> 4. 代词 `it` 指代 stress，需联系上文确认指代对象
