# Women under Stress: Gender Differences in Stress Response 压力下的女性：压力反应的两性差异 中英对照

While still catching up to men in some **spheres** of modern life, women appear to be way ahead in at least one **undesirable category**. ==“Women are particularly **susceptible** to developing **depression** and **anxiety disorders** in response to **stress** compared to men,”== according to Dr. Yehuda, chief **psychiatrist** at New York's Veteran's Administration Hospital.

尽管在现代生活的某些领域仍在追赶男性，女性至少在一个不受欢迎的类别上似乎已经遥遥领先。据纽约退伍军人管理局医院精神科主任耶胡达博士称："与男性相比，女性在应对压力时特别容易患上抑郁症和焦虑症。"

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"way ahead"**：意为"遥遥领先"，此处略带反讽——领先的竟是"不受欢迎的类别"。
> - **"be susceptible to"**：易受……影响的、容易患上（疾病）的，译为"特别容易患上"。
> - **"chief psychiatrist"**：精神科主任医师/精神科主任。
> - **"in response to stress"**：面对压力时、因压力而产生的反应。

Studies of both animals and humans have shown that **sex hormones** somehow affect the **stress response**, causing females under stress to produce more of the **trigger chemicals** than do males under the same conditions. In several of the studies, when **stressed-out** female rats had their **ovaries** (the female **reproductive organs**) removed, their chemical responses became equal to those of the males.

对动物和人类的研究都表明，性激素会以某种方式影响压力反应，使处于压力之下的雌性比处于相同条件下的雄性产生更多的引发应激的化学物质。在其中几项研究中，当压力过大的雌性大鼠被切除卵巢（雌性生殖器官）之后，它们的化学物质反应变得与雄性相同。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"trigger chemicals"**：字面为"触发化学物质"，指引发应激反应/使压力反应加剧的化学物质，译为"引发应激的化学物质"。
> - **"stressed-out"**：压力过大的、因压力而疲惫不堪的。
> - **"than do males"**：比较状语从句中的倒装，正常语序为 than males do，译为"比……雄性（产生的化学物质）更多"。

Adding to a woman's increased dose of stress chemicals, are her increased “opportunities” for stress. “It's not necessarily that women don't **cope** as well. It's just that they have so much more to **cope** with,” says Dr. Yehuda. “Their capacity for **tolerating stress** may even be greater than men's,” she observes, “it's just that they're dealing with so many more things that they become **worn out** from it more visibly and sooner.”

使女性体内应激化学物质剂量进一步增加的，是她们承受压力的"机会"也在增多。"并不是女人应付得不好，而是她们需要应付的事情多得多。"耶胡达博士说。"她们承受压力的能力甚至可能比男性更强，"她观察到，"只是因为她们要处理的事情实在太多，以至于她们会更快、更明显地从这种压力中变得筋疲力尽。"

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **第一句为全倒装句**：主语是 her increased "opportunities" for stress，谓语是 are adding to，正常语序为 Her increased "opportunities" for stress are adding to a woman's increased dose of stress chemicals，翻译时按"使……增加的，是……"处理。
> - **"cope with"**：应付、应对；"so much more to cope with" 译为"需要应付的事情多得多"。
> - **"become worn out from it more visibly and sooner"**：更快、更明显地因此筋疲力尽。

Dr. Yehuda notes another difference between the sexes. “I think that the kinds of things that women are exposed to tend to be in more of a **chronic** or **repeated** nature. Men go to war and are exposed to **combat stress**. Men are exposed to more acts of random **physical violence**. The kinds of **interpersonal violence** that women are exposed to tend to be in **domestic situations**, by, unfortunately, parents or other family members, and they tend not to be **one-shot deals**. The **wear-and-tear** that comes from these longer relationships can be quite **devastating**.”

耶胡达博士指出了两性之间的另一个差异。"我认为，女性所接触的事物往往更具慢性或反复的性质。男性上战场，接触的是战斗压力。男性接触更多的是一些随机的身体暴力行为。而女性所面对的种种人际暴力往往发生在家庭环境之中——不幸的是，施暴者是父母或其他家庭成员——而且这些暴力往往不是一次性的事件。这些较长期关系中产生的损耗，可能是相当具有毁灭性的。"

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"chronic"**：慢性的、长期的（此处为本文核心概念，与 Q23 考点相关）。
> - **"random physical violence"**：随机的身体暴力；**"interpersonal violence"**：人际暴力（发生在人与人之间的暴力）。
> - **"domestic situations"**：家庭环境/家庭情境。
> - **"one-shot deals"**：一次性的事情，此处指"一次性事件"。
> - **"wear-and-tear"**：磨损、损耗，此处比喻长期关系带来的身心消耗，译为"损耗"。

Adeline Alvarez married at 18 and gave birth to a son, but was **determined** to finish college. “I struggled a lot to get the college degree. I was living in so much **frustration** that that was my **escape**, to go to school, and get ahead and do better.” Later, her marriage ended and she became a **single mother**. “It's the hardest thing to take care of a teenager, have a job, pay the rent, pay the car payment, and pay the debt. ==I lived from **paycheck to paycheck**.==”

阿德琳·阿尔瓦雷斯（Adeline Alvarez）18 岁结婚，生了一个儿子，但她决心完成大学学业。"我为了获得大学学位付出了很多努力。我生活在极大的挫败感之中，上学、取得进步、做得更好，就是我的逃避方式。"后来，她的婚姻结束了，她成了一位单身母亲。"照顾一个十几岁的孩子、上班、付房租、还车贷、还债，这是最艰难的事。我的收入仅够勉强糊口，每次发薪就花光了。"

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"was determined to"**：决心做某事。
> - **"get ahead"**：取得进步、出人头地。
> - **"paycheck to paycheck"**：字面为"从一张工资单到下一张工资单"，指收入仅够维持到下次发薪、月月花光，可译为"勉强糊口"或"月光族"（Q24 考点）。

Not everyone experiences the kinds of severe chronic stresses Alvarez describes. But most women today are coping with a lot of **obligations**, with few breaks, and feeling the **strain**. ==Alvarez's experience demonstrates the importance of finding ways to **diffuse stress** before it **threatens** your health and your ability to function.==

并非每个人都经历过阿尔瓦雷斯所描述的那种严重的慢性压力。但如今大多数女性都在应付大量义务，几乎没有喘息之机，并真切地感受到这种压力。阿尔瓦雷斯的经历表明，在压力威胁到你的健康和工作能力之前，找到疏导压力的方法十分重要。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - **"obligations"**：义务、责任；**"with few breaks"**：几乎没有休息/喘息之机。
> - **"feeling the strain"**：感受到紧张/压力。
> - **"diffuse stress"**：疏导、化解压力；**"ability to function"**：正常工作/运作的能力。

## 阅读理解问题 阅读理解问题（中英对照）

21. Which of the following is true according to the first two paragraphs?

    根据前两段，以下哪项是正确的？
    - [A] Women are biologically more vulnerable to stress. — 从生物学角度看，女性更容易受压力的影响。
    - [B] Women are still suffering much stress caused by men. — 女性仍在承受男性造成的巨大压力。
    - [C] Women are more experienced than men in coping with stress. — 女性比男性更有应对压力的经验。
    - [D] Men and women show different inclinations when faced with stress. — 面对压力时，男性和女性表现出不同的倾向。

22. Dr. Yehuda's research suggests that women ____.

    耶胡达博士的研究表明，女性 ____。
    - [A] need extra doses of chemicals to handle stress — 需要额外剂量的化学物质来应对压力
    - [B] have limited capacity for tolerating stress — 承受压力的能力有限
    - [C] are more capable of avoiding stress — 更有能力避免压力
    - [D] are exposed to more stress — 承受着更多的压力

23. According to Paragraph 4, the stress women confront tends to be ____.

    根据第四段，女性所面对的压力往往是 ____。
    - [A] domestic and temporary — 家庭性的、暂时性的
    - [B] irregular and violent — 不规律的和暴力的
    - [C] durable and frequent — 持久且频繁的
    - [D] trivial and random — 琐碎且随机的

24. The sentence “I lived from paycheck to paycheck.” (Para. 5) shows that ____.

    句子"I lived from paycheck to paycheck."（第五段）表明 ____。
    - [A] Alvarez cared about nothing but making money — 阿尔瓦雷斯只关心赚钱
    - [B] Alvarez's salary barely covered her household expenses — 阿尔瓦雷斯的工资勉强够支付家庭开支
    - [C] Alvarez got paychecks from different jobs — 阿尔瓦雷斯从不同的工作中获得工资
    - [D] Alvarez paid practically everything by check — 阿尔瓦雷斯几乎所有的开支都用支票支付

25. Which of the following would be the best title for the text?

    以下哪项最适合作为这篇文章的标题？
    - [A] Strain of Stress: No Way Out? — 压力之累：走投无路？
    - [B] Responses to Stress: Gender Difference — 对压力的反应：两性差异
    - [C] Stress Analysis: What Chemicals Say — 压力分析：化学物质说明了什么
    - [D] Gender Inequality: Women under Stress — 两性不平等：压力之下的女性
