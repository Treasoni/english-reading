# Middle-Class Families Under Financial Risk

During the past generation, the American middle-class family that once could **count on** hard work and **fair play** to keep itself **financially secure** has been transformed by **economic risk** and new realities.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：During the past generation, the American middle-class family that once could **count on** hard work and **fair play** to keep itself **financially secure** has been transformed by **economic risk** and new realities.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: the American middle-class family
> - 谓语 V: has been transformed（被动语态）
> - 状语 A: by economic risk and new realities（被动施动者）
> - 状语 A: During the past generation（时间状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 定语从句 | that | 修饰 the American middle-class family |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to keep | 定语从句内目的状语，说明 count on 的目的 |
> | 介词短语 | During | 时间状语，修饰 has been transformed |
> | 介词短语 | by | 被动施动者，引出 transformation 的来源 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the American middle-class family has been transformed by economic risk and new realities
>   ├── 时间状语: During the past generation
>   ├── 主语 S: the American middle-class family
>   │     └── 定从(that): that once could count on hard work and fair play to keep itself financially secure → 修饰 family
>   │           ├── 谓语 V: could count on
>   │           ├── 宾语 O: hard work and fair play
>   │           └── 目的状语: to keep itself financially secure
>   ├── 谓语 V: has been transformed（被动语态）
>   └── 被动施动者: by economic risk and new realities
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在过去一代人的时间里，曾经可以依靠勤奋工作和公平竞争来保持自身经济安全的美国中产阶级家庭，已经被经济风险和新现实所改变。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 定语从句 `that` 修饰主语 family，使主谓相隔（family ... has been transformed），阅读时须跳过定语找回谓语动词
> 2. `count on`（依靠）、`fair play`（公平竞争）为考研高频词组
> 3. 被动语态 `has been transformed by` 中 `by` 引出施动者，翻译时按"施动者 + 动词"处理更符合中文

Now a **pink slip**, a bad **diagnosis**, or a **disappearing spouse** can reduce a family from solidly middle class to newly poor in a few months.

In just one generation, millions of mothers have gone to work, **transforming** basic family economics. Scholars, policymakers, and critics of all stripes have debated the social implications of these changes, but few have looked at the **side effect**: family risk has risen as well. Today's families have budgeted to the limits of their new **two-paycheck** status.

As a result, they have lost the **parachute** they once had in times of **financial setback**—a **back-up earner** (usually Mom) who could go into the workforce if the primary earner got **laid off** or fell sick.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：As a result, they have lost the **parachute** they once had in times of **financial setback**—a **back-up earner** (usually Mom) who could go into the workforce if the primary earner got **laid off** or fell sick.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: they
> - 谓语 V: have lost
> - 宾语 O: the parachute
> - 状语 A: As a result（结果状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 定语从句（省略 that） | 零关系词 | 修饰 the parachute |
> | 介词短语 | in times of financial setback | 时间状语，修饰 had |
> | 同位语 | 破折号 | a back-up earner (usually Mom) → 解释 the parachute |
> | 定语从句 | who | 修饰 a back-up earner |
> | 条件状语从句 | if | 说明 primary earner 失业或生病的情形 |
> | 介词短语 | into | 与 go 构成搭配，表进入劳动力市场 |
> | 介词短语 | of financial setback | 后置定语，修饰 times |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: they have lost the parachute
>   ├── 结果状语: As a result
>   ├── 主语 S: they
>   ├── 谓语 V: have lost
>   └── 宾语 O: the parachute
>         ├── 定从(省略that): (that) they once had in times of financial setback → 修饰 the parachute
>         │     └── 时间状语: in times of financial setback
>         │           └── 介短(of): of financial setback → 修饰 times
>         └── 同位语(破折号): a back-up earner (usually Mom)
>               └── 定从(who): who could go into the workforce if the primary earner got laid off or fell sick → 修饰 back-up earner
>                     ├── 介短(into): into the workforce → 与 go 搭配
>                     └── 条件状从(if): if the primary earner got laid off or fell sick
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：结果，他们失去了在经济困难时期曾经拥有的保护伞——备用挣钱者（通常是母亲），在主挣钱者失业或生病时可以进入劳动力市场。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 省略关系代词 `that` 的定语从句（the parachute **they once had**），识别省略先行词的定从是高频考点
> 2. 破折号同位语 `a back-up earner` 解释 the parachute，翻译时可顺译为"备用挣钱者"
> 3. `who` 定语从句内套 `if` 条件状语从句，构成定从中含条件状语的多层嵌套结构

This “**added-worker effect**” could support the **safety net** offered by **unemployment insurance** or **disability insurance** to help families **weather** bad times.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：This “**added-worker effect**” could support the **safety net** offered by **unemployment insurance** or **disability insurance** to help families **weather** bad times.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: This "added-worker effect"
> - 谓语 V: could support
> - 宾语 O: the safety net
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（过去分词） | offered | 后置定语，修饰 the safety net |
> | 介词短语 | by unemployment insurance or disability insurance | 施动者，修饰 offered |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to help | 目的状语，修饰 could support |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | weather | 作 help 的宾语补足语（help sb. do 结构） |
> | 并列连词 | or | 并列连接 unemployment insurance 与 disability insurance |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: This "added-worker effect" could support the safety net
>   ├── 主语 S: This "added-worker effect"
>   ├── 谓语 V: could support
>   ├── 宾语 O: the safety net
>   │     └── 过去分词定语(offered): offered by unemployment insurance or disability insurance → 修饰 safety net
>   │           └── 介短(by): by unemployment insurance or disability insurance → 施动者
>   └── 目的状语: to help families weather bad times
>         └── 宾语补足语: weather（help sb. do 结构，weather 作动词"渡过"）
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：这种"附加劳动者效应"本可以支撑起失业保险或伤残保险所提供的安全网，帮助家庭渡过难关。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 过去分词 `offered` 作后置定语，等于定语从句 `that is offered`，识别非谓语替代定语从句
> 2. `help sb. do` 结构中不定式省略 `to`，`weather` 此处为动词，意为"平安渡过"，不可按名词"天气"理解
> 3. `support` 与 `safety net` 的搭配译为"支撑起安全网"

But today, a **disruption** to family fortunes can no longer be **made up** with extra income from an otherwise-stay-at-home partner.

During the same period, families have been asked to **absorb** much more risk in their **retirement income**.

Steelworkers, airline employees, and now those in the auto industry are joining millions of families who must worry about **interest rates**, stock market **fluctuation**, and the harsh reality that they may **outlive** their retirement money.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Steelworkers, airline employees, and now those in the auto industry are joining millions of families who must worry about **interest rates**, stock market **fluctuation**, and the harsh reality that they may **outlive** their retirement money.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Steelworkers, airline employees, and now those in the auto industry（三重并列主语）
> - 谓语 V: are joining
> - 宾语 O: millions of families
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | in the auto industry | 后置定语，修饰 those |
> | 定语从句 | who | 修饰 millions of families |
> | 介词短语 | about | 与 worry 构成搭配，引出担忧对象 |
> | 并列连词 | and | 连接 interest rates / stock market fluctuation / the harsh reality 三个宾语 |
> | 同位语从句 | that | 解释说明 the harsh reality 的内容 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Steelworkers, airline employees, and those in the auto industry are joining millions of families
>   ├── 主语 S（三重并列）: Steelworkers, airline employees, and now those in the auto industry
>   │     └── 介短(in): in the auto industry → 后置定语，修饰 those
>   ├── 谓语 V: are joining
>   ├── 宾语 O: millions of families
>   └── 定从(who): who must worry about interest rates, stock market fluctuation, and the harsh reality → 修饰 families
>         ├── 谓宾: worry about [interest rates / stock market fluctuation / the harsh reality]
>         └── 同位语从句(that): that they may outlive their retirement money → 解释 the harsh reality
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：钢铁工人、航空公司雇员以及现在的汽车行业从业者，正加入到数百万个家庭的行列中，这些家庭不得不为利率、股市波动，以及他们可能比退休金活得久的严酷现实而担忧。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. 三重并列主语 `Steelworkers, airline employees, and those...` 使主语变长，谓语 `are joining` 须与并列主语对齐
> 2. `worry about` 后接三个并列宾语，第三个宾语 `the harsh reality` 后接 `that` 同位语从句
> 3. 区分同位语从句与定语从句：从句成分完整（they may outlive...）则为同位语从句，修饰解释 reality 的内容

For much of the past year, President Bush campaigned to move **Social Security** to a **savings-account model**, with retirees trading much or all of their **guaranteed payments** for payments depending on **investment returns**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：For much of the past year, President Bush campaigned to move **Social Security** to a **savings-account model**, with retirees trading much or all of their **guaranteed payments** for payments depending on **investment returns**.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: President Bush
> - 谓语 V: campaigned
> - 目的状语 A: to move Social Security to a savings-account model（不定式作目的状语）
> - 时间状语 A: For much of the past year
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | For much of the past year | 时间状语，修饰 campaigned |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to move | 目的状语，说明 campaigned 的目的 |
> | 介词短语 | to | 与 move 搭配，表"把……移到……" |
> | with 复合结构 | with | 独立主格，伴随说明改革带来的直接后果 |
> | 非谓语（现在分词） | trading | with 复合结构中作逻辑主语 retirees 的补足语 |
> | 介词短语 | for | 与 trade 搭配（trade A for B），引出换得物 |
> | 非谓语（现在分词） | depending | 后置定语，修饰 payments |
> | 介词短语 | on | 与 depending 构成搭配，引出依赖对象 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: President Bush campaigned to move Social Security to a savings-account model
>   ├── 时间状语: For much of the past year
>   ├── 主语 S: President Bush
>   ├── 谓语 V: campaigned
>   └── 目的状语: to move Social Security to a savings-account model
>         ├── 宾语 O: Social Security
>         └── 介短(to): to a savings-account model → 方向状语
> with 复合结构(独立主格): with retirees trading much or all of their guaranteed payments for payments depending on investment returns
>   ├── 逻辑主语: retirees
>   └── 现在分词(trading): trading much or all of their guaranteed payments for payments
>         ├── 介短(for): for payments → trade A for B，引出换得物
>         └── 现在分词定语(depending): depending on investment returns → 修饰 payments
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在过去一年的大部分时间里，布什总统一直在推动将社会保障制度转变为储蓄账户模式，让退休人员用其大部分或全部有保障的付款，换取依赖投资回报的付款。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `with + 名词 + 现在分词` 构成独立主格结构，作伴随状语，说明主句动作的连带结果
> 2. `trade A for B` 固定搭配"用 A 换取 B"，识别 `for` 的"换取"含义
> 3. 现在分词 `depending` 作后置定语修饰 payments，等于定语从句 `that depend on...`

For younger families, the picture is not any better.

Both the **absolute cost** of **healthcare** and the share of it borne by families have risen—and newly fashionable **health-saving plans** are spreading from legislative halls to Wal-Mart workers, with much higher **deductibles** and a large new dose of **investment risk** for families' future healthcare.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Both the **absolute cost** of **healthcare** and the share of it borne by families have risen—and newly fashionable **health-saving plans** are spreading from legislative halls to Wal-Mart workers, with much higher **deductibles** and a large new dose of **investment risk** for families' future healthcare.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: Both the absolute cost of healthcare and the share of it borne by families（both...and 并列主语）
> - 谓语 V: have risen
> - 并列分句主语 S: newly fashionable health-saving plans
> - 并列分句谓语 V: are spreading
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 并列连词 | Both...and | 连接 the absolute cost 与 the share 两个并列主语 |
> | 介词短语 | of healthcare | 后置定语，修饰 the cost |
> | 非谓语（过去分词） | borne | 后置定语，修饰 the share |
> | 介词短语 | by families | 施动者，修饰 borne |
> | 破折号 + 并列连词 | and | 连接两个并列分句（破折号起强调作用） |
> | 介词短语 | from / to | 说明 spreading 的起止范围 |
> | 介词短语 | with | 伴随状语，补充说明新计划的特点 |
> | 介词短语 | for families' future healthcare | 后置定语，说明风险针对的对象 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 分句1: Both the absolute cost of healthcare and the share of it borne by families have risen
>   ├── 主语 S（both...and 并列）:
>   │     ├── 主语1: the absolute cost of healthcare
>   │     │     └── 介短(of): of healthcare → 修饰 cost
>   │     └── 主语2: the share of it borne by families
>   │           ├── 介短(of): of it → 修饰 the share
>   │           └── 过去分词定语(borne): borne by families → 修饰 the share
>   └── 谓语 V: have risen
> 分句2(破折号+and): and newly fashionable health-saving plans are spreading from legislative halls to Wal-Mart workers
>   ├── 主语 S: newly fashionable health-saving plans
>   ├── 谓语 V: are spreading
>   ├── 介短(from): from legislative halls → 起点
>   └── 介短(to): to Wal-Mart workers → 终点
>         └── with 伴随状语: with much higher deductibles and a large new dose of investment risk for families' future healthcare
>               └── 介短(for): for families' future healthcare → 对象
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：医疗保健的绝对成本以及由家庭承担的部分都在上升——新兴的健康储蓄计划正从立法机构蔓延到沃尔玛的员工，其免赔额要高得多，并给家庭的未来医疗保健带来了大量新的投资风险。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `both...and` 并列主语，谓语用复数 `have risen`，判断并列主语的数一致性
> 2. 过去分词 `borne`（bear 的过去分词）作后置定语修饰 the share，等于 the share **that is borne** by families
> 3. 破折号 + `and` 连接第二个并列分句，`with` 介词短语作伴随状语补充说明（Q33 考点：health-saving plans 增加家庭投资风险）

Even **demographics** are working against the middle class family, as the **odds** of having a weak **elderly parent**—and all the attendant need for physical and financial assistance—have jumped **eightfold** in just one generation.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Even **demographics** are working against the middle class family, as the **odds** of having a weak **elderly parent**—and all the attendant need for physical and financial assistance—have jumped **eightfold** in just one generation.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: demographics
> - 谓语 V: are working against
> - 宾语 O: the middle class family
> - 原因状语从句 A: as the odds of having a weak elderly parent have jumped eightfold in just one generation
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句 | as | 原因状语，说明"人口结构不利"的原因 |
> | 介词短语 | of having a weak elderly parent | 后置定语，修饰 the odds |
> | 插入语 | 破折号 | all the attendant need for physical and financial assistance → 补充说明 |
> | 介词短语 | for physical and financial assistance | 后置定语，修饰 the need |
> | 介词短语 | in just one generation | 时间状语，修饰 have jumped |
> | 副词 | eightfold | 程度状语，修饰 have jumped |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Even demographics are working against the middle class family
>   ├── 主语 S: demographics
>   ├── 谓语 V: are working against
>   ├── 宾语 O: the middle class family
>   └── 原因状从(as): as the odds of having a weak elderly parent have jumped eightfold in just one generation
>         ├── 主语 S: the odds of having a weak elderly parent
>         │     └── 介短(of): of having a weak elderly parent → 修饰 the odds
>         ├── 插入语(破折号): —and all the attendant need for physical and financial assistance— → 补充说明
>         ├── 谓语 V: have jumped
>         └── 状语: eightfold / in just one generation
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：甚至连人口结构也对中产阶级家庭不利，因为家中出现一位体弱年迈的父母的几率——以及随之而来的对身体和财务援助的需求——在一代人的时间里就跃升了八倍。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `as` 引导原因状语从句，主句为 "demographics are working against the middle class family"
> 2. 破折号插入语将从句主语（the odds...）与谓语（have jumped）分隔，阅读时先跳过插入语还原主谓
> 3. `odds` 作名词表"几率、可能性"；`eightfold` 的 `-fold` 后缀表"……倍"，与 `-fold` 变化为高频构词考点

From the middle-class family **perspective**, much of this, understandably, looks far less like an opportunity to **exercise** more **financial responsibility**, and a good deal more like a frightening **acceleration** of the **wholesale shift** of financial risk onto their already **overburdened** shoulders.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：From the middle-class family **perspective**, much of this, understandably, looks far less like an opportunity to **exercise** more **financial responsibility**, and a good deal more like a frightening **acceleration** of the **wholesale shift** of financial risk onto their already **overburdened** shoulders.
>
> **主干提取**：
> - 主语 S: much of this
> - 谓语 V: looks
> - 表语 C: far less like an opportunity ... and a good deal more like a frightening acceleration ...（并列表语）
> - 状语 A: From the middle-class family perspective（观点状语）
> - 插入语 A: understandably（评注性状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | From the middle-class family perspective | 观点/视角状语，修饰 looks |
> | 插入语 | understandably | 评注性状语，插入主谓之间 |
> | 介词短语 | of this | 后置定语，修饰 much |
> | 介词短语 | like | 与 looks 构成搭配（look like） |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to exercise | 后置定语，修饰 an opportunity |
> | 并列连词 | and | 连接 far less like... 与 a good deal more like... 两个比较结构 |
> | 介词短语 | of | 后置定语，修饰 acceleration / shift |
> | 介词短语 | onto | 说明 shift 的方向（转移到……上） |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: much of this looks far less like an opportunity ... and a good deal more like a frightening acceleration ...
>   ├── 观点状语: From the middle-class family perspective
>   ├── 主语 S: much of this
>   │     └── 介短(of): of this → 修饰 much
>   ├── 插入语: understandably
>   ├── 谓语 V: looks
>   ├── 表语1: far less like an opportunity to exercise more financial responsibility
>   │     └── 不定式定语: to exercise more financial responsibility → 修饰 opportunity
>   ├── 并列连词: and
>   └── 表语2: a good deal more like a frightening acceleration of the wholesale shift of financial risk onto their already overburdened shoulders
>         └── 介短链: of the wholesale shift of financial risk onto their shoulders
>               └── 介短(onto): onto their already overburdened shoulders → 方向状语
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：从中产阶级家庭的角度看，可以理解的是，这一切与其说是一个行使更多财务责任的机会，不如说更像一种可怕的加速——财务风险被大规模地转移到了他们已经不堪重负的肩膀上。
>
> **考点提示**：
> 1. `look far less like A and a good deal more like B` 比较结构，译为"与其说是 A，不如说是 B"
> 2. 介词短语链条 `of the wholesale shift of financial risk onto...` 层层后置修饰，翻译时拆为短句
> 3. `understandably` 为评注性状语插入主谓之间，翻译时置于句首作"可以理解的是"
