# Will the European Union make it?

The question would have sounded strange not long ago.

Now even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk of a continent facing a **“Bermuda triangle”** of debt, population decline and lower growth.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Now even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk of a continent facing a “Bermuda triangle” of debt, population decline and lower growth.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: even the project's greatest cheerleaders
> - 谓语 V: talk
> - 介词结构：talk of + 介词宾语，表示“谈论……”
> - 介词宾语 O（of 的宾语）: a continent facing a “Bermuda triangle”
> - 状语 A: Now
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | even | 强调副词 | even | the project's greatest cheerleaders，突出“连……都” |
> | of a continent... | 介词短语 | of | talk，构成 talk of，表示“谈论……” |
> | facing a “Bermuda triangle”... | 非谓语（现在分词短语） | facing | a continent，表示“欧洲大陆正面临” |
> | of debt, population decline and lower growth | 介词短语 | of | a “Bermuda triangle”，具体说明“三角困境”由什么构成 |
> | debt, population decline and lower growth | 并列名词短语 | and | of 的三个并列宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 状语: Now
> 主句: even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk
>   └── 介词结构: of a continent → 构成 talk of
>         └── 介词宾语: a continent
>               └── 非谓语: facing a “Bermuda triangle” → 修饰 a continent
>                     └── 介短: of debt, population decline and lower growth
>                           → 具体说明 “Bermuda triangle” 的三个组成部分
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如今，甚至连这个项目最热心的支持者也开始谈论：欧洲大陆正面临一个由债务、人口下降和经济增长放缓构成的“百慕大三角”。
>
> **考点提示**：`talk of` 是“谈论、提及”的固定搭配；`facing` 是现在分词短语作后置定语，逻辑主语是 `a continent`，不是主句主语 `cheerleaders`；`even` 常译为“甚至、连……都”，用于突出出乎意料的对象。

As well as those **chronic problems**, the EU faces an **acute crisis** in its economic core, the 16 countries that use the single currency.

Markets have lost faith that the euro zone's economies, weaker or stronger, will one day **converge** thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of **devaluation**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Markets have lost faith that the euro zone's economies, weaker or stronger, will one day converge thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of devaluation.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Markets
> - 谓语 V: have lost
> - 宾语 O: faith
> - 宾语从句内容: that the euro zone's economies will one day converge
> - 状语 A: thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | that the euro zone's economies...converge | 名词性从句（同位语从句） | that | faith，说明“信念”的具体内容 |
> | weaker or stronger | 插入语（形容词短语） | — | economies，表示“无论强弱” |
> | thanks to the discipline...currency | 介词短语 | thanks to | converge，表示原因或依据 |
> | sharing a single currency | 非谓语（动名词） | sharing | discipline，说明这种约束来自何处 |
> | which denies...devaluation | 定语从句 | which | single currency，说明单一货币带来的限制 |
> | of devaluation | 介词短语 | of | the quick fix，说明“快捷办法”具体是什么 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Markets have lost faith
>   └── 名词性从句: that the euro zone's economies will one day converge
>         ├── 插入语: weaker or stronger → 修饰 economies
>         ├── 状语: thanks to the discipline
>         │     └── 非谓语: sharing a single currency → 修饰 discipline
>         └── 定语从句: which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix
>               └── 介短: of devaluation → 修饰 the quick fix
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：市场已经不再相信，欧元区的经济体无论强弱，终有一天能够凭借共同使用单一货币所带来的约束而趋于一致；而单一货币使缺乏竞争力的成员无法通过货币贬值这一快捷方式来解决问题。
>
> **考点提示**：`faith that...` 中的 that 从句是名词性同位语从句，具体说明 faith 的内容；`weaker or stronger` 是插入成分，阅读时可先跳过；末尾 `which` 引导非限制性定语从句，指向 `single currency`。

Yet the debate about how to save Europe's single currency from **disintegration** is **stuck**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Yet the debate about how to save Europe's single currency from disintegration is stuck.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: the debate
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: stuck
> - 定语：about how to save Europe's single currency from disintegration（修饰 the debate）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | about how to save...disintegration | 介词短语 | about | the debate |
> | how to save...disintegration | “疑问词 + 不定式”结构 | how + to | about 的宾语，说明讨论的具体内容 |
> | Europe's single currency | 介词短语中的名词短语 | Europe's | save 的宾语 |
> | from disintegration | 介词短语 | from | save，表示避免的结果 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the debate is stuck
>   └── 介短: about how to save Europe's single currency from disintegration
>         └── 疑问词 + 不定式: how to save...
>               ├── 宾语: Europe's single currency
>               └── 介短: from disintegration → 表示避免的结果
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：然而，关于如何拯救欧洲单一货币、避免其解体的讨论陷入了僵局。
>
> **考点提示**：`how to save...` 是“疑问词 + 不定式”结构，整体作介词 `about` 的宾语；主干是 `the debate is stuck`，其中 `stuck` 是形容词补语，表示“陷入僵局”。

It is stuck because the euro zone's dominant powers, France and Germany, agree on the need for greater **harmonisation** within the euro zone, but disagree about what to harmonise.

Germany thinks the euro must be saved by **stricter rules** on borrowing, spending and competitiveness, **backed** by quasi-automatic sanctions for governments that do not obey.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Germany thinks the euro must be saved by stricter rules on borrowing, spending and competitiveness, backed by quasi-automatic sanctions for governments that do not obey.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Germany
> - 谓语 V: thinks
> - 宾语 O: the euro must be saved by stricter rules
> - 被动结构内部主语 S: the euro
> - 被动结构谓语 V: must be saved
> - 状语 A: by stricter rules on borrowing, spending and competitiveness
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | the euro must be saved... | 名词性从句（宾语从句） | 省略 that | thinks 的宾语 |
> | must be saved | 情态动词 + 被动语态 | must + be + V-ed | euro，表示“必须被拯救” |
> | on borrowing, spending and competitiveness | 介词短语 | on | rules，说明规则涉及的领域 |
> | backed by quasi-automatic sanctions... | 过去分词短语 | backed | stricter rules，表示规则由制裁作保障 |
> | for governments that do not obey | 介词短语 + 定语从句 | for / that | sanctions / governments |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Germany thinks [宾语从句]
>   └── 宾语从句: the euro must be saved by stricter rules
>         ├── 被动谓语: must be saved
>         ├── 介短: on borrowing, spending and competitiveness → 修饰 rules
>         └── 过去分词短语: backed by quasi-automatic sanctions
>               └── 定语从句: that do not obey → 修饰 governments
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：德国认为，必须通过对借贷、支出和竞争力实行更严格的规则来拯救欧元，并以近乎自动的制裁作为保障，惩罚那些不遵守规则的政府。
>
> **考点提示**：`thinks` 后接省略 `that` 的宾语从句；`must be saved` 是情态动词与被动语态叠加；句末 `backed...` 为过去分词短语，逻辑上修饰 `stricter rules`，不要误判为修饰最近的 `competitiveness`。

These might include threats to freeze EU funds for poorer regions and EU mega-projects, and even the suspension of a country's voting rights in EU ministerial councils. It insists that economic co-ordination should involve all 27 members of the EU club, among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism and economic rigour; in the inner core alone, Germany fears, a small majority favour French interference.

A **“southern” camp** headed by French wants something different: **“European economic government”** within an inner core of euro-zone members.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：A “southern” camp headed by French wants something different: “European economic government” within an inner core of euro-zone members.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: A “southern” camp
> - 谓语 V: wants
> - 宾语 O: something different
> - 后置说明: “European economic government” within an inner core of euro-zone members
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | headed by French | 过去分词短语 | headed | camp，说明阵营的领导者 |
> | “European economic government” | 同位语/解释性补语 | 冒号 | something different，具体说明“不同的方案” |
> | within an inner core of euro-zone members | 介词短语 | within | European economic government，说明实施范围 |
> | of euro-zone members | 介词短语 | of | inner core，说明核心成员构成 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: A “southern” camp wants something different
>   ├── 过去分词短语: headed by French → 修饰 camp
>   └── 冒号后的同位说明: “European economic government”
>         └── 介短: within an inner core of euro-zone members → 修饰 government
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：由法国领导的“南方阵营”则想要一种不同的方案：在欧元区成员组成的内部核心中建立“欧洲经济政府”。
>
> **考点提示**：`headed by French` 是过去分词短语作后置定语；冒号后并非新的完整句子，而是对 `something different` 的具体解释。原文中的 `French` 在语境中指法国方面或法国政府。

Translated, that means politicians intervening in monetary policy and a system of **redistribution** from richer to poorer members, via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds or complete fiscal transfers. Finally, figures close to the French government have murmured, euro-zone members should agree to some fiscal and social harmonisation: e.g., curbing competition in corporate-tax rates or labour costs.

It is too soon to write off the EU. It remains the world's largest trading block. At its best, the European project is remarkably liberal: built around a single market of 27 rich and poor countries, its internal borders are far more open to goods, capital and labour than any comparable trading area.

It is an ambitious attempt to **blunt the sharpest edges of globalization**, and make capitalism benign.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It is an ambitious attempt to blunt the sharpest edges of globalization, and make capitalism benign.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: It（指代 the European project）
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: an ambitious attempt
> - 后置定语：to blunt the sharpest edges of globalization and make capitalism benign（修饰 attempt，说明尝试的内容）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 成分 | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|------|--------|----------|
> | to blunt...and make... | 非谓语（并列不定式） | to | attempt，说明尝试的目的或内容 |
> | of globalization | 介词短语 | of | edges，说明“棱角”属于全球化 |
> | capitalism benign | 省略 to 的不定式复合结构 | make + 宾语 + 补语 | make 的宾语和宾语补足语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: It is an ambitious attempt
>   └── 并列不定式: to blunt ... and make ...
>         ├── to blunt the sharpest edges of globalization
>         │     └── 介短: of globalization → 修饰 edges
>         └── (to) make capitalism benign
>               ├── 宾语 O: capitalism
>               └── 宾补 C: benign
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：这是一次雄心勃勃的尝试，旨在削弱全球化最尖锐的棱角，让资本主义变得更加温和。
>
> **考点提示**：`to blunt... and make...` 是两个并列的不定式，共用第一个 `to`；`make capitalism benign` 是典型的“make + 宾语 + 宾语补足语”结构，`benign` 不能译成“良性的”而应结合语境译为“温和的、对人有益的”。
