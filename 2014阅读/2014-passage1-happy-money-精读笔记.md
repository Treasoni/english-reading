# 2014 Passage 1：怎样花钱更快乐 · 精读笔记

## 背景

一篇《Happy Money》书评。由彩票中奖者如何花钱切入，说明体验性消费、为他人花钱和适度稀缺为什么可能带来更持久的满足；最后区分书的实用价值与是否同意作者的公共政策建议。难点在于辨认例子服务的观点，以及作者对金钱与幸福的限定。

---

## 文章原文

What would you do with $590m? This is now a question for Gloria MacKenzie, an 84-year-old widow who recently emerged from her small, tin-roofed house in Florida to collect the biggest **undivided lottery jackpot** in history.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · A1
> **原句**：This is now a question for Gloria MacKenzie, an 84-year-old widow who recently emerged from her small, tin-roofed house in Florida to collect the biggest undivided lottery jackpot in history.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: This
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: a question for Gloria MacKenzie
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | now | 时间 |
> | an 84-year-old widow … | 人名的同位语 |
> | who recently emerged … | 修饰 widow，who 作主语 |
> | from her small, tin-roofed house in Florida | came out 的起点，in Florida 修饰 house |
> | to collect … | 出来的目的 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> This is now a question for Gloria MacKenzie
> └── 同位：an 84-year-old widow
>     └── who recently emerged
>         ├── from her … house in Florida
>         └── to collect the biggest undivided lottery jackpot in history
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如今，这个问题落到了格洛丽亚·麦肯齐头上。这位84岁的遗孀不久前走出她在佛罗里达州那栋铁皮屋顶的小房子，去领取史上由一人独得的最高额彩票头奖。
>
> **考点提示**：`This` 回指上句“有5.9亿美元你会怎么花”；`who` 所在从句补充人物身份，不是主句的另一个谓语。

If she hopes her **new-found fortune** will yield lasting feelings of fulfilment, she could do worse than read Happy Money by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · A2
> **原句**：If she hopes her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfilment, she could do worse than read Happy Money by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: she
> - 谓语 V: could do
> - 比较状语 A: worse than read Happy Money by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | If she hopes … | 开放条件 |
> | her new-found fortune will yield … | hopes 的宾语从句，省略 that |
> | lasting | 修饰 feelings |
> | of fulfilment | 说明是哪种感受 |
> | by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton | 标明作者 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> If she hopes
> └── (that) her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfilment
> 主句：she could do worse
> └── than read Happy Money by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如果她希望新到手的这笔财富带来持久的满足感，不妨读读伊丽莎白·邓恩和迈克尔·诺顿的《快乐金钱》。
>
> **考点提示**：`could do worse than do` 不是“这样做会更糟”，而是“还有比这更差的选择”，因此是委婉推荐。

These two academics use an array of behavioral research to show that the most rewarding ways to spend money can be **counterintuitive**. Fantasies of great wealth often involve visions of fancy cars and **extravagant** homes. Yet satisfaction with these material purchases **wears off** fairly quickly. What was once exciting and new becomes **old-hat**; **regret creeps in**. It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · A3
> **原句**：It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: It（形式主语）
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: far better
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | to spend money on experiences | 后置的不定式主语 |
> | far | 加强比较级 better |
> | say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton | 插入报道语，倒装 |
> | like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema | 列举 experiences |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> It is far better
> ├── 后置主语：to spend money on experiences
> │   └── like [interesting trips / unique meals / even going to the cinema]
> └── 插入归属：say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：邓恩和诺顿认为，把钱花在体验上要好得多，比如来一趟有趣的旅行、吃一顿别具一格的饭，甚至去看一场电影。
>
> **考点提示**：Q21 抓住 `experiences` 与上文 `material purchases` 的对比。`like` 接回 `experiences`，不是 `say` 的宾语。

These purchases often become more valuable with time—as stories or memories—particularly if they involve feeling more connected to others.

This slim volume is packed with tips to help **wage slaves** as well as lottery winners get the most “happiness **bang for your buck**.” It seems most people would be **better off** if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television (something the average American spends **a whopping** two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it).

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · A4
> **原句**：It seems most people would be better off if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television (something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it).
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: It（虚指）
> - 谓语 V: seems
> - 补足语 C: (that) most people would be better off …（后置内容从句）
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | if they could … | 限定 better off 的条件 |
> | shorten …, spend … | 共用 could |
> | and less of it watching television | 补出 spend；it 指 time |
> | something … doing | 回指看电视；doing 后有关系词留下的宾语空位 |
> | and is hardly jollier for it | 主语仍是 the average American；it 指看电视 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> It seems
> └── (that) most people would be better off
>     └── if they could
>         ├── shorten their commutes to work
>         └── spend
>             ├── more time with friends and family
>             └── less of it watching television
>                 └── 括号：something [(that) the average American spends … doing]
>                     └── 补充：and (the average American) is hardly jollier for it
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：大多数人如果能缩短上班通勤时间，多陪陪亲友，少看些电视，日子似乎就会过得更好（美国人平均每年竟花两个月看电视，却并没因此高兴多少）。
>
> **考点提示**：Q22 的态度线索是 `whopping` 和 `hardly jollier`。两个有指代的 `it` 分别指时间和看电视，不能都译成“它”。

Buying gifts or giving to charity is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself, and luxuries are most enjoyable when they are consumed **sparingly**. This is apparently the reason McDonald’s restricts the availability of its popular McRib—a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · A5
> **原句**：This is apparently the reason McDonald’s restricts the availability of its popular McRib—a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: This
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: the reason McDonald’s restricts the availability of its popular McRib
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | apparently | 表推测 |
> | McDonald’s restricts … | 修饰 reason，可补 why |
> | of its popular McRib | 说明限制的是何物的供应 |
> | a marketing trick … | 概括前面限量供应的做法 |
> | that has turned … into … | 修饰 trick |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> This is apparently the reason
> └── (why) McDonald’s restricts the availability of its popular McRib
>     └── 破折号补说做法：a marketing trick
>         └── that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：这大概就是麦当劳限制供应其热门产品麦香排骨堡的原因——这一营销手法让人们对这款猪肉三明治着了迷。
>
> **考点提示**：Q23 先追 `This`：它指上一句“奢侈享受偶尔为之最有滋味”，McRib 用来说明稀缺会增加享受，不只是为了证明营销有效。

Readers of Happy Money are clearly a privileged lot, anxious about fulfilment, not hunger. Money may not quite buy happiness, but people in wealthier countries are generally happier than those in poor ones. Yet the link between feeling good and spending money on others can be seen among rich and poor people around the world, and **scarcity** enhances the pleasure of most things for most people. Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas, which range from mandating more holiday time to reducing **tax incentives** for American homebuyers.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · A6
> **原句**：Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas, which range from mandating more holiday time to reducing tax incentives for American homebuyers.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Not everyone
> - 谓语 V: will agree
> - 补足语 C: with the authors' policy ideas
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | with the authors' policy ideas | 同意的对象 |
> | which range … | 非限制性定语从句，指 policy ideas |
> | from mandating … to reducing … | 政策范围；from 和 to 都是介词 |
> | for American homebuyers | 修饰 tax incentives |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas
> └── which range
>     ├── from mandating more holiday time
>     └── to reducing tax incentives for American homebuyers
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：并非所有人都会赞同两位作者的政策主张，这些主张从强制增加休假时间到减少给美国购房者的税收优惠不等。
>
> **考点提示**：`not everyone` ≠ `no one`。这一让步只涉及政策建议，不等于作者否定书的价值；Q24 还需看下一句 `But … money well spent`。

But most people will **come away** from this book believing it was money well spent.

## 翻译对照

### 第 1 段

**English**

What would you do with $590m? This is now a question for Gloria MacKenzie, an 84-year-old widow who recently emerged from her small, tin-roofed house in Florida to collect the biggest undivided lottery jackpot in history. If she hopes her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfilment, she could do worse than read Happy Money by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton.

**中文**

如果有 5.9 亿美元，你会怎么花？如今，84 岁的遗孀格洛丽亚·麦肯齐就要面对这个问题。不久前，她走出自己在佛罗里达州那栋铁皮屋顶的小房子，去领彩票史上由一人独得的最高额头奖。如果她希望这笔新到手的财富能带来持久的满足感，不妨读读伊丽莎白·邓恩和迈克尔·诺顿合著的《快乐金钱》。

> [!note] 翻译提示
> `could do worse than do sth` 是委婉的推荐，意思是「做某事不失为一个好办法」，不是说做这件事会更糟。

### 第 2 段

**English**

These two academics use an array of behavioral research to show that the most rewarding ways to spend money can be counterintuitive. Fantasies of great wealth often involve visions of fancy cars and extravagant homes. Yet satisfaction with these material purchases wears off fairly quickly. What was once exciting and new becomes old-hat; regret creeps in. It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema. These purchases often become more valuable with time—as stories or memories—particularly if they involve feeling more connected to others.

**中文**

这两位学者援引一系列行为研究来说明，最能带来满足感的花钱方式，未必符合直觉。人们幻想自己拥有巨额财富时，脑海里往往会浮现豪车和豪宅。不过，购买这些物质商品带来的满足感很快就会消退。原本令人兴奋、倍感新鲜的东西渐渐失去新意，后悔也悄然而至。邓恩和诺顿认为，把钱花在体验上要好得多：比如来一趟有趣的旅行、吃一顿别具一格的饭，甚至去看场电影。随着时间流逝，这些消费留下的故事或回忆往往越发珍贵；如果这些体验还让人觉得与他人更亲近，就更是如此。

### 第 3 段

**English**

This slim volume is packed with tips to help wage slaves as well as lottery winners get the most “happiness bang for your buck.” It seems most people would be better off if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television (something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it). Buying gifts or giving to charity is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself, and luxuries are most enjoyable when they are consumed sparingly. This is apparently the reason McDonald’s restricts the availability of its popular McRib—a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession.

**中文**

这本薄薄的小书给出了许多建议，帮普通工薪族和彩票中奖者把每一块钱都花出尽可能多的快乐。对多数人来说，如果能缩短上班通勤的时间，多陪陪亲友，少看些电视，日子似乎会过得更好（美国人平均每年看电视的时间竟长达两个月，却并没因此开心多少）。给人买礼物或向慈善机构捐款，往往比给自己买东西更让人快乐；奢侈的享受则是偶尔为之最有滋味。这大概就是麦当劳限制供应其畅销产品麦香排骨堡（McRib）的原因——这一营销手法让人们对这款猪肉三明治着了迷。

> [!note] 翻译提示
> `bang for your buck` 本指「花同样的钱换来多大的回报」。这里加上 `happiness`，把衡量回报的标准换成了快乐。

### 第 4 段

**English**

Readers of Happy Money are clearly a privileged lot, anxious about fulfilment, not hunger. Money may not quite buy happiness, but people in wealthier countries are generally happier than those in poor ones. Yet the link between feeling good and spending money on others can be seen among rich and poor people around the world, and scarcity enhances the pleasure of most things for most people. Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas, which range from mandating more holiday time to reducing tax incentives for American homebuyers. But most people will come away from this book believing it was money well spent.

**中文**

《快乐金钱》的读者显然是一群生活条件优越的人：他们发愁的是如何获得满足感，而不是如何填饱肚子。金钱未必真能买到幸福，但较富裕国家的人通常比贫穷国家的人更幸福。不过，在世界各地，无论贫富，都能看到为别人花钱与心情愉快之间的联系；对大多数人来说，大多数东西越难得，享用时就越觉得愉快。两位作者提出的政策主张，从强制增加休假时间到减少给美国购房者的税收优惠，并不是每个人都会赞同。不过，大多数人读完这本书，应该都会觉得买书的钱花得值。

- [B] A special tour.
- [C] A stylish car.
- [D] A rich meal.

### 22. The author's attitude toward Americans' watching TV is
- [A] critical
- [B] supportive
- [C] sympathetic
- [D] ambiguous

### 23. McRib is mentioned in Paragraph 3 to show that ____.
- [A] consumers are sometimes irrational
- [B] popularity usually comes after quality
- [C] marketing tricks are often effective
- [D] rarity generally increases pleasure

### 24. According to the last paragraph, Happy Money ____.
- [A] has left much room for readers' criticism
- [B] may prove to be a worthwhile purchase
- [C] has predicted a wider income gap in the US
- [D] may give its readers a sense of achievement

### 25. This text mainly discusses how to ____.
- [A] balance feeling good and spending money
- [B] spend large sums of money won in lotteries
- [C] obtain lasting satisfaction from money spent
- [D] become more reasonable in spending on luxuries

### 参考答案与定位

> [!note]- 展开核对（根据原文推断，源文件未附答案键）
> - **21. B**：第 2 段把体验性消费与物质购买对照；特殊旅行是体验的典型例子。
> - **22. A**：`a whopping two months` 和 `hardly jollier for it` 表明作者对大量看电视持批评态度。
> - **23. D**：举 McRib 对应前一句 `luxuries are most enjoyable when … consumed sparingly`，说明稀缺增添享受。
> - **24. B**：末句 `money well spent` 是对买这本书的肯定，并不表示所有读者都会赞成政策建议。
> - **25. C**：首段的 `lasting feelings of fulfilment` 与后文的消费建议共同指向如何从花费中获得持久满足。

## 语法要点

> [!note] 来源说明
> 考点从本文原文提炼。例项引用原文片段；习惯表达另见 [[../intermediate/2014-passage1-happy-money/固定搭配与词组笔记|本篇固定搭配]]。

### 从句：名词性从句

| 结构 | 用法 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `what` 融合关系结构 | `What was once exciting and new` 整体充当 `becomes` 的主语；`what` 在内部也作主语，相当于“原本令人兴奋又新鲜的东西”，不是问句。（第 2 段） | `What was once exciting and new becomes old-hat` |
| 动词 + `that` 内容从句 | `that` 只标记从句边界，不充当内部成分。`to show` 表研究的用途，`that` 后才是研究结论。（第 2 段） | `to show that the most rewarding ways to spend money can be counterintuitive` |
| 内容从句的 `that` 省略 | `hopes` 后接完整命题；`believing` 后同样如此。不能把 `her new-found fortune` 单独当成 `hopes` 的宾语，也别把 `it` 单独当成 `believing` 的宾语。（第 1、4 段） | `she hopes her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfilment`；`believing it was money well spent` |
| `It seems (that) ...` | `it` 不回指前面的书，`seems` 给后面的整个判断加上“似乎”的语气；从句内部另有 `if` 条件。与 [[2014-passage1-happy-money-精读笔记#非谓语动词：不定式]] 中 `It is far better to ...` 的形式主语结构区分。（第 3 段） | `It seems most people would be better off if they could shorten their commutes to work` |

### 从句：定语从句

| 结构 | 用法 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 名词 + `who` 从句 | `who` 作从句主语，直接修饰 `widow`；整个 `an 84-year-old widow ...` 是人名后的同位语。见 [[2014-passage1-happy-money-精读笔记#补充要点：同位语与插入语]]。（第 1 段） | `an 84-year-old widow who recently emerged from her small, tin-roofed house in Florida` |
| `something` + 零关系词从句 | `something` 承接“看电视”这件事；在其后的关系结构中，对应 `doing` 后的宾语空位。`and is hardly jollier for it` 补说同一群体并没有因此更开心；不是 `something` 感到开心。（第 3 段） | `something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it` |
| `the reason` + 零关系词从句 | `reason` 后直接跟句子，可显化为 `the reason why ...`；`McDonald’s` 是从句主语，不是 `reason` 的同位语。（第 3 段） | `the reason McDonald’s restricts the availability of its popular McRib` |
| `that` 限制性 / `, which` 非限制性 | `that` 指回 `trick`，并作 `has turned` 的主语；逗号后的 `which` 指回 `policy ideas`，补充政策范围。与 [[2014-passage1-happy-money-精读笔记#从句：名词性从句]] 中不充当成分的 `that` 对比。（第 3、4 段） | `a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession`；`the authors' policy ideas, which range from mandating more holiday time to reducing tax incentives for American homebuyers` |

### 从句：条件与时间状语从句

| 结构 | 用法 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `if` + 一般现在时；主句有 `could` | `hopes` 表开放条件，`will yield` 嵌在 `hopes` 的内容从句里，并不是 `if` 从句的谓语；主句的 `could` 参与委婉建议，不表示条件不可能实现。（第 1 段） | `If she hopes her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfilment, she could do worse than read Happy Money by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton.` |
| 主句 `would` + `if ... could ...` | 设想某种情形下可能出现的改善，不承诺现状已改善。`could` 统领 `shorten` 和 `spend`，后一个 `spend` 在 `less of it watching television` 前省略。（第 3 段） | `most people would be better off if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television` |
| `when` + 一般现在时 | 说明最能享受奢侈品的是哪种情形，`they` 指 `luxuries`。不是一次性发生的过去事件。（第 3 段） | `luxuries are most enjoyable when they are consumed sparingly` |

### 非谓语动词：不定式

| 结构 | 用法 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 动作 + `to do` / 名词 + `to do` | `to collect` 说明从家里出来做什么，表目的；`to spend` 修饰 `ways`，说明什么方式。先看不定式附着在动词还是名词上。（第 1、2 段） | `in Florida to collect the biggest undivided lottery jackpot in history`；`the most rewarding ways to spend money` |
| `It is + 形容词比较级 + to do` | `it` 是形式主语，`to spend money on experiences` 才是被评价的行为；`far` 加强 `better` 的程度。插入语不改变主干。（第 2 段） | `It is far better to spend money on experiences` |
| `tips to help ... get ...` | `to help` 说明建议的目的；`help` 后接宾语 `wage slaves as well as lottery winners`，再接省略 `to` 的不定式 `get`，不是两个独立谓语。（第 3 段） | `tips to help wage slaves as well as lottery winners get the most “happiness bang for your buck.”` |

### 非谓语动词：动名词与分词

| 结构 | 用法 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `-ing` 作主语或比较对象 | `Buying gifts` 和 `giving to charity` 由 `or` 连接，共用谓语 `is`；比较的是为人赠礼／捐款与为自己购物这两类行为，不是礼物和人相比。（第 3 段） | `Buying gifts or giving to charity is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself` |
| 介词 + `-ing` | `between` 后并列两项；`range from A to B` 中 `from`、`to` 都是介词，所以用 `mandating`、`reducing`，不能把 `to reducing` 读作不定式。（第 4 段） | `between feeling good and spending money on others`；`from mandating more holiday time to reducing tax incentives for American homebuyers` |
| 现在分词短语作补充说明 | `believing ...` 的逻辑主语是 `most people`，说明读者读完后的看法；内容从句见 [[2014-passage1-happy-money-精读笔记#从句：名词性从句]]。（第 4 段） | `most people will come away from this book believing it was money well spent` |
| 过去分词短语作后置定语 | `well spent` 修饰 `money`，可理解为 `money that was well spent`；句中的 `was` 本来连接 `it` 和名词短语 `money well spent`，不能直接把整个从句读成单一的被动谓语。（第 4 段） | `it was money well spent` |

### 语态：被动结构

| 结构 | 用法 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `be + 过去分词`；`情态动词 + be + 过去分词` | `are consumed` 的主语是被享用的 `luxuries`，`can be seen` 的主语是可以观察到的 `the link`。两处都没有写执行者；先还原谁是动作的承受者。（第 3、4 段） | `when they are consumed sparingly`；`can be seen among rich and poor people around the world` |

### 比较结构：比较基准与替代省略

| 结构 | 用法 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `happier than those in poor ones` | `those` 替代 `people`，`ones` 替代 `countries`；比较的是富裕国家与贫穷国家的**人**，不是人和国家相比。（第 4 段） | `people in wealthier countries are generally happier than those in poor ones` |
| `more ... and less of it ...` | 第二个 `spend` 省略；`it` 指 `time`，`less` 对应时间长短；`watching television` 说明时间花在做什么。联读 [[2014-passage1-happy-money-精读笔记#从句：条件与时间状语从句]]。（第 3 段） | `spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television` |

### 否定：范围与程度

| 结构 | 用法 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `not everyone` | 部分否定，意为“并非人人赞同”，不等于 `no one`。后接的 `which` 从句提供政策例子，不改变否定范围。（第 4 段） | `Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas` |
| `may not quite` / `hardly` | `may not quite` 保留可能性、缓和否定，不能读成 `can never`；`hardly` 意为“几乎不”，在文中否定看电视带来的快乐增量，而非否定有看电视这一行为。（第 3、4 段） | `is hardly jollier for it`；`Money may not quite buy happiness` |

### 补充要点：同位语与插入语

| 结构 | 用法 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 插入报道语 | 用逗号隔开的 `say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton` 说明判断出自谁；略去后可直接把 `like` 后的三项接回 `experiences`，不要误将它们看成 `say` 的宾语。（第 2 段） | `on experiences, say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema` |
| 破折号 + 同位说明 | `a marketing trick` 概括前面“限制供应”的做法，而不是给产品 McRib 改名；其后的 `that` 再说明做法的效果。联读 [[2014-passage1-happy-money-精读笔记#从句：定语从句]]。（第 3 段） | `its popular McRib—a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession` |

## 固定搭配与词组

> [!note] 用法
> 下表为原文常用搭配与地道表达，按语义聚合；更多结构见 [[../intermediate/2014-passage1-happy-money/grammar-notes|本篇语法整理]]。

### 固定搭配：财富、消费与回报

| 搭配 | 语境含义与辨析 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `collect a lottery jackpot` | 领取彩票头奖，不是参加或赢得抽奖这一动作；`undivided` 表示奖金没有与别人平分。 | `collect the biggest undivided lottery jackpot in history` |
| `yield lasting feelings of fulfilment` | 带来持久的满足感；`yield` 在此指产生结果，不是“让步”。`fulfilment` 是获得充实、满足的感受，不等于一时兴奋。 | `yield lasting feelings of fulfilment` |
| `bang for your buck` | 口语，指花的钱所换来的回报；`buck` 口语指一美元，此处用 `happiness` 表明回报的单位是快乐，而非金钱。 | `get the most “happiness bang for your buck.”` |
| `spend money on sth/sb` | 把钱花在某事或某人身上；本文两处分别谈体验消费和为别人花钱。 | `spend money on experiences`；`spending money on others` |
| `give to charity` | 向慈善事业捐助；句中不限定具体捐助金额或机构。与 `purchasing things for oneself` 对照。 | `giving to charity` |
| `money well spent` | 花得值的钱；常见于评价某笔支出是否值得，不能译成“钱已经花完了”。最后既收束主题，也暗含推荐。 | `it was money well spent` |

### 固定搭配：感受变化、生活状态与建议

| 搭配 | 语境含义与辨析 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `could do worse than do sth` | 委婉推荐，“不妨……／……也不失为好主意”。字面是另选办法可能更糟，并非说推荐的做法会更糟。 | `she could do worse than read Happy Money` |
| `wear off` | 感觉、效果等逐渐消退。这里消退的是购买物品带来的满足感，不是物品被磨损。 | `satisfaction with these material purchases wears off fairly quickly` |
| `creep in` | 悄然而至、渐渐出现，侧重不知不觉。文中与 `wear off` 连读：满足感消退，后悔逐渐出现。 | `regret creeps in` |
| `be better off` | 状况会更好，视语境可指经济或总体生活；此处是通勤、陪伴和娱乐习惯对生活的影响，不是变得更有钱。 | `most people would be better off` |
| `a whopping + 数量` | 口语，强调数字大得惊人；结合 `hardly jollier for it`，作者不是在赞赏美式电视消遣。 | `a whopping two months a year` |
| `come away from sth` | 字面可指离开；搭配 `believing` 等常指经历之后得到某种印象或感受。此处是读完书之后的判断。 | `come away from this book believing` |

### 固定搭配：供应与公共政策

| 搭配 | 语境含义与辨析 | 原文例项 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `restrict the availability of sth` | 限制某物能被买到或获得的程度；文中指限量或限时供应形成稀缺感，不是商品永久下架。 | `restricts the availability of its popular McRib` |
| `turn A into B` / `an object of obsession` | 把 A 变成 B；`object` 此处是情感所指向的对象，`obsession` 指强烈而持续的迷恋。 | `has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession` |
| `tax incentives for sb` | 通过税收优惠鼓励某类人或行为。文中谈减少购房者享受的优惠，而非减少购房者需要缴纳的税额。 | `reducing tax incentives for American homebuyers` |

> [!tip] 对照复习
> 把 `could do worse than ...`（开头委婉推荐）与 `money well spent`（结尾评价这本书）连起来看，可识别评论者的态度；句法结构回看 [[../intermediate/2014-passage1-happy-money/grammar-notes#否定：范围与程度|否定与语气]]。

### 生词练习

**一、选词填空**（每项限用一次）

> old-hat / a whopping / extravagant / sparingly / counterintuitive / scarcity / better off / wears off

1. The novelty of the new phone often ____ after a few weeks.
2. After years of seeing the same routine, audiences found it ____.
3. It may seem ____ that spending less can make an occasional treat more enjoyable.
4. The family would be ____ if they spent less time commuting.
5. The publisher sold ____ two million copies in a month.
6. Use the strong seasoning ____, or it will overpower the soup.
7. The sudden ____ of tickets drove up the price.
8. They could not afford such an ____ wedding.

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **wears off**
> 2. **old-hat**
> 3. **counterintuitive**
> 4. **better off**
> 5. **a whopping**
> 6. **sparingly**
> 7. **scarcity**
> 8. **extravagant**

**二、短语翻译**

1. `bang for your buck`
2. `undivided lottery jackpot`
3. `new-found fortune`

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **bang for your buck**：花同样的钱获得的回报
> 2. **undivided lottery jackpot**：由一人独得的彩票头奖
> 3. **new-found fortune**：新到手的一大笔财富

**三、语境理解**

1. `regret creeps in` 中的 `creeps in` 指什么？
   A. 被公开否认；B. 悄然产生；C. 迅速消失；D. 被别人强加。
2. 读者 `come away from this book believing …` 时，`come away` 指什么？
   A. 退回书款；B. 忘掉书的结论；C. 读完后带着某种看法；D. 离开读书会。

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. B：强调后悔不是突然到来，而是逐渐出现。
> 2. C：后接 `believing` 指出读完后形成的判断。
