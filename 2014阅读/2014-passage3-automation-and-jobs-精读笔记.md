# 2014 Passage 3：机器与就业 精读笔记

## 背景

2014 年考研英语阅读 Passage 3（第 31–35 题）。本文从经济低迷时更容易察觉的岗位压力说起，借两位研究者与哈格尔的观点，追问岗位为何容易被机器替代，最后把问题转向怎样让人机协作。难度：中等。

> [!abstract] 论证脉络
> 第 1–2 段：岗位消失不只是周期现象 → 第 3–4 段：解释标准化岗位为什么容易被替代 → 第 5–6 段：保留人的主动性，以机器辅助人，并改革工作方式。

---

## 文章原文

The concept of **man versus machine** is at least as old as the industrial revolution, but this phenomenon tends to be most acutely felt during **economic downturns** and **fragile recoveries**. And yet, it would be a mistake to think we are right now simply experiencing the painful side of a **boom and bust cycle**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：And yet, it would be a mistake to think we are right now simply experiencing the painful side of a boom and bust cycle.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: it（形式主语）；后置主语 S: to think … cycle
> - 谓语 V: would be
> - 表语 C: a mistake
> - 状语 A: And yet（承接并转折）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 衔接副词 | And yet | 将前一句的经济周期背景转向对「只是周期波动」的反驳 |
> | 不定式 | to think … | 作后置主语；think 后省略引导内容从句的 that |
> | 宾语从句 | we are … experiencing … | 作 think 的宾语；right now 指眼下，simply 限定经历的性质 |
> | 介词短语 | of a boom and bust cycle | 修饰 the painful side，界定是哪一种难熬阶段 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: It would be a mistake
>   └── 后置主语: to think
>         └── 宾语从句: (that) we are right now simply experiencing
>               └── 宾语: the painful side [of a boom and bust cycle]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：然而，如果以为我们眼下只是在经历经济繁荣与衰退周期中难熬的一段，那就错了。
>
> **考点提示**：作者否定的是「只是暂时的周期波动」，不是否认经济处在低迷阶段；后文的 for good 与 simply 构成关键对照。

Certain jobs have gone away **for good**, **outmoded** by machines. Since technology has such an **insatiable appetite for** eating up human jobs, this phenomenon will continue to **restructure** our economy in ways we can't immediately foresee.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Since technology has such an insatiable appetite for eating up human jobs, this phenomenon will continue to restructure our economy in ways we can't immediately foresee.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: this phenomenon
> - 谓语 V: will continue
> - 补足语 C: to restructure our economy
> - 状语 A: Since technology … jobs（原因）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 原因状语从句 | Since technology has … | since 在这里是「因为」，并非时间起点 |
> | 介词短语 | for eating up human jobs | 说明 appetite 的对象；eating up 把岗位被吞噬写成比喻 |
> | 介词短语 | in ways … | 修饰 restructure，表示重塑经济的方式 |
> | 定语从句 | we can't immediately foresee | 限制 ways；foresee 的宾语关系词可省，immediately 限定预见的时间 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: this phenomenon will continue
>   ├── 原因: Since technology has such an insatiable appetite [for eating up human jobs]
>   └── 不定式补足语: to restructure our economy
>         └── 方式: in ways [定从: (that) we can't immediately foresee __]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：技术对吞噬人类工作岗位的胃口似乎永远填不满，因此这一现象会继续重塑经济，具体方式眼下还难以预见。
>
> **考点提示**：问「预见什么」可找到 foresee 的宾语缺位；对比 A7，in which 填的是从句内部的方式状语。can't immediately 不等于「永远无法」。

When there is rapid improvement in the price and performance of technology, jobs that were once thought to be **immune from** automation suddenly become threatened.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：When there is rapid improvement in the price and performance of technology, jobs that were once thought to be immune from automation suddenly become threatened.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: jobs（由 that 从句限定）
> - 谓语 V: become
> - 表语 C: threatened
> - 状语 A: When there is …（时间或情境）；suddenly（突变）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 时间状语从句 | When there is … | 存在句围绕单数中心词 improvement；in … 说明改善的维度 |
> | 限制性定语从句 | that were once thought … | 修饰 jobs；that 充当从句主语，were thought 是从句的被动谓语 |
> | 不定式 | to be immune from automation | 给出过去人们所作的判断；immune from 表「不受……影响」 |
> | 结果状态 | become threatened | become 是主句系动词，threatened 表进入受威胁的状态 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: jobs [定从: that were once thought [to be immune from automation]] suddenly become threatened
>   └── 时间／情境: When there is rapid improvement [in the price and performance of technology]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：当技术成本迅速下降、性能迅速提升时，一些曾被认为不受自动化影响的岗位突然面临威胁。
>
> **考点提示**：先跳过 jobs 后的定语从句，读出 jobs become threatened；once thought 与 suddenly become 是旧判断和新变化的对照，不能用旧判断回答 Q32。

This argument has attracted a lot of attention, via the success of the book Race Against the Machine, by Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee, who both **hail from** MIT's Center for Digital Business.

This is a powerful argument, and a scary one. And yet, John Hagel, author of The Power of Pull and other books, says Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason why these jobs are so **vulnerable to** technology **in the first place**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：And yet, John Hagel, author of The Power of Pull and other books, says Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason why these jobs are so vulnerable to technology in the first place.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: John Hagel
> - 谓语 V: says
> - 宾语 O: (that) Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason …
> - 状语 A: And yet（转折衔接）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 同位语 | author of The Power of Pull and other books | 补充 John Hagel 的身份，不参与主干 |
> | 宾语从句 | Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss … | 省略 that；主语为两位作者，不是 Hagel |
> | 定语从句 | why these jobs are so vulnerable … | 限定 reason；why 是原因状语，so 修饰 vulnerable，in the first place 强调追溯根本原因 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: John Hagel [同位语: author of …] says
>   └── 宾语从句: (that) Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason
>         └── 定从: why these jobs are so vulnerable to technology in the first place
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：不过，《拉动的力量》等书的作者约翰·哈格尔认为，布林约尔松和麦卡菲没看清这些岗位为何一开始就这么容易受到技术冲击。
>
> **考点提示**：哈格尔质疑的是两位作者没有说清岗位脆弱的成因，并没有否认自动化威胁本身。抓住 says 与 miss 各自的主语。

Hagel says we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be “**tightly scripted**” and “**highly standardized**” ones that **leave no room for** “individual initiative or creativity.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Hagel says we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be “tightly scripted” and “highly standardized” ones that leave no room for “individual initiative or creativity.”
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Hagel
> - 谓语 V: says
> - 宾语 O: (that) we have designed jobs …
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 宾语从句 | (that) we have designed jobs | 作 says 的宾语；现在完成时把既有岗位设计和眼下后果联系起来 |
> | 介词短语 | in the U.S. | 交代设计或岗位所在的美国语境；不改变第一个 that 指向 jobs |
> | 定语从句 | that tend to be … ones | 修饰 jobs；tend to 表倾向，两个形容性短语共同描述 ones |
> | 定语从句 | that leave no room for … | 修饰替代名词 ones；that 作 leave 的主语，for 后指出岗位没有为哪些能力留出空间 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: Hagel says
>   └── 宾语从句: (that) we have designed jobs [in the U.S.]
>         └── 定从: that tend to be [tightly scripted and highly standardized] ones
>               └── 定从: that leave no room for [individual initiative or creativity]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：哈格尔说，我们在美国设计的岗位往往流程规定得很死且高度标准化，不给个人主动性或创造力留余地。
>
> **考点提示**：ones 回指 jobs，不是另一批岗位。Q33 把 leave no room for creativity 改写为设计不利于创造力，并非岗位设计者有意阻止创新。

In short, these are the types of jobs that machines can perform much better at than human beings. That is how we have put a giant target sign on the backs of American workers, Hagel says.

It's time to **reinvent** the formula for how work is conducted, since we are still relying on a very 20th century notion of work, Hagel says.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It's time to reinvent the formula for how work is conducted, since we are still relying on a very 20th century notion of work, Hagel says.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: It（时间表达中的虚主语）
> - 谓语 V: is（It's）
> - 表语 C: time to reinvent the formula …
> - 状语 A: since we are still relying on …（原因）
> - 以上为报道内容；句末报道语的主语 S: Hagel；谓语 V: says
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 不定式 | to reinvent the formula … | 补足 time，说明该做什么 |
> | 嵌入式 how 从句 | how work is conducted | 作 for 的宾语，保持陈述语序，is conducted 为被动 |
> | 原因状语从句 | since we are still relying … | 解释为什么现在该重设工作方式；still 说明旧观念仍在延续 |
> | 报道语 | Hagel says | 句末标示前述判断出自哈格尔，并非 since 从句的一部分 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 报道内容: It's time [to reinvent the formula]
>   ├── formula 的补充: for [how work is conducted]
>   └── 原因: since we are still relying on [a very 20th century notion of work]
> 句末报道语: Hagel says
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：哈格尔说，我们仍沿用一种很有二十世纪色彩的工作观念，因此现在是时候重新设计工作的组织方式了。
>
> **考点提示**：how work is conducted 是嵌在介词后的从句，不倒装；对照末段直接提问 how do we innovate … 的疑问语序。

In our rapidly changing economy, we more than ever need people in the workplace who can **take initiative** and **exercise their imagination** “to respond to unexpected events.” That’s not something machines are good at. They are designed to perform very predictable activities.

As Hagel notes, Brynjolfsson and McAfee indeed **touched on** this point in their book. We need to **reframe** race against the machine as race with the machine. In other words, we need to look at the ways in which machines can **augment** human labor rather than replace it.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In other words, we need to look at the ways in which machines can augment human labor rather than replace it.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: we
> - 谓语 V: need
> - 补足语 C: to look at the ways …
> - 状语 A: In other words（换言之）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 原文线索 | 功能与修饰对象 |
> |------|------|------|
> | 不定式 | to look at the ways … | 补足 need；这里是「研究、考察」而非肉眼观看 |
> | 定语从句 | in which machines can … | 限定 ways；in which 相当于 in those ways，在从句内作方式状语 |
> | 对照结构 | augment … rather than replace … | 比较增强与取代，it 回指 human labor；replace 在 can 的范围内理解 |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```text
> 主句: we need [to look at the ways]
>   ├── 衔接: In other words
>   └── 定从: in which machines can
>         ├── augment human labor
>         └── rather than replace it
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：换句话说，我们需要研究机器可以怎样增强人的工作能力，而不是取代人的劳动。
>
> **考点提示**：Q34 的归属线索是前句的 *indeed touched on this point*：两位作者的书也谈到上一段所说的「工作仍需要人的主动性与想象力」。本句再说明机器应辅助人而非替代人；不能只凭本句判断两位作者讨论了什么。

So then the problem is not really about technology, but rather, “how do we innovate our institutions and our work practices?”

## 翻译对照

### 第 1 段

**English**

The concept of **man versus machine** is at least as old as the industrial revolution, but this phenomenon tends to be most acutely felt during **economic downturns** and **fragile recoveries**. And yet, it would be a mistake to think we are right now simply experiencing the painful side of a **boom and bust cycle**. Certain jobs have gone away **for good**, **outmoded** by machines. Since technology has such an **insatiable appetite for** eating up human jobs, this phenomenon will continue to **restructure** our economy in ways we can't immediately foresee.

**中文**

“人与机器竞争”的观念至少可以追溯到工业革命时期，但在经济低迷和复苏乏力时，人们往往最能切身感受到这一现象。然而，如果以为我们眼下只是在经历繁荣与衰退周期中令人难熬的阶段，那就错了。一些岗位已经被机器淘汰，永远消失了。由于技术对吞噬人类工作岗位的胃口永远得不到满足，这一现象还将继续重塑我们的经济，其具体方式目前还难以预见。

### 第 2 段

**English**

When there is rapid improvement in the price and performance of technology, jobs that were once thought to be **immune from** automation suddenly become threatened. This argument has attracted a lot of attention, via the success of the book Race Against the Machine, by Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee, who both **hail from** MIT's Center for Digital Business.

**中文**

当技术成本迅速下降、性能快速提升时，一些曾被认为不会受到自动化影响的岗位，也会突然面临威胁。随着《与机器赛跑》一书获得成功，这一观点引起了广泛关注。该书由埃里克·布林约尔松和安德鲁·麦卡菲合著，两人都来自麻省理工学院数字商业中心。

### 第 3 段

**English**

This is a powerful argument, and a scary one. And yet, John Hagel, author of The Power of Pull and other books, says Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason why these jobs are so **vulnerable to** technology **in the first place**.

**中文**

这个论点很有说服力，也让人不安。不过，《拉动的力量》等书的作者约翰·哈格尔认为，布林约尔松和麦卡菲没有看到这些岗位之所以如此容易受到技术冲击的根本原因。

### 第 4 段

**English**

Hagel says we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be “**tightly scripted**” and “**highly standardized**” ones that **leave no room for** “individual initiative or creativity.” In short, these are the types of jobs that machines can perform much better at than human beings. That is how we have put a giant target sign on the backs of American workers, Hagel says.

**中文**

哈格尔说，我们在美国设计的岗位往往流程规定得很死，而且高度标准化，丝毫不给个人发挥主动性或创造力的空间。简言之，这些恰恰是机器能比人做得好得多的工作。哈格尔说，我们正是这样在美国劳动者背上贴了个巨大的靶标。

> [!note] 译注
> “贴靶标”是比喻：岗位的设计方式让从事这些工作的人更容易成为自动化替代的对象。

### 第 5 段

**English**

It's time to **reinvent** the formula for how work is conducted, since we are still relying on a very 20th century notion of work, Hagel says. In our rapidly changing economy, we more than ever need people in the workplace who can **take initiative** and **exercise their imagination** “to respond to unexpected events.” That’s not something machines are good at. They are designed to perform very predictable activities.

**中文**

哈格尔说，我们仍沿用一种很有二十世纪色彩的工作观念，是时候重新设计工作的组织方式了。在迅速变化的经济环境中，我们比以往任何时候都更需要能主动行动、发挥想象力来“应对突发情况”的员工。这不是机器擅长的事。机器是为执行可预见性很高的任务而设计的。

### 第 6 段

**English**

As Hagel notes, Brynjolfsson and McAfee indeed **touched on** this point in their book. We need to **reframe** race against the machine as race with the machine. In other words, we need to look at the ways in which machines can **augment** human labor rather than replace it. So then the problem is not really about technology, but rather, “how do we innovate our institutions and our work practices?”

**中文**

正如哈格尔指出的，布林约尔松和麦卡菲确实在书中谈到了这一点。我们需要换个角度，从“和机器比谁跑得快”转向“与机器并肩跑”。换句话说，我们需要研究机器可以怎样增强人的工作能力，而不是取代人的劳动。这样看来，真正的问题不在于技术，而在于：“我们该如何革新我们的制度和工作方式？”

## 语法要点

考点从原文提取。P1–P6 指正文段号，例句栏为原文连续片段。词义与语境另见 [[intermediate/2014-passage3-automation-and-jobs/固定搭配与词组笔记.md|本篇固定搭配与词组笔记]]。

### 从句：名词性从句

先找从句的外层位置，再判断它由省略的 `that` 还是由 `how` 引出。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `it would be a mistake to think (that) …` | P1. `it` 是形式主语，`to think …` 是后置的实质主语；`think` 后的内容从句省略 `that`。`a mistake` 否定的是“眼下只是在经历周期性衰退”这个解释，并非否定周期存在。 | `it would be a mistake to think we are right now simply experiencing the painful side of a boom and bust cycle` |
| `say (that) …` | P3. `says` 后整个分句作宾语；切开后，内层主语为两位作者，内层谓语为 `miss`，并不是哈格尔本人忽略了原因。后接的 `why` 从句见 [[#从句：定语从句]]。 | `says Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason why these jobs are so vulnerable to technology in the first place` |
| `be + how 从句` / `for + how 从句` | P4. `how we have put …` 作 `is` 的表语，说明“我们是如何做到的”。P5. `how work is conducted` 作介词 `for` 的宾语，表示“工作如何开展”。两处都用陈述语序。 | `That is how we have put a giant target sign on the backs of American workers`；`the formula for how work is conducted` |

> [!tip] 对照直接引语
> P6 的 `“how do we innovate our institutions and our work practices?”` 是直接提出的问题，保留 `do we` 疑问语序；不能把这种语序套进 P4、P5 的嵌入式 `how` 从句。

---

### 从句：定语从句

别只看先行词长得像什么；先确定从句内是缺主语、宾语，还是只缺原因、方式一类的状语。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `jobs that + 谓语 … ones that + 谓语` | P4. 第一个 `that` 修饰 `jobs`，虽隔着地点短语 `in the U.S.`，仍在从句作 `tend` 的主语；`ones` 代指一类岗位，带第二个 `that` 从句，后者以 `leave` 为谓语。两处 `that` 作主语，不可省。 | `jobs in the U.S. that tend to be “tightly scripted” and “highly standardized” ones that leave no room for “individual initiative or creativity.”` |
| `people … who + 谓语`；`人名, who + 谓语` | P5. 无逗号的 `who` 从句限定所需人员，`can` 统领 `take` 和 `exercise`。P2 的逗号和 `both` 表明从句补充说明两位作者共同的机构背景，不只修饰紧邻的 McAfee。 | `people in the workplace who can take initiative and exercise their imagination`；`Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee, who both hail from MIT's Center for Digital Business` |
| `reason why + 主语 + 谓语` | P3. `why` 是关系副词，在从句中作原因状语，相当于 `for which`。`these jobs are so vulnerable to technology` 自有主语和表语，不缺宾语。 | `the reason why these jobs are so vulnerable to technology in the first place` |
| `ways + 主语 + 及物动词` / `ways in which + 分句` | P1. `foresee` 缺宾语，`ways` 正是被预见的对象，省的是宾语关系词；前面的 `in ways` 整体才在主句中表方式。P6. `augment` 已有宾语 `human labor`，`in which` 在从句内部作方式状语，相当于 `in those ways`。 | `in ways we can't immediately foresee`；`the ways in which machines can augment human labor rather than replace it` |
| `something + 主语 + be good at` | P5. `at` 留在从句末尾，它的宾语是省掉的关系词 `that`（指 `something`），不同于 P6 表方式的 `in which`。 | `That’s not something machines are good at.` |

> [!warning] 找缺位而非套公式
> 同样是 `ways`，P1 问“无法预见**什么**”，P6 问“机器能以**什么方式**增强人的劳动”。遇到开头已有 `in` 的情况，先看这个介词属于主句还是定语从句。

### 从句：状语从句

将时间背景、原因和补述与核心判断分开，避免错认因果方向。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `when + there is …` | P2. `when` 给出岗位受到威胁的时间与情境；存在句 `there is` 引出中心词 `improvement`，后面的 `price and performance` 不决定 `is` 的单复数。 | `When there is rapid improvement in the price and performance of technology` |
| `since + 分句` | P1. `since` 表原因，说明技术持续改变经济结构的缘由。P5. 同一连词解释为何需要改变现有工作方式；`still` 表旧观念延续，不等于 `since` 表“自……以来”。 | `Since technology has such an insatiable appetite for eating up human jobs`；`since we are still relying on a very 20th century notion of work` |
| `as + 主语 + 谓语, 主句` | P6. `as` 引出评注性的比较从句（“正如哈格尔指出的”）；`notes` 所指内容由后句交代，不表时间或原因。对照 [[#介词与连词：连接与对照]] 的 `reframe … as …`。 | `As Hagel notes, Brynjolfsson and McAfee indeed touched on this point in their book.` |

---

### 语态：被动与状态变化

先识别助动词和实义谓语，再判断 `-ed` 是被动结构的一部分还是表示结果状态。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `tend to be + 过去分词` | P1. 有限谓语是 `tends`；`to be … felt` 是不定式被动。`most acutely` 修饰 `felt` 的程度，而不是说这种现象只在衰退时发生。 | `this phenomenon tends to be most acutely felt during economic downturns and fragile recoveries` |
| `be thought to be + 形容词`；`become + 过去分词形式` | P2. `were once thought to be immune` 可还原为“人们曾认为这些岗位不受影响”。主句谓语是 `become`，`threatened` 表进入受威胁的状态；若把 `were thought` 当作主句谓语，便会漏掉新旧判断的转折。 | `jobs that were once thought to be immune from automation suddenly become threatened` |
| `be + 过去分词` | P5. `is conducted` 在 `how` 从句中为被动，问工作怎样开展；`are designed` 是主句被动，突出机器设计的用途，不必补出施动者。 | `how work is conducted`；`They are designed to perform very predictable activities.` |

---

### 非谓语动词：不定式与分词

把句子主干和附属动作分开；特别留意短语与什么成分搭配，以及动作由谁发出。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `主句, 过去分词 + by …` | P1. `outmoded by machines` 补充说明岗位为何消失，逻辑承受者是 `Certain jobs`；有时态的动词是 `have gone away`。 | `Certain jobs have gone away for good, outmoded by machines.` |
| `名词 + for + -ing` | P1. `for` 是介词，`eating up …` 作其宾语；它说明 `appetite` 的对象，不表示目的。 | `an insatiable appetite for eating up human jobs` |
| `It's time to do` / `动作 + to do` | P5. `It's time to …` 表“到了该……的时候”。引号中的 `to respond …` 说明员工采取主动、发挥想象力的目的。对照上行：`for + -ing` 在那里补足名词，而这里的目的结构是 `to + 动词原形`。 | `It's time to reinvent the formula for how work is conducted`；`take initiative and exercise their imagination “to respond to unexpected events.”` |

### 补充要点：比较与省略

先找比较两端，再判断后半部分省掉了什么；比较程度和转折方向也常是试题线索。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `at least as + 形容词 + as` | P1. 同级比较加 `at least` 给出年代下界，即这一观念的历史至少与工业革命一样久，而非断言它恰好诞生于那一时期。 | `is at least as old as the industrial revolution` |
| `much + 比较级 … than + 名词` | P4. `much` 加强 `better`；比较的是机器与人在这些岗位上的表现，`than human beings` 可理解为对照于人能够做到的程度。原文的 `perform … at` 组合略生硬，理解时可参照 `do these jobs much better than humans can`，不要当作规范搭配背。 | `the types of jobs that machines can perform much better at than human beings` |
| `more than ever` | P5. 修饰 `need`，表示需求比以往任何时候都强；不是给 `people` 添加一个单独的数量修饰语。 | `we more than ever need people in the workplace` |

### 介词与连词：连接与对照

英文对照常通过小词改变问题的重心；先判断两个结构是否占同一个语法位置。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `And yet + 分句` | P1. 将一时的周期波动与永久消失的岗位对照；P3. 先承认旧论证的说服力，再转向对成因的质疑。这里的 `yet` 不是“尚未”。 | `And yet, it would be a mistake`；`And yet, John Hagel, author of The Power of Pull and other books, says` |
| `reframe X as Y` | P6. `as` 引出重新界定的结果；`against` 强调对抗，`with` 改为协作。这里的 `as` 不引导 [[#从句：状语从句]] 中那种有主语和谓语的从句，也不是 [[#补充要点：比较与省略]] 的同级比较。 | `reframe race against the machine as race with the machine` |
| `can do X rather than do Y` / `not …, but rather …` | P6. 前一例将 `augment` 和 `replace` 对照，后者可理解为共享 `can`；后一例修正问题焦点，追问制度与工作方式怎样变革，不等于否认技术本身有影响。 | `machines can augment human labor rather than replace it`；`the problem is not really about technology, but rather, “how do we innovate our institutions and our work practices?”` |

### 岗位风险与设计

|------|------|------|
| 📌 `be immune from automation`；`become threatened` | P2. 不受自动化影响；开始受到威胁。前者只是从前的判断，后者才是新情况；Q32 的线索是越来越多既有岗位面临冲击。 | `were once thought to be immune from automation suddenly become threatened` |

### 人机协作与工作变革

| 搭配 / 词组 | 释义与辨析 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 📌 `reinvent the formula for …`；`rely on a notion of work` | P5. 重新设计……的模式；沿用一种工作观念。`formula` 此处是组织工作的方法，不是数学公式；与末段的制度、工作方式变革呼应。 | `reinvent the formula for how work is conducted`；`relying on a very 20th century notion of work` |

| 📌 `innovate our institutions and our work practices` | P6. 革新制度与工作方式。Q35 看末段如何从机器是否取代劳动转向人该怎样改变制度与实践，避免让首段背景或书名代替全文重点。 | `innovate our institutions and our work practices` |

2. After the final shift, the factory closed ______ and never reopened.
3. Even highly skilled jobs are not necessarily ______ automation.
4. Demand began to recover, but the gains were still ______ and could easily be reversed.

> 2. **for good**（永久地）
> 3. **immune from**（不受……影响）
> 4. **fragile**（脆弱的）
> 5. **insatiable**（无法满足的）
> 6. **restructure**（重组）
> 7. **outmoded**（被淘汰）
> 8. **reframe**（重新界定）

**二、短语翻译**

1. a boom and bust cycle
2. leave no room for individual initiative or creativity
3. take initiative and exercise their imagination

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. 经济繁荣与衰退交替的周期。
> 2. 不给个人主动性或创造力留余地。
> 3. 主动行动并发挥想象力。

**三、语境理解**

1. *It's time to reinvent the formula for how work is conducted* 中 *formula* 的意思是：
   - A. 数学方程
   - B. 工作组织模式
   - C. 产品的配方
   - D. 员工的数量
2. *Brynjolfsson and McAfee indeed touched on this point in their book* 中 *touched on* 的意思是：
   - A. 反驳
   - B. 直接接触
   - C. 完全遗漏
   - D. 谈及

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **B**。*For how work is conducted* 限定它是工作开展的方式。
> 2. **D**。宾语是 *this point*，表示在书中谈到观点，不等于作了详尽论证。

## 心得

> [!tip] 先分清谁说了什么，再做观点题
> 第 2 段的 *This argument* 指技术进步使原先看似安全的岗位也受威胁。第 3 段的 *Hagel … says* 引出成因上的补充，第 6 段的 *indeed touched on this point* 又说明两位作者也涉及人的主动性与想象力。这些判断不互相否定。

> [!warning] 原因、显现条件与结果不是一回事
> *Most acutely felt during economic downturns* 说威胁在低迷期更显著，不等于衰退造成技术替代。*For good* 才是识别结构性变化的信号。

## 延伸

复读第 4–6 段时分别画出两条因果链：① 高度标准化 → 任务易预测 → 机器可替代；② 出现意外 → 需要人的主动性和想象力 → 机器更适合承担辅助角色。第二条是把段落信息合起来的推论，不是对所有岗位的断言。

## 思考题

1. **主旨题**：本文最终要解决的是「如何阻止机器进入工作场所」，还是「如何改变工作方式」？用末段一句话作证。
2. **指代题**：第 5 段 *That's not something machines are good at* 中 *That* 回指什么能力？
3. **推断题**：哈格尔为什么用 *put a giant target sign on the backs of American workers* 描写岗位设计？

> [!check]- 思考题参考答案
> 1. 后者。末句明确追问 *how do we innovate our institutions and our work practices?*
> 2. 主动采取行动、发挥想象力以应对突发事件。
> 3. 严格流程和高度标准化让工作更可预测，增加了机器取代这些岗位的风险；并非说工人真的成为有形靶标。
