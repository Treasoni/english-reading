# 零散语法笔记（合成样本）

- 非限定性定语从句：`Teachers, who once enjoyed considerable autonomy in the classroom, now find themselves accountable to standardized tests, parental demands, and administrative oversight.` 逗号内补充说明教师过去享有的自主权；删去从句后主干仍完整。
- 另一个同类结构：`This attrition rate, which has remained stubbornly high since the 1990s, shows little sign of improvement.` `which` 指代流失率，不是 1990 年代；`since` 连接起点与持续至今的状态。
- 对比结构：`Whether such measures can be scaled nationwide, however, depends less on funding than on a fundamental change in how the public values those who educate its children.` `less on A than on B` 比较的是两个依赖因素的相对重要性，不表示 A 完全不重要。
