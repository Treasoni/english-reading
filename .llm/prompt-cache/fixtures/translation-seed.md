# Teacher Shortage: A Persistent Crisis 师资短缺：持续的危机

The problem of teacher shortage has become more acute as school districts across the country struggle to fill classrooms with qualified instructors. Many observers attribute the crisis to low pay and heavy workloads, but the deeper causes may lie in the changing expectations that society places on the profession.

全国各地的学区难以为各班配齐合格教师，师资短缺因此愈发严峻。许多观察者把这场危机归咎于低工资和繁重的工作量，但更深层的原因或许是社会对教师这一职业的期待正在变化。

Teachers, who once enjoyed considerable autonomy in the classroom, now find themselves accountable to standardized tests, parental demands, and administrative oversight. The result, according to a recent survey, is that nearly one in four new teachers leaves the profession within their first three years. This attrition rate, which has remained stubbornly high since the 1990s, shows little sign of improvement.

教师过去在课堂上有很大的自主权，如今却要对标准化考试、家长的要求和行政监督负责。最近的一项调查显示，其结果是将近四分之一的新教师会在任教的头三年离开这个行业。自 20 世纪 90 年代以来，教师流失率一直居高不下，几乎没有改善的迹象。
