# The Battle over Gene Patents 精读笔记

## 背景

本文是 **2012 年考研英语阅读 Passage 3**，主题是**基因专利之争**。文章以 2010 年美国联邦法院围绕"基因是否可申请专利"的两次相反裁决为线索，呈现生物技术产业、法院、司法部与公益批评者之间的多方博弈，并在结尾把争点引向"企业下一步要争夺的是基因之间的关联信息"。

> [!abstract] 文章概要
> 文章采用"事件反转 → 争议理由 → 遗留问题 → 焦点转移"的论证结构。作者并未直接站队，而是通过引用 BIO 律师的解释、司法部的意见书与 Mayo Clinic 提起的诉讼等事实，暗示基因专利的边界最终仍将由法院划定。

- **体裁**：科技与法律类议论文（《经济学人》风格）
- **难度**：中等偏上
- **核心线索**：基因能否被授予专利尚无定论；真正的专利战场正从"基因本身"转向"基因之间的关联"。

## 文章原文

# The Battle over Gene Patents

In 2010, a federal judge shook America's biotech industry to its core. Companies had won **patents** for **isolated DNA** for decades—by 2005 some $20\%$ of human genes were patented. But in March 2010 a judge ruled that genes were **unpatentable**. Executives were ==violently agitated==. The Biotechnology Industry Organisation (BIO), a **trade group**, assured members that this was just a “**preliminary step**” in a longer battle.

On July 29th they were relieved, at least temporarily. A federal appeals court **overturned** the prior decision, ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of **breast cancer**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：A federal appeals court **overturned** the prior decision, ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of **breast cancer**.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: A federal appeals court（一家联邦上诉法院）
> - 谓语 V: overturned（推翻）
> - 宾语 O: the prior decision（先前的裁决）
> - 状语 A: 无（主句无状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（V-ing） | ruling | 主句谓语 overturned，作结果状语 |
> | 宾语从句 | that | ruling 的宾语从句 |
> | 定语从句 | that | two genes |
> | 介词短语 | to two genes | 修饰 patents |
> | 介词短语 | of breast cancer | 修饰 risk |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: A federal appeals court + overturned + the prior decision
>   └── 非谓语: (ruling ...) → 结果状语
>         └── 宾从: (that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes)
>               ├── 介短: (to two genes) → 修饰 patents
>               └── 定从: (that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer) → 修饰 two genes
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：一家联邦上诉法院推翻了先前的裁决，判定迈里亚德基因公司（Myriad Genetics）确实可以持有两项基因的专利，这两项基因有助于预测女性患乳腺癌的风险。
>
> **考点提示**：ruling 为现在分词作结果状语，其逻辑主语与主句主语一致（a federal appeals court），这是判断非谓语归属的核心依据；句末 that 引导的定语从句修饰 two genes，与前面的 that 宾语从句形成"从句套从句"，翻译时须把定语从句拆成独立分句。

The chief executive of Myriad, a company in Utah, said the ruling was ==a blessing to firms and patients alike==.

But as companies continue their attempts at **personalised medicine**, the courts will remain rather busy. The Myriad case itself is probably not over. Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a ==product of nature==, so it may not be patented; gene patents **suppress** innovation rather than reward it; and patents' **monopolies** restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a ==product of nature==, so it may not be patented; gene patents **suppress** innovation rather than reward it; and patents' **monopolies** restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Critics（批评者）
> - 谓语 V: make（提出）
> - 宾语 O: three main arguments（三条主要理由）
> - 状语 A: 无（主句无状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | against | three main arguments |
> | 同位解释 | 冒号 | three main arguments 的具体内容 |
> | 并列连词（结果） | so | 连接 a gene is a product of nature 与 it may not be patented |
> | 并列比较 | rather than | suppress innovation 与 reward it |
> | 同位举例 | such as | genetic tests |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Critics + make + three main arguments
>   ├── 介短: (against gene patents) → 修饰 arguments
>   └── 冒号解释: arguments 的三项内容
>         ├── 分句一: a gene is a product of nature
>         │     └── 并列(结果): (so it may not be patented)
>         ├── 分句二: gene patents suppress innovation
>         │     └── 比较并列: (rather than reward it)
>         └── 分句三: patents' monopolies restrict access to genetic tests
>               └── 同位举例: (such as Myriad's)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：批评者提出三条反对基因专利的主要理由：基因是自然的产物，因此不应被授予专利；基因专利抑制而非奖励创新；专利垄断限制了人们获得迈里亚德这类基因检测的机会。
>
> **考点提示**：冒号后接三个并列分句，是考研阅读最常见的"总—分"展开结构，三个分句正好对应题目中的三条反对理由，定位题极爱在此设题。rather than 前后须保持形式一致（suppress ... rather than reward ...）；此处的 so 是并列连词引导结果分句，而非从属连词。

A growing number seem to agree. Last year a federal task-force urged reform for patents related to genetic tests. In October the Department of Justice filed a **brief** in the Myriad case, arguing that an isolated DNA molecule “is no less a product of nature...than are cotton fibres that have been **separated** from cotton seeds.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In October the Department of Justice filed a **brief** in the Myriad case, arguing that an isolated DNA molecule “is no less a product of nature...than are cotton fibres that have been **separated** from cotton seeds.”
>
> **主干提取**
> - 状语 A: In October（时间状语）
> - 主语 S: the Department of Justice（司法部）
> - 谓语 V: filed（提交）
> - 宾语 O: a brief（一份意见陈述书）
> - 状语 A: in the Myriad case（范围状语）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（V-ing） | arguing | 伴随状语，逻辑主语 = the Department of Justice |
> | 宾语从句 | that | arguing 的宾语从句 |
> | 比较结构 | no less ... than | product of nature 与 cotton fibres |
> | 定语从句 | that | cotton fibres |
> | 介词短语 | from cotton seeds | 修饰 been separated |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: the Department of Justice + filed + a brief
>   ├── 介短: (In October) → 时间状语
>   ├── 介短: (in the Myriad case) → 范围状语
>   └── 非谓语: (arguing ...) → 伴随状语
>         └── 宾从: (that an isolated DNA molecule is no less a product of nature than are cotton fibres)
>               ├── 比较结构: (no less A than B) → "A 与 B 同样是"
>               └── 定从: (that have been separated from cotton seeds) → 修饰 cotton fibres
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：10月，司法部在迈里亚德案中提交了一份意见陈述书，主张分离出的DNA分子"与从棉籽上分离出来的棉纤维一样，同样是自然的产物"。
>
> **考点提示**：`no less A than B` 意为"A 和 B 一样（都是）"，语气上强调 A 绝不亚于 B，是考研翻译的经典结构（可与 no more than / not more than 的否定含义对比记忆）。than 之后 are cotton fibres 为主谓倒装，因主语较长且自带定语从句；引文中的省略号表示跳读，不影响句法分析。

Despite the appeals court's decision, big questions remain unanswered. For example, it is unclear whether the **sequencing** of a whole **genome** violates the patents of individual genes within it.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：For example, it is unclear whether the **sequencing** of a whole **genome** violates the patents of individual genes within it.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 形式主语 S: it
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: unclear（不清楚的）
> - 真正主语 S: whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents of individual genes within it（whether 引导的主语从句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 主语从句 | whether | 真正主语，it 为形式主语 |
> | 介词短语 | of a whole genome | 修饰 sequencing |
> | 介词短语 | of individual genes | 修饰 patents |
> | 介词短语 | within it | 修饰 individual genes（it = a whole genome） |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: it(形式主语) + is + unclear
>   └── 主从: (whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents of individual genes within it) → 真正主语
>         ├── 介短: (of a whole genome) → 修饰 sequencing
>         └── 介短: (of individual genes) → 修饰 patents
>               └── 介短: (within it) → 修饰 individual genes
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：例如，目前尚不清楚对完整基因组进行测序是否会侵犯其中单个基因的专利。
>
> **考点提示**：it 作形式主语、whether 引导的主语从句后置，是考研高频句型；主语从句只能用 whether 不能用 if。名词短语内的 it 回指 a whole genome，翻译时须还原为"其中"，否则句子读不通。sequencing 由动词 sequence 加 -ing 变为名词，属"动名词化"的抽象名词，是考研阅读中常见的名词化现象。

The case may yet reach the Supreme Court.

As the industry advances, however, other **suits** may have an even greater impact. Companies are unlikely to file many more patents for human DNA molecules—most are already patented or in the **public domain**. Firms are now studying how genes **interact**, looking for **correlations** that might be used to determine the causes of disease or predict a drug's **efficacy**. Companies are eager to win patents for “==connecting the dots==,” explains Hans Sauer, a lawyer for the BIO.

Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue, brought by the Mayo Clinic, which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue, brought by the Mayo Clinic, which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Their success（他们的成功）
> - 谓语 V: may be determined（可能被决定，被动语态）
> - 状语 A: by a suit（由一桩诉讼）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | by a suit | 说明 determined 的施动者 |
> | 非谓语（V-ed） | related to | a suit |
> | 非谓语（V-ed） | brought by | a suit |
> | 定语从句（非限定） | which | a suit（关系代词作 hear 的宾语） |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: Their success + may be determined + (by a suit)
>   └── 后置修饰链 → a suit
>         ├── 非谓语: (related to this issue)
>         ├── 非谓语: (brought by the Mayo Clinic) → 说明诉讼的提起者
>         └── 定从: (which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：他们能否成功，或许取决于梅奥诊所（Mayo Clinic）就这一问题提起的一桩诉讼，最高法院将在下一个开庭期审理此案。
>
> **考点提示**：a suit 后接"两个过去分词短语 + 一个定语从句"三重后置修饰，是考研"名词 + 多重修饰"的典型考法。which 在从句中作 hear 的宾语，故翻译时须把定语从句拆成独立分句（"最高法院将……审理此案"），不能直译成"……的诉讼"。这句还暗含因果链：Mayo Clinic 案的结果 → BIO 成员企业的专利前景。

The BIO recently held a convention which included sessions to coach lawyers on the **shifting landscape** for patents. ==Each meeting was packed.==

## 翻译对照

# The Battle over Gene Patents 基因专利之争 中英对照

In 2010, a federal judge shook America's biotech industry to its core. Companies had won **patents** for **isolated DNA** for decades—by 2005 some $20\%$ of human genes were patented. But in March 2010 a judge ruled that genes were **unpatentable**. Executives were ==violently agitated==. The Biotechnology Industry Organisation (BIO), a **trade group**, assured members that this was just a “**preliminary step**” in a longer battle.

2010年，一位联邦法官令美国生物技术产业受到根本性的震动。数十年来，企业一直在为**分离出来的DNA**申请并赢得**专利**——到2005年，人类基因中约 $20\%$ 已被授予专利。但在2010年3月，一名法官裁定基因**不可申请专利**。企业高管们==群情激愤==。行业团体“生物技术产业组织”（BIO）向会员保证，这只是一场更漫长战役中的“**预备动作**”。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> "shook ... to its core" 直译为"震动到核心"，此处译为"受到根本性的震动"，保留原文强调程度的同时符合中文表达。
> "violently agitated" 字面为"被猛烈地搅动"，指人时意为"极度不安、群情激愤"，取后者。

On July 29th they were relieved, at least temporarily. A federal appeals court **overturned** the prior decision, ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of **breast cancer**. The chief executive of Myriad, a company in Utah, said the ruling was ==a blessing to firms and patients alike==.

7月29日，他们松了一口气——至少暂时如此。一家联邦上诉法院**推翻**了先前的裁决，判定迈里亚德基因公司（Myriad Genetics）确实可以持有两项基因的专利，这两项基因有助于预测女性患**乳腺癌**的风险。总部位于犹他州的迈里亚德公司首席执行官说，这一裁决对企业和患者而言==都是福音==。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> "a blessing to firms and patients alike" 中 alike 表示"两者同样地"，译为"对企业和患者而言都是福音"，保留 alike 的并列意味。

But as companies continue their attempts at **personalised medicine**, the courts will remain rather busy. The Myriad case itself is probably not over. Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a ==product of nature==, so it may not be patented; gene patents **suppress** innovation rather than reward it; and patents' **monopolies** restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's. A growing number seem to agree. Last year a federal task-force urged reform for patents related to genetic tests. In October the Department of Justice filed a **brief** in the Myriad case, arguing that an isolated DNA molecule “is no less a product of nature...than are cotton fibres that have been **separated** from cotton seeds.”

但随着企业继续尝试**个性化医疗**，法院仍将相当忙碌。迈里亚德案本身很可能尚未终结。批评者提出三条反对基因专利的主要理由：基因是==自然的产物==，因此不应被授予专利；基因专利**抑制**而非奖励创新；专利**垄断**限制了人们获得迈里亚德这类基因检测的机会。越来越多的人似乎认同这一点。去年，一个联邦特别工作组敦促对与基因检测相关的专利进行改革。10月，司法部在迈里亚德案中提交了一份**意见陈述书**，主张分离出的DNA分子“与从棉籽上**分离**出来的棉纤维一样，同样是自然的产物”。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> "no less a product of nature...than are cotton fibres" 是 `no less A than B` 结构，意为"A 和 B 一样（都是）"，含"绝不亚于"的强调语气；原文省略号表示引文中的跳读，译文中以"同样是自然的产物"补足，语义不变。
> "filed a brief" 中 brief 为法律术语，指"（向法院提交的）意见陈述书／诉讼摘要"，不是"简短的"。

Despite the appeals court's decision, big questions remain unanswered. For example, it is unclear whether the **sequencing** of a whole **genome** violates the patents of individual genes within it. The case may yet reach the Supreme Court.

尽管上诉法院作出了裁决，重大问题仍未获得解答。例如，目前尚不清楚对完整**基因组**进行**测序**是否会侵犯其中单个基因的专利。此案仍可能上诉至最高法院。

As the industry advances, however, other suits may have an even greater impact. Companies are unlikely to file many more patents for human DNA molecules—most are already patented or in the **public domain**. Firms are now studying how genes **interact**, looking for **correlations** that might be used to determine the causes of disease or predict a drug's **efficacy**. Companies are eager to win patents for “==connecting the dots==,” explains Hans Sauer, a lawyer for the BIO.

然而，随着该产业不断前进，其他诉讼可能产生更大的影响。企业不太可能再为人类DNA分子提交更多专利申请——其中大多数已获专利或属于**公有领域**。企业如今正在研究基因之间如何**相互作用**，寻找可用来确定疾病成因或预测药物**疗效**的**关联性**。生物技术产业组织的律师汉斯·绍尔（Hans Sauer）解释说，企业渴望赢得“==把各个点连起来==”的专利。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> "connecting the dots" 源自连点成图的儿童游戏，此处喻指"把零散的基因信息串联起来"；译文保留"把各个点连起来"的比喻，便于与下文的专利争夺对照理解。

Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue, brought by the Mayo Clinic, which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term. The BIO recently held a convention which included sessions to coach lawyers on the **shifting landscape** for patents. ==Each meeting was packed.==

他们能否成功，或许取决于梅奥诊所（Mayo Clinic）就这一问题提起的一桩诉讼，最高法院将在下一个开庭期审理此案。生物技术产业组织最近举办了一次大会，其中设有专门指导律师应对专利领域**不断变化的形势**的培训环节。==每一场会议都座无虚席。==

> [!note] 翻译说明
> "Each meeting was packed." 是第34题的考点句。packed 意为"挤满人的"，此处为下文"基因专利备受关注"提供依据，不宜译为"被打包"。
> "hear a case" 为法律固定搭配，意为"审理案件"，不是"听见"。

## 语法要点

# The Battle over Gene Patents 语法整理

> [!abstract] 说明
> 本笔记根据文章原文中已标注的长难句分析，并补充其余句子的考研语法考点整理。语法结构与例句均来自本篇文章；固定搭配的词义、语体和辨析另见 [[固定搭配与词组笔记]]。

### 从句：宾语从句与 that 的省略

本文的宾语从句多跟在 `assure`、`rule`、`argue`、`say`、`explain` 等动词之后。`that` 只起连接作用、不充当成分时，口语和新闻文体中常被省略。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **assure sb that + 从句** | 向某人保证……；`that` 不可省（双宾语） | The BIO ... assured members that this was just a “preliminary step”. |
| **rule that + 从句** | 裁定…… | a judge ruled that genes were unpatentable |
| **argue that + 从句** | 主张/辩称…… | arguing that an isolated DNA molecule is no less a product of nature |
| **say + (that) 从句** | 说……；`that` 常省略 | the ruling was a blessing to firms and patients alike |
| **explain + 从句（引述前置）** | 解释说……；主语后置 | explains Hans Sauer, a lawyer for the BIO |

> [!tip] 📌 考研核心考点
> `assure sb that...` 属于"动词 + 间接宾语 + that 从句"，`that` 不能像 `say that` 那样省略；反之 `say`、`think`、`believe` 后的 `that` 省略则十分常见。判断依据是：省略后句子是否会出现"两个宾语并列"的歧义。

---

### 从句：定语从句（限制性 / 非限制性）

定语从句在本文中既修饰具体名词，也出现在名词短语的长修饰链末端。注意"先行词 + 多个后置修饰"这一结构。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **名词 + that + 谓语** | 限制性定语从句，指明"哪一些" | two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer |
| **名词 + that + 被动谓语** | 限制性定语从句中的被动结构 | cotton fibres that have been separated from cotton seeds |
| **名词 + which + 谓语** | 非限制性定语从句，补充说明 | a convention which included sessions to coach lawyers |
| **名词 + which + 主语 + 谓语** | 非限制性定语从句，`which` 作从句宾语 | a suit ... which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term |

> [!warning] 易错点：that 从句是定语从句还是宾语从句
> `ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast...` 中有两个 `that`：第一个紧跟 `ruling`，是**宾语从句**；第二个紧跟 `two genes`，是**定语从句**。判别方法是看 `that` 后面是否缺成分、以及能否用 `which` 替换。

> [!quote]- 相关内容
> - 定语从句拆分译法参见 [[语法总结笔记#从句：定语从句 (Attributive Clauses)]]。
> - 关系代词的省略规则参见 [[语法总结笔记#关系代词的省略规则]]。

---

### 从句：主语从句与形式主语 it

`it` 作形式主语、真正主语后置，是考研阅读中极高频的句型。本文用 `whether` 引导真正主语，表示"是否"。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **It is + adj. + whether + 从句** | 形式主语 + whether 主语从句 | it is unclear whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents |
| **It is + adj. + that + 从句** | 形式主语 + that 主语从句 | it is unclear that ...（本文未用，可对比记忆） |
| **whether 引导主语从句** | 主语从句只能用 **whether**，不能用 if | whether the sequencing ... violates the patents |

> [!warning] 易错点：whether 与 if
> `if` 只能引导宾语从句，不能引导主语从句、表语从句、同位语从句，也不能直接跟在介词后。本句若换成 `if` 即为病句，是考研常见的干扰项设置点。

> [!quote]- 相关内容
> - 参见 [[语法总结笔记#形式主语 It 与主语从句]] 与 [[语法总结笔记#Whether 引导的主语从句]]。

---

### 从句：状语从句与 however 插入语

本文用 `as` 引导时间/原因状语从句，用 `however` 作插入语表示转折，二者在句中位置不同、功能不同。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **as + 主语 + 谓语, 主句** | 随着……；引导时间/原因状语从句 | But as companies continue their attempts at personalised medicine, the courts will remain rather busy. |
| **As + 主语 + 谓语, however, 主句** | `however` 作连接副词插入成分，表转折，用逗号隔离 | As the industry advances, however, other suits may have an even greater impact. |
| **Despite + 名词短语, 主句** | 让步介词短语（注意：`despite` 后只能接名词/动名词，不能接从句） | Despite the appeals court's decision, big questions remain unanswered. |

> [!tip] 📌 考研核心考点
> `however` 有两个身份：① 作连接副词（= no matter how）引导让步状语从句，如 `However hard he tried...`；② 作插入语，用逗号隔开，表示"然而"。本文是第②种。`But` 与 `as` 从句连用时，`But` 仍位于句首承接上文逻辑。

---

### 非谓语动词：分词作状语与后置定语

本文的分词结构密度很高：现在分词短语多作结果或伴随状语，过去分词短语多作后置定语，二者常在同一句中叠加出现。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **主句 + , V-ing ...** | 现在分词作结果状语，逻辑主语 = 主句主语 | A federal appeals court overturned the prior decision, ruling that... |
| **主句 + , V-ing ...** | 现在分词作伴随状语，逻辑主语 = 主句主语 | the Department of Justice filed a brief ..., arguing that... |
| **主句 + , V-ing ...** | 现在分词作伴随状语 | Firms are now studying how genes interact, looking for correlations |
| **名词 + V-ed 短语** | 过去分词短语作后置定语 | a suit related to this issue / brought by the Mayo Clinic |
| **V-ed + 名词** | 过去分词作前置定语 | **isolated** DNA / an **isolated** DNA molecule |
| **be + V-ed** | 被动语态（过去分词作谓语的一部分） | genes were **unpatented** / patented |

> [!note] 逻辑主语判断法
> 分词作状语时，其逻辑主语默认与主句主语一致。`ruling` 的主语是 `A federal appeals court`（法院作出裁决）；`arguing` 的主语是 `the Department of Justice`（司法部提出主张）。若逻辑主语不一致，就应改为独立主格结构。

> [!quote]- 相关内容
> - 参见 [[语法总结笔记#非谓语动词 (Non-finite Verbs)]]。

---

### 非谓语动词：不定式与动名词

不定式在本文中作后置定语、目的状语和宾语；动名词（含动名词化的抽象名词）则常见于 `of + V-ing` 与"动词 + 名词 + 介词 + 动名词"结构。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **名词 + to do** | 不定式作后置定语 | sessions to coach lawyers on the shifting landscape |
| **be used to do sth** | 被用来做某事（`to` 是不定式符号） | correlations that might be used to determine the causes of disease |
| **eager to do sth** | 渴望做某事 | Companies are eager to win patents for “connecting the dots” |
| **attempts at + 名词短语** | 对……的尝试（`at` 后接名词，不接不定式） | companies continue their attempts at **personalised medicine** |
| **of + V-ing** | 名词的后置定语，说明内容 | the **sequencing** of a whole **genome** |
| **动名词化的抽象名词** | 动词加 -ing 变为可数/不可数名词，是考研阅读的抽象化手段 | a **ruling** / the **sequencing** |

> [!warning] 易错点：be used to do vs be used to doing
> `be used to do sth` 中 `to` 是不定式符号，意为"被用来做"；`be used to doing sth` 中 `to` 是介词，意为"习惯于做"。本文 `might be used to determine` 是前者，判断依据是主语 `correlations` 是"被使用"的对象。

---

### 比较结构：no less A than B 与 than 后倒装

`no less A than B` 表示"A 和 B 一样（都是）"，语气上强调 A 绝不亚于 B。`than` 引导的比较从句中，若主语较长，常出现主谓倒装。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **no less A than B** | A 和 B 一样；A 绝不亚于 B | an isolated DNA molecule is no less a product of nature than are cotton fibres |
| **than + 谓语 + 主语（倒装）** | 比较从句中主语长且带定语从句，故后置 | ... than are cotton fibres that have been separated from cotton seeds |
| **rather than** | 而不是；连接并列成分，前后形式须一致 | gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it |
| **no more than / not more than** | 对比记忆：`no more than` = 仅仅；`not more than` = 不超过 | （与本文结构对照记忆） |

> [!tip] 📌 考研核心考点
> 比较结构是考研翻译的固定难点。`no less A than B` 的语气相当于"B 是自然的产物，A 同样是"，翻译时须把比较的双方都译出来，不能只译成"……和……一样"。`rather than` 连接的两个成分必须语法形式对等（本文是两个动词原形 `suppress` 与 `reward`），这是判断选项正误的常见依据。

> [!quote]- 相关内容
> - 比较结构参见 [[语法总结笔记#补充要点：比较结构 No more...than...]]。

---

### 语态：被动语态

本文是被动语态的高密度篇章，用来弱化施事者、突出事件本身。被动语态的翻译常需还原主语或补出"人们/法院"等泛指施事者。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **be + V-ed（一般过去时被动）** | 过去发生的被动动作 | some 20% of human genes were **patented** |
| **be violently agitated** | 被动 + 副词修饰，表人的状态 | Executives were violently **agitated**. |
| **be relieved** | 被动表示人的心理状态 | On July 29th they were **relieved**. |
| **were + 形容词（主系表，非被动）** | 与被动语态区分：系动词 + 形容词 | a judge ruled that genes were **unpatentable**（不是"被不可专利"） |
| **may be determined by** | 情态动词 + 被动 | Their success may be **determined** by a suit |
| **have been separated from** | 现在完成时被动 | cotton fibres that have been **separated** from cotton seeds |

> [!note] 被动语态的翻译策略
> ① 保留被动："基因被授予专利"；② 译成主动："法院推翻了裁决"；③ 译成无主句："（人们）尚未解答这一问题"。本文 `the ruling was a blessing` 是"主系表"而非被动，注意区分 `was + 名词` 与 `was + 过去分词`。

---

### 介词与连词：despite / rather than / such as / 冒号

本文的介词与连词承担了大量逻辑连接功能，尤其是让步、并列对比和举例三类。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **against + 名词短语** | 反对……；后置修饰 arguments | three main arguments against gene patents |
| **despite + 名词短语** | 尽管……（后不接从句） | Despite the appeals court's decision |
| **rather than** | 而不是；并列连词 | suppress innovation rather than reward it |
| **such as + 名词短语** | 举例，同位说明 | genetic tests such as Myriad's |
| **冒号 + 并列分句** | 解释说明前面的名词，构成"总—分"结构 | Critics make three main arguments ...: a gene is a product of nature ...; ... ; and ... |
| **so + 分句** | 并列连词，引导结果分句 | a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented |

> [!warning] 易错点：so 是并列连词，不是从属连词
> `so` 引导结果分句时属于**并列连词**（与 and/but/or 同类），前面通常有逗号，且从句结构完整；而 `so that` 才是从属连词。本文的 `so it may not be patented` 是并列分句，不能改写成 `so that`。

> [!quote]- 相关内容
> - 参见 [[语法总结笔记#介词与连词 (Prepositions & Conjunctions)]] 与 [[固定搭配与词组笔记#Such as 的两种结构（拆分式 vs 连用式）]]。

### 补充要点：同位语与主谓一致

本文大量使用逗号隔开的同位语，用来交代机构性质、人物身份，是长句"膨胀"的主要原因之一。阅读时应先删除同位语，再找主干。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **专有名词 + , a/an + 名词, + 谓语** | 同位语交代性质，不改变主干 | The Biotechnology Industry Organisation (BIO), a trade group, assured members... |
| **人名 + , a + 身份, + 谓语** | 同位语交代人物身份 | Hans Sauer, a lawyer for the BIO |
| **名词 + , a company in Utah, + 谓语** | 同位语补充公司所在地 | The chief executive of Myriad, a company in Utah, said... |
| **a number of / a growing number + 复数谓语** | `a number of + 复数名词` 谓语用复数 | A growing number seem to agree. |

> [!tip] 句法主线
> 阅读时先删除逗号之间的同位语：`The Biotechnology Industry Organisation assured members...`；再恢复 `a trade group` 这一补充信息。这个方法与 [[语法总结笔记#补充要点：插入语 (Parenthesis)]] 可联动复习。

# The Battle over Gene Patents 固定搭配与词组

> [!abstract] 说明
> 本笔记整理本篇文章中的固定搭配、词义和考研语境。句法结构详见 [[grammar-notes]]；跨篇章汇总见 [[固定搭配与词组笔记]]。

### 专利与法律诉讼表达

本组是本文的**主题词汇群**，也是考研法律、科技类文章的通用词表。注意 `patent` 的介词搭配在 `for / on / to` 间切换。

| 搭配 | 含义 | 文章语境 |
|------|------|------|
| **win / hold / file a patent for sth** | 为……赢得/持有/申请专利 | Companies had won patents for isolated DNA |
| **hold patents to two genes** | 持有两项基因的专利 | Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes |
| **rule that + 从句** | 裁定…… | a judge ruled that genes were unpatentable |
| **overturn a decision** | 推翻裁决 | A federal appeals court overturned the prior decision |
| **file a brief** | 提交（向法院的）意见陈述书；诉讼摘要 | the Department of Justice filed a brief in the Myriad case |
| **bring a suit** | 提起诉讼 | a suit ... brought by the Mayo Clinic |
| **hear a case** | 审理案件 | which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term |
| **reach the Supreme Court** | 上诉到最高法院 | The case may yet reach the Supreme Court. |
| **in its next term** | 在下一个开庭期 | the Supreme Court will hear in its next term |
| **violate a patent** | 侵犯专利 | whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents |
| **rule out sth** | 排除；裁定不予（第31题选项 D 的熟词生义） | judges to rule out gene patenting |

> [!tip] 📌 考研核心考点
> `patent` 作名词时后接 `for`（专利内容）或 `on`（专利对象），本文还出现 `patents to two genes`（`to` 表归属）。三者意思相通，命题人正是借此设计近义干扰项。`rule` 一词两义：**裁定**（rule that...）与**排除**（rule out），第31题 D 项考的就是后者。

### 观点与论证表达

本文是一篇"争议 + 多方表态"的论说文，表态类动词和论证类名词是解题定位的关键词。

| 搭配 | 含义 | 文章语境 |
|------|------|------|
| **make arguments against sth** | 提出反对……的理由 | Critics make three main arguments against gene patents |
| **a product of nature** | 自然的产物 | a gene is a product of nature |
| **suppress innovation** | 抑制创新 | gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it |
| **reward innovation** | 奖励/回报创新 | rather than reward it |
| **restrict access to sth** | 限制获得……的机会 | patents' monopolies restrict access to genetic tests |
| **urge reform for sth** | 敦促对……进行改革 | a federal task-force urged reform for patents |
| **assure sb that + 从句** | 向某人保证…… | assured members that this was just a “preliminary step” |
| **a preliminary step** | 预备步骤；第一步 | just a “preliminary step” in a longer battle |
| **be a blessing to sb** | 对某人而言是福音 | the ruling was a blessing to firms and patients alike |

> [!warning] 语义色彩
> `suppress`（压制、抑制）在本文带明显批评色彩，与褒义的 `reward` 构成对立；`restrict access to` 也是评价性表达，暗示垄断损害公共利益。第35题问作者态度，这些词正是判断"批判/客观"的依据。

### 科技与产业表达

本组词语是考研科技类阅读的通用背景词，注意抽象名词与动词的转换关系。

| 搭配 | 含义 | 文章语境 |
|------|------|------|
| **personalised medicine** | 个性化医疗 | companies continue their attempts at personalised medicine |
| **isolated DNA** | 分离出的DNA | Companies had won patents for isolated DNA |
| **the sequencing of a whole genome** | 全基因组测序 | the sequencing of a whole genome |
| **how genes interact** | 基因如何相互作用 | Firms are now studying how genes interact |
| **look for correlations** | 寻找关联性 | looking for correlations |
| **determine the causes of disease** | 确定疾病成因 | correlations that might be used to determine the causes of disease |
| **predict a drug's efficacy** | 预测药物疗效 | predict a drug's efficacy |
| **forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer** | 预测女性患乳腺癌的风险 | two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer |
| **in the public domain** | 属于公有领域；不受专利保护 | most are already patented or in the public domain |
| **connect the dots** | 把零散信息串联起来 | win patents for “connecting the dots” |

> [!note] 名词化现象
> `sequence → the sequencing`（测序）、`interact → how genes interact`（相互作用）、`interact → correlations`（关联性）体现了考研阅读的"动词名词化"趋势：抽象名词承载信息，使句子更紧凑，也更容易成为定位词的替换对象。

### 状态与程度表达

| 搭配 | 含义 | 文章语境 |
|------|------|------|
| **shake sth to its core** | 从根本上震动/动摇…… | a federal judge shook America's biotech industry to its core |
| **be violently agitated** | 极度不安；群情激愤 | Executives were violently agitated. |
| **be relieved** | 松一口气；感到宽慰 | On July 29th they were relieved |
| **remain unanswered** | 仍然悬而未决 | big questions remain unanswered |
| **remain rather busy** | 仍然相当忙碌 | the courts will remain rather busy |
| **be eager to do sth** | 渴望做某事 | Companies are eager to win patents |
| **be unlikely to do sth** | 不太可能做某事 | Companies are unlikely to file many more patents |
| **a growing number** | 越来越多的人/事物（谓语用复数） | A growing number seem to agree. |
| **each meeting was packed** | 每场会议都座无虚席（第34题考点句） | Each meeting was packed. |
| **the shifting landscape** | 不断变化的格局/形势 | coach lawyers on the shifting landscape for patents |
| **coach sb on sth** | 就……对某人进行培训/指导 | sessions to coach lawyers on the shifting landscape |

> [!warning] 易错点：agitated / packed 的感情色彩
> `agitated` 描述人时是"焦躁不安、激动"，不是"被搅动"；`packed` 描述会议是"挤满人的、座无虚席"，不是"被包装"。两者都是被动形式但词义已形容词化，考研常在此设置词义题干扰项。

### 词义辨析：文章中的高频词

| 词语 | 本文义项 | 易混/易错提醒 |
|------|------|------|
| **unpatentable** | 不可申请专利的 | 前缀 `un-` + `patent` + `-able`；对比 `patentable`（可专利的） |
| **core** | 核心；根本 | `to its core` 为固定搭配，表"从根本上的程度" |
| **trade group** | 行业团体；同业公会 | 不是"贸易集团"；本文指 BIO |
| **clinical / clinic** | 诊所；`Mayo Clinic` 为专有名词 | `clinic` 在机构名中不译作"诊所"而译作"医学中心/诊所"，`Mayo Clinic` 通译"梅奥诊所" |
| **brief** | （法律）意见陈述书；诉讼摘要 | 本文不是形容词"简短的" |
| **suppress** | 抑制；压制 | 与 `oppress`（压迫）、`repress`（压抑）辨析 |
| **monopoly** | 垄断；专营权 | 复数 `monopolies`；动词 `monopolise` |
| **efficacy** | 疗效；效力 | 与 `efficiency`（效率）形近义远 |
| **genome** | 基因组 | 区别于 `gene`（基因）；`sequencing` 搭配使用 |
| **correlation** | 关联；相关性 | 常与 `between A and B` 搭配 |
| **critic** | 批评者 | 与 `critical`（批判的）、`criticism`（批评）同根 |
| **scornful** | 轻蔑的（第35题选项 C） | 程度重于 `critical`，作者态度并未达到"轻蔑" |
| **objective** | 客观的（第35题选项 D） | 本文有褒有贬、引多方观点，态度为客观 |

### 生词练习

**一、选词填空**

从方框中选择合适的词汇填入空白处（每词限用一次）：

> 4. **monopolies**（垄断）

> 6. **relieved**（松了一口气的）

**二、短语翻译**

将下列短语翻译成中文：

1. hold patents to two genes

2. no less a product of nature than are cotton fibres

3. coach lawyers on the shifting landscape for patents

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **hold patents to two genes** = 持有两项基因的专利
> 2. **no less a product of nature than are cotton fibres** = 与棉纤维一样是自然的产物（`no less A than B` 表示"A 和 B 一样"）
> 3. **coach lawyers on the shifting landscape for patents** = 就专利领域不断变化的形势对律师进行培训

**三、语境理解**

根据上下文，选择正确的词义：

1. "the Department of Justice filed a **brief** in the Myriad case" 中 **brief** 的含义是：
   - A. 简短的（形容词）
   - B. 意见陈述书；诉讼摘要（法律文书）
   - C. 简介；概览
   - D. 暂时的

2. "**Each meeting was packed.**" 中 **packed** 的含义是：
   - A. 被打包好的
   - B. 挤满人的；座无虚席
   - C. 被装运的
   - D. 被压缩的

3. "patents' **monopolies** restrict access to genetic tests" 中 **monopolies** 的含义是：
   - A. 多种经营
   - B. 垄断；专营权
   - C. 垄断者；垄断企业
   - D. 独家产品

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **B** — `brief` 在本文是法律术语，指提交给法院的"意见陈述书／诉讼摘要"，不是形容词"简短的"。
> 2. **B** — `packed` 描述会议时指"挤满人的"，第 34 题据此推出"基因专利备受关注"，而不是"律师爱开会"。
> 3. **B** — `monopoly` 是抽象名词"垄断（权）"，复数仍指垄断状态本身；C 项指主体，与 `restrict access` 的主语不搭。

## 心得

> [!tip] 阅读技巧
> 1. **抓住裁决的时间反转**：`But in March 2010 a judge ruled that genes were unpatentable.` 与 `On July 29th they were relieved` 构成一退一进，是细节题与态度题共同的定位区间。
> 2. **按"三方立场"梳理观点**：企业（BIO）想拿专利、批评者用三条理由反对专利、司法部（意见书）倾向限制。第 31–35 题的答案几乎都能落回这三方。
> 3. **盯住焦点转移信号**：`As the industry advances, however, other suits may have an even greater impact.` 是全文的转折句，预示论述重心从"基因专利"转向"基因关联专利"。

> [!warning] 易错点
> - `no less A than B` 意为"A 和 B 一样（都是）"，`than` 后为主谓倒装，不能译成"A 不比 B 更……"。
> - `filed a brief` 中 `brief` 是法律术语"意见陈述书"，不是形容词"简短的"。
> - `Each meeting was packed.` 是第 34 题考点句，`packed` 表示"挤满人的"，据此推出"基因专利备受关注"，而非"律师爱开会"。
> - 第 35 题态度题：本文并列呈现多方观点、证据充分而作者不直接表态，属 **objective**；`critical` 是批评者而非作者的态度。

> [!question] 反思
> 如果最高法院最终判定 Mayo Clinic 案中的"基因关联"不可专利，生物技术企业的个性化医疗研发会因此放缓，还是转向别处？文章里哪些表述支持你的判断？
