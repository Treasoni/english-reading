# Pretty in Pink: The Marketing of Gender Colours 精读笔记

## 背景

本文是 **2012 年考研英语阅读 Passage 2**，主题是**性别色彩与儿童市场营销**。文章从粉色在女孩生活中的普及现象切入，通过颜色历史和“toddler”概念的商业化，揭示市场营销如何制造、放大并固化关于性别与儿童发展的观念。

> [!abstract] 文章概要
> 文章采用“日常现象 → 历史反证 → 营销机制 → 观点总结”的论证结构。作者并非单纯批评粉色，而是质疑把某种颜色或年龄分类当作“天然”现象的做法。

- **体裁**：社会文化类议论文
- **难度**：中等偏上
- **核心线索**：粉色并非天然属于女孩；性别差异和年龄分类可能是营销策略塑造的结果。

## 文章原文

# Pretty in Pink: The Marketing of Gender Colours

Pretty in pink: adult women do not remember being so obsessed with the colour, yet it is **pervasive** in our young girls' lives.

It is not that pink is **intrinsically** bad, but it is ==such a tiny slice of the rainbow== and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also repeatedly and firmly **fuses** girls' identity to appearance.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It is not that pink is **intrinsically** bad, but it is ==such a tiny slice of the rainbow== and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also repeatedly and firmly **fuses** girls' identity to appearance.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 分句一 主语 S: it（指代 pink 这一话题）
> - 分句一 谓语 V: is not
> - 分句一 表语 C: that pink is intrinsically bad（that 引导的表语从句）
> - 分句二 主语 S: it（= pink）
> - 分句二 谓语 V: is
> - 分句二 表语 C: such a tiny slice of the rainbow
> - 分句三 主语 S: it（= pink）
> - 分句三 谓语 V: fuses
> - 分句三 宾语 O: girls' identity
> - 分句三 状语 A: repeatedly and firmly；to appearance（与 fuse 构成 fuse A to B）
> - 简化句: Pink is not intrinsically bad, but it is a tiny slice of the rainbow, and it fuses girls' identity to appearance.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 表语从句 | that | 作 is not 的表语，说明"并非……" |
> | 并列连词 | but | 连接前后两个对比分句 |
> | 让步状语从句 | though | 插在并列结构中作让步：尽管粉色能赞美少女时代 |
> | 介词短语 | of | 修饰 slice，说明是彩虹的"一抹" |
> | 副词 | repeatedly and firmly | 修饰 fuses，强调反复而牢固 |
> | 介词短语 | to | 与 fuse 构成 fuse A to B，"把 A 熔合到 B 上" |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 并列句: [It is not (that pink is intrinsically bad)], but [it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow] and [it also repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance]
>   ├── 分句一: It is not + 表语从句 [that pink is intrinsically bad]
>   ├── 分句二: it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow
>   │     └── of the rainbow → 修饰 slice
>   └── 分句三: it also repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance
>         ├── O: girls' identity
>         ├── A: repeatedly and firmly
>         └── to appearance → fuse A to B
>   ※ though it may celebrate girlhood in one way → 插入的让步状语从句，只修饰分句三
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：并不是说粉色本身有什么不好，而是它不过是彩虹中那么微小的一抹；尽管它从某个角度说可以赞美少女时代，但它也一再而坚定地把女孩的身份与外表熔合在一起。
>
> **考点提示**：`not that...but (that)...` 是考研高频对比结构，but 后的 that 常省略，本句 but 后直接跟完整分句。`fuse A to B` 意为"把 A 与 B 熔合"，此处为比喻用法。though 从句插在并列结构中间，须判定它只管分句三而非全句。

Then it presents that connection, even among two-year-olds, between girls as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Then it presents that connection, even among two-year-olds, between girls as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: it（指粉色所承载的观念）
> - 谓语 V: presents
> - 宾语 O: that connection
> - 宾语补足语 C: as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence
> - 状语 A: even among two-year-olds（插入状语）
> - 简化句: It presents that connection as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 介词短语 | between | 修饰 connection，说明是"女孩之间"的联系（被插入语隔开） |
> | 插入状语 | even among two-year-olds | 强调即使在两岁孩子身上也如此 |
> | 介词短语 | as（两处，并列） | 作宾语补足语，说明把这一联系呈现为什么 |
> | 并列连词 | not only...but... | 连接 innocent 与 evidence of innocence |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 主句: Then it presents that connection as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence
>   ├── S: it
>   ├── V: presents
>   ├── O: that connection
>   │     └── between girls → 修饰 connection（被 even among two-year-olds 隔开）
>   ├── 插入状语: even among two-year-olds
>   └── 宾补 C: as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：随后，它把女孩之间的这种联系——即便在两岁孩子身上也存在——不仅呈现为天真，而且呈现为天真的证据。
>
> **考点提示**：`present A as B` 意为"把 A 呈现为 B"，本句 as 短语被 not only...but... 拆成两半，须还原结构才能读懂。`between girls` 被插入语隔开，属考研常见的"修饰语后置＋分隔"干扰。

Looking around, I despaired at the **singular** lack of imagination about girls' lives and interests.

Girls' attraction to pink may seem **unavoidable**, somehow **encoded** in their DNA, but according to Jo Paoletti, an associate professor of American Studies, it is not.

Children were not **colour-coded** at all until the early 20th century: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter, since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Children were not **colour-coded** at all until the early 20th century: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter, since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 分句一 主语 S: Children
> - 分句一 谓语 V: were not colour-coded（被动语态）
> - 分句一 状语 A: at all；until the early 20th century（not...until 结构）
> - 分句二 主语 S: all babies
> - 分句二 谓语 V: wore
> - 分句二 宾语 O: white
> - 分句二 状语 A: in the era before domestic washing machines；as a practical matter
> - 分句三 主语 S: the only way of getting clothes clean
> - 分句三 谓语 V: was
> - 分句三 表语 C: to boil them（since 引导的原因状语从句中的系表结构）
> - 简化句: Children were not colour-coded until the early 20th century, because all babies wore white and the only way to clean clothes was to boil them.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 否定＋时间状语 | not...until | 修饰主句谓语，表示"直到……才" |
> | 冒号 | : | 引出对前半句的解释说明 |
> | 介词短语 | in the era before... | 作时间状语，界定"洗衣机之前"这一时限 |
> | 介词短语 | as a practical matter | 作评注性状语，说明是出于实际考虑 |
> | 原因状语从句 | since | 解释为何所有婴儿都穿白色 |
> | 非谓语（动名词） | of getting clothes clean | 修饰 way，作后置定语 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 主句: Children were not colour-coded at all until the early 20th century
>   └── 冒号 → 解释说明: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter
>         ├── S: all babies
>         ├── V: wore / O: white
>         ├── A: in the era before domestic washing machines
>         ├── A: as a practical matter
>         └── since [the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them] → 原因状语从句
>               ├── S: the only way of getting clothes clean
>               │     └── of getting clothes clean → 修饰 way，作后置定语
>               └── V: was / C: to boil them
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：直到20世纪初，孩子们才被按颜色区分开来：在家用洗衣机出现之前的年代，出于实际考虑，所有婴儿都穿白色，因为把衣服洗净的唯一办法就是把它们煮沸。
>
> **考点提示**：`not...until` 译作"直到……才"，是考研翻译高频句式，不可译成"没有……直到"。冒号后是解释而非并列，翻译时可加"即"或"因为"。`the way of doing sth` 中 `of + 动名词短语` 作后置定语修饰 way；其中 clean 是动名词 getting 的宾语补足语（get sth clean），不要误判为修饰 clothes 的形容词。

What's more, both boys and girls wore what were thought of as **gender-neutral** dresses. When nursery colours were introduced, pink was actually considered ==the more masculine colour==, a **pastel** version of red, which was associated with strength. Blue, with its **intimations** of the Virgin Mary, **constancy** and faithfulness, **symbolised** femininity.

It was not until the mid-1980s, when **amplifying** age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy, that pink fully came into its own, when it began to seem **inherently** attractive to girls, part of what defined them as female, at least for the first few critical years.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It was not until the mid-1980s, when **amplifying** age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy, that pink fully came into its own, when it began to seem **inherently** attractive to girls, part of what defined them as female, at least for the first few critical years.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 强调句框架: It was ... that ...
> - 被强调成分: not until the mid-1980s（时间状语）
> - 主句 主语 S: pink
> - 主句 谓语 V: came into its own
> - 主句 状语 A: fully
> - 简化句: Pink fully came into its own only after the mid-1980s.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 强调句型 | It was...that... | 强调时间状语 not until the mid-1980s |
> | 定语从句 | when | 修饰 the mid-1980s，说明该年代的特征 |
> | 非谓语（动名词） | amplifying age and sex differences | 作第一个定语从句的主语 |
> | 定语从句 | when | 修饰 came into its own 所对应的时间，进一步说明粉色兴起的表现 |
> | 代词指代 | it | 指代 pink |
> | 同位补充语 | part of... | 补充说明"具天然吸引力"的性质 |
> | 名词性从句 | what | 作介词 of 的宾语，what defined them as female |
> | 介词短语 | at least for the first few critical years | 限定范围的时间状语 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 强调句: It was [not until the mid-1980s] that pink fully came into its own
>   ├── 被强调成分: not until the mid-1980s
>   │     └── 定语从句: [when amplifying age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy]
>   │           ├── S: amplifying age and sex differences（动名词短语作主语）
>   │           └── V: became / C: a dominant children's marketing strategy
>   ├── 主干: S: pink / V: came into its own / A: fully
>   └── 定语从句: [when it began to seem inherently attractive to girls, part of what defined them as female]
>         ├── S: it → 指代 pink
>         ├── V: began to seem
>         ├── C: inherently attractive to girls
>         ├── 同位补充: part of what defined them as female
>         │     └── 名词性从句: [what defined them as female] → 作 of 的宾语
>         └── A: at least for the first few critical years
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：直到20世纪80年代中期，当放大年龄与性别差异成为儿童市场营销的主导策略时，粉色才真正盛行起来；从那时起，它开始显得对女孩具有天然的吸引力，成为定义她们身为女性的因素之一，至少在最初那几个关键年份里是如此。
>
> **考点提示**：`It was not until...that...` 是强调句与 not until 叠加，按"直到……才……"翻译，不能译成"它不是……"。句中两个 when 都引导定语从句：第一个修饰 the mid-1980s，第二个修饰其前所述的时间点，须与状语从句区分。`come into its own` 意为"充分发挥价值、盛行起来"。

I had not realised how **profoundly** marketing trends **dictated** our **perception** of what is natural to kids, including our core beliefs about their psychological development.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：I had not realised how **profoundly** marketing trends **dictated** our **perception** of what is natural to kids, including our core beliefs about their psychological development.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: I
> - 谓语 V: had not realised
> - 宾语 O: how profoundly marketing trends dictated our perception of what is natural to kids（how 引导的宾语从句）
> - 从句内部 主语 S: marketing trends
> - 从句内部 谓语 V: dictated
> - 从句内部 宾语 O: our perception
> - 从句内部 状语 A: how profoundly
> - 简化句: I had not realised that marketing trends dictated our perception.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 名词性从句 | how | 作 realised 的宾语；how 修饰从句中的副词 profoundly |
> | 副词 | profoundly | 修饰 dictated，表示"深刻地" |
> | 介词短语 | of | 修饰 perception，说明认知的对象 |
> | 名词性从句 | what | 作介词 of 的宾语，相当于 the thing that is natural to kids |
> | 非谓语（现在分词） | including | 补充说明 perception 的范围 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 主句: I had not realised [宾语从句]
>   └── 宾语从句: how profoundly marketing trends dictated our perception of what is natural to kids
>         ├── A: how profoundly → 修饰 dictated
>         ├── S: marketing trends
>         ├── V: dictated
>         └── O: our perception
>               ├── of what is natural to kids → 修饰 perception
>               │     └── 名词性从句: [what is natural to kids]
>               └── including our core beliefs about their psychological development → 补充说明 perception 的范围
>                     └── about their psychological development → 修饰 beliefs
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：我此前没有意识到，营销趋势在多大程度上深刻地支配着我们对孩子天性的认知，包括我们对他们心理发展的核心看法。
>
> **考点提示**：`how + 副词` 引导的宾语从句常译作"在多大程度上……"，how 本身修饰从句内的副词。`what is natural to kids` 是介词 of 的宾语从句，不能误判为定语从句。`including...` 是分词短语作补充说明，其逻辑主语是 our perception，不是 I。

Take the **toddler**. I assumed that phase was something experts developed after years of research into children's behaviour: wrong.

Turns out, according to Daniel Cook, a historian of childhood consumerism, it was **popularised** as a marketing trick by clothing manufacturers in the 1930s.

Trade publications **counselled** department stores that, in order to increase sales, they should create a “third stepping stone” between infant wear and older kids’ clothes.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Trade publications **counselled** department stores that, in order to increase sales, they should create a “third stepping stone” between infant wear and older kids’ clothes.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Trade publications
> - 谓语 V: counselled
> - 间接宾语 O1: department stores
> - 直接宾语 O2: that they should create a “third stepping stone” between infant wear and older kids’ clothes（that 引导的宾语从句）
> - 从句内部 主语 S: they（指代 department stores）
> - 从句内部 谓语 V: should create
> - 从句内部 宾语 O: a “third stepping stone”
> - 简化句: Trade publications counselled department stores that they should create a stepping stone.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 名词性从句 | that | 作 counselled 的宾语从句，不是定语从句 |
> | 目的状语（插入） | in order to | 插在 that 与从句主语之间，说明"为了增加销量" |
> | 介词短语 | between...and... | 修饰 stepping stone，说明其所处位置 |
> | 引号比喻 | “third stepping stone” | 指介于婴儿装与大孩子服装之间的过渡品类 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 主句: Trade publications counselled department stores [宾语从句]
>   ├── S: Trade publications
>   ├── V: counselled
>   ├── O1: department stores
>   └── 宾语从句: that [in order to increase sales] they should create a “third stepping stone” between infant wear and older kids’ clothes
>         ├── 目的状语（插入）: in order to increase sales
>         ├── S: they → 指代 department stores
>         ├── V: should create
>         ├── O: a “third stepping stone”
>         └── between infant wear and older kids’ clothes → 修饰 stepping stone
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：行业刊物建议百货商店：为了增加销量，应在婴儿装和大孩子的衣服之间设置一块"第三级踏脚石"。
>
> **考点提示**：`counsel sb that...` 中 that 从句是宾语从句，不修饰 department stores；目的状语 `in order to increase sales` 插在 that 与从句主语之间造成"分隔"，是考研常见干扰项。`counsel` 在此作动词"建议"，与 advise 同义。

It was only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term that it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It was only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term that it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 强调句框架: It was ... that ...
> - 被强调成分: only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term（时间状语从句）
> - 主句 主语 S: it（指代 toddler 所代表的说法/阶段）
> - 主句 谓语 V: evolved
> - 主句 状语 A: into a broadly accepted developmental stage
> - 简化句: Toddler evolved into a developmental stage only after the word became common.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |------|------|----------------|
> | 强调句型 | It was...that... | 强调时间状语 only after... |
> | 时间状语从句 | after | 说明"只有在……之后"，从句内 "toddler" 作主语 |
> | 介词短语 | into | 与 evolve 搭配，表示"演变为" |
> | 形容词短语 | broadly accepted | 作前置定语修饰 developmental stage |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```text
> 强调句: It was [only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term] that it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage
>   ├── 被强调成分: only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term
>   │     └── 时间状语从句: after [“toddler” became a common shoppers’ term]
>   │           ├── S: “toddler” → 指"学步儿童"这一说法
>   │           └── V: became / C: a common shoppers’ term
>   └── 主干: S: it → 指代 toddler / V: evolved / A: into a broadly accepted developmental stage
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：只有在"toddler（学步儿童）"成为购物者常用的说法之后，它才演变为一个被广泛接受的发展阶段。
>
> **考点提示**：这是 `It was...that...` 强调句强调时间状语，判别方法是去掉 It was 和 that 后句子仍完整。与第2段的 `not until` 强调句同源，可对照记忆。`evolve into` 意为"演变为"，into 短语作结果状语。

Splitting kids, or adults, into ever-tinier categories has proved a **sure-fire** way to boost profits. And one of the easiest ways to **segment** a market is to **magnify** gender differences—or ==invent them where they did not previously exist==.

# Pretty in Pink: The Marketing of Gender Colours 粉色俏佳人：性别色彩的营销由来中英对照

Pretty in pink: adult women do not remember being so obsessed with the colour, yet it is **pervasive** in our young girls' lives. It is not that pink is **intrinsically** bad, but it is ==such a tiny slice of the rainbow== and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also repeatedly and firmly **fuses** girls' identity to appearance. Then it presents that connection, even among two-year-olds, between girls as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence. Looking around, I despaired at the **singular** lack of imagination about girls' lives and interests.

穿粉色的俏佳人：成年女性已不记得自己曾如此痴迷于这种颜色，然而它在我们年幼女孩的生活中却**无处不在**。这并不是说粉色本身有什么不好，而是它不过是==彩虹中那么微小的一抹==；尽管它从某个角度说可以赞美少女时代，但它也一再而坚定地把女孩的身份与外表**熔合**在一起。随后，它把这种联系——即便在两岁孩子身上也存在——不仅呈现为女孩的天真，而且呈现为天真的**证据**。环顾四周，我对人们在女孩的生活和兴趣方面**异乎寻常地**缺乏想象力感到绝望。

Girls' attraction to pink may seem **unavoidable**, somehow **encoded** in their DNA, but according to Jo Paoletti, an associate professor of American Studies, it is not. Children were not **colour-coded** at all until the early 20th century: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter, since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them. What's more, both boys and girls wore what were thought of as **gender-neutral** dresses. When nursery colours were introduced, pink was actually considered ==the more masculine colour==, a **pastel** version of red, which was associated with strength. Blue, with its **intimations** of the Virgin Mary, **constancy** and faithfulness, **symbolised** femininity. It was not until the mid-1980s, when **amplifying** age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy, that pink fully came into its own, when it began to seem **inherently** attractive to girls, part of what defined them as female, at least for the first few critical years.

女孩对粉色的喜爱似乎**不可避免**，仿佛以某种方式**编码**在她们的DNA之中；但据美国研究专业副教授乔·帕奥莱蒂（Jo Paoletti）所说，事实并非如此。直到20世纪初，孩子们才被**按颜色区分开来**：在家用洗衣机出现之前的年代，出于实际考虑，所有婴儿都穿白色，因为把衣服洗净的唯一办法就是把它们煮沸。此外，男孩和女孩都穿当时被认为**不分性别**的连衣裙。当育婴室用色被引入时，粉色实际上被视为==更偏男性化的颜色==，是红色的一种**淡雅**版本，而红色与力量相联系。蓝色则因其令人联想到圣母玛利亚、**恒心**与忠贞，**象征**着女性气质。直到20世纪80年代中期，当**放大**年龄与性别差异成为儿童市场营销的主导策略时，粉色才真正**盛行起来**——从那时起，它开始显得对女孩具有**天然的**吸引力，成为定义她们身为女性的因素之一，至少在最初那几个关键年份里是如此。

I had not realised how **profoundly** marketing trends **dictated** our **perception** of what is natural to kids, including our core beliefs about their psychological development. Take the **toddler**. I assumed that phase was something experts developed after years of research into children's behaviour: wrong. Turns out, according to Daniel Cook, a historian of childhood consumerism, it was **popularised** as a marketing trick by clothing manufacturers in the 1930s.

我此前没有意识到，营销趋势在多大程度上**深刻地支配**着我们对孩子天性的**认知**，包括我们对他们心理发展的核心看法。以**幼儿期**（toddler）为例。我原以为这个阶段是专家们在对儿童行为进行多年研究之后提出的：错了。事实是，据儿童消费史学家丹尼尔·库克（Daniel Cook）说，它是在20世纪30年代由服装制造商作为一种营销手段**推广开来**的。

Trade publications **counselled** department stores that, in order to increase sales, they should create a “third stepping stone” between infant wear and older kids’ clothes. It was only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term that it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage. Splitting kids, or adults, into ever-tinier categories has proved a **sure-fire** way to boost profits. And one of the easiest ways to **segment** a market is to **magnify** gender differences—or ==invent them where they did not previously exist==.

行业刊物**建议**百货商店：为了增加销量，应在婴儿装和大孩子的衣服之间设置一块“第三级踏脚石”。只有在“toddler（学步儿童）”成为购物者常用的说法之后，它才演变为一个被广泛接受的发展阶段。把孩子——或者成年人——不断切分成越来越细的类别，已被证明是**稳赚不赔**的提升利润之道。而**细分**市场最容易的办法之一，就是**放大**性别差异——或者在原本并不存在这些差异的地方==把它们凭空造出来==。

## 语法要点

# Pretty in Pink: The Marketing of Gender Colours 语法整理

> [!abstract] 说明
> 本笔记根据文章原文中已标注的长难句分析，并补充其余句子的考研语法考点整理。语法结构与例句均来自本篇文章；固定搭配的词义、语体和辨析另见 [[固定搭配与词组笔记]]。

### 从句：表语从句与对比结构

`It is not that...` 用于否定某种表面解释，后接 `but` 引出真正的原因或评价。`but` 后的 `that` 常省略。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **It is not that + 从句, but + 分句** | 不是因为/并非……，而是…… | It is not that pink is intrinsically bad, but it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow. |
| **though + 完整句子** | 引导让步状语从句，可插入并列结构内部 | though it may celebrate girlhood in one way |
| **not only A but (also) B** | 连接并列表语或补足语 | not only innocent but as evidence of innocence |

> [!tip] 📌 考研核心考点
> `not that...but...` 不能机械译为“不是那个……而是那个……”。先确认 `that` 从句是 `is not` 的表语，再看 `but` 后的真正判断。

> [!quote]- 相关内容
> - 让步逻辑参见 [[语法总结笔记#状语从句：让步状语从句]]。
> - 并列结构参见 [[语法总结笔记#介词与连词：并列与对比]]。

---

### 从句：宾语从句与复杂宾语

文章多次使用动词后接从句或 `A as B` 结构。阅读时先找主句谓语，再判断后面的内容是宾语从句还是宾语补足语。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **present A as B** | 将 A 呈现为 B；`as` 短语作宾语补足语 | It presents that connection as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence. |
| **counsel sb that + 从句** | 建议某人……；`that` 引导宾语从句 | Trade publications counselled department stores that they should create a “third stepping stone”. |
| **assume that + 从句** | 认为/假定…… | I assumed that phase was something experts developed after years of research into children's behaviour. |
| **how + 副词 + 主语 + 谓语** | `how` 引导程度宾语从句，说明“在多大程度上” | I had not realised how profoundly marketing trends dictated our perception. |
| **what + 谓语** | `what` 引导名词性从句，兼有先行词和关系词功能 | our perception of what is natural to kids |

> [!warning] 易错点：What 与 that
> `what is natural to kids` 本身是完整的名词性从句，`what` 在从句中作主语；不能把它当作只起连接作用的 `that`，也不能改写成缺少成分的定语从句。

---

### 从句：定语从句

定语从句可修饰名词，也可通过 `what` 形成名词性从句。注意非限制性定语从句前的逗号，以及长句中修饰语与中心词之间的距离。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **名词 + which + 完整/不完整从句** | 非限制性定语从句，补充说明前面的名词 | a pastel version of red, which was associated with strength |
| **what + be/谓语** | 不是普通定语从句，而是名词性从句 | part of what defined them as female |
| **what + be thought of as + 名词** | `what` 引导的名词性结构，整体作宾语 | what were thought of as gender-neutral dresses |
| **where + 主语 + 谓语** | 指代“在……的地方/情形中”，补充说明前面的地点或范围 | invent them where they did not previously exist |

> [!tip] 📌 断句方法
> 看到逗号后的 `which`，先回看其先行词；看到 `what`，先判断它在从句中是否充当主语、宾语或表语。两者不能混为一谈。

---

### 从句：状语从句

文章中的 `though`、`since`、`after` 和 `when` 分别表达让步、原因和时间关系。状语从句内部应具备完整的主谓结构。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **though + 主语 + 谓语** | 让步：尽管…… | though it may celebrate girlhood in one way |
| **since + 主语 + 谓语** | 原因：因为/既然…… | since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them |
| **after + 主语 + 谓语** | 时间：在……之后 | after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term |
| **when + 主语 + 谓语** | 时间或背景说明 | when nursery colours were introduced |
| **when + 动名词短语 + 谓语** | `when` 从句中以动名词短语作主语 | when amplifying age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy |

> [!note] 标点与逻辑
> 冒号后的内容 `in the era before domestic washing machines...` 是对前句的解释说明；其中 `since` 从句进一步说明婴儿穿白色的原因，不要把冒号后的内容误读成独立并列论据。

---

### 非谓语动词：动名词作主语与宾语

动名词可以作主语、宾语或介词后的宾语。遇到 `of + V-ing`，通常先判断它是否作名词的后置定语。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **remember + V-ing** | 记得做过某事 | adult women do not remember being so obsessed with the colour |
| **V-ing 作主语** | 动名词短语整体作主语，谓语通常用单数 | Splitting kids, or adults, into ever-tinier categories has proved a sure-fire way to boost profits. |
| **of + V-ing** | 作名词的后置定语，说明内容或方式 | the only way of getting clothes clean |
| **in order to + 动词原形** | 作目的状语 | in order to increase sales |
| **to + 动词原形** | 作表语或不定式补语 | the only way ... was to boil them；one of the easiest ways ... is to magnify gender differences |
| **Looking around** | 现在分词短语作伴随/方式状语，逻辑主语为主句主语 I | Looking around, I despaired at the singular lack of imagination. |

> [!warning] 易错点：动名词短语的内部结构
> `getting clothes clean` 中 `clean` 是 `get + 宾语 + 宾补` 结构的宾语补足语，不是修饰 `clothes` 的定语。可还原为 `get clothes clean`（把衣服弄干净）。

---

### 非谓语动词：分词与过去分词

分词可作后置补充说明、主语补足语或被动结构的一部分。判断时要区分“被动”与“主动进行”。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **过去分词 + 介词短语** | 作主语补充说明，表示被动状态 | Girls' attraction to pink may seem unavoidable, somehow encoded in their DNA. |
| **be + 过去分词** | 被动语态 | Children were not colour-coded at all until the early 20th century. |
| **including + 名词短语** | 现在分词短语作补充说明 | including our core beliefs about their psychological development |
| **broadly accepted** | 过去分词作前置定语 | a broadly accepted developmental stage |

> [!tip] 逻辑主语
> `somehow encoded in their DNA` 的逻辑主语是 `Girls' attraction to pink`；`including our core beliefs...` 补充说明 `our perception` 的范围，不是修饰主语 `I` 的动作。

---

### 语态：被动语态与复合谓语

被动语态突出动作承受者，在历史叙述和说明文中尤其常见。`were thought of as` 是“被认为是”的被动表达。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **be + V-ed** | 一般过去时被动 | Children were not colour-coded. |
| **be introduced** | “被引入/被采用” | When nursery colours were introduced... |
| **be considered + 表语** | 被视为…… | pink was actually considered the more masculine colour |
| **be thought of as + 名词** | 被认为是…… | dresses that were thought of as gender-neutral |
| **be popularised as + 名词** | 被作为……推广开来 | it was popularised as a marketing trick by clothing manufacturers |

> [!note] 主动还原
> `what were thought of as gender-neutral dresses` 可还原为 `dresses that people thought of as gender-neutral`。被动结构使 `people` 等施事者退居背景。

---

### 倒装与强调：It was ... that ...

`It was ... that ...` 是强调句，去掉 `It was` 和 `that` 后，剩下的句子仍应语法完整。文章两处强调时间状语，并与 `not until/only after` 结合。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **It was not until + 时间 + that + 分句** | 强调“直到……才……” | It was not until the mid-1980s ... that pink fully came into its own. |
| **It was only after + 从句 + that + 分句** | 强调“只有在……之后才……” | It was only after “toddler” became a common shoppers’ term that it evolved into a broadly accepted developmental stage. |
| **not...until** | 普通否定时间结构，表示“直到……才……” | Children were not colour-coded at all until the early 20th century. |

> [!warning] 强调句不是形式主语句
> `It was not until...that...` 中 `it` 不指代具体事物；若去掉强调框架，核心句是 `pink fully came into its own`。翻译时整体处理为“直到……才……”，不能译成“它不是……”。

> [!quote]- 相关内容
> - 参见 [[固定搭配与词组笔记#状态转化与结果表达]]，复习 `come into its own` 与 `evolve into` 的词义。

---

### 补充要点：主谓一致与并列结构

长句中插入语、并列成分和 `one of + 复数名词` 容易遮蔽真正的主语。谓语应根据核心主语判断。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **one of the + 复数名词 + is + to do** | “……之一是……”；核心主语是 one | one of the easiest ways to segment a market is to magnify gender differences |
| **V-ing 短语 + has + 过去分词** | 动名词短语作单数主语 | Splitting kids, or adults, into ever-tinier categories has proved... |
| **插入语：A, or B, ...** | 对前面的名词作补充或改述，不改变主干 | Splitting kids, or adults, into ever-tinier categories... |
| **主句 + 逗号 + 插入评注** | 插入人物身份或来源信息 | according to Jo Paoletti, an associate professor of American Studies |

> [!tip] 句法主线
> 阅读时先删除逗号中的插入语：`Splitting kids into categories has proved...`；再恢复 `or adults` 等补充信息。这个方法与 [[语法总结笔记#补充要点：插入语]] 可联动复习。

**三、语境理解**

根据上下文，选择粗体词在原文中的准确含义：

1. “it is **pervasive** in our young girls' lives” 中 **pervasive** 的含义是：
   - A. 短暂的
   - B. 无处不在的
   - C. 令人厌恶的
   - D. 难以理解的

2. “a **pastel** version of red” 中 **pastel** 的含义是：
   - A. 深红色的
   - B. 金属质感的
   - C. 淡雅柔和的
   - D. 完全透明的

3. “the **singular** lack of imagination” 中 **singular** 的含义是：
   - A. 单数的
   - B. 唯一合法的
   - C. 异乎寻常的
   - D. 数量很少的

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **B** — `pervasive` 强调某事物广泛渗透、到处存在。
> 2. **C** — `pastel` 指色彩柔和、饱和度较低的淡色。
> 3. **C** — `singular` 在此处是熟词生义，表示“非同寻常的”，不是“单数的”。

## 心得

> [!tip] 阅读技巧
> 1. **抓转折和反证**：`but according to... it is not`、`wrong` 等表达会修正前文的直觉判断，常是主旨和细节题的定位点。
> 2. **关注历史时间线**：文章用“20 世纪初—20 世纪 80 年代中期—20 世纪 30 年代”等时间节点证明“性别色彩”和“toddler 阶段”并非自古固定。
> 3. **识别作者的批评对象**：作者批评的不是粉色本身，而是商业力量把颜色、年龄和性别身份绑定，并将其包装成自然事实。

> [!warning] 易错点
> - `It was not until...that...` 是强调句，整体译为“直到……才……”，不要把 `it` 当作指代具体事物。
> - `what were thought of as gender-neutral dresses` 中 `what` 引导名词性结构，`were thought of as` 是被动表达。
> - `one of the easiest ways` 的核心主语是 `one`，谓语用单数；不要被后面的复数名词干扰。

> [!question] 反思
> 如果文章删去关于颜色历史和 “toddler” 营销来源的证据，只保留第一段，作者的批评是否仍然成立？为什么？
