选词填空 + 短语翻译

1 选词填空（每词限用一次）
live off clientele rocketed subsidy frankly cries poor sightseers take in wing
1.Many college graduates have to ______ their parents until they find a job.
2.The restaurant's regular ______ includes many celebrities.
3.The company ______ to avoid paying taxes despite plenty of revenue.
4.The price of housing has ______ in recent years.
5.I must ______ say that I don't agree with your opinion.
答案（折叠展示）
1. live off（依靠……生活）
2. clientele（顾客群）
3. cries poor（哭穷）
4. rocketed（飞涨）
5. frankly（坦白地）
2 短语翻译
bedding down for the night on the flagstones → 露宿在石板路上过夜
break attendance records for three years in a row → 连续三年打破上座率纪录
take on new responsibilities vs take over the business → 承担新责任 vs 接管业务

A. 吸收信息 B. 欺骗 C. 容纳 D. 参观游览 ✓
