# 2009 Passage 3 精读笔记

## 背景

本文讨论**正规教育**与贫困国家**经济增长**之间的关系。作者反驳“教育应当是贫困国家快速经济发展的最高优先事项之一”的传统观点，指出在岗培训同样能够显著提升劳动生产率；同时，持续的经济增长又会反过来推动教育发展。

> [!abstract] 文章概要
> 文章采用“传统观点 → 事实反例 → 历史解释 → 综合结论”的论证结构。核心结论是：正规教育对复杂政治制度和摆脱贫困陷阱十分重要，但短期生产率提升并不必然以正规教育为前提。

- **体裁**：议论文（argumentation）
- **主题**：教育、生产率与经济发展
- **难度**：intermediate
- **文章来源**：2009 年考研英语阅读资料，Passage 3


---

## 文章原文

The relationship between formal education and economic growth in poor countries is widely misunderstood by economists and politicians alike. Progress in both areas is undoubtedly necessary for the social, political, and intellectual development of these and all other societies; however, the conventional view that education should be one of the very highest priorities for promoting rapid economic development in poor countries is wrong.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Progress in both areas is undoubtedly necessary for the social, political, and intellectual development of these and all other societies; however, the conventional view that education should be one of the very highest priorities for promoting rapid economic development in poor countries is wrong.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Progress in both areas
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: necessary for the social, political, and intellectual development of these and all other societies
> - 转折分句主语 S: the conventional view
> - 转折分句谓语 V: is
> - 转折分句补语 C: wrong
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语（介短） | in | 修饰主句主语 Progress，说明“两个领域” |
> | 介词短语（介短） | for | 修饰 necessary，说明进步的作用对象 |
> | 同位语从句（名从） | that | 解释 view 的具体内容 |
> | 非谓语短语 | for promoting | 修饰 priorities，表示“用于促进……” |
> | 介词短语（介短） | in poor countries | 修饰 economic development |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: Progress in both areas + is + necessary ...
>   ├── 介短: (in both areas) → 修饰 Progress
>   ├── 介短: (for the ... development) → 修饰 necessary
>   └── 转折分句: however, the conventional view + is + wrong
>         └── 同位语从句: (that education should be ... priorities)
>               └── 非谓语: (for promoting rapid economic development ...)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：两个领域的进步无疑对这些社会以及其他所有社会的社会、政治和智力发展都是必要的；然而，认为教育应当成为促进贫困国家快速经济发展的最高优先事项之一的传统观点是错误的。
>
> **考点提示**：分号连接两个独立分句，however 表示转折；that 从句是 view 的同位语从句；for promoting 是介词 for 加动名词结构。

We are fortunate that it is, because building new educational systems there and putting enough people through them to improve economic performance would require two or three generations.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：We are fortunate that it is, because building new educational systems there and putting enough people through them to improve economic performance would require two or three generations.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: We
> - 谓语 V: are
> - 补语 C: fortunate
> - 原因状语从句主语 S: building new educational systems there and putting enough people through them
> - 原因状语从句谓语 V: would require
> - 原因状语从句宾语 O: two or three generations
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 名词性从句（名从） | that | 作 fortunate 的补充说明；it 指代前句“这种观点是错误的” |
> | 状语从句（状从） | because | 表示“幸运”的原因 |
> | 非谓语短语 | building... and putting... | because 从句的并列主语 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | through them | 修饰 putting，them 指 educational systems |
> | 非谓语短语 | to improve economic performance | 表示目的，修饰 putting enough people through them |
> | 副词 | there | 修饰 building，指在贫困国家当地 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: We + are + fortunate
>   ├── 名从: (that it is [wrong]) → 说明 fortunate 的具体内容
>   └── 原因状从: (because ... would require two or three generations)
>         ├── 并列主语: building ... and putting ...
>         │     └── 目的非谓语: (to improve economic performance)
>         └── 谓语: would require + 宾语: two or three generations
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：幸运的是，这种观点确实是错误的，因为在那里建立新的教育体系，并让足够多的人接受教育以改善经济表现，需要两三代人的时间。
>
> **考点提示**：that 从句补充说明形容词 fortunate；because 引导原因状语从句；两个动名词短语 building 和 putting 构成并列主语；would require 表示在假设情境下的结果。

The findings of a research institution have consistently shown that workers in all countries can be trained on the job to achieve radically higher productivity and, as a result, radically higher standards of living.

Ironically, the first evidence for this idea appeared in the United States. Not long ago, with the country entering a recession and Japan at its pre-bubble peak, the U.S. workforce was **derided** as poorly educated and one of the primary causes of the poor U.S. economic performance.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Not long ago, with the country entering a recession and Japan at its pre-bubble peak, the U.S. workforce was derided as poorly educated and one of the primary causes of the poor U.S. economic performance.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 状语 A: Not long ago
> - 主语 S: the U.S. workforce
> - 谓语 V: was derided
> - 补语 C: as poorly educated and one of the primary causes of the poor U.S. economic performance
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 独立主格结构 | with | 说明主句发生时的背景 |
> | 非谓语短语 | entering a recession | country 的逻辑动作，作独立主格中的补足成分 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | at its pre-bubble peak | 说明 Japan 所处状态 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | as | 引出 workforce 被嘲讽的身份或原因 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | of | 修饰 causes，说明原因所针对的对象 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: the U.S. workforce + was derided + as ...
>   ├── 时间状语: (Not long ago)
>   ├── 独立主格: (with the country entering a recession
>   │              and Japan at its pre-bubble peak)
>   └── 并列补语: poorly educated + one of the primary causes ...
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：不久前，正值美国步入衰退期、日本处于泡沫破裂前的峰值之际，美国劳动力被嘲讽为受教育程度低，并被视为美国经济表现不佳的主要原因之一。
>
> **考点提示**：with + 名词 + 分词/介词短语构成独立主格结构；was derided as...表示“被嘲讽为/被认为是……”；poorly educated 与 one of... 是并列补语。

Japan was, and remains, the global leader in automotive-assembly productivity. Yet the research revealed that the U.S. factories of Honda, Nissan, and Toyota achieved about 95 percent of the productivity of their Japanese counterparts—a result of the training that U.S. workers received on the job.

More recently, while examining housing construction, the researchers discovered that illiterate, non-English-speaking Mexican workers in Houston, Texas, consistently met best-practice labor productivity standards despite the complexity of the building industry's work.

What is the real relationship between education and economic development? We have to suspect that continuing economic growth promotes the development of education even when governments don't force it. After all, that's how education got started. When our ancestors were hunters and gatherers 10,000 years ago, they didn't have time to wonder much about anything besides finding food. Only when humanity began to get its food in a more productive way was there time for other things.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Only when humanity began to get its food in a more productive way was there time for other things.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 状语 A: Only when humanity began to get its food in a more productive way
> - 谓语 V: was
> - 主语 S: time for other things
> - 存现引导词 there: 引出主语 time
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句（状从） | when | 说明“有时间”发生的条件或时间 |
> | 副词 | only | 修饰 when 从句，表示“只有……” |
> | 非谓语/动词不定式结构 | to get its food | 作 began 的补足成分 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | in a more productive way | 修饰 get，说明获取食物的方式 |
> | 介词短语（介短） | for other things | 修饰 time，说明时间用途 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 正常语序: There was time for other things
>   └── 条件/时间状从: (only when humanity began to get its food ...)
>         ├── 主语: humanity
>         ├── 谓语: began
>         └── 补足成分: to get its food in a more productive way
> 倒装语序: Only when ... + was + there + time ...
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：只有当人类开始以更高效的方式获取食物时，才有时间做其他事情。
>
> **考点提示**：Only + 状语从句置于句首时，主句采用部分倒装，将谓语 was 提到主语 there 之前；不要把 when 从句误判为主句。

As education improved, humanity's productivity potential increased as well. When the competitive environment pushed our ancestors to achieve that potential, they could in turn afford more education. This increasingly high level of education is probably a necessary, but not a sufficient, condition for the complex political systems required by advanced economic performance.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：This increasingly high level of education is probably a necessary, but not a sufficient, condition for the complex political systems required by advanced economic performance.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: This increasingly high level of education
> - 状语 A: probably
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 补语 C: a necessary, but not a sufficient, condition
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语（介短） | for | 修饰 condition，说明该条件对应的对象 |
> | 非谓语短语（过去分词） | required by advanced economic performance | 修饰 political systems，等同于 which are required by... |
> | 介词短语（介短） | by | 修饰 required，表示“由……所要求” |
> | 插入成分 | but not | 补充限制 sufficient，构成必要但不充分的对比 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: This increasingly high level of education + is + a ... condition
>   ├── 副词: probably
>   ├── 对比插入: necessary, but not sufficient
>   └── 介短: (for the complex political systems)
>         └── 过去分词短语: (required by advanced economic performance)
>               └── 介短: (by advanced economic performance)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：这种不断提高的教育水平，很可能是复杂政治制度的必要条件，但不是充分条件；而这些制度正是先进经济运行所需要的。
>
> **考点提示**：necessary but not sufficient 是“必要但不充分”的固定逻辑表达；required by...是过去分词短语作后置定语，可还原为 which are required by...。

Thus poor countries might not be able to escape their poverty traps without political changes that may be possible only with broader formal education.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Thus poor countries might not be able to escape their poverty traps without political changes that may be possible only with broader formal education.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 状语 A: Thus
> - 主语 S: poor countries
> - 谓语 V: might not be able to escape（might + not + be able to + 动词原形）
> - 宾语 O: their poverty traps
> - 条件状语 A: without political changes（修饰“逃脱”这一谓语，表示在缺少该条件的情况下）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 副词 | Thus | 修饰整个主句，表示前文推论结果“因此” |
> | 介词短语（介短） | without political changes | 修饰“逃脱”这一谓语，表示“在没有政治变革的情况下” |
> | 定语从句（定从） | that | 修饰 political changes；that 在从句中作主语 |
> | 情态结构 | may be possible | 位于定语从句中，表示“可能是可行的” |
> | 介词短语（介短） | only with broader formal education | 作 possible 的条件补足语；only 限定 with 短语，表示“只有在有/借助……的条件下才可能” |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: poor countries + might not be able to escape + their poverty traps
>   ├── 结果状语: Thus
>   └── 条件介短: (without political changes)
>         └── 定语从句: (that may be possible only with broader formal education)
>               ├── 主语: that → 指代 political changes
>               ├── 系表结构: may be + possible
>               └── 条件介短: (only with broader formal education) → 表示 possible 的必要条件
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：因此，贫困国家若没有政治变革，可能就无法摆脱贫困陷阱；而这种政治变革也许只有通过更广泛的正规教育才有可能实现。
>
> **考点提示**：that 引导定语从句，先行词是 political changes，且 that 在从句中作主语，而不是名词性从句；without + 名词表示“没有/如果没有……”的条件；only with...表示“只有通过……才有可能”，注意 only 限定 with 短语以及 may be possible 的情态含义。

A lack of formal education, however, doesn't constrain the ability of the developing world's workforce to substantially improve productivity for the foreseeable future. On the contrary, constraints on improving productivity explain why education isn't developing more quickly there than it is.

## 翻译对照

The relationship between formal education and economic growth in poor countries is widely misunderstood by economists and politicians alike. Progress in both areas is undoubtedly necessary for the social, political, and intellectual development of these and all other societies; however, the conventional view that education should be one of the very highest priorities for promoting rapid economic development in poor countries is wrong. We are fortunate that it is, because building new educational systems there and putting enough people through them to improve economic performance would require two or three generations. The findings of a research institution have consistently shown that workers in all countries can be trained on the job to achieve radically higher productivity and, as a result, radically higher standards of living.

经济学家和政治家都普遍误解了正规教育与贫困国家经济增长之间的关系。这两个领域的进步无疑对这些社会以及其他所有社会的社会、政治和智力发展都是必要的；然而，认为教育应当成为促进贫困国家快速经济发展的最高优先事项之一的传统观点是错误的。幸运的是，这种观点确实是错误的，因为在那里建立新的教育体系，并让足够多的人接受教育以改善经济表现，需要两三代人的时间。一家研究机构的研究结果一贯表明，各国工人都可以通过在岗培训实现生产率的大幅提高，进而显著提高生活水平。

Ironically, the first evidence for this idea appeared in the United States. Not long ago, with the country entering a recession and Japan at its pre-bubble peak, the U.S. workforce was derided as poorly educated and one of the primary causes of the poor U.S. economic performance. Japan was, and remains, the global leader in automotive-assembly productivity. Yet the research revealed that the U.S. factories of Honda, Nissan, and Toyota achieved about 95 percent of the productivity of their Japanese counterparts—a result of the training that U.S. workers received on the job.

具有讽刺意味的是，这一观点最初的证据出现在美国。不久前，美国正步入衰退期，而日本处于泡沫破裂前的峰值，美国劳动力因受教育程度低而遭到嘲讽，并被认为是美国经济表现不佳的主要原因之一。日本过去是、现在仍然是全球汽车装配生产率的领先者。然而，研究显示，本田、日产和丰田在美国的工厂达到了其日本同类工厂约95%的生产率——这是美国工人接受在岗培训的结果。

More recently, while examining housing construction, the researchers discovered that illiterate, non-English-speaking Mexican workers in Houston, Texas, consistently met best-practice labor productivity standards despite the complexity of the building industry's work.

最近，研究人员在考察住房建设时发现，得克萨斯州休斯敦市那些不识字且不会说英语的墨西哥工人，尽管建筑业的工作十分复杂，却始终达到了最佳实践劳动生产率标准。

What is the real relationship between education and economic development? We have to suspect that continuing economic growth promotes the development of education even when governments don't force it. After all, that's how education got started. When our ancestors were hunters and gatherers 10,000 years ago, they didn't have time to wonder much about anything besides finding food. Only when humanity began to get its food in a more productive way was there time for other things.

教育与经济发展之间的真实关系是什么？我们不得不认为，即使政府不强制推动，持续的经济增长也会促进教育的发展。毕竟，教育就是这样开始的。1万年前，我们的祖先还是狩猎者和采集者时，他们没有时间去思考寻找食物以外的事情。只有当人类开始以更高效的方式获取食物时，才有时间做其他事情。

As education improved, humanity's productivity potential increased as well. When the competitive environment pushed our ancestors to achieve that potential, they could in turn afford more education. This increasingly high level of education is probably a necessary, but not a sufficient, condition for the complex political systems required by advanced economic performance. Thus poor countries might not be able to escape their poverty traps without political changes that may be possible only with broader formal education. A lack of formal education, however, doesn't constrain the ability of the developing world's workforce to substantially improve productivity for the foreseeable future. On the contrary, constraints on improving productivity explain why education isn't developing more quickly there than it is.

随着教育水平的提高，人类的生产率潜力也随之增加。当竞争环境推动我们的祖先去实现这种潜力时，他们反过来就能够负担得起更多教育。这种不断提高的教育水平，很可能是建立先进经济表现所需要的复杂政治制度的必要条件，但不是充分条件。因此，贫困国家可能无法摆脱贫困陷阱，除非进行政治变革；而这种变革也许只有通过更广泛的正规教育才有可能实现。然而，在可预见的未来，缺乏正规教育并不会限制发展中国家劳动力大幅提高生产率的能力。恰恰相反，阻碍生产率提高的因素解释了为什么那里的教育发展没有更快。

## 语法要点

---

### 并列结构 (Parallel Structures)

文章多处用并列成分压缩信息。阅读时先识别 and 连接的同层结构，再判断它们共同承担的句法功能。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| A, B, and C | 三项并列，保持词性和功能一致 | the **social, political, and intellectual** development |
| V-ing and V-ing | 两个动名词短语并列作主语 | **building** new educational systems there and **putting** enough people through them |
| be + C1 and C2 | 一个被动谓语后接两个并列补语 | was derided as **poorly educated and one of the primary causes** |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 遇到长句中的多个 `and`，先判断它连接的是单词、短语还是分句。`building...` 与 `putting...` 共同构成 because 从句的主语，不要把 putting 误判为新的谓语。

---

### 从句：定语从句 (Attributive Clauses)

定语从句通常紧跟名词，为先行词补充限定或说明。本文中的定语从句既有限制性修饰，也有过去分词短语形式的定语压缩。

| 类型 | 引导词/形式 | 例句 | 修饰对象 |
|------|-------------|------|----------|
| 限制性定语从句 | that | political changes **that may be possible only with broader formal education** | political changes |
| 过去分词短语作后置定语 | required by | the complex political systems **required by advanced economic performance** | political systems |
| 关系代词作主语 | that | changes **that may be possible** | that 指代 changes，并在从句中作主语 |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `that may be possible...` 紧跟 `political changes`，是修饰 changes 的定语从句，不是名词性从句。判断方法是看 that 是否指代前面的名词并在从句中充当成分。

> [!note] 联动复习
> 过去分词后置定语可还原为定语从句：`systems required by advanced economic performance` = `systems which are required by advanced economic performance`。可结合 [[#非谓语动词 (Non-finite Verbs)]] 一并复习。

---

### 从句：状语从句 (Adverbial Clauses)

状语从句说明时间、原因或条件，常位于主句前后。识别从句边界后，先提取主句主干，再处理从句内部结构。

| 类型 | 引导词 | 例句 | 逻辑关系 |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| 原因状语从句 | because | We are fortunate that it is, **because** building new educational systems there... | 原因 |
| 时间状语从句 | when | **When** our ancestors were hunters and gatherers 10,000 years ago, they didn't have time... | 时间 |
| 时间/条件状语从句 | only when | **Only when** humanity began to get its food in a more productive way was there time... | “只有当……才……” |
| 让步结构 | despite | ...met best-practice labor productivity standards **despite** the complexity of the building industry's work. | 尽管 |

> [!tip] 阅读方法
> `Only when...` 既标记从句，又会触发主句倒装；它与 [[#倒装与强调 (Inversion & Emphasis)]] 是联动考点。`despite` 后接名词或动名词，不接完整句子。

---

### 从句：名词性从句 (Noun Clauses)

名词性从句在句中充当宾语、表语或形容词的补充说明。本文的 `that` 从句需要结合前面的中心词判断其功能。

| 类型 | 引导词 | 例句 | 句法功能 |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| 同位语从句 | that | the conventional view **that education should be one of the very highest priorities...** | 解释 view 的具体内容 |
| 形容词补充说明 | that | We are fortunate **that it is** | 补充说明 fortunate；it 指代前文观点是错误的 |
| 宾语从句 | that | The research revealed **that the U.S. factories... achieved about 95 percent...** | 作 revealed 的宾语 |
| 宾语从句 | that | We have to suspect **that continuing economic growth promotes the development of education** | 作 suspect 的宾语 |
| 宾语从句 | why | constraints on improving productivity explain **why education isn't developing more quickly there than it is** | 作 explain 的宾语 |

> [!warning] `that` 的功能辨析
> `view that...` 中的 that 从句解释名词 view，属于同位语从句；`revealed that...` 和 `suspect that...` 中的 that 从句分别作动词的宾语。不要仅凭 that 一个词判断从句类型。

---

### 非谓语动词 (Non-finite Verbs)

非谓语动词在文章中用于压缩原因、目的、伴随和后置修饰信息。判断时重点关注逻辑主语及主动/被动关系。

| 形式 | 用法 | 例句 | 逻辑关系 |
|------|------|------|----------|
| V-ing | 动名词短语作主语 | **building** new educational systems there and **putting** enough people through them | 两个动作共同作 because 从句主语 |
| V-ing | 现在分词短语作背景/伴随 | with the country **entering** a recession | country 主动进入衰退 |
| V-ing | 省略主语的分词短语 | while **examining** housing construction | researchers 在考察住房建设时 |
| to V | 作目的状语 | putting enough people through them **to improve** economic performance | 表示目的 |
| to V | 动词补足成分 | humanity began **to get** its food in a more productive way | 作 began 的补足成分 |
| V-ed | 过去分词作后置定语 | political systems **required** by advanced economic performance | 被先进经济表现所要求 |

> [!tip] 逻辑主语判断
> `while examining housing construction` 的逻辑主语是主句主语 researchers；`with the country entering...` 中 entering 的逻辑主语是 country。分词短语的逻辑主语必须能在上下文中合理对应。

> [!note] 联动复习
> `required by...` 是过去分词短语作后置定语，和 [[#从句：定语从句 (Attributive Clauses)]] 中的 which are required by... 相互转换。

---

### 被动语态 (Passive Voice)

被动语态突出动作承受者，常见结构为 `be + 过去分词`，也可与情态动词、补语和介词短语共同出现。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be + V-ed | 一般过去时被动 | the U.S. workforce **was derided** as poorly educated |
| can be + V-ed | 情态动词后的被动 | workers in all countries **can be trained** on the job |
| be + V-ed + as + C | 被动谓语后接身份/性质补语 | was **derided as** poorly educated |
| be required by | “被……所要求” | political systems **required by** advanced economic performance |

> [!tip] 翻译技巧
> 英文被动句不必机械翻译成“被……”。`workers can be trained on the job` 可译为“工人可以接受在岗培训”，`was derided as...` 可根据语境译为“被嘲讽为/被认为是……”。

---

### 倒装与强调 (Inversion & Emphasis)

以 `Only + 状语` 开头时，主句通常采用部分倒装，将助动词、情态动词或 be 动词置于主语前。本文还出现了 there 引导的存现结构。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| Only + 状语从句 + be + S | “只有……才……”的部分倒装 | **Only when** humanity began to get its food in a more productive way **was there** time for other things. |
| There + be + S | 存现句，there 不是真正主语 | **There was** time for other things. |

> [!warning] 语序还原
> `Only when humanity began... was there time...` 的正常语序是 `There was time for other things only when humanity began...`。不要把 when 从句当成主句，也不要把 there 当作真正主语。

> [!note] 联动复习
> 倒装由句首的 `Only` 触发；时间从句由 `when` 引导。做题时先切分从句，再判断倒装主句，可结合 [[#从句：状语从句 (Adverbial Clauses)]] 复习。

---

### 介词与连词 (Prepositions & Conjunctions)

介词和连接词决定句子之间的逻辑关系，也是翻译长难句时的重要线索。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| however | 句间转折副词 | ...societies; **however**, the conventional view... |
| as a result | 结果连接语 | ...higher productivity and, **as a result**, radically higher standards of living. |
| on the contrary | 反向转折连接语 | **On the contrary**, constraints on improving productivity explain... |
| without + 名词 | “没有/若没有”，表示条件缺失 | ...escape their poverty traps **without political changes**... |
| only with + 名词 | “只有借助/在……条件下” | ...may be possible **only with broader formal education** |
| despite + 名词 | “尽管”，表示让步 | ...standards **despite the complexity** of the building industry's work |
| on the job | 在岗、工作中 | can be trained **on the job** |

> [!tip] 逻辑词定位
> `however`、`as a result` 和 `on the contrary` 分别提示转折、结果和反向论证。先圈出逻辑词，再判断句间关系，能减少长句翻译时的逻辑错位。

---

### 固定搭配与词组 (Fixed Collocations)

以下搭配直接来自文章，适合结合语境记忆。固定搭配的识别有助于避免逐词直译。

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be widely misunderstood | 被普遍误解 | The relationship ... is widely misunderstood. |
| promote the development of | 促进……的发展 | continuing economic growth promotes the development of education |
| put somebody through a system | 使某人接受某体系的训练/教育 | putting enough people through them |
| achieve higher productivity | 实现更高生产率 | achieve radically higher productivity |
| as a result of | 由于/作为……的结果 | a result of the training that U.S. workers received on the job |
| escape a poverty trap | 摆脱贫困陷阱 | escape their poverty traps |
| for the foreseeable future | 在可预见的未来 | substantially improve productivity for the foreseeable future |
| be able to do | 能够做某事 | might not be able to escape their poverty traps |

> [!note] 搭配辨析
> `be able to` 表示能力或可能性；`might not be able to` 是“可能无法”，其中 `might` 表示不确定推测，`not` 否定后面的能力结构。

---

### 综合长句拆解 (Integrated Sentence Patterns)

将多个语法点叠加后，形成考研英语常见的高密度句型。

| 句型 | 关键结构 | 文章例句 |
|------|----------|----------|
| 名词 + 同位语从句 + 表语 | view + that... + is wrong | the conventional view that education should be one of the very highest priorities... is wrong |
| 主句 + without 介短 + 定语从句 | escape... without changes that... | poor countries might not be able to escape their poverty traps without political changes that may be possible only with broader formal education |
| Only 状语前置 + 倒装主句 | Only when... + was there... | Only when humanity began to get its food in a more productive way was there time for other things. |
| 独立主格 + 被动主句 | with + 名词 + V-ing + was derided | with the country entering a recession and Japan at its pre-bubble peak, the U.S. workforce was derided... |

> [!tip] 考场操作顺序
> 先找主句谓语，再圈出逻辑连接词，随后处理从句和非谓语，最后回填介词短语与后置定语。不要一开始按单词顺序逐个翻译。

### 生词练习

**一、选词填空**

从方框中选择合适的词汇填入空白处（每词限用一次）：

> broader / building / contrary / despite / formal / job / possible / primary / required / result

1. The policy aims to provide ________ access to education in rural areas.

2. ________ a new educational system may take several generations.

3. The workers received most of their training ________.

4. The reform was considered ________ only after sufficient funding was secured.

5. Education was one of the ________ concerns discussed by the government.

6. The political system is ________ by the country's economic structure.

7. ________ the complexity of the task, the workers met the productivity standard.

8. Productivity rose and, as a ________, living standards improved.

9. ________ formal education, workers can still improve their productivity through training.

10. ________, the evidence suggests that the lack of schooling is not the only constraint.

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **broader**（更广泛的）
> 2. **Building**（建设）
> 3. **on the job**（在岗；在工作中）
> 4. **possible**（可能的；可行的）
> 5. **primary**（主要的）
> 6. **required**（所需要的）
> 7. **Despite**（尽管）
> 8. **result**（结果）
> 9. **Without**（没有；若没有；此处考查短语 without formal education）
> 10. **On the contrary**（恰恰相反）

**二、短语翻译**

将下列短语翻译成中文：

1. be derided as poorly educated

2. be trained on the job

3. on the contrary

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **be derided as poorly educated** = 被嘲讽为受教育程度低
> 2. **be trained on the job** = 接受在岗培训
> 3. **on the contrary** = 恰恰相反

**三、语境理解**

根据上下文，选择加粗词在原文中的准确含义：

1. “the U.S. workforce was **derided** as poorly educated”中 **derided** 的含义是：
   - A. 被培训
   - B. 被嘲讽
   - C. 被组织
   - D. 被雇用

2. “political changes that may be **possible** only with broader formal education”中 **possible** 的含义是：
   - A. 可能实现的
   - B. 已经完成的
   - C. 被禁止的
   - D. 不必要的

3. “the complex political systems **required** by advanced economic performance”中 **required** 的含义是：
   - A. 被批评的
   - B. 被取代的
   - C. 所需要的
   - D. 被推迟的

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **B** — `deride` 表示“嘲笑、嘲讽”，这里指美国劳动力因受教育程度低而受到嘲讽。
> 2. **A** — `possible` 表示“可能的、可行的”，说明政治变革只有在更广泛的正规教育基础上才可能实现。
> 3. **C** — `required by` 是过去分词短语，表示“由……所要求的”。

## 心得

> [!tip] 阅读技巧
> 文章开头先提出并修正传统观点，中间用美国汽车工厂和休斯敦墨西哥工人的例子提供证据，结尾再回到教育与经济发展的长期关系。做主旨题时，应把握“短期生产率提升不完全依赖正规教育”与“长期制度发展仍需要更广泛教育”这两个层次。

> [!warning] 易错点
> 不要把作者对“教育是快速经济发展的最高优先事项”这一观点的否定，误读成作者否定教育本身的重要性。文末明确指出，正规教育仍可能是复杂政治制度和摆脱贫困陷阱的必要条件。

> [!note] 论证线索
> `on the job`、`as a result`、`Only when...was there...`、`necessary but not sufficient` 是理解全文逻辑和句法结构的关键表达。
