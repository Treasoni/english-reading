# 2009 Passage 1 精读笔记

## 背景

本文讨论**习惯**与**创造力、创新**之间的关系。文章从华兹华斯“不是选择，而是习惯支配着不加反思的群体”的名言切入，指出“习惯”一词在当代语境中已带有负面含义；随后借助脑科学研究发现，论证有意识地培养新习惯能够开辟全新的思维路径、甚至产生全新的脑细胞，从而使创新成为可能。

> [!abstract] 文章概要
> 文章采用“提出问题 → 科学发现 → 反常识论证 → 权威引证 → 联系现实”的论证结构。核心结论是：不必费力消除旧习惯，而应通过有意识地培养新习惯来创建绕开旧路径的平行通路；阻碍创新的，正是对“做决定”和标准化的过分强调。

- **体裁**：议论文（argumentation）
- **主题**：习惯、创造力与创新
- **难度**：intermediate
- **文章来源**：2009 年考研英语阅读资料，Passage 1

## 文章原文

Habits are a funny thing. We reach for them mindlessly, setting our brains on auto-pilot and relaxing into the unconscious comfort of familiar routine. “Not choice, but habit rules the unreflecting **herd**,” William Wordsworth said in the 19th century. In the ever-changing 21st century, even the word “habit” carries a negative implication.

So it seems paradoxical to talk about habits in the same context as creativity and innovation. But brain researchers have discovered that when we consciously develop new habits, we create parallel paths, and even entirely new brain cells, that can jump our trains of thought onto new, innovative tracks.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：But brain researchers have discovered that when we consciously develop new habits, we create parallel paths, and even entirely new brain cells, that can jump our trains of thought onto new, innovative tracks.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: brain researchers
> - 谓语 V: have discovered
> - 宾语 O: that 从句
> - 主句简化：Brain researchers have discovered that...
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词/形式 | 修饰对象与作用 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 名词性从句 | that | 作 discovered 的宾语，说明研究者发现的内容 |
> | 状语从句 | when | 作 develop 的时间状语，表示“当……时” |
> | 定语从句 | that | 修饰 parallel paths and entirely new brain cells，说明这些路径和脑细胞的作用 |
> | 插入成分 | and even entirely new brain cells | 插入主句宾语 create parallel paths，补充说明新形成的脑通路及脑细胞 |
>
> **结构图解**
> 主句：brain researchers + have discovered + [that 从句]
>   └── 宾语从句：when we consciously develop new habits, we create parallel paths and new brain cells
>       ├── 时间状语从句：when we consciously develop new habits
>       └── 定语从句：that can jump our trains of thought onto new, innovative tracks → 修饰 paths 和 brain cells
>
> **参考译文**：但是，脑科学研究人员发现，当我们有意识地培养新习惯时，就会形成平行的思维路径，甚至产生全新的脑细胞；这些路径和脑细胞能够把我们的思维列车引向新的、富有创新性的轨道。
>
> **考点提示**：重点是 that 引导的宾语从句、when 引导的时间状语从句，以及关系代词 that 引导的定语从句。句中的插入成分使宾语结构被拉长，翻译时应先确定 create 的并列宾语，再处理末尾定语从句。


Rather than dismissing ourselves as unchangeable creatures of habit, we can instead direct our own change by consciously developing new habits.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Rather than dismissing ourselves as unchangeable creatures of habit, we can instead direct our own change by consciously developing new habits.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: we
> - 谓语 V: can direct
> - 宾语 O: our own change
> - 状语 A: instead；by consciously developing new habits
> - 主句简化：We can direct our own change.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词/形式 | 修饰对象与作用 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 介词短语 | Rather than + -ing | 作让步/取舍状语，表示“不……而……” |
> | 介词短语 | as | 作 dismissing 的宾语补足语，说明把 ourselves 看作什么 |
> | 非谓语短语 | by developing | 作方式状语，说明如何 direct our own change |
> | 非谓语短语 | consciously developing | developing 的副词修饰，表示“有意识地培养” |
>
> **结构图解**
> 主句：we + can direct + our own change
>   ├── Rather than dismissing ourselves as unchangeable creatures of habit → 让步/取舍状语
>   └── by consciously developing new habits → 方式状语
>
> **参考译文**：我们不必把自己看成一成不变的习惯动物，而可以通过有意识地培养新习惯来主动促成自身的改变。
>
> **考点提示**：Rather than 后接动名词，表示选择或对比；as 短语在这里是宾语补足语，不是原因状语；by + 动名词常考作方式状语。

In fact, the more new things we try—the more we step outside our comfort zone—the more **inherently** creative we become, both in the workplace and in our personal lives.

But don't bother trying to kill off old habits; once those ruts of procedure are worn into the brain, they're there to stay. Instead, the new habits we deliberately press into ourselves create parallel pathways that can bypass those old roads.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Instead, the new habits we deliberately press into ourselves create parallel pathways that can bypass those old roads.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: the new habits
> - 谓语 V: create
> - 宾语 O: parallel pathways
> - 状语 A: deliberately；instead
> - 主句简化：The new habits create parallel pathways.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词/形式 | 修饰对象与作用 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 副词 | Instead | 修饰 create，表示“相反/而是” |
> | 定语从句 | that | 修饰 parallel pathways，说明这些路径能够绕开旧路径 |
> | 插入成分 | we deliberately press into ourselves | 省略关系代词 that 的定语从句，修饰 the new habits |
> | 介词短语 | into ourselves | 与 press 构成 press into，表示“刻意灌输给自己” |
>
> **结构图解**
> 主句：the new habits + create + parallel pathways
>   ├── 定语从句：we deliberately press into ourselves → 修饰 the new habits
>   └── 定语从句：that can bypass those old roads → 修饰 parallel pathways
>
> **参考译文**：相反，我们有意灌输给自己的新习惯会形成平行的路径，从而绕开那些旧有的思维道路。
>
> **考点提示**：句中有两个定语从句：第一个省略了关系代词 that，第二个由 that 引导。判断第一个从句时，可观察 press 的宾语缺位；翻译时应将两个定语从句分别还原，避免把 we 误判为主句主语。


“The first thing needed for innovation is a **fascination** with **wonder**,” says Dawna Markova, author of The Open Mind. “But we are taught instead to 'decide,' just as our president calls himself 'the Decider'.” She adds, however, that “to decide is to kill off all possibilities but one. A good innovational thinker is always exploring the many other possibilities.”

All of us work through problems in ways of which we're unaware, she says. Researchers in the late 1960s discovered that humans are born with the capacity to approach challenges in four primary ways: analytically, procedurally, relationally (or collaboratively) and innovatively. At the end of adolescence, however, the brain shuts down half of that capacity, preserving only those modes of thought that have seemed most valuable during the first decade or so of life.

The current emphasis on standardized testing highlights analysis and procedure, meaning that few of us inherently use our innovative and collaborative modes of thought.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The current emphasis on standardized testing highlights analysis and procedure, meaning that few of us inherently use our innovative and collaborative modes of thought.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: The current emphasis on standardized testing
> - 谓语 V: highlights
> - 宾语 O: analysis and procedure
> - 结果说明 A: meaning that few of us inherently use...
> - 主句简化：The emphasis highlights analysis and procedure.
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 类型 | 引导词/形式 | 修饰对象与作用 |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | 介词短语 | on standardized testing | 修饰 emphasis，说明“强调”的对象 |
> | 非谓语短语 | meaning... | 现在分词短语，表示前面整件事造成的结果或推论 |
> | 名词性从句 | that | 作 meaning 的宾语，说明具体结果 |
> | 介词短语 | of thought | 修饰 modes，表示“思维模式” |
> | 并列修饰语 | innovative and collaborative | 并列修饰 modes of thought |
>
> **结构图解**
> 主句：The current emphasis on standardized testing + highlights + analysis and procedure
>   └── 结果说明：meaning [that few of us inherently use our innovative and collaborative modes of thought]
>       ├── 从句主语：few of us
>       ├── 从句谓语：use
>       └── 从句宾语：our innovative and collaborative modes of thought
>
> **参考译文**：当前对标准化考试的重视突出了分析能力和程序性思维，这意味着我们中很少有人会自然而然地运用创新性和协作性的思维方式。
>
> **考点提示**：meaning 是现在分词，逻辑主语是前面的整个主句，而不是 procedure；that 引导宾语从句。注意 few 表示“很少的”，带有否定意味；standardized testing 和 modes of thought 中的介词短语分别作后置定语。

“This breaks the major rule in the American belief system—that anyone can do anything,” explains M. J. Ryan, author of the 2006 book This Year I Will…and Ms. Markova’s business partner. “That’s a lie that we have **perpetuated**, and it fosters **commonness**. Knowing what you’re good at and doing even more of it creates excellence.” This is where developing new habits comes in.

## 翻译对照

Habits are a funny thing. We reach for them mindlessly, setting our brains on auto-pilot and relaxing into the unconscious comfort of familiar routine. “Not choice, but habit rules the unreflecting herd,” William Wordsworth said in the 19th century. In the ever-changing 21st century, even the word “habit” carries a negative implication.

习惯是一件奇妙的事情。我们会不假思索地依赖它们，让大脑进入自动驾驶状态，沉浸在熟悉惯例带来的无意识舒适之中。“不是选择，而是习惯支配着不加反思的群体。”19世纪的威廉·华兹华斯这样说道。在不断变化的21世纪，甚至“习惯”一词也带有负面含义。

So it seems paradoxical to talk about habits in the same context as creativity and innovation. But brain researchers have discovered that when we consciously develop new habits, we create parallel paths, and even entirely new brain cells, that can jump our trains of thought onto new, innovative tracks.

因此，在创造力和创新的语境中谈论习惯，似乎自相矛盾。但是，脑科学研究人员发现，当我们有意识地培养新习惯时，我们会形成平行路径，甚至产生全新的脑细胞；这些路径和脑细胞能够把我们的思维列车引向新的、富有创新性的轨道。

Rather than dismissing ourselves as unchangeable creatures of habit, we can instead direct our own change by consciously developing new habits.

我们不必把自己看成一成不变的习惯动物，而可以通过有意识地培养新习惯来主动促成自身的改变。

In fact, the more new things we try—the more we step outside our comfort zone—the more inherently creative we become, both in the workplace and in our personal lives. But don't bother trying to kill off old habits; once those ruts of procedure are worn into the brain, they're there to stay. Instead, the new habits we deliberately press into ourselves create parallel pathways that can bypass those old roads.

事实上，我们尝试的新事物越多——越走出自己的舒适区——我们就变得越具有内在创造力，无论在工作场所还是在个人生活中都是如此。但是，不必费力去消除旧习惯；一旦那些程序性的思维定式刻入大脑，它们就会一直留在那里。相反，我们有意识灌输给自己的新习惯会形成平行的路径，从而绕开那些旧有的思维道路。

“The first thing needed for innovation is a fascination with wonder,” says Dawna Markova, author of The Open Mind. “But we are taught instead to 'decide,' just as our president calls himself 'the Decider'.” She adds, however, that “to decide is to kill off all possibilities but one. A good innovational thinker is always exploring the many other possibilities.”

《开放思维》一书的作者道娜·马尔科娃说：“创新所需的首要条件是对‘惊奇’的迷恋。但我们却被教导要‘做决定’，正如我们的总统自称‘决策者’一样。”然而，她补充说：“做决定意味着扼杀掉除一个以外的所有可能性。一位优秀的创新型思考者总是在探索其他许多可能性。”

All of us work through problems in ways of which we're unaware, she says. Researchers in the late 1960s discovered that humans are born with the capacity to approach challenges in four primary ways: analytically, procedurally, relationally (or collaboratively) and innovatively. At the end of adolescence, however, the brain shuts down half of that capacity, preserving only those modes of thought that have seemed most valuable during the first decade or so of life.

她说，我们所有人都以自己并未意识到的方式处理问题。20世纪60年代末的研究人员发现，人类天生就具备以四种主要方式应对挑战的能力：分析式、程序式、关系式（或协作式）和创新式。然而，到了青春期结束时，大脑会关闭其中一半的能力，只保留那些在生命最初十年左右看起来最有价值的思维模式。

The current emphasis on standardized testing highlights analysis and procedure, meaning that few of us inherently use our innovative and collaborative modes of thought.

当前对标准化考试的重视突出了分析能力和程序性思维，这意味着我们中很少有人会自然而然地运用创新性和协作性的思维方式。

“This breaks the major rule in the American belief system—that anyone can do anything,” explains M. J. Ryan, author of the 2006 book This Year I Will…and Ms. Markova's business partner. “That's a lie that we have perpetuated, and it fosters commonness. Knowing what you're good at and doing even more of it creates excellence.” This is where developing new habits comes in.

2006年出版的《今年我将……》一书的作者、马尔科娃女士的生意伙伴M·J·瑞安解释说：“这打破了美国信仰体系中的一条重要规则——人人都能做任何事。这是我们长期宣扬的一个谎言，它助长了平庸。知道自己擅长什么，并投入更多精力去做，才能成就卓越。”这正是培养新习惯发挥作用的地方。

## 语法要点

### 内容索引

- **来源/起源表达**：come from、stem from、derive from、spring from、originate from/in
- **动词结构与语态**：stem from 的非被动结构、derive 的不及物/被动/及物结构
- **原因与结果搭配**：be rooted in、result from、result in、arise from
- **介词辨析**：onto（动态位移）vs on（静态）；onto（连写）vs on to（分写）
- **固定搭配**：move on to、hold on to、log on to、catch on to、get on to

### 来源/起源词群 + onto vs on 介词辨析

---

### 📘 第一部分：来源/起源短语五虎将

#### 一、核心画面感对比

| 短语 | 画面意象 | 适用对象 | 情感色彩 |
|------|---------|---------|---------|
| **come from** | 最通用，无画面 | 出生地、国家、物理来源、基本起因 | 中性 |
| **stem from** | 🌿 植物的"茎/主干"（枝叶从主干长出） | 问题、分歧、压力、疾病、冲突的根源 | ⚠️ **消极** |
| **derive from** | 🌊 分支/衍生（从源头派生新事物） | 词源、化学提取、文化/理论演变 | 中性/学术 |
| **spring from** | ⛲ 泉水涌出 / 突然爆发 | 情感、灵感、想法、生活经历 | 强烈 / 突发 |
| **originate from/in** | 🏛️ 起始点 / 源头 | 历史发明、文化、风俗、运动项目 | 正式 / 书面 |

> 💡 **核心规律：** 中文都译为"来自/源自"，但**意象**、**对象**、**语域**（口语 vs 书面）完全不同。

---

#### 二、五大短语例句精讲

##### 1. come from —— 最通用最口语
- He **comes from** Canada.（他来自加拿大。）
- Where does the idea **come from**?（这个想法从何而来？）

##### 2. stem from —— 消极根源专属
- Most of their problems **stem from** poor communication.
（他们的大多数问题根源于沟通不畅。）

##### 3. derive from —— 学术溯源 + 派生
- The English word "mother" **derives from** Latin.
（英语单词 mother 起源于拉丁语。）

##### 4. spring from —— 情感灵感爆发
- His anger **springs from** a sense of unfairness.
（他的愤怒源于一种不公感。）

##### 5. originate from/in —— 正式历史溯源
- The custom **originated in** the 19th century.
（这一习俗起源于 19 世纪。）

---

#### 三、考试三大必考陷阱 ⚠️

##### 🚨 陷阱 1：stem from 绝对无被动！

| 错误（❌） | 正确（✅） |
|-----------|-----------|
| The conflict **is stemmed from** misunderstanding. | The conflict **stems from** misunderstanding. |

> 📌 **高频考点：** 高考改错 / 四六级语法题最爱挖的坑

---

##### 🚨 陷阱 2：derive 的"双重结构"

| 用法 | 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **不及物** | derive from | The word derives from French. |
| **被动式** | be derived from | The word **is derived from** French.（同样地道） |
| **及物** | derive **A from B**（从 B 中获得 A） | She **derived great pleasure from** reading.（她从阅读中获得极大乐趣。） |

---

##### 🚨 陷阱 3：originate 介词搭配三件套

| 介词 | 搭配对象 | 例句 |
|------|---------|------|
| **originate in** | 地点 / 时间 / 领域 | The Olympic Games **originated in** Greece.（起源于希腊） |
| **originate from** | 源头 / 雏形 | The story **originated from** a true event.（源自真实事件） |
| **originate with** | 提出者 / 创始人 | The plan **originated with** the manager.（最初由经理提出） |

---

#### 四、高级写作拓展库（议论文分析原因）

| 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **be rooted in** | 深深扎根于…… | His love for art **is deeply rooted in** his childhood. |
| **result from** | 起因于（❌勿与 result in 混淆） | Traffic accidents often **result from** carelessness. |
| **arise from** | 因……而产生 / 出现 | Many problems **arise from** a lack of trust. |

> ⚠️ **致命区别：**
> - `result from` = A 因 B 而产生（B → A）
> - `result in` = A 导致 B（A → B）

---

#### 五、记忆口诀 🎯

> **"come from 最万能，stem from 找问题；**
> **derive 词源可被动，spring 情感灵感涌；**
> **originate 正式溯源，写作升级 result/arise！"**

---

#### 六、一句话速记卡片

> **"问题矛盾用 stem from，词源提取 derive from（还可被动 be derived），灵感情感 spring from，历史溯源 originate！"**

---

### 📘 第二部分：onto（动态位移）vs on（静态）

#### 一、核心画面区分

| 介词 | 动作性质 | 画面感 | 例句 |
|------|---------|--------|------|
| **onto** | 🔼 **动态（位移）** | 从别的地方**移动并落到**表面上（由外到上） | The cat jumped **onto** the table.（猫从地上跳到了桌子上。） |
| **on** | 📍 **静态 / 局部** | 已经处于表面，或在该表面范围内活动 | The cat is jumping **on** the table.（猫已在桌子上，在桌面上蹦来蹦去。） |

> 💡 **语感对比：**
> - `jump onto that table` → 原本**不在**桌上，起跳后**落到**桌面
> - `jump on that table` → **人已在**桌上，在桌面上跳跃（或口语泛指）

---

#### 二、🔥 顶级易错点：onto（连写）vs on to（分写）

> **核心判断标准：on 是不是前面动词短语的一部分？**

##### 1. 连写 **onto**（单一介词）

**功能：** 作为独立介词，直接表示"到……之上"。

**例句：**
- Step **onto** the scale, please.（请踩上体重秤。）
- Water spilled **onto** the keyboard.（水泼到了键盘上。）

---

##### 2. 分写 **on to**（动副短语 + 介词/不定式）

**功能：** 这里的 **on 是副词**，跟前面的动词绑定（move on, hold on, log on, catch on…），而 **to 是独立的介词或不定式标记**。

**例句：**

| 动词短语 | on 的角色 | 例句 |
|---------|----------|------|
| **move on** | 继续前进 | Let's move **on to** the next topic.（让我们进入下一个话题。） |
| **hold on** | 坚持 / 抓住 | He held **on to** the rope tightly.（他紧紧抓住绳子。） |
| **log on** | 登录 | Please log **on to** the website.（请登录该网站。） |
| **carry on** | 继续 | Carry **on to** the end.（继续到结束。） |

---

#### 三、判断流程图 🧭

```
看到 "on to"
    │
    ├─ 前面是动词短语（move/hold/log/catch + on）？
    │       │
    │       ├─ YES → ✅ 分写 "on to"
    │       │
    │       └─ NO → ❌ 应连写 "onto"
    │
    └─ 表示"位移到……之上"？
            │
            └─ YES → ✅ 连写 "onto"
```

---

#### 四、常见 on to 动词短语清单 📋

| 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| move on to | 继续到 / 转入 | Let's move on **to** grammar. |
| hold on to | 抓住 / 坚持 | Hold on **to** your dream. |
| log on to | 登录（系统） | Log on **to** your account. |
| catch on to | 理解 / 熟悉 | I caught on **to** his idea quickly. |
| get on to | 联系上 / 开始处理 | I'll get on **to** it right away. |

---

#### 五、记忆口诀 🎯

> **"动作用 onto（位移上去），**
> **静态用 on（已在上面）；**
> **on + 动词短语必须分，**
> **move/hold/log + on to 看清！"**

---

#### 六、一句话总结

> **"动态位移用 onto，静态位置用 on；on 是动词短语一部分时分写，独立介词才连写。"**

## 心得

> [!tip] 阅读技巧
> 本文的核心论证是一组隐喻：**新习惯 = 平行路径（parallel pathways）**，能够**绕开旧路（bypass old roads）**。抓住这条隐喻主线，全文结构一目了然——开篇对“习惯”的负面印象 → 脑科学研究对“培养新习惯促进创新”的支撑 → 两位作家（Markova、Ryan）的权威引证 → 对标准化测试的批评收尾。做主旨题时应把握“不是消灭旧习惯，而是有意识地培养新习惯以促成创新”这一主线。

> [!warning] 易错点
> 1. 不要把 Markova 对“做决定”（decide）的批评误读为她否定一切决定；她反对的是用“一次决定”扼杀掉其他所有可能性（kill off all possibilities but one）。
> 2. 第 7 段 `few of us inherently use...` 中的 `few` 含否定意味，是“几乎没有人”，不是“少数人”，语义判断极易设题。
> 3. `to decide is to kill off all possibilities but one` 中的 `but` 作介词，意为“除了”，与 `anything but` 结构可类比记忆。

> [!note] 论证线索
> `Not choice, but habit`（对照强调）、`Rather than...`（取舍）、`Instead`（转折替代）、`meaning that...`（结果推论）、`This is where...comes in`（点题收束）是把握全文逻辑的关键表达。
