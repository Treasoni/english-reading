# DNA Testing 精读笔记

## 背景

本文围绕 **DNA 检测** 的商业化展开：文章先介绍亲子鉴定工具包（PTK）的普及、价格和常见用途，再转向对祖源检测准确性、参考数据库质量以及同行评审不足的批评。

文章的论证路径是“市场现象 → 使用场景 → 局限与问题”，难度主要集中在长定语结构、过去分词后置修饰、比较结构和并列谓语。

# DNA Testing

## Reading Passage

It is a wise father that knows his own child, but today a man can **boost** his paternal (fatherly) wisdom—or at least confirm that he’s the kid’s dad.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：It is a wise father that knows his own child, but today a man can boost his paternal (fatherly) wisdom—or at least confirm that he’s the kid’s dad.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: It（强调句的形式主语）；第二分句为 a man
> - 谓语 V: is；第二分句为 can boost / confirm
> - 宾语 O: 第二分句中 boost 的宾语为 his paternal wisdom；confirm 的宾语为 that 从句
> - 补语 C: a wise father（被强调的成分）
> - 状语 A: today（修饰第二分句）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 强调结构 | It is a wise father that... | 强调 a wise father，基本结构可还原为 A wise father knows his own child |
> | 定语从句/强调分句 | that knows his own child | 说明被强调的 a wise father |
> | 并列结构 | but today a man... | 连接前后两个转折分句 |
> | 名词性从句 | that he’s the kid’s dad | 作 confirm 的宾语 |
> | 省略结构 | or at least confirm... | 与 can boost 并列，共用 can；完整形式为 can at least confirm... |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 复合句
> ├── 分句一：It is a wise father that knows his own child
> │   ├── 强调成分：a wise father
> │   └── that 从句：knows his own child
> └── but 分句二：today a man can boost his paternal wisdom
>     └── or（省略 can）at least confirm [that he’s the kid’s dad]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：只有聪明的父亲才了解自己的孩子；但如今，一个男人可以增强自己作为父亲的“智慧”——或者至少确认自己就是孩子的父亲。
>
> **考点提示**：重点掌握 `It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who...` 强调句；注意 `or` 后省略与前面相同的情态动词 `can`，以及 `that he’s the kid’s dad` 作 `confirm` 的宾语从句。

All he needs to do is shell out $30 for a **paternity testing kit (PTK)** at his local drugstore—and another $120 to get the results.

More than 60,000 people have purchased the **PTKs** since they first became available without prescriptions last year, according to Doug Fogg, chief operating officer of Identigene, which makes the over-the-counter kits.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：More than 60,000 people have purchased the PTKs since they first became available without prescriptions last year, according to Doug Fogg, chief operating officer of Identigene, which makes the over-the-counter kits.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: More than 60,000 people
> - 谓语 V: have purchased
> - 宾语 O: the PTKs
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: since they first became available without prescriptions last year；according to Doug Fogg 为来源状语
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 状语从句 | since they first became available without prescriptions last year | 说明购买行为发生的起点时间 |
> | 介词短语 | without prescriptions | 修饰 became available，表示无需处方 |
> | 介词短语 | according to Doug Fogg | 表明信息来源 |
> | 同位语 | chief operating officer of Identigene | 补充说明 Doug Fogg 的身份 |
> | 定语从句 | which makes the over-the-counter kits | 修饰 Identigene |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句：More than 60,000 people have purchased the PTKs
> ├── since 从句：they first became available without prescriptions last year
> ├── according to Doug Fogg
> │   └── 同位语：chief operating officer of Identigene
> │       └── 定从：which makes the over-the-counter kits
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：Identigene 公司首席运营官道格·福格表示，自这些亲子鉴定工具包去年首次可以无需处方购买以来，已有超过 6 万人购买。
>
> **考点提示**：`have purchased` 是现在完成时，常与 `since` 引导的时间状语从句搭配；`chief operating officer of Identigene` 是 `Doug Fogg` 的同位语，句末 `which` 定语从句修饰公司名 Identigene，而非前面的具体职位。

More than two dozen companies sell **DNA tests** directly to the public, ranging in price from a few hundred dollars to more than $2,500.

Among the most popular: **paternity and kinship testing**, which adopted children can use to find their biological relatives and families can use to track down kids put up for adoption.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Among the most popular: paternity and kinship testing, which adopted children can use to find their biological relatives and families can use to track down kids put up for adoption.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: paternity and kinship testing
> - 谓语 V: [are]（省略）
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: among the most popular
> - 状语 A: 无
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 介词短语前置 | Among the most popular | 作表语，完整语序为 Paternity and kinship testing are among the most popular |
> | 定语从句 | which adopted children can use... and families can use... | 修饰 paternity and kinship testing |
> | 不定式短语 | to find their biological relatives | 表示 adopted children 使用检测的目的 |
> | 不定式短语 | to track down kids put up for adoption | 表示 families 使用检测的目的 |
> | 过去分词短语 | put up for adoption | 修饰 kids，意为被送养的孩子 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 冒号后的省略结构：Among the most popular [are] paternity and kinship testing
> └── which 定从：
>     ├── adopted children can use [it] to find their biological relatives
>     └── families can use [it] to track down kids
>         └── put up for adoption
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：其中最受欢迎的项目包括亲子关系和亲属关系检测。被收养的孩子可以利用这类检测寻找自己的生物学亲属，家庭也可以借此追踪那些被送养的孩子。
>
> **考点提示**：冒号前的 `Among the most popular` 是前置表语，句中省略了 `are`；`which` 引导定语从句，且从句内部有两个由 `and` 连接的并列谓语；`put up for adoption` 是过去分词短语，修饰 `kids`。

**DNA testing** is also the latest rage among passionate genealogists—and supports businesses that offer to search for a family’s geographic roots.

Most tests require collecting cells by **swabbing** saliva in the mouth and sending it to the company for testing. All tests require a potential **candidate** with whom to compare DNA.

But some observers are skeptical. “There’s a kind of **false precision** being hawked by people claiming they are doing **ancestry testing**,” says Troy Duster, a New York University sociologist. He notes that each individual has many ancestors—numbering in the hundreds just a few centuries back. Yet most ancestry testing only considers a single lineage, either the Y chromosome inherited through men in a father’s line or mitochondrial DNA, which is passed down only from mothers.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Yet most ancestry testing only considers a single lineage, either the Y chromosome inherited through men in a father’s line or mitochondrial DNA, which is passed down only from mothers.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: most ancestry testing
> - 谓语 V: considers
> - 宾语 O: a single lineage
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: Yet（转折副词）；only（限制 considers 的范围）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 同位语 | either the Y chromosome... or mitochondrial DNA | 具体解释 a single lineage 包含的两种可能 |
> | 过去分词短语 | inherited through men in a father’s line | 修饰 the Y chromosome |
> | 介词短语 | through men in a father’s line | 说明 Y 染色体的传递路径 |
> | 定语从句 | which is passed down only from mothers | 修饰 mitochondrial DNA |
> | 介词短语 | from mothers | 说明线粒体 DNA 的传递来源 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句：most ancestry testing only considers a single lineage
> └── 同位语：either the Y chromosome or mitochondrial DNA
>     ├── 过去分词短语：inherited through men in a father’s line
>     └── which 定从：which is passed down only from mothers
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：然而，大多数祖源检测只考虑单一谱系：要么是沿父系男性传递的 Y 染色体，要么是只由母亲传递的线粒体 DNA。
>
> **考点提示**：`either...or...` 在此作同位语，具体说明 `a single lineage`；过去分词 `inherited` 作后置定语，`which` 引导的非限制性定语从句补充说明 `mitochondrial DNA`。

This DNA can reveal genetic information about only one or two ancestors, even though, for example, just three generations back people also have six other great-grandparents or, four generations back, 14 other great-great-grandparents.

Critics also argue that commercial genetic testing is only as good as the **reference collections** to which a sample is compared.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Critics also argue that commercial genetic testing is only as good as the reference collections to which a sample is compared.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Critics
> - 谓语 V: argue
> - 宾语 O: that commercial genetic testing is only as good as the reference collections to which a sample is compared（that 引导的宾语从句）
> - 补语 C: only as good as the reference collections...（宾语从句中的表语结构）
> - 状语 A: also（修饰 argue）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 名词性从句 | that commercial genetic testing is only as good as... | 作 argue 的宾语 |
> | 比较结构 | only as good as the reference collections | 表示“质量只能达到参照对象的水平” |
> | 定语从句 | to which a sample is compared | 修饰 the reference collections；介词 to 前置 |
> | 被动结构 | a sample is compared | sample 是 compare 的承受者，完整含义为 compared to the collections |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句：Critics also argue [宾语从句]
> └── commercial genetic testing is only as good as the reference collections
>     └── to which a sample is compared
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：批评者还认为，商业基因检测的质量取决于样本所比对的参考数据库。
>
> **考点提示**：识别 `argue that...` 引导的宾语从句；`only as good as...` 是“与……一样好/其好坏取决于……”的比较结构；`to which` 是“介词 + 关系代词”引导的定语从句，不能将 `to` 遗漏。

Databases used by some companies don't rely on data collected systematically but rather **lump** together information from different research projects.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Databases used by some companies don't rely on data collected systematically but rather lump together information from different research projects.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Databases
> - 谓语 V: don't rely on / lump together（两个并列谓语）
> - 宾语 O: data collected systematically；information from different research projects（分别对应两个谓语）
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: 无（`by some companies` 和 `systematically` 分别属于内部修饰成分）
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 过去分词短语 | used by some companies | 修饰 Databases，意为“被一些公司使用的” |
> | 过去分词短语 | collected systematically | 修饰 data，意为“被系统收集的数据” |
> | 介词短语 | from different research projects | 修饰 information，说明信息来源 |
> | 对比并列结构 | don’t rely on A but rather lump together B | 连接两个并列谓语，表示“不是依赖 A，而是把 B 混在一起” |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句：Databases don't rely on data but rather lump together information
> ├── 主语：Databases
> │   └── 过去分词短语：used by some companies
> ├── 谓语一：don't rely on data
> │   └── data
> │       └── 过去分词短语：collected systematically
> └── but rather 谓语二：lump together information
>     └── 介词短语：from different research projects
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：一些公司使用的数据库并不依赖系统收集的数据，而是把来自不同研究项目的信息混杂在一起。
>
> **考点提示**：重点掌握 `not...but rather...` 的“不是……而是……”结构；`used` 和 `collected` 都是过去分词作后置定语；`lump together` 表示“把……混在一起”。

This means that a DNA database may have a lot of data from some regions and not others, so a person's test results may differ depending on the company that processes the results.

In addition, the computer programs a company uses to estimate relationships may be patented and not subject to **peer review** or outside evaluation.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In addition, the computer programs a company uses to estimate relationships may be patented and not subject to peer review or outside evaluation.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: the computer programs
> - 谓语 V: may be
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: patented and not subject to peer review or outside evaluation（两个并列补语，共用 may be）
> - 状语 A: In addition
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 修饰类型 | 具体结构 | 修饰对象/功能 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 定语从句 | (that) a company uses to estimate relationships | 修饰 the computer programs；关系代词 that 作 uses 的宾语，被省略 |
> | 不定式短语 | to estimate relationships | 表示一家公司使用这些程序的目的 |
> | 并列补语 | patented and not subject to... | 两个补语共用 may be；patented 为过去分词，not subject to 为形容词短语 |
> | 介词短语 | to peer review or outside evaluation | 作 subject to 的宾语，说明程序不接受的审查方式 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句：the computer programs may be patented and not subject to peer review or outside evaluation
> ├── 主语：the computer programs
> │   └── 定语从句：（that）a company uses
> │       └── 不定式短语：to estimate relationships
> ├── 谓语：may be
> │   ├── 补语一：patented
> │   └── 补语二：not subject to peer review or outside evaluation
> │       └── 介词短语：to peer review or outside evaluation
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：此外，一家公司用来估算亲缘关系的计算机程序可能已经获得专利，而且不接受同行评审或外部评估。
>
> **考点提示**：`a company uses` 是修饰 `computer programs` 的定语从句，关系代词作宾语时可以省略；`to estimate relationships` 是目的不定式；`patented` 与 `not subject to...` 是共用 `may be` 的并列补语；`subject to` 中的 `to` 是介词，不是不定式标志，表示“受……支配/接受……审查”。

# DNA Testing 中英对照翻译

## Reading Passage 阅读文章

It is a wise father that knows his own child, but today a man can boost his paternal (fatherly) wisdom—or at least confirm that he’s the kid’s dad. All he needs to do is shell out $30 for a **paternity testing kit (PTK)** at his local drugstore—and another $120 to get the results.

只有聪明的父亲才了解自己的孩子；但如今，一个男人可以增强自己作为父亲的“智慧”——或者至少确认自己就是孩子的父亲。他只需在当地药店花 30 美元购买一套**亲子鉴定工具包（PTK）**，再花 120 美元获取检测结果。

More than 60,000 people have purchased the **PTKs** since they first became available without prescriptions last year, according to Doug Fogg, chief operating officer of Identigene, which makes the over-the-counter kits. More than two dozen companies sell **DNA tests** directly to the public, ranging in price from a few hundred dollars to more than $2,500.

Identigene 公司首席运营官道格·福格表示，自这些**亲子鉴定工具包（PTK）**去年首次可以无需处方购买以来，已有超过 6 万人购买。超过 24 家公司直接向公众销售**DNA 检测**，价格从几百美元到 2500 多美元不等。

Among the most popular: **paternity and kinship testing**, which adopted children can use to find their biological relatives and families can use to track down kids put up for adoption. **DNA testing** is also the latest rage among passionate genealogists—and supports businesses that offer to search for a family’s geographic roots.

其中最受欢迎的项目包括：**亲子关系和亲属关系检测**。被收养的孩子可以利用这类检测寻找自己的生物学亲属，家庭也可以借此追踪那些被送养的孩子。**DNA 检测**同样成为热衷家谱研究者中的最新潮流，并催生了一些为家庭寻找地理祖源的企业。

Most tests require collecting cells by swabbing saliva in the mouth and sending it to the company for testing. All tests require a potential candidate with whom to compare DNA.

大多数检测都要求用棉签擦拭口腔中的唾液来采集细胞，然后将样本寄给公司进行检测。所有检测都需要一个潜在的候选对象，以便与其进行 DNA 比对。

But some observers are skeptical. “There’s a kind of **false precision** being hawked by people claiming they are doing **ancestry testing**,” says Troy Duster, a New York University sociologist. He notes that each individual has many ancestors—numbering in the hundreds just a few centuries back. Yet most ancestry testing only considers a single lineage, either the Y chromosome inherited through men in a father’s line or mitochondrial DNA, which is passed down only from mothers. This DNA can reveal genetic information about only one or two ancestors, even though, for example, just three generations back people also have six other great-grandparents or, four generations back, 14 other great-great-grandparents.

但一些观察人士对此持怀疑态度。纽约大学社会学家特洛伊·达斯特说：“那些声称自己在进行**祖源检测**的人，正在兜售一种**虚假的精确性**。”他指出，每个人都有许多祖先——仅仅追溯几百年，祖先数量就可达数百人。然而，大多数祖源检测只考虑单一谱系：要么是沿父系男性传递的 Y 染色体，要么是只由母亲传递的线粒体 DNA。这些 DNA 只能揭示一两位祖先的遗传信息；但例如，向前追溯三代时，一个人还有另外六位曾祖父母，向前追溯四代时，还有另外 14 位高祖父母。

Critics also argue that commercial genetic testing is only as good as the **reference collections** to which a sample is compared. Databases used by some companies don't rely on data collected systematically but rather lump together information from different research projects. This means that a DNA database may have a lot of data from some regions and not others, so a person's test results may differ depending on the company that processes the results. In addition, the computer programs a company uses to estimate relationships may be patented and not subject to **peer review** or outside evaluation.

批评者还认为，商业基因检测的质量取决于样本所比对的**参考数据库**。一些公司使用的数据库并不是建立在系统收集的数据之上，而是将来自不同研究项目的信息混杂在一起。这意味着，某个 DNA 数据库可能拥有来自某些地区的大量数据，却缺少其他地区的数据，因此，一个人的检测结果可能会因处理结果的公司不同而不同。此外，公司用来估算亲缘关系的计算机程序可能已经申请专利，因而不接受**同行评审**或外部评估。

## 语法要点

# be subject to 完全攻略：语法整理

### 介词与连词：subject 与 to 的语法本质

本笔记的核心是固定结构 **be subject to**。其中，**subject 在这里是形容词，to 是介词**。

| 词项 | 词性 | 本笔记中的含义 |
|---|---|---|
| subject | 名词 | 主题、学科 |
| subject | 形容词 | 受……支配的；取决于……的 |
| to | 介词 | 引出影响、批准、管辖等对象 |

> [!tip] 识别方法
> 看到 **be subject to** 时，先判断 `subject` 是形容词，再判断 `to` 后面的成分。不要把这里的 `to` 当作不定式符号。

### 固定搭配：be subject to 的语境一——易受……影响的 / 可能遭受……的

**核心语义：** 描述事物可能会受到外部因素（天气、市场、政策等）的影响而发生变化或延误。

**高频搭配：**

- `be subject to change` 可能会发生变动
- `be subject to delay` 可能会延误

**例句：**

| 例句 | 翻译 |
|---|---|
| Flight schedules **are subject to delay** due to bad weather. | 由于天气恶劣，航班时刻可能会有所延误。 |
| Prices **are subject to change** without prior notice. | 价格随时可能变动，恕不另行通知。（商用公告/条款最高频句式） |

### 固定搭配：be subject to 的语境二——取决于…… / 须经……核准

**核心语义：** 表示某项决定、协议或合同并非最终定案，必须以某项条件满足为前提。

**高频搭配：**

- `be subject to approval` 须经批准
- `be subject to confirmation` 须经确认
- `be subject to revision` 须经修订

**例句：**

| 例句 | 翻译 |
|---|---|
| The proposal **is subject to** the approval of the committee. | 该提案须经委员会批准后方能执行。 |
| The contract **is subject to** the CEO's final confirmation. | 该合同须经CEO最终确认后方可生效。 |

### 固定搭配：be subject to 的语境三——服从于…… / 受……管辖与支配

**核心语义：** 表示任何人或组织都处于法律、规则或某机构的管辖之下。

**高频搭配：**

- `be subject to the law` 受法律约束
- `be subject to regulation` 受规章约束
- `be subject to jurisdiction` 受……管辖

**例句：**

| 例句 | 翻译 |
|---|---|
| All citizens **are subject to** the law. | 所有公民都必须遵守法律 / 受法律管辖。 |
| The company **is subject to** government regulation. | 该公司受政府监管。 |

### 介词与连词：be subject to 三大语境速查

| 语境 | 含义 | 关键词 | 典型场景 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **语境 1** | 易受影响 | change, delay, fluctuation | 天气、市场、价格波动 |
| **语境 2** | 取决于 / 须经 | approval, confirmation, revision | 合同、提案、决议 |
| **语境 3** | 受管辖 / 服从 | law, regulation, jurisdiction | 法律、组织、机构 |

关联复习：[[#非谓语动词：to 后接名词、代词或动名词|to 后接什么成分]] ↔ [[#语态：be subject to 与 be subjected to|subject 与 subjected 的区别]]。

### 非谓语动词：to 是介词，后接名词、代词或动名词

> [!warning] 考试避坑雷区一
> **to 是介词，后面绝对不能跟动词原形！**
>
> 这是单选和语法填空最容易丢分的考点。`to` 后可以接名词、代词或动名词。

| 错误（❌） | 正确（✅） |
|---|---|
| The plan is subject to ~~change~~（误把 change 当动词原形） | The plan is subject **to changes**.（名词复数）<br>The plan is subject **to being revised**.（动名词被动） |

**关键判别：**

| 后面词性 | 正确示例 |
|---|---|
| **名词** | be subject to **change(s)** / **delay** / **approval** |
| **代词** | be subject to **it** / **them** |
| **动名词** | be subject to **being revised** / **changing** |
| ❌ **动词原形** | 绝对不可！ |

> [!note] 源笔记一致性提醒
> 原笔记前文将 `be subject to change` 列为高频搭配，后文避坑表又将 `change` 划为错误示例。两处表述存在内部不一致；整理时均予以保留，复习时请特别核对语境和词性。

关联复习：[[#介词与连词：subject 与 to 的语法本质|to 是介词]] → 本节的“名词 / 代词 / 动名词”判别。

### 语态：be subject to 与 be subjected to 的区别

> [!warning] 考试避坑雷区二
> **be subject to** 与 **be subjected to** 是最易混淆的两个结构。

| 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **be subject to** | 形容词短语（易受影响 / 取决于 / 服从） | The schedule **is subject to** change.（时刻表可能会变。） |
| **be subjected to** | 被动语态（遭受 / 经历） | The prisoners **were subjected to** cruel treatment.（囚犯遭受了残酷对待。） |

**对比一目了然：**

| 句子 | 含义 | 区别 |
|---|---|---|
| He **is subject to** criticism. | 他**容易受到**批评。 | 强调"易受影响" |
| He **was subjected to** criticism. | 他**遭受了**批评。 | 强调"已经发生" |

关联复习：[[#固定搭配：be subject to 的语境一——易受……影响的 / 可能遭受……的|be subject to 的语境含义]] ↔ 本节的被动结构 **be subjected to**。

### 补充要点：be subject to 的近义表达拓展

| 表达 | 含义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| **be liable to** | 易于……；可能遭受…… | The glass **is liable to** break.（玻璃易碎。） |
| **be prone to** | 倾向于……；易于…… | She **is prone to** headaches.（她容易头痛。） |
| **be vulnerable to** | 容易受到……（伤害） | Children **are vulnerable to** infection.（儿童易受感染。） |
| **be susceptible to** | 易受……影响 | Plants **are susceptible to** frost.（植物易受霜冻影响。） |
| **be exposed to** | 暴露于…… | Don't **be exposed to** the sun for too long.（不要在阳光下暴晒太久。） |

### 复习总结：口诀与一句话终极总结

> [!tip] 记忆口诀
> **“subject to 三兄弟：**
> **容易变（change）、等批准（approval）、受管辖（law）；**
> **to 是介词不带原形，subjected 才表被动！”**

> [!note] 一句话终极总结
> **“subject to 看语境：易变 / 须批 / 受管；to 是介词接名词或 -ing；subjected to 表被动遭经历。”**

**联动复习路径：**

1. 先在 [[#介词与连词：subject 与 to 的语法本质|语法本质]] 中确认 `to` 的词性。
2. 再到 [[#非谓语动词：to 是介词，后接名词、代词或动名词|非谓语判别]] 判断 `to` 后的名词、代词或动名词。
3. 最后在 [[#语态：be subject to 与 be subjected to 的区别|语态辨析]] 中区分形容词结构与被动语态。

## 心得

> [!tip] 阅读策略
> 文章前半部分主要呈现 DNA 检测的市场现象，后半部分通过 **But**、**Yet** 和 **In addition** 逐层引入质疑与问题。遇到主旨题时，应重点关注转折后的批评内容。

1. **抓住论证转折**：**But some observers are skeptical** 是观点转折点，后文的 **false precision** 直接指向祖源检测的局限。
2. **识别限定范围**：**only considers a single lineage** 和 **only as good as...** 都在限制商业检测结论的可靠范围。
3. **关注并列与省略**：**not...but rather...**、**either...or...**、**may be patented and not subject to...** 是理解句间逻辑和主干的关键。
4. **结合题干定位**：第 26—27 题关注 PTK 的可获得性和亲子鉴定用途；第 28—30 题集中考查祖源检测的准确性、数据库问题和文章主旨。

> [!warning] 易错点
> 不要把 **subject to** 中的 **to** 误判为不定式符号；这里的 **to** 是介词，后面可接名词、代词或动名词。
