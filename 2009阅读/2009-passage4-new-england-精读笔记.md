# New England Intellectual Life 精读笔记

## 背景

本文讨论17世纪新英格兰的知识生活与殖民社会。文章先介绍受过良好教育、具有影响力的牧师和政治领袖，再转向普通定居者，说明新英格兰社会内部存在明显的教育背景与宗教投入差异。

> [!abstract] 文章概要
> 文章并非简单歌颂清教领袖，而是通过“受教育的精英”与“普通定居者”的对照，展示新英格兰知识文化的形成过程及其局限。阅读时应关注作者如何用 John Dane 和沿海民众的例子修正对新英格兰人的单一化认识。

- **题源**：2009 年考研英语阅读真题 Passage 4
- **主题**：17 世纪新英格兰的知识生活、教育背景与宗教投入
- **体裁**：历史文化类议论文
- **难度**：intermediate
- **精读重点**：否定地点状语倒装、被动语态、非谓语动词、从句嵌套、比较省略与介词短语链

## 文章原文

# New England Intellectual Life

The most thoroughly studied **intellectuals** in the history of the New World are the ministers and political leaders of seventeenth-century New England. According to the standard history of American philosophy, nowhere else in colonial America was “so much importance attached to **intellectual pursuits**.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：According to the standard history of American philosophy, nowhere else in colonial America was “so much importance attached to **intellectual pursuits**.”
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: so much importance
> - 谓语 V: was attached
> - 宾语 O: 无（被动结构中没有宾语）
> - 补语 C: to **intellectual pursuits**（介词补足语）
> - 状语 A: According to the standard history of American philosophy；nowhere else in colonial America
> - 简化句：So much importance was attached to intellectual pursuits nowhere else in colonial America.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语（来源状语） | According to | 整个主句，说明判断依据 |
> | 介词短语（地点状语） | in | colonial America，说明范围 |
> | 副词短语（地点状语） | nowhere else | 整个被动结构，表示“其他任何地方都没有” |
> | 介词短语（补足语） | to | intellectual pursuits，说明重视的对象 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句（地点状语前置引起倒装）: [nowhere else in colonial America] + [was attached] + [so much importance]
>   ├── 来源状语: (According to the standard history of American philosophy)
>   ├── 地点状语前置: (nowhere else in colonial America)
>   └── 被动结构: was attached to intellectual pursuits
>         └── 介词补足语: (to intellectual pursuits)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：根据美国哲学的通行史，在殖民地美洲，没有任何其他地方像这里一样如此重视知识追求。
>
> **考点提示**：否定意义的地点状语 **nowhere else** 置于句首时，主句采用部分倒装，将系动词 **was** 提到主语 **so much importance** 之前；同时要识别 **attach importance to** 的被动形式。

According to many books and articles, New England’s leaders established the basic themes and preoccupations of an unfolding, dominant **Puritan tradition** in American intellectual life.

To take this approach to the New Englanders normally means to start with the Puritans' **theological innovations** and their distinctive ideas about the church—important subjects that we may not neglect.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：To take this approach to the New Englanders normally means to start with the Puritans' **theological innovations** and their distinctive ideas about the church—important subjects that we may not neglect.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: To take this approach to the New Englanders
> - 谓语 V: means
> - 宾语 O: 无（mean 后接不定式作补足成分）
> - 补语 C: to start with the Puritans' theological innovations and their distinctive ideas about the church
> - 状语 A: normally
> - 简化句：To take this approach normally means to start with important subjects.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 非谓语（不定式） | To take | 作主语，表示“采取这种方式” |
> | 介词短语 | to | approach，说明“针对新英格兰人” |
> | 非谓语（不定式） | to start | 作 mean 的补足成分，说明意味着什么 |
> | 介词短语 | with | start，说明从哪些内容开始 |
> | 介词短语 | about | ideas，说明观点涉及的对象 |
> | 同位语 | 破折号 | important subjects 同位说明前面的两类内容 |
> | 定语从句 | that | 修饰 important subjects；that 在从句中作 neglect 的宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [To take this approach to the New Englanders] + [normally means] + [to start with ...]
>   ├── 主语不定式: To take this approach to the New Englanders
>   ├── 状语: normally
>   └── 补足成分不定式: to start with
>         ├── with 的并列宾语: the Puritans' theological innovations
>         └── with 的并列宾语: their distinctive ideas about the church
>               └── 同位语: important subjects
>                     └── 定语从句: that we may not neglect
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：通常，采用这种方式来审视新英格兰人，就意味着要从清教徒的神学创新及其对教会的独特见解入手——这些都是我们不应忽视的重要议题。
>
> **考点提示**：句首 **To take...** 是不定式短语作主语；**mean to do** 表示“意味着做某事”。破折号后的 **important subjects** 是前文并列名词短语的同位语，末尾 **that** 引导定语从句且在从句中作宾语。

But in keeping with our examination of southern intellectual life, we may consider the original Puritans as carriers of European culture, adjusting to New World circumstances. The New England colonies were the scenes of important episodes in the pursuit of widely understood ideals of **civility** and **virtuosity**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The New England colonies were the scenes of important episodes in the pursuit of widely understood ideals of **civility** and **virtuosity**.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: The New England colonies
> - 谓语 V: were
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: the scenes
> - 状语 A: 无（后面的介词短语均修饰表语名词 scenes 或其内部名词）
> - 简化句：The New England colonies were the scenes.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语 | of | scenes，说明这些场所中发生的内容 |
> | 介词短语 | in | episodes，说明这些事件发生于何种活动中 |
> | 非谓语（过去分词） | widely understood | ideals，表示“被广泛认可的” |
> | 介词短语 | of | pursuit，说明所追求的具体理想 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [The New England colonies] + [were] + [the scenes]
>   └── 表语名词的后置修饰: of important episodes
>         └── 介词短语: in the pursuit
>               └── 介词短语: of widely understood ideals
>                     ├── 过去分词短语: widely understood → 修饰 ideals
>                     └── 介词短语: of civility and virtuosity → 修饰 ideals
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：新英格兰殖民地曾是许多重要事件的舞台；这些事件发生在追求普遍认同的文明与精湛技艺理想的过程中。
>
> **考点提示**：**be the scene(s) of...** 中 **of** 短语修饰表语名词 **scenes**，不能误判为谓语 **were** 的宾语；多个介词短语层层嵌套时，要沿着中心名词逐级确定修饰对象。**widely understood** 是过去分词短语作前置定语，可还原为 **ideals that are widely understood**。

The early settlers of Massachusetts Bay included men of impressive education and influence in England. Besides the ninety or so learned ministers who came to Massachusetts churches in the decade after 1629, there were political leaders like John Winthrop, an educated gentleman, lawyer, and official of the Crown before he journeyed to Boston.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Besides the ninety or so learned ministers who came to Massachusetts churches in the decade after 1629, there were political leaders like John Winthrop, an educated gentleman, lawyer, and official of the Crown before he journeyed to Boston.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: political leaders like John Winthrop
> - 谓语 V: were（there be 结构中的谓语）
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: 无（后置同位语不属于主句补语）
> - 状语 A: Besides the ninety or so learned ministers...；before he journeyed to Boston
> - 简化句：There were political leaders like John Winthrop.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 介词短语（范围/补充状语） | Besides | there were political leaders，表示“除……之外” |
> | 定语从句 | who | 修饰 learned ministers |
> | 介词短语（时间状语） | in | the decade，说明 came 的时间 |
> | 介词短语 | after | 1629，限定 decade |
> | 介词短语 | like | political leaders，举例说明领袖类型 |
> | 同位语 | 逗号 | an educated gentleman, lawyer, and official of the Crown 同位说明 John Winthrop |
> | 状语从句 | before | he journeyed to Boston，说明同位描述成立的时间背景 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句（there be 存在句）: There + [were] + [political leaders like John Winthrop]
>   ├── 补充状语: Besides the ninety or so learned ministers ...
>   │     └── 定语从句: who came to Massachusetts churches
>   │           └── 时间状语: in the decade after 1629
>   ├── 举例短语: like John Winthrop
>   └── 同位语: an educated gentleman, lawyer, and official of the Crown
>         └── 时间状语从句: before he journeyed to Boston
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：除了1629年后十年间来到马萨诸塞教会的约90名博学牧师外，还有约翰·温思罗普这样的政治领袖；在前往波士顿之前，他是一位受过教育的绅士、律师和王室官员。
>
> **考点提示**：**there be** 结构中真正的主语是其后的名词短语 **political leaders**；**who came...** 是修饰 ministers 的定语从句；John Winthrop 后的名词并列成分是同位语，不是新的谓语。

These men wrote and published extensively, reaching both New World and Old World audiences, and giving New England an atmosphere of **intellectual earnestness**.

We should not forget, however, that most New Englanders were less well educated. While few craftsmen or farmers, let alone dependents and servants, left literary compositions to be analyzed, it is obvious that their views were less fully intellectualized.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：While few craftsmen or farmers, let alone dependents and servants, left literary compositions to be analyzed, it is obvious that their views were less fully intellectualized.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: it（形式主语）
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: obvious
> - 状语 A: While few craftsmen or farmers, let alone dependents and servants, left literary compositions to be analyzed
> - 真正主语（后置主语）: that their views were less fully intellectualized
> - 简化句：It is obvious that their views were less fully intellectualized.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 状语从句（让步/对比） | While | 主句 it is obvious，说明背景 |
> | 插入成分 | let alone | dependents and servants，表示递进排除“更不用说” |
> | 非谓语（被动不定式） | to be analyzed | literary compositions，表示“可供分析” |
> | 名词性从句（主语从句后置） | that | is obvious，具体说明“显而易见”的内容 |
> | 副词 | less fully | intellectualized，修饰过去分词，表示程度 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [It] + [is] + [obvious]
>   └── 真正主语: that their views were less fully intellectualized
>         ├── 主语: their views
>         └── 谓语: were less fully intellectualized
> 状语从句: While [few craftsmen or farmers ... left literary compositions]
>   ├── 插入递进: let alone dependents and servants
>   └── 宾语补足成分: to be analyzed → literary compositions
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：虽然很少有手工业者或农民，更不用说被抚养者和仆人，留下可供分析的文学作品，但显而易见，他们的观点没有得到充分的理性化。
>
> **考点提示**：**It is obvious that...** 使用形式主语 **it**，真正的主语是后置的 **that** 从句；**while** 在此表示让步或对比；**let alone** 表示递进，“更不用说”。

Their thinking often had a traditional **superstitious** quality. A tailor named John Dane, who emigrated in the late 1630s, left an account of his reasons for leaving England that is filled with signs. Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope—all came together in a decisive moment when he opened the Bible, told his father that the first line he saw would settle his fate, and read the magical words: “Come out from among them, touch no unclean thing, and I will be your God and you shall be my people.”

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope—all came together in a decisive moment when he opened the Bible, told his father that the first line he saw would settle his fate, and read the magical words: “Come out from among them, touch no unclean thing, and I will be your God and you shall be my people.”
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope
> - 谓语 V: came together
> - 宾语 O: 无
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: in a decisive moment；when he opened..., told..., and read...
> - 简化句：Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope all came together in a decisive moment.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 同位/强调成分 | all | 回指并强调三个并列主语 |
> | 介词短语（时间状语） | in | a decisive moment，说明 came together 的时间 |
> | 定语从句（时间） | when | 修饰 a decisive moment |
> | 并列谓语 | opened, told, and read | when 从句的三个并列动作 |
> | 名词性从句（宾语从句） | that | told 的宾语，说明告诉父亲的内容 |
> | 定语从句 | he saw | 修饰 the first line，关系代词省略且作 saw 的宾语 |
> | 情态结构 | would settle | the first line he saw 的谓语，表示过去视角下的将来 |
> | 直接引语 | 冒号 | 说明 the magical words 的具体内容 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope] + [all came together] + [in a decisive moment]
>   └── 时间定语从句: when he opened the Bible, told his father ..., and read the magical words
>         ├── 并列谓语1: opened the Bible
>         ├── 并列谓语2: told his father
>         │     └── 宾语从句: that the first line he saw would settle his fate
>         │           └── 定语从句: he saw → 修饰 the first line
>         └── 并列谓语3: read the magical words
>               └── 直接引语: “Come out ... my people.”
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：性方面的困惑、经济上的挫折和宗教上的希望——这一切都汇聚在一个决定性的时刻：他打开《圣经》，告诉父亲，他看到的第一行字将决定自己的命运，然后读到以下带有魔力的话语：“你们务要从他们中间出来，与不洁净的不要接触；我就要作你们的上帝，你们也要作我的子民。”
>
> **考点提示**：主句使用三个并列名词作主语，破折号后的 **all** 起回指强调作用；**when** 引导修饰 moment 的时间定语从句，内部又包含三个并列谓语；**that** 引导 told 的宾语从句，且其中 **he saw** 是修饰 **the first line** 的定语从句。

One wonders what Dane thought of the careful sermons explaining the Bible that he heard in Puritan churches.

Meanwhile, many settlers had slighter religious commitments than Dane's, as one clergyman learned in confronting folk along the coast who mocked that they had not come to the New World for religion.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Meanwhile, many settlers had slighter religious commitments than Dane's, as one clergyman learned in confronting folk along the coast who mocked that they had not come to the New World for religion.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: many settlers
> - 谓语 V: had
> - 宾语 O: slighter religious commitments
> - 补语 C: 无
> - 状语 A: Meanwhile（语篇状语）；as one clergyman learned... 是对前述事实的补充说明从句，不计入主句主干
> - 比较成分：than Dane's（省略了 religious commitments，补全为 than Dane's religious commitments）
> - 简化句：Many settlers had slighter religious commitments than Dane's.
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------|----------|
> | 副词（时间/语篇状语） | Meanwhile | 整个主句，表示“与此同时” |
> | 比较结构 | than | slighter，比较定居者与 Dane 的宗教投入程度 |
> | 非限制性 as 从句（补充说明） | as | 指代前面“许多定居者宗教投入较少”这一事实；as 在从句中作 learned 的宾语 |
> | 介词短语（动名词结构） | in | learned，说明牧师了解到这一点的情境 |
> | 介词短语 | along | folk，说明民众所在的位置 |
> | 定语从句 | who | 修饰 folk；who 在从句中作主语 |
> | 名词性从句（宾语从句） | that | mocked 的宾语，说明民众嘲笑的内容 |
> | 介词短语 | to | come，说明前往的目的地 |
> | 介词短语 | for | come，说明来到新大陆的目的 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```text
> 主句: [Meanwhile] + [many settlers] + [had] + [slighter religious commitments] + [than Dane's]
>   └── 非限制性 as 从句（补充说明）: as one clergyman learned ___ in confronting folk ...
>         ├── 主干: one clergyman learned ___
>         ├── 情境状语: in confronting folk along the coast
>         │     ├── 地点修饰: along the coast → folk
>         │     └── 定语从句: who mocked ... → folk
>         │           └── 宾语从句: that they had not come to the New World for religion
>         │                 ├── 主语: they
>         │                 ├── 谓语: had not come
>         │                 ├── 目的地: to the New World
>         │                 └── 目的状语: for religion
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：与此同时，许多定居者的宗教投入不如戴恩的坚定，正如一位牧师在与沿海民众打交道时所了解到的那样；那些人嘲笑说，他们来到新大陆并不是为了宗教。
>
> **考点提示**：**as** 在此引导非限制性补充说明从句，指代前面的整个事实，并可理解为 **learned** 的宾语，不是原因状语。注意 **who mocked...** 修饰 **folk**，而 **that they had not come...** 是 **mocked** 的宾语从句；**than Dane's** 中省略了与前文相同的名词 **religious commitments**，**in confronting** 中 **confronting** 是动名词。

"Our main end was to catch fish."

## 翻译对照

# New England Intellectual Life 新英格兰的知识分子生活

The most thoroughly studied **intellectuals** in the history of the New World are the ministers and political leaders of seventeenth-century New England. According to the standard history of American philosophy, nowhere else in colonial America was “so much importance attached to **intellectual pursuits**.” According to many books and articles, New England’s leaders established the basic themes and preoccupations of an unfolding, dominant **Puritan tradition** in American intellectual life.

新大陆历史上受到最充分研究的**知识分子群体**，是17世纪新英格兰的牧师和政治领袖。根据美国哲学的标准史，在殖民地美洲的其他任何地方，都没有像这里一样“如此重视**知识追求**”。许多书籍和文章认为，新英格兰的领导者确立了一个不断发展且占据主导地位的**清教传统**在美国思想生活中的基本主题和关注重点。

To take this approach to the New Englanders normally means to start with the Puritans' **theological innovations** and their distinctive ideas about the church—important subjects that we may not neglect. But in keeping with our examination of southern intellectual life, we may consider the original Puritans as carriers of European culture, adjusting to New World circumstances. The New England colonies were the scenes of important episodes in the pursuit of widely understood ideals of **civility** and **virtuosity**.

采用这种方式看待新英格兰人，通常意味着要从清教徒的**神学创新**及其关于教会的独特观念入手——这些是我们不能忽视的重要议题。但与我们对南方知识生活的考察一致，我们也可以把最初的清教徒看作欧洲文化的承载者，在适应新大陆的环境。新英格兰殖民地见证了追求广义上的**文明**与**才艺**理想的重要事件。

The early settlers of Massachusetts Bay included men of impressive education and influence in England. Besides the ninety or so learned ministers who came to Massachusetts churches in the decade after 1629, there were political leaders like John Winthrop, an educated gentleman, lawyer, and official of the Crown before he journeyed to Boston. These men wrote and published extensively, reaching both New World and Old World audiences, and giving New England an atmosphere of **intellectual earnestness**.

马萨诸塞湾的早期定居者中，有一些人在英格兰受过良好教育并颇具影响力。除1629年后十年间来到马萨诸塞教会的约90名博学牧师外，还有约翰·温思罗普这样的政治领袖；他在前往波士顿之前，是一位受过教育的绅士、律师和王室官员。这些人广泛写作并出版，读者既有新大陆的受众，也有旧大陆的受众，从而赋予新英格兰一种浓厚的**求知氛围**。

We should not forget, however, that most New Englanders were less well educated. While few craftsmen or farmers, let alone dependents and servants, left literary compositions to be analyzed, it is obvious that their views were less fully intellectualized. Their thinking often had a traditional **superstitious** quality. A tailor named John Dane, who emigrated in the late 1630s, left an account of his reasons for leaving England that is filled with signs. Sexual confusion, economic frustrations, and religious hope—all came together in a decisive moment when he opened the Bible, told his father that the first line he saw would settle his fate, and read the magical words: “Come out from among them, touch no unclean thing, and I will be your God and you shall be my people.” One wonders what Dane thought of the careful sermons explaining the Bible that he heard in Puritan churches.

不过，我们不应忘记，大多数新英格兰人的受教育程度较低。虽然很少有手工业者或农民，更不用说被抚养者和仆人，留下可供分析的文学作品，但显而易见，他们的观点没有得到充分的理性化。他们的思想往往带有传统的**迷信**色彩。一位名叫约翰·戴恩的裁缝于17世纪30年代末移民，他留下了一份关于自己离开英格兰原因的记录，其中充满了各种征兆。性方面的困惑、经济上的挫折和宗教上的希望——这一切都汇聚在一个决定性的时刻：他打开《圣经》，告诉父亲，他看到的第一行字将决定自己的命运，然后读到以下带有魔力的话语：“你们务要从他们中间出来，与不洁净的不要接触；我就要作你们的上帝，你们也要作我的子民。”人们不禁会想，戴恩会如何看待他在清教徒教会听到的那些细致讲解《圣经》的布道。

Meanwhile, many settlers had slighter religious commitments than Dane's, as one clergyman learned in confronting folk along the coast who mocked that they had not come to the New World for religion. "Our main end was to catch fish."

与此同时，许多定居者的宗教信仰并不像戴恩那样虔诚。一位牧师在面对沿海民众时了解到，这些人嘲笑说，他们来到新大陆并不是为了宗教。“我们主要的目的可是捕鱼。”

## 语法要点

# New England Intellectual Life 语法整理

## 核心概览

本文的主要考点集中在**介词短语层层修饰**、**非谓语动词**、**定语从句与名词性从句嵌套**、**否定地点状语引起的部分倒装**以及 **there be** 存在句。阅读时应先找谓语和主干，再沿着介词短语、分词和从句逐层拆解。

> [!tip] 长难句通用拆解顺序
> 先圈出限定谓语，再确定主句主语；随后检查是否有倒装、形式主语、非谓语和从句。介词短语通常不能直接充当主句谓语的宾语，要回到中心名词判断修饰关系。

### 时态：一般过去时、过去完成时与过去将来时

文章主要叙述17世纪历史事实，因此大量使用一般过去时；当一个过去动作发生在另一个过去动作之前时，使用过去完成时；过去视角下的将来动作使用 **would + 动词原形**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **were / had / came / wrote** | 一般过去时，叙述历史事实或过去状态 | The New England colonies **were** the scenes of important episodes. |
| **had not come** | 过去完成时，表示在过去某一时刻之前已经发生或未发生的动作 | They **had not come** to the New World for religion. |
| **would settle** | 过去将来时，表示从过去视角看将要发生的动作 | The first line he saw **would settle** his fate. |

> [!note] 联动复习
> **would settle** 与定语从句 **he saw** 嵌套在宾语从句中；先找从句谓语，再判断其时间参照点。

### 语态：被动语态与被动不定式

被动语态强调承受动作的对象，而不是动作执行者。本文中既有一般过去时的被动结构，也有嵌入名词短语中的被动不定式。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **was attached to** | 一般过去时被动语态；主语是被重视的对象 | So much importance **was attached to** intellectual pursuits. |
| **were less fully intellectualized** | 一般过去时被动语态；说明观点没有被充分理性化 | Their views **were less fully intellectualized**. |
| **to be analyzed** | 被动不定式，作 literary compositions 的补足成分，表示“可供分析” | left literary compositions **to be analyzed** |

> [!warning] 易错点
> **attach importance to** 中的 **to** 是介词，后接名词；被动形式为 **importance is/was attached to...**，不能把 **to** 误判为不定式标记。

### 非谓语动词：不定式、动名词与分词

非谓语动词在本文中承担主语、补足成分、后置修饰和伴随说明等功能。判断逻辑主语时，要回到其所在短语的中心名词或主句主语。

| 形式 | 功能 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **To take...** | 不定式作主语 | **To take this approach to the New Englanders** normally means... |
| **to start...** | 不定式作 mean 的补足成分 | means **to start** with the Puritans' theological innovations |
| **in confronting...** | 介词 **in** 后接动名词，表示情境 | learned **in confronting** folk along the coast |
| **widely understood** | 过去分词作前置定语，可还原为 ideals that are widely understood | widely **understood** ideals |
| **adjusting...** | 现在分词作伴随状语，逻辑主语是 the original Puritans | Puritans as carriers of European culture, **adjusting** to New World circumstances |
| **reaching / giving** | 现在分词作伴随或结果说明，逻辑主语是 These men | These men wrote and published extensively, **reaching** ... and **giving** ... |
| **explaining** | 现在分词作后置定语，修饰 sermons | the careful sermons **explaining** the Bible |

> [!tip] 逻辑主语判断
> 分词短语位于主句后面时，通常优先寻找主句主语作为逻辑主语；**adjusting** 的逻辑主语是 Puritans，**reaching / giving** 的逻辑主语是 These men。

### 从句：定语从句

定语从句修饰名词或代词。本文既有关系代词作主语的定语从句，也有关系代词省略、关系副词 **when** 引导的时间定语从句。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **who + 谓语** | **who** 作从句主语，修饰 ministers | ministers **who came to Massachusetts churches** |
| **who + 谓语** | **who** 作从句主语，修饰 folk | folk along the coast **who mocked** ... |
| **that + 主语 + 谓语** | **that** 作从句宾语，修饰 subjects | important subjects **that we may not neglect** |
| **名词 + 主语 + 谓语** | 关系代词省略，先行词作从句宾语 | the first line **he saw** |
| **when + 完整句子** | 修饰 moment，表示事件发生的时间 | a decisive moment **when he opened the Bible** |
| **that is filled with signs** | **that** 作主语，修饰 account | an account ... **that is filled with signs** |

> [!warning] 定语从句与宾语从句辨析
> **folk who mocked that...** 中，**who mocked...** 是修饰 folk 的定语从句；其中 **that they had not come...** 又是 **mocked** 的宾语从句。判断 **that** 的作用，要先看它前面的谓语是否需要宾语。

### 从句：名词性从句

名词性从句可以充当主语、宾语或表语。本文重点包括形式主语结构和动词后的宾语从句。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **It is obvious that...** | **it** 是形式主语，that 从句是真正主语 | It is obvious **that their views were less fully intellectualized**. |
| **tell + 人 + that...** | **that** 引导 tell 的宾语从句 | told his father **that the first line he saw would settle his fate** |
| **mock that...** | **that** 引导 mock 的宾语从句，说明嘲笑的内容 | mocked **that they had not come to the New World for religion** |
| **as + 主语 + 谓语** | 非限制性补充说明从句，指代前面整个事实 | ..., **as one clergyman learned** ... |

> [!tip] 形式主语识别
> 看到 **It is + 形容词 + that 从句**，先把 **it** 标为形式主语，再将 that 从句还原到句首：**That their views were less fully intellectualized is obvious.**

> [!note] 联动复习
> **as** 从句与 **that** 宾语从句不能混淆：**as** 补充说明前面的整个事实，**that** 则直接充当 **tell / mock** 的宾语从句引导词。

### 从句：状语从句与让步、时间关系

状语从句修饰主句或主句谓语，本文出现 **while** 让步/对比从句、**before** 时间从句以及具有补充说明作用的 **as** 从句。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **While + 从句, 主句** | 表示让步或对比 | **While few craftsmen or farmers...**, it is obvious that... |
| **before + 从句** | 表示过去动作发生的时间背景 | an official of the Crown **before he journeyed to Boston** |
| **as + 从句** | 补充说明主句事实成立的依据或发现过程 | ..., **as one clergyman learned in confronting folk** ... |

> [!warning] while 不一定表示“当……时”
> 本文 **while** 后面强调“尽管很少有人留下记录”，与主句“他们的观点不够理性化”形成让步/对比关系，不能机械翻译成时间。

### 倒装与强调：否定地点状语前置

当具有否定意义的地点或范围状语 **nowhere else** 置于句首时，主句采用部分倒装，把系动词 **was** 放到主语 **so much importance** 之前。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **Nowhere else + be + 主语 + ...** | 否定地点状语前置引起部分倒装 | Nowhere else in colonial America **was** so much importance attached to intellectual pursuits. |
| **正常语序** | 还原后便于识别主干 | So much importance **was attached** to intellectual pursuits nowhere else in colonial America. |

> [!tip] 倒装还原法
> 先把 **nowhere else in colonial America** 移回句末，再找主语 **so much importance** 和被动谓语 **was attached**。

> [!note] 联动复习
> 否定地点状语前置与被动语态同时出现时，先识别倒装，再识别 **attach importance to** 的被动结构；不要因 **was** 提前而误判主语。

### 比较结构：比较级与名词省略

**slighter ... than Dane's** 中，**than Dane's** 省略了前文已经出现的名词 **religious commitments**，完整形式为 **slighter religious commitments than Dane's religious commitments**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **比较级 + than + 名词性所有格** | 比较两个对象的同类性质或程度，后面的中心名词可省略 | slighter religious commitments **than Dane's** |
| **Dane's** | 名词所有格，代替 Dane's religious commitments | than **Dane's** |

### 介词与连词：长定语链中的逻辑关系

阅读长句时，应先判断介词短语的中心词，而不是把所有 **of / in / to / for** 都看成谓语后的成分。

| 表达 | 功能 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **according to** | 表示信息来源或依据 | **According to** the standard history... |
| **in keeping with** | 表示与……一致 | **in keeping with** our examination... |
| **besides** | 表示“除……之外” | **Besides** the ninety or so learned ministers... |
| **in the pursuit of** | 表示“追求……” | episodes **in the pursuit of** widely understood ideals |
| **along the coast** | 表示地点，修饰 folk | folk **along the coast** |
| **for religion** | 表示目的或动机 | had not come to the New World **for religion** |
| **let alone** | 表示递进，“更不用说” | craftsmen or farmers, **let alone** dependents and servants |
| **than** | 引出比较对象 | slighter ... **than** Dane's |

> [!tip] 中心词定位
> 在 **episodes in the pursuit of ideals of civility and virtuosity** 中，先找中心词 **episodes**，再判断 **in the pursuit...** 修饰 episodes，**of civility and virtuosity** 修饰 ideals。

### 固定搭配：高频动词与名词组合

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **attach importance to** | 重视…… | importance was **attached to** intellectual pursuits |
| **in pursuit of** | 追求…… | in the **pursuit of** widely understood ideals |
| **come together** | 汇聚在一起 | Sexual confusion ... **came together** |
| **settle one's fate** | 决定某人的命运 | would **settle his fate** |
| **think of** | 想到；认为 | One wonders what Dane **thought of** the sermons. |
| **come out from among** | 从……中出来、脱离…… | “**Come out from among** them...” |
| **leave sth. to be analyzed** | 留下某物供分析 | left literary compositions **to be analyzed** |

> [!note] 搭配辨析
> **attach importance to** 中的 **to** 是介词；**in pursuit of** 中的 **of** 引出追求对象。固定搭配的介词不能仅凭中文直译替换。

## 综合复习路径

1. 先找主句限定谓语：**were / had / is / came**。
2. 检查句首是否有倒装触发词：如 **nowhere else**。
3. 圈出非谓语：区分不定式、动名词和分词，并判断逻辑主语。
4. 给每个从句找引导词和先行词/中心谓语。
5. 最后处理介词短语、比较省略和固定搭配。

> [!tip] 本文核心联动
> **否定地点状语倒装 ↔ 被动语态**；**形式主语 ↔ 名词性从句**；**定语从句 ↔ 介词短语后置修饰**；**比较结构 ↔ 名词省略**。

| **leave sth. to be analyzed** | phr. | 留下某物供分析 | Few craftsmen or farmers left literary compositions **to be analyzed**. |
| **let alone** | conj. phr. | 更不用说 | Few craftsmen or farmers, **let alone** dependents and servants, left literary compositions... |
| **mock** | v. | 嘲笑；嘲弄 | The folk **mocked** that they had not come to the New World for religion. |
| **nowhere else** | adv. phr. | 其他任何地方都没有 | **Nowhere else** in colonial America was so much importance attached to intellectual pursuits. |
| **Puritan tradition** | n. phr. | 清教传统 | New England's leaders established the basic themes of a dominant **Puritan tradition**. |
| **pursuit** | n. | 追求；从事 | The New England colonies were scenes of episodes in the **pursuit** of ideals. |
| **religious commitments** | n. phr. | 宗教投入；宗教信仰程度 | Many settlers had slighter **religious commitments** than Dane's. |
| **settle one's fate** | phr. | 决定某人的命运 | The first line he saw would **settle his fate**. |
| **scenes** | n. | 场所；发生地；舞台 | The New England colonies were the **scenes** of important episodes. |
| **slighter** | adj. | 较少的；较弱的；较轻的 | Many settlers had **slighter** religious commitments than Dane's. |
| **superstitious** | adj. | 迷信的 | Their thinking often had a traditional **superstitious** quality. |

**三、语境理解**

根据上下文，选择粗体词在原文中的准确含义：

1. “Nowhere else in colonial America was so much importance attached to intellectual pursuits.” 中 **nowhere else** 的含义是：
   - A. 在某个偏远地方
   - B. 在其他任何地方都没有
   - C. 在新大陆之外
   - D. 在同一个地方反复发生

2. “The New England colonies were the scenes of important episodes in the pursuit of widely understood ideals of civility and virtuosity.” 中 **virtuosity** 的含义是：
   - A. 宗教虔诚
   - B. 政治影响力
   - C. 精湛技艺或高超技巧
   - D. 家庭财富

3. “Many settlers had slighter religious commitments than Dane's.” 中 **commitments** 的含义是：
   - A. 宗教投入或信仰程度
   - B. 文学作品
   - C. 法律判决
   - D. 经济收入

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. **B** — **nowhere else** 表示“其他任何地方都没有”，与句首倒装结构共同构成否定范围。
> 2. **C** — **virtuosity** 指在某项技艺上的高超水平，本文与 **civility** 并列作理想的内容。
> 3. **A** — **religious commitments** 指一个人对宗教的投入、虔诚程度或信仰承诺。

## 心得

> [!tip] 主旨定位
> 文章先写新英格兰的牧师和政治领袖，再写普通定居者，形成“精英—大众”的对照。作者真正强调的是：新英格兰的知识生活受到鼓励，但参与者的教育程度和宗教投入并不一致。

> [!tip] 结构识别
> 第一段提出传统观点，第二、三段补充清教徒和知识精英的文化作用，第四、五段通过 John Dane 及沿海民众揭示普通人的不同面貌。遇到主旨题，应优先概括这种“精英成就 + 大众差异”的双层结构。

> [!warning] 易错点
> - **nowhere else ... was ...** 是否定地点状语前置引起的部分倒装，不要把 **nowhere else** 当作主语。
> - **It is obvious that...** 中 **it** 是形式主语，真正主语是后置的 **that** 从句。
> - **who mocked that...** 中，**who** 引导修饰 **folk** 的定语从句，**that** 再引导 **mocked** 的宾语从句。
> - **than Dane's** 省略了 **religious commitments**，不能把 Dane's 理解成独立的比较对象。

> [!question] 复习反思
> 作者为什么既介绍受教育的领导者，又补充普通定居者的情况？这组对照如何服务于文章主旨？
