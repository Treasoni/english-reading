# Fairness in Capuchin Monkeys 卷尾猴的公平意识 精读笔记

## 背景

本文选自2005年考研英语阅读第一篇，主题为动物行为学领域——**卷尾猴的公平意识**。文章通过 Sarah Brosnan 和 Frans de Waal 发表在《自然》杂志上的实验研究，探讨了猴子是否具有与人类相似的公平感和义愤情绪。

> [!note] 文章特点
> - 科普类议论文，结构清晰：提出现象 → 实验设计 → 实验结果 → 结论与展望
> - 考研命题重点：细节题 + 推断题，命题人倾向考察因果逻辑和对比关系
> - 来源：*Nature*（《自然》杂志），学术类阅读的经典文章来源

## 文章原文

# Fairness in Capuchin Monkeys

Everybody loves a **fat pay rise**. Yet pleasure at your own can **vanish** if you learn that a colleague has been given a bigger one. Indeed, if he has a reputation for **slacking**, you might even be **outraged**. Such behaviour is regarded as "all too human", with the underlying assumption that other animals would not be capable of this finely developed sense of **grievance**. But a study by Sarah Brosnan and Frans de Waal of Emory University in Atlanta, Georgia, which has just been published in *Nature*, suggests that it is all too monkey, as well.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Yet pleasure at your own can vanish if you learn that a colleague has been given a bigger one.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | S (主语) | pleasure at your own |
> | V (谓语) | can vanish |
> | A (状语) | if you learn that a colleague has been given a bigger one |
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 | 说明 |
> |------|--------|----------|------|
> | 介词短语 | at | pleasure | 限定主语，"自己的那份（加薪）" |
> | 条件状语从句 | if | 主句 can vanish | 说明喜悦感消失的前提条件 |
> | 宾语从句 | that | learn | 充当 learn 的宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: [Yet] pleasure (at your own) can vanish
>   ├── 介短: at your own → 修饰 pleasure（"自己的那份"）
>   └── 状从(条件): if you learn (that a colleague has been given a bigger one)
>         └── 名从(宾语): that a colleague has been given a bigger one → learn 的宾语
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：但如果你得知某个同事加薪更多，你那份加薪的喜悦感就会消失。
>
> **考点提示**：
> > [!tip]- 考研语法考点
> > 1. **条件状语从句嵌套宾语从句**
> > 2. **被动语态**：`has been given`
> > 3. **比较替代**：`a bigger one` 中的 `one`

>

The researchers studied the behaviour of female brown **capuchin** monkeys. They look cute. They are **good-natured, co-operative** creatures, and they share their food readily. Above all, like their female human **counterparts**, they tend to pay much closer attention to the value of "goods and services" than males.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Above all, like their female human counterparts, they tend to pay much closer attention to the value of "goods and services" than males.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | S (主语) | they |
> | V (谓语) | tend to pay |
> | O (宾语) | much closer attention |
> | A (方向状语) | to the value of "goods and services" |
> | A (比较状语) | than males |
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词/标志 | 修饰对象 | 说明 |
> |------|-------------|----------|------|
> | 连接性状语 | Above all | 整个主句 | 递进关系 |
> | 介词短语(类比) | like | 整个主句 | 与人类女性类比 |
> | 介词短语(方向) | to | attention | 固定搭配 pay attention to |
> | 介词短语(所属) | of | value | 限定归属 |
> | 比较状语(省略) | than | 整个谓语 | 比较级结构 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: [Above all,] (like...), they tend to pay much closer attention (to ...) than males
>   ├── 连接性状语: Above all
>   ├── 介短(类比): like their female human counterparts
>   ├── 介短(方向): to the value (of "goods and services")
>   └── 比较状语(省略): than males
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：最重要的是，与人类女性一样，它们往往比雄性更关注"商品和服务"的价值。
>
> **考点提示**：
> > [!tip]- 考研语法考点
> > 1. **比较结构省略**
> > 2. **like vs as 区分**
> > 3. **连接性状语 Above all**

Such characteristics make them perfect candidates for Dr. Brosnan's and Dr. de Waal's study. The researchers spent two years teaching their monkeys to exchange **tokens** for food. Normally, the monkeys were happy enough to exchange pieces of rock for slices of cucumber. However, when two monkeys were placed in separate but **adjoining chambers**, so that each could observe what the other was getting in return for its rock, their behaviour became markedly different.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：However, when two monkeys were placed in separate but adjoining chambers, so that each could observe what the other was getting in return for its rock, their behaviour became markedly different.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | S (主语) | their behaviour |
> | V (谓语) | became |
> | C (主语补足语) | markedly different |
> | A (时间状语从句) | when two monkeys were placed in separate but adjoining chambers, so that each could observe what the other was getting in return for its rock |
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 | 说明 |
> |------|--------|----------|------|
> | 连接性状语 | However | 整个主句 | 转折逻辑 |
> | 时间状语从句 | when | 主句 became | 设定时间条件 |
> | 目的状语从句 | so that | when 从句 | 说明安排目的 |
> | 宾语从句 | what | observe | what = the thing that |
> | 介词短语 | in return for | getting | 说明交换方式 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: [However,] their behaviour became markedly different
>   └── 状从(时间): when two monkeys were placed...
>         └── 状从(目的): so that each could observe (what...)
>               └── 名从(宾语): what the other was getting
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：然而，当两只猴子被放置在分开但相邻的隔间中，以便每只都能观察到对方用石块换到了什么时，它们的行为变得明显不同。
>
> **考点提示**：
> > [!tip]- 考研语法考点
> > 1. **so that 目的状语从句 vs 结果状语从句**
> > 2. **时间状语从句中的被动语态**
> > 3. **what 引导的名词性从句**

In the world of capuchins, grapes are **luxury goods** (and much preferable to cucumbers). So when one monkey was handed a grape in exchange for her token, the second was **reluctant** to hand hers over for a mere piece of cucumber. And if one received a grape without having to provide her token in exchange at all, the other either tossed her own token at the researcher or out of the chamber, or refused to accept the slice of cucumber. Indeed, the mere presence of a grape in the other chamber (without an actual monkey to eat it) was enough to induce **resentment** in a female capuchin.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：So when one monkey was handed a grape in exchange for her token, the second was reluctant to hand hers over for a mere piece of cucumber.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | S (主语) | the second |
> | V (谓语) | was |
> | C (表语) | reluctant |
> | A (不定式补足语) | to hand hers over for a mere piece of cucumber |
> | A (时间状语从句) | when one monkey was handed a grape in exchange for her token |
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 | 说明 |
> |------|--------|----------|------|
> | 连接性状语 | So | 整个主句 | 因果逻辑 |
> | 时间状语从句 | when | 主句 was reluctant | 设定前提条件 |
> | 介词短语 | in exchange for | was handed | 获取方式 |
> | 不定式短语(补足) | to | reluctant | 说明不愿意的行为 |
> | 介词短语 | for | hand over | 交换标的物 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: [So] (when...), the second was reluctant (to hand hers over for...)
>   ├── 状从(时间): when one monkey was handed a grape (in exchange for...)
>   └── 不定式短语(补足): to hand hers over (for...)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：所以当一只猴子用代币换到一颗葡萄时，另一只就不愿意为了区区一片黄瓜而交出她的代币。
>
> **考点提示**：
> > [!tip]- 考研语法考点
> > 1. **被动语态的双宾语结构**
> > 2. **形容词补足语 (to do)**
> > 3. **省略**：`the second` 后省略 `monkey`

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：And if one received a grape without having to provide her token in exchange at all, the other either tossed her own token at the researcher or out of the chamber, or refused to accept the slice of cucumber.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | S (主语) | the other |
> | V1 (谓语1) | tossed |
> | O1 (宾语1) | her own token |
> | A1 (地点状语) | at the researcher or out of the chamber (并列) |
> | V2 (谓语2) | refused |
> | O2 (宾语2) | to accept the slice of cucumber |
> | A (条件状语从句) | if one received a grape without having to provide her token in exchange at all |
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 | 说明 |
> |------|--------|----------|------|
> | 连接词 | And | 整个主句 | 并列衔接 |
> | 条件状语从句 | if | 主句 | 设定前提 |
> | 介词短语(否定伴随) | without | received | 否定伴随 |
> | 动名词短语 | having to provide...at all | without 的宾语 | 作介词宾语 |
> | 介词短语 | at the researcher | tossed | 方向 |
> | 介词短语 | out of the chamber | tossed | 方向 |
> | 不定式短语(宾语) | to accept | refused | 充当宾语 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: [And] (if...), the other either tossed ... or refused ...
>   ├── 状从(条件): if one received a grape (without having to provide her token in exchange at all)
>   └── 并列谓语:
>         ├── [either] tossed her own token (at.../out of...)
>         └── [or] refused (to accept...)
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如果一只猴子完全不用交出代币就得到了葡萄，另一只要么把自己的代币扔向研究人员或扔出隔间，要么拒绝接受那片黄瓜。
>
> **考点提示**：
> > [!tip]- 考研语法考点
> > 1. **并列谓语 either...or...**
> > 2. **without + 动名词**
> > 3. **at all 强调否定**
> > 4. **条件状语从句嵌套复杂介词结构**

The researchers suggest that capuchin monkeys, like humans, are guided by **social emotions**. In the wild, they are a co-operative, group-living species. Such co-operation is likely to be stable only when each animal feels it is not being cheated. Feelings of **righteous indignation**, it seems, are not the preserve of people alone. Refusing a lesser reward completely makes these feelings abundantly clear to other members of the group. However, whether such a **sense of fairness** evolved independently in capuchins and humans, or whether it **stems** from the common ancestor that the species had 35 million years ago, is, as yet, an unanswered question.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Refusing a lesser reward completely makes these feelings abundantly clear to other members of the group.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | S (主语) | Refusing a lesser reward |

> | O (宾语) | these feelings |
> | C (宾语补足语) | abundantly clear |
> | A (方向/对象状语) | to other members of the group |
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 标志词 | 修饰对象 | 说明 |
> |------|--------|----------|------|
> | 动名词短语(主语) | Refusing | 整个谓语 | 表抽象行为 |
> | 副词 | completely | makes | 程度 |
> | 副词 | abundantly | clear | 程度 |
> | 介词短语(对象) | to | makes clear | 向谁表达 |
> | 介词短语(所属) | of | members | 限定群体归属 |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> 主句: (Refusing a lesser reward) [completely] makes these feelings [abundantly] clear (to...)
>   ├── 动名词(主语): Refusing a lesser reward
>   ├── 副: completely → 修饰 makes
>   ├── 宾补: [abundantly] clear

>
> **参考译文**：完全拒绝较少的报酬，可以清楚地向群体中的其他成员传达这些感受。
>
> **考点提示**：
> > [!tip]- 考研语法考点
> > 1. **动名词短语作主语**
> > 2. **make + 宾语 + 形容词(宾补)**
> > 3. **副词修饰的层级关系**

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：However, whether such a sense of fairness evolved independently in capuchins and humans, or whether it stems from the common ancestor that the species had 35 million years ago, is, as yet, an unanswered question.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | S (主语) | whether such a sense of fairness evolved independently in capuchins and humans, or whether it stems from the common ancestor that the species had 35 million years ago |

>

>
> | 类型 | 引导词 | 修饰对象 | 说明 |
> |------|--------|----------|------|
> | 连接性状语 | However | 整个主句 | 转折逻辑 |
> | 主语从句1 | whether | 主句 is | "各自独立进化" |

> 主句: [However,] (whether ... or whether ...) is, [as yet], an unanswered question

> > [!tip]- 考研语法考点

> > 2. **并列主语从句的识别**
> > 3. **定语从句关系词作宾语**
> > 4. **as yet 固定短语**

## 翻译对照

# Fairness in Capuchin Monkeys 卷尾猴的公平意识

Everybody loves a **fat pay rise**. Yet pleasure at your own can vanish if you learn that a colleague has been given a bigger one. Indeed, if he has a reputation for slacking, you might even be **outraged**. Such behaviour is regarded as "all too human", with the underlying assumption that other animals would not be capable of this finely developed sense of **grievance**. But a study by Sarah Brosnan and Frans de Waal of Emory University in Atlanta, Georgia, which has just been published in *Nature*, suggests that it is all too monkey, as well.

人人都喜欢大幅加薪。但如果你得知某个同事加薪更多，你的喜悦感就会消失。事实上，如果他还有偷懒的名声，你甚至可能会感到**愤慨**。这种行为被认为是"人之常情"，其潜在假设是其他动物不会有这种高度发达的**不满意识**。但是，由佐治亚州亚特兰大埃默里大学的 Sarah Brosnan 和 Frans de Waal 进行的一项研究——刚刚发表在《自然》杂志上——表明，这也是"猴之常情"。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> "all too human" 直译为"太人类了"，意译为"人之常情"；对应的 "all too monkey" 译为"猴之常情"，形成呼应。

---

The researchers studied the behaviour of female brown **capuchin** monkeys. They look cute. They are **good-natured, co-operative** creatures, and they share their food readily. Above all, like their female human counterparts, they tend to pay much closer attention to the value of "goods and services" than males.

研究人员研究了雌性棕色**卷尾猴**的行为。它们看起来很可爱。它们是**性情温和、善于合作**的动物，并且乐于分享食物。最重要的是，与人类女性一样，它们往往比雄性更关注"商品和服务"的价值。

---

Such characteristics make them perfect candidates for Dr. Brosnan's and Dr. de Waal's study. The researchers spent two years teaching their monkeys to exchange **tokens** for food. Normally, the monkeys were happy enough to exchange pieces of rock for slices of cucumber. However, when two monkeys were placed in separate but adjoining chambers, so that each could observe what the other was getting in return for its rock, their behaviour became markedly different.

这些特征使它们成为 Brosnan 博士和 de Waal 博士研究的理想对象。研究人员花了两年时间教这些猴子用**代币**交换食物。通常情况下，猴子们很乐意用石块换取黄瓜片。然而，当两只猴子被放置在分开但相邻的隔间中，使每只都能观察到对方用石块换到了什么时，它们的行为变得明显不同。

---

In the world of capuchins, grapes are **luxury goods** (and much preferable to cucumbers). So when one monkey was handed a grape in exchange for her token, the second was **reluctant** to hand hers over for a mere piece of cucumber. And if one received a grape without having to provide her token in exchange at all, the other either tossed her own token at the researcher or out of the chamber, or refused to accept the slice of cucumber. Indeed, the mere presence of a grape in the other chamber (without an actual monkey to eat it) was enough to induce **resentment** in a female capuchin.

在卷尾猴的世界里，葡萄是**奢侈品**（比黄瓜受欢迎得多）。所以当一只猴子用代币换到一颗葡萄时，另一只就不**愿意**为了一片黄瓜而交出她的代币。如果一只猴子完全不用交出代币就得到了葡萄，另一只要么把自己的代币扔向研究人员或扔出隔间，要么拒绝接受那片黄瓜。事实上，仅仅在另一个隔间里出现一颗葡萄（即使没有猴子去吃它），就足以引起雌性卷尾猴的**不满**。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> "reluctant" 在否定语境中译为"不愿意"比"不情愿"更符合实验情境——猴子明确地拒绝交易。

---

The researchers suggest that capuchin monkeys, like humans, are guided by **social emotions**. In the wild, they are a co-operative, group-living species. Such co-operation is likely to be stable only when each animal feels it is not being cheated. Feelings of **righteous indignation**, it seems, are not the preserve of people alone. Refusing a lesser reward completely makes these feelings abundantly clear to other members of the group. However, whether such a **sense of fairness** evolved independently in capuchins and humans, or whether it stems from the common ancestor that the species had 35 million years ago, is, as yet, an unanswered question.

研究人员认为，卷尾猴和人类一样，受**社会情感**的引导。在野外，它们是合作性的群居物种。只有当每只动物都感到自己没有受骗时，这种合作才可能保持稳定。**义愤**感似乎并非人类独有。完全拒绝较少的报酬，可以向群体中的其他成员清楚地传达这些感受。然而，这种**公平意识**是在卷尾猴和人类身上各自独立进化而来，还是源于3500万年前这两个物种的共同祖先，目前仍是一个未解之谜。

## 语法要点

### 固定搭配：表示"源于"的同义替换

本篇文章末句出现了多个表示"源自/来源于"的动词短语，是考研阅读中高频出现的同义替换考点。

> [!warning] 同义替换必记
> 以下四组短语均表示"源于"，在考研阅读中可互换使用，但词源色彩略有不同：
> - **stem from** — 最学术，强调因果关系
> - **originate from** — 最正式，强调起源出处
> - **spring from** — 最形象，强调突然涌现
> - **come from** — 最基础，通用表达

| 结构 | 词源联想 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **stem from** | stem 名词"茎"→ 动词"像草木那样从某处长出来" | Whether such a sense of fairness **stems from** the common ancestor... |
| **come from** | 最基础的"来自"表达 | (通用表达，适用于多数语境) |
| **spring from** | spring 名词"泉水/春天"→ 动词"像泉水一样涌出" | The idea **springs from** a deep understanding of human nature. |
| **originate from** | 名词 origin 派生而来，强调起源地/起源时间 | The custom **originates from** an ancient tradition. |

> [!tip] 统一公式
> **A + stem/come/spring/originate from + B**
> - **核心逻辑**：A 是结果，B 是原因
> - **脑内快速画箭头**：B → A（因为有 B，才导致了 A）

---

### 补充要点：考研阅读因果逻辑命题点

> [!warning]- ⚠️ 超级重要：颠倒因果是考研阅读的高频陷阱
> 考研阅读最喜欢在选项里 **颠倒因果**。如果文章说"A 源于 B"，错误选项通常会写成"A 导致了 B"（即把 `B → A` 写成 `A → B`）。
>
> **避坑策略**：
> - 看到 `stem from` / `come from` / `spring from` / `originate from` 这类词
> - 立即判断：谁是因、谁是果？
> - 在原文中标注箭头方向 `B → A`
> - 与选项逐一核对因果关系是否匹配

> 本文采用 **引子（人类现象）→ 类比过渡（猴子也有）→ 实验设计 → 实验结果 → 结论与开放问题** 的经典科普论证结构：
> 1. **引言段（P1）**：从人类加薪心理引出主题，通过"all too human"→"all too monkey"的类比过渡到动物研究
> 2. **实验对象介绍（P2）**：解释为何选择雌性卷尾猴
> 3. **实验过程（P3）**：代币训练 → 对比实验设置
> 4. **实验结果（P4）**：三组对比——公平交换 vs 不公平→拒绝 vs 不劳而获→愤慨
> 5. **结论（P5）**：公平感非人类独有 → 开放性问题——独立进化还是同源？
