# 2005 Passage 4 — Formal English 精读笔记

## 背景

本文选自2005年考研英语阅读第四篇，主题为**语言文化**领域。文章以 John McWhorter 的著作 *Doing Our Own Thing: The Degradation of Language and Music and Why We Should, Like, Care* 为切入点，探讨了20世纪60年代反文化运动（counter-culture）对正式英语衰落的影响。

> [!note] 文章特点
> - 书评类议论文：先介绍观点 → 补充论证 → 让步转折 → 结论升华
> - 考研命题重点：细节题 + 推理题，尤其关注作者态度与观点区分
> - 文化背景浓厚，涉及语言学、社会变迁和价值观冲突
> - 核心人物：John McWhorter（语言学家，兼有自由与保守观点）

---

## 文章原文

## Text 4

Americans no longer expect public figures, whether in speech or in writing, to **command the English language** with skill and gift. Nor do they aspire to such command themselves. In his latest book, *Doing Our Own Thing: The Degradation of Language and Music and Why We Should, Like, Care*, John McWhorter, a linguist and controversialist of mixed liberal and conservative views, sees the triumph of 1960s **counter-culture** as responsible for the **decline of formal English**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Americans no longer expect public figures, whether in speech or in writing, to command the English language with skill and gift.
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | 主语 | Americans |
> | 谓语 | expect |
> | 宾语 | public figures |
> | 宾语补足语 | to command the English language |
> | 插入语（修饰 public figures） | whether in speech or in writing |
> | 方式状语（修饰 command） | with skill and gift |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> Americans
> ├── no longer expect（谓语，表否定）
> ├── public figures（宾语）
> │   └── whether in speech or in writing（插入语，表让步）
> └── to command the English language（宾语补足语）
>     └── with skill and gift（方式状语）
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：美国人不再期望公众人物在演讲或写作中有技巧、有天赋地掌握英语。
>
> **考点提示**：
> - "whether...or..." 结构引导插入语，考研阅读中常作"无论是……还是……"理解
> - "expect sb. to do sth." 是重要搭配，expect 后接不定式作宾语补足语
> - "no longer" 否定词置于谓语前，注意否定范围

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：In his latest book, *Doing Our Own Thing: The Degradation of Language and Music and Why We Should, Like, Care*, John McWhorter, a linguist and controversialist of mixed liberal and conservative views, sees the triumph of 1960s counter-culture as responsible for the decline of formal English.
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | 主语 | John McWhorter |
> | 同位语 | a linguist and controversialist of mixed liberal and conservative views |
> | 谓语 | sees |
> | 宾语 | the triumph of 1960s counter-culture |
> | 宾语补足语 | as responsible for the decline of formal English |
> | 状语 | In his latest book, *Doing Our Own Thing:...* |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> [状语] In his latest book, *Doing Our Own Thing:...*
> [主语] John McWhorter
>   └── [同位语] a linguist and controversialist of mixed liberal and conservative views
> [谓语] sees
> [宾语] the triumph of 1960s counter-culture
> [宾补] as responsible for the decline of formal English
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：约翰·麦克沃特在其新书《做自己的事：语言和音乐的退化以及我们为什么应该——嗯——在意》中，这位兼具自由和保守观点的语言学家和辩论家，认为20世纪60年代反文化的胜利导致了正式英语的衰落。
>
> **考点提示**：
> - 主语和谓语之间被长的同位语分隔，考研阅读中常设"打断结构"题，需快速找到主谓
> - "see...as..." 视……为……，考研高频动词短语
> - 长书名含多重并列结构（"The Degradation of Language and Music and Why We Should, Like, Care"），注意并列项的划分

Blaming the permissive 1960s is nothing new, but this is not yet another criticism against the decline in education. Mr. McWhorter's academic speciality is language history and change, and he sees the gradual disappearance of "whom", for example, to be natural and no more regrettable than the loss of the **case-endings** of Old English.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Mr. McWhorter's academic speciality is language history and change, and he sees the gradual disappearance of "whom", for example, to be natural and no more regrettable than the loss of the case-endings of Old English.
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | 并列分句1 — 主语 | Mr. McWhorter's academic speciality |
> | 并列分句1 — 谓语 | is |
> | 并列分句1 — 表语 | language history and change |
> | 并列分句2 — 主语 | he |
> | 并列分句2 — 谓语 | sees |
> | 并列分句2 — 宾语 | the gradual disappearance of "whom" |
> | 并列分句2 — 宾语补足语 | to be natural and no more regrettable than the loss of the case-endings of Old English |
> | 插入语 | for example |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> [并列分句1]
> Mr. McWhorter's academic speciality
> └── is language history and change
> [并列连词] and
> [并列分句2]
> he
> └── sees
>     ├── the gradual disappearance of "whom"（宾语）
>     │   └── ,for example,（插入语）
>     └── to be natural and no more regrettable than...（宾补）
>         └── the loss of the case-endings of Old English（比较对象）
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：麦克沃特的学术专长是语言史和语言演变，他认为——比如"whom"一词的逐渐消失是自然的，并不比古英语中格尾的消失更令人惋惜。
>
> **考点提示**：
> - "no more...than..." 结构 = "和……一样不……"，表示前后两者都被否定，这是考研高频考点
> - "see...to be..." 为 "see...as..." 的变体，意为"认为……是……"
> - "for example" 作为插入语打断句子，阅读时可先忽略，提取主谓宾主干

While even the modestly educated sought an elevated tone when they put pen to paper before the 1960s, even the most well regarded writing since then has sought to capture spoken English on the page. Equally, in poetry, the highly personal, **performative genre** is the only form that could claim real liveliness. In both oral and written English, ==talking is triumphing over speaking, spontaneity over craft==.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：While even the modestly educated sought an elevated tone when they put pen to paper before the 1960s, even the most well regarded writing since then has sought to capture spoken English on the page.
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | 主句 — 主语 | even the most well regarded writing |
> | 主句 — 谓语 | has sought |
> | 主句 — 宾语 | to capture spoken English on the page |
> | 主句 — 时间状语 | since then |
> | 让步状语从句 — 主语 | even the modestly educated |
> | 让步状语从句 — 谓语 | sought |
> | 让步状语从句 — 宾语 | an elevated tone |
> | 嵌套时间状语从句 | when they put pen to paper before the 1960s |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> [While 让步状语从句]
> While even the modestly educated
> └── sought an elevated tone
>     └── [嵌套时间状语从句] when they put pen to paper before the 1960s
> [主句]
> even the most well regarded writing
> ├── since then（时间状语）
> └── has sought to capture spoken English on the page
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：在20世纪60年代之前，即使是受过一般教育的人在落笔时也追求高雅的格调；而自那以后，即使是最受好评的文章也力求在书面上捕捉口语。
>
> **考点提示**：
> - "While" 在此表示对比/让步，不是"当……时"，考研阅读常考 while 的这层含义
> - "the modestly educated" = "the + adj." 表示一类人：受过一般教育的人
> - "before the 1960s" 与 "since then" 形成时间对比，是段落主旨的关键——1960s 前后写作风格的对比

Illustrated with an entertaining array of examples from both high and low culture, the trend that Mr. McWhorter documents is unmistakable. But it is less clear, to take the question of his subtitle, why we should, like, care. As a linguist, he acknowledges that all varieties of human language, including non-standard ones like Black English, can be powerfully expressive — there exists no language or dialect in the world that cannot convey complex ideas. He is not arguing, as many do, that we can no longer think straight because we do not talk proper.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Illustrated with an entertaining array of examples from both high and low culture, the trend that Mr. McWhorter documents is unmistakable.
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | 主语 | the trend |
> | 定语从句（修饰 trend） | that Mr. McWhorter documents |
> | 系动词 | is |
> | 表语 | unmistakable |
> | 状语（过去分词短语） | Illustrated with an entertaining array of examples from both high and low culture |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> [状语] Illustrated with an entertaining array of examples
>         from both high and low culture
>   └── 过去分词短语作状语，逻辑主语 = 主句主语 the trend
> [主语] the trend
>   └── [定语从句] that Mr. McWhorter documents
> [系动词] is
> [表语] unmistakable
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：麦克沃特先生用一系列来自高雅文化和通俗文化的有趣例子来说明，他所记录的趋势是确凿无误的。
>
> **考点提示**：
> - 句首过去分词短语作状语，其逻辑主语等于主句主语，考研阅读中需准确识别修饰关系
> - "that Mr. McWhorter documents" 为定语从句，先行词是 trend，that 在从句中作宾语
> - "unmistakable" — 前缀 un- + mistake + -able，否定前缀派生词，注意词汇推导能力

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：He is not arguing, as many do, that we can no longer think straight because we do not talk proper.
>
> | 成分 | 内容 |
> |------|------|
> | 主语 | He |
> | 谓语 | is not arguing |
> | 插入语 / 方式状语从句 | as many do |
> | 宾语从句 | that we can no longer think straight because we do not talk proper |
> | 宾语从句 — 主语 | we |
> | 宾语从句 — 谓语 | can no longer think |
> | 宾语从句 — 状语 | straight |
> | 宾语从句内嵌 — 原因状语从句 | because we do not talk proper |
>
> **结构图解**：
> ```
> [主语] He
> [谓语] is not arguing
>   ├── [插入语] ,as many do,（方式状语从句）
>   └── [宾语从句] that we can no longer think straight
>         └── [原因状语从句] because we do not talk proper
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：他并没有像许多人那样认为——因为我们说话不规范，我们就再也不能正确思考了。
>
> **考点提示**：
> - "as many do" 为 as 引导的方式状语从句作插入语，可先跳过抓主干
> - 多重否定："not arguing"（不主张）+ "no longer"（不再）+ "not...proper"（不规范）= 需要理清否定层次
> - "think straight" — straight 作副词，意为"清晰地/有条理地"
> - "talk proper" — 作者有意使用不规范语法（proper 应为 properly）来呼应文章主题：正式语的退化

Russians have a deep love for their own language and carry large chunks of memorized poetry in their heads, while Italian politicians tend to elaborate speech that would seem old-fashioned to most English-speakers. Mr. McWhorter acknowledges that formal language is not strictly necessary, and proposes no radical education reforms — he is really grieving over the loss of something beautiful more than useful. We now take our English "on **paper plates instead of china**". A shame, perhaps, but probably an inevitable one.

## 翻译对照

## Text 4

Americans no longer expect public figures, whether in speech or in writing, to **command the English language** with skill and gift. Nor do they aspire to such command themselves. In his latest book, *Doing Our Own Thing: The Degradation of Language and Music and Why We Should, Like, Care*, John McWhorter, a linguist and controversialist of mixed liberal and conservative views, sees the triumph of 1960s **counter-culture** as responsible for the **decline of formal English**.

美国人不再期望公众人物在演讲或写作之时可以运用技巧和文采来驾驭英语，而公众人物自己也不渴求有这样的能力。约翰·麦克沃特是一名语言学家同时又是混杂着自由和保守观点的好争论者。在他的新书《做自己的事——语言和音乐的退化，以及为什么我们应该，呃，在意》中，他认为20世纪60年代反文化运动的胜利要为正式英语的衰退负责。

> [!note] 书名翻译
> "Why We Should, Like, Care"中的"like"是口语中的插入语（类似中文的"呃"），译文用"呃"保留原文的随意口吻，呼应书中对非正式英语的讨论主题。

---

Blaming the permissive 1960s is nothing new, but this is not yet another criticism against the decline in education. Mr. McWhorter's academic speciality is language history and change, and he sees the gradual disappearance of "whom", for example, to be natural and no more regrettable than the loss of the **case-endings** of Old English.

指责放纵的60年代并不是什么新鲜事，但这次算不上是对教育衰退的又一场批判。麦克沃特先生的学术专长在于语言史和语言演变。举例来说，他认为"whom"一词的逐渐消失是很自然的，并不比古英语中词格尾缀的消失更让人惋惜。

---

But the **cult of the authentic and the personal**, "doing our own thing", has spelt the death of formal speech, writing, poetry and music. While even the modestly educated sought an elevated tone when they put pen to paper before the 1960s, even the most well regarded writing since then has sought to capture spoken English on the page. Equally, in poetry, the highly personal, **performative genre** is the only form that could claim real liveliness. In both oral and written English, ==talking is triumphing over speaking, spontaneity over craft==.

然而，"做自己的事"这一崇尚真实和个性的信条，已经导致了正式的演讲、写作、诗歌和音乐的消亡。在20世纪60年代以前，甚至那些仅受过一般教育的人在下笔时都要寻求一种更高雅的腔调，但从那以后，即使是最受好评的文章也开始逮住口语就往纸上写。同样，在诗歌领域，高度个性化和富有表现力的创作风格是唯一可以表达真正活力的形式。不管是在应用口语还是书面语中，随意的言谈胜过了正式的言辞，而自我发挥也压过了精心准备。

> [!note] 对比结构
> "talking is triumphing over speaking, spontaneity over craft" — talking（随意言谈）vs speaking（正式言辞），spontaneity（自我发挥）vs craft（精心准备），两组对比层层递进，中文以"胜过""压过"对应。

---

Illustrated with an entertaining array of examples from both high and low culture, the trend that Mr. McWhorter documents is unmistakable. But it is less clear, to take the question of his subtitle, why we should, like, care. As a linguist, he acknowledges that all varieties of human language, including non-standard ones like Black English, can be powerfully expressive — there exists no language or dialect in the world that cannot convey complex ideas. He is not arguing, as many do, that we can no longer think straight because we do not talk proper.

麦克沃特先生从上层和下层的文化中列举了一系列有趣的例子，由此可知他所记录的这种趋势是明确无误的。但其副标题所提的问题："为什么我们应该，呃，在意？"答案还不够清晰。作为一名语言学家，他认为所有类型的人类语言，其中包括黑人英语这样的非标准语言都有很强的表达力——世界上不存在任何不能表达复杂思想的语言或方言。不像其他大多数人，麦克沃特先生并不认为我们说话不规范就会使我们不再能准确地思考。

---

Russians have a deep love for their own language and carry large chunks of memorized poetry in their heads, while Italian politicians tend to elaborate speech that would seem old-fashioned to most English-speakers. Mr. McWhorter acknowledges that formal language is not strictly necessary, and proposes no radical education reforms — he is really grieving over the loss of something beautiful more than useful. We now take our English "on **paper plates instead of china**". A shame, perhaps, but probably an inevitable one.

俄罗斯人深爱着自己的语言，并在脑海中记下了大量诗歌，而意大利的政客们则往往精心准备演讲，即使此举对大多数讲英语的人来说已经过时。麦克沃特先生认为正式英语并非绝对必要，也没有提出进行彻底的教育改革——他只是为那些美好事物而不是实用品的逝去而哀伤。我们现在把英语放在"纸盘子"而非"瓷盘子"上。或许，这是可悲的，但可能又是不可避免。

> [!note] 比喻翻译
> "paper plates instead of china" — 纸盘子（一次性、实用但廉价）vs 瓷盘子（精美、可反复使用但昂贵）。比喻口语化英语与正式英语的区别：前者实用便捷，后者精美优雅。

## 语法要点

> [!note] 本文核心语法主题
> 本篇涉及 9 类核心语法点：否定倒装、比较结构、While 对比、过去分词作状语、双重否定、插入语、同位语、whether...or...结构、平行省略。以下逐类展开，每类均附原文例句与中文翻译。

---

### 1. 否定倒装

> [!tip] 否定词 / 半否定词置于句首时，主谓需部分倒装（助动词 + 主语 + 谓语）。

| 结构 | 原文例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|------|
| Nor + 助动词 + 主语 + 谓语 | **Nor do they aspire to such command themselves.** | "Nor" 为否定副词，位于句首引起倒装；主语 "they" 与助动词 "do" 互换位置。 |

**译文**：他们自己也不渴望掌握这样的语言能力。

> [!warning] 易错提醒
> 注意 "Nor" 引导倒装时，前面必须是否定分句。"Nor" 不能单独开始一个段落或篇章——它是对前文否定内容的延续。本文第一句为 "Americans **no longer** expect..."（否定），所以第二句才能用 "Nor" 接续。

**类似结构一览**：

| 否定词 | 正常语序 | 倒装语序 |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Nor | They do not aspire to... either. | Nor do they aspire to... |
| Never | I have never seen... | Never have I seen... |
| Not only | He is not only a linguist... | Not only is he a linguist... |
| No sooner | I had no sooner arrived... | No sooner had I arrived... |

---

### 2. 比较结构

#### 2.1 no more...than...（否定比较）

> [!note] "no more regrettable than" 不是"比……更不遗憾"，而是"和……一样不遗憾"——对两者同时否定。

| 结构 | 原文例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|------|
| no more + adj. + than + n. | ...he sees the gradual disappearance of "whom" ... to be natural and **no more regrettable than the loss of the case-endings of Old English**. | "no more regrettable than the loss" = "与古英语格尾消失一样不值得遗憾"；对"whom 的消失"和"古英语格尾消失"两者都持否定态度。 |

**译文**：他认为"whom"的逐渐消失是自然的，与古英语格尾消失一样不值得遗憾。

> [!tip] 区分三组易混比较结构
>
> | 结构 | 含义 | 例句 |
> |------|------|------|
> | not more...than... | 不比……更……（侧重比较） | This is not more regrettable than that.（这个不比那个更遗憾。） |
> | **no more...than...** | 和……一样不……（两者否定） | This is no more regrettable than that.（这个和那个一样不遗憾。） |
> | not so much...as... | 与其说……不如说…… | He is grieving over the loss of something beautiful **more than** useful.（他哀悼的是美丽之物，而非实用之物。） |

#### 2.2 more...than...（取舍比较）

| 结构 | 原文例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|------|
| something + adj.1 + more than + adj.2 | ...grieving over the loss of something beautiful **more than** useful. | "more than" 在此不是"比……更多"，而是"与其说……不如说"的取舍含义。 |

**译文**：他哀悼的是一种美丽多于实用的事物的消失。

---

### 3. While 引导对比

> [!warning] While 的三种含义辨析
> - **时间**："当……时候"（同时发生）
> - **让步**："虽然"（等于 although）
> - **对比**："而，然而"（前后对比两种相反情况）

本文使用 **对比含义**：

| 结构 | 原文例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|------|
| While + clause A, clause B (contrast) | **While even the modestly educated sought an elevated tone when they put pen to paper before the 1960s, even the most well regarded writing since then has sought to capture spoken English on the page.** | "While" 连接两个对比分句：分句 A = 1960 年代前（连受教育一般的人也用高雅的笔调）；分句 B = 1960 年代后（连最受推崇的写作也追求口语化）。前后形成时间与行为双重重对比。 |

**译文**：在 20 世纪 60 年代以前，连受教育程度一般的人在动笔时都追求高雅笔调；而自那以后，连最受推崇的写作也追求在书面上捕捉口语。

> [!note] 同一段落中 While 的第二处对比
>
> | 原文 | 说明 |
> |------|------|
> | Russians have a deep love for their own language... **while** Italian politicians tend to elaborate speech... | 同样是对比——俄罗斯人热爱本国语言 vs 意大利政客的繁琐讲话。 |

---

### 4. 过去分词作状语

> [!tip] 过去分词短语（V-ed）置于句首时，一般作**原因、条件、时间、伴随**状语。其逻辑主语是主句的主语，且与主语之间存在**被动关系**。

| 结构 | 原文例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|------|
| V-ed + ..., + 主句 | **Illustrated with an entertaining array of examples from both high and low culture, the trend that Mr. McWhorter documents is unmistakable.** | "Illustrated" = "被（各种例子）所例证"，逻辑主语是主句主语 "the trend"，表被动（= The trend is illustrated with...）。翻译时可处理为"通过……的例证"。 |

**译文**：通过一系列来自高雅和通俗文化的有趣例证，麦克沃特先生所证明的趋势是确凿无疑的。

> [!warning] 过去分词 vs 现在分词
>
> | 类型 | 语态 | 含义 | 例 |
> |------|------|------|-----|
> | **Illustrated** with...（过去分词） | 被动 | "被……例证" | Illustrated with examples, the trend is clear. |
> | **Illustrating**...（现在分词） | 主动 | "例证了……" | Illustrating the trend, he provided many examples. |
>
> 判断方法：看逻辑主语和动词的关系——主语是动作的**承受者**则用 V-ed，是**发出者**则用 V-ing。

---

### 5. 双重否定

> [!note] 双重否定 = 两个否定词叠加，结果 = 强烈肯定。这是一种**强调手段**，比直接肯定语气更强。

| 结构 | 原文例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|------|
| no + n. + that/who + cannot / 否定词 + 否定词 | ...**there exists no language or dialect in the world that cannot convey complex ideas.** | 双重否定 = "There exists **no** language... that **cannot** convey" = "所有语言都能表达复杂思想"。比直接说 "Every language can convey" 语气更强。 |

**译文**：世界上不存在任何一种语言或方言不能表达复杂的思想。

> [!tip] 考研常见双重否定结构
>
> | 结构 | 含义 | 强度 |
> |------|------|------|
> | cannot...too... | 再……也不为过 | 强 |
> | no...without... | 没有……就不…… | 强 |
> | not...unless... | 除非……否则不…… | 条件性 |
> | no...that cannot... | 没有……不能……的 | 强（本文用法） |
> | never...but... | 每当……总…… | 强 |

---

### 6. 插入语

本文共出现 4 处不同类型的插入语：

#### 6.1 不定式短语作插入语

| 原文 | 插入语 | 功能 |
|------|--------|------|
| But it is less clear, **to take the question of his subtitle**, why we should, like, care. | to take the question of his subtitle | 不定式短语作插入语，表示"拿……来说"，类似 "to take ... as an example"。 |

**译文**：但若拿他副标题所提出的问题来说——我们为何"应该，比如，在意"——就不那么清楚了。

#### 6.2 副词性插入语

| 原文 | 插入语 | 说明 |
|------|--------|------|
| he sees the gradual disappearance of "whom", **for example**, to be natural | for example | 最常见的插入语之一，用于举例说明。 |

#### 6.3 方式状语从句作插入语（as 插入结构）

| 原文 | 插入语 | 说明 |
|------|--------|------|
| He is not arguing, **as many do**, that we can no longer think straight because we do not talk proper. | as many do | "as many do" = "as many people argue"（正如许多人那样）。插入句中对主句内容进行补充说明。 |

**译文**：他并非像许多人那样，争论说因为我们说话不标准，我们就无法再清晰地思考了。

#### 6.4 语气词"like"作插入语

| 原文 | 插入语 | 说明 |
|------|--------|------|
| why we should, **like**, care | like | 语气词 "like" 在这里作为口语化的插入语，表示"比如说""大概"等模糊语气。作者 McWhorter 故意使用这个口语词来呼应书名的反讽意味。 |

> [!warning] 插入语在考研阅读中的考点
> 插入语常常将一个完整句子的主干拆散，增加长难句阅读难度。阅读策略：
> 1. 先用括号或逗号标出插入语
> 2. 先读主干（去掉插入语后的完整句子）
> 3. 再回头看插入语的含义
>
> 例如：He is not arguing, ~~as many do,~~ that we can no longer think straight...
> 去掉插入语后主干为："He is not arguing that we can no longer think straight" — 是一个清晰的宾语从句嵌套原因状语从句的结构。

### 7. 同位语

> [!tip] 同位语是对前面名词的补充说明，与前面名词**在语法上成分相同**，通常用逗号与主句隔开。

| 原文 | 同位语 | 说明 |
|------|--------|------|
| John McWhorter, **a linguist and controversialist of mixed liberal and conservative views**, sees the triumph... | a linguist and controversialist of mixed liberal and conservative views | 对 John McWhorter 的职业和学术立场的补充说明。核心名词 = a linguist and controversialist，后接 of 短语修饰。 |

**译文**：约翰·麦克沃特——一位兼有自由和保守观点的语言学家与辩论家——认为……

> [!note] 同位语在写作中的应用
> 同位语可以让句子更紧凑。试比较：
> - **啰嗦版**：John McWhorter is a linguist and controversialist. He has mixed liberal and conservative views. He sees the triumph...
> - **精练版**：John McWhorter, a linguist and controversialist of mixed liberal and conservative views, sees the triumph...
>
> 考研英语写作中适当使用同位语，可以展现语言驾驭能力。

---

### 8. Whether...or... 结构

> [!note] "whether...or..." 表示"无论是……还是……"，引导**选择性的让步状语**，相当于 "no matter whether...or..."。

| 结构 | 原文例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|------|
| whether + prep. phrase + or + prep. phrase | Americans no longer expect public figures, **whether in speech or in writing**, to command the English language... | "whether in speech or in writing" 是对 "public figures" 的范围进行限定——无论是在演讲中还是在写作中。 |

**译文**：美国人不再期望公众人物——无论是在演讲中还是在写作中——娴熟地驾驭英语。

> [!tip] Whether...or... 在考研中的常见变体
>
> | 形式 | 例 |
> |------|-----|
> | whether A or B（完整从句） | whether you agree or disagree |
> | whether A or B（省略主语和系动词） | whether in speech or in writing（= whether they are in speech or in writing） |
> | whether A or not / whether or not | whether or not formal language is necessary |

---

### 9. 平行省略

> [!tip] "平行结构" 指在并列连词（and, or, but）连接的成分中，前后结构保持一致。"省略" 指后一部分重复的词可省去，避免冗余。

| 原文 | 平行结构 | 省略内容 |
|------|----------|----------|
| talking is triumphing over speaking, **spontaneity over craft** | spontaneity (is triumphing) over craft | 省略了重复的 "is triumphing"。完整形式为："spontaneity is triumphing over craft"。 |

**译文**：在口语和书面语中，随意的交谈胜过了正式的演讲，即兴发挥胜过了精心雕琢。

> [!warning] 平行省略的判断方法
> 看到 "A is doing X, B over C" 这种格式时要高度警惕：B 和 C 之间没有谓语动词，说明前面平行结构的谓语部分被省略了。还原方法：找到第一个平行结构的谓语，复制到第二个结构中。
>
> 本文案例：
> - 第一个平行：talking **is triumphing over** speaking
> - 第二个平行：spontaneity **[is triumphing over]** craft
>
> 还原后：spontaneity **is triumphing over** craft

### 10. 定语从句

本文出现多处定语从句，分两类：

#### 10.1 that 引导的限制性定语从句

| 原文 | 先行词 | 关系词 | 说明 |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| the only form **that could claim real liveliness** | the only form | that | 当先行词被 "the only" 修饰时，关系代词只能用 "that" 不能用 "which"。 |
| the trend **that Mr. McWhorter documents** | the trend | that | 关系代词 "that" 在从句中作宾语（= documents the trend），可省略。 |
| elaborate speech **that would seem old-fashioned to most English-speakers** | elaborate speech | that | 关系代词 "that" 在从句中作主语（= the speech would seem...），不可省略。 |

#### 10.2 双重否定中的定语从句

| 原文 | 先行词 | 关系词 | 说明 |
|------|--------|--------|------|
| there exists no language or dialect in the world **that cannot convey complex ideas** | no language or dialect | that | 先行词被 "no" 修饰，形成"no...that cannot"双重否定结构。关系代词在从句中作主语。 |

> [!note] 定语从句关系词选择记忆口诀
> 只用 that 不用 which 的情况：**前有 only, 前有最高级, 前有序数词, 不定代词先行**
> 只用 which 不用 that 的情况：**介词提前时, 非限制性定语**

---

### 11. 动名词作主语

> [!note] 动名词（V-ing）作主语时，谓语动词用**第三人称单数**形式。

| 结构 | 原文例句 | 说明 |
|------|----------|------|
| V-ing... + is/are... | **Blaming the permissive 1960s** is nothing new... | "Blaming the permissive 1960s" 是动名词短语作主语，谓语 "is" 用单数。 |

**译文**：归咎于放纵的 20 世纪 60 年代并不新鲜。

> [!tip] 动名词作主语 vs 不定式作主语
>
> | 主语形式 | 特点 | 例 |
> |----------|------|-----|
> | 动名词 V-ing | 泛指、一般性行为 | **Blaming** the 1960s is nothing new.（归咎于 60 年代——泛指这种行为） |
> | 不定式 to V | 特指、具体某一次 | **To blame** the 1960s would be unfair.（归咎于 60 年代——特指这一次） |
>
> 本文使用动名词，强调"归咎于 60 年代"这一普遍现象，而非具体的某次指责。

### 12. 固定搭配与词组

#### 12.1 动词短语

| 搭配 | 原文例句 | 含义 |
|------|----------|------|
| command the English language | ...expect public figures...to **command the English language** with skill and gift. | 娴熟掌握英语（command = 驾驭、掌握） |
| aspire to | Nor do they **aspire to** such command themselves. | 渴望、追求 |
| see...as... | ...sees the triumph of 1960s counter-culture **as** responsible for... | 将……视为…… |
| see...to be... | ...sees the gradual disappearance of "whom" ... **to be** natural... | 认为……是……（see...as / see...to be 可互换） |
| spell the death of | ...has **spelt the death of** formal speech... | 导致……的终结（spell 的过去式 = spelt） |
| put pen to paper | ...when they **put pen to paper** before the 1960s... | 动笔写作 |
| capture spoken English | ...has sought to **capture spoken English** on the page. | 捕捉/再现口语 |
| take...instead of... | We now take our English "on paper plates **instead of** china". | 用……而不是……（比喻用法） |
| grieve over | ...he is really **grieving over** the loss of something beautiful more than useful. | 为……感到悲伤/惋惜 |
| think straight | ...we can no longer **think straight**... | 清晰地思考 |

#### 12.2 名词短语

| 搭配 | 原文 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|
| decline of formal English | ...sees the triumph of 1960s counter-culture as responsible for the **decline of formal English**. | 正式英语的衰落 |
| cult of the authentic and the personal | But the **cult of the authentic and the personal**... | 对真实与个性的崇尚 |
| an entertaining array of examples | **Illustrated with an entertaining array of examples**... | 一系列有趣的例证 |
| paper plates instead of china | We now take our English "on **paper plates instead of china**". | 纸盘而非瓷盘（比喻：一次性 vs 精致）|

#### 12.3 形容词短语与介词短语

| 搭配 | 原文 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|
| responsible for | ...sees...as **responsible for** the decline of formal English. | 对……负责 / 是……的原因 |
| no more...than... | ...**no more** regrettable **than** the loss... | 和……一样不…… |
| in both...and... | **In both** oral **and** written English... | 在……和……两方面 |
| old-fashioned to | ...elaborate speech that would seem **old-fashioned to** most English-speakers. | 对……来说显得过时 |

#### 12.4 强调表达

| 表达 | 原文 | 功能 |
|------|------|------|
| nothing new | Blaming the permissive 1960s is **nothing new**... | 强调"并非新鲜事" |
| yet another | ...this is not **yet another** criticism... | "又一个"——表达"不过又是一次……" |
| unmistakable | ...the trend...is **unmistakable**. | "确凿无疑"——加强肯定语气 |

## 心得

> [!tip] 语言学类文章的阅读策略
> 考研英语阅读中涉及语言学主题的文章往往含有大量长难句和抽象概念。阅读此类文章时，需特别注意：
> - **区分作者观点 vs 引述观点**：本文中 McWhorter 的观点是"正式英语衰落源于60年代反文化运动"，而作者对此的态度较为中立——既承认趋势的存在（"unmistakable"），又对其深层意义存疑（"less clear"）。
> - **注意双重否定和高频比较结构**："no more...than..."、"not arguing...that...because..."等结构是命题人的重点设题区域。
> - **修辞手法识别**：本文结尾的"paper plates instead of china"是精妙的比喻，常考推断题。

> [!warning] 常见误区
> 1. 将 "While" 错误理解为"当……时"而非"虽然/而"——本文 The third paragraph 开头的 "While" 是考研高频考点，表示让步对比而非时间。
> 2. 将 "no more...than..." 误解为"不比……更……"——实际含义是"和……一样不……"，两者都否定。
> 3. 忽视 "as many do" 插入语——误以为 McWhorter 持有"不规范语言导致无法清晰思考"的观点，实际这正是他所反对的。

> [!question] 深度思考
> - McWhorter 既认为正式英语的衰落是不可逆转的（"probably an inevitable one"），又为此感到惋惜（"grieving over the loss"），这是否是一种矛盾？这种矛盾态度在学术写作中常见吗？
> - 如何理解作者在文末"paper plates instead of china"这个比喻？是否暗示了现代英语虽更实用但失去了精致性？
> - 对比俄罗斯人和意大利人对语言的态度（第四段开头），作者想说明什么？是否暗示正式语言的珍视度与文化的保守性相关？

## 思考题

1. According to the text, what does John McWhorter see as the main cause of the decline of formal English?
   > A) The decline in education standards.
   > B) The triumph of 1960s counter-culture.
   > C) The loss of case-endings in Old English.
   > D) The influence of Black English and non-standard dialects.

2. The phrase "talking is triumphing over speaking, spontaneity over craft" (Paragraph 3) suggests that:
   > A) spoken English is becoming more important than written English.
   > B) informal, spontaneous expression is replacing formal, crafted language.
   > C) people now prefer talking to writing.
   > D) modern writing requires more spontaneity than craft.

3. What is McWhorter's attitude toward the loss of formal English?
   > A) He strongly advocates for radical education reforms.
   > B) He believes it will impair people's ability to think clearly.
   > C) He regrets it but considers it inevitable.
   > D) He celebrates it as a sign of cultural progress.

4. The analogy of "paper plates instead of china" (last paragraph) is used to illustrate that modern English is:
   > A) more durable and practical.
   > B) more expensive but less beautiful.
   > C) more convenient but less refined.
   > D) both necessary and inevitable.

> [!note]- 参考答案
> 1. **B** — 第一段末尾明确说明：McWhorter "sees the triumph of 1960s counter-culture as responsible for the decline of formal English"。A 错在这是作者否定的观点（"this is not yet another criticism against the decline in education"）。
> 2. **B** — 该句是对前文"正式语退化、口语化盛行"的总结概括。talking（随意言谈）vs speaking（正式讲话），spontaneity（即兴发挥）vs craft（精心雕琢）。
> 3. **C** — 最后一段："he is really grieving over the loss of something beautiful more than useful"（惋惜）→ "A shame, perhaps, but probably an inevitable one"（但认为不可避免）。
> 4. **C** — 纸盘子（一次性、实用但廉价）vs 瓷盘子（精美、可反复使用）。比喻现代英语实用便捷（convenient）但失去了精致与优雅（less refined）。
