# 健康应用与数据隐私：35 万个健康应用背后的监管缺口 精读笔记

## 背景

本文选自 **2024 年考研英语阅读 Passage 4**，讨论**健康应用与个人健康数据隐私**：当 35 万个健康应用随时收集心率、睡眠、生育等敏感数据时，现行以医院病历为对象的隐私法是否还够用。

文章沿"**现象 → 法律缺口 → 案例 → 监管困境 → 出路**"展开，属典型的**问题—分析—对策型（problem–analysis–solution）**结构：

- **现象（P1）**：健康应用无处不在，市场上 35 万个应用中仅 2020 年就上线 9 万个。
- **法律缺口（P2）**：个人信息如何被收集、保护、共享并不透明；HIPAA 只覆盖医院、医生办公室、诊所与保险公司，应用与可穿戴设备得不到同等保护。
- **案例（P3）**：Flo Health 在明确隐私声明之下掌控用户生育数据并与第三方共享，FTC 以《同意令》要求其先取得用户明确肯定同意，并指示第三方删数据。
- **监管困境（P4）**：FTC 依第 5 条只能在公司误导或造成不合理损害后"事后"执法，而应用上市的速度使挑战格外巨大。
- **出路（P5）**：联邦立法短期无望，各州先行；加州领跑，弗吉尼亚、科罗拉多、犹他跟进。

**难度说明**：较高。句式上集中出现**两处 `while` 让步**（`While these apps have a great deal to offer ...` / `While the FTC is doing what it can ...`）、**三处省略关系代词的定语从句**、**`the way` 后省略 `that/in which`**、**数词 + `of which`**、**介词 + `which`**、**`it` 作形式主语**、**现在分词后置定语**、**不定式作宾语补足语**，以及**名词化与弱化表达**（`may / potentially / seems / suggest / alleged`）。论证上适合练习**同义改写识别**（`express affirmative consent` ↔ `explicit permission`）、**"覆盖范围"与"执法力度"的概念区分**，以及**正反混淆类干扰项**的排除。

## 文章原文

If you look at the apps on your phone, chances are you have at least one related to your health—and probably several.

Whether it is a mental health app, a fitness tracker, a connected health device or something else, many of us are taking advantage of this technology to keep better track of our health in some shape or form.

Recent research from the Organization for the Review of Care and Health Applications found that 350,000 **health apps** were available on the market, 90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Recent research from the Organization for the Review of Care and Health Applications found that 350,000 **health apps** were available on the market, 90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Recent research
> - 后置定语: from the Organization for the Review of Care and Health Applications
> - 谓语 V: found
> - 宾语从句 O: that 350,000 health apps were available on the market
> - 宾语从句主干: 350,000 health apps + were + available
> - 非限制性定语从句: 90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | from the Organization ... | research |
> | 宾语从句 | that | found |
> | 数词 + of which 引导的非限制性定语从句 | 90,000 of which | 350,000 health apps |
> | 形容词作表语 | available | were |
> | 介词短语作地点状语 | on the market | were available |
> | 时间状语 | in 2020 alone | launched |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Recent research (from the Organization ...)〕+ found + 〔that 宾语从句〕
>   ├── 宾从主干: 〔350,000 health apps〕+ were + 〔available〕+ (on the market)
>   │     └── 非限定定从: 90,000 of which + launched + (in 2020 alone)
>   │           ※ which = 350,000 health apps，从存量中拆出 9 万增量
>   └── 数据功能: 35 万（存量）+ 9 万（2020 单年增量）→ 渲染"普遍性"
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：医疗与健康应用评估组织最近的一项研究发现，市场上可用的健康应用有 35 万个，其中仅 2020 年就有 9 万个上线。
>
> **考点提示**：① `found that ...` 是**宾语从句**，`that` 在正式书面语中通常保留（语法上仍可省略），从句内部 `350,000 health apps were available` 自带主谓；② `90,000 of which launched` 是**"数词 + of which"引导的非限制性定语从句**，`which` 指 `350,000 health apps`，不是指 `the market`；③ `launched` 在此是**不及物动词过去式**"上线、推出"，不是被动语态；④ `alone` 意为"仅仅、单是"，不是"独自"；⑤ 本句功能是**用数据渲染普遍性**，第 36 题 [A] `the prevalence of health apps` 即其同义概括，[C] `the popularity of smartphones` 只抓了句首的手机，属**以偏概全**。

While these apps have a great deal to offer, it is not always clear how the personal information we input is collected, **safeguarded** and shared online.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：While these apps have a great deal to offer, it is not always clear how the personal information we input is collected, **safeguarded** and shared online.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 让步状语从句 A: While these apps have a great deal to offer
> - 形式主语: it
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: not always clear
> - 真正主语（主语从句）: how the personal information we input is collected, safeguarded and shared online
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 让步状语从句 | While | 主句 |
> | 形式主语 | it | is not always clear |
> | 主语从句（真正主语） | how | 形式主语 it |
> | 省略关系代词的定语从句 | (that/which) | the personal information |
> | 平行被动 | is + collected / safeguarded / shared | the personal information |
> | 地点状语 | online | is shared |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔it〕+ is + 〔not always clear〕+ 〔how 主语从句〕
>   ├── 让步状语从句: While + 〔these apps〕+ have + 〔a great deal to offer〕→ 先扬后抑
>   └── 主语从句: 〔the personal information (we input)〕+ is + collected, safeguarded and shared + (online)
>         ├── 省略关系代词: we input → 修饰 information
>         └── 三个过去分词共用 is → 平行被动：被收集、被保护、被共享
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：尽管这些应用能提供很多东西，但我们输入的个人信息如何被收集、保护和在线共享，却并不总是清晰透明。
>
> **考点提示**：① `While` 在句首且主句用 `not always clear` 转折，是**让步**"尽管"，不是"当……时"；② `it is not always clear how ...` 中 `it` 是**形式主语**，真正主语是 `how` 引导的**主语从句**，从句用陈述语序，**不倒装**；③ `the personal information we input` 是**省略关系代词**的定语从句，`we input` 不是主句谓语；④ `is collected, safeguarded and shared` 是**三个过去分词共用一个 `is`** 的平行被动，翻译时三个动作要分别落地；⑤ `not always clear` 是**部分否定**（`not always` = "并不总是"），不是全部否定——作者并未说"完全不清楚"，这为下文批评"法律覆盖不足"留出余地。

Existing health privacy law, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, is primarily focused on the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Existing health privacy law, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, is primarily focused on the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Existing health privacy law
> - 插入语（举例）: such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
> - 谓语 V: is primarily focused on
> - 宾语 O: the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online
> - 定语从句: (that/in which) hospitals ... store health records online
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 举例插入语 | such as | Existing health privacy law |
> | 省略关系词的定语从句 | (that/in which) | the way |
> | 四个并列名词作从句主语 | and | hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics, insurance companies |
> | 方式副词 | primarily | is focused |
> | 地点/方式状语 | online | store |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Existing health privacy law〕+ is + primarily focused on + 〔the way ...〕
>   ├── 插入语: such as the HIPAA → 举例，删除后主干完整
>   └── 定从（省略 that/in which）: 〔hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies〕+ store + 〔health records〕+ (online)
>         └── 四个主体并列 → 法律的覆盖范围
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：现有的健康隐私法，例如《健康保险携带和责任法案》，主要关注医院、医生办公室、诊所和保险公司如何在线存储健康记录。
>
> **考点提示**：① `the way` 后接定语从句时用 `that` 或 `in which`，**且两者都可省略**；**不能用 `how`**（`the way how ...` 是错误搭配）；② `such as ...` 是**插入举例**，删除后 `Existing health privacy law is primarily focused on ...` 主干完整；③ `is primarily focused on` 中 `primarily` 修饰 `focused`，强调"主要"而非"全部"，为后文"应用不受保护"留出对比空间；④ 本句列出**四类受监管主体**，恰好对应第 37 题 [A] `Its coverage needs to be extended` 中"coverage（覆盖范围）"一词——法律只管医院诊所，不管应用。

The health information these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting typically do not receive the same legal protections.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：The health information these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting typically do not receive the same legal protections.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: The health information
> - 定语从句: (that/which) these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting
> - 状语 A: typically
> - 谓语 V: do not receive
> - 宾语 O: the same legal protections
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 省略关系代词的定语从句 | (that/which) | The health information |
> | 并列主语 | and | these apps / health data tracking wearables |
> | 复合名词作定语 | health data tracking | wearables |
> | 频度副词作状语 | typically | do not receive |
> | 形容词短语作定语 | the same | legal protections |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔The health information (these apps and ... wearables are collecting)〕+ typically + do not receive + 〔the same legal protections〕
>   ├── 定从: 〔these apps and health data tracking wearables〕+ are collecting → 修饰 information
>   │     └── 主语并列: these apps ↔ health data tracking wearables
>   └── 主谓一致: information（不可数）+ do not receive → 按"各类信息"理解
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：而这些应用和健康数据追踪可穿戴设备所收集的健康信息，通常得不到同等的法律保护。
>
> **考点提示**：① 句子真正的谓语是 `do not receive`，前面的 `are collecting` 是**定语从句谓语**，读到 `are collecting` 就断句会找不到主句；② `The health information ... do not receive` 中 `information` 通常不可数，此处用复数谓语属**意义一致**（按"各类信息"理解），不要误判主语为 `wearables`；③ `health data tracking wearables` 是**多层名词定语**，中心词是 `wearables`，整体译"健康数据追踪可穿戴设备"；④ `the same legal protections` 中的 `same` 回指上句医院病历所受的保护，是第 37 题 [A] `Its coverage needs to be extended` 的**直接依据**——"同等保护"缺失即"覆盖范围需扩大"。

Without additional protections in place, companies may share (and potentially **monetize**) personal health information in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Without additional protections in place, companies may share (and potentially **monetize**) personal health information in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 条件状语 A: Without additional protections in place
> - 主语 S: companies
> - 谓语 V: may share
> - 宾语 O: personal health information
> - 插入语: (and potentially monetize)
> - 方式状语（定语从句）: in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语表条件 | Without | 主句 |
> | 后置定语/补足语 | in place | additional protections |
> | 括号插入并列谓语 | and potentially | share |
> | 省略关系词的定语从句 | (that/in which) | a way |
> | 现在完成时推测 | may not have + 过去分词 | consumers |
> | 并列过去分词 | or | authorized / anticipated |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: (Without additional protections in place) + 〔companies〕+ may share + 〔personal health information〕
>   ├── 括号插入: (and potentially monetize) → 与 share 并列，加强批评
>   └── 方式状语（定从）: in a way (consumers may not have authorized or anticipated)
>         └── 推测语气: may not have done → "可能从未同意过"
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：如果没有额外的保护措施，公司可能会以消费者未曾授权或预料的方式共享（并可能从中获利）个人健康信息。
>
> **考点提示**：① `Without ...` 是**介词短语表条件**"如果没有"，相当于 `If there are no ...`；② `in place` 是习语"**到位、落实**"，`protections in place` = "已落实的保护措施"，不能译"在地方"；③ 括号 `(and potentially monetize)` 是**插入的并列谓语**，`potentially` 是**态度词**——不只是"共享"，还可能"变现"，作者对公司的批评正在此处；④ `in a way consumers may not have authorized` 中 `in a way` 后**省略了 `that/in which`**，`consumers may not have ...` 是定语从句；⑤ `may not have authorized or anticipated` 是**"情态 + 完成式"的推测**："可能从未授权、也未曾预料"，语气比 `did not` 委婉，但否定含义明确。

In 2021, Flo Health faced a Federal Trade Commission(FTC) investigation.

The FTC alleged in a complaint that “despite express privacy claims, the company took control of users’ **sensitive fertility data** and shared it with third parties.”

Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a **Consent Order** requiring the company to get app users’ **express affirmative consent** before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a **Consent Order** requiring the company to get app users’ **express affirmative consent** before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Flo Health and the FTC
> - 谓语 V: settled
> - 宾语 O: the matter
> - 方式状语 A: with a Consent Order
> - 现在分词后置定语: requiring the company to get ... as well as to instruct ...
> - 宾语补足语 1: to get app users' express affirmative consent
> - 时间状语: before sharing their health information
> - 宾语补足语 2: to instruct the third parties to delete the data
> - 定语从句: they had obtained
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 现在分词短语作后置定语 | requiring ... | a Consent Order |
> | 不定式作宾语补足语 | to get ... | the company |
> | 时间状语（介词 + 动名词） | before sharing | to get |
> | 并列不定式 | as well as to instruct | to get |
> | 不定式作宾语补足语（嵌套） | to delete | the third parties |
> | 省略关系代词的定语从句 | (that/which) | the data |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Flo Health and the FTC〕+ settled + 〔the matter〕+ (with a Consent Order)
>   └── 分词定语: requiring + 〔the company〕
>         ├── 要求 1: to get + 〔app users' express affirmative consent〕+ (before sharing their health information)
>         └── 要求 2: as well as to instruct + 〔the third parties〕+ to delete + 〔the data (they had obtained)〕
>               ※ as well as 后为附加要求，层次低于要求 1
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：Flo Health 与 FTC 以一项《同意令》了结此事，该命令要求公司在共享用户健康信息之前，必须获得应用用户的明确肯定同意，并要求其指示第三方删除已获取的数据。
>
> **考点提示**：① `requiring ...` 是**现在分词作后置定语**，逻辑主语是 `a Consent Order`（命令"要求"公司），不是 `Flo Health`；② `require sb to do` 中 `to get` 是**宾语补足语**；③ `before sharing ...` 是**介词 + 动名词**作时间状语，`sharing` 的逻辑主语是 `the company`；④ `as well as to instruct` 与前面的 `to get` **并列**，但 `as well as` 后的成分层次较低（附加要求）；⑤ `instruct the third parties to delete` 是**嵌套的宾补结构**（instruct sb to do），`to delete` 的逻辑主语是 `the third parties`；⑥ 第 38 题 [D] `obtain their explicit permission` 正是 `get app users' express affirmative consent` 的同义改写，[A] `seek the approval of the FTC` 主客颠倒——是 FTC 要求公司，不是公司求 FTC 批准。

Section 5 of the FTC Act empowers the FTC to initiate **enforcement action** against unfair or **deceptive** acts, meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析
> **原句**：Section 5 of the FTC Act empowers the FTC to initiate **enforcement action** against unfair or **deceptive** acts, meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm.
>
> **主干提取**：
>
> - 主语 S: Section 5 of the FTC Act
> - 谓语 V: empowers
> - 宾语 O: the FTC
> - 宾语补足语 C: to initiate enforcement action
> - 状语 A: against unfair or deceptive acts
> - 解释性状语（分词）: meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if ...
> - 条件状语从句: if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm
>
> **修饰成分**：
>
> | 类型 | 引导词 / 形式 | 修饰对象 |
> |------|--------------|----------|
> | 介词短语作后置定语 | of the FTC Act | Section 5 |
> | 不定式作宾语补足语 | to initiate ... | the FTC |
> | 介词短语作状语 | against ... | initiate |
> | 现在分词作解释性状语 | meaning | 前句整句 |
> | 条件状语从句 | if | can only act |
> | 并列谓语 | or | are misleading / cause |
> | 形容词作定语 | unjustified | consumer harm |
>
> **结构图解**：
>
> ```
> 主句: 〔Section 5 (of the FTC Act)〕+ empowers + 〔the FTC〕+ 〔to initiate enforcement action〕+ (against unfair or deceptive acts)
>   └── 解释状语: meaning + 〔the FTC〕+ can only act + (after the fact) + 〔if 条件从句〕
>         └── 条件: 〔a company's privacy practices〕+ are misleading + or + cause + 〔unjustified consumer harm〕
>   ※ only ... if = 必要条件："只有……才能"
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：《联邦贸易委员会法》第 5 条授权 FTC 对不公平或欺骗性行为提起执法行动，这意味着，只有当公司的隐私做法具有误导性、或造成不合理的消费者损害时，FTC 才能在事后采取行动。
>
> **考点提示**：① `empower sb to do` = "授权某人做某事"，`to initiate` 是**宾语补足语**，不是目的状语；② `against` 与 `initiate enforcement action` 搭配，表"对……采取行动"；③ `meaning ...` 是**现在分词作解释性状语**，逻辑主语是前句整句，作用是把"授权"限定为"只能事后执法"；④ `only ... if ...` 是**必要条件**"只有……才"，不是"如果……就"，命题若改成"FTC 可随时执法"即为曲解；⑤ `misleading`（误导性的）与 `deceptive`（欺骗性的）是**两个不同层次的词**：前者看结果，后者看手段，不能混为一谈；⑥ 第 37 题 [B] `Its enforcement needs strengthening` 是**干扰项**——本段讲的是 FTC 执法**权限有限、只能事后**，而第 37 题问的是"现有健康隐私法"的覆盖范围（上段），两者不能张冠李戴。

## Paragraph 1

If you look at the apps on your phone, chances are you have at least one related to your health—and probably several. Whether it is a mental health app, a fitness tracker, a connected health device or something else, many of us are taking advantage of this technology to keep better track of our health in some shape or form. Recent research from the Organization for the Review of Care and Health Applications found that 350,000 **health apps** were available on the market, 90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone.

如果你看看手机上的应用，很可能至少有一个与健康有关——而且可能有好几个。无论是心理健康应用、健身追踪器、联网健康设备，还是别的什么，我们中的许多人都正在以某种形式利用这项技术，来更好地追踪自己的健康状况。医疗与健康应用评估组织最近的一项研究发现，市场上可用的**健康应用**有 35 万个，其中仅 2020 年就有 9 万个上线。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `chances are (that) you have ...` 是**省略 that 的表语从句**，`chances are` = "很可能"，比 `probably` 更口语化；`at least one related to your health` 中 `related to ...` 是**过去分词短语作后置定语**，修饰被省略的 `app`。
> - `Whether it is A, B, C or something else` 是**让步状语从句**"无论是……还是……"，列举四类产品后引出主句，阅读时可整体跳过。
> - `take advantage of` = "利用"（中性偏褒），与 `make use of` 近义；`keep better track of` = "更好地追踪/记录"，`track` 在此为名词。
> - `in some shape or form` 是**习语**"以某种形式"，相当于 `in one way or another`。
> - `90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone` 是**"名词/数词 + of which"引导的非限制性定语从句**，`which` 指 `350,000 health apps`；`alone` 在此意为"仅仅、单是"，不是"独自"。
> - 本段用"人人都在用 + 35 万/9 万"两组数据，功能是**引出话题并渲染普遍性**，对应第 36 题 [A] `the prevalence of health apps`；数据本身不是论点，而是论点的支撑。

## Paragraph 2

While these apps have a great deal to offer, it is not always clear how the personal information we input is collected, **safeguarded** and shared online. Existing health privacy law, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, is primarily focused on the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies store health records online. The health information these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting typically do not receive the same legal protections.

尽管这些应用能提供很多东西，但我们输入的个人信息如何被收集、保护和在线共享，却并不总是清晰透明。现有的健康隐私法，例如《健康保险携带和责任法案》，主要关注医院、医生诊所、诊所和保险公司如何在线存储健康记录。而这些应用和健康数据追踪可穿戴设备所收集的健康信息，通常得不到同等的法律保护。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `While these apps have a great deal to offer` 中 `while` 表**让步**"尽管"，不是"当……时"；`have a great deal to offer` = "有很多可取之处"。
> - `it is not always clear how ...` 中 `it` 是**形式主语**，真正主语是 `how` 引导的**主语从句**；`how the personal information ... is collected, safeguarded and shared` 中三个过去分词并列，共用 `is`。
> - `the personal information we input` 是**省略关系代词**的定语从句（= `that we input`），`input` 在此为动词"输入"。
> - `is primarily focused on the way hospitals ... store health records online` 中 `the way` 后**省略了 `that/in which`**，是考研高频考点；`doctors' offices` 指"医生诊室"，不是"医生办公室"的行政含义。
> - `The health information these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting` 同样是**省略关系代词的定语从句**，主语核心是 `The health information`，谓语 `do not receive` 用复数是因为主语中心词 `information` 在此按"各类信息"理解（也可视为命题者笔误，考试时以"这些信息"整体理解即可）。
> - 本段是**转折段**：`While ... to offer` 先扬，`it is not always clear` 后抑，落点在"法律保护不一致"，为第 37 题"覆盖范围需扩大"埋下伏笔。

## Paragraph 3

Without additional protections in place, companies may share (and potentially **monetize**) personal health information in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated. In 2021, Flo Health faced a Federal Trade Commission(FTC) investigation. The FTC alleged in a complaint that “despite express privacy claims, the company took control of users’ **sensitive fertility data** and shared it with third parties.” Flo Health and the FTC settled the matter with a **Consent Order** requiring the company to get app users’ **express affirmative consent** before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.

如果没有额外的保护措施，公司可能会以消费者未曾授权或预料的方式共享（并可能从中获利）个人健康信息。2021 年，Flo Health 公司面临联邦贸易委员会（FTC）的调查。FTC 在一份投诉中称，"尽管有明确的隐私声明，该公司却控制了用户的**敏感生育数据**，并将其与第三方共享。"Flo Health 与 FTC 以一项**《同意令》**了结此事，该命令要求公司在共享用户健康信息之前，必须获得应用用户的**明确肯定同意**，并要求其指示第三方删除已获取的数据。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `Without additional protections in place` 是**介词短语表条件**"如果没有……"，`in place` = "到位、落实"，不是"在地方"。
> - `may share (and potentially monetize) personal health information` 中括号是**插入补充**，`monetize` = "变现、从中获利"，是本文批评商业公司的关键词。
> - `in a way consumers may not have authorized or anticipated` 中 `in a way` 后**省略 `that/in which`**，`consumers may not have ...` 是定语从句；`authorize`"授权"与 `anticipate`"预料"并列，强调"既未同意也未想到"。
> - `alleged in a complaint that ...` 中 `allege` = "（未经证实地）指控、宣称"，新闻法律类文章高频词；`complaint` 在此是法律术语"起诉书、投诉书"。
> - `despite express privacy claims` 中 `express` 是形容词"明确的"（不是动词"表达"），`claims` 指"（隐私）声明"。
> - `took control of users' sensitive fertility data` 中 `fertility data` 指"生育/生殖健康数据"，是本段最敏感的数据类型；`took control of` 比 `collected` 语气更重，暗含"擅自掌控"。
> - `settled the matter with a Consent Order` 中 `settle` = "了结（纠纷）"，`Consent Order` 是法律术语"同意令"（企业不认罪但接受监管要求）。
> - `requiring the company to get ... as well as to instruct ...` 中 `requiring` 是**现在分词作后置定语**修饰 `Consent Order`，后接**两个并列的不定式** `to get` 与 `to instruct`；`express affirmative consent` = "明确、肯定的同意"，`affirmative` 强调"主动作出"而非默示，对应第 38 题 [D] `obtain their explicit permission`。

## Paragraph 4

Section 5 of the FTC Act empowers the FTC to initiate **enforcement action** against unfair or **deceptive** acts, meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm. While the FTC is doing what it can to ensure apps are keeping their promises to consumers around the handling of their sensitive health information, the rate at which these health apps are hitting the market demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is.

《联邦贸易委员会法》第 5 条授权 FTC 对不公平或**欺骗性**行为提起**执法行动**，这意味着，只有当公司的隐私做法具有误导性、或造成不合理的消费者损害时，FTC 才能在事后采取行动。尽管 FTC 正尽其所能确保应用在敏感健康信息的处理上对消费者信守承诺，但这些健康应用进入市场的速度表明，这一挑战有多么巨大。

> [!note] 翻译说明
> - `empowers the FTC to initiate enforcement action against ...` 中 `empower sb to do` = "授权某人做某事"；`initiate` = "发起、提起"；`enforcement action` = "执法行动"。
> - `unfair or deceptive acts` 是**法律固定表述**"不公平或欺骗性行为"，`deceptive` = "欺骗性的"，与后文 `misleading`（误导性的）呼应。
> - `meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if ...` 中 `meaning` 是**现在分词作解释性状语**（= "也就是说"），后接 `if` 条件从句；`after the fact` = "事后"，点明监管的**滞后性**。
> - `cause unjustified consumer harm` 中 `unjustified` = "无正当理由的、不合理的"，是法律文本的限定词，不能漏译。
> - `While the FTC is doing what it can to ensure ...` 中 `while` 表**让步**；`what it can` 是**宾语从句**（= "它所能做的"）；`ensure apps are keeping their promises` 后省略 `that`。
> - `the rate at which these health apps are hitting the market` 是**"介词 + which"引导的定语从句**，`at the rate` 是固定搭配；`hit the market` = "进入市场、上市"。
> - `demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is` 中 `how immense of a challenge this is` 是**感叹意义的宾语从句**，`immense` = "巨大的"；全句逻辑是"监管只能事后补救 + 新应用涌入速度极快 = 挑战巨大"，对应第 39 题 [B]。

## Paragraph 5

As to the prospects for federal legislation, commentators suggest that **comprehensive federal privacy legislation** seems unlikely in the short term. California has been at the forefront of state privacy efforts with the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018. > [!note] 翻译说明
> - `As to the prospects for federal legislation` 中 `as to` = "至于、关于"，`prospects` 用复数表"前景"；`legislation` 是**不可数名词**"立法"，不是"法律条文"。
> - `commentators suggest that ... seems unlikely` 中 `suggest` 在此是"认为、表示"，**不用虚拟语气**（虚拟用法常见于 `suggest that sb (should) do`）。
> - `comprehensive federal privacy legislation` = "全面的联邦隐私立法"，`comprehensive` 是本文与"碎片化州法"对照的关键词。
> - `implementing their own solutions to shore up protections` 中 `to shore up` 是**不定式表目的**，`shore up` 原义"支撑、加固"，引申为"加强、巩固"，是外刊高频短语。
> - `consumer-generated health data` = "消费者生成的健康数据"，指用户自己上传、记录的数据，与医院病历相对。
> - `at the forefront of` = "处于……的最前沿"，`with the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018` 是**with 短语表凭借/伴随**。
> - 本段结构：**联邦层面不行（unlikely）→ 各州先行（States have begun）→ 加州领跑 → 另三州跟进**，落点是"州层面已有立法支持"，对应第 40 题 [D] `has gained legislative support in some states`。

## 一、从句

### 从句：whether ... or ... 引导的让步状语从句

**概述**：`whether A, B, C or D` 在此不是"是否"的选择疑问，而是**让步状语从句**"无论是……还是……"，用来列举多种情形后引出主句。判断依据：`or` 连接的是**并列名词**，且主句不受其选择影响。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `Whether it is A, B, C or something else, 主句` | 让步：无论属于哪一类 | **Whether it is** a mental health app, a fitness tracker, a connected health device **or** something else, many of us are taking advantage of this technology |
| `whether ... or ...`（名词性从句） | "是否……"（本文未出现，对比记忆） | —（如 `It is unclear whether apps comply`） |

> [!warning] 易错点
> 本句 `Whether` 从句后**有逗号**，且 `or` 连接四项并列名词，因此是让步状语从句而非宾语从句。若把它读成"是否心理健康应用……"，句子会失去主句落点。**看到 `Whether ... or ... , 主句` 的结构，一律先按"无论……"理解。**

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 让步从句列举的目的是**扩大范围**：从"一个应用"扩到"所有健康类应用"，为 Next 句的"35 万个"数据做铺垫。第 36 题问引用数据的目的，此处已埋下"普遍性"的语义方向。

### 从句：that 引导的宾语从句（found / alleged / suggest）

**概述**：本文三个引述动词后都接 `that` 宾语从句：`found that`（研究发现）、`alleged that`（指控）、`suggest that`（评论者认为）。宾语从句的 `that` 在口语中可省，但在**正式书面语与法律文本**中通常保留。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `find that + 完整句` | 研究"发现"某事实 | Recent research ... **found that** 350,000 health apps were available on the market |
| `allege in a complaint that + 完整句` | 法律语境"指控、宣称"（尚未证实） | The FTC **alleged in a complaint that** "despite express privacy claims, the company took control of users' sensitive fertility data" |
| `suggest that + 完整句` | 评论者"认为、表示"（非虚拟语气） | commentators **suggest that** comprehensive federal privacy legislation seems unlikely in the short term |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `suggest` 有两种用法：① "建议" → `suggest that sb (should) do`（虚拟）；② "认为、表明" → 从句用**陈述语气**。本句 `seems unlikely` 是陈述语气，说明是第 ② 种。判断法：看从句谓语是否为动词原形；不是原形就不是虚拟。

> [!note] 补充说明
> `allege` 与 `claim` 都译"声称"，但 `allege` 是**法律语境**的"（未经证实）指控"，常与 `complaint / lawsuit` 搭配；`claim` 更中性。本文用 `alleged` 而非 `said`，暗示 FTC 的指控是"单方主张"，这层语气在细节题里可能成为干扰点。

### 从句：how 引导的主语从句（it 作形式主语）

**概述**：`it is not always clear how ...` 中 `it` 是**形式主语**，真正主语是 `how` 引导的主语从句。`how` 在从句中作**方式状语**，从句本身是完整陈述句语序，**不倒装**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `It is + 形容词 + how + 主语 + 谓语` | 形式主语 + 主语从句："……如何……并不清楚" | **it is not always clear how** the personal information we input is collected, safeguarded and shared online |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 这类句子的信息重心在 `how` 之后，且 `how` 从句内部常藏**并列谓语**（`is collected, safeguarded and shared`）。做细节题时，三个动作要分别对应选项，不能只抓一个 `collected`。

> [!warning] 易错点
> `how` 从句中 `the personal information we input` 又套了一个**省略关系代词的定语从句**（见下条）。一句里两层嵌套，是本文第一处"读着读着断线"的位置，建议先划出 `how ... is collected` 的主干，再回头补修饰。

### 从句：while 引导的让步状语从句

**概述**：本文两处 `while` 都是**让步**"尽管"，不是"当……时"，也不是对比"而"。判断依据：主句用 `it is not always clear` / `the rate ... demonstrates` 作**转折落点**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `While + 从句, 主句` | 让步："尽管……但是……" | **While** these apps have a great deal to offer, it is not always clear how the personal information we input is collected |
| `While + 从句, 主句` | 让步："尽管……"（句首） | **While** the FTC is doing what it can to ensure apps are keeping their promises, the rate at which these health apps are hitting the market demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `while` 三义辨析：① "当……时"（时间）；② "尽管"（让步，主句转折）；③ "而"（对比，两分句地位对等）。本文两处均为 ②，因为主句都在**削弱**从句的正面评价（`have a great deal to offer` → `not always clear`；`doing what it can` → `immense challenge`）。
> 联动：对比用法见 [[intermediate/2024-passage3-aging-drivers/grammar-notes.md|2024 Passage 3]] 的"while 表对比"。

### 从句：省略关系代词的定语从句

**概述**：当关系代词在定语从句中作**宾语**时可省略，形成"名词 + 主谓"的紧邻结构。本文连续出现三处，且都夹在长句中，是**断句失误的高发区**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `the personal information (that) we input` | 省略作宾语的关系代词 | the personal information **we input** is collected, safeguarded and shared |
| `The health information (that) these apps ... are collecting` | 主语 + 长定语 + 谓语，易误判主谓 | The health information **these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting** typically do not receive the same legal protections |
| `in a way (that/in which) consumers may not have authorized` | 省略关系词的方式状语从句 | in a way **consumers may not have authorized or anticipated** |

> [!warning] 易错点
> 第二例中，读者容易把 `these apps ... are collecting` 当成主句谓语，从而找不到真正的谓语 `do not receive`。**破解法**：先找"名词 + 动词 + 名词"的完整组合，`The health information ... do not receive ... protections` 才是主干，中间整段是修饰。

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 37 题问"现有健康隐私法暗示了什么"，定位句正是 `The health information ... do not receive the same legal protections`。**省略关系代词的定语从句越长，越可能是出题点**，因为命题人需要"信息密集但结构隐蔽"的句子来制造区分度。

### 从句：the way 后省略 that/in which

**概述**：`the way` 作先行词时，后面的定语从句常用 `that` 或 `in which` 引导，且**两者都可省略**，形成 `the way + 完整句`。这是考研高频"隐形定语从句"。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `the way (that/in which) + 从句` | "……的方式" | is primarily focused on **the way** hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies **store** health records online |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `the way` 后**不能**用 `how` 引导定语从句（`the way how ...` 是错误搭配）。若选项中出现 `the way how`，可直接排除。正确形式：`the way (that) ...` / `the way in which ...` / `how ...`（三选一，不能叠加）。

### 从句：数词 + of which 引导的非限制性定语从句

**概述**：`数词/代词 + of which/whom` 引导非限制性定语从句，`which` 指代前文的复数名词，`of` 表"其中"。这种结构把**数据拆分**，是数据类段落的标准写法。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `数词 + of which + 谓语` | "其中……个" | 350,000 health apps were available on the market, **90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone** |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `90,000 of which launched` 中 `launched` 是**不及物动词的过去式**（"上线、推出"），不是被动。若误读为被动，会以为"9 万个应用被发射"，语义完全跑偏。同类：`the product launched last year`（产品去年上市）。

### 从句：介词 + which 引导的定语从句（at which）

**概述**：`介词 + which` 是正式书面语的定语从句形式，介词由**先行词与从句动词的搭配**决定。本文 `the rate at which ...` 中 `at` 来自固定搭配 `at the rate of`。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `the rate at which + 从句` | "……的速度" | **the rate at which** these health apps are hitting the market demonstrates ... |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 遇到 `介词 + which`，先把先行词代入从句还原：`these health apps are hitting the market **at the rate**`。还原后语序正常，说明介词选对了。第 39 题问 FTC 面临的挑战，答案 `The rapid increase in new health apps` 就来自 `the rate at which ... are hitting the market`。

### 从句：if 引导的条件状语从句

**概述**：`if` 条件从句在此限定 FTC 的**执法前提**：只有"误导"或"造成不合理损害"时才可行动。条件从句是**限定监管权力**的关键。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `主句 + if + 条件从句` | 条件："只有……才……" | the FTC can only act after the fact **if** a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm |

> [!warning] 易错点
> 本句 `only` 与 `if` 配合，构成"**只有……才**"的**必要条件**，不是"如果……就"的充分条件。命题若改成"FTC 可以随时执法"，即为曲解。

## 二、非谓语动词

### 非谓语：现在分词短语作后置定语

**概述**：现在分词作后置定语表**主动、进行**，相当于 `which/that + 主动语态` 的定语从句。本文用于"要求公司……的（命令）"。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `名词 + 现在分词短语` | 主动后置定语，说明该名词的内容 | a Consent Order **requiring the company to get app users' express affirmative consent** |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> `requiring` 的逻辑主语是 `a Consent Order`（命令"要求"公司），不是前面的 `Flo Health`。若挂错主语，会读成"Flo Health 要求公司……"，主客颠倒。**分词作定语时，先找它紧挨的名词。**

### 非谓语：过去分词短语作后置定语

**概述**：过去分词作后置定语表**被动**或**完成**。本文有三类：被动（`connected`）、完成/状态（`consumer-generated`）、评价性（`unjustified`）。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `过去分词 + 名词`（前置） | 被动/状态定语 | a **connected** health device（联网的健康设备） |
| `名词 + 过去分词`（复合形容词） | "由……产生的" | **consumer-generated** health data |
| `形容词性过去分词 + 名词` | 评价："无正当理由的" | **unjustified** consumer harm |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `consumer-generated health data` 是"**复合形容词 + 名词**"结构，`consumer-generated` 整体作定语，相当于 `health data generated by consumers`。同类：`user-uploaded content`、`state-sponsored program`。

### 非谓语：不定式作目的状语

**概述**：不定式表目的时相当于 `in order to do`，可置于句中或句尾。本文目的链清晰：**利用技术 → 追踪健康**、**各州行动 → 加强保护**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `do sth to do sth` | 做某事以达到某目的 | many of us are taking advantage of this technology **to keep better track of our health** |
| `do sth to do sth` | 目的："以……" | States have begun implementing their own solutions **to shore up protections** for consumer-generated health data |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `to keep better track of our health` 中 `track` 是**名词**（`keep track of` 固定搭配），不是动词；`to` 是不定式符号，不是介词。判断法：`to` 后接动词原形 → 不定式；接名词/动名词 → 介词。

### 非谓语：不定式作宾语补足语

**概述**：`empower / require / instruct / ensure` 等动词后接 `sb to do`，不定式作**宾语补足语**，说明宾语要做的动作。本文法律文本中此类结构密集。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `empower sb to do` | 授权某人做某事 | Section 5 of the FTC Act **empowers the FTC to initiate enforcement action** |
| `require sb to do` | 要求某人做某事 | requiring the company **to get app users' express affirmative consent** |
| `instruct sb to do` | 指示某人做某事 | **to instruct the third parties to delete the data** they had obtained |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 38 题问 Flo Health 被要求做什么，答案就在 `requiring the company to get app users' express affirmative consent`。三个 `to do` 中，只有 `to get ... consent` 是"公司必须做的"，`to instruct ... to delete` 是"公司必须指示第三方做的"——**主体不同，不能混答**。

### 非谓语：现在分词作解释性状语（meaning ...）

**概述**：`meaning` 引导的现在分词短语作**解释性状语**，对前文做"也就是说"的补充说明，逻辑主语是前文整句。它常引出**关键限定**，不能跳过。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `..., meaning + 完整句` | 解释前句含义："也就是说" | the FTC can only act after the fact ..., **meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if** a company's privacy practices are misleading |

> [!note] 补充说明
> 本句 `meaning` 后重复了 `the FTC can only act after the fact`，属于**同义复现**（作者怕读者漏掉"事后监管"这一限定）。阅读时遇到 `meaning / that is / in other words`，后面往往是**命题人最喜欢改写的关键句**。

## 三、语态

### 语态：被动语态

**概述**：本文被动语态集中在"信息被收集/共享""法律聚焦于""数据被获取"三处。被动使**动作发出者缺席**，恰好服务于"谁在收集、谁在负责说不清"的主题。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `be + 过去分词`（一般现在） | 客观陈述流程 | the personal information we input **is collected, safeguarded and shared** online |
| `be + 过去分词`（一般现在） | 说明法律关注点 | Existing health privacy law ... **is primarily focused on** the way hospitals ... store health records |
| `be + 过去分词`（一般过去） | 数据"可得"的状态 | 350,000 health apps **were available** on the market |
| `may not have + 过去分词`（被动完成） | 推测"可能未被授权" | in a way consumers **may not have authorized** or anticipated |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `is collected, safeguarded and shared` 三个过去分词**共用一个 `is`**，构成平行被动。翻译时要分别落到"被收集、被保护、被共享"三个动作，不能合并成"被处理"。
> 联动：被动语态"施动者缺席"的表达效果，见 [[intermediate/2024-passage3-aging-drivers/grammar-notes.md|2024 Passage 3]] 的"被动语态"条目。

### 语态：被动语态中的责任模糊

**概述**：本文多处用被动或名词化**隐藏施动者**，形成"问题存在、但无人担责"的修辞效果。这是议论文批评性写作的常见策略。

| 结构 | 隐藏的施动者 | 例句 |
|------|-------------|------|
| `is collected, safeguarded and shared` | 公司/平台（未点名） | how the personal information we input **is collected, safeguarded and shared** online |
| `were available on the market` | 开发者/应用商店（未点名） | 350,000 health apps **were available** on the market |
| `the data they had obtained` | 第三方（被动含义） | instruct the third parties to delete **the data they had obtained** |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 作者批评的对象是"**公司**"而非"技术"：`companies may share ... personal health information`。第 40 题 [A] `has been embraced by health app developers` 与全文批评基调相反，可优先排除。

## 四、时态

### 时态：一般现在时表现状与制度

**概述**：说明性、议论性文章的主干时态。本文用于陈述**现行法律、普遍现象与制度安排**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般现在时 | 普遍现象 | many of us **are taking** advantage of this technology（现在进行时，强调持续） |
| 一般现在时 | 现行法律 | Existing health privacy law ... **is** primarily focused on ... |
| 一般现在时 | 制度授权 | Section 5 of the FTC Act **empowers** the FTC to initiate enforcement action |
| 一般现在时 | 各州现状 | States **have begun** implementing their own solutions（现在完成时） |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 观点题与细节题的答案通常落在**一般现在时**的句子上，因为那是"作者陈述的事实/判断"。过去时句子多用于**举例**（Flo Health 案），例子的功能是支撑现在时的论点。

### 时态：现在完成时表已发生并持续影响

**概述**：`have/has + 过去分词` 强调"从过去持续到现在"或"已完成但对现在有影响"。本文用于"各州已开始行动""近期已通过立法"。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `have begun doing` | 已开始做某事（并仍在进行） | States **have begun implementing** their own solutions |
| `have also recently passed` | 最近已通过（完成，影响 present） | Virginia, Colorado and Utah **have also recently passed** state consumer data privacy legislation |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `have begun implementing` 中 `begin` 后接**动名词**，强调"开始并持续"；若接 `to do` 则更强调"开始做（一次性）"。两种都可，但 `begin doing` 更常见于"长期过程"。

### 时态：一般过去时表具体事件

**概述**：引用具体案例、具体年份的事件时用过去时。本文 Flo Health 案整段用过去时，与前后段的现在时形成"**举例 vs 论点**"的层次。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 一般过去时 | 2021 年具体事件 | In 2021, Flo Health **faced** a Federal Trade Commission investigation |
| 一般过去时 | 指控内容 | The FTC **alleged** ... the company **took** control of ... and **shared** it |
| 一般过去时 | 和解结果 | Flo Health and the FTC **settled** the matter with a Consent Order |

> [!note] 补充说明
> 段落时态切换是**结构性信号**：过去时段落 = 案例/论据；现在时段落 = 现状/论点；完成时段落 = 趋势/进展。读文章时先看时态，能快速判断"哪句是观点、哪句是例子"。

## 五、强调与句式

### 句式：it 作形式主语

**概述**：真正主语（从句或不定式）太长时，用 `it` 占位前置，把长主语后移，避免"头重脚轻"。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `It is + 形容词 + 从句` | 评价某事："……是……的" | **it is not always clear how** the personal information we input is collected |
| `it is desirable and ethical to do`（对比记忆） | 评价做某事（见 Passage 3） | — |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `it is not always clear how ...` 中 `it` **不是**指代前文的某个名词，而是纯形式主语。若强行找指代对象，会误读成"个人信息不清楚"。判断法：`it is + 形容词 + 从句/不定式` 一律按形式主语处理。

### 句式：插入语与补充说明

**概述**：括号、破折号、逗号插入的成分**删除后句子仍完整**。本文用括号插入 `(and potentially monetize)` 与 `(FTC)`，前者是**语气加强**，后者是**缩写说明**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `share (and potentially monetize) personal health information` | 括号补充，加强批评 | companies may **share (and potentially monetize)** personal health information |
| `Federal Trade Commission(FTC)` | 括号给出缩写 | a **Federal Trade Commission(FTC)** investigation |
| `such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act` | 举例插入 | Existing health privacy law, **such as the HIPAA**, is primarily focused on ... |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 括号里的 `potentially monetize` 是**作者态度词**：不只是"共享"，还可能"**变现**"。第 40 题 [A] `has been embraced by health app developers` 若成立，作者就不会用"变现"这种贬义表述——**态度词常在括号里**。

### 句式：平行结构

**概述**：平行结构要求**词性、形式一致**。本文有两处典型：三个过去分词共用一个 `be`；两个不定式共用 `requiring`。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `is + 过去分词 A, 过去分词 B and 过去分词 C` | 三个动作平行 | the personal information ... **is collected, safeguarded and shared** online |
| `requiring sb to do A as well as to do B` | 两个不定式平行 | requiring the company **to get** app users' express affirmative consent **as well as to instruct** the third parties to delete the data |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `as well as` 连接的两个不定式**地位不完全对等**：`as well as` 前的 `to get ... consent` 是核心要求，后的 `to instruct ...` 是附加要求。翻译时用"并要求"体现层次，不要译成"和……一样"。

## 六、介词与连词

### 介词短语作后置定语与状语

**概述**：本文介词短语密集，且介词选择携带语义：`in` 表领域/状态、`at` 表速率/位置、`of` 表所属、`to` 表方向。掌握"介词 + 名词"的固定搭配，是读懂长句的关键。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `in some shape or form` | 以某种形式（方式状语） | to keep better track of our health **in some shape or form** |
| `without additional protections in place` | 如果没有……（条件状语） | **Without additional protections in place**, companies may share ... |
| `in a way + 从句` | 以……的方式 | **in a way** consumers may not have authorized or anticipated |
| `at the rate at which ...` | 以……的速度 | **the rate at which** these health apps are hitting the market |
| `at the forefront of` | 处于……的最前沿 | California has been **at the forefront of** state privacy efforts |
| `in the short term` | 短期内 | seems unlikely **in the short term** |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `in place` 不是"在地方"，而是"**到位、落实**"：`protections in place` = "已落实的保护措施"。同类：`the law is in place`（法律已生效）。这是外刊高频短语，考研常考。

### 连词：while 的双重功能（让步与对比）

**概述**：本文两处 `while` 均为**让步**，但 Passage 3 出现过**对比**用法。两种用法的区别在于：让步的 `while` 从句与主句**语义相反**；对比的 `while` 两分句**地位对等、只是不同**。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `While + 从句（正面）, 主句（负面）` | 让步："尽管" | **While** these apps have a great deal to offer, it is not always clear how ... |
| `While + 从句（努力）, 主句（困难）` | 让步："尽管" | **While** the FTC is doing what it can ..., the rate ... demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is |
| `A ..., while B ...`（对比记忆） | 对比："而" | 见 Passage 3：Some states require ..., **while** a few consider a report a breach |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 判断 `while` 是让步还是对比，看**主句是否在削弱从句**：削弱 → 让步；并列陈述 → 对比。本文两处主句都在说"但是还有问题"，所以是让步。**让步句的落点永远在主句**，做题时读主句即可。

### 连词：as to / despite / such as

**概述**：`as to` 表"关于"，`despite` 表"尽管"（后接名词，不接从句），`such as` 表"例如"（后接名词/名词短语）。三者都是**篇章衔接词**，标示论证方向。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `As to + 名词` | 关于、至于（转换话题） | **As to** the prospects for federal legislation, commentators suggest ... |
| `despite + 名词` | 尽管（后接名词，不接句子） | **despite** express privacy claims, the company took control of ... |
| `such as + 名词` | 例如（举例） | Existing health privacy law, **such as** the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act, is ... |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `despite` 后**不能接完整句子**（那是 `although` 的用法）。`despite express privacy claims` 是"尽管有明确的隐私声明"，`claims` 是名词。若写成 `despite the company claimed privacy`，即为语法错误。

## 七、名词化与表达

### 名词化结构

**概述**：把动词或形容词转化为名词，是**学术英语与新闻英语压缩信息**的核心手段。名词化后句子更短，但**动作的施动者被隐藏**，阅读时须还原成动词短语。

| 名词化形式 | 还原为动词短语 | 例句 |
|-----------|---------------|------|
| `the collection and sharing of health data` | companies collect and share health data | （本文以被动形式出现）`is collected, safeguarded and shared` |
| `enforcement action` | the FTC enforces the law | initiate **enforcement action** against unfair or deceptive acts |
| `comprehensive federal privacy legislation` | the federal government legislates comprehensively on privacy | **comprehensive federal privacy legislation** seems unlikely |
| `consumer-generated health data` | health data generated by consumers | protections for **consumer-generated health data** |
| `express affirmative consent` | users expressly and affirmatively consent | get app users' **express affirmative consent** |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 名词化常是**同义替换题的源头**：`express affirmative consent` = "明确、肯定的同意"，第 38 题 [D] `obtain their explicit permission` 正是其同义改写；`enforcement action` 对应第 37 题 [B] `Its enforcement needs strengthening` 的"enforcement"。

### 弱化表达与责任限定

**概述**：议论文常用情态动词、副词、引述动词来**弱化断言**，避免绝对化。这类词是**推断题的关键**：它们标出"作者确定"与"作者留有余地"的边界。

| 结构 | 弱化程度 | 例句 |
|------|---------|------|
| `may + 动词` | 可能性（非必然） | companies **may** share ... personal health information |
| `potentially` | 潜在（未发生） | (and **potentially** monetize) |
| `seems unlikely` | 委婉判断（非绝对） | legislation **seems unlikely** in the short term |
| `commentators suggest` | 转述他人观点（非作者断言） | **commentators suggest that** ... |
| `alleged` | 未经证实 | The FTC **alleged** ... |
| `typically` | 通常（留例外） | **typically** do not receive the same legal protections |

> [!warning] 易错点
> `seems unlikely` 不等于 `impossible`：作者说的是"**短期内**似乎不太可能"，不是"永远不可能"。第 40 题 [B] `has been a focus of federal policy-making` 属**正反混淆**——联邦层面恰恰"不太可能"，焦点在州层面。

### 复合形容词与名词化定语

**概述**：本文多处使用复合形容词或名词作前置定语，浓缩一个从句，是考研阅读的"信息密度点"。

| 复合形容词/定语 | 构成 | 例句 |
|----------------|------|------|
| `connected` | 过去分词作定语 | a **connected** health device |
| `health data tracking` | 名词 + 动名词（复合定语） | **health data tracking** wearables |
| `consumer-generated` | 名词 + 过去分词 | **consumer-generated** health data |
| `state consumer data privacy` | 多层名词作定语 | **state consumer data privacy** legislation |
| `express affirmative` | 形容词并列作定语 | **express affirmative** consent |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `health data tracking wearables` 中 `health data tracking` 整体修饰 `wearables`（可穿戴设备），不是"健康数据在追踪"。同理 `state consumer data privacy legislation` = "州级消费者数据隐私立法"，中心词是 `legislation`。**多层名词定语从后往前译**。

## 八、补充要点

### 主谓一致：The health information ... do not receive

**概述**：`information` 通常是**不可数名词**，谓语用单数；但本文用 `do not receive`（复数），因为主语被理解为"这些应用与设备所收集的（各类）健康信息"，作者按**复数概念**处理。这是考研中偶见的"意义一致"现象。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `The health information ... typically do not receive` | 意义一致（按"各类信息"理解） | **The health information** these apps and health data tracking wearables are collecting **typically do not receive** the same legal protections |

> [!warning] 易错点
> 不要因为看到 `do` 就怀疑句子错误，也不要因此把主语误判为 `wearables`。**主语中心词是 `information`**，谓语用复数属作者的处理方式；考试时按"这些信息通常得不到同等保护"理解即可，不必纠结单复数。

### 代词指代：which / these apps / the two

**概述**：本文代词指代密集，且跨句指代多。做细节题时，**指代对象判断错误 = 定位错误**。

| 代词 | 指代对象 | 例句 |
|------|---------|------|
| `which` | `350,000 health apps` | 90,000 of **which** launched in 2020 alone |
| `these apps` | 前文的 health apps | **these apps** and health data tracking wearables are collecting |
| `their`（users'） | app users | get app users' express affirmative consent before sharing **their** health information |
| `they`（third parties） | third parties | instruct the third parties to delete the data **they** had obtained |
| `this` | 前文整句"监管只能事后 + 新应用涌入" | demonstrates just how immense of a challenge **this** is |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> `this / these` 指代前文**整句或整段**时，答案往往是对前文的**概括**，不是某个词。第 39 题问"FTC 面临的挑战"，`this` 指的就是"监管滞后 + 应用激增"这一组合，选项 [B] `The rapid increase in new health apps` 只抓了后半，但仍是四个选项中最贴近的——**推断题要选"最贴近"，不是"最完整"**。

### 句法：同义复现与关键词链

**概述**：本文围绕"privacy / protection / consent / legislation"四个关键词展开，同义复现频繁。抓住关键词链，就能快速定位题目。

| 关键词链 | 复现形式 | 功能 |
|---------|---------|------|
| privacy | `privacy claims` / `privacy practices` / `privacy law` / `privacy legislation` | 全文主题 |
| protection | `safeguarded` / `legal protections` / `additional protections` / `shore up protections` | 论证主线 |
| consent | `authorized` / `express affirmative consent` / `explicit permission` | 第 38 题依据 |
| legislation | `federal legislation` / `state ... legislation` / `passed ... legislation` | 第 40 题依据 |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 40 题问"最后一段能得知健康数据保护……"，关键词 `legislation` 在末段复现三次（`federal legislation` / `state ... legislation` / `passed ... legislation`），而 `passed` 对应 [D] `has gained legislative support in some states`。**同义复现密度最高的地方，就是答案所在。**

## 跨节联动复习

| 关联点 | 联动内容 |
|--------|---------|
| `while` 让步 ↔ `while` 对比 | 本文两处让步（主句削弱从句）；Passage 3 一处对比（三种州法并列）。判断法：主句是否在"削弱"从句 |
| 省略关系代词的定语从句 ↔ `the way` 省略 | 二者都是"名词 + 完整句"的隐形定语从句；`the personal information we input` 与 `the way hospitals store ...` 结构相同 |
| `it` 形式主语 ↔ `it` 形式宾语 | 本文只有形式主语；形式宾语（`make it clear that`）可对比记忆 |
| 被动语态 ↔ 名词化 | 二者都**隐藏施动者**：`is collected` 不说"谁收集"，`enforcement action` 不说"谁执法" |
| 弱化表达 ↔ 推断题 | `may / potentially / seems / suggest / alleged` 标出"作者留有余地"的边界，是推断题排除绝对化选项的依据 |
| 平行结构 ↔ 细节题 | `is collected, safeguarded and shared` 三个动作要分别对应选项，漏一个就可能错选 |

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 38 题问 Flo Health 被要求做什么，核心动词是 `get ... consent`（获得同意），同义改写为 [D] `obtain their explicit permission`。**`get / obtain / secure + consent / permission / approval` 是同一语义族**，考研喜欢在这组词上做同义替换。

> `take control of` 比 `collect` 语气重得多：`collect` 是"收集"，`take control of` 是"**擅自掌控**"。FTC 用这个词是为了强调公司**越界**，不是中性描述。若把 `took control of` 理解为"收集了"，会漏掉作者的批评态度。

| **express affirmative consent** | 明确、肯定的同意 | get app users' **express affirmative consent** |
| **Section 5 of the FTC Act** | 《联邦贸易委员会法》第 5 条 | **Section 5 of the FTC Act** empowers the FTC |
| **empower sb to do** | 授权某人做某事 | **empowers the FTC to initiate** enforcement action |
| **unfair or deceptive acts** | 不公平或欺骗性行为 | against **unfair or deceptive acts** |
| **after the fact** | 事后 | the FTC can only act **after the fact** |
| **misleading** | 误导性的 | if a company's privacy practices are **misleading** |
| **unjustified** | 无正当理由的、不合理的 | cause **unjustified** consumer harm |
| **settle**（法律义） | 了结（纠纷） | **settled** the matter |
| **shore up protections** | 加强保护 | **shore up protections** for consumer-generated health data |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `Consent Order` 是美国监管中的常见和解工具：企业**不承认违法**，但接受监管机构的具体要求（如本案的"获得明确同意 + 要求第三方删数据"）。它与"罚款"不同，重点在**行为整改**。

> [!warning] 易错点
> `allege` ≠ `prove`。`The FTC alleged ...` 只是"FTC 指控"，**未经法院证实**。选项若写 `The FTC proved that ...`，即为**偷换语气**。同理，`complaint` 在此不是"抱怨"，而是法律文书"起诉书/投诉书"。

## 五、易混词辨析

| 词对 | 区别 | 本文用法 |
|------|------|----------|
| **privacy vs. confidentiality** | `privacy` 是"隐私（权）"，侧重个人控制信息；`confidentiality` 是"保密（义务）"，侧重不向外泄露 | 本文用 **privacy**（health privacy law）；Passage 3 用 **confidentiality**（a breach of confidentiality） |
| **allege vs. claim** | `allege` 是法律语境"（未经证实）指控"；`claim` 更中性"声称" | The FTC **alleged** ... |
| **ensure vs. assure** | `ensure` = "确保（某事发生）"，接 that 从句；`assure` = "向某人保证"，接 sb | **ensure** apps are keeping their promises |
| **authorize vs. anticipate** | `authorize` = "授权、同意"；`anticipate` = "预料、预期" | consumers may not have **authorized or anticipated** |
| **protect vs. safeguard** | 同义，`safeguard` 更正式，常与数据/权利搭配 | is collected, **safeguarded** and shared |
| **share vs. disclose** | `share` 中性"共享"；`disclose` 正式"披露"（常含"本应保密"意味） | companies may **share** ... personal health information |
| **unfair vs. deceptive** | `unfair` = "不公平"（结果层面）；`deceptive` = "欺骗性"（手段层面） | **unfair or deceptive** acts |
| **comprehensive vs. overall** | `comprehensive` = "全面的、综合的"（强调覆盖面）；`overall` = "总体的" | **comprehensive** federal privacy legislation |

> [!note] 补充说明
> `privacy` 与 `confidentiality` 的区分是本文与 Passage 3 的**联动考点**：Passage 3 讲医生上报是否构成 `breach of confidentiality`（违反保密义务），本文讲健康应用是否侵犯 `privacy`（隐私）。前者是**职业伦理**，后者是**数据权利**。

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> 第 37 题问"现有健康隐私法暗示了什么"，答案 [A] `Its coverage needs to be extended` 的依据是：法律只覆盖 `hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies`，而 `health apps and wearables ... do not receive the same legal protections`。**"覆盖范围"对应 `the same legal protections` 的缺失**，不是"执法不力"（[B]）——本文批评的是**覆盖范围**，监管滞后是 P4 的另一层问题。

## 心得

> [!tip] 解题技巧
> - **五题与段落一一对应**：36→P1、37→P2、38→P3、39→P4、40→P5。每段只出一题，定位成本很低，真正的区分点在**同义改写**，而不是搜索速度。
> - **先分清"法律覆盖"与"执法能力"**：P2 讲 coverage（管谁），P4 讲 enforcement（怎么管、何时管）。第 37 题问 existing health privacy law，答案只能落在 P2 的"覆盖范围"；[B] 把 P4 的执法问题搬进 P2 的题，是**跨段嫁接**。
> - **引语题先定主体**：第 38 题问 Flo Health 被要求做什么，`requiring the company to get ... consent` 的主体是公司，`to instruct the third parties to delete` 的主体是第三方。[A] `seek the approval of the FTC` 把"被监管者"写成"申请者"，主客颠倒。
> - **数据与速度句看论证功能**：P1 的"35 万 / 9 万"服务于"渲染普遍性"，P4 的 `the rate at which ... are hitting the market` 服务于"说明挑战之大"；两者都不是孤立事实，选项必须落在其功能上。
> - **末段抓关键词链**：`federal legislation` → `States have begun` → `passed ... legislation`，`passed` 直接对应 [D] `has gained legislative support in some states`。

> [!warning] 易错点
> - `not always clear` 是**部分否定**（并不总是清楚），不是"完全不清楚"；选项若把它绝对化为"毫无透明度"，即为**程度拔高**。
> - `may not have authorized or anticipated` 是"情态 + 完成式"的推测，语气委婉但否定明确；不要因为 `may` 就理解成"也许授权了"。
> - `suggest` 在 P5 是"认为、表示"，从句用陈述语气 `seems`；若按"建议"去套虚拟语气，会误判整个句子的结构。
> - `seems unlikely in the short term` 有**双重限定**（"似乎" + "短期内"），[B] `has been a focus of federal policy-making` 把"不太可能"改成"已是焦点"，属**正反混淆**。
> - `The health information ... do not receive` 的谓语是复数，但主语中心词仍是不可数的 `information`；不要误把 `wearables` 当主语。

> [!question] 反思问题
> - 如果 P2 把 `do not receive the same legal protections` 改成 `receive less attention`，第 37 题 [A] 还成立吗？"保护缺失"与"关注不足"在选项设置上如何区分？
> - 第 39 题 [D] `The difficulty in assessing consumer harm` 为什么"看似合理"却不对？原文的 `unjustified consumer harm` 是**执法门槛**，还是**执法困难**？

- [C] 说明 FTC 的执法效率很高
- [D] 论证生育数据比其他数据更敏感
- **答案：B**。P2 提出"无同等保护"的判断，P3 用 Flo Health 案落地：`took control of ... and shared it with third parties` 正是 `may share (and potentially monetize)` 的实例。[A] 由个案推全体；[C] 与"只能事后"相反；[D] 文中未作比较。

### 5. 写作迁移

请用 80–120 词写一段"法律滞后于技术"的论证，至少使用本文两个结构：

- 参考结构：① `Existing law is primarily focused on X, while Y ... do not receive the same legal protections`；② `the rate at which ... demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is`。
- 参考思路：先指出现行制度只覆盖旧对象 → 再说明新对象处于保护真空 → 最后以"变化速度"强调立法难度，并提出"分层监管 + 事后追责"的过渡方案。
