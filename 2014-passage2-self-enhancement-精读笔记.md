# 2014 Passage 2：自我美化与外貌吸引力 · 精读笔记

## 背景

文章从“高于平均效应”引入，介绍尼古拉斯·埃普利让人从加工过的一组照片中认出原照的实验，并将发现联系到社交媒体上的选择性展示。难点在于区分实验设计、观察结果和未获支持的解释。

---

## 文章原文

An article in Scientific American has pointed out that **empirical research** says that, actually, you think you're more beautiful than you are. We have a **deep-seated** need to feel good about ourselves and we naturally employ a number of **self-enhancing strategies** to achieve this. Social psychologists have **amassed** oceans of research into what they call the “**above average effect**”, or “**illusory superiority**”, and shown that, for example, 70% of us rate ourselves as above average in leadership, 93% in driving and 85% at getting on well with others—all obviously statistical impossibilities.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · B1
> **原句**：Social psychologists have amassed oceans of research into what they call the “above average effect”, or “illusory superiority”, and shown that, for example, 70% of us rate ourselves as above average in leadership, 93% in driving and 85% at getting on well with others—all obviously statistical impossibilities.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Social psychologists
> - 谓语 V: have amassed … and (have) shown …
> - 宾语 O: oceans of research …；that 从句
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | into what they call … | 限定 research 的主题 |
> | what … | 介词 into 的宾语，what 在内部作 call 的宾语 |
> | or “illusory superiority” | 同一概念的别名 |
> | for example | 举例标记 |
> | 93% in driving / 85% at getting on well … | 承接 of us rate ourselves as above average |
> | —all … | 总结三个比例 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> Social psychologists have
> ├── amassed oceans of research
> │   └── into what they call [the “above average effect”, or “illusory superiority”]
> └── shown that
>     ├── 70% of us rate ourselves as above average in leadership
>     ├── 93% (… rate ourselves as above average) in driving
>     ├── 85% (… rate ourselves as above average) at getting on well with others
>     └── 破折号：all obviously statistical impossibilities
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：社会心理学家围绕他们所谓的“高于平均效应”或“虚幻优越感”积累了大量研究。他们发现，例如，70%的人认为领导能力高于平均水平，93%的人这么评价自己的驾驶能力，85%的人认为自己比一般人更善于与人相处——按原文的说法，这些比例从统计上看显然不可能。
>
> **考点提示**：Q26 的数字是“这样评价自己的人占多少”，不是“能力高出平均值多少”。作答抓自评偏高，不必把作者的统计说法当成数学定理。

We **rose-tint** our memories and put ourselves into **self-affirming** situations. We become defensive when criticized, and apply **negative stereotypes** to others to boost our own esteem. We stalk around thinking we're **hot stuff**.

Psychologist and behavioural scientist Nicholas Epley oversaw a key study into self-enhancement and attractiveness. Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others, he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves from a **lineup** including versions that had been altered to appear more and less attractive.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · B2
> **原句**：Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others, he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves from a lineup including versions that had been altered to appear more and less attractive.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: he
> - 谓语 V: asked
> - 宾语 O: them
> - 补语 C: to identify an original photograph of themselves from a lineup
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | Rather than have … | 排除另一种实验设计 |
> | have people rate … | 使役 have 后用不带 to 的不定式 |
> | compared with others | 在与他人对比的条件下，语义上比较外貌 |
> | including versions … | 说明一组照片的构成 |
> | that had been altered … | 修饰 versions，过去完成时被动 |
> | to appear more and less attractive | 改动的目的；有的更好看，有的更不好看 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> 主句：he asked them
> ├── to identify an original photograph of themselves
> │   └── from a lineup
> │       └── including versions
> │           └── that had been altered to appear more and less attractive
> └── 对照：Rather than have people [simply rate their beauty compared with others]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：他并没有让参与者简单地与他人相比、给自己的相貌打分，而是请他们从一组照片中认出自己未经修改的那张，其中有些照片被改得更好看，有些则被改得不那么好看。
>
> **考点提示**：别把两种实验方式合成一个。`rather than` 后是被排除的方法；主句才是实际操作。

Visual recognition, reads the study, is “an automatic psychological process, occurring rapidly and **intuitively** with little or no apparent **conscious deliberation**”.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · B3
> **原句**：Visual recognition, reads the study, is “an automatic psychological process, occurring rapidly and intuitively with little or no apparent conscious deliberation”.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: Visual recognition
> - 谓语 V: is
> - 表语 C: an automatic psychological process
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | reads the study | 报道语，the study 为主语 |
> | occurring … | 补充说明 process |
> | rapidly and intuitively | 方式副词 |
> | with little or no apparent conscious deliberation | 几乎没有可察觉的有意识思考 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> Visual recognition is an automatic psychological process
> ├── 插入归属：reads the study
> └── occurring
>     ├── rapidly and intuitively
>     └── with little or no apparent conscious deliberation
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：研究报告写道，视觉识别是“一种自动发生的心理过程，既迅速又凭直觉，几乎看不到有意识思考的过程”。
>
> **考点提示**：Q27 找同义转述：`intuitively` 对应凭直觉的反应；文中没有说这是自我防御，也没有说是有意识选择。

If the subjects quickly chose a **falsely flattering** image—which most did—they genuinely believed it was really how they looked.

Epley found no significant gender difference in responses. Nor was there any evidence that those who self-enhanced the most (that is, the participants who thought the most positively **doctored** pictures were real) were doing so to **make up for** **profound insecurities**.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · B4
> **原句**：Nor was there any evidence that those who self-enhanced the most (that is, the participants who thought the most positively doctored pictures were real) were doing so to make up for profound insecurities.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: there（存在句引导成分）
> - 谓语 V: was
> - 补足语 C: any evidence …（存在的事物；Nor 使 was 提到 there 前）
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | Nor | 接续上一句的否定 |
> | that those … were doing so … | evidence 的内容从句，that 不作成分 |
> | who self-enhanced the most | 限定 those |
> | that is, the participants … | 换言解释 |
> | who thought … were real | 修饰 participants；thought 后省 that |
> | doing so | 回指 self-enhanced |
> | to make up for … | 表假设的补偿目的 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> Nor was there any evidence
> └── that [those who self-enhanced the most] were doing so
>     ├── 括注：that is, the participants
>     │   └── who thought [(that) the most positively doctored pictures were real]
>     └── 目的：to make up for profound insecurities
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：也没有证据表明，最倾向于美化自我形象的人（即把美化程度最高的照片认作原照的参与者）是为了弥补内心深处的不安全感才这么做的。
>
> **考点提示**：`Nor` 只使主句倒装。逻辑上否定的是“有证据支持这种动机”，不是断言参与者没有不安全感。

In fact, those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale were real directly corresponded with those who showed other markers for having higher self-esteem.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · B5
> **原句**：In fact, those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale were real directly corresponded with those who showed other markers for having higher self-esteem.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: those who thought …
> - 谓语 V: corresponded
> - 补足语 C: with those who showed other markers for having higher self-esteem
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | In fact | 承上转入正面观察 |
> | who thought … | 限定前一组人 |
> | that the images … were real | thought 的宾语从句 |
> | higher up the attractiveness scale | 后置修饰 images |
> | directly | 修饰 corresponded |
> | who showed … | 限定后一组人 |
> | for having higher self-esteem | 说明 markers 表征的是什么 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> In fact, [those who thought …] directly corresponded with [those who showed …]
> ├── 第一组：who thought that [the images higher up the attractiveness scale] were real
> └── 第二组：who showed [other markers for having higher self-esteem]
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：事实上，把吸引力评分较高的照片认作原照的人，恰好也在其他指标上表现出较高的自尊水平。
>
> **考点提示**：Q28 把两组 `those who` 对齐：较高自尊与更积极的外貌判断对应，`corresponded with` 并未表明哪一方导致另一方。

“I don’t think the findings that we have are any evidence of personal **delusion**,” says Epley. “It’s a reflection simply of people generally thinking well of themselves.” If you are depressed, you won’t be self-enhancing.

Knowing the results of Epley's study, it makes sense that many people hate photographs of themselves **viscerally**—on one level, they don't even recognise the person in the picture as themselves.

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · B6
> **原句**：Knowing the results of Epley's study, it makes sense that many people hate photographs of themselves viscerally—on one level, they don't even recognise the person in the picture as themselves.
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: it（形式主语）
> - 谓语 V: makes
> - 宾语 O: sense
> - 后置主语 S: that many people hate …
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | Knowing the results … | 解释判断依据；理解者是作者或读者，不是 it |
> | of Epley's study | 限定 results |
> | that many people hate … | 后置内容从句 |
> | viscerally | 修饰 hate |
> | —on one level … | 破折号后独立分句，补说心理原因 |
> | in the picture | 修饰 person |
> | as themselves | recognise 的识别结果 |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> 认识前提：Knowing the results of Epley's study
> 主句：it makes sense
> └── 后置主语：that many people hate photographs of themselves viscerally
> 破折号补说：on one level, they don't even recognise
> └── the person in the picture as themselves
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：知道了埃普利的研究结果，也就不难理解为什么许多人会本能地厌恶自己的照片——从某种意义上说，他们甚至不觉得照片里的人是自己。
>
> **考点提示**：Q29 从破折号后的解释推断 `viscerally` 侧重本能反应。分析 `Knowing` 时不要硬把没有实际指称的 `it` 当作执行者。

Facebook, therefore, is a self-enhancer's paradise, where people can share only the most flattering photos, **the cream of** their wit, style, beauty, intellect and lifestyles. It's not that people's profiles are dishonest, says Catalina Toma of Wisconsin-Madison University, "but they portray an **idealised** version of themselves."

> [!abstract]- 长难句分析 · B7
> **原句**：It's not that people's profiles are dishonest, says Catalina Toma of Wisconsin-Madison University, "but they portray an idealised version of themselves."
>
> **主干提取**
> - 主语 S: It（构式引导成分）；they
> - 谓语 V: is；portray
> - 宾语 O: an idealised version of themselves（第二分句）
> - 表语 C: not that people's profiles are dishonest（第一分句）
>
> **修饰成分**
>
> | 成分 | 功能 / 归属 |
> | --- | --- |
> | not that people's profiles are dishonest | 否定对现象的解释 |
> | says Catalina Toma … | 插入报道语 |
> | of Wisconsin-Madison University | 交代所属机构 |
> | but they portray … | 给出更准确的解释，与前项对照 |
> | of themselves | 限定 version |
>
> **结构图解**
> ```
> 纠偏构式：It's not that …, but …
> ├── 排除：people's profiles are dishonest
> ├── 插入归属：says Catalina Toma of Wisconsin-Madison University
> └── 修正：they portray an idealised version of themselves
> ```
>
> **参考译文**：威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校的卡塔利娜·托马说，并不是说人们的个人主页不诚实，而是说他们呈现的是理想化的自己。
>
> **考点提示**：Q30 把 `only the most flattering photos` 与 `idealised version` 合读：不展示不理想的一面，与编造个人资料不是一回事。

## 翻译对照

### 第 1 段

**English**

An article in Scientific American has pointed out that **empirical research** says that, actually, you think you're more beautiful than you are. We have a **deep-seated** need to feel good about ourselves and we naturally employ a number of **self-enhancing strategies** to achieve this. Social psychologists have **amassed** oceans of research into what they call the “**above average effect**”, or “**illusory superiority**”, and shown that, for example, 70% of us rate ourselves as above average in leadership, 93% in driving and 85% at getting on well with others—all obviously statistical impossibilities.

**中文**

《科学美国人》上的一篇文章指出，实证研究表明，你其实觉得自己比实际长得更好看。我们内心深处有一种需要，那就是对自己有良好的感觉；为此，我们自然而然地采用一系列自我提升策略。社会心理学家围绕所谓的“高于平均效应”，也就是“虚幻优越感”，积累了大量研究。他们发现，比如，70% 的人认为自己的领导能力高于平均水平，93% 的人对自己的驾驶水平这么看，85% 的人则认为自己比一般人更善于与人相处——原文称，这些比例从统计上看显然不可能。

> [!note] 译注
> “统计上不可能”是作者的说法。若以算术平均值为界，超过半数的人高于平均值并非数学上不可能；本段重点是人们倾向于高估自己。

### 第 2 段

**English**

We **rose-tint** our memories and put ourselves into **self-affirming** situations. We become defensive when criticized, and apply **negative stereotypes** to others to boost our own esteem. We stalk around thinking we're **hot stuff**.

**中文**

我们会美化自己的回忆，主动置身于能让自己获得自我肯定的情境中。受到批评时，我们会摆出防御姿态；我们还会用负面的刻板印象看待别人，以提高自我评价。我们昂首阔步，觉得自己了不起。

> [!note] 译注
> 本文的 self-enhancement 指让自我评价或自我形象显得更好，不是指实际提升能力。

### 第 3 段

**English**

Psychologist and behavioural scientist Nicholas Epley oversaw a key study into self-enhancement and attractiveness. Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others, he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves from a **lineup** including versions that had been altered to appear more and less attractive. Visual recognition, reads the study, is “an automatic psychological process, occurring rapidly and **intuitively** with little or no apparent **conscious deliberation**”. If the subjects quickly chose a **falsely flattering** image—which most did—they genuinely believed it was really how they looked.

**中文**

心理学家兼行为科学家尼古拉斯·埃普利主持了一项关于自我提升与外貌吸引力的重要研究。他没有让参与者简单地拿自己和别人比、给自己的长相打分，而是请他们从一组照片中辨认出自己未经修改的那一张。照片中既有经过修饰、显得更好看的版本，也有改得不那么好看的版本。研究报告写道，视觉识别是“一种自动发生的心理过程，既迅速又凭直觉，几乎看不出有意识思考的痕迹”。如果受试者很快选中一张把自己美化了的照片——大多数人确实这么选——那他们是真的相信自己就长那个样子。

### 第 4 段

**English**

Epley found no significant gender difference in responses. Nor was there any evidence that those who self-enhanced the most (that is, the participants who thought the most positively **doctored** pictures were real) were doing so to **make up for** **profound insecurities**. In fact, those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale were real directly corresponded with those who showed other markers for having higher self-esteem. “I don’t think the findings that we have are any evidence of personal **delusion**,” says Epley. “It’s a reflection simply of people generally thinking well of themselves.” If you are depressed, you won’t be self-enhancing.

**中文**

埃普利发现，受试者的反应没有显著的性别差异。也没有证据表明，自我提升倾向最强的人（也就是把美化程度最高的照片当成原照的参与者）这么做，是为了弥补内心深处的不安全感。事实上，把吸引力评分较高的照片认作原照的人，恰好也在其他指标上表现出更高的自尊水平。埃普利说：“我不认为我们的研究结果能证明人们是在自欺。这只不过反映出人们普遍对自己的评价比较好。”如果你处于抑郁状态，就不会有这种拔高自我评价的倾向。

### 第 5 段

**English**

Knowing the results of Epley's study, it makes sense that many people hate photographs of themselves **viscerally**—on one level, they don't even recognise the person in the picture as themselves. Facebook, therefore, is a self-enhancer's paradise, where people can share only the most flattering photos, **the cream of** their wit, style, beauty, intellect and lifestyles. It's not that people's profiles are dishonest, says Catalina Toma of Wisconsin-Madison University, "but they portray an **idealised** version of themselves."

**中文**

知道了埃普利的研究结果，也就不难理解为什么许多人会本能地厌恶自己的照片——从某种意义上说，他们甚至不觉得照片里的人是自己。因此，Facebook 成了乐于美化自我形象者的天堂：在那里，人们可以只分享最衬自己的照片，只展示自己在机智、风格、美貌、才智和生活方式等方面最出彩的一面。威斯康星大学麦迪逊分校的卡塔利娜·托马说：“这并不是说人们的个人主页不诚实，而是说他们呈现的是理想化的自己。”

## 阅读理解题

### 26. According to the first paragraph, social psychologists have found that ____.
- [A] our self-ratings are unrealistically high
- [B] illusory superiority is a baseless effect
- [C] our need for leadership is unnatural
- [D] self-enhancing strategies are ineffective

### 27. Visual recognition is believed to be people's ____.
- [A] rapid matching
- [B] conscious choice
- [C] intuitive response
- [D] automatic self-defence

### 28. Epley found that people with higher self-esteem tended to ____.
- [A] underestimate their insecurities
- [B] believe in their attractiveness
- [C] cover up their depressions
- [D] oversimplify their illusions

### 29. The word “viscerally” (Para. 5) is closest in meaning to ____.
- [A] instinctively
- [B] occasionally
- [C] particularly
- [D] aggressively

### 30. It can be inferred that Facebook is a self-enhancer's paradise because people can ____.
- [A] present their dishonest profiles
- [B] define their traditional lifestyles
- [C] share their intellectual pursuits
- [D] withhold their unflattering sides

### 参考答案与定位

> [!note]- 展开核对（根据原文推断，源文件未附答案键）
> - **26. A**：第 1 段不同领域的高自评比例共同指向自我评分偏高。
> - **27. C**：研究对视觉识别的解释包含 `intuitively` 和 `little or no … conscious deliberation`。
> - **28. B**：第 4 段把认作原照的图片吸引力较高与其他高自尊指标联系起来，只给出对应关系。
> - **29. A**：`viscerally` 表示近乎本能的反应，破折号后解释这种不适可能来自认不出照片里的自己。
> - **30. D**：`share only` 表明可以不展示不理想的一面；最后一句明确区别理想化与不诚实。

## 语法要点

根据文章原文提取考点；词义与用法另见 [[intermediate/2014-passage2-self-enhancement/固定搭配与词组笔记.md|本篇固定搭配与词组笔记]]。例句栏为连续原文片段。

### 从句：名词性从句

先确认从句的句法位置，再确定 `that`、`what` 或 `how` 在从句内是否承担成分。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `point out that … says that …` | P1. 两层 `that` 依次引导 `pointed out`、`says` 的宾语从句，内层还有 `think` 的零 `that` 宾语从句。`actually` 是插入性副词。 | `has pointed out that empirical research says that, actually, you think you're more beautiful than you are` |
| `what + 主语 + call + 名称` | P1. `what` 引导的名词性结构作介词 `into` 的宾语，`what` 本身是 `call` 的宾语，名称是其宾补。`or` 后为同一现象的另一称呼。 | `research into what they call the “above average effect”, or “illusory superiority”` |
| `believe (that) it was how …` | P3. `believed` 后省略 `that`；嵌套的 `how they looked` 作 `was` 的表语，意为“他们长相的真实样子”，不是“他们如何去看”。 | `they genuinely believed it was really how they looked` |
| `evidence that + 完整分句` | P4. `that` 说明“证据所支持的命题”，在从句中不作成分。判断完整性时先圈掉括号解释。 | `any evidence that those who self-enhanced the most (that is, the participants who thought the most positively doctored pictures were real) were doing so to make up for profound insecurities` |
| `it makes sense that …` | P5. `it` 是形式主语，`that` 从句是后置的实际主语（“……是可以理解的”）。句首 `Knowing …` 的理解者是读者，并不是形式主语 `it`。 | `Knowing the results of Epley's study, it makes sense that many people hate photographs of themselves viscerally` |

> [!tip] 区分两个 `that`
> 本节的 `evidence that …` 可问“是什么命题”；[[#从句：定语从句]] 的 `findings that we have` 可问“我们有什么”，关系词在 `have` 后填补宾语空位。

### 从句：定语从句

关注先行词以及从句内部的缺位；遇到嵌套时，先找最内层，再找外层主干。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `名词 + that + 被动谓语` | P3. `that` 指 `versions` 并作从句主语，`had been altered` 为过去完成时被动；修改发生在辨认照片之前。`more and less attractive` 指有的版本被改得更好看，有的则更不好看。 | `versions that had been altered to appear more and less attractive` |
| `those who thought that …` | P4. `those` 指人，外层 `who` 作定语从句主语，`thought` 后接 `that` 宾语从句；`were real` 是内层谓语。 | `those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale were real` |
| `名词 + that + 主语 + 及物动词` | P4. `that` 指 `findings`，作 `have` 的宾语，因而可以省略；不同于 `evidence that …`。 | `the findings that we have` |
| `先行词, where + 完整分句` | P5. `where` 为关系副词，指的是 Facebook 这个供人展示形象的空间，在非限制性定语从句中作地点状语。 | `a self-enhancer's paradise, where people can share only the most flattering photos` |

---

### 从句：状语从句

把条件或场景从主要命题中剥离；只有当省略部分可由主句补出时，才按完整形式理解。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `when + 过去分词` | P2. 时间状语从句省略 `we are`；`criticized` 是被动，不是主句的第二个谓语。 | `We become defensive when criticized` |
| `if + 一般过去时, 主句一般过去时` | P3. 在过去的实验情境下陈述作为判断依据的条件，不表示与过去事实相反的虚拟假设；破折号内另说明多数受试者确实这样选。 | `If the subjects quickly chose a falsely flattering image—which most did—they genuinely believed it was really how they looked.` |

---

### 非谓语动词：不定式与分词

区分谓语和附属结构：先确定谁做动作，再判断是目的、宾补、定语还是伴随情况。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `rather than + 动词原形`；`have + 人 + 动词原形` | P3. 句首 `rather than have …` 排除一种实验做法；`have` 是使役动词，`people` 后接不带 `to` 的 `rate`。主句 `asked them to identify` 的宾补却必须带 `to`。 | `Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others, he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves` |
| `名词 + -ing 短语` | P3. `including versions …` 说明 `lineup` 包含哪些照片；其内部 `versions` 又由定语从句修饰。 | `a lineup including versions that had been altered to appear more and less attractive` |
| `名词, -ing 短语` | P3. `occurring …` 补充说明前面的 `process` 如何发生。不能把 `occurring` 误作句子谓语，去掉插入的 `reads the study` 后，主干仍是 `Visual recognition … is …`。 | `an automatic psychological process, occurring rapidly and intuitively with little or no apparent conscious deliberation` |
| `to + 动词原形` 作目的状语 | P2. 目的成分回答“为何那样评价他人”：借贬低别人来提升自我评价。P4 的 `to make up for …` 同样表目的，但该目的处于“无证据表明”的范围内。 | `apply negative stereotypes to others to boost our own esteem` |

> [!warning] 不要逢 `to` 就当不定式
> `to boost` 前的 `to` 标记不定式，`to others` 则是介词短语；同句中先看 `to` 后接动词还是名词性成分。搭配见 [[intermediate/2014-passage2-self-enhancement/固定搭配与词组笔记.md#认知与评价|认知与评价]]。

### 倒装与强调：否定倒装与纠偏

否定副词前置可能触发倒装；而 `not that … but …` 的重点在于纠正推断，并不触发倒装。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `Nor + be / 助动词 / 情态动词 + 主语…` | P4. 承接 `found no significant gender difference`，表示“也没有……”。此处还原普通语序为 `there was not any evidence …`，只把 `was` 移到 `there` 之前；后面的 `that` 从句不倒装。 | `Nor was there any evidence` |
| `It's not that …, but …` | P5. 先排除“主页不诚实”这一过强推断，再说呈现的是理想化的一面。`says Catalina Toma …` 是引语归属，不打断两分句的语义对照。 | `It's not that people's profiles are dishonest, says Catalina Toma of Wisconsin-Madison University, "but they portray an idealised version of themselves."` |

---

### 补充要点：比较与并列省略

判断被省略或共享的成分时，先还原可重复的结构，再比较各分项的信息。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `比较级 + than + 主语 + be` | P1. `than you are` 的比较项是实际的外貌；`are` 后不重复形容词，理解为“比你实际上更漂亮”。 | `you think you're more beautiful than you are` |
| `百分比 + 不同领域` 的并列省略 | P1. 后两项承接 `of us rate ourselves as above average`；百分比是自评者的比例，不是能力比平均值高出的幅度。 | `70% of us rate ourselves as above average in leadership, 93% in driving and 85% at getting on well with others` |
| `have + 过去分词 … and + 过去分词 …` | P1. `amassed` 与 `shown` 共享主语 `Social psychologists` 和助动词 `have`。别把第二个谓语误归给嵌套结构 `what they call …` 中的 `they`。 | `Social psychologists have amassed oceans of research into what they call the “above average effect”, or “illusory superiority”, and shown that` |

---

### 补充要点：插入语与动词替代

引语标记和括号补充可暂时跳过以找主干；替代词则要回指前文，才能译出具体动作。

| 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `, reads the study,` | P3. 插入语用来交代引文出处，`reads` 表示“报告上写道”，主语是 `the study`。其外层 `Visual recognition … is …` 不受影响。 | `Visual recognition, reads the study, is` |
| `—which most did—` | P3. 关系结构补充说明多数受试者确实选了那样的照片；`did` 避免重复前面的选择行为。 | `quickly chose a falsely flattering image—which most did` |
| `do so` | P4. `doing so` 回指 `self-enhanced`，不能译成模糊的“那样做”而不交代行为。括号里的 `that is` 是换言说明，写的是对应的实验操作定义。 | `were doing so to make up for profound insecurities` |

> [!tip] 联动复习
> `Nor was there …` 的否定范围、`evidence that …` 的内容从句、`doing so` 的回指都在同一句。可先划出 `Nor was there any evidence`，再进从句找 `those … were doing so`，最后用括号内容辨认“最倾向于自我美化的人”如何在实验中被界定。

## 固定搭配与词组

按语境收录原文搭配与术语。句法见 [[intermediate/2014-passage2-self-enhancement/grammar-notes.md|本篇语法要点]]。例句栏为连续原文片段；📌 标记直接帮助定位题目线索的表达。

### 认知与评价

| 搭配 / 词组 | 释义与辨析 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `empirical research` | 实证研究：以观察或实验所得的证据为基础，而不只凭理论或直觉。 | `empirical research says that` |
| `a deep-seated need to do sth` | 一种根深蒂固的需要。`deep-seated` 在此说明心理需要深层而持久，不必译成贬义的“顽固”。 | `a deep-seated need to feel good about ourselves` |
| `self-enhancing strategies` | 使自我评价更积极的策略；这里不是通过练习真正提高能力。 | `employ a number of self-enhancing strategies` |
| `amass oceans of research into sth` | 积累大量关于某事的研究。`oceans of` 是夸张说法，相当于“大量的”。 | `amassed oceans of research into what they call` |
| 📌 `above average effect` / `illusory superiority` | 高于平均效应／虚幻优越感，即倾向于将自己的相对表现评得过高。P1 给出自评数据，是 Q26 的定位依据。 | `what they call the “above average effect”, or “illusory superiority”` |
| `rate oneself as … in …` | 将自己在某方面评为……。`as` 后接评价结果，`in` 后接评价领域；技能也可用 `at + -ing` 表示。 | `rate ourselves as above average in leadership` |
| `get on well with sb` | 与某人相处融洽。此处不是“继续”，应把 `get on with` 与表示关系程度的 `well` 一起读。 | `getting on well with others` |
| `rose-tint one's memories` | 把回忆美化；字面上是“给回忆染上一层玫瑰色”，可联想到用玫瑰色眼镜看事情。 | `rose-tint our memories` |
| `apply negative stereotypes to sb` | 用负面的刻板印象看待某人；`stereotype` 是对某类人形成的模式化看法。 | `apply negative stereotypes to others` |
| `boost one's own esteem` | 提高自我评价。在本文与后文 `self-esteem` 呼应，不是提高别人给自己的评价。 | `boost our own esteem` |
| `be hot stuff` | 口语中指“很了不起、很有魅力”。在 `thinking we're …` 中是带调侃色彩的自我想象。 | `thinking we're hot stuff` |

### 实验、照片与自我评价

| 搭配 / 词组 | 释义与辨析 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| `identify … from a lineup` | 从一排或一组候选对象中辨认出某个目标；本篇的 `lineup` 是一组照片，不是警方列队。 | `identify an original photograph of themselves from a lineup` |
| 📌 `rapidly and intuitively`；`with little or no apparent conscious deliberation` | 迅速且凭直觉，几乎看不出有意识的权衡过程。Q27 要区分“凭直觉反应”和“有意识选择”；`apparent` 指看得出来的。 | `occurring rapidly and intuitively with little or no apparent conscious deliberation` |
| `a falsely flattering image` | 一张把本人拍得或改得比实际更好看的照片；`flattering` 描述照片使外表更讨好，不是照片在“拍马屁”。 | `a falsely flattering image` |
| `positively doctored pictures` | 被朝着更好看的方向修饰过的照片。动词 `doctor` 指改动、加工（有时暗含篡改）；此处只对照照片的实验处理方向。 | `the most positively doctored pictures` |
| `make up for profound insecurities` | 弥补很深的不安全感。P4 的“没有证据”否定的是这个补偿动机有证据支持，而非说受试者都没有不安全感。 | `make up for profound insecurities` |
| 📌 `higher up the attractiveness scale` / `markers for having higher self-esteem` | 更靠近吸引力量表高端／更高自尊水平的指标。Q28 的线索是较高外貌自评和较高自尊同时出现在同一人群中，不表示一者造成另一者。 | `the images higher up the attractiveness scale`；`other markers for having higher self-esteem` |
| `think well of oneself` | 对自己评价较好。`think well of sb` 也可换成人称宾语，表示对某人评价高；不像 `personal delusion` 那样暗含严重失真。 | `people generally thinking well of themselves` |

### 社交媒体与形象展示

| 搭配 / 词组 | 释义与辨析 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 📌 `hate sth viscerally` | 近乎本能地强烈厌恶某物。`viscerally` 侧重来自内心深处的直接反应，不指频率，也不等于有攻击性；Q29 对应 `instinctively`。 | `hate photographs of themselves viscerally` |
| `recognise A as B` | 识认出 A 就是 B；这里不是承认某种称号或地位。 | `recognise the person in the picture as themselves` |
| `the cream of sth` | 某事物最精华、最出色的部分。这里把个人才智、品位和生活方式中最好的一面比作“精华”。 | `the cream of their wit, style, beauty, intellect and lifestyles` |
| 📌 `portray an idealised version of oneself` | 呈现一个理想化的自己。Q30 的关键在 `only`：筛选掉不理想的部分，并不能直接推出所有保留下来的信息都是假的。 | `portray an idealised version of themselves` |

**三、语境理解**

1. `the most positively doctored pictures` 里的 `doctored` 指什么？
   A. 经医生审阅；B. 经过加工修饰；C. 出处不明；D. 带有医疗信息。
2. `the cream of their wit, style, beauty …` 中 `the cream of` 指什么？
   A. 平均水平；B. 最难理解的部分；C. 最出色的部分；D. 不愿公开的部分。

> [!abstract]- 答案
> 1. B：实验者改变了照片中人物看起来的吸引力。
> 2. C：形容人们只展示自己最得意的一面。

## 心得

> [!tip] 阅读策略
> 阅读实验型文章时，给“怎么做”“发现什么”“哪种动机没有证据”分别划线。区分没有证据、证明没有和观察到相关三种说法。
